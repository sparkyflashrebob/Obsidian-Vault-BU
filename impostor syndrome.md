# Imposter Syndrome (Impostor Syndrome)

> Comprehensive master study note on this topic has been compiled at:
> ➡️ **[[Authenticity]]** *(Authenticity, Wholeness, and Overcoming Imposter Syndrome)*

### Core Highlights:
* **The Root in Buddhist Psychology**: Imposter syndrome is diagnosed as an inverted form of **Māna (Conceit / The Measuring Mind)**—specifically *Hīna-māna* ("I am worse / I don't belong / I am a fraud"). The antidote is not striving to feel superior, but dropping the measuring tape entirely.
* **The Split of the Divided Life**: The painful gap between the external performer mask and the internal vulnerable experience, creating sympathetic nervous system hypervigilance.
* **The Committee of the Mind**: Recognizing the imposter critic as just one conditioned sub-personality rather than who you fundamentally are.
* **Wholeness vs. Perfection**: Healing through radical self-honesty, somatic grounding, and touching innate completeness (*Pabhassara Citta*).
