#### 1. Analyzing Simon.app (getsimon.io)

This tool is specifically built for **automated ebook creation** .

* **The "Zero Money" Catch:** While it has an intuitive workflow for turning a manuscript into an ebook, it is generally a **pay-per-use** or subscription service.
* **The Use Case:** It is excellent if you have a finished manuscript and want to format it for Kindle (EPUB) in minutes. However, if your budget is strictly $0, there are even more cost-effective paths.

#### 2. The "Zero Budget" Professional Stack

Since you already use **Obsidian** , you are 80% of the way there. Here is how to finish the book without spending money:

##### **Drafting & Organization: Obsidian**

* Keep using Obsidian. It’s free, and by using the **"Longform" plugin** , you can manage chapters just like the expensive "Scrivener" software.
* **The Omi Connection:** Your Omi wearable transcripts can be pasted directly into Obsidian notes. This costs you $0 in software fees.

##### **Formatting: Reedsy Book Editor**

* Instead of Simon.app, look at **Reedsy Book Editor** .
* **Cost:** Completely **Free** .
* **Function:** It is a web-based "What You See Is What You Get" editor. You paste your text in, and it handles the professional typesetting, chapters, and front matter. It exports perfectly formatted files for Amazon (KDP) and IngramSpark.

##### **Cover Design: Canva (Free Version)**

* You don't need a professional designer to start. Use the free version of Canva; they have "Book Cover" templates specifically sized for Kindle and print-on-demand.

##### **Publishing: Amazon KDP**

* Publishing through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) is free. They take a percentage of sales, but they don't charge you to upload or host the book.

#### 3. Expert Advice for the "Solo Author"

As you transition into retirement, your **time** is your greatest asset. To avoid the "cost trap" Simon Golden mentioned:

1. **Self-Edit First:** Use free tools like the **Hemingway Editor** (web version) to tighten your prose. This reduces the need for expensive professional editors later.
2. **Beta Readers:** Instead of paying for a book coach, find 3–5 trusted peers (perhaps from your mindfulness or AV circles) to read your draft in exchange for a "Special Thanks" in the credits.
3. **The "Simon Says" Confusion:** Be careful not to confuse *Simon.app* (the ebook maker) with *Simon Says* (an AI transcription service). You already have **Omi** for transcription, so you don't need to pay for another transcription service.
