Yesterday we saw how our thoughts lead to our feelings and our feelings develop our attitude. Let us go through the remaining stages of this process today.
1. **My Attitude Brings Me Into Action.**
My attitude dictates my outlook, behaviour and response to people and situations. *So my attitude drives my actions* . Using yesterday's example - I am an employee whose attitude towards my office is one of belongingness. This attitude brings me into action. I give my best performance. I work passionately and align with my company goals.
1. **Repeated Actions Form My Habits.**
Repeating any action twice, thrice, or ten times [makes it a habit.](https://www.brahmakumaris.com/wisdom/blog/eng/neuro-associative-conditioning-habit-change) When I am a high-performing employee, I enjoy all the associated benefits. *It increases my happiness and motivates me to do better in the next task, then the next.* So I get into a habit - of remaining happy, performing and succeeding.
1. **Habits Define My Personality.**
I become what I repeatedly do. So all my habits combined together [shape my personality.](https://www.brahmakumaris.com/wisdom/soul-sustenance/eng/executing-right-habits-consistently) They become an integral part of my life, flowing into my behaviour and guide my way through life. If I am a happy employee, it flows in my personality. In this way my happiness is not restricted to **my workplace but accompanies me everywhere** , leaving its impression in everything I do.
1. **Personality In Action Creates My Destiny.**
As will be my personality, so will be my every action. As will be my action, so will be the result of the action. That result is my Destiny.
**Summary** : This is how all elements come together - my thoughts create feelings, feelings develop my attitude, attitude comes into action, actions repeatedly done cultivate habits, habits influence my personality, and [my personality scripts my destiny.](https://www.brahmakumaris.com/meditation/commentary/each-moment-asks-for-a-virtue-are-you-listening) Therefore I choose how I want to feel the whole day, my whole life. I decide the list of emotions to use and the ones to avoid. I have the power to write a destiny of my choice. So when I create a positive thought, it creates a happy destiny.
