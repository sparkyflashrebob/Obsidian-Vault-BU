Eight Pieces

Eight Pieces of Silk Brocade:

A Qigong exercise

Deep Breathing

Inhale slowly. Fill chest and abdomen with air.

Exhale slowly. Expel last trace of air from body.

Inhale slowly. Fill chest and abdomen with air.

Exhale slowly. Expel last trace of air from body.

Inhale slowly. Fill chest and abdomen with air.

Exhale slowly. Expel last trace of air from body.

Warm-up Swinging Arms

Relax body. Let go completely.

Exercise 1: Two hands upholding the sky

Ready in upright position. Feet shoulder distance apart.

Lace hands. Bring to chest level. Palms down.

Push down slowly.

Stretch to bring hands above head.

Slowly move hands down, drawing a full circle.

Push down slowly.

Stretch to bring hands above head.

Slowly move hands down, drawing a full circle.

Exercise 2: Pulling the Bow

Ready in horse stance.

Raise hands to chest level. Palms facing chest.

Extend one arm outward fully.

Bring hands back to chest position. Return to ready position.

Repeat on other side.

Raise two fingers and focus on fingers.

Exercise 3: Crane spreading its wing

Ready in upright position. Feet shoulder distance apart.

Bring hands to chest level. Palms facing chest.

Rotate one hand outward. Palm away from body.

Move arm above head. Palm faces sky.

Bring arm down, drawing a half circle.

Bring hands to chest level. Palms facing chest.

Rotate one hand outward. Palm away from body.

Move arm above head. Palm faces sky.

Bring arm down, drawing a half circle.

Exercise 4: Looking backward

Ready in upright position. Feet shoulder distance apart.

Raise hands to chest level. Palms facing down.

Turn head to one side when exhaling.

Face side, halfway through exhale arms at 45˚

Return to ready position at end of exhale.

Repeat on the other side.

Turn head to one side when exhaling.

Face side, halfway through exhale arms at 45˚

Exercise 5: Left and right swing

Ready in horse stance.

Turn head. Swing body fully to one side.

Turn head. Swing body fully to the other side.

Exercise 6: Up and down stretch

Ready in upright position. Feet shoulder distance apart.

Stretch both hands upward. Extend fully.

Move hands and head down. Relax completely.

Bend knees. Straighten back.

Bring body to ready position.

Stretch both hands upward. Extend fully.

Move hands and head down. Relax completely.

Bend knees. Straighten back.

Bring body to ready position.

Exercise 7: Diagonal knock

Ready in horse stance.

Make fists. Bring them to chest level. Palms down.

Knock the fist out diagonally. Palms facing sky.

Bring fist back to chest then raise both hands above head.

Move both hands down, drawing a full circle.

Repeat on other side.

Knock the fist out diagonally. Palms facing sky.

Bring fist back to chest then raise both hands above head.

Move both hands down, drawing a full circle.

Exercise 8: Toe and heel bounce

Swing arms slightly to front.

Swing arms towards back, raising both heels.

Swing arms to front, lowering heels.

Repeat this slow movement twice.

Swing arms slightly forward and back.

Bounce heel up and down quickly and forcefully.

Perform this fast movement seven times.
