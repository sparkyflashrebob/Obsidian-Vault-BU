https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zhan\_zhuang&action=edit&section=1

The original zhan zhuang were health methods used by

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoism

; in recent centuries, martial artists who already had static standing methods combined these with the internal mechanics of zhan zhuang to create a superior exercise. The goal of zhan zhuang in martial arts has always been to develop a martially capable body structure,

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-1

but nowadays most practitioners have again returned to a health-preservation orientation in their training, and few teach zhan zhuang as a martial method.

zhan zhuang

is the modern term; it was coined by

Wang Xiangzhai

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang\_Xiangzhai

. Wang, a student of

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xingyiquan

, created a method of kung fu based entirely upon

zhan zhuang

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiquan

, "Intent Fist." Yiquan's method of study is

zhan zhuang

plus movements that continue the feeling of the Standing Post in action.

The most common zhan zhuang method is known as

, "Completely Round," "Round Smoothness") or

, "Tree Hugging" stance). This posture is entirely Taoist in its origins, has many variations, and is the main training posture in all branches of yiquan. This practice has recently also become common practice in tai chi and qigong schools. In xingyiquan, San Ti Shi ( simplified Chinese: 三体势; traditional Chinese: 三體勢; pinyin:

; lit. ' Three Body Postures') has been a root practice for centuries.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-2

https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Zhan\_zhuang&action=edit&section=2

Nitzan Oren, demonstrating a zhan zhuang posture which combines the San Ti posture of xingyiquan and a Hun Yuan hand variation

Those unfamiliar with zhan zhuang can experience severe muscle fatigue and subsequent trembling at first. Later, once sufficient stamina and strength have been developed, the practitioner can use zhan zhuang to work on developing the sensation of "opposing forces," as well as one's central equilibrium and sensitivity to specific areas of tension in the body.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-3

Zhan zhuang has a strong connection with

Traditional Chinese Medicine

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional\_Chinese\_Medicine

. Some schools use the practice as a way of removing blockages in

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi

flow, believing zhan zhuang, when correctly practiced, has a normalizing effect on the body; they claim any habitual tension or tissue shortening (or lengthening) is normalized by the practice, and the body regains its natural ability to function optimally. It is claimed that a normalized body will be less prone to muscular-skeletal medical conditions, and it is also believed that zhan zhuang, when practiced for developing relaxed postures, will lead to a beneficial calming effect.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-4

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-5

is also involved in the practice of zhan zhuang.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-6

The amount of time spent practicing zhan zhuang varies between styles and schools; one may spend anywhere from two minutes to two hours standing in one posture.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhan\_zhuang#cite\_note-7

Many styles, especially the

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neijia

styles, combine zhan zhuang with qigong training and other coordinated-body methods to develop

whole-body coordination

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neijin

for martial purposes. The martial practice is thought to strengthen the body's

Central Nervous System

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central\_Nervous\_System

and develop the coordination required for effective martial performance. In yiquan, a clear distinction is made between

postures and

martially oriented

postures. In

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baguazhang

's circle-walking practice, the upper body is held as a zhan zhuang posture, while the lower body is more dynamic.
