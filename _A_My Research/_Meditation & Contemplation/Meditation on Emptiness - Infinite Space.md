Start out as usual
	1. Take your seat - getting the body and mind in position so it may be at ease and at the same time awake and alert
	2. I invite you to bring your attention inward. Maybe taking a few deep breaths. Noticing any tension you might be present.  And on the exhale letting go.
	3. As we start this journey, we begin by thinking of yourself alone sitting outside a cafe - maybe with a cup of tea or coffee...   Taking in the whole seen... not being attracted to any one thing... just noticing the business of people moving about... maybe cars or a bus or two passing by. Open. Spacious. 
	4. Widening our field of view - rising above - placing yourself above with this same scene... having a birds eye view... so that you still notice that there is some activity but you are more aware of it being a small city.... not trying to pay attention to any particular feature. 
	5. Now moving up even further. Noticing the edged of the town. Getting the whole picture of the city. Not paying attention to any particular feature. Barely noticing any movement. Relaxing. Open to the whole view of the city.
	6. And now moving back even further.  You notice that there is a forest surrounding the city. The city becoming smaller and smaller.  Just a speck now in the middle of this vast forest of green.  Not being able to discern any hills, or valleys or mountains.  No streams or rivers.  Just a vast forest.
	7. As you continue to pull back even further, the city has disappeared, no longer distinguishing the trees but just a vast green forest.
	8. And as you pull back even further, noticing that this forest is in the middle of a continent with no distinguishing features other than the forest which begins to melt into the middle of this vast continent.
	9. Pulling back even further we see that this continent is surrounded by a vast ocean.  The land receding back into the sea of blue. There are no islands that you can discern. No clouds. You mind open like the vastness of the sea.  
	10. As we pull back even further, now not being able to see the continent, and only a vast ocean, we notice that we are on a planet. The darkness of space starting to fill in the edges and the earth shrinking back into the background.   Other than our planet the space being featureless. 
	11. And now even the planet has disappeared so all that we know is the vastness of space. There being no edge and no objects other than the vastness of space itself. We rest in this open spaceous awareness.
	12. As we rest in this open awareness of space we can notice this mind that is aware. Turning our attention toward consciousness itself. Open. Aware. Resting in this awareness.
