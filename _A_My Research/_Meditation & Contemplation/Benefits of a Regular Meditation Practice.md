> A regular meditation practice, and the perspective that it gives, helps us get through the hardest times with balance, understanding, compassion and perspective. It keeps us from being overwhelmed when things are frightening or depressing. With a meditation practice, we are less likely to get lost in worry about the future or stuck in the past with regret. It helps us to live more in the present, skillfully processing whatever challenges we face while appreciating all the blessings in our lives.
> 
James Baraz
