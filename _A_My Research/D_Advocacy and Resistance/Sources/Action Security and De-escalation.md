# “KEEP IT MOVING”

# A Review of Options

##### Try to engage in dialogue, do not argue. Ask questions. Hecklers are frequently frustrated, and if they feel someone has listened to their concerns or feelings they may stop. Keep folks moving, do not allow a group to collect around the heckler.

##### With people fighting, try to isolate them from the larger group. Tell others to stay away. Position yourself between the scuffle and event participants: recruit others to form a human line around the scuffle. Urge people not to join with the conflict.

##### If a brawl threatens to erupt, you may have people sit down. It’s hard to fight sitting down, and any aggressive moves are clearly by your opponents.

##### Don't touch or push people, always approach people from the front or side, not from behind. Keep your hands in view.

##### Don't run toward fights. Running can stimulate panic; walk quickly and quietly. Stay calm. It's only a fight.

##### Continue to observe what is happening and mentally note who is around you and the series of events. Film, if safe. (Or encourage media/independent journalists to film.)

##### And always: Be polite. Smile: nothing is more disarming.
