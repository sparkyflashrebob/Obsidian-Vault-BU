The Action Lab is an incubator for creative campaigns and cultural interventions that share a beautiful-troublemaking sensibility.

We provide fiscal sponsorship and basic organizational infrastructure to a small number of groups and individuals seeking to get a campaign or creative effort off the ground.

We strive to create a safe, inclusive, supportive, and liberatory environment in which transformative action, personal growth, and collective solidarity can flourish.

Got an idea for a great action or creative project that needs some organizational muscle to help it take off? Get in touch and tell us a bit more about what you have in mind and what you’d need to make it happen.

If instead of incubation, you simply need urgent funding to pull off an action, our Get Up, Rise Up Direct Action Fund <http://directaction.beautifultrouble.org/> is set up to provide just-in-time support.
