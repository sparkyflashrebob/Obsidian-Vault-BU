According to the video, choosing the right social justice focus requires shifting from scattered, reaction-based effort to a strategic, sustainable "one lane" approach.

##### The "Four Signals" Filter

To choose a focus without guilt, your chosen cause should pass these four criteria [12:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=762):

* **Values Fit:** You can calmly and clearly defend the "why" behind your commitment [12:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=762).
* **Proximity:** You have lived experience, credible access to the community, or a deep commitment to listening to those impacted [12:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=762).
* **Capacity Match:** The commitment fits your realistic time and energy for this specific season of your life [12:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=762).
* **Contribution Edge:** You are using your specific skills and resources to create unique leverage [12:42](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=762).

##### The Do's and Don'ts

| **Do** | **Don't** |
| --- | --- |
| **Choose one lane** to become deeply effective rather than resetting with every crisis [02:12](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=132). | **Don't chase trends** or perform "random acts of activism" based on current social media cycles [03:41](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=221). |
| **Select partners** who have community credibility and clear, measurable outcomes [17:58](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=1078). | **Don't let outrage drive you.** Outrage-first leadership often leads to burnout and performative output [03:41](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=221). |
| **Define your focus in one sentence:** "I focus on [X] so that [Y] for [Z]" [16:35](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=995). | **Don't try to help everywhere.** Doing so leaves you fragmented and ensures you build nothing of depth [06:22](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=382). |
| **Seek measurable impact** , prioritizing tangible deliverables over vague "awareness" [02:59](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=179). | **Don't mistake "busy" for "committed."** More action does not automatically equal more impact [00:30](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=30). |

##### Summary of the Strategy

The video emphasizes that effective activism is **built, not improvised** [07:24](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=444). By establishing a "yes/no" boundary through a clear focus, you eliminate decision fatigue and ensure your efforts compound over time rather than evaporating [16:35](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfNUApwuXVI&t=995).
