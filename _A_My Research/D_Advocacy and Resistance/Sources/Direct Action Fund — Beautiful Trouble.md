GET UP RISE UP: Direct Action Fund – Call for Applications for Africa Week of Action

*IMPORTANT:* This is *not* your traditional INGO/NGO/CSO grant. This fund supports *disruptive, creative, and strategic nonviolent direct actions* – not trainings, not panel talks, not charity work. Please read the eligibility criteria carefully *before applying*. Let’s not waste each other’s time. Thank you!

Africa Week of Action focus - TOTALEnergies

For this round, the *GURU Fund* is prioritizing applications from *Africa-based* groups planning *direct actions targeting TotalEnergies* as part of the *#KickTotalOutofAfrica Africa Week of Action* <https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRNr7YFPD1QWK9MR8jCQBuMwKzLbQyyLItslHjcI-E8ndU5K8-_QxhBwnN0dSb7COLquhJFVTDrIRKw/pub>*.* TotalEnergies continues to drive displacement, environmental destruction, and fossil fuel expansion across the continent. This is a moment to resist – boldly, nonviolently, and creatively.

We welcome applications from *movements, collectives, informal or formal groups* organizing *SMART* (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) *nonviolent direct actions* that expose, disrupt, or resist TotalEnergies’ destructive influence – including actions opposing the *East African Crude Oil Pipeline (EACOP)*. Actions could be *Mass demonstrations & marches, Community speak-outs or town halls, Artivations* (murals, theatre, installations, protest music), *Public teach-ins, forums, Disruptive direct actions, Digital storms, photo and video solidarity campaigns*

This round is in partnership with *Beautiful Trouble* <https://beautifultrouble.org/>*, the StopEACOP Coalition, \**350.org* <https://350.org/?r=KE&c=AF>*, \**Climate Action Network* <https://climatenetwork.org/>\*, and \* *Africans Rising* <https://www.africansrising.org/>. We encourage actions that contribute knowledge and tactics back to the wider movement.

Applicants must agree and accept the Get Up Rise Up fund criteria - you can read more through the application form.

Grant size: Up to $1,500 USD

Applications are open until *July 28th, 2025*.
