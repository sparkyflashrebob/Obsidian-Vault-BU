*Can’t find your crystal ball? Invite the mysterious forces of Chance and Fate to inspire you!*
MATERIALS Shuffled cards
PLAY 1. Formulate your question. Think of a question that’s troubling you, such as,
“What would help me navigate my drama queen relationship?” or “How do we get past this stuck point in our campaign?” or “What do I need to learn to become a better troublemaker?”
2. Focus. While holding this question in your mind’s eye, shuffle the card deck. 3. Divine. Spread the cards out, and select one. This is your answer! A word,
phrase, image, or aura has a message for you.
OPTIONS: ● Ice-Breaker: Pick a card to introduce yourself to the group. ● Bibliomancy: Put your phone-camera over the QR code on this card to learn
*more about the card you chose and to uncover its deeper meaning — or just flip to a page in the offline Beautiful Trouble book on a whim and read.*
● Past, Present and Future: Draw 3 cards to get insights about your journey as a changemaker. Consider how, together, these cards tell a story of your journey as a changemaker. Be as imaginative or as critical as you wish in telling this story, and learning from it.
TIPS FOR FACILITATORS ● Divine the Future works great as an ice-breaker or closing activity. At
the start or end of a training or meeting, have each participant draw a card from the deck. Cards can be drawn at random from a face-down spread, or you can lay out cards face-up so participants can choose the card they are most drawn to. Invite pairs or small groups to share how this card is relevant to them in their life, as a changemaker, or in a campaign or project they are currently working on.
● Encourage participants to learn more. Each card includes a unique “pop-up code.” Turn your smartphone camera on and hover over the card. See what happens?! Your smartphone automatically pops up a link to the full version of that card in the Beautiful Trouble online toolbox.
● Don’t forget to capture your best ideas. For all of these activities, you may want to designate a notetaker who can write down — or quickly take
pictures with their phone — any ideas (and visions for your future!) you generate during the game.
