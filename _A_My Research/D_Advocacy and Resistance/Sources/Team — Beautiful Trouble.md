Luckily, the people who came together to write Beautiful Trouble stayed together and became an extraordinary alliance of artists and organizers, trainers and practitioners, sharing their best practices and schooling our movements in the most effective and strategic creative organizing approaches. Our dynamic network spans coast to coast in the US, and across three continents.

Want to bring one or more of these hot shots to your community or campus to super-charge your organizing? Request a training with us.
