##### The poem "First, They Came..." is a short, powerful piece by Martin Niemöller, a German pastor who initially supported the Nazi party but later became a vocal opponent. The poem is a warning about the dangers of apathy and silence in the face of injustice. It highlights how persecution can escalate when people fail to speak out against the targeting of others, eventually leading to the persecution of everyone.
