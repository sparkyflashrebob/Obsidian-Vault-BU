The MK Gandhi Institute for Nonviolence provides a resource that details 198 Methods of Nonviolent Action, inspired by Gene Sharp's work [1-3]. These methods are categorized into three main types: **Nonviolent Protest and Persuasion**, **Social Noncooperation**, **Economic Noncooperation (The Strike)**, **Political Noncooperation**, and **Nonviolent Intervention** [2, 4-7].

Here is a list of methods by type:

**I. The Methods of Nonviolent Protest and Persuasion** [2]
These actions are used to express disapproval, promote solidarity, and highlight unjust situations [8, 9].

* **Formal Statements** [2]
  + Public Speeches [2, 10]
  + Letters of opposition or support [2, 10]
  + Declarations by organizations and institutions [2, 10]
  + Signed public statements [2, 10]
  + Declarations of indictment and intention [2, 10]
  + Group or mass petitions [2, 10]
* **Communications with a Wider Audience** [2, 11]
  + Slogans, caricatures, and symbols [2, 11]
  + Banners, posters, and displayed communications [2, 11]
  + Leaflets, pamphlets, and books [11, 12]
  + Newspapers and journals [11, 12]
  + Records, radio, and television [11, 12]
  + Skywriting and earthwriting [11, 12]
* **Group Representations** [12, 13]
  + Deputations [12, 13]
  + Mock awards [12, 13]
  + Group lobbying [12, 13]
  + Picketing [12, 13]
  + Mock elections [12, 13]
* **Symbolic Public Acts** [12, 14]
  + Displays of flags and symbolic colors [12, 14]
  + Wearing of symbols [12, 14]
  + Prayer and worship [12, 14]
  + Delivering symbolic objects [12, 14]
  + Protest disrobings [12, 14]
  + Destruction of own property [12, 14]
  + Symbolic lights [4, 15]
  + Displays of portraits [4, 15]
  + Paint as protest [4, 15]
  + New signs and names [4, 15]
  + Symbolic sounds [4, 15]
  + Symbolic reclamations [4, 15]
  + Rude gestures [4, 15]
* **Pressures on Individuals** [4, 16]
  + Haunting officials [4, 16]
  + Taunting officials [4, 16]
  + Fraternization [4, 17]
  + Vigils [4, 17]
* **Drama and Music** [4, 18]
  + Humorous skits and pranks [4, 18]
  + Performances of plays and music [4, 18]
  + Singing [4, 18]
* **Processions** [4, 19]
  + Marches [4, 19]
  + Parades [4, 19]
  + Religious processions [4, 19]
  + Pilgrimages [4, 19]
  + Motorcades [4, 19]
* **Honoring the Dead** [4, 20]

**II. The Methods of Social Noncooperation** [4]
These tactics involve intentionally disrupting the status quo by refusing to comply with demands or withdrawing support, thereby removing sources of power from an authoritarian regime [23, 24].

* **Ostracism of Persons** [4, 25]
  + Social boycott [4, 25]
  + Selective social boycott [4, 25]
  + Lysistratic nonaction [4, 26]
  + Excommunication [4, 26]
  + Interdict [4, 26]
* **Noncooperation with Social Events, Customs, and Institutions** [4, 27]
  + Suspension of social and sports activities [4, 27]
  + Boycott of social affairs [4, 27]
  + Student strike [4, 27]
  + Social disobedience [4, 27]
  + Withdrawal from social institutions [5, 27]
* **Withdrawal from the Social System** [5, 28]
  + Stay-at-home [5, 28]
  + Total personal noncooperation [5, 28]
  + "Flight" of workers [5, 28]
  + Sanctuary [5, 28]
  + Collective disappearance [5, 28]
  + Protest emigration (hijrat) [5, 28]
* **Action by Consumers** [5]
  + Consumers’ boycott [5]
  + Nonconsumption of boycotted goods [5]
  + Policy of austerity [5]
  + Rent withholding [5]
  + Refusal to rent [5]
  + National/international consumers’ boycott [5]
* **Action by Workers, Producers, and Middlemen** [5, 29]
  + Workmen’s boycott [5, 29]
  + Producers’ boycott [5, 29]
  + Suppliers’ and handlers’ boycott [5, 29]
* **Action by Owners and Holders of Financial Resources** [5, 30]
  + Traders’ boycott [5, 31]
  + Refusal to let or sell property [5, 30]
  + Lockout [5, 30]
  + Refusal of industrial assistance [5, 30]
  + Merchants’ “general strike” [5, 30]
  + Withdrawal of bank deposits [5, 30]
  + Refusal to pay fees, dues, and assessments [5, 30]
  + Refusal to pay debts or interest [5, 31]
  + Severance of funds and credit [5, 31]
  + Revenue refusal [5, 31]
  + Refusal of a government’s money [5, 31]
* **Action by Governments** [5, 32]
  + Domestic embargo [5, 32]
  + Blacklisting of traders [5, 32]
  + International sellers’ embargo [5, 32]
  + International buyers’ embargo [5, 32]
  + International trade embargo [5, 32]
