## NotebookLM Limitations for Google One AI Premium Members with 5TB of Storage (May 2025)

For users subscribed to the Google One AI Premium plan with 5TB of storage, you gain access to **NotebookLM Plus**, the enhanced version of Google's AI-powered research and writing assistant. While your 5TB of storage primarily pertains to Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos, your AI Premium subscription unlocks significantly higher usage limits and premium features within NotebookLM itself. However, it's important to understand that certain limitations still exist.

**Here's a breakdown of what you can expect with your Google One AI Premium plan and 5TB of storage as of May 2025:**

**Significantly Increased Usage Limits (NotebookLM Plus):**

- **Notebooks:** You can create up to **500 notebooks**, a substantial increase from the 100-notebook limit in the free tier.
    
- **Sources per Notebook:** Each notebook can accommodate up to **300 sources**, allowing for more comprehensive research projects compared to the 50-source limit for free users.
    
- **Source Size:** While specific increases haven't been widely detailed, you can generally expect to upload larger individual source files than the free tier's 200MB/500,000-word limit.
- **Queries:** You can perform up to **500 queries per day** across all your notebooks, a significant jump from the 50-query limit per notebook in the free version.
- **Audio Overviews:** You can generate up to **20 audio overviews per day**, offering more flexibility for auditory learners and content creators.
    

**Premium Features:**

Your Google One AI Premium subscription also grants you access to exclusive NotebookLM Plus features:

- **Advanced Sharing Options:** You gain more granular control over how you share your notebooks, including the ability to share a "Chat-only" version, which allows collaborators to interact with the AI and sources without having full editing permissions.
    
- **Notebook Analytics:** You can access analytics for your shared notebooks, providing valuable insights into how others are engaging with your research and writing.
    
- **Potentially Customizable AI Interaction:** While specifics may evolve, NotebookLM Plus aims to offer more control over the AI's response style and tone, allowing you to tailor its output to your needs.
    

**Limitations That Still Apply:**

Despite the significant advantages of NotebookLM Plus, certain inherent limitations remain:

- **Underlying AI Capabilities:** As with all large language models, NotebookLM can still occasionally produce inaccurate information or "hallucinations." It's crucial to critically evaluate all AI-generated content and cross-reference information with your sources. Biases present in the training data can also influence outputs.
    
- **File Type Support:** While NotebookLM supports common formats like PDFs, Google Docs, and web URLs, limitations may still exist for less common or highly specialized file types.
    
- **Integration Capabilities:** While integration with Google Workspace is a core strength, the depth and breadth of these integrations, as well as connections with third-party applications, may still be under development.
- **Offline Access:** NotebookLM primarily operates as a cloud-based tool, meaning a stable internet connection is generally required for full functionality.
    
- **Complex Data Analysis:** While proficient with text, NotebookLM may still face challenges in deeply understanding and analyzing highly complex, nuanced, or structured data beyond textual information.

In essence, your Google One AI Premium plan with 5TB of storage provides you with the most powerful version of NotebookLM currently available. The increased limits and premium features cater to users with demanding research and writing needs. However, it's essential to remain aware of the inherent limitations of AI technology and the specific functionalities of the platform.
