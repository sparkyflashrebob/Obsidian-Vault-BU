### How Community Can Help With Trauma

* Ava
* September 6, 2024
  Contact Us Today According to the World Health Organization, around [70% of people will experience a potentially traumatic event](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/post-traumatic-stress-disorder#:~:text=Around%2070%25%20of%20people%20globally,in%20their%20lives%20(2).) during their lifetime. Trauma, left unresolved, can significantly hurt your mental health and well-being. Unfortunately, many trauma sufferers may feel like they are alone, and that no one can relate or understand what they are going through and experienced in the past. In this article, we’ll uncover how community can help with trauma so you can learn, grow, and heal alongside others who can relate to your struggles.
  At The Bridge to Recovery, we offer a top-rated [mental health workshop](https://www.thebridgetorecovery.com/) committed to providing a safe space for individuals to heal from family of origin trauma. #### **6 Ways Community Can Help With Trauma**

Trauma can make many people feel isolated and alone. However, building community can help with trauma by connecting people to others who share similar struggles and experiences. Keep reading to learn how community can help with trauma below.

##### **1. Connect with Others Who May Share Similar Struggles**

First, as mentioned above, a community can help connect you with others who share struggles and past experiences with you. For example, connecting to peers in group therapy or a support group can be an effective way to connect with others who may share similar trauma, experiences, or struggles as you do.

##### **2. Provide Emotional Support and Connection**

Community also provides you with the emotional support and connection you need to work through and heal from your trauma. You should never feel like you have to face your trauma all on your own.

##### **3. Judgment-Free, Safe Space to Talk about Your Trauma**

It can be scary and difficult to talk about your trauma with others. However, many support groups and group therapy sessions offer a safe and judgment-free space for members to open up and talk about what they are going through and the trauma they may have endured in the past.

##### **4. Gain Insight From Others On How to Cope with Trauma**

Next, you can also gain valuable insight from others who have been in your shoes and share similar struggles and trauma. This can be highly important as you can learn how people who may be further along their recovery journey may have coped and healed with their trauma effectively.

##### **5. Make You Feel Less Alone with Your Trauma**

Likewise, community helps make you feel less alone with your trauma. Again, trauma can make you feel isolated and alone. Yet, being surrounded by community (like a support group or group therapy) gives you the opportunity to talk and listen to others who resonate with what you’ve experienced.

##### **6. Learn, Grow, and Heal Alongside People Who Understand What You’re Going Through**

Last but not least, community gives you the chance to learn, grow, and heal your trauma alongside others who understand what you’re going through. It can be easy to feel the need to hide or ignore your trauma because of feelings of guilt or shame. However, concealing your trauma does not heal it. Community gives you the chance to talk about and heal from your trauma alongside people who can relate to what you’ve been through.

#### **Help Is Available**

Now you know more about how community can help with trauma. Trauma can make individuals feel isolated as if no one could relate or understand what they’ve experienced. However, community offers the opportunity to connect with people who share similar struggles as you. This can not only give you support in feeling less alone, but also offers the chance to learn, grow, and heal alongside other people who share similar struggles as you. If you or someone you know is struggling with trauma or another mental health condition, it’s critical you seek professional support to lead a healthier, happier quality of life.
