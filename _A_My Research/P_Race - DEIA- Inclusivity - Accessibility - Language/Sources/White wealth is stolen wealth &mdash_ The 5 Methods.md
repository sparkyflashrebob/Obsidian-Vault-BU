# White wealth is stolen wealth

Oct 11

Written By [Nikki Bambauer](/blog?author=5d4e1b449e1f6d000121c5e6)

*Why do we have it?* ***White wealth is stolen wealth.*** *The global capitalist economy is anti-Black. Ending white supremacy necessitates a wholesale transformation of the economic system, starting with a radical redistribution of resources that can enable the construction of a new economy. Divestment is an essential practice for white people, especially those of us who have access to personal wealth. Holding on to stolen and hoarded resources to maintain a lifestyle built on the backs of Black people undermines our mission to destroy white supremacy.* (From CRC’s [Divestment Principles](https://www.the5methods.org/principles/#Divestment))

White folks, especially those of us who are wealthy, can demand the space to move as slowly as we want to – or not at all.

CRCAA led me into doing work. Divestment gave me a clear and confident path forward, with principles and connection to a structure of organizational support and accountability to effective, transformative Black leadership in CRC. It supported me into a [Divestment practice](https://www.the5methods.org/principles/#Divestment), guided by deep political clarity of “how” and “why”. It trained me with the skills and stamina to organize wealthy, white people in my networks to join me – something I had wanted, but struggled, to do.

Divestment doesn’t offer ‘purity’, but it does offer a choice: within a violent, anti-Black economic system, coming from and with stolen wealth, we get to decide – through actions – which side of this fight we are on.

If you are white, anti-racist, and have access to wealth or wealthy networks: I want you to join us! I want us to practice Divestment as an active contribution to Black self-determined economic systems. I want us to learn from and with each other as we get more effective at returning what’s not ours, as we choose our way forward. We need all of us – active, organized, and moving at scale together – to invest in the systemic transformations that are both necessary and possible.

– Thea, CRCAA organizer
