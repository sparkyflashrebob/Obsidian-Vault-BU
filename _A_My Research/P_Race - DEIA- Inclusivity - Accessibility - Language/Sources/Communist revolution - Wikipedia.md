  A **communist revolution** is a [proletarian revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proletarian_revolution) inspired by the ideas of [Marxism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism) that aims to replace [capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism) with [communism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communism).[ 1 ] Depending on the type of government, the term [socialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism) can be used to indicate an intermediate stage between capitalism and communism and may be the goal of the revolution, especially in [Marxist–Leninist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism%E2%80%93Leninism) views.[ 2 ] The idea that a proletarian revolution is needed is a cornerstone of [Marxism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism);[ 3 ][ 4 ] Marxists believe that the [workers of the world must unite and free themselves from capitalist oppression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workers_of_the_world,_unite!) to create a world run by and for the [working class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Working_class).[ 5 ] Thus, in the Marxist view, proletarian revolutions need to [happen in countries all over the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proletarian_internationalism).

#### Theory

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Communist_revolution&action=edit&section=1)]
[Karl Marx](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Communist_revolution&action=edit&section=1) saw revolution as a necessity for communism, where the revolution would be based on class struggle led by the organised proletariat to overthrow capitalism and the [bourgeoisie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourgeoisie), followed by the establishment of a [dictatorship of the proletariat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictatorship_of_the_proletariat).[ 1 ]
Leninism argues[ 6 ][ 7 ] that a communist revolution must be led by a [vanguard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanguardism) of "professional revolutionaries", men and women who are fully dedicated to the communist cause and who can then form the nucleus of the [revolutionary movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutionary_movement).[ 8 ] Thus meaning that under Lenin's framework a communist revolution is not necessarily a proletarian revolution.[ 9 ] Some Marxists, such as [Rosa Luxemburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Luxemburg),[ 10 ][ 8 ] disagree with the idea of a vanguard as put forth by Lenin, especially [left communists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_communism).[ 11 ][ 12 ][ 13 ] Another line of criticisms insist that the entire working class—or at least a large part of it—must be deeply involved and equally committed to the socialist or communist cause in order for a proletarian revolution to be successful. To this end, they seek to build massive [communist parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_party) with very large memberships.

#### Communist revolutions and coups throughout history

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Communist_revolution&action=edit&section=2)] This list is [incomplete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Lists#Incomplete_lists); you can help by [adding missing items](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Communist_revolution&action=edit). *( February 2024 )* The following is a list of successful and unsuccessful communist revolutions and coups throughout history. Among the lesser-known revolutions, a number of borderline revolutions have been included which may or may not have been communist revolutions. The nature of unsuccessful revolutions is particularly contentious since one can only speculate as to the kinds of policies that would have been implemented by the revolutionaries had they achieved victory.

##### Successful

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Communist_revolution&action=edit&section=3)] *To arms, everyone!* , a [Yugoslav Partisan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslav_Partisan) propaganda poster

* 1917–1923: The [October Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_Revolution) and the subsequent [Russian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Civil_War) led to the establishment of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).
* 1921: The [Mongolian Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Revolution_of_1921) ended imperial Chinese rule over Mongolia and established the [Mongolian People's Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_People%27s_Republic).
* 1927–1949: The [Chinese Communist Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Revolution) led to the establishment of the [People's Republic of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) and fleeing of the Republic of China to Taiwan.
* 1941–1945: The [Yugoslav People's Liberation War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_in_Yugoslavia) led to the defeat of axis powers in the Balkans, abolishment of the Yugoslav monarchy, and the establishment of the [Federal People's Republic of Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_People%27s_Republic_of_Yugoslavia).
* 1942–1945: The [National Anti-Fascist Liberation Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Liberation_Movement_(Albania)) in Albania established the [People's Socialist Republic of Albania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Socialist_Republic_of_Albania).
* 1944: The [Bulgarian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1944_Bulgarian_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) led to the immediate abolishment of the Bulgarian monarchy, followed soon after by the establishment of the [People's Republic of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Republic_of_Bulgaria).
* 1945: The [August Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Revolution) forced [Emperor Bảo Đại](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdication_of_Emperor_B%E1%BA%A3o_%C4%90%E1%BA%A1i) to abdicate and established the [Democratic Republic of Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Vietnam).
* 1946–1954: [First Indochina War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Indochina_War)
* 1948: [Czechoslovak coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_Czechoslovak_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat).
* 1953–1959: The [Cuban Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuban_Revolution) overthrew the dictatorship of [Fulgencio Batista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulgencio_Batista), reestablishing the Republic of Cuba as a socialist state.
* 1955–1975: [Vietnam War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War)
* 1959–1975: [Laotian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laotian_Civil_War)
* 1961–1974: [Angolan War of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angolan_War_of_Independence)
* 1961–1979: The [Nicaraguan Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicaraguan_Revolution) sees the Sandinistas oust the Somoza government.
* 1963: Les Trois Glorieuses in [Congo-Brazzaville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_the_Congo).
* 1968–1975: [Cambodian Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodian_Civil_War)
* 1969: The [Corrective Move](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrective_Move) in [South Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yemen).
* 1969: The [Somali coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Somali_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) led to the formation of the [Somali Democratic Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somali_Democratic_Republic).
* 1972: [Mathieu Kérékou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathieu_K%C3%A9r%C3%A9kou) leads a military coup in Benin, leading to the creation of the [People's Republic of Benin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Republic_of_Benin).
* 1974: [Carnation Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnation_Revolution) (while the revolution overthrew the [Estado Novo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estado_Novo_(Portugal)) and created a governing alliance between army captains and the Communists,[ 14 ] the demands for a workers' democracy were quashed by a new coup on 25 November 1975 that installed a [minority government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_government) of the center-left [Socialist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_(Portugal))[\[ 15 \]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Party_(Portugal)) with the backing of the United States and its Western allies[ 16 ])
* 1974: The [1974 Ethiopian coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1974_Ethiopian_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat).
* 1978: The [Saur Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saur_Revolution) leads to the establishment of the [Democratic Republic of Afghanistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Afghanistan) and the start of the [Afghan conflict](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_conflict).
* 1979: The [New Jewel Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jewel_Movement) overthrow's [Eric Gairy's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Gairy) government in Grenada, creating the [People's Revolutionary Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Revolutionary_Government).
* 1983: The [Upper Voltan coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_Upper_Voltan_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) led by [Thomas Sankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Sankara) and [Blaise Compaoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaise_Compaor%C3%A9). [Upper Volta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Upper_Volta) was renamed [Burkina Faso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkina_Faso). [Blaise Compaoré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaise_Compaor%C3%A9) led the [1987 Burkina Faso coup d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Burkina_Faso_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat), which killed [Thomas Sankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Sankara) and reversed his far-left policies.
* 1996–2006: [Nepalese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepalese_Civil_War).
