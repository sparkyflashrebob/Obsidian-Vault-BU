## Who We Organize

**CRCAA has been tasked by CRC to organize all white people - the entire spectrum of the white population - because all white people perpetuate white supremacy.**

White people have many other identities in addition to our whiteness, identities which range across class, gender, sexuality, age, ethnicity, ability, political orientation, and more. Whenever white people engage with CRCAA, we are committing to address our whiteness and how we either perpetuate or combat white supremacy. We know that we are not going to agree on everything, and we do not need to. We come together, across all of our differences, around CRCAA’s points of unity:

1. We respect and support Black self-determination.
2. White supremacy is the air we breathe. It is pervasive and invasive, impacting institutions, organizations, communities, families, and individuals alike.
3. There is value in fighting white supremacy everywhere.
4. We learn to see opportunities to fight white supremacy where we find ourselves. ‘Get in where you fit in.’
5. Every white person has something to contribute to ending white supremacy.
6. We will work to end white supremacy by practicing CRC’s 5 Methods to Divest of White Power and Weaponize White Privilege: Education, Organization, Contribution (Not Control), Intervention, and Divestment.

If you are a white person who can agree with these Points of Unity, then you’re in the right place! Join one of our [upcoming events](https://events.the5methods.org/), and sign up for our [regular updates](https://events.the5methods.org/sign-up).
