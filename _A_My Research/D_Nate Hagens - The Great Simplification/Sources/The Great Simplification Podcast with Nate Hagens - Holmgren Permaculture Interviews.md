### The Great Simplification Podcast with Nate Hagens

David was recently interviewed by Nate Hagens for the The Great Simplification podcast.
In the episode, David discusses his experience within the [permaculture movement](https://holmgren.com.au/about-permaculture/) and what it might look like for more systems to be designed using permaculture in the future. While often thought to be an agricultural tool, [permaculture thinking](https://holmgren.com.au/about-permaculture/) is meant for designing human systems to be embedded in nature – an important principle for a future where societies will need to re-synchronise with natural flows. What does it mean for permaculture design to ‘scale up’, and how is it different from how we usually think about growing a system? How will permaculture design change as we move through different phases of resource availability? More importantly, how can the ‘[small and slow](https://permacultureprinciples.com/principles/small-slow-solutions/)’ foundation of permaculture help human societies adapt to a lower throughput future as we navigate The Great Simplification?
