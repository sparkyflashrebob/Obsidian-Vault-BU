In a state of total volatility, Charlie burned angelica root and projected a prayer for survival. A Red-tailed Hawk appeared, communicating "telepathically" to guide the crew through a specific, non-obvious escape route to safety.
    *   **Analysis of the "So What?" Layer:**  This illustrates the necessity of "Intuitive Pattern Recognition." *   **Subject: The Black Bear, the Bobcat, and Wind-Wolf (Karuk)**
    *   **Narrative Synopsis:**  A toddler, Wind-Wolf, was targeted by a large Bobcat—a spirit ally sent by a source of "bad power" (spiritually weaponized intent). Following a prayer for protection and the use of sacred smoke (cedar and angelica), a young Black Bear manifested to engage the Bobcat. The Bear maintained a protective perimeter for four days until the crisis (the child's fever) broke.
    *   **Analysis of the "So What?" Layer:**  The Bear represents the "Counter-Measure" to negative environmental intent. For the modern professional, this highlights the role of "Spiritual Hygiene"—maintaining a clean energetic environment to neutralize external "bad medicine" or toxic organizational cultures.

"Thunder Man" ( *Wakíŋyaŋ Wičháša* ) appeared in the lightning, terrifying the giants and driving them permanently into the underworld.
