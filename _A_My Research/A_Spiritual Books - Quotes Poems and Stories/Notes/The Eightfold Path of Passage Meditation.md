Eknath Easwaran's eight-point program is a comprehensive spiritual practice centered around "passage meditation," designed to help individuals train their minds and lead a more fulfilling, spiritual life [1, 2]. The core principle of the program is that we become what we think; by concentrating deeply on inspired words, those ideals sink into our consciousness and become an integral part of our personality, constantly expressed in our thoughts, words, and actions [1].

The eight points of the program are:

1. **Meditation on a Passage**: Practicing slow, sustained concentration on the words of inspirational passages for one-half hour each morning [3].
2. **Repetition of a Mantram**: Silently repeating a sacred phrase or name that has the power to transform consciousness, done whenever possible during the day or night [3].
3. **Slowing Down**: Reducing the tension caused by rushing by carefully setting priorities [3].
4. **One-Pointed Attention**: Giving full and complete concentration to the matter at hand [3].
5. **Training the Senses**: Overcoming conditioned habits and learning to enjoy what is truly beneficial [4].
6. **Putting Others First**: Finding joy in helping others in order to gain freedom from selfishness and separateness [4].
7. **Spiritual Fellowship**: Regularly spending time with other passage meditators for mutual inspiration and support [4].
8. **Spiritual Reading**: Drawing inspiration from the scriptures of all religions and writings by or about spiritual figures [4].
