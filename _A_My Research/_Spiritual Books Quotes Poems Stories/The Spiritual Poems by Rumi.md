Introduction

While a pleasant breeze touches my face and body, and I hear the song of birds, I am glad to write this introduction for an enthralling poetry

collection: The Spiritual Poems by Rumi.

What fascinates me about Rumi is that he never wrote his poems. His followers were ready and wrote the poems as soon as he began to say them. Rumi spontaneously spoke verses from his connection to the Infinite Love. The poems are not from Rumi’s mind but from his heart. You will find the flame of love, peace, happiness, and acceptance in this collection of poetry. Rumi passed away centuries ago, but his words continue to illuminate hearts around the world. The truth is eternal, while the mundane is temporal.

A Persian Sufi poet and mystic, Rumi lived in the 13th century. He was a

dignitary scholar before he met Shams-e-Tabrizi, an unknown, disheveled

mystic through whom Rumi experienced Divine Love. In an ecstatic state, Rumi began to whirl spontaneously, expressing his inner joy with his

physical body. To this day, Sama or Sufi whirling is practiced by some Sufis

while Rumi’s rhythmic poems are chanted. Rumi says in his poem:

Sama became a window toward your flower gardens, The hearts and ears of lovers are over its windows.

Sama was a unique expression of Rumi's spiritual experience, a

connection to the Infinite Love. Experiencing Divine Love is unparalleled for every human, and its expression is manifested in poetry, dancing, art, architecture, painting, kindness, acceptance, understanding, and all other beauties that we can find. And sometimes, experiencing Divine Love is just as simple as staying in the present moment and enjoying its flow in our being.

This collection is almost a word-for-word translation of 110 spiritual poems of Rumi from his original masterpiece poetry collection, Divan-e-Shams. The poems have been carefully chosen from over 3,200 poems in

Divan-e-Shams and lovingly translated to convey the spiritual meanings as

purely as possible. As a translator of Rumi's legacy, the authenticity of the poems’ translation is crucial to me.

Some words have different meanings in Rumi’s poems from their usual use. In this case, I have used a capital letter to differentiate the meaning of a

word from its usual meaning. For example, in this collection, Wine means Divine Love, while wine in its original definition means the alcoholic drink.

Rumi’s pen name in his poetry collection is Khamosh, meaning Silent.

This emphasizes the importance of a quiet mind before speaking from the light. When Rumi spoke, he became the tongue of the Infinite Love to shed light into the world through his poetry.

As the human body needs food to live, the soul needs spiritual

nourishment to flourish and stay spirited. The Spiritual Poems by Rumi is an

excellent nourishment for the soul. I hope you enjoy this collection of poetry and experience more peace and happiness in your heart. S. Morteza Lajevardi

The Reeds Are Dancing

I am seated by your door May loyalty rise, May you open a door, Saying, “Get up; come in.” Drawn in by your ambery and musky scent My soul waits by your door. May a hundred thousand blessings be upon your good face forevermore. Tipsy and reckless, we are oblivious to what others do, If the world goes away altogether, May you be forever. Your love clapping, creating a hundred new worlds, A hundred new centuries merging out of the vacuity and universes. O, smiling love like a flower, Handsome like wisdom, O, chivalrous sage! Suppress the Sun! Today, we are your guests, Tipsy with your smiling face. Upon saying your name, I swear to God My heart jumps out. O sweet-mannered Tapster!1 Where is a roof other than yours? Where is a name other than yours? Where is a cup other than yours? If I find a vivacious soul, I’ll clutch the clothes, As I am tipsy and jolly for the charming eyes. One, seeing your face

Without going crazy in every alley, Is stonehearted. Is any suffering for the soul worse than being unconscious of you? O King of humans! May you not make anyone inwardly blind. Souls are flowing toward the soul’s seashore, Disconnecting from the familiars, Becoming familiar with the sea. A flood flows eagerly, Another one loses the way. O Sun, becoming the Tapster for the thirsty, You give to your servants with generosity and blessing. Suddenly, a flower saw your face, Tore off its clothes. The deplored lute is embarrassed before you. Who is the most fortunate in the Venus asterism? The Ney flute. For it puts its lips on yours To learn the melody. The reeds are dancing, Longing to be chosen, Meaning, “You honor whomever you wish.” Without you, The lute and Ney are tristful. One is in your arm, The other is kissing you, Tambourine said, “Beat on my face So that my visage becomes valuable.” Tear off the torn soul beautifully, Making it drunk So what was missed last night can be compensated for.

Either give me the Wine2 without reasoning Or get up and go. With your favor, Treat your servant with a Sufi manner.

The Soother of Lovers

Where am I from? Where is the advice from? Tapster! Serve the Wine around, O Soother of lovers! Place the cup of soul in my hand. Far away from the lips of strangers, Bring the cup secretly. Give bread to the poor, greedy lover of bread, Let him sleep in the corner. O Soul of soul of soul of soul! We haven’t come for the bread! Jump up, Don’t hesitate before the King’s feast. First, put the dear cup in the master's hand, As the master gets drunk, O Tapster! Go toward the drunks. O hope! Persist! What is shame? What is tipsiness? Do you feel shameful? O Tapster! Pour the cup and drown the shame. Get up, O Tapster! And Come! O Foe of shame and shyness! Come smiling So that our fortune becomes smiley.

The Restless Wind

O restless wind! Tell the flower our message: O flower! When separated from the flower garden, scurry to the sweetness! O flower, your origin is from sweetness, You are more deserving of sweetness. Sweetness is good, Flowers are good, Both are the sweetest loyalty. Place your face on the face of sweetness, Feel the pleasure and release the fragrance. By the fortune of sweetness,

Be free from the annihilation3 of bitterness. Now that you have become a flower-sweet, You are the strength of the heart. Oh, the sight’s light! Rise from the flower, Pass the heart. Where is that from? Where is this from? Like mind being befriended by soul, You have been befriended by thorns. From the Earth, run to heaven, Step by step, Until the Divine meeting. You are passing the world secretly, Entering the path stealthily, Garden by garden, Where figures fade away. O flower!

You are a rare bird, Flying, unlike any other bird. A message from Divine: “Let your feathers fall away, Come without feathers.” O flower! You have seen this, So, you laugh at the world, Tearing off the clothes. Oh, sage wearing a rosy cassock! Flowers are shouting in heaven: “Whoever wants a ladder to sublimation, Be ready for a test.” Distil yourself from the world sky Like rosewater through a condenser, Pass beyond the inverted cup, like a soul. You are blessed and happy with roseate faces, We were like you, We became soulful. Be subtle! The flower-sweet is our aim, Our being is the Truth’s favor. Our being is ferrous, The Truth’s favor is magnetic. O, heart, whispering aromatic words! This talk has no end. I won’t tell others what you tell me.

The Empyreal Wine

O lovers! O lovers! It is time for connection and gathering! A call can be heard from the sky: O beautiful faces! Be awake and ready. O happy ones! O happy ones! The skirt-pulled cheerfulness has come. We have grabbed its chain, It has grabbed our skirts. The Empyreal Wine has come! O devil of sorrow! Sit on the corner! O death-thinking soul! Go. O Eternal Tapster! Come. O You with whom seven firmaments are tipsy! We are but beads in your hands. Oh, our beings are born from your Being! Enough reason to be a hundred thousand times happy. O sweet-breath singer! Move the bell in each moment. O pleasance! Don’t breeze just on the horse, O Zephyr! Beat on our souls. O sweet sound of the Ney! Your call has a sweet flavor, Finding me in the evening and dawn, Promising the whiff of loyalty. O stunning Sun! Begin once again, Tune up the strings! Mince for the worthy! Be silent! Don’t divulge! Drink from the jug of silence. Conceal faults! Conceal faults! Learn forbearance from God.

Sun-Chasing

I was negligent and needy, Aspiration released me. His thought-parrot Devoured me like sugar. The Sun of eternity’s shining, Nourisher of the soul and universe, Ripened and raised me With attributes of a flower and sweetness. I said, “O firmament! I am not one whom you oppress.” It said, “He found me punny, O pure!” O, King of firmament’s chess, My checkmate won you, O King! Your throne is my backgammon board. O Sea! I am thirsty for your waters, Your rare goodness made me whimsical in two worlds. One like you won’t let me be alone. In autumn, Flabbergasting, I moved toward the rose flowers for your parting. All my yellow leaves Became lamenters. The face like daylight will make you lovers’ sedition, My night-seeker heart will make me famous, Like your standing flag, I am dancing in the air. Open my wings So that I fly beautifully. Morning frost follows the Sun,

As my cold self is chasing your Sun.

One piece separated from another piece creates pain,

But my piece joined the Whole with no pain. I am the servant of one who innocently afflicts me, As he has the pull of the Moon afflicting me. Everybody has a caprice, A portion of a fate, His love brought fate, A gift for the way. O, horse of words! Don’t ride longer! Don’t turn back on the path of the soul. Though the kohl of soul itself turned toward me.

The Darling of the Tavern

The Darling of the Tavern4 has come To bring us home, Show the new spring, And make us fresh. He showed His sign, Loaded His bow To pierce our path. He threw a hundred points, Making a hundred traps and tricks, Losing backgammon in a surprise. All to have us. Go, Become the shade of His cypress, Running before and after Him, Though He eradicates us like new seedlings. Even if His heart is thorny, Don’t escape and don’t run away. First, He kills us, Then He draws us. As Beloved coquettes stealthily inside our hearts, To all kings, Hundreds of caresses arrive. The Long Life has returned, The Goodness and Coquetishness have come To mark us. The Soul and the World have come, The Secret Treasure has come, The Pride of Kings has come, To share our secrets.

You are my Soul and World, Why should I care about the soul and world? You are my Flowing Treasure, Why should I care about loss or profit? One moment, I am the Wine fellow, Another moment, I am the Kebab fellow! Being in such a dilemma, What can I do about turning time? I drove away from all, Abandoning all. I am not hidden, Nor appeared. Why should I care about the universe and existence? Since the connection with you, I am hungover. Not distracted by others, As you are my hunt and prey, Why should I care about the arrow and bow? As I am alone in the river, I don’t know where to go or what to find. What can I say about the flowing river? When keeping the secret of existence, I can't bear even a chaff. When my shepherd is a wolf, How can I mollycoddle the shepherd? O love! When the cup is in the hand, How joyful you are! What a drunkenness! Happy where you are seated! Happy is the soul’s sight! From you, every particle is a universe, Every drop is a soul.

One with a sign from you Won’t need name and fame. To get the sublime gem in the ocean of truths, I travel through my mind, I have no need for running feet. With the unique arm, You hit my way, Taking all my clothes, Nothing left for the catchpoll! From the radiance of the Shining Moon And the curve of the curled plait, My heart is light. O Soul! Give me the Fine Wine! Do not see the affliction, See love and affection. Do not see the oppression, See the chamberlain. Call sorrow “Favor,” Revel in sorrow and affliction. Ask Goodness for opening, safety, and peace, Choose an attentive ear. Listen to the mouth But be silent.

Where All Goodness Begins

O You, where all goodness begins! O Flower of our garden! Where are you? The lily with a hundred tongues didn’t show me you, Saying, “Go; don’t ask anything of me But prayer and encomium.” O Moon! From your hand, The garden of mouth is full of sugar, Nevertheless, With all the blessings It isn’t aware of you. If the cypress grows taller, How can it reach your height? Narcissus, if it had eyes, didn’t see you. If the bird preached, The branch sprinkled flowers, And the grass drove fast, Non would be the cure. Flowers drink from the clouds, But hearts drink from patience. Clouds are the comrades of greens, Patience is the comrade of Zephyr. Say whatever you want, Seek wherever, But, if guidance does not come to you, You cannot advance, Even as long as a hair strand. From the beating of the Sun

Water is warmed, The same Sun elevates the water, Gradually so, Not noticeable. The shine of Beloved Takes the pure. I become silent, But the firmament will call you all night.

The Tuneful Nightingale

Again, violet arrived next to two lilies, Again, the ruby-worn flower tore off the cassock. They’ve arrived cheerily, From the other world like a wind, Tipsy, mincing, and happy, They wore green cassocks. The flag-bearer cypress burnt the autumn with zeal, The sweet-looking tulip appeared on top of the hill. Hyacinth is saying to Yasmin, “Peace be upon you,” Yasmin replies, “May peace be with you, Come in the grass, O noble!” Clapping like the cypress, Dancing like the wind, Sufis in every corner Have become well-known. The bud, like mystics, Has covered its face, The wind is pulling the bud's veil, Saying, “Oh, pure! Show your face. Sweetheart is in our alley, Water is in our runnel, Why are you thirsty and yellow? You are the adornment of the lily.” The clove says to the willow, “You are my hope! My single house is your sanctuary.” Apple tells the bergamot, “Why are you miffed?” It said,

“For the unlit eyes, I don’t show myself.” The ringdove is coming, saying, “Where is the sweetheart?” The tuneful nightingale points to the flower. Beyond the world’s spring Is another hidden spring, O Moon-Like Face! Mellifluous! O Tapster! Give the Wine! O Moon! Rise in the darkness, His light overcomes the Sun.

Oh, Honey Mine!

Where is the favor I saw in your face all night? Where is the sweet anecdote that I heard you sing all night? Though my heart was burning like a butterfly by your candle, I flew around your pretty face’s candle all night. Night was pitching a tent before your moon-like face. I was opening the night’s tent like the Moon all night. For your verve, the soul was licking its lips like a cat, I bit my fingertips like kids all night. As the chest was tumultuous like a beehive, Oh, Honey Mine! I extracted nectar from you all night. The night trap came and grabbed the creations’ souls, Like a bird’s heart, I beat in that trap all night. One to whom all souls are under His command, In that trap, I sought Him all night.

The Pen Breaks for Love

When writing your insignia, The pen breaks for love. For your burdensome parting, Wisdom loses the way. Who can be your companion? Who can be your supplant? Who can escape your ambush? Who bends your bow? For love, My face is yellowish, From you, A thousand impressions are on me. O Darling! Gaze at me, I am like this for your Soul! Like Abraham In the fire, I am happy for the heat of your fire. Open my twisted work, I’ve lost my heart, Give me a heart. Who can come to your alley Except through your scent? What is the reason for searching for you? The flowers were scattered by you. Dominion, people, and fairy, Reign, king, and martial, Firmament, Venus, and Jupiter Are embarrassed at your doorstep. When you pull the phoenix of the soul to test, It fails in the test,

Like a fly sinking in a drink. With your sublime hints And healing enunciations, Every beggar has become an angel. With the interpretation of your breath Every creature, Like ants, Is running toward your harvest. From your granting tablecloth, All creatures get their sustenance. Following every tribulation, Your treasure cures, what a cure! Your placeless endows every place, what a melody! The body’s greed is your grace, The heart’s greed is your elegance. The body’s glimpse is in your name, The heart’s caprice is you. You showed the ladder to the givers and honest hands, The caravan is moving toward your skies. O heart! Be silent! Don’t say more.

O Handsome! Dance

The Spring of Souls is here! O fresh branch! Dance,

As Joseph5 entered Egypt, O Egypt and Sweet! Dance. O love-nurturing King! Like mother’s milk. O Lion-seeker! Come, for the father’s sake! Dance. When you saw the polo's beautiful plait, Like a ball, without head and foot, You arrived! Now, without head and foot, dance. For love, Crown’s holders are in His wheel like rain, What is the use of wearing a cassock here? O handsome! Dance. The annihilation order has been received. For journey’s sake, dance! In hand, the Wine cup, My Beloved has arrived afoot. For King’s sake, dance. The warfare is over, The lute sound is heard, Joseph is saved from the well, O artless! Dance. Until when is the promise? Until when should this head prostrate? O concerto sound! Dance. Who is at that time saying, “O unwitting one! Be annihilated, O conscious! Dance"? Our peacock enters,

Those beautiful colors appear, Singing with the heart’s bird: O featherless, Dance! O trees and branches! In His graceful spring, Dance.

The Eternal Banquet

O soul! Who is sitting in the heart's home? Who can be on the King’s throne but the King and the Prince? King is pointing His hand, “Tell me what you want from me.” What would a drunk on Wine want, But sweet, cup, and Wine? A sweet suspended in the heart, A cup of the infinite light, In the solitude of Truth, Has set the eternal banquet. Many cunning ones stay at the Wine drinkers' banquet, Be careful not to fall, O soft, naive man! Be careful not to fall, O soft, naive man! Beware not to fall into the trickery circle, Like a blindfolded bud Or a gape flower. The universe is like a mirror, The image is of love perfection. O people! Who has seen a part grow bigger than the whole? Like grass, be afoot, Beloved is the rider, The rest are all afoot. He is the sword And the puller, too, He is the martyr And the killer, too. He is All-Wisdom And Wine-Giver to the mind, too.

Friend is mine, Cave is mine, The burning love is mine. Friend is you, Cave is you, Dignitary is you. Noah is you, Soul is you, Conqueror is you, Conquered is you. Light is you, Banquet is you, Victorious fortune is you. Droplet is you, Ocean is you, Favor is you, Wrath is you. Sweet is you, Bane is you, Don’t bother me anymore. The Sun’s chamber is you, Venus’ home is you. The homily of hope is you, Let me in, O Friend! Day is you, Fast is you. Water is you, Jar is you, Water me this time! Birdseed is you, Trap is you,

Wine is you, Cup is you. Ripe is you, Unripe is you, Don’t keep me unripe. If this body hinders less, My heart’s path would be less uneven. Be my path So that I don’t talk much.

Draw Me Like a Beautiful Moon

O Beloved! You know how, You give worth, We hold tightly to you, You are the Hunting Lion, The hatredless Beloved, The Candle of our hearts’ chest. O Beloved! In two worlds, In two halls, You know how. Particle by particle Is at your door, Prostrating, Servant and assistant, Ahh, what a friend you are! O Beloved. In every breath, I get hungrier, He said, “Do you drink the sea?” I said, “Yes, Beloved!” Whoever isn’t apart from you, Will never die. If there were a death, That would be before you, O Beloved! I don’t have a business or shop, I am the workless of the world, As I know no executor but you. Whether night or dawn,

I am not aware of either. Who is in the news? What is the news? You are the Auditor of days. During the day, I see you, During the night, you sunder the sadness, From you, My night becomes day, You are like daylight, O Beloved! O my Full-Blessing Garden! O my Ornamented Flower Tree! None have ever seen, Never has there been, A spring like you, O Beloved! You make my body dust, Purify my mud, And you draw me Like a beautiful moon, O Beloved! My existence is my philosophy, Knowing you is my drunkness.

Dwell in the Heart

I saw the face of a flower garden, The leading light of the shine, The Qabla and the sanctuary of the soul, The elation and the safety. Heart said, “There is where to die for, Letting go of being and ego.” Soul came in dancing, Beginning clapping, Wisdom came in and said, “What should I say about this fortune and sublime felicity?” The flower scent that had made the cypress lofty bent both. In love, everything changes. O soul! You’ve reached the Soul of soul, You’ve left the mundane body. What is a hermitage? This is the heart’s home, It is accustomed to dwelling in the heart. They bring the eternal, salubrious cup To the heart’s home. Be silent, Let go of talkativeness. The heart is the home of faith, In your heart, keep faith.

The Gem of Meaning

O, who is the comfort of my soul in distress! O, who is the flowing treasure in indigent bitterness! That which is unreachable by imagination, wisdom, and conception— You gave it to me, For that, You are my Qabla. For your generosity, I gaze at life with coquetry, How can a mortal mammon deceive me? O King! For me, Even a call in a dream, Receiving news from you Is better than all the songs. In my prayers, sit your fantasies, O King! For the sinful ones, You are their merciful paraclete. You are the High, the Master, For me, You are gentle-hearted. If the Eternal Benefection presents dominions, For me, He gives me the word, the hidden treasure. I prostrate heartily, Placing my face on the ground, Telling all ears about my Divine Love. The eternity before me is the connection to time, No time can contain it. Life is like a cup, With a pure drink inside, Without you, What is the pain of the cup for?

Thousands of wishes I had before, For His caprice, now I have no wish. He is the Gem of meaning, Filling my soul and heart. Though my flesh finds no favor, My soul is chasing His connection While still conscious of the body.

Flying Toward Your Joy

Tapster! My soul is flowing toward love, But my tongue is slow for your lovelorn. I fly like an arrow toward your joy, O Friend! Don’t break my bow. Like travelling in a tent, With a step, I arrive, O Friend! Bring me to your pavilion, sit me! Put the cup’s brim on my dried lips, Then hear the real magic in my words. Listen to the Babylon news and Vayel’s myth,6 Through thinking, I am an explorer of the world. Accept my apology if sensations overwhelm, Since love doesn’t let me rest for a moment. The moment you are lonesome, I am lonesome for you. If you leave me, I will bite my fingers. The night you shine like the Moon until the dawn, I am flowing toward your Moon like a planet. The day you rise from the East like the Sun, I am all soul, like the Sun. The day you become a soul invisible to eyes, I am like the bird’s heart beating from thoughts. The day your light shines into my eyelet, At home, I dance like a particle. Silence the talk, Move stealthily like thought, So no one finds my trace.

Dancing Before Zephyr

Gaze at the figure and style, Look at the height, the face, the hands, and the feet. Look at the color, the poise, the Moon in cassock. Should I talk about the cypress or the grass? Tulip or jasmine? Should I talk about the candle, The candlestick, Or the flower dancing before Zephyr? O love sparkling like a fire temple with figure and face! You are hitting the heart’s Caravan, For a moment, give me a break, O gentle! From night until day, I am burning, Oh, my victorious blissfulness from the sunshine! I weave around His Moon, Saying “Hello” to Him, lipless. I knock myself down when hearing His call. You are the rose garden of the universe, The leading light, The pain and scorcher of the universe, too, Why do you act with cruelty? I come, offering my soul, You say, “Don’t bother, go.” Serving until I am waning, Then you say, “Crazy! Come.” The thought has become the companion of fervent lovers, May your face not be hidden from our eyes, Even for a blink. O heart! What happened to your promise and affair? You are sleepy whether it is day or evening! Heart said,

“His face is goodness, His magical flowers, His hyacinth eyebrows, And His sweet story lips.” Oh love! You have a reputation with everyone, A lot of fame, Last night, I called you another name, “The medicineless pain.” O who my soul’s brightness is from you! Like a wheel, my revolving comes from you. Send me wheat! So that the mill isn’t dazed. I won't continue this, I say this word, and it is enough, My soul smelts for your caprice, O Lord! Unite us.

The Golden Wine

O Tapster of soul! Fill the prior cup, That bandit of heart, That leader of the sect. A Wine erupted from the heart, Fused to the soul, Its effervescence makes the God-wary eyes tipsy. There are cups for that wine, And there are cups for this Wine. Without breaking that cup, You cannot sip this one. That Wine, for only a jiff, makes the heart painless, It never kills the sadness, It never eradicates hatred. But a drop of this Wine, Will make your work like gold, I give my soul to this Golden Wine. This state, if ever it happens, Will happen in the dawns, For those who leave the bed And are awake. Beware of a vicious friend Who will tempt you To break the King’s covenant. If you get hurt, Look for another wound, How useful are flowers and daffodils In the army?

With Enchanting Darling

You bestow the cup and Wine, You give value to the gathering And the Tavern. You make the tipsy narcissus drunk, Bringing the sweetheart, darling. How would the restless heart Be patient and in equanimity Without your Divinity? Draw your sword, O Sun! Shine on this ruined corner.

Your Qaf7 is the phoenix home, You are the Candle, The soul is like a butterfly. Flow the reviving stream in every direction, Tell the tale and myth. Make this infidel body drunk and useful. Show me the strong Wine That would tame the devil. Being half-hearted isn’t handy, Where He makes a hundred sage hearts senseless. From today’s dawn Is such a good gathering, With Enchanting Darling. Your eyes make me break a hundred promises, Your plait makes a hundred combs tipsy. O Darling! For a moment, rise, And make Hannaneh dance. The lock explains The tip of the dent, “We Opened” And its points,

The king says, He is hearing me. I leave the thrall-like talk.
