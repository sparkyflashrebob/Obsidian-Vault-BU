She's in the sun,
the wind, the rain,
she's in the air you
breathe with every
breath you take.
She sings a song
of hope and cheer,
there's no more pain,
no more fear.
You'll see her in
the clouds above,
hear her whisper
words of love
You'll be together
before long, until then
listen for her song. --

Christy Ann Martine
