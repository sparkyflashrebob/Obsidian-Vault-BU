### 4 FOUNDATIONS OF MINDFULNESS

###### [1st Foundation of Mindfulness: Body Sensations](https://insighttimer.com/recoverydharma/guided-meditations/basic-meditation-4)

Awareness of sensations and responses in the body.

###### [2th Foundation of Mindfulness: Feeling Tones](https://insighttimer.com/recoverydharma/guided-meditations/basic-meditation-4)

Explores the reactions to positive, negative, and neutral senses.

###### [3th Foundation of Mindfulness: Mind Processes](https://insighttimer.com/recoverydharma/guided-meditations/basic-meditation-4)

Awareness of acknowledging the processes of the mind.

###### [4th Foundation of Mindfulness: Mindfulness of Dharmas](https://insighttimer.com/recoverydharma/guided-meditations/basic-meditation-4)

A combination, expansion and deepening of 4 practices.
