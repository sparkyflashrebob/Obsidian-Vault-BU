"If you can start the day without caffeine or pep pills, if you 
can be cheerful ignoring aches and pains, if you can resist complaining and boring people with your troubles, if you can take criticism and blame without resentment, if you can face the world without lies and deceit, if you can relax without liquor, if you can sleep without the aid of drugs, then you are probably a dog."
