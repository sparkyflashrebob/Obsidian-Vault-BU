### Psychological Research on Happiness
> *"It’s a cliché by this point that the path to happiness is paved by achievement: graduate from a top university, score the dream job, find love, retire early in a sunny beachfront town. But psychological research flips this conventional formula for happiness on its head. It turns out, positive psychology research reveals that happiness precedes success, not the other way around."*
