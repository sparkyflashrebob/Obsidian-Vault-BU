“In the immediate present, mature spirituality invites us to ‘walk our talk,’ to act and speak and touch one another as a reflection of deep understanding. We become more alive and more present. We discover that our very breath and body and human limitations are a part of the divine. This maturity listens to our body and loves it all, the body of joy and of grief; it listens to the heart and loves the heart’s capacity to feel. This immediacy is the true source of compassion and understanding.”


—Jack Kornfield
