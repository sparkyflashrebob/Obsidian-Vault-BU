Hafis - "Admit something. Everyone you see, you say to them, 'Love me.' Of course, you don't say this out loud. Otherwise, someone would call the cops. Still, though, think about this-- this great pull in us to connect. Why not become the one who lives with the full moon in each eye that is always saying with that sweet moon language-- what every other eye in this world is dying to hear?"
