Here is a summary of the video, which explains attention and how to train it:

- **The problem with attention:** There is too much information for the brain to fully process, so attention helps prioritize and subsample [[00:01](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=1)].
- **Attention is more than focus:**
    - **Focus (Flashlight):** Narrows mental energy in a precise way, prioritizing some information [[00:47](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=47)].
    - **Alerting System (Floodlight):** Broad, diffused, and receptive, noticing what's going on without privileging anything [[01:42](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=102)].
    - **Executive Control (Juggler):** Manages goals and ensures coordination of multiple complex tasks [[02:23](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=143)].
- **Breath-focus practice:** A method to train and protect attention from mind-wandering [[03:12](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=192)].
    - Anchor your attention on your breath [[03:36](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=216)].
    - Notice your body sitting and breathing [[03:51](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=231)].
    - Zoom in on the vivid sensations tied to your breath [[04:18](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=258)].
    - Redirect your attention back to your breath when your mind wanders [[06:14](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=374)].
- **Applying the practice:** The steps of focusing, noticing, and redirecting can be used in daily life [[06:58](https://youtu.be/YkYrQR8tFzk?si=NGusf5YRUp-rYMkP&t=418)].
