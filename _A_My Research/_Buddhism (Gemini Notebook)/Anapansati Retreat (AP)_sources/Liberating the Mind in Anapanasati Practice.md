Releasing clinging leads to a sense of being "so much more peaceful, so much more satisfying" \[6\].

#### Overcoming Suffering:

The suffering and dissatisfaction we create for ourselves are fed and sustained by clinging attachment \[6\]. Attending to liberation cuts through this \[6\].

In summary, Liberating the Mind is the penultimate or ultimate stage in the Anapanasati sequence, representing the active letting go of clinging and defilements, particularly the sense of self and the desire to control. This release, supported by the tranquility and insight cultivated in earlier stages, leads to peace, satisfaction, and the ultimate freedom from suffering that is the goal of the Buddhist path. It encompasses qualities like desirelessness, signlessness, dispassion, cessation, and experiential emptiness, which are all aspects or avenues of liberation.
