The primary purpose of concentrating the mind at this stage in Anapanasati is to

It involves using the

Concentration requires

Specific techniques, like bringing energy to the body for sloth/torpor \[16-18\] or using settling objects for restlessness \[19\], help counter these hindrances, allowing the mind to settle.

As meditation deepens and the mind settles,

The sources highlight that concentration is not an end in itself but a vital tool to be skillfully applied \[33\].
