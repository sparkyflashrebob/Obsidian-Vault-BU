The Study Guides below are anthologies of readings compiled and explained by Ṭhānissaro Bhikkhu that focus on a particular topic or theme, and are designed as aids for individual or group study. The texts are drawn from the Pali canon, teachings of the great Thai forest

and other sources. For additional Study Guides see

dhammatalks.org

https://dhammatalks.org/ebook\_index.html#study\_guides

Another useful collection of study materials may be found in

Piya Tan's collection of "Sutta Discovery" essays and translations

http://dharmafarer.org

http://dharmafarer.org

, by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (1999; ??pages/1KB) Passages from the Pāli Canon arranged in five sections: the Buddha as physician; diagnoses of the conditions of aging, etc.; the conditions as motivations for heedfulness and diligence; specific remedies; and examples set by the Buddha and his disciples.

https://dhammatalks.org/ebook\_index.html#mindful\_body

https://dhammatalks.org/ebook\_index.html#BurdenOffMind

Recognizing the Dhamma: A Study Guide

https://dhammatalks.org/ebook\_index.html#RecognizingTheDhamma

, by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (2004; 50pp./151KB) In an age when the marketplace is teeming with spiritual books and teachings of every description, it is reassuring to know that the Buddha left behind clear guidelines by which we can judge the validity of a spiritual teaching.
