# Identity View and Stream Entry

This document discusses the Buddhist concept of "identity view" and its role in attaining stream entry. It clarifies that identity view isn't about avoiding certain questions about existence but rather having the answers, specifically through understanding dependent origination. The document explains that ignorance of the four noble truths and dependent origination leads to rebirth in samsara.

#### Key points include:

Identity view is replaced by understanding dependent origination.

People often misunderstand identity view as simply not asking existential questions.

The document argues that having the answers to these questions eliminates the need to ask them.

Ignorance of the four noble truths is the root cause of continued existence.

Seeing the impermanence of intention (sankhara) is essential for attaining stream entry.

Understanding no-self and realizing that one's own beliefs perpetuate existence is crucial.

Clinging, craving, and "I-making" (conceit) are the causes of continued existence.

Not identifying with what one perceives eliminates preferences, craving, and aversion.

An Arahant's "good and bad compass" is recalibrated based on the arising and ceasing of sensations, rather than subjective preferences within sensuality.

Essentially, the document argues that overcoming identity view involves understanding the mechanisms of existence through dependent origination and recognizing that suffering arises from clinging to a sense of self and subjective preferences.
