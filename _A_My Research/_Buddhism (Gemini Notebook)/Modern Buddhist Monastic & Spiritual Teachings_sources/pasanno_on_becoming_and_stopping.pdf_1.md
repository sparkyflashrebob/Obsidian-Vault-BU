We can’t resist becoming; we’re suckers for

becoming; we’re slaves to becoming. We don’t

resist it, nor do we know how to resist it. We

look for ways to justify becoming, to make it a

It is important to recognize that the nature of

becoming is generated through the force of

desire, tanha, of craving—craving for sensuality,

craving for being—for a sense of self, to be

somebody, to be something, or craving for non-

What does that seeking look like? If it seeks an

external object, we focus our attention on

to generate a view or an opinion about, to

But all of this – internal or external—is in the

We need to be able to recognize that the

be enticed by the voices of reason, the voices of

We want to be able to watch the fear, to see it,

I believe that is the insight Sariputta expressed

cessation of becoming.” That’s the whole
