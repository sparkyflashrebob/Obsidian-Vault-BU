# An Excellent Man's Path to Enlightenment

\*A Manual of the Excellent Man\*, also known as \*Uttamapurisa Dīpanī\*, is structured as concise answers to nine questions posed by a layman named Maung Thaw in July 1900 \[1, 2\]. While the book addresses all nine questions, the provided sources explicitly detail only some of them:

The questions known from the provided text are:

1.  \*\*Regarding the five aspirants:\*\* How does the aspirant fulfill the perfections (\*pāramī\*) to achieve his respective goal, specifically for (i) a Perfectly Enlightened Buddha (\*sammāsambuddha\*), (ii) a Solitary Buddha (\*paccekabuddha\*), (iii) a Chief Disciple (\*aggasāvaka\*), (iv) a Great Disciple (\*mahāsāvaka\*), and (v) an Ordinary Disciple (\*pakatisāvaka\*)? \[3\]
2.  \*\*Concerning the ten perfections:\*\* What are their definition, nature, and significance, with particular reference to an aspirant to Supreme Enlightenment? \[3, 4\]
3.  \*\*The fifth request\*\* is a question of assimilation: "The Buddha said that nothing falls outside the scope of the Four Noble Truths, and that nothing cannot be employed as a fruitful subject for contemplation. Would the Venerable Sayādaw kindly give us a guide to the practical application of the Dhamma so that, when we do any meritorious deed, we can be mindful of the Four Noble Truths and the three characteristics of impermanence, unsatisfactoriness, and not-self, thus fulfilling the threefold training, cultivating the ten perfections and simultaneously bearing in mind dependent origination, and the twenty-four conditional relations?" \[5\]
4.  \*\*The sixth request\*\* pertains to: "the Buddha’s victory over the five māras; the definition, characteristics and significance of the five māras; and the difference between the body of Prince Siddhattha, the bodhisatta, and that of the Buddha." \[6\]
5.  \*\*The seventh question\*\* was: "I would like to know the method of taking refuge in the Three Gems." \[7\]

The provided excerpts do not explicitly list the content of the third, fourth, eighth, and ninth questions.
