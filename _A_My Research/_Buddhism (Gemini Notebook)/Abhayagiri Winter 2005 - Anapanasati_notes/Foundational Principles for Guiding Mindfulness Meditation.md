# Foundational Principles for Guiding Mindfulness Meditation

Excellent. Let's start with Step 1: The Physical Foundation.

When guiding your students, begin by helping them establish a balanced posture. Instruct them to sit slightly forward so their weight rests on their sit bones rather than their tailbone \[1\]. This allows the spine to follow its natural curve, supported gently by the abdominal muscles instead of straining the back or shoulders \[2\]. 

For the meditation itself, emphasize that this is not a breathing exercise. Instruct them not to control or force the breath, but simply to know clearly if a breath is short or long \[3, 4\]. 

You can offer them two primary focal points to anchor their attention:
\*   \*\*The Abdomen:\*\* This is excellent for grounding and very helpful for beginners. The physical movement of the belly rising and falling is clear and gives a wandering mind a tangible object to engage with \[5, 6\].
\*   \*\*The Tip of the Nose:\*\* This is ideal for honing subtle, one-pointed attention. Have them feel the temperature and texture of the air exactly where it enters and exits \[7, 8\].
