Program and teacher support

There are two ways in which you can contribute to this program, both are self-assigned donations and no offering is required to participate. These teachings are freely offered.

1) Offer support for the operational costs:

After completing the registration form, you should be directed to a web page for offering operational support,

you can also contribute via

https://vipassanametta.org/wp/admin-support/

Please note, none of these amounts go to the teachers.

2) Offer danā for the teachers:

Kamala and Steve freely offer these teachings, as has been the tradition since the time of the Buddha.

Throughout the year we will extend the opportunity to offer teacher dāna to support Kamala and Steve’s teaching and livelihood. This shared giving and receiving has supported the Dhamma’s transmission for over 2,600 years.

We are deeply grateful for all contributions and we thank you for your support. The Buddha, Dharma, and the Sangha could not have sustained 2,600 years (so far!) without the generous giving of yogis and benefactors.

No individual should feel they cannot participate for lack of funds.

No offering is required and no amount is too small.
