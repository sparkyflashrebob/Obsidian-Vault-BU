Chapter one, the requisites of enlightenment bodhip pakia dharma. I shall now concisely describe the 37 bodhicipia dharmas. The requisites of enlightenment which should be practiced with energy and determination by those persons who wish to cultivate tranquility and insight and thus make worthwhile the rare opportunity of rebirth as a human being within the present Buddha sasa. The bodhicipakia dharmas consist of seven groups totaling 37 factors. First satipathana foundations of mindfulness four factors. Second sama right efforts four factors. Third idipada Places of success four factors. Fourth indria controlling faculties five factors. Fifth bala mental powers five factors. Sixth pjanga factors of enlightenment seven factors.
