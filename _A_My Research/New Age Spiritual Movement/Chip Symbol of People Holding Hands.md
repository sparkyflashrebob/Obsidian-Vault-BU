Teach me peace of mind, 
to be still, 
and find the courage, compassion and skill, 
to trust the spark of light within, 
and serve the selfless will 

-Sir George Trevelyan

a prominent 20th-century British educational pioneer and a key figure in the modern New Age spiritual movement. 
The invocation is used to center oneself in meditative stillness, trust inner guidance, and dedicate one's actions to an altruistic purpose.
