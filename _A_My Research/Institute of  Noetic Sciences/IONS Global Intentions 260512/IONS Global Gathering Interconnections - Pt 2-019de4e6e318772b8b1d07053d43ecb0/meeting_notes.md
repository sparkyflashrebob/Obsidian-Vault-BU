# IONS Global Gathering Interconnections - Pt 2 - May, 01

### Meeting Title: Global Gathering – Interconnections & Group Intention
📅 May 1, 2026
👥 Speaker 2 (Facilitator), Susan (Speaker 3), Jackson (Speaker 4), Joanne (Speaker 5), Cecilia (Speaker 6), Lloyd (Speaker 7), Andy McGuire
### Outline
### Group Intention and Energetic Influence in Professional Settings
- A nurse with fifty years of experience shared that entering a hospital with a conscious intention to have the best day ever was enough to shift the emotional atmosphere of entire units.
- Staff members who were otherwise caught in routines of drudgery and low morale would, by the end of the day, be engaged and experiencing a sense of joy.
- This phenomenon was attributed to the inherent energy available in caregiving work, which the speaker felt was counterproductively suppressed by academic and institutional systems.

### Exponential Power of Collective Focused Intention
- Research and teachings from Kryon suggest that when a group focuses on the same specific intention simultaneously, the power generated increases exponentially with each additional participant.
- A small weekly group of approximately six people who recited and meditated on a shared written intention was described as capable of generating the energetic equivalent of a stadium full of individuals.
- Two factors were identified as critical to the effectiveness of such practices, namely the number of participants and the specificity of the shared focus.

### Global Intention Practices and Organizational Collaboration
- A participant expressed strong belief in organized group intention practices directed toward global peace and planetary well-being, referencing Lynn McTaggart's work on the Power of Eight and the Intention Experiment as a prominent example.
- Ions was noted to have conducted research on group meditation, including studies involving monks meditating on water and plants, led by researcher Dean Radin.
- A recent Ions-sponsored global meditation for Earth Day was mentioned as a direct example of organized collective intention at scale.
- The idea was raised of creating a library or directory of groups around the world engaged in global intention practices, as a means of amplifying interconnectedness.
- Rather than recruiting younger generations into existing organizations, it was proposed that established groups seek to interconnect with youth-led groups already forming independently, honoring the shared purpose across generational lines.
- Ions was noted to be building connections with aligned organizations such as HeartMath, and Lynn McTaggart had previously spoken at an Ions conference on the Power of Eight.

### Collective Intention Shifting Environmental Conditions
- A participant recalled attending a meeting of a healing professionals group called the Healing Connection, held in a noisy restaurant environment in the late 1980s.
- When the group leader guided participants to become quiet, ground themselves, and hold a shared intention of quietness, the surrounding restaurant environment quickly became noticeably quieter.
- This experience was described as a memorable demonstration of how a coherent group field can influence the broader energetic environment.

### Noetic Practices in Organizational and Team Settings
- Ions was described as beginning every internal team meeting and full staff gathering with a noetic practice as a standard operating approach.
- The science team specifically sets a group intention at the start of their meetings, with participants noting a perceptible energetic shift as a result.
- Christopher Bache's concept of the Living Classroom was cited as a foundational model for Ions' facilitation approach, based on the principle that every participant holds a piece of collective wisdom that contributes to a shared field.
- A distinction was drawn between facilitator-led structure and the understanding that wisdom and contribution are distributed equally among all participants in a gathering.

### Formats and Variations of Group Intention Setting
- Collective intention practices were described as varying in form depending on the context, sometimes involving a stated group intention spoken aloud and other times inviting each individual to set a personal internal intention.
- Individual intentions were described as collectively intertwining to form the energetic field of a given meeting, regardless of whether they were explicitly verbalized.

### Ions Community Groups and Membership Access
- Ions community groups were explained as having originated as regional in-person gatherings before transitioning to online formats during the Covid-19 pandemic, which expanded their membership reach globally.
- Many groups remained online after the pandemic due to increased accessibility and broader participation from geographically diverse members.
- A map-based directory of community groups is available on the Ions website, and prospective members were directed to contact programs@noetic.org for access to the community groups page.
- New community groups were noted as not currently being opened, though a number of existing groups remain active and open to new members.
