### Explanation of MACD and RSI Indicators

- MACD was created by Jerry Appel and was originally designed for use on a five-year weekly chart, where buy and sell signals are generated by crosses above and below the zero line.

- Standard 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI) measures the strength of an index or stock relative to itself, with readings above 50 considered healthy and readings at or around 30 historically representing strong buying opportunities.

- Higher price levels accompanied by declining RSI readings indicate that successive rallies are losing momentum compared to prior ones.

- During the tariff tantrum, RSI hit a low of 26, while the current correction shows RSI holding above 50, reflecting relative resilience.

- A one-year moving average (52-week) and a three-year moving average (156-week) are used as key dynamic support levels, with a touch of the three-year moving average historically preceding significant market reversals.

- During the Fed rate-hiking cycle, the S&P 500 dipped below the three-year moving average for progressively shorter periods, from six weeks to three weeks to one week, before staging a strong rally.
