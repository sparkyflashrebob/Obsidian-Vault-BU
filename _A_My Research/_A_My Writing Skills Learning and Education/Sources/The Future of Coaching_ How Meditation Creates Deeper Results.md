### The Future of Coaching: How Meditation Creates Deeper Results

[Home](https://coachingfederation.org/) / [Events & Professional Development](https://coachingfederation.org/community-events/events-calendar-course-directory/) / Courses
Join us for this session, The Future of Coaching: How Meditation Creates Deeper Results, where you'll explore how meditation can be integrated into coaching to support emotional regulation, clarity, and focus. You'll gain practical insight into how meditation deepens the work, strength.

#### Who Is This For?

```
*  Professional and experienced coaches who want to deepen sessions using meditation and energy‑based tools.
*  Newer and aspiring coaches who are looking to build confidence integrating mindfulness into their practice.
*  Leaders, HR professionals, and internal/external coaches who want to support stressed or overwhelmed clients with practical regulation tools.
*  Coach educators, assessors, and mentor coaches who want to expand their toolkit with grounded approaches that enhance presence and clarity.
```

#### What You Will Learn:

```
*  How to integrate simple meditation and mindfulness practices into coaching sessions
*  Ways to help clients regulate stress and settle their nervous system, especially at the beginning of a session
*  When and how to work skillfully with space and silence to support deeper insight and clarity
*  Practical energy awareness tools that help keep sessions grounded and focused
*  How to support your own presence and well-being as a coach so you do not feel depleted after sessions
*  How to begin developing a personal meditation practice that strengthens your coaching work
```

#### Course Details

Today's coaching clients are navigating intense stress, overload, and constant pressure—often arriving to sessions mentally scattered or emotionally unsettled. This session explores how meditation coaching can help clients ground themselves, regulate their emotions, and access deeper clarity before diving into the work. By integrating simple mindfulness practices, coaches can create a more open, honest, and focused environment that naturally leads to stronger breakthroughs and lasting results.
Led by Sura Kim—TEDx speaker, global meditation trainer, and founder of the Meditation Coaching Alliance—this session introduces a practical, accessible approach that blends meditation, coaching, and energy awareness. Attendees will experience Sura's three-step meditation coaching framework firsthand, learn how to incorporate short guided practices at key moments in a session, and discover how to work skillfully with space, silence, and presence.
You'll leave with tools you can use immediately with clients, as well as strategies for supporting your own presence and well-being as a coach. Whether you work with executives, caregivers, leaders, or clients facing high stress, this session will help you create deeper, more grounded coaching experiences that foster insight, emotional balance, and meaningful change.
