**Discrepancies Between Subject List and Audio Dates**

There are several instances where the dates in the subject list [1, 2] do not match the dates mentioned in the audio sources for the corresponding topics.

* **Seven Factors of Awakening:**
  + The subject list entry for the introduction (Talk 37) gives the date October 25th, 2020 [1]. However, the audio explicitly states that the group "dove into these seven factors of awakening" on **November 15th, 2020** [21].
  + Subsequent topics on the individual factors (Investigation, Mindfulness, Energy, Rapture, Calm, Concentration, Equanimity - Talks 38-44) are listed between November 15th, 2020, and January 17th, 2021, in the subject index [1]. Audio from **January 3rd, 2021**, introduces the seventh factor, Samadhi (concentration), and lists all seven factors [22, 23]. This indicates the discussion of the factors occurred across different dates than listed. Audio from **November 9th, 2020**, mentions plans to discuss mindfulness, investigation, and energy the following week [24].
* **The Four Noble Truths (Individual Truths):**
  + The subject list entries for the Noble Truths of Suffering (Talk 46), Craving and Clinging (Talk 47), Cessation (Talk 48), and The Noble 8-fold Path (Talk 50) are listed sequentially from January 31st, 2021, to February 21st, 2021 [1]. However, audio from **February 7th, 2021**, states that discussing the third noble truth that week means going into the fourth noble truth next week [25]. This suggests the third truth was covered on 2/7/2021 and the fourth truth would be covered on 2/14/2021, which differs from the list's dates.
* **Right View & Right Intention / Sila:**
  + The subject list has two entries for Talk 51, listing Right View & Right Intention on 2/21/2021 and Sila on 2/28/2021 [1]. While audio from **March 7th, 2021**, follows up on a discussion about right speech that happened "last week" (which would have been February 28th) [26, 27], the audio doesn't explicitly confirm the topics of Right View & Right Intention on 2/21/2021 or Sila on 2/28/2021. Right View is mentioned as the start of the noble eight-fold path in audio from November 9th, 2020 [24].
* **Sense Spheres Continued:**
  + The subject list lists Talk 36, Sense Spheres Continued, on October 29th, 2020 [1]. This date falls on a Thursday, while most listed dates are Sundays. Audio discussing Sense Spheres and Aggregates occurs on October 25th, 2020 [28], which is a Sunday. The specific date October 29th, 2020, is not confirmed in the provided audio.
* **Closing Out the MOB:**
  + The subject list lists Talk 115, Closing Out the MOB, on August 28th, 2022 [2]. Audio from **August 29th** (year implied 2021 in the specific excerpt, but 2022 in the context of the MOB series) welcomes attendees to the second half of the meeting on that date [29]. While the subject aligns with concluding the MOB series around this time, the specific date is slightly different (28th vs 29th).
* **Ajahn Chah - No Abiding:**
  + The subject list lists Talk 116, Ajahn Chah - No Abiding, on September 18th, 2022 [2]. Audio from **September 25th, 2022**, discusses Ajahn Chah and quotes him regarding being aware of one's mind state regardless of posture, linking it to the idea of "not abiding" [30]. This indicates the discussion of this topic occurred on a different date than listed.

These discrepancies suggest that the subject list might serve as a general guide or record of topics and approximate dates, but the actual schedule and coverage in the weekly meetings, as captured in the audio recordings, sometimes varied.
