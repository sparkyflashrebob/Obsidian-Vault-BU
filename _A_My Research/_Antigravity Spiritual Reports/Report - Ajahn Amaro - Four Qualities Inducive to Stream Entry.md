## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata & Doctrinal Architecture

* **Context:** A condensed, potent afternoon teaching delivered during the *Wising Up* intensive with London Insight.
* **Humorous Sutta Note:** Ajahn Amaro jokes about the Buddha as the ultimate ancient "list-maker," noting that the numerical lists in the suttas provide a practical checklist for spiritual liberation.
* **The Four Factors of Stream-Entry (*Sotāpattiyaṅga* / SN 55.5):**

| Factor | Canonical Pāli | Meaning & Action | Practical Application |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | *Sappurisasaṃseva* | Association with good, virtuous people (*kalyāṇamittā*). | Surrounding oneself with people whose conduct and presence uplift the heart. |
| **2** | *Saddhammassavana* | Hearing and absorbing the authentic, liberating Dhamma. | Listening to authentic teachings that point toward renunciation and peace. |
| **3** | *Yoniso Manasikāra* | Wise reflection / attention directed to the origin. | Looking beneath appearances; investigating experience through Anicca, Dukkha, Anattā. |
| **4** | *Dhammānudhamma-paṭipatti* | Practicing the Dhamma in accordance with the Dhamma. | Aligning daily choices, livelihood, and relationships with the Dhamma without compromise. |

## 💬 Notable Verbatim Quotes by Ajahn Amaro

> *"Stream-entry is entering the current that flows inexorably to the ocean of Nibbāna. These four qualities are not arbitrary rules; they are the exact conditions that make entry into that stream inevitable."*  
> — **[00:46]**

> *"Practicing the Dhamma in accordance with the Dhamma means we don't try to bend the Dhamma to fit our personal desires; we adjust our lives to align with the truth."*
