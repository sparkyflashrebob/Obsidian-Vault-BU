# Humility: The Dr. Bob Plaque Reflection
> Contemplation on the perpetual striving for humility, ego-deflation, and non-entitlement.

---

## Document Text & Principles

__Humility__

__     "Perpetual quietness of heart\. It is to have no trouble\. It is never to be fretted or vexed, irritable or sore; to wonder at nothing that is done to me, to feel nothing done against me\.  __

__It is to be at rest when nobody praises me, and when I am blamed or despised, it is to have a blessed home in myself where I can go in and shut the door and pray to my Father in secret and be at peace, as in a deep sea of calmness, when all around and about is seeming trouble\."__
