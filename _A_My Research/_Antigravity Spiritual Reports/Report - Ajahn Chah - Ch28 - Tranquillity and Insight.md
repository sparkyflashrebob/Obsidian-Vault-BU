## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Location:**
  * *Original Spoken Context:* Delivered to assemblies of monastics and lay disciples at Wat Nong Pah Pong, Wat Pah Nanachat (Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand), and during tours abroad.
  * *Modern Preservation:* Recorded and published as the standard audio compendium of Ajahn Chah's life teachings by the Amaravati Sangha.
* **Theme of the Discourse:**
  * Piercing through theoretical study to encounter the truth of the mind directly. Moving beyond worldly extremes of indulgence and self-mortification to discover the peaceful Middle Way within.
* **Condensed Version of the Teachings:**
  * To calm the mind means to find the right balance. If you try to force your mind too much it goes too far; if you don’t try enough it doesn’t get there, it misses the point of balance.
* **Expanded Version of the Teachings:**
  * Normally the mind isn’t still, it’s moving all the time. We must strengthen the mind. Making the mind strong and making the body strong are not the same. To make the body strong we have to exercise it, to push it, but to make the mind strong means to make it peaceful, not to go thinking of this and that. For most of us the mind has never been peaceful, it has never had the energy of samādhi; so we must establish it within a boundary. We sit in meditation, staying with the ‘one who knows’.
