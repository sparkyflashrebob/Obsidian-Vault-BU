##### Year One Learning Objectives

L E A R N I N G O B J E C T I VE S — Y E A R 1
After engaging with the materials and assignments in the first year of the training, you will be able to...
Teach the basics of meditation

* Summarize basic terms and definitions in the field of mindfulness and meditation
* Give examples of how meditation can be used as a valuable tool for personal and spiritual growth
* Cultivate a committed mindfulness practice informed by the findings of neuroscience
* Describe the fundamental guiding principles of mindfulness practice
* Respectfully acknowledge the Asian cultural and historical roots of mindfulness meditation practice
* Name the different aspects of human experience that can be the focus of mindfulness practice (known traditionally as “The Foundations of Mindfulness”)
* Guide others to develop mindfulness of breath, body, emotions, and thoughts using the materials provided
* Understand, practice, and lead others in the practice of RAIN
  Skillfully offer a variety of mindfulness and heart practices
* Recognize the fundamental heart practices (lovingkindness, compassion, joy, equanimity, gratitude, forgiveness, and generosity) and guide others through these practices using the materials provided
* Examine the list of core competencies and begin skill building for giving talks, guiding meditations, and answering FAQs
* Recognize why, when, and how to offer basic movement practices to your students within the context of a mindfulness meditation session
* Adapt basic movement meditations for bodies of all types, sizes, and abilities

##### Year Two Learning Objectives

L E A R N I N G O B J E C T I VE S — Y E A R 2
Content ©2022 BY THE AWARENESS TRAINING INSTITUTE
After engaging with the materials and assignments in the second year of the training, you will be able to...
Practice stepping into the role of the teacher

* Refine and develop skills for giving talks, guiding meditations, and answering FAQs
* Examine and inquire into the role of the meditation teacher and the common challenges that may arise
* Design and teach two basic introductory mindfulness courses according to the practicum guidelines
* Know skillful principles of creating, forming, tending and ending a group
* Employ the practices of vipassana-out-loud
* Carry out community building skills to cultivate and nurture meaningful relationships in your practicum classes
* Review foundational principles of ethics and the MMTCP Code of Ethics and reflect on best practices to bring mindfulness and understanding to topics such as sexuality, money and power
  Navigate conflict skillfully
* Summarize the role of mindfulness and compassion in the domain of conflict
* Understand common conflicts that arise in communities and cultivate inner resources for responding to them as a teacher
* Meet challenges within your communities—including your MMTCP mentoring group— as opportunities to learn and practice mindful communication and conflict resolution
* Recall guidelines and agreements on ways to speak & listen skillfully while in conflict to catalyze growth and resilience
* Examine ways to work with anger internally and when communicating with others

  Continued
* Recognize the principles behind truth, reconciliation, and restorative justice
  Create safe containers for multicultural group practice
* Examine the relationship between intention, impact, and privilege and identify how these show up in the mindfulness classroom
* Recognize the relationship between color blindness, spiritual bypassing, and universal connection and understand how to direct students to more skillful ways of relating to self and others.
* Reflect on areas of personal growth that can contribute more fully to a multicultural mindfulness community
* Give examples of how you can create containers of safety and inclusivity with multicultural mindfulness communities, as a practitioner and a teacher
  Apply mindfulness teachings to complex life situations
* Recognize how and why addictive behaviors can be formed from a socio-cultural and neurological perspective and recall at least three guidelines on how to teach on the subject
* Understand the relationship between attachment and suffering through the lens of desire, applying mindfulness to skillfully guide students from a sense of lack to a greater sense of wholeness
* Apply the dimensions of mindfulness to your teachings on the wisdom of grief, impermanence, and uncertainty and give examples of how you might use personal experiences of loss and practices to support grief to become a portal to loving awareness
  Inquire into Engaged Mindfulness (Compassion in Action)
* Understand the inseparability of inner awakening and compassionate living
* Describe mindful and compassionate service
* Summarize the two dimensions of mindful presence and mindful response
* Teach intention and finding ways to alleviate suffering
* Recognize the principles of mindful activism

  Learn the Business of Teaching Mindfulness
* Investigate your own scope of training and practice
* Understand the traditional principles of wise livelihood
* Recognize the range of professional options and opportunities for mindfulness teachers and reflect on what path is best for you
* Review ethical principles as related to the business of teaching mindfulness
* Examine and carry out the basic steps for designing, promoting and offering mindfulness classes
