### Racial Awareness Training

MINDFULNESS MEDITATION TEACHER CERTIFICATION PROGRAM

##### Required Affinity Home Group Exercises for White Participants

Format and Process
Brief check-in, 5-minute meditation at beginning of each session; brief checkout at the end.
Please rotate the facilitation role among the group members, with a different person facilitating each meeting. The facilitator will keep time, ring the bell at the beginning and end of the meditation, and guide the group to follow the agenda. The meeting timeline should be held flexibly; if the group needs more time for a particular item, the time frames can be adjusted by agreement of the group.
*Each group member is encouraged to help monitor process by speaking up skillfully if the group is drifting off point and/or by offering gentle reminders of the Agreements for Multicultural Interactions.*
One of the best ways to ensure real inclusivity is by practicing mindful listening, avoiding cross-talk, and methodically going around the circle in response to a question or discussion topic. This practice systematically invites all voices. Anyone who feels like speaking first starts the circle for a question or activity. This way, the same person isn’t starting every time. When the first person is finished sharing, they call on the next person, and so forth, until everyone has spoken. It’s OK to pass on any given round. Use the Right to Pass agreement sparingly and take time to reflect if you are passing more than once in a meeting.
