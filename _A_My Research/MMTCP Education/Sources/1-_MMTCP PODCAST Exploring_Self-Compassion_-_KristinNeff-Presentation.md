# Self-Compassion Scale (Neff, 2003)

### Self-Kindness Subscale: I try to be understanding and patient toward those aspects of my personality I don't like. Self-Judgment Subscale: I’m disapproving and judgmental about my own flaws and inadequacies. Common Humanity Subscale: I try to see my failings as part of the human condition. Isolation Subscale: When I fail at something that's important to me, I tend to feel alone in my failure. Mindfulness Subscale: When I'm feeling down I try to approach my feelings with curiosity and openness. Over-identified Subscale: When something upsets me I get carried away with my feelings.
