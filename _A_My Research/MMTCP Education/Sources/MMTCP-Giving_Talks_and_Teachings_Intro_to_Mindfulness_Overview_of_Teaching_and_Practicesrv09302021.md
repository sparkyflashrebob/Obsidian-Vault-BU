#### Giving Talks and Teachings #1

MINDFULNESS MEDITATION TEACHER CERTIFICATION PROGRAM
Introduction to Mindfulness Overview of Teaching and Practices
TEACHINGS
Nature of Suffering First and Second Noble Truths
Wise Attention What is mindfulness and how does it serve awakening and freedom? Concentration? Lovingkindness or compassion? (This could be framed within two wings of understanding and compassion.)
Hindrances This includes how the second foundation of feeling gives rise to expressions of clinging and aversion
Wise Effort The effort to be present; cultivating skillful mind states and not fueling unskillful mind states
Wise Attitude Toward practice and path; acceptance, kindness, non-judgment; patience; perseverance
Wise Intention Aspiration
Ethics Non-violence, reverence for life, compassion in action
Wise Understanding Seeing impermanence; cause of suffering; emptiness of self and all phenomena
PRACTICES
Concentration Choosing an anchor (primarily breath; possibly sound, sensations, etc.) and developing some capacity to quiet and focus mind
Mindfulness Recognize what is happening without judgment in the moment-to-moment flow of experience

* Important to describe relationship between concentration and mindfulness
* Helpful to use two questions: What is happening right now? Can I be with this?
* Introduce foundations—primarily focus on body/breath, emotions, thoughts
* Supports: Relaxation; body scan (embodied awareness); noting; lenses (microscopic to wide angle)
* Introduce RAIN in dealing with difficult experience (recognize, allow, investigate, non-identification)
* Choiceless awareness—mindfulness of whatever is predominant (not directing or controlling anything, just noticing and allowing)
  Lovingkindness, Forgiveness, and Compassion Meditations
  Mindfulness in Daily Life Using the pause; conscious breathing; body as anchor; power of intention; possibly introduce eating meditation and/or walking meditation
