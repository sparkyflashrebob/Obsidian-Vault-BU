TARA BRACH: So sitting in a comfortable way, closing your eyes, coming into stillness. This
guided practice of resourcing or taking refuge is entitled "Calling on Your Future Self," but you
can feel free to substitute whatever word resonates for you. Could be "high self," or "awakened
heart," "manifestation of bodhicitta." Any word that-- words that point to the most evolved
expression of your being.
You might take several long, deep breaths to collect your attention. Inhaling and filling the chest
and the lungs. A slow out-breath, feeling the sensations of the breath as you release.
Deep, deep, full inhale. And a slow out-breath, letting go, letting go. Again, inhaling deeply and
fully, filling the chest and the lungs. Sensing with the out-breath you can release any tension
that's accumulated in your body, letting go.
And even as the breath resumes in its natural rhythm, sense you're relaxing with the movement
of the breath.
Now imagine that you could journey into the future, depending on how old you are now, maybe
5 years, or 10, or 20 years ahead. So you'll be encountering your future self, which is an older,
more awake, evolved version of yourself.
The future self is how your awakened heart is manifested over time. And you might visualize
your future self teaching others in any setting that comes to mind. Future self might be teaching
just one person, or a small group, large group, formal setting, informal setting.
And picture how your future self looks-- clothing, hair or lack of hair, facial expression. Notice
how they're sitting. Notice the look in their eyes as they engage with students. They might be
giving a talk, or responding to individual questions, listening. Notice what most touches you
about the quality of their presence, heart, transmission.
Now imagine the students are gone and you're sitting with your future self. Imagine, sense your
future self looking at you with a real kindness and complete welcome. And sense that you're
letting your future self know about your emerging experience, teaching mindfulness, teaching
compassion on this path.
And let them know whatever feels most challenging, including wherever you feel most
vulnerable, or insecure, or uncertain. As you're letting them know, just asking any questions you
want about fulfilling your potential and teaching. And then listen, listen with an open heart.
As you take in whatever you are experiencing being with your future self, sense the message
your future self most wants you to remember, something that will serve you in the moments of
your current life, something you can turn towards, take in, be reminded of as you serve others.
And you might be open to this message being in the form of words. There also might be some
gesture, or touch, or some visual image that comes to mind. Just be open.
Continuing in that receptivity, take some moments to imagine and sense the awareness and
wisdom and heart of your future self filling you. Pouring in, bathing the cells and spaces
between the cells, spaces around you, filling you fully. Relaxing open to rest in that sense of
