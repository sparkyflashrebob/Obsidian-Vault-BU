11/9/25 Zohran Mamdani Campaign Discussion
OUTLINE
What Happened: Election Results ● Zohran Mamdani, a democratic socialist and DSA member, won the general election for
mayor of NYC with 50.4% of the vote in a three-way race. ● Former governor Andrew Cuomo, who ran as an Independent after losing the
Democratic Primary to Mamdani, received 41.6% of the vote. ● Republican Curtis Sliwa received 7.1% of the vote. Trump’s last-minute endorsement of
Cuomo likely shifted a significant share of Sliwa’s votes to Cuomo. ● The election saw record turnout, with more than two million voters participating, nearly
double that of the last mayoral election. Mamdani is the first candidate since 1969 to receive more than one million votes in a mayoral election.
● Mamdani dominated among young voters (+60), south Asian voters (+34-36), Muslim voters (+30), public transit commuters (+29). He beat Cuomo in precincts with majority Black (+26), Latino (+20), and Asian (+4) residents, in precincts with majority renters (+20), in districts with public housing (+10), and in neighborhoods with majority low-income households (+2).
● Mamdani won Brooklyn (+20), Manhattan (+10), Queens (+5), and the Bronx (+11). Cuomo won Staten Island (+33).
How was the campaign run? ● Zohran maintained a relentless focus on an economic populist platform with three
central planks: (1) freezing the rent for tenants in rent stabilized apartments, (2) delivering free universal childcare, and (3) making city buses fast and free. He’s also proposed raising the minimum wage, creating municipal grocery stores, and other measures to make the city more affordable for working-class people. Policies requiring additional revenue would be paid for by taxing the rich (top 1% of NYC residents).
● While focusing on affordability, he consistently maintained principled positions opposing the genocide in Gaza, condemning Trump’s ICE raids and mass deportation program, defending Trans rights, and confronting both Islamaphobia and anti-semitism.
● Zohran’s campaign created an unprecedented field program consisting of more than 100,000 volunteers who knocked on over 3 million doors and made 4.5 million phone calls. NYC-DSA was particularly instrumental in developing this program and providing experienced staff and volunteer leaders, trained from previous electoral campaigns. (NYC-DSA has grown from around 5,000 members to more than 11,000 over the course of Zohran’s campaign.)
● The campaign ran a robust communications campaign , especially on social media, where they used short compelling videos to dramatically expand their reach to new—especially younger—audiences.
● The campaign benefitted from NYC’s public campaign finance system , which provides candidates with generous public matching funds for money they raise from NYC residents.
DISCUSSION PROMPTS
Campaign Debrief ● What made Zohran’s campaign so effective? How did it overcome the combined forces
of corporate oligarchs, MAGA Republicans, and the Democratic establishment, who are all arrayed against it?
● What is/was the significance of Zohran’s affordability agenda? What distinguishes it from mainstream Democratic campaign platforms? Is it a socialist agenda? Why or why not?
● What is/was the significance of the Zohran campaign’s volunteer field program? ● What elements of the Zohran campaign are replicable in other places? What elements
might be more unique to NYC or other large Democratic-leaning cities? ● What role did NYC-DSA play on the campaign? What was the relationship between
Zohran’s campaign and NYC-DSA? What lessons can other chapters learn from NYC-DSA’s electoral program?
Challenges of Governing ● What challenges is Zohran’s administration likely to face in implementing its affordability
agenda? How might these be overcome? ● How important is it for Zohran’s administration to deliver on its campaign promises?
What’s at stake if it succeeds or fails? ● How should NYC-DSA relate to Zohran once in office? As inside supporters? Outside
agitators? Both? ● What does it mean for a socialist to take the reins of a capitalist city dominated by
capitalist institutions? What contradictions might this entail? ● What is the significance of Zohran being a cadre candidate?
