● You claim to have “nuance” regarding what is going on in Palestine, but just where is the nuance in providing billions in weaponary to a state formally accused of genocide by many human rights organizations? Rather than nuance, many people would see your funding of the Netanyahu regime as an extremist position, that you dogmatically cling to despite massive evidence that this funding protects no one.
● In December 2023 you voted in favor of H.Res 894 introduced by Republicans that misdefined antisemitism to include anti zionism, a political ideology introduced in the 19th century. Congressman Raskin did not support 894. Many warned it was meant to criminalize those who work for peace and justice in the Middle East. We were right.
● Just one month ago, 15 paramedics were brutally murdered by the Israeli army and then buried with bulldozers along with their ambulances. You may have been horrified by this along with the rest of us, but the fact remains that you sponsored this crime, knowing that targeting of paramedics is routine by the Israeli military. Do you ever worry you may have to answer to a court in the Hague for your complicity?
● Zionist organizations like Betar, Stop Antisemitism, the San Francisco Jewish Community Relations Council, and the Anti Defamation League have supported the deportation campaigns against people exercising their rights to free speech, people like Mahmoud Khalil and Mohsen Mahdawi. Some have even admitted to giving the Trump admin specific lists of people who should be targeted. At one point on Twitter two years ago, you said that “anti-Zionism is often antisemitic.” Do you still believe that, or can you see how we were right to be protesting against Zionist organizations that very quickly adopted fascist tactics domestically?
● You have consistently said that you believe that Israel should remain a Jewish state. Do you believe that Burma should be a Buddhist state, even if the cost to achieve that is the genocide of the Rohingya Muslims? Do you believe that India should be a Hindu state, even if the cost to achieve that is expansive pogroms against its own Muslim populations? Why would you not promote the creation of a single, democratic Palestine for all, when the price of the creation of an ethnoreligious state is almost necessarily genocide?
● You claim to support a two-state solution. Do you think that by giving money and weapons to Israel, that frees them up to devote more of their domestic resources towards destroying any potential of a Palestinian state, as we are seeing now in Gaza and the West Bank? How much land does Israel need to seize and how many more people do they need to kill before you acknowledge the one-state reality?
● Amnesty International, the International Court of Justice, the UNs Special Rapporteur on the Occupied Palestinian Territories, the Center for Constitutional Rights, at least 14 nations, and many more organizations and scholars have labeled what Israel and the US are doing to Palestinians as a genocide. Why do you continue to simply call this a war against Hamas, and how many dead Palestinians will it take for you to call this a genocide?

ruins, an apocalyptic scene. Do you think you have emboldened Christian Nationalists in this country by helping to bring about this prophecy?
● You recently spoke at the Hands Off protest in San Rafael, and must have noticed that the average age was much older than recent protests for Black lives and protests for Palestinian liberation. Here today, I may be one of the youngest people to ask you a question. Have you begun to reckon with the fact that a large swath of youth will never trust the Democratic party because of their support for Israel and the genocide of Palestinians? How can you effectively fight against domestic fascism without buy-in from a demographic that you need? As Ta-Nehisi Coates recently said, “If Democrats can’t draw the line at genocide, they can’t draw the line at democracy.”
