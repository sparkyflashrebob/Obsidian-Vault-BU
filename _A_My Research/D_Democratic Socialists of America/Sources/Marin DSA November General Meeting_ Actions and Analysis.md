Policy Adoption and Compliance

* Members unanimously approved a resolution to adopt DSA National's uniform grievance policy and local code of conduct to bring the chapter in line with national standards
* Bylaws were amended to remove the anti-Communist vestigial clause prohibiting membership in democratic-centralist organizations, which was deemed redundant and outdated
* Two harassment and grievance officer positions were established as required for chapters over 100 members, with nominations to remain open for the next month
* These changes ensure compliance with national DSA requirements and protect the chapter's dues sharing arrangement

Moran Mondani Campaign Analysis

* Moran Mondani won the New York City mayoral election with 50.4% of the vote in a three-way race, receiving over 1 million votes with record turnout of more than 2 million voters
* Campaign success was attributed to relentless focus on economic populist platform including rent freeze, free universal childcare, and free city buses funded by taxing the wealthy
* Field program consisted of over 100,000 volunteers who knocked on 3 million doors and made 4.5 million phone calls, with New York City DSA growing from 5,000 to 11,000 members during the campaign
* Campaign benefited from New York City's public finance system providing generous matching funds for small donations, sometimes up to nine-to-one ratios
