The "First Year" Special Rule

Because you retired in April 2026, you may qualify for the Monthly Earnings Test (the "Special Rule"). This is a critical exception for people who retire mid-year after already earning a high salary.

Under this rule, for the first year of retirement only, Social Security will pay you a full benefit for any month you are "retired," regardless of your total annual earnings.
