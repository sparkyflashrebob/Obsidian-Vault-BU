Now this brings us to the last feature in

this video: the Ozone Maximizer. Mixing

multiple audio tracks at once can sometimes

lead to high or inconsistent output levels,

causing your signal to clip, sound distorted, or

even be too quiet. If you're mixing in Traktor,

we always recommend applying an additional

headroom in the application to reserve some

space for effects or drum patterns that you might

want to apply on top, without risking distortion

or hitting a limiter. You might not always

use up the extra headroom, which can result

in your setup not running at maximum volume.

Now we've solved this with the Ozone Maximizer.

Aside from limiting and keeping your signal

clear of distortion, it will additionally

boost the signal to full scale whenever you're

not making full use of the headroom. In the

Traktor preferences, you can adjust the headroom

and maximize your boost settings in a way that

fits your way of mixing best. My setup works

with 6 dB of headroom and a corresponding 6 dB

boost for the maximizer. Let's listen to how
