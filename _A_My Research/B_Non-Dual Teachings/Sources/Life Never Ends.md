### Over and Beyond the World

#### One does not ‘get into’ this book. Rather, one discovers what flows out of it. Inspirited by Not-Knowing in Not-Being, the author accompanies the reader beyond the internal boundaries of what is considered the real world. He offers an invitation to look more deeply, that each person discovers and realizes fully the ultimate reality that is within oneself.

#### Those who incessantly stock knowledge suffer in the end under the gravity of their piled up wealth of information. Trapped under this shroud, humans also fear that the treasure of knowledge gained could be lost or forgotten through illness or old-age, or that some aspects of their knowledge could in some cases be mistaken. People want everything, but what they don’t want is to be seen by others as an ignorant fool.

#### Knowledge is only useful when we can remember the information stored in the brain and communicate it in a structured and plausible manner. They, the others, will test and analyze what they have understood and respond accordingly. Will they staunchly fixate on all the particulars they have heard to satisfy their relentless search for a needle in a haystack? Or will they mercilessly expose our weaknesses and uncertainties, merely to triumph and stand over us? Indeed, knowledge can be a terrible weapon!

#### What standard shall we use to judge or condemn another person? Recognize this clearly: those who judge or condemn others entangle themselves in the exterior and stray from internal equilibrium. Without being aware of it, they themselves are at fault, even when they feel justified in their convictions. They harm others and do harm to themselves as well. Those who raise themselves above others will fall. Wise people withdraw and let the driftwood float past them, for true order only comes through Not-Doing. Not-Doing is not to be confused with nonparticipation or indifference. Rather, it is an enormous divine intensity, as the reader will discover in this book.

### Do You Remember Your Future?

#### What exactly can you remember? You certainly cannot remember your future. Those things you can remember are for the most part the countless positive and negative experiences you have gone through, and the knowledge gained, which you have saved and stored in your brain. This entire deluge of information originates in your past. What you live now in the present is derived completely from your past. And you believe in your future. This is so because time appears to move and expand in only one direction: forward. All that you can remember determines your life and your being in the world. From the remembered past, you forge the present of your everyday life. However, your everyday life is not really life lived consciously throughout the day. Rather, it is mostly an unconscious series of reflexes, reactions, and automatized patterns out of your past. You experience your yesterday today over and over without being aware of it.

#### Your continuous activity, the directing and shaping of your life, is actually nothing but a redeployment of your yesterday in today, whereby your today already becomes your past in the blink of an eye. This is how you plow your way through life, in the end never encountering anyone else but you yourself.

#### Your life’s path is formed by you walking it. This is how your being in the world is. This is the actual state of existence and how things change within the world.

#### That which you experience in your sense of the present is dependent on your past, for each of your conceived and structured thoughts originates in your remembered past. You cannot remember nothing. There has to be something there for you to remember, something that you can think about. Nothing gives you nothing to think about. This something then is the compilation of your remembered past. Look there and you will recognize this: you are a person from yesterday who lives inside a belief and a concept of an imaginary today.

#### All your experiences as a human being sustained as thoughts and images within your brain automatically turn into what you believe yourself to be. It

#### becomes that which you process and consolidate in your brain. You are not able to control, guide, or influence these operations. All of it happens within your being, but without your ‘I’. Nature has its own dynamics.

#### In the blink of an eye, a flurry of information, stimulation and reflexes in the sense organs turns the world of experiences into a formed consciousness. This construct reflects what is internal in the external, and also reflects the external internally. It is through this reflex that consciousness arises. In other words, consciousness becomes aware of itself as consciousness.

#### There must be something there that can become aware of consciousness. This something is that which nourishes consciousness. That which nourishes and that which is nourished are not separate from one another, just as you cannot exist separate from Totality.

#### External worlds are guided by an invisible, inconceivable, intelligent force. Direct your attention here! Penetrate the external shell which you in one way are and, in another way, never were. The brain is the switchboard which makes conscious life possible. It wants to get to know itself, think about itself, and examine itself. This investigation of the mind is of course only possible within time and space, where it has been developing in an evolutionary process over thousands of years.

#### Still, there is this strange urge within us to look deeper, to investigate further, to ultimately burst through and move beyond inner boundaries and limitations. Something within consciousness would like to find out what is beyond these boundaries. Maybe this is because human beings have a hard time bearing thoughts of their temporal existence. Every cell of the human body is programmed with a thirst for life and survival. Life desires to live!

#### But really, what is life? I don’t mean stories of a life or experiences which shape and distinguish one life from another. No. I ask, what is life itself? Indeed, it is an unidentifiable mysterious essence which makes stories of a life possible, without being the stories or getting involved with the stories, just as the sun never involves itself with shadows. Yet, it is this that makes these experiences possible. It is only from the standpoint of Not-I that the shadows of the appended worlds of form can be seen through and transcended.

#### One who awakens and looks out on the world from the light of eternity sees with all-embracing clarity what lives within time and what dies within time.

#### He is free and detached from these processes. Neither the past nor the future are able to have influence on the awakened one. His presence is pre-worldly and without shape or form.

#### Realize this for yourself and enter the pure land of silence! An immeasurable serenity will permeate your being and flow through you. Awakening does not lead to a euphoric floating through our days with pretentious smiles on our faces. One who lives from the depths of being holds on to nothing. He dwells quite unremarkably in the Inconceivable.

#### Awakening doesn’t happen in such a way that one suddenly ruminates from the perspective of eternity for an immeasurable fraction of a second on the processes of the past in order to make corrections. Rather, it is a silent not-thinking, a silent not-interfering, a silent not-acting, an awareness which arises while one abides deeply within oneself, where the awakened one sees clearly everything that is unreal within the real. The awakened one sees without interfering or identifying with what happens. His field of vision is empty and vast like the radiant blue sky. White clouds and dark clouds move through. The blue sky never changes. Phenomena arise and vanish again, but the awakened one, who is rooted in primordial essence, remains unaffected.

#### Beyond the recognizable lies the beginning and the end of the world. Space absorbs all phenomena and gives them a home. But how can the fully emancipated one ever be moved by this, one who no longer orients externally and who is free from subjective biases? The externally oriented intellect and externally focused instruments of the senses have no access to what is unfathomable, unimaginable, and invisible. Yet, the experience of the primordial exists, where the one experiencing has melted away and is completely absent. Free of all that is past and all that is the future, the awakened one dwells in the silence of the Great Mystery.

### Let Go of Yourself!

#### All of your memories are deposited and retained in a gigantic invisible reservoir in your brain. Memories are actually inactive energies, sleeping forces that can be awakened and reactivated at any moment. Your everyday life is compiled of what you remember, and what you can remember is derived entirely from your past.

#### Memories arise permanently within your consciousness and activate your thinking and your intellect. The relentless infusion of knowledge which tenaciously floods and harasses the brain can under certain circumstances catalyze massive disruptions, which rob you of your peace and detract from the quality of your life.

#### Polarities, which are generated through conceptual thinking, seize the psyche and constantly compel it to find new modes of thinking. These new attitudes serve primarily to shape one’s personal reality. It is very clear what results from this mental combat. Each is ready to defend the personal reality in which one lives to the bitter end.

#### Thought creates and incorporates specific realms the individual being is involved with and identifies with. However, the boundlessness beyond the narrow conceptual world of thought from which these transient personal realms and mind-states within consciousness arise is infinitely wide. Observed very closely, it is again clear that these constructs of thought have been imposed on consciousness and that the world of thought is, in reality, unreal. Their base is actually a mixed cluster of speculative knowledge.

#### You cling to forms and objects that actually do not exist at all, except within your imagined world of thought. Whatever takes on shape and form is destroyed again. You know this, and yet, your consciousness elegantly shields itself from this fact. It identifies itself with the body and ultimately wants only one thing: to survive. Above all, it must avoid dying.

#### You are actually aware of your impermanence and the impermanence of all things. Nevertheless, you attach yourself to everything impermanent as if it is real and permanent. This is tragic, for it is exactly this attachment to

#### objects and bodies which produces fear, suffering, and a wide spectrum of worries.

#### The fear of losing someone or something you love, a being or an object that is important to you, arouses deep anxiety, a feeling of decline and ruin. This feeling is very destabilizing. The significance you award to the bodies and forms is the cause. The world and all bodies within it are constantly changing. Living beings appear on the stage of physical existence and then disappear again. What once was, is no more.

#### Detach yourself from external illusions! You will not experience a descent into a strange dark night once you discover the heavenly radiance within your being. Don’t be afraid. Awaken from the self you are not! Allow that which you never were to melt away! When the body falls away, your pre-worldly being does not suffer any loss.

#### Allow the processes of change to be! Don’t try to influence or control them. Don’t worry about the unavoidable! Be aware that you can only let go of that which you really are not. You can never let go of you yourself.

#### Be silent and observe how happiness and unhappiness, love and hate, joy and suffering, health and illness, and life and death constantly alternate. Be silent and aware that these forms of being which flow by and never stop changing can never be you. The witness can never be that which is witnessed. You objectify the processes you observe. Still, you are never that which you objectify. The world you witness is not what you are, and the body you witness is also not you. You can never be what you observe. The subject can never become an object. The world never said to you that it exists. Your body has never announced that it belongs to you – you make these claims. The problem lies here: you observe the world and your body as your personal possession, and you behave accordingly.

#### Your seeing is always influenced by the one seeing and remembering. You are always alone in your seeing. No matter what you see, it is a reflection of your externally projected construct within your consciousness, which you perceive and accept as your world and your body.

#### Nevertheless, there must be something, a something that allows for the other to exist, an invisible foundation, an inconceivable, all-encompassing essence which is not separate from that which you eternally are. You are convinced of your perception and experience of having an inner life independent of the external world. But seen more precisely, you live only in mirrored images of your own making, which you continually occupy and

#### animate with your thoughts. That which sees through the eyes is not the eye itself. That which hears

#### through the ears is not the ear itself. That which smells through the nose is not the nose itself. That which tastes with the tongue is not the tongue itself. And that which feels with the skin is not the skin itself. Something fundamental and essential is there, which allows conscious perception through the senses.

#### Your true presence is not how your externally oriented mind conceives and imagines it to be. Yet, one thing is certain: you are not here to live trapped by earthly worries. You don’t have to suffer and let yourself be conquered by them.

#### One who is detached carries heaven and earth within and unifies them. He is the equilibrium between the two. In his Not-Doing, he generates serenity and peace for all living beings. Never be careless with thoughts and words! They are truly precious divine instruments.

#### Laziness in analytical thinking is an illness from which one should awaken and recover as quickly as possible. Life offers much more than what is experienced through the parameters of a methodically organized everyday life with its automated routines and habits.

#### You will not find meaning and purpose in your ideas, concepts, and dogmatic models of belief. But you can find it in that which exists within you prior to all that is thought or conceived. Not-I is not dependent on ‘I-am’, which appears in time and space. One does not condition the other. Not-I exists independent of ‘I-am’, though indeed, ‘I-am’ has no foundation and cannot manifest without Not-I. From the perspective of ‘I-am’, Not-I is unreal and non-existent. From the perspective of Not-I, the superficial ‘I-am’ is unreal and as such has never truly existed. A committed penetrating look into these distinct perspectives breaks open the nucleus, the focal point where both ways of seeing reside. A gate into eternity opens. Here, contradictions and polarities have never existed.

#### Yes, it is a paradox. The world exists, and at the same time, it doesn’t. And still, that which co-exists is dependent on something with which it can co-exist. Therefore, detach yourself from co-existing things and co-existence. Cling to nothing! That which you believe yourself to be is not what you really are. What you really are evades your thinking mind, your intellect,

#### your beliefs, all your knowledge, and all your memories. That which appears through thinking as concepts in consciousness is

#### added to memories already stored there. It influences your way of seeing and conditions everything you experience in the outer world, everything you assume to be your life. From this perspective, you mainly experience ideas and images which are superimposed and added, and not what is primordial and eternal. Reality is absolute vitality which makes an inseparable individual life on earth possible, though it never mixes or interferes with what appears as individual, just as oil never mixes with water.

#### Don’t be afraid to be unthinkable. That which you can think of is never your true unassailable being. Everything which you have ever conceived of is your non-self, which you never were.

#### Be aware that you were never for one moment a guest in the external, impermanent world. Indeed, you are eternally here and now. Although you appear in the external world, you are not the external world. Your true being is pre-worldly pure essence, the unthinkable Mystery.

#### In the morning when you awaken in your bed, your physical life shows itself to you. You have returned to the waking state after emerging from a sleep state, and you become busy according to your habits with all the things and objects you encounter. With your thoughts and feelings, you involve yourself with the world you feel and perceive. You identify and authenticate everything which your ‘I’-consciousness accepts as real and true. Moments earlier, during sleep, your entire psychodynamic system was resting in an inactive state of consciousness in a place where everything is nothing, and yet where nothing is nothing.

#### While sleeping, you were not conscious of your body nor of the waking world where the body exists and functions. In truth, sleep is a kind of unconsciousness in which the world and all knowledge about the world are completely absent. Why is it like this? Because it is as it is!

#### We are unable to control or direct the expansions and contractions, the appearances and disappearances, and all the other grand cycles within the cosmos, just as we can do little about the changing tides of the great oceans of the world. Just as tides rise and recede again, each night you submerge into sleep and are washed up again into a waking state again every morning.

#### Human beings who act with inner freedom and integrate without resistance

#### with the great cosmic cycles are those that transform within light. They create without possessing and act without aspirations. They don’t disturb the world in the slightest. Those who remain steadfast within the primordial see the limits of the body and at the same time are aware of the limitlessness of their true presence.

#### An attentive look at how you examine your feelings, your thoughts, your deliberations, and actions will shed light on how you manipulate the space of your external world. This incessant activity of the brain which allow the corporeal and the outer world to appear is quiet and inactive during sleep. Do you therefore cease to exist while you sleep? Or do you continue to exist without your body or world in the womb of consciousness? The experiences of wakefulness and sleep determine the physical existence of all living beings. Every morning, all emerge from sleep and return to the waking world.

#### A few minutes or a few hours ago, you were still in deep sleep. You were without consciousness of your body, without a world to experience, and without a knower who is convinced of knowing anything. Without the knower, individuality does not arise, because the knower is the architect of the entire building complex one refers to as the ego. The knower is that which it knows. When consciousness is spontaneously stimulated and shows itself as this form, the form assumes itself to be an individual. That which has coalesced and is processed within the brain leaves various traces of memory behind with which the brain can orient itself. The capacity and speed with which the brain learns from its memories defy the tenets of the limited intellect and exceed human perceptive faculties.

#### Everything that you believe yourself to be exists exclusively as a concept inside your brain, which tirelessly reflects itself. Nothing happens and nothing exists beyond the activity within your brain, including your idea of having an individual existence. Seen in this way, you are nothing other than a construct of your own mental activity.

#### At this point, the following questions arise: Do you have a brain, or are you the brain? Does your brain belong to you? Does the world, an assemblage of sensory perceptions stored in the brain, belong to you? Or does the brain make this claim due to its intense identification with the appearances within it and the intense feelings that subsequently arise? What belongs to whom, and why must everything always belong to someone? In reality, this someone who makes these claims does not exist.

#### There was not a single moment when your true eternal being lived in the external world and experienced or enjoyed anything in particular. Your eternal being will also not experience your death, for the one who experiences is only a phenomenal construct of consciousness. This construct of consciousness is certainly a manifestation of your true being, but actually nothing more than a hovering layer of mist below the shining sun.

#### The deconstruction of external illusions leads to fulfillment, to internal emptiness, within which the perpetual generation of an external transient life no longer takes place. External life is not a goal and physical death is not liberation.

#### The sense organs function like spotlights. Depending on how the intellect applies them, light is thrown onto an object or an entire landscape. To the thinking mind, the perceived objects appear real, to have a true existence of their own. Because of this, a series of identification-processes ensue, which leads to the coveting of those objects identified in the world arena. Through lopsided sentiments and attachments to temporal forms, the radiance of divine being is dulled. Finally, it is suppressed and degraded. The unconditioned becomes the conditioned, and the living become the dead. These energies which expand and are externally focused lead to a cocooning, an encapsuling of human beings in their own projections.

#### The world is constantly changing. Thoughts, feelings, and concepts of life shift relentlessly. No moment is the same as the next. Ultimately, these are all only reflections in consciousness, ‘I’-magingings of an imagined world. Everything is an expression of the Eternal, of consciousness becoming conscious, of the invisible becoming visible – like the mist of your breath on a mirror.

#### This experience happens without effort in the absence of an experiencer, without ‘I’-consciousness. The internal connects to the external without attaching itself or interfering with it. In this way, becoming never takes place. There is only one light, one power, one being, and that is you!

#### Countless bodies are produced and destroyed. This process never affects what you truly are. Time permeates the world with its purpose and its virtue. The sun and moon ascend and fade from view again. Don’t allow yourself to be carried off by the millenniums! Take the universe gently in your arms and accompany it back into eternity – out of nothingness and into Not-Being.

#### Awaken! Don’t allow time-conditioned longings to carry you to your

#### grave. Your true being is timeless, primordial, and eternal. Don’t get caught up in the external world! Don’t mix and get mixed up with bodies and objects which appear and vanish again within your consciousness! Don’t hold on to temporal things and don’t cling to your body! All of the materials from which the exterior world is compiled and constructed will disperse and dissolve again in due time and return to their original components and elements. Nothing endures. Nothing, which is born, produced, formed, and cultivated will persist, other than what you eternally are.

#### Those who maintain that what is unreal is real and base their actions on this perspective have lost sight of the primordial light and continue to wander around in vague uncertainty.

### Why Do You Live?

#### Yes, why do you actually live and what do you live for? Why are you here in this transient world? Can it be that your presence here fulfills an unidentified, predestined purpose? Is there any meaning to your presence here, or do you just imagine there is one?

#### Your life consists mainly of conditioned, embodied thoughts, supported by compulsive forces of the will. Within this narrow space, you try to develop yourself and achieve happiness. You look for the hidden, mysterious, spiritual self, but this search follows registered traces of your memory and is guided by your operating intellect. You look for yourself because you seem to sense that something is not quite right with you. You look for the reasons why you experience suffering, anxiety, and fear, and then you want to know why you have an ‘I’ and why you define yourself as ‘I-am’.

#### Without an ‘I’ there would be none of this unpleasantness. Don’t cling to anything that is temporal! Stabilize yourself within the primordial! Then, the energies of not-self will gradually transform. In this transformation, desires, greed, and worry fade and ultimately vanish like smoke from a fire.

#### Turn completely within! Allow your senses to rest within consciousness and stop adding anything external. That which you really are will then naturally reveal itself.

#### The less you become, the deeper you are able to dis-cover your true being. All objects and all forms have a beginning and an end. They testify to the limited, unstable world of the senses. Nevertheless, the intellect bequeaths a great meaning to all things and all forms. Through this conduct of the thinking mind, greed is ignited within consciousness. The flame of possessiveness is fanned, which leads to clinging to transient bodies and objects. Obviously, the happiness one wants so badly and has worked hard for is never external or separate from oneself. No object provides happiness in and of itself. You simply project your sense of happiness which arises through a surge of hormones onto the objects you desire. All that is temporary can only provide temporary joy and satisfaction, which ultimately leads to dissatisfaction and disappointment.

#### Emerge from the turbulence of time! Something mysterious in the depths of your soul wants to shine forth. You are light. The body and the world are mere dancing shadows. See that you can only overcome and detach yourself from what you in reality are not. You cannot let go of yourself. So then, see through this illusory realm of not-self and allow it to evaporate. Merge your being within the one inseparable reality! And still, be aware that this is not about some special exalted state where one floats off on cloud nine.

#### There is a deep silence, a contentment that has no cause and cannot be explained with words. This silence is the luminous essence that yields nothing, but indeed is everything that exists. This is the primordial Mystery, your true nature.

#### It is this source of ever-present vitality which allows life to be possible on the earth. This divine essence never interferes with the physical world. It never becomes something, never takes a temporal form, and remains eternally untouched by the ephemeral world. Silence never becomes noise; otherwise, it would not be silence.

#### Vitality is much more than a concept and does not allow itself to be analyzed, measured, or weighed. It exists beyond all causes and effects. It is your true nature! Within this Mystery, the finite and the infinite, the heavy and the light, and the mortal and the immortal are all unified.

#### You are never that which you believe yourself to be. That which you believe yourself to be is certainly something other than that which you really are. Because you are convinced that you are a personality living and acting in a physical body at a particular location, you have lost sight of it.

#### You stare out into the external world, into nothing, and you are convinced that the vivid reflections you perceive within consciousness are something real, something solid, something which truly exists. You are completely convinced that your life as it presents itself to you through your senses is true and real. These externally oriented, firmly entrenched images and concepts are, in reality, your non-reality.

#### In the external world, you are pulled around from one death to another by your mental schemes, by the assertions of your will, and by your concepts of the world, and you never find peace.

#### Don’t you think that right now, yes, this very moment would be the right time to say goodbye to this death march and ultimately free yourself from these forces which compel you to lose yourself in the external world? The

#### value of superficial daily activities is actually exhausted. This old, brokendown, internal abode cannot be continually renovated and re-inhabited over and over again. Take your hands away from all that is dead. You will not suffer any loss. Awaken and stop living inside of and out of what you truly are not!

#### Don’t think that it is difficult or not possible for you. Such patterns of thought weaken your being and provide the sustenance for your death march through time. Don’t gallop after some concept of life! Don’t harm your fellow human beings! Rid yourself of your desiring personality and its desires! Rid yourself of all animosity and greed! Where you involve yourself in watching processes of development, the inseparable One fades from view and what has meaning becomes meaningless. Be aware that your deepest inner core is pre-worldly and eternally so, free of concepts, formless, without characteristics, and impossible to describe with words. One doesn’t get to the outermost point by going out. One discovers it there where all things in their separateness have never begun to separate.

#### Life-energy is the spiritual foundation of everything that has ever manifested. Manifestation originates in the mind, and the mind is the source out of which thoughts flow forth. Without life-energy, there would be no manifestation, no mind, and no thoughts. With your thoughts and with your mind you create your own physical and psychic structure in which you live and take refuge.

#### You live in your own imaginary world. Wearing this psychological clothing, you present yourself externally to other people. It is from this personalized and individual perspective that you observe, analyze, and judge the psychological attire of others. You do this with the hope of experiencing more about them – and about yourself.

#### The strong depictions that you have of yourself and of others vibrate actively in your conditioned and traumatized consciousness. Your narrow, standardized view of things compels you to see and experience your conceived illusory universe as an inescapable reality. Your physical, temporal manifestation with its countless ideas, desires, and fantasies was nevertheless born and will also die at some point in the foreseeable future. It will eventually disappear from the world you were able to see and experience.

#### The tragedy here is that you identify yourself with the invented objects of your desire. You insist that they exist, you cling to them tightly, and you constantly try to increase their number. A subtle greed has invaded and

#### infected your psyche. Every single object appears to exist in and of itself. And indeed, everything which appears is nourished by a unifying essence which underlies all existence, and which will also destroy it all again. Without this unrecognizable, inconceivable energy nothing would be perceived or experienced.

#### Be constantly aware that your true presence exists prior to all manifestations. In truth, you are completely absent within the world, and at the same time totally present within the world. What appears and disappears can never affect your true being.

#### How does this divine essence function? Does it even function at all? An example might help to clarify these questions. Without electricity, the countless lamps and various electronic devices and machines in the city would not function at all. It is the same current that flows through all those objects. It flows through all the gadgets in your home: your appliances, radios, televisions, computers, lighting systems and through many more machines there. Electricity allows this vast multitude of objects to operate within their own particular forms of functionality and utility. Without electricity, all this would not be possible.

#### Similarly, it is one divine essence that operates and flows through billions of divergent forms of life and makes their bodily existence possible. Without this underlying essence there would be no physical life. Something incomprehensible is there which allows the comprehensible to exist. Nothing can exist separately or outside of this heavenly essence. Everything which appears exists within the same realm, within the same consciousness. Divine essence is eternal. The world within it changes constantly though, just as shadows dance under bright light. The Eternal is never touched by influences from the exterior world. Therefore, awakened beings no longer focus their activity on the outer world and no longer entangle themselves with things that come and go. Living within time, they live timelessly.

#### Why do you believe that you exist separately, independent of other people and of other living beings? Why do you see and experience your being as separate and external to yourself and why do you search for the meaning of life there? Who are you and where outside of yourself are trying to get to? Whom do you want to encounter beyond yourself?

#### You know a lot and you believe many things. What you know and all you have come to believe have been assembled and draped over your pure eternal

#### presence and are therefore your superimposed notself. You see the unreal as real and persistently give life to this unreality. That is your real dilemma.

### A Random Result

#### You are the product of a sexual act undertaken by your father and your mother. When they came together in this way, they certainly were not thinking about you. In these moments, they could not have known that fertilization had taken place. You are the random outcome of that act, perhaps not even planned at all, and thus not exactly a desired result. However it happened, you emerged from your mother’s womb nine months later.

#### But from that very first day in the womb, you were permanently surrounded by death, for life and death are twins and arrive at conception at the same time. In each moment and with each breath, life can be unexpectedly devoured by death. It is never to be taken for granted that you who are reading these words are alive right now. Many dangers lurk where cause and effect arise.

#### Where were you and who were you before your conception? Were you nothing, that at birth became a something or a someone? No one asked you whether you would like to be born. You had no choice and no options as to who your parents should be. Seen from the purely physical perspective, you are a bonded servant of your parents, since they produced your body, protected it, nourished it, and allowed it to grow. You were told, “You are our child.” This pronouncement is actually not so inaccurate, since the genetic material, physiognomic features, and character traits you have indicate that they are actually correct. But you remain completely convinced that you are the sole proprietor of your body.

#### Where do these thoughts of possessiveness arise, this claim to ownership? Does it come from you, from your parents, or from all simultaneously? Where does this sense of yours and mine come from?

#### You say or you think that this is my body, but it is only you that says this and not your body. Flesh and bones do not speak. The body has never said that it belongs to you − you say this. You sense yourself as a subject and the body as an object. Your body is your instrument, which you use adeptly. You utilize it to carry out your commands and fulfill the desires of your will with specific behavior. You decide where your body may go and where it may not,

#### and you decide what actions are appropriate and which are not. At times, you treat it well and at other times badly. You are the ruler of your body.

#### You also project this slaveholding mentality onto other bodies and other people, those you sense as ‘mine’ and declare as ‘mine’. My wife, my husband, my children, my house, my car, my, my, my……Through the hypnotic power of this concept of ‘my’, you attach yourself to everything you call your own and you inevitably identify yourself with it all. In this way, a union with everything mortal arises, a conformity and synchronization with everything impermanent. The concept of ‘my’ is a mental state that fundamentally influences, shapes, and determines how the world is understood. This peculiar disposition governs the way you see, experience, and understand yourself and your physical presence in the world.

#### Greed-filled, egocentric behavior crudely carves out its path until it can establish its place in the world. This space was obviously built up and paved with forces of ego. It is through these forces that this overlaying construct is sustained and maintained, and when necessary, defended. All this behavior leads to unhealthy constraint and self-imposed isolation. A wall of shadows like this was never intended by nature, which is why the luminous soul suffers. The invisible links of an egoistic thoughtchain nail the soul to impermanence and to death. And death – physical death is not what is meant here – is the bitter result of this absurd, externally oriented activity.

#### ‘I’-centered behavior produces nothing but a core of suffering and a detrimental state, for one’s actions arise from various dark attitudes and somber moods. This is not a good resting place within, as the subtle heartbeat of the universe is no longer heard. When the roots of heavenly being erroneously gaze outwards and only see material and bond with it, then chaos, insensitivity, resentment, and exploitation reign supreme. Without a doubt, egocentricity is a damaging state which initiates heartless behavior unworthy of human beings!

#### Daily life is extremely fragile and can be thrown out of balance at any moment by bizarre, unexpected events. The psychodynamic being in the world is like a delicate cloth which could rip apart at any moment because it is woven by the exhausting struggle for survival and subsequent memories of strife. Your emergence from these old forces of ego is possible in every moment, when a person is ready to look inwards with vigilance. If one sees through the operating manner and expanding tendencies of the ‘I’-forces, the

#### old, ramshackle construct which was built by these forces gradually begins to collapse.

#### Doubts nourish the things that are temporal and prolong suffering. End the inner relationship to your weaknesses! Take refuge in pre-worldly strengths and powers, which you are, here and now in all eternity. Something suffers deeply within you beneath the tightly meshed egocentric shroud that limits, oppresses, and weighs you down. This miserable condition robs you of your joy, your enthusiasm, and your serenity, and inhibits generosity and compassion.

#### Yes, all things come to an end. But one who detaches themselves from rigid patterns of behavior and forces of the ego, overcomes this dismal temporary compound and flows back – in unwavering purity and beauty, into an inconceivable and splendid silence. That which is without name, form, or body is pre-worldly and never affected by the world of appearances. This pre-worldly suchness shines brighter than a myriad of suns. It is a white light, a brilliance that doesn’t blind and a radiance that is never perceptible to the physical senses. Discover what you never were! Discover what you eternally are! Discover supra-personal, ‘I’-less Being! Discover yourself!

#### As soon as you become truly conscious of your foolish egoistic behavioral patterns and clearly see the disorder and turbulence that they generate in your brain and in your psyche, you will resolutely stop granting them attention and no longer feed or animate them. A silent inner attentiveness and letting go of the not-self, a composed listening-in to the vital pre-worldly depths of the soul opens this wondrous invisible gate into the eternally un-manifested, unbecoming, unformed, and unborn, into that which you eternally are and were.

#### Because you are in the world, the world exists within you and manifests through you. Yes, it is there because your body and your psyche dwell on the earth. The world is configured in your brain through external impulses and reflexes within consciousness at a speed that is hardly measurable and then maintained and solidified through a collective psyche-program. This psychodynamic system through which the world in your brain consciously appears and disappears again is, of course, what you never were. These patterns and processes operate autonomously, and you are ultimately incapable of guiding, influencing, or controlling them.

#### The body and the world appear within space and time simultaneously.

#### They are never detached from one another, and one never appears before or after the other. Always be aware that your true being is pre-worldly and that you never are what manifests as an appearance. The problem is that consciousness immediately identifies itself with what appears and then senses and perceives itself as such. This is how the infinite becomes finite and becomes entangled and mixed up with everything that is perceived in the impermanent outer world.

#### Yet, the outer world is nothing but a projection of the interior world within consciousness and the interior world consists solely of deeply engrained patterns and acquired habits. On the one hand, both interior and exterior worlds seem to exist. On the other hand, both are unreal. In eternal reality, which is without form, they have never existed. So then, be aware that that which is reflected and presented within consciousness is not your true being.

#### You are a hidden, unformed, mysterious divine energy which does indeed reveal itself on the furthest fringes of consciousness as form and the world. But these appearances have never had access to or affected your true being.

#### Why is this so? Because it simply is like it is! The exterior form comes and goes. The mysterious, unknown, luminous essence is eternal, unchanging, and pure. Before anything appeared, you were.

#### Formed bodies exist like bubbles of water on the ocean. They rise to the surface and then burst again, along with everything they themselves have produced. They were never more than temporarily existing phenomena, just like bubbles on the ocean-surface. Worlds reflect themselves within divine essence and worlds dissolve again within this divine essence.

#### This mysterious heavenly force, this intelligence that dwells in all things, is a creative power which reflects itself and reveals itself within consciousness as creator and things created. This divine all-pervading force grants life and is the source from which Mother Earth grants birth.

### Detachment from Not-Self

#### Who determines what belongs to whom and how much should belong to which person? Who decides the standard by which these things are to be measured? When such questions are posed, a multitude of involuntary thought-patterns are activated. Demanding energies which abide in people’s minds are always keen to assert their supremacy. If winning or losing is at stake, the ego doesn’t mess around. Its focus is command and control, to have or not have, war or peace.

#### Those who see and experience the world from such a narrow, egocentric viewpoint are unable to perceive the radiance and beauty of true inner freedom. Greed is a cold calculating power which dominates people’s views of the world and expresses it. One’s inner life becomes evident through words and deeds in everyday life, which are a clear visible and audible blueprint of one’s attitude towards life. It is very good that people develop themselves, but as long as they are still enveloped in these outer forces, they cannot know their original condition, and confuse what is unreal for reality.

#### Only the smallest part of a massive iceberg is visible from the surface of the ocean. In the same way, the greater mass of people’s thoughts remain concealed in their inner world. One can never really know who other people are and what they are feeling. Perhaps they don’t even know it themselves, since everyday life with all its challenges is constantly in flux and transition. This unsteadiness causes people oriented exclusively on the external world to become instable and unpredictable.

#### The true activity of life takes place internally, beyond earthly tumult and the powers of fate. Pre-worldly freedom is no mythos and awakening is not merely an invention of the mind.

#### Would you water your garden during a hard rain? Does a fish have to drink water to appease its thirst, or does it even know the feeling of being thirsty? Don’t lose yourself to highs and lows! Absolve yourself from that which you never were and discover the heavenly expanse of magnificent Not-Being! Be free from all that surrounds you!

#### You fill your intellect with all kinds of images, ideas, and impressions. Keep in mind that whatever you cherish and call your own does not exist separate from your thinking mind. Remember that your thinking mind consists solely of images and thoughtconstructs. The idea of an ‘I’ and of a ‘mine’ is nothing but a cleverly conceived but confused concept which was contrived and endorsed by the greedy, externally oriented intellect.

#### You maintain that the world is yours and you want to keep as much of it as possible as your very own. But then, what does the world cost? Do you merely live in order to buy the outer world, to possess and control it? This gluttony, this craving to possess relates not only to material goods and objects, but also to all physical bodies in your family, which you also refer to and believe to be ‘mine’.

#### To live this way, to be this way, with all the striving, hoping, and desiring and by maintaining countless externally focused, goal-oriented ambitions, you produce a sense of continuity in your life. This feeling gives you satisfaction. The ‘I’ orients itself constantly on what has happened previously. It sees in this past its future. But the past as well as the future are bound to the superficial mortal realm.

#### The grassy playground upon which the past becomes the future is soaked with tears. Like powerful gusts of wind, a person’s alternating moods have given birth to apprehension and suffering. All frantic efforts to hold onto one’s place in exterior life eventually fail. The instability of things is too great. The one who wants to maintain everything overlooked the exit gate which leads to eternal being. Outer striving and unceasing activity are and have always been ineffective and useless. The incomplete never completes itself.

#### A sense of time and continuity gives rise to a feeling that things will always go on, including after death if possible. This strong, solidified perception of continuation constitutes the foundation upon which human beings structure their lives. It goes on, always on and on…. but where does it actually go?

#### Be internally free, independent of striving, hoping, wanting, believing, and all concepts of continuity. Then, the sublime realm of the pre-worldly reveals itself, where nothing has ever begun, and nothing will ever come to an end – your true spiritual home. At this point, all words fade. All knowledge comes to an end, as do all earthly experiences.

#### What remains is awareness and astonishment. Within the Mystery, the

#### hidden life is revealed and made perceptible. The awakened one sees the external world as something not to be interfered with. The river forming the frontier that sends one back into time will never be crossed again.

#### Detachment from not-self soothes inner storms and calms archaic cravings. IT is so close, and IT was never far away. And yet, one constantly negates and fails to recognize IT. Perhaps, this is because IT is of no use to the ego and cannot be known.

#### Open your eyes and recognize the beauty and importance of diffusing the ego and detaching your being from it! Abide in activity without acting! Forces of the ‘I’ flow out of an internal, invisible center and produce causes and effects. Causes change into effects and effects change to causes. That which emerges from this inner ‘I’- core determines your relationship to your fellow human beings and to the objects which surround you. And yet, causes do not exist separate from their effects. In this way, you experience the world, and this world is that which you in reality are not.

#### In actuality, you are living in the dungeon of your egocentric personality-structure where the thoughts you propagate are predominantly self-seeking. Varying in accord with your predisposition, you are tirelessly engaged in securing growth, prosperity, and proliferation for the seeds you have scattered. What was sown and then reproduces itself will at some point in time diminish and ultimately be destroyed. That which arises has its demise. That which is useful becomes useless. The egocentric harvest is like a thorny field of bushes; dense and impenetrable. The fruit taken is, in fact, poisoned. The crop has yielded nothing but life and death and endless suffering.

#### The dynamic of this externally oriented manner of being shapes and forms the ephemeral life of human beings. The foundation of this fabricated structure is made up of longings, cravings, hopes, and lustful urges, which in turn form the foundation for disappointment, pain, and suffering. The spectrum of cause and effect appears to be infinitely vast and complex. These conflicting energies determine and guide the patterns people follow in their journey through time.

#### You search constantly for effects because you believe in the cause. You believe in the endless intoxicating process of following desires you are trapped in. You are convinced that each fulfilled desire will grant you satisfaction and gratification. And this may be the case. However, the elation which might accompany this sense of satisfaction lasts only a very short time.

#### In the future, this feeling will subside, and the psyche will sink again into a kind of apathetic dissatisfaction once again, returning to its hunt for this addicting sense of pleasure. Nothing, in truth, is permanent on this earth, and that includes your thoughts and feelings which strive tirelessly to manifest and perpetuate eternally within the boundaries of time.

#### You believe you have a free will. Is this really true? In order to manifest a particular action, many factors are involved. Most actions are conditioned by a person’s genetic makeup. They are further shaped by how one was raised, the moral and ethical values one has adopted. The concentration of hormones between the individual brain cells also has an influence. Nothing happens so easily.

#### You actually want only one thing: to be happy. This is normal and natural. But you seek this state of happiness on the wrong side of your being, on the side that is external and separate from you. You seek it in the body, in objects and things that are impermanent and subject to destruction and dissolution at any moment. Why are you so convinced that you can find contentment in temporal things? The fact is that no object, no thing contains happiness in and of itself. You are the one that projects this sense of happiness onto the objects and things that you would like to have and possess. This illusory contentment comes into being through the values you give to those objects and things.

#### The source of all desiring is the intellect. Observe and analyze it in greater depth and you will find that it doesn’t even exist. This insight, this realization will show you the relative nature of your desires and eventually dissolve the one who desires. When this happens, a deep serenity sets in. A contentment without cause, an enduring satisfaction can enter which has nothing to do with an external existence. Not-desiring and not-existing fill any and all noticeable gaps, which in reality never existed.

#### Stop dividing and separating the world into ‘mine’ and ‘yours’. It is precisely this subdivision that characterizes and governs your volatile, outwardly focused psychological state. See how and where your vision of the world originates and manifests within you. You will come to realize that all of it is nothing but a superimposed construct of thoughts, an add-on to your conditioned pre-conceptions. Why have you gotten so lost in something that doesn’t even exist within you?

#### Why do you stubbornly hold on to all those things you thought and imagined for yourself? Why are you such a believer in your thoughts? Don’t you see how your everyday life is an assemblage of thoughtconfigurations and images, and that all things thought and imagined dissipate and vanish into nothing? It happens whether you like it or not.

#### Your external life originates out of a stream of thoughts. This leads to the establishment of a thoughtworld, within which you live confined. Your life is actually nothing but a colorfully strewn arrangement of thoughts. By penetrating this superimposed thought world, one can clearly see that nothing deliberate or intentional, nothing external can cling to pre-worldly being. Each act of cognitive recognition is dependent on something that is external and separate from true, eternal Being. Those who awaken are aware that the one recognizing and the thing recognized do not exist separate from one another. There is never the slightest border or gap between them.

#### Certainly, that which has no limits never seeks out limits. Why should it? And why should you seek yourself when you are you already? The limited looks into the dream of the dreamer. The unfathomable, however, never allows itself to be fathomed. It is eternally empty and yet encompasses all.

#### Actions are fully formed and condensed thoughts. They are the doings of the thinking mind. As long as the externally oriented intellect is active, chaos and discord rule. The intellect allows the possibility of understanding and analyzing thoughts. But you don’t think exclusively about your own thoughts. You consider the countless thoughts of other people. The intellect processes and analyzes the words of others, and this is how one’s own thoughts get compounded with the thoughts of others. And then, new thoughts arise through this compounding, which you observe, understand, and claim as your own.

#### Everything you think about, including the environment you live in, is inseparable from the thinker. All these things exist in the same realm. You are conscious of your present state and the external world in which you function. These are not two separate things, as you might believe. Nothing exists independently. Everything exists within you, with you, and through you. Everything that exists within the space of the world changes constantly; everything except that which you eternally are. That which truly is never changes. Your true being is free and beyond space and time.

#### You appear in the world as ‘I-am’. As Not-I, you exist beyond time and

#### space and are never touched by the world. Completely present in the world and completely absent in the world: this is the awakened one’s being. This sounds paradoxical and even absurd for the intellect. But the intellect itself is not aware that it is only a part of an externally manifested world and has no true independent existence of its own. It is an instrument that primarily serves the ego and helps it to establish its egoistic existence in the world.

#### You are persistently occupied with trying to understand things. That which you don’t understand causes you anxiety and makes you feel insecure. In contrast, everything that you can understand or believe you can understand gives you a sense of contentment. What you know and understand conveys a sense of safety and comfort.

#### Those things which you can understand are merely thoughts which the intellect itself has produced. It keeps you busy, mainly with its own productions, which it considers true and real. It is also ready to defend them. The intellect is tirelessly active in naming and arranging forms and objects within space and time.

#### Be deeply aware that all bodies, all names, all forms, all objects, and all places are enveloped by an inconceivable, boundless emptiness. This emptiness is not nothing. It is rather the essence of what you really are. Bodies, places, forms, and all names are ephemeral. This unfathomable emptiness, on the other hand, is eternally here and now.

#### All material objects and all bodies are external things. Time and space act as the container within which they live and function. But, these uncountable objects and bodies that dwell in space never touch your true pre-worldly presence.

#### Those searching outwardly are mistakenly convinced of being the external thing which appears in consciousness as the body. They identify with this image which they in actuality are not and never were. In this way, the mind binds itself to the transitory without being aware of it. They become that which they identify themselves with, namely a temporal body that suffers.

#### You experience your outward presence as natural and normal, and you observe the progression of your individual life in this three-dimensional existence. This is truly the dream of a dreamer.

#### Don’t wed yourself to life or to death! Move beyond the last barrier! If not now, then when? Your true presence is completely beyond the world of objects and detached from all the things and bodies which appear and

#### disappear within consciousness. Don’t identify yourself with that which appears in consciousness! Don’t identify yourself with your erratic thought-images which you perceive internally and consolidate into external experiences!

#### The equilibrium which human beings seek and strive for lies in the recognition and realization of non-active activity. Let things be as they are! Don’t interfere, for life lives itself very well. Basically, everything would be aligned in the greater order of things if you would detach yourself from subjectively produced disorder. It is advisable to contemplate these words deeply, to understand them and transfer their innermost meaning into the depths of your own life. And don’t forget; you are that which you seek!

#### The forces which drag one’s life into the external world like an ocean’s undertow and clamp down the psyche to the material world are incredibly strong. The path up and back is fraught with ploys and pitfalls. The difficulty, which we now know is that each and every perception is dependent upon something that exists outside of yourself. But the door to intuitive logic and insight is always wide open and the proficiency of celestial powers is available at all times. Nothing can exist separately from you, for you are always here and now.

#### Divine radiance shines through all the senses as if passing through open doors and windows and illuminate a person’s entire physical and psychical being. Dulled eyes are not able to see this radiance, for they are attached to the debilitating forces of the external ‘I-am’.

#### Through light, all things are seen. Darkness enters through the absence of light. Misunderstandings arise through ambiguities, divergences, and confused thinking. This happens when respect, modesty, deference, and humility have gone astray, and when arbitrary moods and insolence guide and determine the life of the individual. Through divine light, these harmful external shadows are seen clearly and eventually disperse within this heavenly radiance. This happens because it happens and not because someone is there who does or doesn’t want it.

#### The one appearing internally as well as the one appearing externally are nothing but movements in consciousness which arise through stimulation and reflexes of the ‘I-am’ configuration. The internal and the external replicate and condition each other. Divine light allows the ultimate dissolution of this peripheral worldly dance.

#### Your thoughts are occupied solely with objects and things which you are not. Be constantly aware that you can never be that which you think about. Your image of being a body accompanies you through your entire life until you die. But what is death? Is death nothing but a reflection in consciousness? Consciousness is the light in which images are reflected. Through the appearance of something which takes a form, consciousness becomes conscious of itself as consciousness.

#### The moment you see your mirrored image in the mirror, the mirror ceases to exist for you. At the moment you perceive the world with your senses and think about what you have perceived, you forget that you can never be that which you perceive and that which you think about. The light of the world is not the world. Rather, it is that which you are before the world appears in consciousness.

#### That which is aware exists beyond all that is known or understood. It is pure spirit, pure divine light, essence – that which you are eternally, without ever being something. This mystery is your spiritual home.

#### When you become aware of that which exists beyond name, form, and the intellect, all illusions and thought-constructs disappear. Within this awareness, they dissipate away like smoke from a fire. Vaporous and hollow inside and illusory and imperious outside – this is the nature of superimposed appearances in consciousness.

#### Inventions of thought appear in consciousness as something concrete, something truly existing. They leave deep etchings in the memory. These forceful traces shape and determine the personal path and perspective of the individual. Thoughts arise out of a remote emptiness and are themselves empty and hollow. But this emptiness is not nothing. Rather, it is essence, the fuel without which physical life would not be possible, just as appliances in the home would not be able to function without electricity.

#### The world which you inhabit arises and derives its existence exclusively from your thoughts. Beyond the dualistic thought-world you generate, there is no you. That which you think is that which you believe, and that which you believe appears to actually exist.

#### The intellect is convinced of its own importance and excited about the possibility of understanding itself. Indeed, it is brilliant in comprehending external things. But the mind has no access to what is essential, what is not induced by memory. It can only understand the transient and the finite and is

#### not effectively aware of its own superficial illusory existence. Thoughts produce the external world and one’s everyday life. They

#### station themselves on this playground and vehemently defend their territory. The ‘I-am’ in the external world is not the boundless eternal Self.

#### Be always conscious that you are never that which you think about. You think about a table, about its width and height and in which room you would like to put it. But this table which you devote your attention to is not you. It is the same with your body which you think about, but which you are not. The thoughts that multiply in the mind are extremely dynamic, purposeful, and creative energies. One should never underestimate their effects or repercussions.

#### The intellect becomes active through externally perceived objects and examines them for their utility. What is not useful awakens no interest, meaning no activity in consciousness is aroused.

#### Both desiring and denying are deliberations and decisions of the ego which moves domineeringly in the outer realm, where it positions and distinguishes itself. Those who promote themselves are drawn to the external and will eventually fall. Internally blind, they wander from death to death and are hardly conscious of it.

#### Generally, people orient outwardly and are active outwardly. But in this way, they are not aware at all of the incredible splendor and power of activity through Non-Acting.

#### The Eternal never acts, and yet, billions of living beings with body-forms are nourished by this divine energy and transform. The pre-worldly Mystery allows this nourishment. Still, a mingling or merging with those nourished never takes place. The one will never be the other, although it makes the other possible.

#### Activities undertaken by human beings in the world produce the concept and the feeling of ‘I-am’ and a sense of continuity. The idea arises that life always goes on without interruption, perhaps even beyond death. This continuity is manifested by the ‘I-am’ which appears in space and time. But the ‘I-am’ as well as the concept and feeling of continuity do not exist in eternal being. Awaken and see clearly that you will never find refuge in that void!

#### At the external edge of consciousness within the difficulties of everyday life, the intellect may have its importance. But other than providing order and

#### structure to the swirling thoughts in the brain, from the perspective of eternity, it is useless. Seen this way, you can only understand what you in reality are not. You are that which observes and objectifies movements in consciousness from the standpoint of Not-I. Interference, altering, or identifying do not occur.

#### No matter what your thoughts are concerning the countless objects and things of the world, be aware that you have nothing to do with all those things, that they are not you, nor is anything that has ever touched you. You are neither the thinker nor the thoughts which you have about the external things within the world. Don’t make yourself into a something with your thoughts, into an object that you in actuality never were. Don’t tether yourself through narrow-mindedness. When you do, you dig your own grave and bury yourself. You yourself are the graveyard, the gravedigger, and the corpse in the grave.

#### Go beyond your limited, superficial individuality and enjoy the beauty of unattached Not-Being. Be effective without being active, and be without being someone or something! True depth never dies, for it is not rooted anywhere.

#### In the thought-world, countless individuals seem to exist. But this multiplicity exists only as an external perception within one single consciousness. By defining and specifying forms and things with names, the One becomes two and the two become three, and so on. This is how the image of a multitude arises from one consciousness. The One appears as many, but that isn’t the case when observed more closely.

#### The many big and small things one perceives with their classifications and conventions do not lead beyond time and space. The object you can perceive is not what you are. You are not that which you imagine yourself to be.

#### Objects appear and disappear from your eye’s view and from your consciousness. So don’t cling to things which come and go. Don’t dive into this, and don’t orient your mind on the external! Don’t identify yourself with things that you are not!

#### As long as you remain trapped in the chains of empty concepts, you cannot be aware of the glow of immortal illumination. This luminosity is still and empty, and nevertheless immeasurably powerful. The beauty and abundance of the world are always there. The guard at this border steps aside

#### and allows the awakened ones to pass, for they have shed the forces of destiny. Those burdensome energies and their power struggles were discarded, just as a heavy old coat is taken off and falls to the ground without a sound. The imposing tumult of the world cannot penetrate the heart of the awakened ones. They remain empty and silent.

#### To discard the external is not a physically or religiously motivated withdrawal from the world, and certainly not just a mystifying mental scheme. Insight into the pre-worldly causes the worldly and impermanent to collapse and disperse. Only that which you are not can break away and dissolve into nothing. The overlay is discarded − that which you imagine yourself to be outwardly, which with great vigor has swept you along through time and space from one life to another life and from one death to another death.

#### Unfortunately, you are fascinated and literally hypnotized by the colorful dynamic processes within the transient world of mortality. You mix yourself up with destructive, nebulous movements in consciousness generated by external influences and reflexes. Therefore, awaken now! Then, the untrue and the unreal inside you can die, and a mix-up no longer takes place. Indeed, you should die before your physical death, so you can clearly recognize what actually dies.

#### Everything your knowledge may incorporate has been uploaded and activated through volition and concepts. This knowledge builds the foundation upon which you have constructed your outer life. This fortress knows no reason to let itself be banished or dissolved. Such a rejection brings no tangible profit for one’s personal life, and consequently is of no use in externally focused daily life.

#### The dissolution of delusion is not a calculation of cost versus utility, not an exchange through which one gives something unimportant away and receives something important in return. This dissolution happens through insight and intuitive logic. It is an experience without one who experiences, because precisely this one who experiences is, through insight, seen in proper relation and then dissolved. It is an awareness within which neither an ‘I’ nor a ‘you’ nor any gap of thought have ever existed.

#### Eternal silence, immeasurable splendor, heavenly radiance, luminous essence – these are words used by humans in an effort to describe and express something which in reality never allows itself to be decorated or

#### labelled with words. The intellect doesn’t like this not-knowing at all, for its mission is to

#### know and understand. It feels threatened, annoyed, and frightened, because it senses that this ‘other’, this unfathomable Mystery could mean the end of its dominance and authority. It therefore does everything it can to prevent it. The intellect seems to have importance in everyday life. This is the platform where it claims its domain. Here, it is king. When a human being awakens, the one who knows and all things known are brought into clear perspective, as well as the external platform upon which the intellect dances.

#### There is a life before death, and there is a life after death. But who is the knower who is convinced that it knows this, and who takes refuge in this belief? In reality, there is not a ‘before’ or an ‘after’; no ‘above’ and no ‘below’. In pre-worldly awareness of that which you really are, you are truly in the world, but no longer of this world.

#### Acquisition, accumulation, and attachment to things of the world harbor the danger that such a behavior, such an attitude leads to you becoming a thing yourself. You become an owner, who is then captured by what you possess. Discover what it is that makes some objects desirable objects, and then observe what effects they have on your psychological state!

#### Don’t get stuck on what is outside. Live in presence and not in semblance! Roam the world and enjoy its beauty. Delight in the glorious divine force that resides in everything! But a warning: never come to a standstill within, not anywhere, cling to nothing, and don’t allow yourself to be dragged along with all that changes! Things change by themselves, so allow them to change and don’t interfere with what changes on its own.

#### Be calm, stay relaxed, and remain unimpressed by all that you perceive and experience through the senses! Do not allow yourself to be distracted and carried away by glittering appearances! Cease to orient in external realms and don’t confuse yourself again! The stratum of all existence never sinks down, and depths of spirit know no heights. So, where are you looking?

#### Don’t shield yourself off in hard-heartedness and mind-states that lack love. Right here is where rock-hard egocentrism is established. With it comes the steep and rapid descent into the world of gray shadows where clear seeing is impossible.

#### Those who lapse into such habits and attitudes open dark chasms within themselves and also within the world they live in. Through their conduct they

#### produce misery and suffering for their fellow men and women, but for themselves as well, for every action causes a reaction.

#### Do not be so engrossed and hypnotized by that which you see and experience as good and bad. All external things condition each other and are dependent upon each another. Be at ease and abide prior to the constantly moving and changing and be aware that you are the eternal inner core and never that which is involved in external processes.

#### Those who awaken have no personal ‘I’ left. They observe their bodies and the world as their not-self. In order to enter the Eternal, one must be free of busyness and bustle. The truly awakened ones do not seek their happiness in their inner freedom and bliss. They have crossed over a threshold and entered into an emptiness where neither warm nor cold exist.

#### What appears at one spontaneous moment to be out of order is not necessarily something that creates disorder. That which arises from the personal ‘I’ creates nothing but disorder within the greater order and produces confusion and animosity.

#### That which has to happen will happen. That which doesn’t have to happen or can’t happen will never happen. This is not a dry fatalistic perspective. Rather, it describes the detached manner of the awakened ones who abides within. Their non-activity penetrates the world and brings contentment, for they live within the greater equilibrium and in accordance with the greater order. Understanding this is worth nothing, but to realize it transforms everything. One who has realized this and lives this way dwells in deep silence. They have left all discrepancies and all the old paths behind them once and for all. They roam the earth without leaving footprints. The states of mind which generate birth and death no longer have any effect on these awakened beings. They are at home and remain steadfast in the pre-worldly realm.

#### Our external life is a gift and not a possession. Life itself clings to nothing in the world. Those who have not realized authentic original being do not know the pure divine energy that inhabits all things. Awakened ones condense millenniums within themselves, coalesce them internally, and fulfill them in transcendence.

#### When you constrict yourself within the influences of the outer world, you lose yourself without being aware of it and distance yourself from your primordial origin. One who has discovered his source in eternal being no

#### longer lingers in the exterior world and is settled in deep stillness. He lives within the Mystery, a hidden life within the world, but one that is no secret. Enter! Discover that, which is truly closer to you than your hands or feet!

#### Thoughts and feelings which appear in consciousness cannot exist isolated or separated from the one indivisible reality. That which manifests is an expression of the one Mystery which encompasses all and is all that is.

#### From the Not-Being of Not-Being-being, the One emerges. One can contemplate the One, but one can’t contemplate Not-Being-being. In Not-Being, neither name nor form exist. And yet, Not-Being is the essence of the vitality that is everything that lives. Vitality is not something that came about at some point in time long, long ago and suddenly showed itself. Vitality is the Mystery within the Here and Now. What is within the Here and Now knows no past and no future. You are now!

#### That which advances beyond the Here and Now and pushes its way into external existence gets involved in concepts and generates life and death. A human being experiences and senses itself within this movement as separate from pure universal being: as an individual ‘I-am’. The shadow cast is nothing but a superimposed illusion, an unstable not-self-entity.

#### Be aware of your body and the world within consciousness, and be aware within awareness, that you can be neither the body nor the world within awareness. You can never be that thing that you are aware of. The subject can never be the object. You can only be aware of what appears to exist outside your true being – what is superimposed upon it.

#### All that is created with thought, the entirety of mental constructs, is not what you are. So, don’t cling to that which temporarily appears in consciousness as the body and the world.

#### Don’t concern yourself with that which builds itself up. Life lives itself best when one doesn’t interfere. Discover all the things that you have created through your thinking. Yet, be aware that you can create anything, just not you yourself. That which you truly are was already there before your thinking was activated. The entire thought-world is the result of ‘I-am’, the ruler and creator of the external world in space and time.

#### To claim that the external world doesn’t exist would be dumb. To claim that the external world truly exists would be even more stupid.

#### It is not about erasing or repressing thoughts through meditation. No, thought is a valuable instrument and has its function and importance within the world

#### from which it was born. Thought itself poses no problem. All that human beings think up is, on the contrary, truly problematic in many respects.

#### One who awakens is impenetrable from the outside and lives within divine silence; he is sealed off from duality and is embedded in eternal being. This realization occurs without the slightest energetic effort. It happens when it happens, dissolves what is to be dissolved, just as morning dew upon the leaves and grass quietly evaporates under the rising sun. Detach yourself naturally from illusions and the temporal not-self. That which you seek is what you already are.

### Searching for the ‘I’

#### What exactly is awakening and how does the awakened one live in the world? Indeed, his presence is modest and unobtrusive. Although he has left the world behind, he participates in society. He lives among people, but no one knows him. While most people are restlessly pushed around by their life-stories, he remains still and silent. One who is established in the divine Mystery lives in the world without being tethered to the transient. One who lives in this way never harms another and becomes a source of inspiration. The deeds of the awakened one leave no traces. He does not become a someone in the world. He remains free from longings and affects the world through his non-activity.

#### One who awakens stops seeking happiness in the external world. That which seeks and hopes is dissolved within him. The great turmoil is over. Nothing more rushes out; nothing more returns. Also, the pressure to be something or someone is dissolved. The immeasurable power which resides within the one who has become silent has its effects without his doing. All things which have lost their balance are restored to their proper order.

#### Those who believe themselves to be important and display themselves as such come to harm other living beings and sow the seeds of heartlessness and misunderstanding in the world. Those who behave this way have lost sight of the true source. They descend into self-seeking and cover the space around them with a gray shroud which darkens bright pathways. The consequences of such behavior should not be underestimated, for each effect has its rebound-effect. One who moves blindly in the outer world becomes inwardly blind.

#### Humility and discretion are testimony to a matured soul, one that abides in Not-I. Not-I does not originate in the external ‘I-am’. This external ‘I-am’ does not at some point revert into Not-I, just as a shadow can never revert back to sunlight.

#### When the intellect analyzes itself, it finds nothing. The intellect itself is only a system of thought-processes, an instrument of consciousness which

#### functions within the physical body. Pre-worldly awareness dispatches with the relative and opens the door to

#### divine Not-Being. Something unreal dissolves. The real is and was eternally here and now. Only something unreal can dissolve. One should be constantly mindful of this. Divine being will never strive to explain itself. The not-self, on the other hand, tries to do so.

#### Raise yourself above these dualistic modes of seeing. Stay wide awake and see through that which you are not! Don’t cling to relative concepts and ideas! Don’t cling to appearances in consciousness and don’t cling to what comes and goes! The world will indeed come to an end; at the latest when you die. This ending will never ever touch that which you really are.

#### A path exists because one recognizes it and follows it. Things exist because one perceives them through the senses, names them, and classifies them rationally. All paths and all things attain their individual existence this way. Everything that comes into existence in this way cannot exist in and of itself. It is dependent on something else. Paths are dependent on being perceived and followed as paths, Things are dependent on being recognized and acknowledged with names and verified for their utility.

#### Who is this someone who is so completely convinced of living coexistent with those things one has recognized and named? This someone, whoever it is, cannot live autonomously and exist on its own. Its existence is dependent on consciousness, from which it initially arises. Without consciousness, there would not be this someone.

#### Consciousness, in turn, is dependent on this someone. Consciousness is only conscious of itself as consciousness through becoming conscious of specific things and personal paths. Thus, consciousness cannot exist autonomously. It is not only dependent on things and paths, but also on the existence of pure spirit, which makes the existence of consciousness possible.

#### Spirit is the essence, the source of all vitality, the Great Mystery. Spirit is Not-Being; spirit is true being. External being exists only as a phenomenon of spirit within consciousness. Spirit is the divine donor of life, the unfathomable eternal Mystery.

#### The awakened one lives where the existence of things has not yet begun, where separation has not yet entered, where there is neither affection nor aversion, neither affirmation nor negation. When limited external existence fades, then Not-Being, that which cannot be explained with words, reveals

#### itself. One who awakens cultivates no more activity originating in the ‘I’. Rather, one abides in deep silence and acts through non-action. Life flows best when external ‘I’-centeredness is dispelled and can no longer generate hindrances and misunderstandings.

#### If there is a beginning to the world, there is also something prior to this beginning: namely, Not-Being. Whether Not-Being is the final and absolute reality is not something one can know, for everything that one can know is relative and limited within space and time. Transcendence distances itself from all human knowledge. Ultimate emptiness is and remains impenetrable and inconceivable.

#### Don’t lose yourself in words. Your true divine being is eternally free from explanations and prior to the thoughts discharged by consciousness to establish themselves in the external world. Physical and psychological limitations are determined through natural laws within time and space. The origin of these natural laws which guide and shape human existence and the entire universe is unknown to us. Also, the foundation upon which our subjective perception of the world forms itself and which constantly influences it is unknown. The spatial dimensions within which we live are determined to be true, real, and verifiable. However, where something verifiable exists, something must exist that doesn’t allow itself to be verified.

#### We live in a multi-dimensional and multi-sensorial world where many things are impossible to understand or explain because, to a great extent, they exceed the confines of intellectual understanding. Whether limitless reality can be found in the differences between things, where subject-object relationships exist, is questionable. If there is something like an absolute reality, one can be sure that it exists beyond relational perceptions or definitions, beyond words, thoughts, and concepts, and thus, beyond the analytical mind and the objects that it analyzes. Those who try to verify the truth of Not-Being in thoughts and words will quickly lose the courage and strength to gain clear sight of their own words and thoughts and transcend them. They remain trapped in the external, stuck in the old game of subjects and objects.

#### All thoughts and feelings about the body and the world are incorporated within the narrow confines of time-space boundaries. Those who would like to breathe where such borders do not exist go with the flow beyond all limitations and into limitless heavenly light. They offer up their external ‘I’ to

#### the invisible divine altar. They stride upon the broadest frontiers of the conceivable and the imaginable and surrender their entire being to the unfathomable, to what cannot be studied. Here, they find liberation, meaning, and their true vocation.

#### In awakened ones, there is no need or longing to explain or prove their realization to anyone in any way. They live in expansive equilibrium. They have unified heaven and earth within themselves and have ceased to be something or someone.

#### In the wake of this non-dual realization, can one even speak of existence, when nothing and no one exists anymore? Does one actually cease to exist, or does one continue to exist eternally? Such questions arise in consciousness before the last boundary is transcended. Here lie the last uncertainties, the final fears. Such questions arise just before one has said farewell to the world and has been swallowed up by the shapeless and formless. This no-longer-existing within universal existence is pure grace, magnificent splendor. Welcome home! Welcome into the light of all lights!

#### You cannot know what you don’t know. Be aware that the entirety of your gathered knowledge which you have acquired in relation to the things of the world are useless regarding ultimate, non-dual realization. There is no connecting branch, no path which leads from the ‘I’ to Not-I.

#### Life and death change; the ultimate realization of Not-I never does. This realization is not a realization of something specific. Therefore, one cannot identify it, want it, know it, or possess it. When ‘I-am’ disappears, the concept of an external, manifested, relative world and the superimposed idea of ‘I-am’ also disappear.

#### When a person is awake, he can contemplate the meaning of his dreams. When he dreams, he cannot contemplate the meaning of his waking hours. Spiritual awakening is an awareness wherein the dreamworld and the waking world are recognized as superimposed activities and processes within consciousness. That which this world manifests also generates a mysterious darkness arising from hidden and unfathomable depths. Hidden, secretive, and incredible powers arise from deep within the original elements. These manifestations are the evidence.

#### Life emerges. Life returns. Mysterious darkness reveals itself. The darkness retreats and hides itself, and yet, everything is eternally revealed. Nothing changes within the divine source of eternal being, the source which

#### never springs up, but is nevertheless the all-encompassing, all-determining divine force within all manifested life. Do not be afraid of death. Your body and your intellect are only visitors in this life and exist merely as reflections of the eternal divine spirit, which is your true being. Don’t turn back to the outer world. Enjoy the beauty and the silence of eternal Not-Being!

#### One who awakens and has gone beyond all internal boundaries and limitations has left behind the dust, turbulence, and suffering in the outer world forever. One who awakens never goes anywhere. That one is eternally here and now, not residing anywhere and yet at home everywhere. He no longer attracts the things of the world and no longer pushes those things of the world aside. That one abides where all is nothing and nothing is everything.

#### The awakened one knows Not-I and knows the Great Silence, although there is nothing more to know. The awakened one is aware of the divine, omnipresent Mystery and is that of which he or she is aware.

#### It is brightest where sun, moon, and stars do not shine, for they only testify to an ephemeral light.

#### Don’t try to find out what you are. Discover that which you are not and don’t try to conquer anyone but yourself! Conquer your delusions and overcome all heartlessness! Discover and do not expect any returns or prizes from what you have discovered. Do not think that when you have conquered and overcome a small thing that you receive a large reward for it. Leave such immature thoughts behind and see just how corrupt ‘I-am’ truly is. Through the gradual dissolution of the ‘I-am’-concept, a fresh elasticity and integrating quality unfold within oneself, allowing the old illusions to fade away and the Eternal to reveal itself.

#### This transformation happens in stillness, in silence. It is hardly noticeable, and yet is incredibly powerful and infinitely beautiful! What actually spiritualizes our embodied being is not known. But one knows and experiences that spiritualization is happening, and in a profound and all-embracing manner. When we devote ourselves naturally and without guile to that which wants to spiritualize us, the great metamorphosis completes itself on its own. The entire being of a person is illuminated and becomes sacred. This inconceivable, indescribable, divine spiritualization is accomplished gently, soundlessly, and with infinite love.

### See Through the Intellect!

#### Your true being exists prior to any consciousness of things. True being is prior to the intellect and its characteristic perception of content; of things and objects in the external world. The concept ‘I-am’, which was kindled in consciousness, has ignited a wildfire that has spread rapidly in all directions. Egocentric behavioral patterns created a kind of anti-world, where ‘I-am’ devised its kingdom with pre-determined boundaries. Only the bright light of reason is able to see through and transcend forms and objects that appear in consciousness.

#### Through birth, you have received a clearly constructed form, which you will keep until your physical death. You live in this form created by your parents without knowing exactly why.

#### Because you sense yourself as this physical result, you form the thought of ‘I-am’, the concept of ‘I-am’. In this way you nail the soul to the external form and submerge yourself in the world of death and impermanence. Using the word soul is not an indication of a religious something that lives on after death. Much more than a phantom, soul is a reference to the psyche of human beings, to their psychic state.

#### Many things stated here may sound abstract, such as the contradiction between form on one side and formlessness of being on the other. Or, for example, how Not-Being is mistakenly identified as a body-product which appears in the external world as not-self. Not-Being is not no-being. The physical entity exists at the periphery of consciousness like a bubble on the surface of the ocean. The bubble bursts, while the ocean remains the same, untouched by this occurrence.

#### Everything that appears has its beginning and its ending; everything except Not-Being, the home of the awakened one. The awakened one never sees a beginning and never an end, for he is established in the pre-worldly. One who awakens is wide awake and never loses himself in the external. A spirit that orients inwardly is constantly in disidentification with that which is external and perceptible. Dis-identification has nothing to do with turning away from

#### physical objects, and also does not suggest a deliberately reclusive lifestyle. What falls away is that which you never were. That is what dissolves in disidentification and disappears forever. Consequently, there is neither loss nor gain for ‘I’-consciousness in disidentification, and certainly no spiritual cost-benefit calculation.

#### Externalities remain externalities as long as the mind is not resolutely oriented inward. One must also be deeply conscious of the meaning of this reversal. Awareness has nothing to do with observation of the outer world. Rather, it is a seeing where nothing internal or external has ever existed.

#### Devastating surprises from one’s own past shake a person into waking up. It happens if and when heavenly light is able to penetrate the tightly structured life-form. The divine flows through it, allowing a fresh, bright seeing to arise, opening boundless horizons. From this inner seeing, a profound capacity of discernment arises. The intellect pulls back and makes way for intuitive reason.

#### Escape into externalities, into the abysses of mortality and ignorance, is a true tragedy. This blinding energy only loses its velocity and power when a human awakens from its deadening slumber. The ever-changing present oppresses human beings throughout their lives and dangles a future in front of them that, objectively seen, doesn’t even exist.

#### The worldly perspective of the ‘I’ appearing in time and space is tied to the external in the external and trapped in this series of knots, just as a fish gets trapped in a fisherman’s net. The detached one has untied the ties and seen through the superimposed, superficial being and transcended it. He abides in the Great Silence, beyond the waking world and the dreamworld.

#### You were never something superimposed or superficial, just as a shadow was never the sun. One who wanders around in the fog forgets that he comes from the light.

#### As long as you see the unreal as real and remain trapped in this misperception, you suffer. The not-self is temporal and changes constantly and is subject to cheerful and despondent moods. So, look deeper and discover what never changes within you, what abides in great equilibrium!

#### Refrain from using unnecessary words and stop following your fleeting thoughts which compel you away from the light and into darkness! Pay attention to where you send your thoughts, for the seeds of thought produce chains which tie the soul to space and time.

#### An awakening from an old dream that the dreamer once dreamed opens

#### the gate to divine light. In examining the depths of one’s life, a human being discovers something truly astounding: this examination ends in vast emptiness. At the beginning there was something; in the end there is nothing at all!

#### The intellect would like to be active and think about the nature of consciousness. It would like to know and understand. But what should be clear to us in the meantime is that there must be something upon which the intellect can contemplate – an object, a body, the world - so that it can analyze it. It cannot think about nothing. It therefore must see and accept everything that it can imagine, invent, and organize to be true and real.

#### According to one’s viewpoint and manner of observation, external things really exist or are only the field of appearances within consciousness and have no autonomous existence of their own.

#### What is certain is that an externally oriented manner of observing coupled with human feelings are always subject to error because feelings are constantly oriented on the people around us and adapting to external surroundings. Because external life is constantly subject to change, one’s feelings change as well. There must be something existing in the external one can feel. Whatever it is that feels, it must always be fundamentally different than what one oneself is. Strangely, one needs something other than oneself in order to feel, and is nevertheless convinced that one can depend on personal feelings – which, of course, is not accurate, though one doesn’t want this to be true.

#### Feelings can elevate human beings into ecstatic joy. Moments later, those same people can crumple into deep sorrow and suffering. True happiness can only be found in the absence of ‘I-am’; where the tumult of the outer world never reaches. Only in Not-Being and Not-Acting is it possible to know contentment without cause. When the ancient seeds are burned away and nothing more is planted, a human being can really be free. Then, the great melting of heaven and earth takes place, and the ancient fire is forever extinguished.

### In the Face of Life

#### What you see and think is always influenced and governed by your memories. Each person sees their own world, lives in their own world, and is also always alone in their seeing, thinking, and feeling.

#### What you conceptualize is assembled with other thoughts and superimposed over your true preworldly nature. It is like a crust that gets thicker and thicker and becomes a shadow that darkens your true original being. The world is neither good nor bad. It is exactly how you think internally and how you act and function externally.

#### In pre-worldly presence, no dichotomy exists. Eternal being knows no conceptually created outer world. The ever-present singular force in the center allows images and forms to appear in consciousness and have their various shapes and characteristics, despite their relative unreal existence.

#### Whoever you believe or imagine yourself to be, this thought-edifice is nothing but a blinding, externally focused image in consciousness. Why are you so driven to always be something or someone? Are you afraid of Not-Being? Whether you want to or not and whether you are aware of it or not, true being is formless, shapeless, and beyond all thoughts and earthly feelings. Yes, you want everything, just not to be nothing. But you were never nothing, just not that which you imagine and believe yourself to be. What you believe yourself to be was invented by the thinking mind. This invented image covers and darkens your true everlasting being.

#### Paths of knowledge have brought good to the world we live in, but they have also wrought malice and destruction there. You can know a great deal, just not that which you really are. The entirety of stored knowledge is primarily food for the intellect, which processes countless thoughts and stores them in the brain. When you try to analyze the intellect, you will find nothing. Why is there nothing there? Because the intellect is simply a functional aspect of ‘I’- consciousness.

#### All information which is processed by the intellect leaves traces in one’s memory. And precisely these impressions become energies which steer and

#### guide an individual’s life in how the world is viewed and what has meaning. One who wanders on the outer edges of consciousness among the shadows of old, egocentric powers has forgotten that one’s origin is the light.

#### Although the intellect exists merely as a passing phenomenon, it senses itself as ‘I-am’. I am this body. I am this person. Such thoughts are only thoughtconstructs, imaginings, which came into being with identification with the body. What happens is an exodus from the light into shadow, from abundance to deficiency. For the one thinking, the world around him seems to truly exist. Indeed, the body is visible and can be contemplated and experienced within the realm it inhabits. Clearly, the body is the focal point and the hub upon which the entire outer world moves and unfolds in an evolutionary manner.

#### ‘I-am’ is the creation of the operating intellect, the atelier of the ego. And what is produced by it will die. The intellect acts like a raging fire within consciousness, which grows bigger through the continuous perception of external objects.

#### The entire world is like the single child of the one ever-present consciousness which is reflected as form and shape and is consciously perceived through consciousness. Seen this way, everything that takes on a form and a shape is ever-present, all-encompassing consciousness.

#### Visible and physical landscapes are appearances of a higher intelligence within consciousness. Consciousness is in fact only a means to a purpose and not the last reality. Living being are born within consciousness and living beings die within consciousness. Pure and eternal being is formless, unborn, non-becoming, and eternally unaffected by all external fleeting occurrences.

#### One who overcomes the foundational forces that generate birth and death flows back into the luminous glow of limitless emptiness, into the essence of the Great Mystery. It requires a bright and wakeful inner eye to truly objectify and see through the entire superimposed field of appearances within consciousness. The intellect is not adept in this intuitive awareness, because it is the architect of this overlay, this externally oriented, ephemeral, and superficial realm of life.

#### One who is silently steadfast in Not-Acting observes the effectivity of this disintegrating power wherein internal and external life-realms are diffused. One who is steadfast in Not-Doing becomes a master of non-activity. A master of non-activity is, however, not a lazy one. The opposite is true.

### Inattention

#### The operating efficiency of the world excites and fascinates human beings. However, the tendency of always wanting more of everything and permanent grasping on to the many temporal things of the world compel the psyche into the custody of death. The burden of the transient world puts pressure upon the soul, which is why the soul is always searching for escape routes. It seeks inner freedom and possibilities where the impermanent doesn’t dare to go. It searches there, where neither cool moonlight nor hot sunlight have ever infiltrated.

#### Only when the old mask falls away will heavenly light permeate your being. This will transform your body and your entire being and fundamentally create you anew. Only then will you discover the enduring power which has never touched the world, the force which gives your life meaning and shows you your true calling. And yet, there is not someone or something which calls you personally with the intention of making you someone important. No, the emptier you are, the more useful you are. What is true is never a blinding light.

#### Sooner or later, the old house that has covered over what is true will collapse. Leave it now, before you suffer harm or severe injury. Don’t be careless!

#### Return to yourself, to the pre-worldly origin. This is the shortest way into the eternal depths of life. Be aware that the unreal never has power over you. When you awaken, you will no longer be carried away by appearances. What worries do you still have when you are no longer preoccupied with life and death or winning and losing?

#### One who doesn’t act doesn’t produce anything. One who doesn’t produce anything doesn’t possess. Pure heavenly light has never identified itself with the body or the world and has never mingled with transitory things. This has happened because you have mistaken the temporal and finite with the Eternal. All your attention is focused outwardly. The supporting celestial powers never rumble around in the external. They act through non-activity and supply abundance free of desire.

#### Whatever you think, see, speak out, and perform in order to function in the outer world, it all takes place within your body, although you may not be at all aware of it.

#### Eternal being is never what manifests, though without eternal being there would be no world of manifestations within consciousness. Penetrate right here and transcend the manifesting! Be aware that you are the one objectifying these manifestations and never the objectified object! You can never be that which you objectify.

#### Deviations prevent clear-sightedness and create the opportunity to become mired down again in confusion and ignorance. Your timeless presence here and now should not move hastily in the external world, causing it to distance itself from the Great Silence.

#### Be what you eternally are! Quietly observe the processes taking place externally, and don’t allow yourself to be carried away by these happenings. There is no reason to rush out of the light and back into shadows.

#### Never think that it is difficult or even impossible to realize the Eternal. That would be pointless. What is definitely required is a deep internal about-face which allows opening and insight into the depths of your being.

#### Some still bathe in the shadows of death and have resigned themselves to live out their lives immersed in the physical and psychic suffering of the world. This reversal of attention is hardly something they would like to concern themselves with.

#### Don’t allow surging currents of thoughts and feelings to pull you outward and into the world of life and death. Don’t allow yourself to be seduced by strange ideas or passions. The external universe is how it is, and you are how you are – deathless, formless, and deeply embedded in divine light.

#### The compulsion towards wakeful consciousness which eventually sways back and becomes a journey into sleep and the obscure unconscious demonstrates how the physical, psychical world of the wakeful state arises in time and space and is then fluidly dispersed again in sleep, dissolved into nothing. From form to the formless, from the named to the nameless – this rhythm is not determined by a human being, but by a superior cosmic intelligence.

#### Sri Ramakrishna, a well-known sage from India, described what is referred to above in a wonderful graphic example: If one would take salt out of the sea and form a statue from it, two different things appear to exist: first the sea, and then the statue made of sea salt. If the statue would then be

#### placed back in the sea, it would dissolve immediately and turn into that which it always was: salt water. The ocean never took notice of salt being removed from it and had no knowledge of the statue. The ocean also never noticed or felt anything as the statue was set in water again where it dissolved back into its original essence.

#### This is how it is with the human being and the eternal shoreless ocean of light. In the example of the sea and the statue made of salt, one can understand that without the ocean there would be no statue made of salt. Without divine essence there is no conscious life on earth. Something must be there that something else can exist and appear. This something is the unfathomable Mystery.

#### Outside of the thinking, analyzing intellect, no independent individual exists. The intellect confirms what is perceived through the senses. It names and defines forms and things in the world and at the same time examines their usefulness.

#### The conceptual ideas of the world exist barren of magnificent silence and separated from the divine Mystery. Yet, this Mystery is not something hidden away or impossible to find. No, it is your true, real, eternal being. The infinite essence of eternal being is not something specific that moves through time. Not-Being knows neither corners nor edges and is not a place where one could go.

#### The problem is that you are excited and captivated by all that comes your way as things that are tangible and palpable, and you don’t notice that you are lost and a captive of your captivation. You see and experience yourself as something indeed. At the same time, you are always on the lookout of something else, something that supplements and complements. This fascination produces desires and aspirations and sets countless internal fires alight. The tensions between wanting and not-wanting, between winning and losing, between mine and yours, between man and woman, these tensions cruelly cut you off from your original eternal being. And still, these aggressive tensions which stir up so much turbulence in your life are, when examined deeply, not what you are. Due to this constant pathological striving for things which one would like to own and possess, one deflects the divine powers away without really ever being aware of it. This egocentric behavior leads to the establishment of shadow worlds and murky mindstates. One lives outside of oneself, thereby forgetting that one comes from the light.

#### Your attitude towards your presence in the world is not fundamentally bad. But inexhaustible desires for external objects have made your perception murky and dull.

#### If one forces things in life, one comes into harm’s way and loses the ability to relax. Without relaxation, one becomes ill.

#### What your eyes perceive is filled with joy and suffering and will take you on a ride through highs and lows. Orient yourself rather on the unifying force and remain in the middle. Don’t let yourself be driven into confusion by compulsions based on selfpreservation. Trust and allow the heavenly powers to guide your being. Be aware that that which you eternally are is eternally present, and all you perceive and experience in the external world with your eyes is your not-self. That which you really are is never what you believe you are.

#### Nothing can appear separated from the great Mystery, from the source of any and all possible thoughts and experiences. The ‘I’ arises out of Totality along with all consciously perceived processes of movement within the world. But within the absolute One there is no second one. You yourself are then not so much actually. You are the one who imagines possessing many things and experiencing many things and knowing many things in the external world of appearances. In truth, you are absolute, indivisible, imperishable Being, and not the mental schemes which appear in consciousness and then disappear again. Everything that you perceive and experience through your body and the senses beyond yourself is nothing but the fleeting dream of a dreamer.

#### All memories arise with the ‘I’. They are what the ‘I’ experienced and what molded it. The ‘I’ was the one who spun the web of all memories and organized the labyrinth of connections in the outer world. This tightly knit network composed of countless images, memories, and energies has stretched itself out like a thick gray shroud over Totality. This dim shadowprojection is the substance of ‘I-am’.

#### And yet, without light there could be no shadow. Light is forever. Shadows are merely passing phenomena which impart the absence of light. Internal light is the essence of the Great Mystery. One who awakens within it is anchored in this pre-worldly essence and doesn’t run away from things of the world nor move towards them. The heart of the awakened one is silent.

#### Be aware of what significance and importance you appropriate to the

#### body and the superimposed world and to everything you conceive and imagine yourself to be. In the end, it is all nothing more than trivial mental game staged by the ego. Thoughts generate knowledge, and in all knowledge, there is nothing enduring. It changes and is constantly shifting around, and what changes with it are the many conceptual images you have of yourself. In Not-Being there is nothing to know, nothing diverging, and no identifying. This is no descent into madness or stupidity, but rather the ascent towards wisdom. You can know everything except what you eternally are.

#### Dare to realize Not-Being and Not-Acting. All things will then integrate and return to the greater order. And don’t forget: Not-Doing does not mean being nothing, and Not-Doing does not mean nothing is done!

#### Knowledge is certainly nothing bad. In order to live and function in the world, one must know some things, of course. But accumulated knowledge has not provided the world with peace or calm or equanimity or true contentment. Knowledge is the source of myriad misunderstandings and heartless concepts and the mechanism that has set countless manipulative and corrupt actions in motion. Knowledge can be an extremely dangerous weapon.

#### The fact is that your knowledge guides your life. It defines it and shapes it. The entire organism of society is based on what you know and what you believe. Whatever you know or believe yourself to be, the known is never what you are. The known is always something added, something that represents your not-self. For the realization of unborn divine reality, external, relative knowledge is useless.

#### The awakened one has left his earthly nature behind him. He has said farewell to the forces of fate. He has died before he was dead. He is indeed in the world, but he is no longer of this world. Freed from knowing himself as an individual, he shines with universal clarity and wisdom within the radiance of the divine Mystery.

#### Those who extend themselves too far in the external forget where they came from and are trapped in the webbings of fate. Come back and abide within as quickly as possible, for everything you find that is external and separate from you is never that which you really are. Abide in yourself, abide in Not-Being and Not-Acting! Ultimately, everything that you possess possesses you, and what possesses you holds you prisoner. See how knowledge functions and

#### transcend the known. This is not the dulling of your mind. Rather, this is open, pure, intuitive logic − clear seeing. This clarity sees through and penetrates all appearances at their root and at their seed and peers down to their deepest depths.

#### This is a pure, burning, loving, silent seeing that transforms and brings to fulfillment all that is seen. There is no one there that acts or wants something, but still, the bent becomes straight and the disorderly comes into order. Nothing has a more powerful impact or is more all-embracing than silent Not-Acting.

#### As long as you inhabit the body, earthly knowledge will be there. When you awaken, you will leave knowledge to knowledge and leave the world and the things in it just as they are. Intuition has nothing to do with knowledge. It penetrates the unreal within reality without moving into the external. This way of being is referred to as awakening.

#### Pull back so that reality can reveal itself. Live in the world and be still within your being. Don’t allow yourself to get caught up in unnecessary willful activities. The internal goal is not a place one could create. The aim is contentment without any cause, free from the inner world and free from the outer world.

#### Life lives itself best when no interference in external matters takes place and no interference in internal matters happens either.

#### Whatever you think and hope for and expect for your own life, it will always take place in space and time and will be bound and limited to the thinking intellect. Don’t waste your time trying to align yourself with the things of the world. It doesn’t work.

#### Go beyond the illusory limitations within yourself, the limitations which appeared to exist as real and true for you until now. That is what built the foundation of your external being. Never think that this transcendence is difficult or impossible. Such thoughts have actually strengthened your sense of limitation and weakened you.

#### Through neglect and ambiguity, you have formed a perspective that is exclusively oriented on the external; focused on what is impermanent. All your concentration is focused on what is not your true nature. This is a tragedy. Why do let your heart become unsettled and then use your intellect to try to possess and govern the world. Draw your energy back within the boundless and nameless and don’t allow your thoughts to wander in all

#### directions and remain superficial! Leave the place where life and death feel at home! Be in the world, but not of the world!

#### Within unity, two separate things never exist. Oneness never becomes duality. Unity was never good or bad, never beautiful or ugly, never strong or weak, never masculine or feminine, and never today or tomorrow.

#### Don’t busy yourself with things that appear and disappear on the outside. Don’t busy yourself with anything. Be active through Non-Action. Things come and go without you, whether you want them to or not. Life lives itself, without restricted ‘I’-consciousness.

#### Don’t spread yourself around continually in exterior realms. Everything that can spread out is merely the not-self. Be aware of activity in Non-Action and don’t orient yourself again in the external. Enjoy the abundance! Don’t become divided within yourself by deviating and orienting outwardly. Your true home is not a place where thoughts are able to penetrate and also not a place from which thoughts come into existence.

#### Elevate yourself. Let all which you have seen through and recognized as unreal within you melt away. Don’t cling to what is not real. Observe your true presence without any unnecessary thoughts or actions. Be light, detached, and unworried and don’t get carried away in what seems to influence you externally. Otherwise, you distance yourself from yourself and forget your true origin. Don’t believe in things that you are not. Don’t sit there dreaming with open eyes. The external world has nothing but suffering, attachment, and death to offer you. Don’t get lost in appearances moving in consciousness. Don’t lose yourself in not-self: in that which you never were.

#### The innermost is unfathomable. The external is an expression of the Unfathomable. You do live in a body, but don’t reside within it. Dwell in pre-worldly eternal being!

#### Those who make plans plan their lives and get lost in hopes and desires. What you really are never has a plan. You are not a plan. Don’t use the world. Rather, flow with it in eternal being. Go with the flow that doesn’t flow anywhere.

#### In the span of one life, your body goes through uncountable shifts. There is never a moment when it is not changing, aging. This is the progression determined by Nature within the limitations of space and time, of birth and death. Age takes the hustle and bustle out of life and provides some tranquility. Death is actually a space for silence and comfort, as deep and

#### fathomless as the great oceans. Do not fear death. Rather, discover the unchanging within yourself before you are dead. Death and destiny should not unsettle you. They simply follow the laws of impermanent Nature. Open your inner eye and see deeply into that which has never touched the world!

#### One who prevails is humble, relaxed, friendly, respectful, and modest. One who awakens loves life, loves the one highest heavenly power which embraces everything and is everything. One who awakens lives in the land of silence, in Not-Being. One who awakens loves nothing in particular. The awakened one is the source of divine love.

#### The Supreme transcends all form and yet all things formed and impermanent emerge from this supreme. Confidence in the Supreme is also confidence in the formed. Nothing exists separate from this one Supreme.

#### That which you eternally are never recedes, goes somewhere else, or gets lost. Be aware of that!

#### All human bodies, all animal bodies, and all plant bodies exist undivided within the Supreme and are expressions of the Supreme. The Mystery is pure spirit, therefore, everything which appears is spiritualized Nature. One who flows back from form to the unformed and unborn while still living in a physical form will be permeated with divine light, with divine energy, with divine love, and will be further spiritualized by pure spirit.

#### Spirit does not have a particular form and never mingles with the formed, the born, and the becoming, even though it is the essence of everything and in everything. Spirit was there before heaven and earth appeared. It is older than the oldest and younger than the youngest. It is that which you eternally are.

#### One who accomplishes has an empty heart. The accomplished one has brought the work of the heavens to completion and ultimately moved beyond the outer universe and left it behind.

#### When a person is truly open, all dichotomies dissolve in this openness. Without words and without motion: this is the inner domain of the awakened one. He speaks as a service to others, but his presence is absolutely silent. His last words were lovingly removed from him by the divine source when he crossed over the final boundary. The dream of a past, a present, and a future was dreamed away and abandoned. And when he permanently overcame the burden of heavy earthly energies, he entered the unseen realm where time does not exist.

#### The awakened one is aware of the unreality of the multi-sensorial variety of appearances in consciousness. This variety arises from wholeness, just as

#### waves appear on the ocean. Therefore, variety can never exist as autonomous and separate from the whole. The awakened one observes this activity but doesn’t flow with the outer current and doesn’t allow himself to be divided up. He doesn’t move anywhere, for he has united all celestial cycles within himself.

#### Just as waves result from movements of the elements and the ocean, the world and the body are mere phenomena: moods of Nature. When the concept of ‘I am this… I am that,’ is completely dissolved, then wholeness is realized.

#### Whatever you want to be or are striving to become, all related thoughts and activities occur somewhere foreign to your eternal presence and there they adeptly adhere themselves to your illusory not-self. This means, you create your own world of desires with your thought-energies. This is where you live, and you are thoroughly convinced of its reality.

#### This external thought-construct which you call your life and perceive as such is actually not what you are and never was what you were. But you are not yet deeply and thoroughly conscious of this. Knowledge of an object or subject is quite distinct from the knower, and the knower is distinctly different from Totality. In Totality, there is neither a knower nor something that could come to be known.

#### Birth, life, injury, fatal disease, and death compose one external entity. Totality encompasses and enables all things without ever being involved with those things. One who integrates completely in the course of things becomes silent and free of the belief of being an autonomous individual or of existing in a particular place. The individual and the world change constantly. That which you eternally are, never changes at all.

#### The one who awakens lives in great equilibrium and experiences deep peace. The awakened one doesn’t add anything: neither to life nor to death. Free and untouched by the regulating forces of polarities, he lives in unison with everything that is.

### Formless, Nameless, and Happy

#### Where there is love for things created and formed, fear and suffering lurk as well. The impermanence of things creates anxiety for ‘I’-consciousness, as does the enduring and the unknown. The impermanence of the body and thoughts of its inevitable demise at death looks like a blackbox to the intellect and is incredibly disturbing. Is death of the body only one particular aspect of life which is destroyed at the end of physical existence? Do the remains of this debris assemble again later through some magnetic force to create new forms? Possibly. Nothing gets lost in the universe, and all things are constantly in transition and transformation. Life is lived best when one doesn’t impact or dilute it through one’s own concepts about it.

#### The mature soul is distinguished by its serenity and composure. It’s functioning expands throughout the entire world. The awakened one pulls back and lives in the silence of Not-Being. He doesn’t confront the world and doesn’t flee from it either. He lives within eternal being without wishes and plans. Inexhaustible energy, boundless expression, simplicity, and humility adorn his being in the world.

#### Everything that life added was swept away and released without being acted upon. The deep insight that all objects and things appearing in consciousness and perceived there were never endowed with real existence swept away all that was added to his life. Pre-worldly power remained. The illusory expressions of it dissolved into nothing like smoke leaving a fire.

#### Be free of desires and be free of the laws which bind you to the body and the world. Be one with the earth and be one with the sun and all the stars in the evening sky. Be one with all living things. But do not attach yourself to anything or to anyone! Be free!

#### Be still and don’t allow your thoughts to drift and get caught in so many places. Don’t stray from eternal being and don’t mistake appearances for reality. There is no reason for you to descend into delusion. Remain at home in the one greater order and don’t attempt to create order beyond your true being in the realm of the impermanent. Otherwise, you will get stuck in

#### concepts and trapped in all kinds of illusions. Everything is in order in the eternal order. Don’t create disorder in the divine order with your confused thoughts and ideas.

#### The awakened one has gone wayward from the push and shove of the external world and its clamor. Those things have all melted away and dissolved into nothing. All appearances which were brought forth in consciousness have transformed and flowed back into Not-Being. So it is with life. So it is with death.

#### Recognize that only that which has become something can then dissolve into nothing. A nothing cannot dissolve back into nothing. Discover your true being in Not-Being.

#### All thoughts are manifestations that have become something. By taking a closer look, one sees that they are nothing but arbitrary, unpredictable forms of the ‘I-am’ concept expressing itself. Look precisely and stop attaching yourself to the spatial, to the transient, to the outer world. Besides the implanted not-self, nothing exists there. Remain in your spiritual home in Not-Being, the limitless, inviolable Here and Now. Stay detached and enjoy divine, all-encompassing, all-penetrating silence. Enjoy that which exists prior to all appearances!

#### Those who awaken no longer add on. They are no longer from this world, but also not from another world - they are eternal being.

#### What you see outwardly is what you perceive inwardly, and what you hear in the outer world is what you hear inside. What you sense and feel externally is sensed within, and also what you smell and taste in the outer world you smell and taste internally. The entire subtle multi-sensorial perception of the external belongs to the psychic world of the body within you. Physical death, which appears externally as a transformational process, will also play out in the invisible internal realm. The entire external physical world is actually nothing but a shadow cast by the primordial light – real and, at the same time, unreal.

#### Everything that manifests develops in a particular direction and condenses itself into a visible outer world that you can experience and which has become your home. Within this world, you cultivate yourself and make your mark on a broad scale. Be aware that the entire internal and external worlds originate in an immeasurable divine force, an all-embracing essence, a pure and immanent intelligence springing from the source of all being. One

#### who silently and luminously abides in this one divine energy which encompasses everything will never be affected by dichotomies, and life and death will not dare come near.

#### Eternal being is free of thought and can exist without the thought-world. The thought-world, on the other hand, cannot manifest without external being. Eternal being is not individual; it is universal. Eternal being appears in the consciousness of human beings as variety, which presents itself in its spiritual expression as the body and the world. In the apparent variety of objects, a human being senses itself as one among many and acts as one among many in the world. A person usually sees the many in one but doesn’t see the One in the many. One who is deeply established within the One pervades heaven and earth without moving at all.

#### Don’t get lost in events and experiences. Recognize and accept that you will not find happiness or satisfaction in their causes nor in their effects. The opposite is true: causes and effects pull you away and out of original being and force you to roam through the streets of death. Therefore, don’t become involved in the external anymore and don’t add another thing. External life is a dynamic movement of the operating intellectual mind, which invents intriguing stories and is entertained by them. If necessary, it is ready to defend these stories vehemently, which are sensed as true and real.

#### Awakening means to see through these stories and the bulging image of an independent individual within the stories. It means to recognize how the individual clasps tightly to the story’s composition. Expose and debunk what you are not! Don’t propel your life towards the external any longer. Don’t try to influence anything and allow life the opportunity to live itself.

#### One can do countless things in the external world. There are endless possibilities to achieve and possess. As long as one is not living from the abundance of Not-Being and Not-Acting, all that one does and all that one owns is empty and meaningless.

#### Possession is what is possessing you. And what possesses you ignites the fire of craving for more, of possessing more… and more. The energy of greed compels human beings into a blind state, into murky abysses of egocentricity. Such crude behavior defies the tenets of reason and true human love, which are expressions of the hidden Mystery, which is available to all human beings at all times.

#### It is not about having no possessions and remaining poor. The point is

#### rather to have an exceptionally intense mindful awareness that doesn’t allow anything in the external world to possess you internally. Nothing, including the physical body, should possess you and hold your psyche in its clenches. What holds you in its clenches externally nails you down internally – onto the bed of nails called the ego. Don’t hold on to anything. Everything which you grasp on to weighs you down and won’t let you go free again. And what weighs you down pulls you away from your natural presence.

#### Those who use the thought-organ merely for the acquisition of external things impede their own lives and wander through their own delusion. When someone opens their mouths in order to delude and manipulate others, to satisfy their own greed and avarice, they have distanced themselves from benevolence, openheartedness, goodness, and spiritual clarity. The laws of abundance and fulfillment are unknown to them.

#### The inner life of such people is darkened, damaged, and contaminated. And what is really terrible is that they are not even the slightest bit aware of it. This behavior reveals what makes life complicated, heavy, and lacking in love, and illuminates how self-seeking can rob human beings of relaxation and a high quality of life. The external is an echo of the internal. One can recognize the inner life of someone by observing how they represent themselves and how they conduct themselves in the external world.

#### All living beings in the world are permeated and nourished by the divine light that sustains the earth. But the mind began to dream and bound itself to what it dreamed of. That is why the thirst for turning back and awakening vibrates in the souls of human beings. Consciousness wants to awaken from the dream-world, recognize itself, and rid itself of the mortal and impermanent.

#### No one is perfect. But here, there is no discussion of perfection, but of awakening – awakening from the self-designed, externally oriented world of illusory appearances and completely discarding the not-self.

#### Admittedly, everyday life is not easy, especially when one makes it unnecessarily difficult. Remain light within yourself, light as a feather and not heavy as lead! Don’t burden your life with unnecessary baggage! One who lives in resonance with the earthly and the heavenly is steadfast in the primordial and no longer sets external things in motion. Turn away from the crude forces that detain and torture the heart. Don’t act like a prisoner of the world, for your true being is eternally free. To turn within enables

#### transformation. Allow this to happen and don’t get in the way!

#### You make yourself busy and are very busy. You want to win and avoid losing. You experience joy and ecstatic feelings, but you also know hurt, doubt, and suffering. In this intoxicating way of life, you forget the unreality of these events and processes which have made their nests in your consciousness and delude you into believing that they really exist. You are utterly fascinated with the unreal and the impermanent, because the unreal compels you to see it as truly existing and you also sense it within your being very deeply.

#### You unconsciously allow yourself to be dominated by your own illusions and you hardly notice. You orient yourself primarily towards your knowledge and the refined intellectual concepts which help you to organize and stabilize your daily life. That which is prior to anything arising in consciousness is not very interesting for most people. Perhaps, as a superficial philosophical game of thought, you might consider it, but your attention is mostly aimed at the effects of your contemplations in the external world, within the formed, the cultivated, the seen, and the experienced. You believe in the ephemeral, analyze the ephemeral, and seek satisfaction, fulfillment, and happiness in the ephemeral – isn’t that rather absurd!

#### You are driven by something you are not, and in your externally focused delirium, you are mistakenly convinced you are that which dies and is temporal. This misunderstanding robs life of its ease and beauty and instills fear and insecurity instead. Life is worth living if one doesn’t get lost and trapped inside it.

#### Don’t try to possess or control the external world: it really doesn’t belong to you. Claims of ownership over unreal temporal things lead to dependence and dull monotony. The soul gets caught up in a dance with foolhardy worldly energies and loses itself in the embrace of life and death.

#### Don’t allow yourself to be possessed by what you imagine you possess. Rise up and let go once and for all of the deluded individual with all its delusions – if not now, then when? Life is a passing dream, but it mustn’t be a nightmare. Be aware that your natural state is Not-Being and that this pure awareness has nothing to do with the external world of appearances where your ‘I’ constantly vibrates. Pure awareness never touches the world. It sees through the entire energetic field of not-self, which has an important effect: it

#### causes a successive melting away of illusions. This is not just a knee-jerk reaction, but rather a refined inner movement, a gradual inner turning back to a timeless silence and joy that has no origin.

#### Universal consciousness is without form and without body, but worlds, forms, and bodies are reflected within it. Just as mountains, trees, and underbrush are reflected at night in a still mountain lake under the full moon, the world is reflected in universal consciousness. The moon is illuminated by the sun, consciousness by spirit. When you awaken, you will cross over this mountain of reflections and never trap yourself in reflected images again. Be aware of their unreality!

#### There, where spirit wants to go, is where you already are – before you began your journey. You look for yourself outside of yourself and hope to find yourself there and experience realization. But that which you can understand is always in relation to something external, to something that you, in the deepest sense, never were. Of course, knowledge is important for everyday life within society. However, for the realization and actualization of Totality, this knowledge is worthless. You can know everything; just not yourself!

#### Everything that you have learned and understood is worthless and meaningless in relation to timeless being. The Timeless cannot be known, nor can it be understood. Where there is nothing to know, there is also nothing to understand.

#### One who awakens lives in accordance and harmony with eternal being, with eternal Not-Being, immersed in a vast and inconceivable celestial silence. The empty appearances of things no longer seduce, distract, or dull the eyes of the awakened ones. They enjoy formless being within form and within the silence prior to all which was ever formed or manifested.

#### Focus sharply and look precisely, and you will become conscious that the majority of your acquired insights are nothing but illusionary concepts, images, and ideas based on the concept you carry around of ‘I-am’. This concept is composed of conditioned thought-constructs originating in regulating moral, religious, and ethical ideas. The influence of these regulating concepts determines the lives of human beings to a large extent, though their influences vary according to the cultural conventions and social structures in which they exist.

#### Those who want to be free of the world should pay attention to the energetic

#### and hypnotic influences of possessing bodies and objects. Most importantly, they should notice how the repercussions and impact of claiming ownership harm and corrupt the psyche of human beings. The external contaminates and corrupts the internal, and the internal contaminates and corrupts the external. For those who remain trapped in the interactions of ‘mine’ and ‘yours’, in having and losing, the transcendence of the world is nothing more than a trite and useless theory. One doesn’t need to reject religion or choose a life of voluntary poverty. Rather, a deep, clear, all-encompassing seeing is called for, which discerns what clinging to material objects and bodies does to the psyche of human beings.

#### One who transcends leaves everything behind without losing anything and is happy without any urge to have. One who lives in this manner no longer lives in the external and doesn’t deviate from this. The eyes and the hearts of the awakened abide in pure light, in the light that earthly eyes can never see.

#### One who lives in harmony with the world and Nature within Totality lives with relaxation, enthusiasm, and joy. He forces nothing. He knows the secret of letting things happen and is a master of doing nothing.

### Beyond Divergences

#### The thought that I am a Mr. John Doe or Ms. Jane Doe and that I live in a particular city on a particular street in a particular country is actually only an image born from the conceptual idea of ‘I-am’. Everything resulting from the thought of ‘I-am’ becomes a frayed, inconsistent life-story where ‘I-am’ encounters itself. Such dull and narrow ideas push a person into an unhappy corner of his or her cosmic being. Lay down this heavy load!

#### You incessantly identify yourself with the body and all objects that share the same space with it. Everything perceptible is considered to be real and to truly exist. The paradox is this: the world exists, and at the same time it does not exist. Master Jesus proclaimed this with the following words: “I am in the world, but not of the world.”

#### Everything that has arisen within you is impermanent and certainly not eternal. Everything that is visible and recognizable was draped onto your ‘I’ from the outside over the inside like a heavy suit. It was superimposed on the surface of you. Everything that has arisen in you was a covering added to you. It will one fall away again as soon as you see through this heavy burden and its origins. Be free of the world, free of all that has arisen, free of all the added ideas and images you are conscious of. Nothing of that which you are conscious of externally is you. You suffer under that which you are not because you have tied yourself to that which you never were.

#### Everything that manifests externally has no independent existence. It is an expression of one universal consciousness, like a footprint in the sand. In truth, external life never truly happens. It is only imagined ideas, concepts, and states which the thought-organ invented and manufactured. This thought-organ tirelessly creates new concepts, images, and ideas, because that is what its dynamic function is. We should really dare to leap beyond thinking if we want to come in contact with and deeply realize the timeless and the deathless. For it is only then that a person inhales the magnificent, limitless depths and discovers true Being.

#### However, the thinker believes its thoughts and believes in the things within time and space that were created and made tangible through thoughts.

#### You live in a world overcrowded with ideas and concepts, which you sense as real and assume to be real. How do you know all that you know, and why are you so convinced that what you figure out and validate is real and true?

#### Neither the body nor body-consciousness are the substance of that which you eternally are. They were added on to you and will leave and fall away from you at a certain point in time. To shed these superimposed illusions is called awakening. This awakening brings about a melting and merging with the Totality of Not-Being. The inner universe extends its breath and penetrates all space. Let us breathe with the universe and not just with the limited physical body!

#### Human beings strive for knowledge; no one strives for Not-Knowing. Who is this knower who wants to know everything, and where does this striving, driving force come from, a force which pushes and carries human beings on a linear track through time and space?

#### Humans have been persuaded into thinking that knowledge is power. They also found out that this is indeed the case. Knowledge is often employed as a treacherous weapon to manipulate other people and inflict harm upon them. How real and true is the mass of information stored in the brain and the acquired mechanisms that go along with it? Does knowing a great deal really provide happiness, and does knowing little lead therefore to unhappiness?

#### Don’t go fishing in murky waters and don’t fish in clear water. Discover pre-worldly being, which you eternally are. Everything which manifests within you fosters suffering and confusion more than anything. The decrees of knowledge create turmoil in the world and rob humans of their life-energy and faculties of relaxation. Confusion flourishes. Better to be wise and not knowledgeable! To feel at ease in Non-Being and Non-Doing and to be content without any cause is foreign to the world and the worldly. Certainly, the increase of knowledge is important and necessary for the wider development of humanity. But clearly, there are dismal sideeffects to the pursuit for knowledge which inhibit empathy and compassion.

#### The escalation of knowledge-power compels human beings into the realms of will and wanting, and not towards transcendence. Restless forces of thinking and knowledge-seeking interfere with the gentle and natural current of life because the brain is constantly active and always scheming up new concepts

#### and ideas. The feeling of being present in the world as someone uniquely individual pleases you. By being a specific something, you can identify yourself and learn how to assert yourself in the world. As long as you identify yourself with the body and the world, the Mystery of the Totality will definitely not be realized. The temporal will not become the Eternal.

#### Only the personalized ‘I’ appears in the world, and not that which you eternally are. Be deeply aware of this! That which you eternally are cannot be seen, figured out, or understood by you – and yet, you exist and you are Here and Now.

#### You could never even begin to contemplate the one absolute unity if it wasn’t the foundation of your being. The problem here is that the moment you think about unity, you separate and remove yourself, and you immediately begin to create images and conceptions. A clear representation is the meaning of this narrative: You are at home and then call several friends with a great doubt in your mind and ask them all to tell you the way to your home. You have gotten lost. Your friends ask you, “Where are you calling from?” You answer, “Well, obviously, I’m at home.”

#### A river only flows in one direction. Its sparkling clear water satisfies our thirst and provides multitudes of people with the opportunity to live. You are at home in this river: the home of the original divine source. Where else do you want to go to?

#### You want to find out whether Not-Being is perhaps something you can identify yourself with. You want everything; just not to be nothing. You can see light and contemplate light because you yourself are light. But there must be something there to allow that this ‘other’ can be objectified. The object cannot objectify itself. This ‘other’ exists as an overlay like a bubble on the surface of the ocean. In the same way, the world exists superimposed upon a formless, inexpressible, mysterious essence. Life and death are set out on one stratum, which doesn’t affect the awakened one, for he is submerged in that mysterious essence.

### Inner Turmoil – Outer Chaos

#### Yes, many roads lead to Rome, but none lead to eternity. Thus, the wise person abides in himself and remains silent and unstirred. He knows that he knows nothing and lives modestly. In the world, he is inconspicuous. He stays within the internal, the primordial, and never returns again to the external. To remain within the internal doesn’t mean to stop participating in everyday life. It refers to a suprapersonal intensity, to a limitless profound stillness where nothing has ever happened. Letting things be and enjoying an orderly life within Not-Doing: this is how the wise live. Immersed in heavenly radiance, the concepts and ideas arbitrarily added over a lifetime are gradually elucidated and erased.

#### One who lives in society is obviously not a hermit. This is also not necessary to attain insight into the highest truth. Every single person can awaken, and one who truly awakens never falls asleep again.

#### Through a constant sense of craving something, a person becomes anxious and discontent. One is consumed with passions, resentments, greed, ambition, and jealousy and loses stability. Truly, it cannot be the meaning of life to run after objects and bodies one’s entire life! Such behavior causes the spirit of a person to become dull and dissatisfied. Those who are dissatisfied harm themselves, harm others, and harm the natural order in which they are embedded. The insatiable thirst for objects and bodies chains human beings to life and death and holds them as captives in the dungeons of suffering. Those who live in such a way have disconnected themselves from the harmony of true being.

#### Those who cling tightly to transient things and try to retain them as ‘mine’, live in entanglements and may possibly become ill-tempered and violent. Indeed, the confusion of such people is not only a problem for them, but for all those around them. Those who bind and attach themselves to the external and the transitory lack familiarity with internal freedom. They wander through the rough streets of suffering with eyes open but blinded on a path cutoff from the greater order. They ultimately dwell in a self-created

#### non-reality. Why people are like this and act in such a way…, well, they probably do not know themselves.

#### In Not-Being and Not-Acting, a person lives in proper relationship with all appearances and with all that doesn’t appear. Such a person will never lack anything. The awakened one doesn’t act, but through his silent presence, the world is transformed. The influences of the outside world no longer spill back on him. Nothing can touch the Great Silence which he is.

Within the interior of this miserable manner of being lurk egocentric thought-constructs and emotions. These dreamlike phantoms lead the mind to a not-so-pleasant place. It is clearly foolish and absurd to hold tightly to all that is transient and mortal, for this intoxication of the heart entangles it in death-transporting energies.

#### But then, a turn of fate comes about. The hard shell is cracked open and patterns of egocentric behavior collapse. An unplanned event, a radical, shocking experience initiates this transformation. With a loud groan, the soul looks to loosen and rid itself of the ropes that have tied it down. Deep within, a soft voice rises up, desiring only to break down the barriers and walls as rapidly as possible. Human beings are much more than they know and can do more than they think. Their true presence is limitless.

#### Captivity and isolation are not natural states, and the skin of the body is not the border of one’s physical presence in the world. The unthinkable is not nothing. It is a frontier that leads into the unknown, into divine being. The pure land is not a place somewhere in ‘the beyond’. It has never touched the world of life and death and never mixed with the fleeting world.

#### One who sees through the ‘I’-wall activates a collapse. This is certainly one of the most important and noble tasks that a person can be dedicated to while still living within the frame of a physical body.

#### If the student is ready, the master is not far away. With a loving heart, he takes his students by the hand and accompanies them through intense inner

#### storms and internal high waters. His masterful light penetrates all realms. Without doing anything, he brings about transformation and maturation within the students. For those who are truly ready to flow back, he unveils pure celestial powers which set heaven and earth in motion. Through these powers, all that is temporal and entrapping is dissolved and all that was sick is healed. One who transforms within this primal force is emptied of the contents of the transient world and detached from small external matters.

#### The outer world is a temporary construct, a playground in the house of death and destruction. This construct seems very forceful and to exist autonomously. But in truth, it is nothing but a mechanical game of the senses operated by ‘I’-consciousness. Thought and the intellectual mind participate in this play of shadows with the best of intentions. Of course, because it is the one who directs and drives the project forward. All the developments within the world do exist, but at the same time, they really don’t. It always depends on the standpoint from which one observes being and Not-Being.

#### Life is never dull. It is extremely dynamic and full of vitality. Through the discovery of one’s own vital depths, the meaning of our presence here on earth is clarified. The bud unfurls into a blossom.

### Together We Are Everything

#### The word ‘we’ generates special feelings of togetherness and belonging. Feelings of security and comfort can also arise. Yet, how far does our sense of ‘we’ expand? Does it apply globally and include the entire world and all living beings, or is it limited and only relevant to your immediate personal environment? If that is the case, then your sense of ‘we’ categorically excludes almost all other human beings on the earth, and that is a large number of people.

#### The problem with a universal perspective lies in that, in its all-encompassing enormity and vastness, it evades individual worlds of thought and feeling completely. For this reason, when it comes to a universal perspective, one’s personal realms of thought and experience have no place. A universal perspective doesn’t correspond within those worlds and is not compatible with it.

#### However, if the idea of an ‘I’ or of being a personality is seen through and removed, then a supra-personal power of being can blossom, a power that never depends on culture, tradition, morality, religion, or tolerance.

#### Tolerance is given a highly respectable position in our society. But the concept of tolerance can be manipulated when expressed as an attitude towards other human beings, cultures, or religions. There is usually no criterion upon which tolerance is measured. Compatibility, dislike, and the opinions of the majority play an important role. Tolerance can also be an expression of cowardice when used cleverly for a certain purpose. One can dodge conflicts or maneuver to pass over them. Under the slogan of tolerance, facing problems that could be confronted and clarified can be skillfully avoided. Where such inaction occurs, illumination loses its brightness.

#### Solid boundaries define the finite. The Eternal is eternally free and unlimited. So, uncover your true being!

#### Never try to understand Eternity. Trust your true being in Here and Now. That which you eternally are is unattainable, and that which is attainable is not what you are. One who clings to external forms creates suffering and

#### confusion more than anything else. Disentangle yourself and be happily untangled! Flow with life without any resistance and be conscious of the obstacles that try to creep in.

#### When the running engine of I-being begins to relax and egotistical thoughts are no longer nurtured, they then dry out and die away like a poisonous plant you no longer supply water to. Limits and limitations recede and are dissolved. In this way, the fundamental returns to the fundament, within the essence prior to all manifestation.

#### What you truly and eternally are fills the entirety of space. What fills all space is Not-I. When you awaken, the dust of the world never again touches your feet. Eating and drinking, life and death: it all seems to be a dream that you, in reality, never actually dreamed. The source of divine energy flows up from fathomless depths. Bathe in this pure water. It will free you. The divine force of love excludes no living being, nor does it include them. It knows no subjective emotional confusion based on feelings. Divine love is the light that penetrates all things, that illuminates all things, and that makes human beings timeless. Not-Being and Not-Acting is how the awakened one stands within the world: a light in the darkness. His ‘I’-less love flows everywhere and moves the heavens and the earth, brings order and unites them.

#### One who does not integrate with this divine presence can only cause turbulence and confusion. Disorder is imposed upon order. Such behavior seems to be natural and normal for ‘I’-consciousness. But for the awakened one, it is abnormal and absurd.

#### Everything is possible for the awakened one. And at the same time, nothing more is possible. He lives within unity and is unity. He adapts to the world without being affected by the world. To turn away from the world or to judge the world is far from his way of being, for he left behind the foul abysses where the good, the bad, the near, and the far appear long ago.

#### To retreat from the world means nothing. To stand right in the middle of the world, conscious of one’s duties and participating in social life, and then to conquer life and death: this means everything. That is the true masterpiece – and it is possible for all people who are patient!

#### Variety is embedded in unity. This is why all living beings on the earth are of equal value and have the right to live. One who is truly conscious of this pays attention to and protects all life and all living beings. This has nothing to do

#### with morality or with religious convictions. Rather, it comprehensively describes exactly what pure love, reason, and true humanity are: attributes of the awakened one.

#### Love is like our breath. The inhaling and exhaling of air happen without our doing anything, without desiring and without thinking. It simply happens – free from ‘I’-consciousness.

#### What is rigid eventually breaks apart. What is soft flows on. Life lives itself. Life in the physical body is bound to pain and suffering. Of course, the awakened one must also face these circumstances. But, because he abides in silence, he doesn’t follow these external impulses. He feels pain and he experiences pain, but he senses it on the outermost periphery of his being and not as something affecting his true being. Peace and relaxation distinguish one who has transcended form and the body. He lives in equilibrium between heaven and earth, in sacred unity with everything that is. His actions are the activity of unity and not of the transient personality.

#### Where an ‘I’-authority no longer exists on the inside, there is also no longer an ‘I’-authority on the outside. That which exudes from the core meets with no external opposition or limitation. That which flows out from Not-Acting and Not-Desiring generates intimacy between everything that exists externally and the limitless inner core.

#### The soft footsteps of the awakened one moving through space and time is saturated with unfathomable love and divine power. His presence testifies to the luminosity and beauty of the ever-present Mystery. The awakened one moves through the world without being trapped in it. His being is detached from space and time, free of the constraining energies that produce life and death. Although he moves through space, he no longer manifests and never returns.

#### Nourishing cosmic energy is offered without striving and effort. It knows no conditions nor desires. Love is not a tool for external action, and yet it opens the gates of the heavens. One who awakens flows with divine light and never goes anywhere.

### On the Meaning of Life

#### The meaning of life is not to be found in personal achievements and individual success stories. Of course, without success, a person feels unhappy, is despondent and insecure and feels himself at the mercy of circumstances. But when one constantly struggles and sustains a strong will to succeed, one loses serenity and relaxation. This never-ending, goal-oriented qualifying and striving wears down people’s resources and exhausts their vital energy. Exhaustion, burnout, depression, and illness are the fallouts of a stressful, unsatisfying life.

#### What do you do with time and what does time do with you? Do you have time or take time, or are you permanently without enough time, in constant stress? People believe that meaning and fulfillment will come with success. This has been communicated to us our entire lives by a society based on competition. And, it is precisely with this same scheme, with the same hunger for success, that the spiritual and religious quest for something higher has been followed. Naturally, such ambitions and strivings for a higher, ethereal goal are filled with hopes of finding something better, brighter, and more beautiful as a reward for all of one’s efforts. The intellectual thinking mind is constantly busy weighing the short-term and long-term utility of its undertakings. A human being is a calculating being. So, when something is not useful at the moment, perhaps it will be of use in ‘the beyond’ or in a future life. Nevertheless, divine silence never allows itself to be cultivated or acquired and is never going to be a successful achievement. Can a shadow strive to become the sun?

#### Things of the world should not disturb your heart or move it around. Let all the wanting, believing, and hoping settle down like the still, translucent water of a mountain lake on a clear full moon night.

#### Society loves and reveres those who are successful. Those without success, on the other hand, drift out of sight. Such an attitude is a cruel catastrophe. Of course, we are not celebrating failure. But it is true that success often reinforces egoism and ends up in a cold and callous heart.

#### Egoism and greed are deathly traps, which darken the spirit and destroy opportunities for true humanity to shine forth. Countless dreadful stories circulate throughout the world every day, spread and planted in people’s minds with malicious intent. Thoughts mold and record the processes of the world in which we live.

#### Within the flow of time, today creates tomorrow. Thus, yesterday is a memory which one can remember today. But now cannot be remembered, though certainly the reverberations from now, which arise like waves, can become memories.

#### Thought, which always moves forward, creates ideas of tomorrow here today. Because the observed and the one observing cannot exist separated from each other, it seems that what is conceived of and felt today as a tomorrow really exists.

#### Only that which is eternal remains eternally untouched by the finite and the conceptual. It is free from the energies within us which produce ideas of success and failure, of day and night. That which is eternal is what is meaningful and gives purpose. It is undividable and always interconnected.

#### Meaningfulness is never meaningless. Our existence can therefore never be meaningless. To truly realize this and not to understand it is meaningful. Meaning means ‘being one’, and ‘being one’ never gets mixed up with the overlay of the portrayed world of appearances. One who awakens no longer becomes immersed in the external.

#### Positive thoughts and ideas of wanting to improve the world may have their validity. But recognize first that you exist prior to thinking and whatever you think about. Discover It. This is fulfillment and something immeasurably profound, beautiful, and powerful. This your true being!

#### The world is very big. And yet, it is limited. That which you eternally are is eternally unlimited. Things in the conventional world are subject to constant change. You are not. A shadow can never touch light, and what is subject to conditions and change is never your true being.

#### The large exists within the small, and the small exists within the large. These very logical sounding ideas relating to polarities and dichotomies belong to the external world of thought. Don’t allow yourself to be captivated by visions and idiosyncratic systems of thought which seem to be of intellectual value, but are actually not. Be wise! Don’t toss around thoughts which spawn life and death and catapult you far away from your eternal

#### being!

#### You cannot calculate your true presence, nor can you determine it geometrically. Detach yourself from such concepts and ideas. You are truly something different than what you can think about or calculate.

#### No doubt, the world is a geometric, mathematical wonder. One can only marvel at the incredible beauty of it. Nevertheless, your true being transcends everything that appears and everything temporal. Truly, the power and the solidarity which constitute heaven and earth are your eternal home – here in the center, in great equilibrium.

#### Thoughts follow the eyes and become words, which then condense into concrete action. This merry-go-round of deeds piloted by the senses is what gives shape and form to everything outside of yourself which you see and experience. Look precisely and recognize that everything with shape and form is your not-self. Don’t be afraid! Detach yourself and transcend these external limitations dwelling within you!

#### Thought-energies create, sustain, and maintain the external world. They modify and renew themselves constantly. They adapt to particular situations, particular areas of functioning, and particular needs. All people produce and animate their own life-stories. One is the producer and actor on the stage of one’s own epic drama.

#### Through differentiated and specified activities of the senses, stimuli occurring through external impulses are processed and coordinated within the brain. With tremendous speed, all information is integrated into a world of experience. Through various reflexes and the thoughts and feelings which arise, something that is palpable and perceptible for the brain is sparked and generated.

#### The intellect has the capacity to understand thoughts and categorize feelings that are generated. It also employs the brain, which makes thinking and understanding possible, in active reflection. In other words, the brain thinks about itself.

#### And still, there must be something there, some object or feeling, which the brain can reflect on. It cannot reflect on nothing. But this something cannot be you. It is something else, something external, something that exists separate from you. Without an ‘I’ there is no ‘that’, and without a ‘that’ there can be no ‘I’. ‘I’ and ‘that’ condition and nourish each other. There must be an object available that the subject can reflect on and use to validate its

#### existence. A drop of water or a piece of fruit cannot think about itself. You think

#### about them, and you recognize and name them as such. The drop of water never told you that it is a drop of water. Your body has never told you that it belongs to you! Flesh does not speak, nor does the piece of fruit, nor does the world. You speak about it! The game that you are playing in the world is called ‘I and That’!

#### You are never that which you think about, and beyond your thinking, no ‘something’, no object, and no world exists. And nevertheless, there is never a moment when that which you truly are, the Unthinkable, is not here and now – with or without an ‘I’, with or without a body, and with or without a world. There, where an ‘I’ and a ‘that’ cease to exist is your real home: the splendor of eternity, the Great Silence, the original radiant light.

#### Through direct and precise observing within yourself, this ‘I’ and this ‘something’ are exposed as added on and unreal images, as only mirages in the desert. Where there is something superimposed and unreal, there must be something prior to this superimposition – that which you truly and eternally are.

#### You are so strongly influenced by the automatization of the senses that you believe you are a someone, an individual person living, acting, and dying in the world. These processes and operating patterns are impossible for you to guide, influence, or control with your will. They happen because they happen and not because an ‘I’ is there that wants this or doesn’t want that. Nature is the way it is.

#### Curiously, you are completely convinced that everything that you perceive and feel through the senses is actually happening beyond your body. You have no doubt that these perceptions and feelings are real and true. Of course, this is not the whole truth. The body itself is merely a perception within consciousness in the waking state. That which is born into existence through sensory activity and thought-movement is held together by these same birth-giving energies and is monitored by ‘I’-consciousness. Always be aware of what you are not!

#### The light which remains hidden from your view is the light that shows the path where the polarities of the world can be overcome. Don’t cling to concepts and optical illusions. You have no reason for doing so. That which you really are will never be found beyond yourself, and there will never

#### come a point where you will possess that. You are not a ‘something’ that could ever possess something else, and certainly nothing special, even if you might like to believe otherwise.

#### What is special is the reality that you exist eternally here and now, without being someone. Stop mistaking the unreal for the real and stop confusing yourself with things that in reality don’t exist! What could be easier than that? Don’t believe in the things you believe in. Blind belief is unhealthy and dangerous. That which you really are knows neither past nor future.

#### Don’t concern yourself with what comes and goes. Be natural and without over-exertion or extravagance. Be yourself! There is no requirement of proof to show that you exist here and now. This does not refer to the present, for there is no feeling of time in here and now. The present is the hub around which yesterday and today are processed in the mind and where the idea of a tomorrow is generated and nurtured. Everything is today. But everything which is today is also the result of a hundred yesterdays, all of which exist only as concepts in consciousness. ‘I’-consciousness is always willing to accept the things it believes as true and real. But it is not conscious that it itself is only an added-on accomplice to death.

#### The senses catalyze the external internally and store the external there within. But the entirety of one’s life-story is carried to its grave when the physical body dies. Whether this end is sad for a select few people or not is uncertain. In the end, this question is buried or cremated as well. Nature offers its exhalation, and no inhalation follows. Death is not a departure. It is a transition. For those who are detached and already dead before death comes, it is neither nor.

#### Movements of thought are like rivers and creeks which flow away from you in all directions. Discover the source from which everything emerges. Your thought-constructs always break apart and break down, liquify and cause fear, strong doubt, and suffering. It requires constant strenuous effort to keep all your thought-structures in proper order, to constantly enhance them, and ultimately to defend them. Don’t trap yourself in these willful forces. They only lead to chaos and nowhere else. Abide as a silent observer at the source and don’t wander out with the water! Remain at home. Stay in the boundless ocean filled with silence and light!

#### A hundred paths lead into the far distance. One who awakens no longer

#### follows any of them and abides in peace. The awakened one has turned his glance within and sees neither life nor death.

#### The further one wanders outwards, the longer and more complicated the path back becomes. Many dangers, deceptions, and distractions lurk in the distance, which lead to unclear actions. One who leaves the order of things withdraws into disorder. When disorder becomes the normal state, a human being is trapped in life and death. The boundary where life and death can be transcended remains hidden.

#### The individual human being is nothing but a mere pebble in an enormous mountain range, but nevertheless a part of it. One is nothing more than a grain of sand on a long sandy beach, and yet one is a part of the beach. That which one knows is nothing in comparison to what one doesn’t know. The incredible expanses, magnitudes, and depths of infinite being and the vast and incalculable rotations of the universe with which one is always connected cause a human being to marvel and become humble and silent. When a person becomes aware of this infinite measure, all sense of superiority and self-importance vanish. The relevance of the limited individual compared with the unlimited and infinite nature of the universe appears ridiculous, even grotesque. Your true pre-worldly being exists due to primordial powers of light which carry all of heaven and earth within them.

#### Refrain from being someone or something important. Integrate yourself with the divine, supra-personal primordial powers! The depths which are revealed through this integration cannot be explained with human language. Here, the so-called big and the so-called small dissolve into nothingness. The immeasurable variety of things melts into one. The One is neither something nor a someone nor a thing. It is timeless, nameless, and formless. It is that which you really and eternally are.

#### Look out over the wide-open inner ocean and leave the loveless shores of limitations and constrained ‘I’-ness. Leave it behind. It has never actually had an existence within you. Don’t allow yourself to be carried away by the flooding forces of the dull ‘I’. Awaken within the Great Mystery!

#### Once you discover what you really are not, the forces of the ego collapse and dissipate like smoke from a fire. You are not nothing. You discover and realize the true magnitude of your being beyond the external world and beyond the internal world, which are both products of superimposed ‘I’-ness.

#### Your true being transcends large and small, near and far, life and death. The awakened one holds onto nothing: not to happiness nor unhappiness; not

#### to the good nor the bad, not to success nor to failure, not to joy nor suffering, and not to life and death. In the realization that all these states are nothing but ephemeral waves in consciousness, the awakened one, who has never slept, abides in pure silence as he observes the movements of the world.

#### One can discuss unity and integration and philosophize about these topics. But no matter how well-chosen and intelligent the words may be, they are never the true expression of the inseparable, the completely integrated, the eternally detached.

#### Through recognition and realization of the Unchangeable, all ideas and concepts of a yesterday and a tomorrow, of coming and going dissolve. Life and death cease to exist, but you are eternally here and now. Although this Unchangeable resides in all beings, it remains untouched and untouchable by anything that changes, by everything that is born and dies.

#### The Unchangeable is not something that existed before and must now be re-produced, re-invented and re-formed, and it is also not something that will end at some point in the future. One cannot gain it, nor can one lose it. But one can realize it and actualize it when one is deeply aware of what one never was.

#### You should never animate the Unchangeable with subjective thought-constructions or sentiments, thus condensing it and pulling it down into transient changeability. This kind of activity tears you away from your true being.

#### Awareness is universal and never individual. All paths and all concepts dissolve within universal consciousness. Don’t align your life solely with what you know. Rather, attain insight and knowledge of that which believes it knows. Bring all into proper relationship and see through to the core of these external forces which portray themselves as ‘I-am’. Recognition allows transcendence of the knower and leads the awakening one back into the greater order. Live your life in and out of the greater order! It is that which you really are. When you are able to do this, thoughts and actions lead to transformation and fulfillment. The awakened one doesn’t judge or condemn. Things are no longer discrimination in the external world. Within unchangeable being, all is one; but not of one form.

#### Without a lamp there would be no light. Without light there is no perception and no awareness of any object in space. There is only darkness. But

#### darkness is nothing but the absence of light. Light emerges through the functionality of the lamp. But it is not the lamp itself.

#### The body is like a hologram which appears in the waking state in space and time. Through the essence of heavenly light, it is nourished and made functional. Your true being is light, eternally free from that which appears in light and which through light has become tangible and capable of being experienced.

#### That which appears splits apart. Through the one light within the body and infusing the senses, it is perceived as variety in the external world. The light itself is not extinguished when the body dies. Only that which was made visible and tangible through the light and had an appearance in the world is dead and extinguished. The light moving within the world is not the world. It is essence: the foundation of the world.

#### Your true being is prior to appearances in consciousness and not those appearances in consciousness. Your true presence is more than a network of thoughts. You are universal ‘Not I-am’.

#### When the sense of ‘I-am’ sneaks up into consciousness, a subject-object situation immediately comes into being. Identification with the body and things of the world installs itself, and one becomes intertwined with the non-essential. In this worrisome state, the soul is trapped and incapable of allowing the creative awakening forces to flow through it.

#### Causes generate effects. They produce much that is good, but also bad karma shows itself over the course of a lifetime. A human being is caught within these ancient forces of fate. One lives in one’s own self-constructed prison and suffers there. One is indeed trapped within time and space whether one’s karma is good or bad, the only difference being that, with bad karma one is tied to the world with a chain made of lead, and with good karma the chain is made of gold. The fact that one is held prisoner remains the same, of course, though a brighter experience is certainly easier to go through than a dark one. The moment you become deeply aware of your actual situation in the transient world, cracks in the prison wall begin to open, and gradually the building will fall apart. You will then realize that neither the prison nor the prisoner ever really existed.

#### One who awakens is free of illusion and also free of the desire for enlightenment. Which external temporary state could you still hold onto or content yourself with when Not-I and Not-Being have been truly realized?

#### The arising of fears, doubts, hopelessness, and suffering within the psyche of human beings testifies to the fact that they have lost sight of their original nature. They have attached themselves and must endure the energetic storm of the conceptual, imaginary external world, where they encounter exactly that which they are not. At the outer layer of consciousness, life and death transpire. Health and illness manifest, as well as joy and suffering. Human beings identify themselves with these developments and are convinced of the reality of such happenings. Suffering pervades the world and life is certainly not a simple thing. Salty tears accompany human beings on the sharp edge of external existence, where one feels at times lonely and adrift. But even in the darkest moments, the gemstone within has never ceased to shine. The timeless call is never hushed.

#### Life is a treasure chest, where a multitude of wonders is hidden. One who opens the lid will be amazed!

#### The awakened one is dis-illusioned. He is no longer affected by what he sees, hears, feels, tastes or smells, but he is never indifferent. He is aware that the activity of the senses belongs to the functionality of human nature, but nothing there touches divine Not-Being. Detached from doing and not doing, he lives there where nothing could ever be achieved, and no event has ever taken place. He is aware that there was never anything like an individual existence. Not-Being is your true and natural state, for prior to anything ever appearing, you are.

#### Life displaced to superficial externalities makes us strangers in our own house, a house without walls. But recognizing how one thing affects another, a human being becomes silent, humble, and harmonious. One lives in all-encompassing, universal equilibrium.

#### All living beings emerge from the same one mysterious source and become Nature, which we all are, internally and externally. The pervasive, collective, evolutionary processes of becoming that take place on earth never allow themselves to be constrained or restrained. An individual cannot control or influence the cyclic processes of the world or the general course of things. Existence only becomes heavy when a human being is trapped in the dull gravitational forces of the concept ‘I-am’ and has distanced oneself and abandoned the harmony of divine unity.

#### All living beings exist in the same essential state of being. How could you or they then exist independently and separate from that which you really are?

#### It isn’t possible. Seen this way, we together are a unified One, not in our various forms, but prior to the appearance of variety. External things change; that which you really are, never! The world and the entire universe exist in one reality. For this reason, one can refer to it as reality. Everything which appears within the one reality as shape and form is subject to change and temporary. What has been pushed into the external and is subject to change can be referred to as illusory. This is also evident when observing the collective processes of life and death affecting all living beings.

#### One who sees through changing phenomena becomes aware of their unreality. And still, there is not one moment when Eternal Being is not. It is always here and now. You are always here, always now, and you don’t need to reinvent the world in order to realize what you eternally are.

#### Where there is light, no darkness exists. Where there is clarity, there is no confusion. And where wisdom reigns, there is no ignorance. Awakening happens here and now and is neither a process nor a progression.

#### Direct intuitive awareness is timeless and completely free of becoming and one who becomes. To step out of time voids all forms of becoming and erases any traces. External consciousness of birth, death, and rebirth are nowhere to be found, for a slipping from primordial being into time and space never really happened.

#### Awakening erases time-bound knowledge. Everything which one knows or can know is temporary and is of no use for awakening. There is never a subject, never an object, for eternal being never allows itself to be grasped or known. It is that which looks through the eyes, but the eyes can never see it, and the intellect will never understand it.

#### Allow boundless freedom to live your life and don’t worry about limits and limitations. Don’t disturb the silent current of being! The intellect drowns beauty and destroys love. Through the intellect, the world slips away from true divine presence. The tragedy of it is that human beings are not at all conscious of the superficiality of their existence. They constantly fall into states of restlessness and anxiety, thereby distancing themselves from the great equilibrium.

#### The conditions which are hereby manifested determine and shape the activity and the perspectives of the world for these people. Life is guided and formed around transitory phases of joy and suffering. Those pulled along in this way do damage to their psychic being as well as their physical being.

#### Insight and clarification of internal blockages make awakening possible.

#### Turning away from the old and facing the new leads one back into the great equilibrium. Only fools close their eyes and ears and live on in dull ignorance and suffering.

#### All manifestations of being change in wave-like currents of movement. Bodies are born and consumed at death. The individual human being has no influence on this forceful course of worldly happenings in which all life-forms are embedded. One is powerless. The Mystery is responsible for the transformation of the world and not the individual person. And still, floods of tears fill the world. The fact that by being born one is fated to die drove a deep thorn into the soul that penetrated deeply.

#### When one is able to harmoniously integrate within this enormous progression of worldly happenings, a person becomes one with the immense divine world-force, is free of identification with the individual personality, and flows back to the original source. This is the end of drifting back and forth. The taste of an individual life is completely gone, and the outer world is dissolved within. What is revealed beyond all borders and boundaries cannot be explained in words. All constructed thoughts create only external things. The awakened one looks into nothingness and sees nothing, yet is aware of his pure, ever-existing presence.

#### Don’t worry about your thoughts which jump around and are difficult to control. Avoid the things that pull you into the external and be aware that you are never that which you imagine yourself to be!

#### The one who awakens lives very naturally and quietly in Not-I, in harmonious accordance with the world. Free from wanting, free from goals, one remains unaffected by the shifting circumstances of life. One no longer throws out a hook to catch something. Whatever one might catch is transient and only serves to prolong the illusion and increase suffering.

#### There is nothing and no one that holds you back from looking deeply. Open your eyes! See the divine reality of eternal being! Stop roaming around. You are already home!

### When Death Died

#### Ultimately, one cannot really know how life after death really is. Gathered knowledge about it expands through space and time and creates limitations. Life after death doesn’t allow itself to be known in this way!

#### Through observation of our lives and with various accessible analogies and references, certain assumptions are filtered out, which allow one to conclude that life is not bound to death. The creative, multi-faceted cosmos of the thinking mind arrives at the boundary of the unthinkable, at what is incomprehensible on the level of rational thought. ‘I’-consciousness suffers here in its narrowly woven existence and thrashes around like a fish trapped in a net.

#### A human being wants to know whether life continues after physical death, or whether one’s existence is completely extinguished on the other side of this dark threshold. One hopes that the visible forms one is accustomed to and the personal thought-world will come along in a subtle, spiritual, invisible, alternative world, following the principle of, “As it is below, so above; as it is outside, so it is within.” No one actually knows whether this invisible continent exists, as is suggested very often over millenniums by various cultures with differing systems of belief. There is much to indicate that it is so.

#### When thoughts arise, forms and multitudes of images are gathered together. The concept of ‘I-am’ is the central control-panel from which all concepts and ideas arise. Establishing itself through countless links and inter-connections, this self-proclaiming construction operates as a system with one principal mission: not to succumb, not to die. This strong drive to survive is present in all living beings.

#### Thinking is the root source of the ego, and the ego is the root source of all knowledge and memory. Human beings orient and define themselves according to that which they know and what they can remember. Knowledge is what human beings have experienced and can then remember. Death is the only event that a person cannot remember, which is irritating. One feels afraid of it, because one doesn’t know what will happen, or if there is even a

#### hereafter. If there is, how will it be? To exchange what is known for the unknown is no joyful perspective for ‘I’-consciousness. It is not ready to accept that the body and the external world are transient things, are the not-self. Thoughts cling to the earth and to form. Here, things can go in any direction. Non-clinging and detachment are taboo words for the ego.

#### Where proliferation is no longer possible, the ‘I-am’ finds no satisfaction. The existential foundation of the energies generated by ‘I-am’ are disempowered and deprived of their significance and purpose. What a ridiculous ego-game! Don’t get lost in dreamy illusions! Pull back and discover the spirit of the depths that never die!

#### What you consider your life to be is tied to your experiences and the perspectives of your established world view. In order to go beyond these limitations and see the expansive inner open sea, the personalized, superimposed layers and coverings of ‘I’-consciousness must first be seen through and set aside. The greater order is eternally uncovered. It has no overlay. It is the divine being that you really are.

#### Nobody knows just how many centuries he or she has wandered in oblivion from death to death through space and time. Knowledge can never know this.

#### Human beings constantly seek to discover the depths of their relationship between themselves and the Great Mystery. One searches. One researches. One tries to know and understand. During the search, one creates a current and delves deeper and deeper into the inner realm. But one’s current is finite and the eternal ocean is infinite. This is the seeker’s major conflict. One searches for something that one already was before the search began. The one searching searches for oneself, but one doesn’t know exactly what it actually is that one is searching for. Still, something drives the search onward.

#### The eternal ocean holds the power of the current within itself. Human beings are not aware of it. They act like fish swimming around in water tirelessly searching for water. Throughout the outer world there is nothing that is enduring, nothing that remains the same. Therefore, there is also no tranquility and no true equilibrium there. All states have a beginning and an end. All things that arise as appearances will be destroyed by the same forces that created them. Everything near and everything far away dissolves into nothing.

#### To look ahead or to look back is no longer possible for the awakened one. The onlooker who clung to what he was looking at has ceased to exist. Neither life nor death allow themselves to be held onto. Everything that is limited to a physical form can be big or small, coarse or fine, and capable of receiving or refusing. But what has come into being and become form cannot escape change and impermanence and is subject to permanent dissolution.

#### The awakened one is never harmed by change. The substance of his being is eternal and unchangeable. The ambitious greed for life, for being someone or for clasping onto something is dissolved. In this dissolving, all attachments to the forces of destiny also vanish.

#### The good can never be separated from the bad. Love can never be divorced from hate. Therefore, the awakening one doesn’t animate the one or the other. How could there be valuable things or worthless things for one who has left the things of the world behind? One who lives in the greater order knows nothing of disorder, and one who dwells in the timeless knows nothing of time.

#### The time with which you live in your body can never be compared to the time in which you live without a body. The astounding vastness of being is not a thing that can be comprehended or observed from the perspective of the limited body. Still, a human being always tends to do exactly that.

#### Nothing within space and time can objectify and determine the spaceless and the timeless. Every effort to objectify is flawed, as its sole reference is the inner and outer environment of the imagined world constructed by the senses.

#### The compulsive tendency to unite with other physical beings and unify with the body is deeply entrenched in the flesh of all living beings. The sexual drive to procreate and propagate is intensely present in every insect and elephant, in every flower, tree, and human being. The entire order of physical Nature is produced and built upon this one energetic force. This also leads to strong attachment and identification with the body.

#### Erotic moments filled with desire are the cause of your body being born and also the cause of its death at some point in time. Whether you like it or not, your parents are the cause, and you are the effect. You were literally forced to enter the world of human beings and figure out how to get along there.

#### Everything which you believe to be true and everything you think you know is information you have heard or learned from someone else. You identify yourself with this acquired storehouse of knowledge, and while using it as your referential base, you are convinced that you exist as an ‘I-am’ individual being. But who exactly do you believe yourself to be after everything you have heard and learned from others is set aside?

#### Your body, which your parents willingly or accidently produced, is actually not your own. You are their product. But you still say, “This is my body.” So then, how much of you really exists in that body? Who were you before your pile of external knowledge was dumped on you? This knowledge has certainly allowed you many experiences. Your physical being consists of nothing but external knowledge. Your body is there because it is there and not because you wanted it or didn’t want it. Your birth and becoming in the world couldn’t be wished for by you, nor could it be stopped by you.

#### The feeling of existing, of being ‘I-am’, is based solely on your identification with the body and your strong attachment to it brought about by external knowledge. The resulting perspective of life is therefore narrow and dull, oriented only towards acquisition and utility. This encapsuled way of being is far away from pure, divine light. It confines a human being to the finite.

#### Be aware of this: you exist truly here and now, but you were never a someone or something in the Here and Now. You are that which the world has never touched, and which never entangles itself with the impermanent and mortal. You were also never the consciousness through which everything appears and disappears. Birth and death are nothing but a massive deception generated by the conceptual idea of ‘I-am’.

#### No one was born and no one dies, because this someone has, in truth, never existed. Your physical body-cloak will pass away, but your eternal being is bodiless. You are not nothing. But that which you really are simply cannot be expressed in human language. What you truly are is immeasurably divine. It is this beauty and tenderness, this perfect, radiant light-power not from this world which constitutes your true eternal presence.

#### Nevertheless, a deluge of misunderstandings and illusions covers up the source and blocks the free-flowing current. Not-Being is also Not-Appearing. It is boundless and without beginning. Never was there a starting place where this current first began to flow.

#### Don’t go back into harm’s way! Trust and observe that life lives itself

#### very well. Everything is in order in the greater order!

### How the Eternal Shows Itself

#### The forces of entanglement and attachment that engage human beings in the physical world of bodies and forms are incredibly strong. These elementary circumstances result in people thinking that only what is visible and conceivable truly exists. Everything they cannot see or conceive doesn’t relate to their materialistic ideas of their own lives or their materialistic views of the world.

#### The brain searches unceasingly for patterns and structures, for fixated and clustered positions where it can orient itself. It requires something to focus on, something it can recognize. Images and information are spontaneously assembled and unified, but then also seen and experienced as something existing outside of the brain. Through this automated functionality, things get fuzzy and confused. One thing appears like another thing, and from one, two arise, and then more. This externalized responsiveness to things is superimposed and has no autonomous reality or existence.

#### Superimposed objects here refer to the body and also the world in which it appears. Through process-oriented operations within the brain, a sense of something inside and something outside arises: an inner world and an external world which condition each other.

#### Everything that appears through the senses is subject to the laws of impermanence. Everything formed, everything the eye perceives, is doomed to death and destruction. At the time of physical death, the central headquarters, the brain, will no longer be capable of functioning, and due to the disfunction of the body, the world also disappears. The one seeing and the thing seen, the thinker and the thing thought about, all cease to function and exist and ultimately vanish.

#### The foundation of the five senses which operate in the experiential world is consciousness. Continual consciousness of the body and the world ends at the time of physical death. Life lives itself and death also dies on its own.

#### The body is the nourishment through which consciousness can be conscious of itself as consciousness. Without consciousness, there would be

#### neither ‘the world’ nor ‘the beyond’. The individual being which experiences birth and death hopes for a life after death, and that life should also be quite similar to the one experienced before physical death.

#### A human being hopes that the continuation of being as was previously known will not be interrupted or come to an end. ‘I-am’ only wants one thing: to live on with or without the physical body. Does life as we know it fade and dissolve when death reveals itself? Is it like smoke leaving a fire, or do we live on?

#### Will we actually be able to bring the entire contents of our thought-world and all our religious beliefs along when we cross the threshold of death, and will this content suffice in the ‘other world?’ Do we stay just as we are?

#### Who really knows what eternity is? Is eternity a continuously expanding, never-ending extension of time, or is it perhaps something completely different, something without time, something free of becoming? One uses terms like ‘eternity’ as if the words were the factual thing itself.

#### A human being has invented countless conceptions to provide names, categories, and explanations for objects and things in the world perceived by the senses. But all of these notions are not something real or essential of themselves. They are purely concepts which creatively arise and are thought through by the brain to rationally interpret the perceptible world and make it comprehensible and useful.

#### One’s subjective image of the world is a product of the thinking intellect and reason. On the one hand, it’s real, but on the other hand, it is completely unreal, because fantasies and odd ideas have invaded the foundation of it. One’s perspective of the world ultimately exists purely from the substance of what is perceived and is then nothing but an assembled construction within the brain. No matter what you believe yourself to be, that is exactly what you, in reality, never were.

#### Deep within your innermost core is the unadulterated evidence of what you eternally are, beyond all divergencies and polarities. You live in the world, but it doesn’t live in you. It is only a game of shadows your consciousness is playing. Your true being doesn’t allow itself to be explained or objectified. It is neither this nor that. Don’t lose yourself in not-self! Look deeply within yourself and be aware of the source from which all misunderstandings arise! The powerful influences which come about through knowledge of the external world and push you around need to be thoroughly

#### penetrated and transcended. Don’t underestimate the power of these influential forces, or before you realize what is going on, they become the foundation of your external life. You see and experience this construct as your own life, and it becomes what you identify yourself as.

#### When the unreal fades within the real, the restlessly flickering flame of the external world within consciousness is extinguished. The human being has now turned within and no longer restores emphasis on the outer world – the place where a something never really existed.

#### Discover your true, untainted nature in Here and Now. Don’t attach yourself to ideas and concepts which you never were. Always be aware that you are never that which you conceptualize, for your true presence is pre-worldly and inconceivable. Don’t worry about what you believe yourself to be. Discover what you are eternally!

#### Your past is nothing but a remembered edifice constructed by thoughts and memories. Your future is nothing but an unknown journey of discovery which you imagine in your present. You have an erroneous idea of yourself, which has arisen through identification with the body and objects in the world. Strangely, you trust more in the impermanent things which appear in your consciousness than in what you really are.

#### Finite things are more familiar to you that the infinite, and that is only because you cannot get to it with your thoughts. You are convinced that only things which you can think of really and truly exist. The inconceivable is unreal for you because it is not a thing or an object.

#### Why don’t you trust that which encompasses all things without identifying with any of them. The essential is unrestricted, unlimited, timeless, and always here and now. Not-Being doesn’t exclude or include anything and never allows itself to be grasped or let go of.

#### Eternity does not encompass an area where individual beings are born or die. Eternity exists in all directions. It moves into no areas and has no beginning and no end.

#### Imagine that you are standing alone in a dessert and are thirsty. You see something shimmering in the distance. You see water, a lake. Your thirst drives you forward, but the sea seems to recede further and further away the closer you come to it. Suddenly you become conscious that the lake is nothing but a reflection, an optical illusion misleading your senses, a mirage. After you have seen through this illusion taking place within your

#### consciousness, you will never again run after drinking water from a lake that doesn’t actually exist.

#### Don’t go anywhere internally. Stop putting distance between yourself and eternal Not-Being, the eternal Self. When you realize what you really are not, you will no longer move around in the external world and seek your happiness and satisfaction there in temporal bodies and objects.

#### Remain at home at the pre-worldly source. Then, your thirst and hunger will be forever pacified. When the ancient shadows are put aside and fade away completely, the awakened one sees countless worlds, bodies, and objects, and observes them in silence. He sees the manifestation, but he doesn’t enter into it. He realizes that that which really is and that which is unreal exist in the Here and Now at the same time. He sees the unreal in the real.

#### What appears, appears spontaneously. What disappears, disappears spontaneously. Why do you worry so much about things and processes which you can’t influence or control at all?

#### Where were you before you were born? Where will you be when you no longer exist as yourself with a physical body? How much influence do you really have on your true being and how much influence do you have on your untrue being? Because you have entangled yourself by identifying with the outer margin of being, you have generated a world where the unreal is real, and you have constructed an internal boundary between you and the eternal Mystery. You have sealed yourself off from Totality, your true being, and you are not really conscious of it.

### Life and Dream

#### For millenniums, religions and philosophies have occupied themselves with the questions about life after death, re-incarnation, and immortality. From science’s perspective, there is no life after death, nor is there re-incarnation or immortality. And still, millions of human beings search for paths and possibilities to overcome death and achieve immortality. This inner burning, this desire for an extended eternal life sits deeply within the psyche, within the souls of people. Indeed, powerful forces are at work, igniting this intense spiritual fire and affecting the deepest inner core of human beings.

#### Limitations are not founded in original being. Stubborn and to some extent absurd dogmas often obstruct and even prevent a deeper look into one’s own being. Religious ways of seeing the world have been established by authoritative institutions that do not tolerate deviance. Belief in authority and the fear which accompanies such notions obscures the human capacity of reason. Rigid belief-systems are repeatedly exclusive as they arrogantly proclaim their singular importance. With concepts of good and bad, people are successfully bound to strings of fear and are explicitly told how and why the good may go to a paradise and are immortal, and why the bad are evil and controlled by dark forces coming from hell-realms. But what is immortality? Does immortality mean to be taken to some unknown place where one is always happy and eternally free of pain? What do you actually know about your own death? Death is a big obstruction for people within their lives. A profound fear of the body’s destruction and the loss of the whole personality is deeply rooted in the minds of human beings and creates irritation in their psyches. And even worse, no one comes away free of it.

#### You are afraid of no longer existing. This thought is difficult for an individual to accept. You are now someone, but after death you will be no one and nothing. The security you have established of being someone is, on the one hand, your best friend, and on the other hand, your worst enemy. The questions of the meaning of one’s being and of being nothing torture the brain as death approaches.

#### In open awareness, in the midst of these contradictions and tensions, is an inner gate through which the awakened one looks into the essential, into true, undivided, eternal being. Through this deep seeing, everything individual gently dissolves and flows back into Not-I, from an individual’s limited, imagined, earthly life back into ever-present, timeless being.

#### Death knows nothing of life and life knows nothing of death. Both exist solely as superimposed thought-constructs. Within true, non-dualistic being, no manifestations exist.

#### The actual difficulty is that you are constantly overtaken and overwhelmed by your own concepts, and thus you are not aware of your natural presence. You are constantly busy with your pressing thoughts as you dash resolutely in the opposite direction of that which you truly and eternally are.

#### The intellect contemplates its demise and gazes from within at the prospect of its ultimate end, at the place where the king of thought falls from its throne. I live now, and later I will be dead. Maybe there is life after death after all? But why do I have to be dead first in order to live on afterwards? What dies, and what actually continues to live on?

#### The intellect thinks it knows. It celebrates everything that it can know and everything that it already knows. It holds reign over knowledge about spirituality and enjoys all the insights and explanations it has gained. But the intellect and the one who understands are nothing but an externalized formation of not-self, nothing but a bundle of active thoughts which have assembled within the brain, and which will again disband at some point.

#### The eternal does not allow itself to be grasped through thoughts or thinking. To realize eternal being, the intellect and thought are useless.

#### When pure divine light is revealed within human existence, all barriers and limitations collapse. Light from the old lamps is extinguished. There was never a real basis for ‘I-am’. It never existed. Being is never false seeing.

#### The intellect is knowledge of the world, and the world and the intellect reside in consciousness. Within consciousness, the waking state flows back into the dream world. Everything flows into and out of everything else, entering and exiting again and again. This is how the cosmic procession of conscious external existence moves, a succession of movements within which all living beings are embedded. That which you eternally are, however, doesn’t move and is never affected by these cyclic movements. Realize the one inseparable

#### reality of true being! Realize within this one reality what you never were! Nothing exists separately within the one and only all-encompassing consciousness.

#### A still and protective silence is there. From it, one can hear a gentle inner voice. This delicate tone which the ear cannot perceive tirelessly reminds us of our calling and smoothens the path for us back to our original home. The hidden inner nature of existence is filled with unspoiled grandeur and divine power. One who enters into it fulfills this calling and transcends the last barrier.

#### The intellect is the world. Consciousness is the universe. Nothing exists separate from anything else. One who awakens and penetrates with clear vision the external layers of temporal existence and then leaves it behind is one who lives free of concepts and external knowledge. What nourishes is detached from knowledge, from the knower, and from the known. It is rare that someone has the courage and the required intensity to completely transcend the world and world-consciousness, because human beings are afraid of being nobody and nothing. In this state, they are hardly aware that the person they believe they are, in reality, nothing but a concept, a fleeting, ephemeral movement within consciousness.

#### Most spiritual and religious seekers find their psychic home and enlightenment in inner conceptual and dogmatic realms. Here, it is essential to recognize that all seeking, all striving, and all doubts always take place within one consciousness. Nothing can exist separate or outside of consciousness.

#### Within world-consciousness, the concept of ‘I-am’ established itself through identification with objects and the body. It condensed further and the perception of an ‘I’ and an ‘other’, of ‘mine’ and ‘yours’ was activated. From this dualistic energy, the resulting ignorance and all suffering have increased. The ephemeral has covered eternal divine being with a dark shadow. This dualistic ignorance has to be deactivated and removed so that pure, eternal, divine being can shine within your heart and transform you. You are not that which comes and goes!

#### Within externally focused consciousness, you live as a person in the physical world, where you are so excited and consumed with your being as a body that thoughts of giving up and letting go of things makes you totally uncomfortable. You would rather control your consciousness and hold onto

#### all the things that belong to you and tie you down. You are able to take all your belongings with you wherever you go and cling tightly to all objects and bodies.

#### However, when death offers you its kiss, it also extinguishes greedy conceptual thoughts about your things. Nothing that you believed was yours can be taken over this invisible threshold. The core-energy of these representative behavioral patterns is, of course, deeply engrained within collective world-consciousness. It apparently continues to vibrate even without the physical body. No one knows how long this goes on.

#### The material goods that you possess evidently possess you. You definitely want to keep everything that you call your own and you want to protect these things. Every kind of loss causes pain for the psyche and can at times create fear, worry, and insecurity. Fear-filled memories of loss flare up again and again from unconscious layers and draw a dark shadow over everyday life. Blurry thoughts and feelings influence the way one looks at life and the way one acts in society. While being continually pushed around and adrift in a world of feelings, both one’s internal, intangible foundation and the perceived external realm of the physical world fall apart over and over again. The unstable is never stable; the ephemeral is never eternal.

#### Only shadow-erasing divine light is able to dry the wet gown drenched in tears. Awaken and stop orienting yourself on knowledge and temporal things! Don’t look back again! The heights and the depths of eternal spiritual being do not allow themselves to be analyzed or figured out.

#### Compulsive striving for self-preservation is oriented on solving problems and is always on high alert. But something deep within human beings searches for serenity, harmony, and equilibrium. Unfortunately, ambitious hyper-activity generally dominates the spiritual search. People secretly hope for some kind of benefit or profit for their lives, and even perhaps for a future life. This mind-set of spiritual bargaining is widespread and shows how brilliantly the corrupt human ego functions.

#### The ego is a system of domination that wants only to direct and control. It wants to be right, and it is ready to diligently apply itself in all realms of life. Why human beings are the way they are is truly a conundrum! This earth which is populated with so many human beings could be very different, if only the illness of greed did not exist. Thankfully, this wretched disease is not incurable.

### Leap Into the Unknown

#### The vitalizing power at the source of all existence is pure, free, and unattached. It has never become something, and yet it is the foundation of everything that is. It is that which you eternally are. Awakening means to regard life and all things with a deep and fresh perspective. Our true spiritual presence exists prior to the physical form and prior to everything which one concocts with the thinking mind.

#### All the things you have done and experienced in your life have left traces in consciousness. These stored up, vivid energies arise in the brain through the noise of countless thoughts. They act as a kind an echo which enters into the external realm in the form of words audible to other people. These energies determine and guide your everyday life. Your entire life in the external realm is an expression of these willful governing energies.

#### Consciousness, which resides within the body, and which is made possible through physical existence, is flawless in and of itself. The problem is the thinking intellect which identifies itself with the body and all appearing objects. Identification with external things casts a dark shadow over pure, flawless consciousness and generates a sense of separateness.

#### You put your nervous system under incredible strain with this shadowy ‘I’-energy, and in your isolation you make yourself sick. You only stare out at external forms and ephemeral things and identify yourself continually with the body and objects. As long as you remain trapped in this ‘mine’ and ‘yours’ -ego-game, pure, primordial light remains hidden from you.

#### The inner star-realm from which you originally came is completely beyond your thoughts and feelings. Discover it! Don’t try to understand it! To realize Totality is the realization of the great primordial silence, the foundation of your real life. This magnificent silence absorbs the noise of the world and also all ideas of being someone living in this superimposed, transient world.

#### One who realizes Totality is always the same – unaffected by surroundings and unmoved by the course of events in the world. The conceptual ideas of existence and non-existence, of unity and variety, have all

#### melted away − for once and for all. The body and the world do appear in consciousness, but these appearances possess neither an autonomous existence nor an individual reality.

#### The projected, shifting images activated in the brain through external stimulation of the senses can be compared to a film that plays out in consciousness. The lead actor of this film is the body and thoughts of ‘I-am’. Yet, the activities of the sense organs are of course nothing else but functioning components of consciousness. The external circumstances of your life are based solely on the operations of the senses within consciousness. The external world reflects this inner world and is sensed there as true and real. Yes, the body and the world appear within the one reality since nothing exists separate or beyond Totality. But your true eternal being is pre-worldly, uncreated, and prior to all appearances. You are Being, and not the one making an appearance!

#### The ever-present vitality flowing through your body allows it to be conscious and capable of living in the world. But vitality itself is not an experience and offers no experiences. Vitality is the essence which, through becoming conscious, allows for birth and physical life to be possible. This essence is the fuel, but never the vehicle. Vitality remains eternally unaffected and unmoved by everything that arises as an appearance and everything that fades away, just as sunlight is eternally unaffected by shadows falling over the earth.

#### Experiences arise through identification with the body and perceived objects. When these ideas arise, a separation from eternal being immediately takes place, a descent into time-conditioned, phenomenal not-self. In this way, countless causes are born along with their effects, producing nothing but anxiety and suffering.

#### Nature is endowed with physical life and also with the death of all physical life. That which permeates it and gives it life is pre-worldly and entirely beyond all physical existence. Truly, your real being is an inconceivable, divine mystery.

#### The body is born and dies again. Your absolute presence remains untouched and unaffected by these events. What you eternally are never links itself to what can be seen and experienced. Unfortunately, you are not able to be truly aware of this, because you identify yourself with your body and the objects you perceive. You are convinced that everything you perceive really

#### and truly coexists with you. Body-consciousness and the five senses constantly compel you to act. They are the matrix: that which constitutes the psychodynamic existence of all living beings in the world.

#### You are constantly doing, constantly becoming, but are you aware of the fundamental essence which underlies everything? The body and the world are temporal, are that which you really are not. Be aware, that you are never that which you are aware of!

#### Eternal being is limitless and never the limited body, which you believe exists in time and space. Everything you have built up, figured out, and imagined over these many years is not you. But you have determined for yourself that your not-self-being is your reality. Therefore, you experience the unreal as the real. You apply your thoughts mainly to the external, ephemeral world and fail to recognize your true eternal being.

#### The ever-present arises externally like a dream and disappears as if it was a dream once again. To believe that this dream is real would be stupid, and to believe that this dream is unreal would be even more stupid. The ever-present is of itself eternal and essentially that which you eternally are.

#### At the time of transitioning out of sleep and into the waking state, you become immediately aware of your body and of objects in the space around you. Spontaneous connections and interrelationships with these things arising in consciousness are carried out within your awareness. The construct which results is externalized, superimposed, and attached to the senses, but without hesitating at all, you accept it as an existing fact. You can only know what inhabits space external to your eternal presence, that which is fundamentally different than what you really are. No matter what you think about, that thing exists separate and outside of you. The one thinking and the thing thought about are only operating functions of time-bound consciousness, which knows nothing of divine Not-Being.

#### The subjective seer needs something it can see. Its existence depends on it. The subject and the object precondition each other. You believe in that which was born and will die. You mistake appearances for reality. Everything which appears will come to an end. The only thing that never ends is that which you really are. There was never ever a beginning to something. It takes place only in your imagination.

#### With its birth, the end of the physical body is already pre-arranged. But why should you be concerned about this, you who were never a body? Discover

#### what lies behind the appearance, and that which you are not will disappear. You fail to notice what really is, and strangely, you hold on tightly to what is unreal.

#### There is not one moment when that which you eternally are doesn’t exist. When there is only this one moment, it means that you yourself are this one timeless moment. You don’t have to invent anything new to be yourself. Here and Now is not something which you hadn’t been before or will be one day in the future. Only the externally oriented intellect is capable of creating such a preposterous and illusory internal thought-structure. Thought is the essential component of the intellect. Its home is the physical body. As soon as it dies, the intellect, the capacity of understanding intellectually, and all that is intellectually understood dies as well. They dissolve into nothing. As long as you inhabit the body, be alertly conscious that you are neither this vessel within the world nor the intellect. Those things are added with the birth of the body, and identification with the body consolidates and activates them. The body and the intellect do not know you. You know them and ceaselessly breathe life into them with your ‘I-am’ ego trip, giving nourishment to the not-self.

#### Don’t allow yourself to be controlled by your thoughts and preconceptions. Although they are there, they are never you. Thoughts and preconceptions arise within you, in your consciousness, and never exist separate or outside of you. It is of greatest importance to recognize this. You are the consequences of the causes you yourself have set in motion, and your outer life reflects the state of your I-consciousness. This dynamic is generated by the superimposed not-self against the background of eternal, pre-worldly being.

#### Why the world is the way it is, no one knows. And indeed, the essence of our eternal presence is impossible to explain. What one can understand diverts from eternal being. What one recognizes as unreal within the real exposes superimposed ‘I-am’-consciousness and dissolves it. Discover where the perception of an ‘inner’ and an ‘outer’ arise within you. At this intersection you can see through, overtake, and transcend both.

#### Awakening occurs in silence, in thorough Not-Doing, without any willful striving. It is as if one opens a window in the bathroom and fresh cool air streams into the room. The dampness which hangs over the mirror like a dark mist gently dissolves by itself. The mirror knows nothing of the dark mist,

#### and fresh air knows nothing about the evaporating process. Recognize how thoughts arise from the intellect and claim their

#### importance. The world is that which you perceived through your senses and your body and is what you experience in the external world. But neither the body nor the intellect nor the material world can be that which you are, prior to the world and in all eternity.

#### The landscape painting of an artist can never be the artist himself, even though it was done by and through the artist. The painting is never the artist, and the artist is never the painting.

#### Even when the world is moving all around you, stay still! Remain silent and don’t allow yourself to be swept away by ambiguities and untruths! All objects, including the body and your surroundings, exist internally and expand from one single energy, from the same single consciousness. Nothing can exist independent or separate from this one power. The world around you shifts constantly. Your true being never does.

#### You have the possibility to think about anything and everything. The outer world in which you live is actually your own thought-construct. You can think of anything and everything except about yourself. That which you are eternally truly escapes all thoughts. Don’t give attention to ‘I-am’ and the unsettling thoughts and feelings that are derived from it! Always be aware of the luminous essence inside you, behind all appearances.

#### Thought creates many diverse spheres of life. Each sphere has its own dynamic and its own particular thought-pattern. But these patterns are all mental and are therefore imaginary. The perspective gained from being aware of your true presence is crucial. Listen within; listen into yourself, listen into and through all the layers of your inner world! Discover the Great Silence, your true being!

### The Flow Prior to Thought

#### No one knows exactly what the substance of thoughts really is. Things imagined or contemplated can be objectified and known. That which makes thought possible cannot be known nor objectified.

#### Thoughts transport the information that is copied and stored in memory. Through moment-to-moment sensory stimulation and ensuing reflexes, this information is continually re-activated and processed within the brain. Thoughts are natural material, are fast as lightning, and usually function autonomously. They are indeed gaseous, or perhaps with an ultra-fine fluid consistency.

#### Everything you have thought up for yourself consists exclusively of the ephemeral world of appearances established through complex chemical processes within the brain. With incredible swiftness, reflexes and stimulation of the senses are collected into a unified world of experience. Bundled thoughts interact autonomously and take forms according to particular natural laws. Through this process, they slow down and become denser until they arrive as tangible forms in the external world. The externalized, projected thought-world is still not separate or anything other than the internalized world, for the internal becomes the external, and the external becomes the internal. Inside and outside condition each other.

#### Your vaporous thoughts are condensed, which allows them to be perceived and experienced. That which is perceptible is mirrored within the brain and processed by the intellect. But the manifestation itself is nothing but a play of shadows within the light of spirit. Playing with shadows is always a restricted and limited game. The original light is eternal and everlasting. It is your true spiritual home!

#### The individual spiritual search and all the striving involved with it leads ultimately to the inexhaustible revitalization and reinforcement of the ongoing, ever-changing shadow game of the spirit within consciousness. The ‘I’-person seeks an ‘I’-experience and ‘I’-fulfillment, and not true fulfillment in ‘I’-less being.

#### Everything that exists has its origin in eternal being. Although expressions seem to vary, everything abides in the one grand Mystery, out of which all divine powers express themselves. That which you eternally are permeates the entire universe. Your true being never stops at a particular time at a specific place. Your true being is formless, timeless, and without a place. Still, it is not nothing.

#### Why do you fear unlimited, empty, luminous space? Because it is inconceivable and beyond all description? Why do you fear yourself, your true eternal being? As long you content yourself and identify yourself with spiritual games within consciousness and constrict yourself in mental activities, you will continue to be pulled along and suffer from the phenomena which present themselves in your consciousness as life and death. Those who identify themselves with the external and impermanent diverge and suffer. They die and wander on and on from death to death. Something is conceived, is born, and then dies. Only that which you really are is deathless.

#### The pre-conception you have of what your life is can never be true and real, because the preconception was generated from ‘I’-ness in its inception, and ‘I’-ness is what senses and considers it to be something truly existing. But externally oriented consciousness is nothing but an erratic flickering shadow within heavenly light. God knows nothing of the shadow, and the shadow knows nothing of God.

#### This dynamic, egocentric shadow-energy is restless and has one primary characteristic: it constantly seeks its own gratification and comfort. Ego-energy is equipped with desires, cravings, and compulsions, and is pacified by them. These shadowy energies plague human beings throughout their lives and drive them away from eternal being.

#### Your perception of the world is always influenced by your memories and is dependent on them. No matter what you see, know, and name, you are always isolated and alone in your perspective. This means, you always see your own world and your own world only. You think about your world inside your world.

#### In order to see, you require things you can see: objects which inhabit the same space you do. Your body is only one of countless living beings which inhabit the vast world. Just what this gigantic container actually is, no one knows. Empty space cannot be seen or objectified, but it’s there.

#### Whether these objects, bodies, and things which appear and disappear

#### again in wakeful consciousness really and truly exist is certainly questionable. Without consciousness, neither time nor space exist, nor do those things which are perceived in space. All bodies are consciousness and are consciously perceived within consciousness. So, be what you eternally are without being or becoming something. Whatever you want to become, it will never be that which you really are. Recognize that the place you wanted to go inside is the place you already were before you started on the path to yourself. Unfortunately, misunderstandings and ambiguities have led you astray. But the kingdom of God is closer than your hands or feet; a blink of an eye and you are there, as Master Jesus proclaimed.

### In the Light of Spirit

#### Enlightenment is not a product of a subjectively understood truth. Enlightenment is not something that one can have or achieve through willful effort or the study of doctrines. Enlightenment is not an experience of illumination for the ‘I’.

#### Wisdom is never produced. One can neither want it nor learn it. But when the ‘I’ disappears, wisdom reveals itself. Wisdom comes from the divine and not from human beings. It shines in Not-I.

#### In deep detachment, ‘I-am’ flows back into Not-Being. Thus, the awakened one is at the disposal of the world and emanates a superior divine energy which gently extracts the visible, finite image of the world from the minds of human beings, so they can be guided to a radiant realm they have no knowledge of. In the silent presence of the awakened one, the hearts of people begin to glow and emit light. They become aware of their original divine being.

#### In all truth, you are the light that shines throughout the night. Life and death have no power over you. If you truly awaken, if you have penetrated once and for all and overcome the unreal within the real, then you are one with all that is and one with all that is Not-I. Blessed by eternal being which permeates all that you are, you take your place in the world and fill the space with divine light. The world has never touched you, never hurt you, and never traumatized you. Indeed, your true being has never entered the earthly realm.
