A master directory and index of formal guided meditation scripts, centering exercises, and practice instructions curated across the vault.

## 1. Teacher Certification Practice Scripts (MMTCP)
*Structured guided scripts with timing cues, pause markers, and somatic invitations adapted from the Mindfulness Meditation Teacher Certification Program (Jack Kornfield & Tara Brach):*

- **[[Arriving Where We Are]]** *(10 minutes)*  
  *Core opening practice: establishing posture, arriving fully in the present moment, grounding in the seat, and checking in with the somatic weather of the body.*  
  *(See also: [[Guided Meditation - Arriving Where We Are]])*
- **[[Script_ BASIC MINDFULNESS PRACTICE_ BREATH]]** *(8 minutes)*  
  *Foundational breath awareness practice: releasing facial and shoulder tension, inviting a soft belly, and tracking natural inhalations and exhalations.*
- **[[Script_ BASIC MINDFULNESS PRACTICE_ BREATH AND BOD]]** *(7.5 minutes)*  
  *Integrated somatic sit: combining breath anchoring with wide-field bodily sensations, feeling from the inside out.*
- **[[Guided Meditation - Mindfulness of Emotions]]**  
  *Somatic inquiry for meeting emotional tension in the solar plexus, chest, and throat with gentle holding and non-interference.*
- **[[2.3 Guided Meditation Calling on Your Future Self - Tara Brach]]**  
  *Guided visualization connecting with one's future wise, compassionate, and awakened self.*
- **[[Opening Meditation Instructions - Christiana Wolf]]**  
  *Clear facilitator cues on establishing the group container, physical alignment, and alert ease.*
