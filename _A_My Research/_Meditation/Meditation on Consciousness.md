A brief meditation can help you to understand. 

After you read the next three sentences, look up from the book. 

Sit quietly and try to stop being aware. 
Don’t be conscious of any sounds, any sights, any sensations, or any thoughts. 
Try it. 

Immediately you will discover that you can’t do it. Sights, sounds, feelings, and thoughts continue to be known by consciousness. Sense how you cannot stop this conscious awareness. Notice how consciousness knows the whole variety of experiences without closing off to one in favor of another. This is the mirror-like nature of consciousness: reflective, luminous, untarnished, and peaceful.

Kornfield, Jack. The Wise Heart (p. 39). Random House Publishing Group. Kindle Edition.
