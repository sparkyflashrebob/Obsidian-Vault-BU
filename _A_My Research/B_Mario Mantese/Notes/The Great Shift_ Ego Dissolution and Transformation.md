The **Great Shift (Experiences and Transformation)** catalyzed by Mario Mantese (Master M) profoundly impacts the seeker by dismantling the conditioned egoic **personality** structure and dissolving deeply rooted **fears** and insecurities. This process is often sudden, intense, and universally effective, leading the individual from a state of internal turmoil to one of clarity, freedom, and grounded existence.

### 1. Dissolution of the Egoic Personality

The core of the Great Shift involves the annihilation of the false self, which Mario Mantese approaches with **"uncompromising, ego-shattering methods"** [1].

* **Penetration and Exposure:** M.M.'s presence acts as a powerful universal force [1] and is described as a light that penetrates deep beneath the surface of the individual. His gaze sinks into the eyes, penetrating **"through the personality, through one’s clothing, all the layers of skin, into hidden recesses, to the very core of a person"** [2]. This exposure reveals one's deep-seated illusions [3].
* **The Path of Fire:** The intensification of this work, particularly in the "Inner Circle," is likened to the **"Path of Fire"** [4, 5]. This "I-shattering light-force of energy" [5] causes the ego to dissolve, as **"Your egos are like cold water being heated and boiled. First it becomes steam. Then it’s gone"** [4]. The liberation work is defined as **"dissolving the forces of the ego"** [6].
* **Surfacing of Shadows and Resistance:** As the ego structure dissolves, **"dark shadows from the depths of my being"** surface, often **"with great ferocity"** [5]. These are the repressed elements that the student had previously sidestepped and avoided [5]. For one student, the difficulty of the work created inner chaos and fever [7]. For another, the exposure of old emotional and psychic sufferings led to a sense of **unbelievable rage, loneliness, and immense love, all swirling uncontrollably** [8].
* **Breaking the Fortress:** The personality is frequently symbolized as a **"fortress"** built by the self [9]. M.M. bores a small hole in this fortress, but the student must take responsibility for enlarging the hole and **"opening the gateway between yourself and the world"** [10]. The overall shift causes **"all the stiffness and resistance"** to melt [11], allowing the student to stop lying to and deceiving oneself [3, 12].

### 2. Elimination of Fears, Worries, and Insecurity

The transformative process systematically eradicates fears, which the sources identify as merely **"thought-constructs"** or **"shadows"** sustained by the ego [13-15].

* **Fears Melting Away:** Students report that after connecting with Mario, **"Fears have melted away; expectations have shrunk"** [16]. One student, plagued by paralyzing fear since her teenage years, found that M.M. casually inquired about it a year later, only for her to realize that **"the great 'angst' had disappeared"** without her even noticing [17]. Another realized his earlier patience in conflicts was based on **fear of unpleasant confrontation** [18].
* **Confronting the Fear of Death:** M.M. directly challenges the fear of death, explaining that it arises from **images and impressions lodged in the mind** rather than actual reality, and that the person is **"deathless"** [19-22]. The body is temporal, but **"the radiance of eternity permeates this sickly body"** [23].
* **Healing Insecurity and Trauma:** The transformation provides release from chronic insecurity and doubt. A reflexology therapist found that **"all my fears and insecurities about work disappeared"** as a result of M.M.'s instruction [24]. A former actor suffering panic attacks before a show was immediately reassured by M.M. that his **"nightmare would certainly not come true"** [25]. The immense light from M.M. leads the student **"out of my inner darkness into the eternal light"** [5].
* **Ending Self-Pity and Isolation:** The work actively dissolves internal states of misery. The experience of the light-force caused one individual to have a **"flood of tears"**, described as a **"deep purification"** [26, 27]. M.M.'s constant energy dissolves the seeker's **"sense of unbearable loneliness and isolation until it vanished completely"** [28]. Students are guided away from self-pity and encouraged to stand up and **"leave the old pains inside me behind"** [29-31].

### 3. Transformation of the Inner State

The resulting impact of the Great Shift is a radical change in the individual's inner foundation, moving them from a state defined by reactions and concepts to one defined by effortless being.

* **Clarity and Serenity:** Old obstacles are dissolved, allowing an **"unimaginable and unexplainable awakening in love and kindness"** [1]. Clarity emerges and brings serenity [32]. The transformation leads to **"a great sobriety and calm"** [33] and the feeling of having **"entered a great silence"** where **"Consciousness was present"** [34].
* **New Foundation:** The dissolution of egoic patterns leads to a renewed sense of **responsibility** and stability [29, 35]. For one student, the newly flowing light became the **"cornerstone of my existence,"** leading to **"a refined intuitive perception, a pure sensitivity"** [36]. The tightly bound energies lose their strength, and **"behavior becomes more lucid, loving, and sensitive"** [37].
* **Transformation as a Natural Process:** The spiritual shift is described as an inevitable and often gentle internal process, even when dealing with intense psychological content. M.M. explained that these purification reactions (*kriyas*) are a **"very normal experience"** [27]. The power of transformation is immense, yet sometimes occurs without the student being **"aware of when the shifts took place"** [20]. Ultimately, the result is that the individual, having transcended the illusionary self, is **"unburdened and unbound"** [38] and capable of experiencing life **"filled with beauty"** [39].
