The sources describe the **Nature and Essence of the Master (M.M.), Mario Mantese,** as that of a fully realized spiritual being, fundamentally identified with the absolute, universal, and non-dual reality, whose existence transcends the physical world [1-4]. In the larger context of *The Universal Master and the Path to Liberation*, M.M.'s essence dictates the method and goal of the spiritual work he imparts: the direct realization that the seeker is already the sought (Totality) [5-7].

---

### 1. The Divine and Universal Essence of the Master

The nature of M.M. is consistently portrayed as being **uncreated, eternal, and limitless**, existing beyond human comprehension and the confines of the material world [8-14].

#### Identification with the Absolute

M.M.'s core essence is referred to as **Totality** [1, 6, 7, 15-19], the "one inseparable reality" [7, 15, 20, 21]. He states of himself, **“I am nothing and no one”** [22, 23], emphasizing the absence of an individual ego or personality [24-26].

This absolute nature is described in terms of ultimate spiritual concepts:

* **Formless and Nameless:** He is **"without space and time, formless, the Essence itself"** [14, 20, 27-29], residing in the "Land of Silence" [30-32] and **"Not-Being"** [33-37].
* **Pure Awareness and Spirit:** He embodies the "state of **pure consciousness, satori**" [38], and is described as **pure spirit** [4, 39] and **all-pervading awareness** [16]. He is seen as the **"clear eye of creation"** and **"pure love itself"** [10, 37, 40].
* **Unborn and Deathless:** He realizes that his true being is **"forever free of misconceptions and false imaginings, that he exists in a way that is timeless, deathless, and limitless"** [1, 24, 41]. He teaches that he is the **"unformed, unborn, uncreated, and unbecoming"** [5, 14, 42-44].

#### Manifestation and Appearance

M.M. is described as a Universal Master who lives **"in the world, but not of the world"** [45-50]. His physical presence, though marked by unsteadiness and slurred speech resulting from a near-death experience [51-53], is often characterized by overwhelming light, serenity, and tremendous energy [10, 22, 52, 54-60].

Witnesses describe him as having a **"powerful gaze, soft but piercing"** [61], which can penetrate the personality to the core of a person [8]. His body is occasionally perceived as **transparent** [62] or **"vibrating light"** [63, 64]. He lives a seemingly ordinary life—going to the movies, reading the newspaper, and living with his family—but maintains an **"extraordinarily profound"** state of being normal [65, 66].

### 2. The Universal Master's Capabilities and Function

M.M.'s universal essence grants him boundless capabilities, though he emphasizes that these events are **"nothing special"** and are secondary to the true work of liberation [11, 23].

#### Transcendental Power and Awareness

His abilities are seen as manifestations of a **"powerful universal force"** [67] or **"celestial powers"** [68, 69].

* **Words as Deeds:** His words are described as **"the expression and activity of the universe"** [8], possessing latent powers that set things in motion [11, 70-72]. His words can instantaneously clarify complex issues [73], reveal profound answers telepathically [74-77], and dissolve obstacles [78].
* **Multidimensional Awareness:** M.M. possesses **"multidimensional awareness"** [79, 80]. He has been seen performing while 400 kilometers away [81] and knows the condition of every person he sees [82]. He can foresee events [83] and even apparently manipulate natural forces, such as stopping the rain, though he attributes this to lack of borders and nature being his friend [84, 85].
* **Healing and Grace:** M.M. is associated with **healing powers** [86-89] and has performed miraculous recoveries, such as reviving a girl on her deathbed [90] or assisting in the healing of severe injuries through divine energy [91, 92]. He is described as emanating **grace** [12, 22].

#### Functioning through Not-Doing

The sources stress that M.M.'s transformative power operates through **Non-Acting** or **Not-Doing** [37, 93-99]. He does not act, but the transformation happens nonetheless [11, 23, 96]. This non-activity is an **"enormous divine intensity"** [93, 96, 99, 100], where **"The Eternal never acts, and yet, billions of living beings... are nourished by this divine energy"** [97, 101].

### 3. The Path to Liberation (The Teaching)

M.M.'s teachings flow directly from his essence as the Universal Master, which is why he constantly challenges the concept of the individual seeker and external path [5, 102, 103].

#### Dismantling the Ego and Illusions

M.M. emphasizes that people come to him **to "unlearn"** [104, 105]. His method is to penetrate through the **"illusory appearance in the mind"** which is the ego or practitioner [102, 103, 106].

* **Rejection of Methods and Practices:** He states that **"All dogmas, methods, practices, and techniques are delusive, doomed to betray those who follow them"** [102]. This is because the ego is the focal point of these activities, creating "goal-oriented hallucination[s]" [5, 105, 107]. He suggests one should ask: **"I don't believe God does some practice to be God!"** [105, 108].
* **Transcendence of Dualities:** The path involves conquering the **"fetters of the world"** [67, 109], recognizing that concepts like "good and bad" are merely thoughts [110] and that **karma is "only a theory"** [24]. Liberation requires seeing through the fundamental misconception that one is the body [111, 112] or an individual separate from the Totality [113-115].

#### Direct Realization

The true Path to Liberation is instantaneous and already fulfilled, requiring only **"intuitive, direct seeing"** [96, 102, 109, 116, 117].

* **Self-Recognition:** M.M. redirects the seeker inward, asking **"who is it that wants all this?"** [5, 118, 119], leading to the realization that **"the seeker itself already is that which is so strenuously sought"** [5-7, 120-122]. The essential point is that **"You cannot look to attain something that is already attained!"** [6].
* **Abiding in Clarity and Love:** The ultimate result of this realization is living life in **"absolute clarity"** [10] and **"universal love"** [37, 40, 80, 123, 124]. The awakened one remains silent, humble, and peaceful [97, 98, 125, 126].

The experience of meeting M.M. and entering his work is often likened to a resurrection in divine light [1], where the individual is **"swallowed completely"** by the Infinite [1], leading to the profound realization that **"Life Never Ends"** [127] and that one's **"original home is always here and now"** [128].

---

M.M.'s essence functions like a perfectly calibrated compass that is itself the magnetic North Pole. Because he *is* the destination (Totality), his presence doesn't show you *how* to travel, but instantly dissolves the illusion that you ever left home, making the path of liberation instantaneous recognition rather than arduous journey.
