/  The Way of Council

The Way of Council can be used in many different ways, ie, for sharing stories in order to find a deeper meaning, for grief to be witnessed, for the purpose of clearing or for coming to a decision. It is an ancient practice of sitting in circle and using a talking piece to listen and speak from the heart. There are 4 sacred agreements of council (see below) that create a safe container and from this container can arise deeper, more honest and compassionate communication. I believe it has the capacity for healing to happen and for greater understanding to take place. 
  It is because of the many earth-based people of the world, particularly the indigenous people of the US, who’s elders used to (a few still do) sit in circle for their own connections that we have the privilege to also sit in circle with each other in ways that matter. I wish to honor and acknowledge these people, most of whom had their traditions taken away from them, along with their land and most of the population. May I always offer these listening circles with this in heart and mind. I also wish to pay thanks to those who made it more accessible to the West, such as Joan Halifax, Jack Zimmerman and Gigi Coyle.

The 4 Agreements of Council are ~

Listen from the Heart

Share from the Heart

Be lean of word – try not to proliferate/speaking for the highest good

Be spontaneous – try not to plan what you’re going to say

The agreements create a safe space as does the talking piece. When we are holding the talking piece we know that we will not be interrupted, that we can be in silence, shed tears, or sing and that this will be listened to and witnessed by others in the circle. Everything is welcome.
