# The Lorica Prayer:

"Encircle us, Great Spirit, with your love.Enclose us with your comfort.Keep truth and healing within; keep despair and addiction out.Keep compassion within; keep hard-heartedness out.Keep light within; keep darkness out.May the solid granite beneath us sustain us.May the open sky fill us.Encircle us with your permanent presence." \[1\] (https://aquietwalk.com/category/circle-prayer/), \[2\] (https://christinesine.substack.com/p/spiritual-practice-writing-prayers), \[3\] (https://aquietwalk.com/2015/03/17/encircled-in-the-arms-of-prayer-prayerful-tuesday/)
