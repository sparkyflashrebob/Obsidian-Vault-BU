
Based on your notes, here are some strategies for dealing with difficulties such as trauma or pain:

1. Practice mindfulness: Bring mindful attention to your sensations and attitude towards pain or trauma. This can help dissolve identification with the suffering and bring awareness to the present moment.

2. Use resourcing: Move your attention to pleasant or neutral experiences, both internal and external, to create a sense of ease and safety. This can include focusing on positive memories or images.

3. Titrating and pendulating: Approach the intensity of pain or trauma in small increments, touching it briefly and then returning to a place of safety. This allows for gradual exposure and helps prevent overwhelm.

4. Cultivate a sense of space: Recognize that pain or trauma is just one aspect of your experience and that there is also space for other sensations and emotions. This can help shift your perspective and provide a sense of openness.

5. Offer loving-kindness: Practice compassion towards yourself and others who may be experiencing pain or trauma. This can help shift your relationship with suffering and promote healing.

6. Seek support: Consider seeking professional help or joining support groups to receive guidance and connect with others who may be going through similar experiences.

Remember, these strategies may vary in effectiveness for different individuals, so it's important to find what works best for you.
