> “It’s a normal reaction to a lot that goes on in our life,” says Corey. “Sadness and fear are normal. But sadness can become depression, and fear can become anxiety when it persists. Equally, when you stay in a state of languishing for too long, it becomes a very debilitating, dangerous thing, just like depression. I’m not trying to pathologise languishing. I’m simply saying, if you don’t listen to that empty silence in yourself and do something about it, it will get worse. Trust me, I’ve been there.”
