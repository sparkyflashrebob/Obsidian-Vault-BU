## 1. Key Pedagogical Vocabulary
* **`Upāyakosalla`**: Skill-in-means; the ability to adapt the Dhamma to the listener's mental faculty without distorting truth.
* **`Anupubbikathā`**: The step-by-step gradual teaching sequence (Generosity $\rightarrow$ Virtue $\rightarrow$ Renunciation $\rightarrow$ Four Noble Truths).
* **`Indriyaparopariyatta-ñāṇa`**: The Tathāgata's intuitive knowledge of the spiritual inclinations and faculties of others (*MN 12*).
* **`Atthuppatti`**: The specific historical, emotional, or situational origin that prompted a discourse.

---

## 2. Vocabulary of Concord & Community Harmony
* **`Samaggā`**: Living in harmonious, peaceful concord without factions or disputation.
* **`Khīrodakībhūtā`**: Blending together like milk and water poured into a single bowl (*MN 31*).
* **`Citta-parivattana`**: The conscious turning and relinquishment of personal self-will in favor of community harmony.
* **`Piyacakkhu`**: Regarding companions with gentle, kindly eyes, both in their open presence and in private absence.

---

## 3. Vocabulary of Obstacles & Thorns
* **`Upakkilesa`**: The eleven subtle mental corruptions of samādhi (*MN 128*).
* **`Āsava`**: Deep intoxicants/inflows sustaining rebirth (Sensuality, Becoming, Views, Ignorance).
* **`Kaṇṭaka`**: Sharp sensory thorns that disrupt meditation stages (*AN 10.72*).
* **`Avippaṭisāra`**: The state of blameless non-remorse arising from ethical purity.

---

## 4. Vocabulary of Liberation
* **`Nibbāna`**: Cool extinguishment of the fires of greed, hatred, and delusion.
* **`Āsavakkhaya`**: The total destruction and drying up of all mental outflows.
* **`Akuppā Cetovimutti`**: Unshakeable deliverance of the heart.
* **`Amatadhātu`**: The deathless, unconditioned dimension of non-arising.
