# 👥 Master Mind Map: Dramatis Personae (People & Interlocutors)

> *"Just as a skilled physician prescribes medicine according to the specific disease, even so the Tathāgata presents the Dhamma according to the specific spiritual faculties, inclinations, and temperaments of living beings."*  
> — ***Papañcasūdanī***

## 1. The Inner Monastic Circle
* **Venerable Sāriputta**: The Chief Disciple in wisdom (*paññā*); systemizer of the Dhamma (*Saccavibhaṅga Sutta*, MN 141; *Mahāhatthipadopama Sutta*, MN 28).
* **Venerable Mahā-Moggallāna**: Chief Disciple in psychic abilities (*iddhi*) and energy; master of the jhānas.
* **Venerable Mahākassapa**: Foremost in austere forest asceticism (*dhutaṅga*); leader of the First Buddhist Council following the Buddha's passing.
* **Venerable Ānanda**: Cousin and personal attendant for 25 years; remembered every sutta by heart; advocate for the ordination of women.
* **Venerable Anuruddha**: Foremost in the divine eye (*dibbacakkhu*); living in silent forest harmony with Nandiya and Kimbila (`[[Culagosinga_Sutta_MN_31_Living_in_Concord_Blending_Like_Milk_and_Water]]`).

---

## 2. Enlightened Nuns (*Therīs*)
* **Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī**: The Buddha's foster mother and maternal aunt who led 500 Shakyan women on foot to Vesālī to secure monastic ordination for women.
* **Khemā Therī**: Chief Nun in great wisdom; former royal queen whose vanity dissolved upon seeing a celestial maiden age and decay before her eyes.
* **Uppalavaṇṇā Therī**: Chief Nun in psychic powers; victim of assault who demonstrated that awakened consciousness cannot be soiled.
* **Paṭācārā Therī**: Lost her husband, two infant sons, parents, and brother within days; transformed catastrophic dissociation into arahantship.
* **Kisāgotamī Therī**: Traversed the city with her dead child seeking medicine; awakened through the Buddha's prescription to fetch a mustard seed from a house where no one had died.

---

## 3. Kings & Worldly Rulers
* **King Pasenadi of Kosala**: Lifelong friend who confessed that while his royal court was plagued by betrayal and lawsuits, the Buddha's Sangha was the only place he saw peace (*MN 89 Dhammacetiya*).
* **King Bimbisāra of Magadha**: Built the Veḷuvana Bamboo Grove; tragically starved in prison by his ambitious son Ajātasattu.
* **King Ajātasattu**: Tormented by guilt over murdering his father; sought refuge in the Buddha on a moonlit night (*DN 2 Sāmaññaphala*).

---

## 4. Seekers & Wanderers (*Paribbājakas*)
* **Vacchagotta**: The persistent intellectual who asked the 10 speculative questions; guided by the fire metaphor to ordain and attain liberation (*MN 71, 72, 73*).
* **Dīghanakha**: The nephew of Sāriputta who claimed *"Nothing pleases me"*; the Buddha replied: *"Does that view please you?"*
* **Saccaka the Nigaṇṭha-son**: Renowned philosopher humbled by the Buddha’s patient, unruffled debate presence (*MN 35, 36*).

---

## 5. Brahmins & Vedic Scholars
* **Assalāyana**: Pushed forward by 500 Brahmins at age 16 to defend caste purity; conceded that human biology, fire, and moral karma operate identically across all classes (*MN 93*).
* **Caṅkī**: Wealthy scholar who learned the crucial distinction between *preserving* truth (*"this is our tradition"*) and *discovering* truth through direct verification (*MN 95*).

---

## 6. Devout Lay Supporters
* **Anāthapiṇḍika**: Paid a field of gold coins to purchase Jetavana Grove; received on his deathbed the profound teaching on non-identification with sense fields (*MN 143*).
* **Visākhā (Migāramātā)**: Built the Eastern Park (*Pubbārāma*); trusted mediator of dispute settlements among the nuns.
* **Jīvaka Komārabhacca**: Abandoned royal physician who cured the Buddha and established the ethical principles of medical non-harming (*MN 55*).

---

## 7. Extremity, Trauma & Outcasts
* **Aṅgulimāla**: Feared mass murderer stopped by the Buddha’s grounded stillness; rehabilitated into a peaceful monastic enduring social retribution with total patience (*MN 86*).
* **Bāhiya Dārucīriya**: Wandered in bark clothes believing himself an arahant; achieved full liberation instantly upon hearing: *"In the seen, let there be only the seen."*
* **Sunīta the Scavenger**: Untouchable night-soil cleaner whose humility was met by the Buddha’s outstretched hand inviting him to ordain.
