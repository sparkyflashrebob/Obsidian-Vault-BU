### Significance of Indriyasamvara-sila

[Glossary](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/indriyasamvara-sila) [Concepts](about:invalid#zCSafez)
Navigation: [All concepts](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept) ... [Starts with I](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept?l=i) ... [In](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept?l=in)
Indriyasamvara-sila, according to Theravada, encompasses a strict self-restraint practice acknowledged by the King, focusing on guarding the senses to prevent defilements. It emphasizes maintaining mindfulness when perceiving stimuli and outlines ethical conduct through sense-control. This moral framework stresses the importance of restraining the senses to avoid lustful thoughts, aiming to cultivate mindfulness and ethical behavior. Overall, Indriyasamvara-sila serves as a guide for practitioners in their journey toward moral integrity and self-discipline.

#### Buddhist concept of 'Indriyasamvara-sila'

[Buddhism](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/concepts/) [Books](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/)
Indriyasamvara-sila in Buddhism emphasizes the restraint of senses to prevent lustful thoughts and defilements, promoting mindfulness and ethical conduct through self-restraint, as practiced and recognized by the Venerable and the King.
Significance in Theravada (major branch of Buddhism):
[Theravada](https://www.wisdomlib.org/theravada/concepts/) [Books](https://www.wisdomlib.org/theravada/)
From: [Maha Buddhavamsa—The Great Chronicle of Buddhas](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/the-great-chronicle-of-buddhas)
(1) The precepts concerning the control of one's senses, emphasizing the importance of mindfulness in maintaining ethical conduct. [\[1\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/the-great-chronicle-of-buddhas/d/doc365120.html) (2) The precept of restraining the senses, particularly in relation to avoiding lustful thoughts. [\[2\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/the-great-chronicle-of-buddhas/d/doc365122.html) (3) Guarding the senses and faculties to prevent the arising of defilements by maintaining mindfulness when perceiving stimuli. [\[3\]](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/book/the-great-chronicle-of-buddhas/d/doc365114.html) (4) A strict observance related to self-restraint that the Venerable practiced and was acknowledged by the King.
