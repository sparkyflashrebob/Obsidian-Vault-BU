### Significance of Indriyasamvara

[Glossary](https://www.wisdomlib.org/definition/indriyasamvara) [Concepts](about:invalid#zCSafez)
Navigation: [All concepts](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept) ... [Starts with I](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept?l=i) ... [In](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept?l=in)
Indriyasamvara, in Theravada Buddhism, is understood as moral discipline that emphasizes the control of the senses. This concept underscores the importance of managing one's sensory experiences and perceptions to cultivate mindfulness and ethical behavior. By practicing Indriyasamvara, individuals aim to achieve a greater sense of spiritual awareness and self-regulation, which contributes to their overall moral development and enhances their path toward enlightenment.
Synonyms: [Self-restraint](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/self-restraint), [Control of the senses](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/control-of-the-senses), [Moderation](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/moderation), [Self-discipline](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/self-discipline), [Sense control](https://www.wisdomlib.org/concept/sense-control)

#### Buddhist concept of 'Indriyasamvara'

[Buddhism](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/concepts/) [Books](https://www.wisdomlib.org/buddhism/)
Indriyasamvara in Buddhism signifies moral discipline through the regulation of the senses, emphasizing self-control and mindfulness as essential practices for spiritual development and ethical living.
