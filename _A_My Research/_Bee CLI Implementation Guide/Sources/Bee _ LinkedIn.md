  There’s a kind of noticing that used to only happen between people who knew each other deeply. “You’ve been quieter lately.” “You talk about work differently than you used to.” “You’re more like yourself when you’ve slept.” It’s the kind of thing a partner says. Or a sibling. Or a friend who’s known you long enough to see the shape of you, not just the surface. Grow is a feature inside Bee built around that idea. It does longitudinal pattern recognition across your Bee conversations and, with your permission, your calendar, email, and HealthKit. You choose what it watches: personal, work, family, health, wellness. All of them, or just the ones you care about. And then it tells you what it sees. Not what you said. How you’re changing. The shape of your attention over weeks. The way one part of your life is quietly affecting another. The thing you keep circling back to. Grow updates as you do, so the patterns it surfaces six months from now won’t be the ones it surfaces today. You won’t be the same person.
