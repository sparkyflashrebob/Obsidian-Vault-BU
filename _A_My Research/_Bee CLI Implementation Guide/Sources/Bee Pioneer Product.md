##### How do Bee's microphones work?

##### How do I charge Bee?

Bee charges via the USB-C port located on the back of the device. With its 7-day battery life, you won't need to charge frequently. A USB-C cable is **not** included in the box.

#### FAQ

##### About Bee

##### What is Bee?

Bee is the first personal AI that truly lives alongside you, building genuine understanding through continuous presence. With industry-leading battery life and advanced processing, it transforms how AI fits into your daily life.

##### How long does the battery last?

Bee offers an industry-first 7-day battery life (160+ hours), making continuous AI presence finally possible in your daily life.

##### Privacy & Security

##### How does Bee handle my voice recordings?

We never store audio recordings. All conversations are:
\* Processed in real-time
\* Immediately deleted after processing
\* Never saved or stored

##### What control do I have over my data?

You have complete control:
\* Delete anything at any time
\* All deletions are permanent and immediate
\* Integrate with our developer API
\* Access your data whenever you want

##### Will my data be used for AI training or sold?

Absolutely not. We commit to:
\* No AI model training with your data
\* No selling or monetizing your data
\* No sharing with third parties

##### What security measures are in place?

We maintain the highest security standards:
\* Regular third-party security audits
\* Industry-standard data protection
\* Encryption at rest and in transit
\* Privacy-first architecture
\* Continuous security monitoring

##### What are your future plans around privacy?

Our goal is to make Bee smarter without ever requiring access to your raw personal data. We're actively building toward that future through a set of clear, near- and long-term priorities:
\* Geo and Topic Fencing: In the future, users will be able to set boundaries around what Bee is allowed to learn. You'll have the ability to define topics that should never be processed, and designate physical locations where Bee will automatically pause learning.
\* Advanced on-device AI: We're testing next-generation local models that can run directly on device. These models are already being evaluated by trusted partners and early testers. We're not ready to release this broadly yet, but it's on our future roadmap.

##### Features & Capabilities

##### What can Bee help me with?

Bee enhances your daily life in numerous ways:
\* Creates summaries of important conversations and moments
\* Identifies patterns in your routines and relationships
\* Manages professional meetings and client interactions
\* Maintains perfect recall of your interactions
\* Provides meaningful insights about your day

##### How does Bee understand me so well in different environments?

Bee uses an advanced dual-microphone system with sophisticated noise filtering, ensuring perfect understanding whether you're in a quiet office or a busy construction site.

##### What languages does Bee support?

Bee supports 40 languages, making it accessible to users worldwide. It can understand and process conversations across multiple languages seamlessly.

##### What integrations are available with Bee?

Bee seamlessly connects with key Google services to help organize your digital life:
\* Gmail - manage and interact with your emails
\* Google Calendar - stay on top of your schedule and appointments
\* Google Contacts - easily access and manage your contacts
We're actively developing more integrations to expand Bee's capabilities - stay tuned for exciting updates!

##### Is there a subscription available for Bee?

In the future, Bee will have a Premium monthly subscription, which will unlock enhanced features like advanced search capabilities, access to specialized agents and deep personalization options. Details will be forthcoming.

##### Can multiple people use the same Bee?

Bee is designed as a personal AI that builds deep understanding of its primary user. It's optimized for single-user experience to maintain privacy and personalization.

##### Device Compatibility

##### Does Bee work with my Apple Watch?

Yes, Bee integrates seamlessly with Apple Watch, extending your personal AI experience to your wrist.

##### Can I use Bee on Android?

An early access build to Bee's Android app is available on the [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bee.android&pcampaignid=web_share). This includes a few core features such as summaries, suggested to-dos and the chat assistant, but is not actively being supported right now. We're committed to bringing the full Bee experience to Android over time and will share updates as development progresses.

##### How do I set up my Bee?

Setting up Bee is simple and takes just a few minutes. You'll be guided through:
\* Initial device pairing
\* Language preferences
\* Voice training during onboarding so your Bee can recognize you as the owner
\* Setting up your preferred notifications and integrations

##### How do I move Bee from one device to another?

Your Bee is tied to your account, not the operating system, so you can seamlessly transition between platforms. To move Bee to a new device, you'll have to disconnect it from the first device by rapidly pressing the action button five times until the light flashes blue-green.

Your Bee Pioneer comes with:

##### How do I wear Bee?

Bee is modular and can be worn in multiple ways:
\* As a bracelet using the included yellow or black bands

##### How do I charge Bee?

Bee charges via the USB-C port located on the back of the device. With its 7-day battery life, you won't need to charge frequently.

##### Is Bee waterproof?

Bee is water-resistant but not waterproof. While it can handle light exposure to water, you should not wear it while swimming, showering, or in any situation where it would be submerged in water.

##### What controls does Bee have?

Bee features a single button with multiple functions:
\* Short press: Mute/unmute
\* Long press: Activate walk-and-talk mode
