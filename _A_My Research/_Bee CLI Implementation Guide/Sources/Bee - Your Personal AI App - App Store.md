##### Good start with great potential

```
1.
2.
3.
4.
5. 11/29/2025 RAGusto Networking is a big part of my job role. I have been meeting with multiple people in many places (events, conferences, restaurants) with lots of background noise. Sometimes I am unable to catch everything the other person says. I tell people I'm wearing an AI device that helps transcribes what we are discussing and no one has objected. Bee's little microphone is the real amazing part of this app. Each night it summarizes each of my meetings and captures details I couldn't hear. Often I wear it on one of the lower pockets of a sport coat or a pocket on my pants, so it's not right in front of me or the other person. Its ability to accurately pick up conversations has been very impressive. And I can get three long days of work before I need to recharge using the convenient USB-C socket. It's important to note that Bee doesn't deliver an audio recording, only a transcription. I feel I have covered my ethics by telling people it's a transcription device not a recorder. My biggest complaint about Bee is I need to copy everything from my phone to my Mac to put it into my CRM or other files. I would love to see a way to access the summaries directly, but that may require transferring info through a cloud service which could compromise security. MORE
```
