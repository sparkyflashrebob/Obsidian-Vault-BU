#### Highlights & Notes

> The Visuddhimagga,

> This method is distinguished by its emphasis on the initial development of the meditative absorptions called jhāna, and the precise discernment of the ultimate realities of mind and matter.

> One who stops trains of thought As a shower settles a cloud of dust, With a mind that has quelled thoughts Attains in this life the state of peace. &mdash; THE ITIVUTTAKA1

> They may shift back and forth between concentration and mindfulness practices while endeavoring to overcome distraction, self-interest, and hindrances.

> The continuum of pleasant and painful practice experience is determined by how strongly our nature is disposed to lust, hatred, and delusion.

> And the sluggish-to-quick continuum is determined by the strength of the five controlling faculties of faith, energy and effort, mindfulness, concentration, and wisdom.
