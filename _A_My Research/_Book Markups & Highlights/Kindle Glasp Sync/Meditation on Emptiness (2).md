> Hearer

> Solitary Realizer

> Bodhisattva,

> Nāgārjuna&rsquo;s works teach that realization of the subtle emptiness of all phenomena is a prerequisite for the path of liberation from cyclic existence.

> As long as the aggregates are [mis]conceived,         So long is there [mis]conception of an I.

> One cannot free oneself from cyclic existence merely through cognition of the coarse selflessness of the person.

> One must

> realize the final subtle suchness of the person and of the mental and physical aggregates.

> One must eradicate the innate non-analytical intellect that misconceives the nature of the person and other phenomena.

> When selflessness is seen in objects,         The seeds of cyclic existence are destroyed.

> These [extreme conceptions] do not arise, just as there is no fire when there is no fuel.

> With one voice all the Mahāyāna masters proclaim that analysis of objects, and not mere withdrawal of the mind from them, is the path to liberation.

> It is very important to analyze again and again with discriminating wisdom.
