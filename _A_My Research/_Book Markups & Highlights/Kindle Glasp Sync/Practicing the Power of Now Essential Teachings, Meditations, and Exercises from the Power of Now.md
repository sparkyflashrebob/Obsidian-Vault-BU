#### Highlights & Notes

> When your consciousness is directed outward, mind and world arise. When it is directed inward, it realizes its own Source and returns home into the Unmanifested.

> Being can be felt, but it can never be understood mentally.

> To regain awareness of Being and to abide in that state of &ldquo;feeling-realization&rdquo; is enlightenment.
