#### Highlights & Notes

> staying present to my own experience,

> no matter

> how impossible it felt.

> I didn't need to transform my loss or look to a brighter future. I didn't need to leave the terrible reality of now. I only needed to stay right there, in the center of my own pain, listening.

> Finding Inner Courage is about that very practice. It describes a path to inner bravery, the quiet power of standing by yourself, with yourself, for yourself, in the depths of your own heart.

> It takes deep courage&mdash;and some skill&mdash;to look into your inner life, to listen to your own true experience. It takes deep courage to sit beside the stories you find there.

> life is an example, not an instruction.

> The original use of the word courage means to stand by one's core.

> living from the Center is what enables us to face whatever life has to offer.

> how to find our way to our core, to stand by our core, and to then sustain the practice of living from our core&mdash;to live out of our courage.
