## Metadata
- Author: [[Isabella Chase]]
- Full Title: 7 Traits of People Who Didn’t Receive Much Affection as a Child
- Category: #articles
- Summary: People who did not receive much affection as a child may exhibit certain traits in their adult lives. These traits include difficulty forming close relationships, being overly independent, constantly seeking approval, struggling with physical touch, feeling unworthy of love, being overly affectionate, and having difficulty expressing emotions. These traits can be a result of a lack of affection during childhood and can impact one's emotional well-being and relationships. However, recognizing these traits and practicing self-love can help individuals break the cycle and develop healthier habits and relationships. Seeking professional help or support from loved ones is also encouraged.
