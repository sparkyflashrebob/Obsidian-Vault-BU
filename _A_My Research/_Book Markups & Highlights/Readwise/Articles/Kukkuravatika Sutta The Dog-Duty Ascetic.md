- In this sutta the Buddha classifies kamma into four groups:
  1. dark with a dark result;
  2. bright with a bright result;
  3. dark and bright with a dark and bright result;
  4. neither dark nor bright with a neither dark nor bright result. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hbmh5p3ykyar5yxn3b76y9be))
- Dark (evil) kamma does not give a bright (happy) result, nor does bright (beneficial) kamma lead to dark (miserable) result. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hbmh61b1y2af33h0h6600rej))
- Kamma can be mixed, where an action is done with a variety of motives, some good, some evil. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hbmh68pxh1zjgw95cytfapm1))
- And that kind of kamma also exists which gives up attachment to and interest in the other three and so leads beyond the range of kamma. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hbmh6tc7jsh6jzr66xwnjqj2))
