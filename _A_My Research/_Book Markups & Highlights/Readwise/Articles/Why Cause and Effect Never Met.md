For if you place a seed in your hand, it will not turn into a sprout, nor can a sprout endure without ongoing conditions other than itself.  Innumerable factors are necessary for the sprouting of the seed, such as moisture, heat, oxygen, light or sometimes darkness, to name a few examples.
