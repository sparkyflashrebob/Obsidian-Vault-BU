## Metadata
- Author: [[Psychology Today]]
- Full Title: Understanding Conspicuous Consumption
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: The text discusses "conspicuous consumption," which refers to the spending of large sums of money on luxury goods as a means of displaying one's economic standing to others. The post also examines how patterns of conspicuous consumption vary among racial groups in the US, specifically with regards to spending on "visible goods," such as jewelry, clothing, and cars. The post discusses the reasons for why people might engage in such behavior, noting that people are typically interested in signaling their economic standing to others, especially in the context of sexual attraction and social status. The post concludes by examining how marginal value plays a role in how people engage in conspicuous consumption.
