- Document Note: According to Stephanie Harrison, a happiness and well-being expert, people have the superpower to make another person glow with small acts of kindness. She suggests nine phrases to instantly brighten someone's day, such as "I am proud of you," "I see your gifts," and "I love you as you are." These phrases can help build people's confidence, show gratitude and appreciation, and remind them of their purpose. These phrases can also boost people's self-efficacy and create a more positive cycle.
