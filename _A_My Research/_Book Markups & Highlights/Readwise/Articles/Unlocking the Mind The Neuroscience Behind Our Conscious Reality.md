([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130fw0qxx6bjyg22qdacjvn))
- This ability to toggle between conscious and unconscious states hinges on the complex interplay within a network of brain regions called the ascending reticular activating system (ARAS), which includes the thalamus and the brainstem. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130g4cy908v987db0avwwgf))
- Yet, simply being awake is not equivalent to being conscious. Take, for instance, conditions like ‘[locked-in syndrome](https://neurosciencenews.com/consciousness-disorders-21508/)‘ or ‘vegetative state,’ wherein individuals, though awake, may lack awareness. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130gj37ymkk21s9nkg29j78))
- This points to a higher level of consciousness – the conscious content, which includes our ability to perceive, think, and understand. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130gr5xfpv3hm7wg44xbd07))
- Global Neuronal Workspace Theory ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130h3m8nxqgq10ezfhs9efx))
- According to this theory, consciousness arises when information is globally available to multiple cognitive systems in the brain – a state achieved by a network of neurons, predominantly in the prefrontal cortex, broadcasting signals across various brain areas. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130hddqgngfwbawmet2k7bv))
- These ‘ignitions’ of widespread brain activity are thought to correlate with conscious perception. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130hfwvsw9rrynvg2vpj89z))
- Integrated Information Theory ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130hmshq452jc7q1sqzqr6f))
- posits that consciousness is a fundamental aspect of the universe, much like space and time. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130hxgyhdk6q8t8f265hsr1))
- According to this theory, any system – biological or artificial – that possesses a certain degree of integrated information, termed as ‘Phi,’ experiences consciousness. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h130j20b02qd2sgnq22cfyqj))
