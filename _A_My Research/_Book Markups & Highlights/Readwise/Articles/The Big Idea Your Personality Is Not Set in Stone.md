## Metadata
- Author: [[David Robson]]
- Full Title: The Big Idea: Your Personality Is Not Set in Stone
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: This document discusses recent research which suggests that people's personalities are not set in stone, and that with the right psychological strategies and enough effort, people can successfully mould their core traits into the shape they desire. It outlines the "big five" traits that comprise our most fundamental characteristics: extraversion, conscientiousness, agreeableness, neuroticism, and openness to experience. It also discusses how studies have shown that people can successfully change their personalities with regular activities that reflect the traits they wish to adopt, and how this can have positive effects on mental health. Finally, it encourages people to be aware of their potential for personality change and to take control of their own psychological resilience.
