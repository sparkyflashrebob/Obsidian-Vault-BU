- what if consciousness arises naturally as a result of our brains maximising their information content? In other words, what if consciousness is a side effect of our brain moving towards a state of entropy? ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hd372ncvp64hqg3gdjj6emjd))
- Entropy is basically the term used to describe the progression of a system from order to disorder. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hd372b5kwpg6pq0ygr8atchn))
- Picture an egg: when it's all perfectly separated into yolk and white, it has low entropy, but when you scramble it, it has high entropy - it's the most disordered it can be. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hd374ag7hxbbx2ye81jgj1vh))
- This is what many physicists believe is [happening to our Universe](https://www.sciencealert.com/human-consciousness-could-be-a-side-effect-of-entropy-study-suggests/scientists-propose-a-mirror-universe-where-time-moves-backwards). After the [Big Bang](https://www.sciencealert.com/big-bang), the Universe has gradually been moving from a state of low entropy to high entropy, and because the second law of [thermodynamics](https://www.sciencealert.com/thermodynamics) states that entropy can only increase in a system, it could explain why the [arrow of time only ever moves forwards](https://www.sciencealert.com/human-consciousness-could-be-a-side-effect-of-entropy-study-suggests/time-might-only-exist-in-your-head-say-physicists).
