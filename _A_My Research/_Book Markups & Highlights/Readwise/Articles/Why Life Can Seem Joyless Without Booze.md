# Why Life Can Seem Joyless Without Booze

![rw-book-cover](https://cdn2.psychologytoday.com/assets/styles/manual_crop_1_91_1_1528x800/public/teaser_image/blog_entry/2023-07/kid-ge73205542_1920.jpg?itok=NEiBqsHv)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Psychology Today]]
- Full Title: Why Life Can Seem Joyless Without Booze
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: Alcohol is often associated with joy but this idea is just an illusion the brain creates from habit, desire, and narrowed focus. Habits are automated sequences of actions and each habit consists of three parts: a cue, a routine, and a reward. Through repetition, these three parts create a habit loop that lessens the brain's participation. Desire is the fuel for this automation and it narrows the brain's attention to only one pursuit, blocking out other sources of fun. Drinking becomes a well-practiced habit for many and it narrows their vision to only one pursuit, trapping them in a cycle that leads to disappointment. To rediscover joy, it is important to recognize that alcohol is not the only source of pleasure.
