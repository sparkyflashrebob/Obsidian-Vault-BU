- *Ekaggatā* is identified within the Buddhist teachings as:
  • One of the [seven universal mental factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_factors_(Buddhism)#Seven_universal_mental_factors) within the [Theravada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theravada) [abhidharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhidharma) teachings.
  • One of the qualities associated with the second [jhāna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jh%C4%81na), according to the Pali Canon.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaggata#cite_note-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekaggata#cite_note-4)
  • Antidote to sensory desire (kāmacchanda) within the [five hindrances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_hindrances). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h4h6ychrxwdb9fftm19d1ggy))
