- Document Note: The article discusses the importance of identifying, processing, and expressing emotions, and how emotional suppression can lead to problems in personal and professional life. Emotions serve as an inner compass that helps individuals understand their needs and problems. Suppressing emotions for a long time can lead to physical and mental health issues. Acknowledging emotions and expressing them in a healthy way can lead to a better understanding of oneself, improved relationships, and better decision-making skills.
