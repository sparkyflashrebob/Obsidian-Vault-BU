([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk621w1kz3ngfrs8bgzkp16z))

As I've told many a client, "Don't should all over yourself." And, in some cases, that can be amended to "Don't should all over others." Even though certain beliefs may have been part of your family system and how you grew up, you can choose to live differently. - Mindfulness Practice for Letting Go of Shoulds and Expectations ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk623kn9rfbte3m5z4v5vttb))
- • Name one expectation that gets you upset. Is it when someone doesn’t text you right back, doesn't say “excuse me” after bumping into you, or doesn’t do his or her job as well as you? Or, maybe there’s a personal expectation like getting the best review at work or throwing that perfect “Martha Stewart” party. The list is endless.
  • Make an executive decision to let go of your expectation for an afternoon, an hour, or the entire day. You can always return to it later if you want.
  • Choose to adopt a stance of [openness](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/basics/openness) and acceptance toward this expectation. Think of it as your preference instead of a “should.”
  • Ask yourself, “Realistically, what’s the worst thing that could happen in the next five minutes if this expectation is not met?”
  • Respond differently. Choose a behavior that takes you off autopilot and lets you respond in a flexible and adaptable way.
  • Think of one benefit of loosening up on your expectations. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk623vbq4saj60gt17v38h6d))
