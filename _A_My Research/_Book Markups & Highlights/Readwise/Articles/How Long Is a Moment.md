- A moment of consciousness is the smallest unit by which we can “measure” ultimate reality. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h68bcah5czpg98bz9nwz0jwj))
- A single one of these blips is millions of times shorter than a two-second practice moment. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h68bcfa1f8gd2grh7cyc0zm0))
- These mind-moments are appearing and vanishing one by one all the time, whether or not we recognize them. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h68bcmpmyfn2rjbwpky1d7ef))
- It is possible for the meditator to progress from observing the first kind of moment to seeing the second. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h68bcw23zg7wtyesb5p6sts4))
