- Types of Meditation
  You are not limited to one way of meditating. You can [choose from a variety of meditations](https://www.verywellmind.com/different-meditation-techniques-for-relaxation-3144696) if you are seeking to begin a practice. Here is a list of [popular kinds of meditation](https://chopra.com/articles/explaining-the-difference-between-mindfulness-meditation):
  • **Breath-awareness meditation:** This involves your focus on different breathing techniques.
  • **Loving-kindness meditation:** This involves your focus on yourself and loved ones while thinking kind and caring thoughts
  • **Mantra-based meditation:** This involves chanting a word or phrase, aloud or in your mind.
  • **[Visualization](https://www.verywellmind.com/visualization-for-relaxation-2584112) meditation:** This involves using mental imagery for relaxation and to calm your mind.
  • **[Movement meditation](https://www.verywellmind.com/what-is-meditation-2795927#:~:text=Specific%20Meditation%20Types&text=Mantra%20meditation%3A%20This%20practice%20involves,around%20you%20during%20a%20walk.):** This involves focusing on body parts and movement as you take a walk.
  • **[Body-scan meditation](https://www.verywellmind.com/body-scan-meditation-why-and-how-3144782):** This involves scanning the body and noticing physical sensations.
  • **Focus meditation:** This involves focusing attention on a specific object, sound or your breathing. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01grp83yxrv1141v50r07gve39))

- How to Be Mindful Without Meditating
  You don’t have to sit in a lotus position or practice any of the various forms of meditation we mentioned in order to be mindful. If you have no interest in meditating, there are other ways to embrace mindfulness. *[Deepak Chopra](https://chopra.com/articles/how-to-be-mindful-without-meditation) says, “*When you are consciously bringing your wandering mind back to the present moment,” you are developing mindfulness.  
  To practice mindfulness, start by noticing your everyday life through your senses:
  • [Savor](https://www.verywellmind.com/how-to-savor-the-moment-3144633) that delicious dark chocolate as you eat slowly.
  • Listen to the birds as you walk to your car.
  • Look at the sunset and appreciate it.
  • Feel how happy you are after hugging your partner. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01grp852738bbegqezjcjv56wr))

  • Slow down
  • Use your senses in your surroundings
