Studying the influence of the breathing upon the body involves more than just long and short breaths. Length was a convenient way to begin. Now, we should also notice speed (fast-slow) and texture (coarse-subtle). Texture, or quality, is the most important because it has the greatest influence on the calmness of the body. In this step, we will discover the kind of breathing that most effectively calms the body.
