Language barrier is perhaps the biggest challenge that many of us face while travelling abroad. For instance, if you don't know Taiwanese or Vietnamese and are travelling to Taiwan or Vietnam, communicating with local people may be a problem. This is where the Google Translate app comes in handy as it allows users to communicate in almost any language. You need an active data plan or international roaming plan to use the app. However, in case you do not have one, there is still a way to use the app. Like Google Maps, Google Translate too has an offline translation feature that allows users to download a certain language and use it without internet connectivity. Wondering how to use offline translation in Google Translate, follow our step-by-step guide:
**Things to keep in mind**
To use the offline translation feature with Google Translate, you will need to download the pack of the language you need to use at the place you are travelling to. Downloading language packs can take up some space on your phone's storage. You need Google account to download the language.
**Download Google Translate app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store**
Before your trip, make sure to download the Google Translate app on your smartphone or tablet from the Google Play Store (for Android) or the App Store (for iOS).
**Know the language of the country you are visiting**
Another important thing to know is the native language of the country you are travelling to.
**Download the language pack**
Open the Google Translate app and go to the settings. Look for the "Offline Translation" option and select it. From there, you can choose the languages you want to use offline. Tap on the download button next to each language to download the corresponding language pack.
**Using offline translation**
Once you have downloaded the language packs, you can use Google Translate without an internet connection. Simply, open the app and select the languages you want to translate to. You can type in text or use the camera feature to scan and translate written text. The app will process the translation locally on your device, making it available even when you're offline.
