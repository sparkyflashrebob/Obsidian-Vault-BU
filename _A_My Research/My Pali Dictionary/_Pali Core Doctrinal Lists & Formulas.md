# 📊 Pāli Core Doctrinal Lists & Canonical Formulas
### *The Foundational Numerical Matrices of Early Buddhism*

---

## 1. The Four Noble Truths *(Cattāri Ariyasaccāni)*

| Truth | Pāli Term | Meaning | Duty / Task |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Suffering** | `Dukkha-sacca` | Unsatisfactoriness, friction, stress, impermanent conditioning | **To be fully understood** *(Pariññeyya)* |
| **2. Origin** | `Samudaya-sacca` | Craving (*Taṇhā*) for sensuality, becoming, and non-becoming | **To be abandoned** *(Pahātabba)* |
| **3. Cessation** | `Nirodha-sacca` | Extinguishment of craving and greed/hatred/delusion (*Nibbāna*) | **To be realized** *(Sacchikātabba)* |
| **4. Path** | `Magga-sacca` | The Noble Eightfold Middle Path leading to the end of suffering | **To be cultivated / developed** *(Bhāvetabba)* |

---

## 2. The Noble Eightfold Path *(Ariya Aṭṭhaṅgika Magga)*

| Division | Path Factor (Pāli) | English Translation | Practical Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wisdom** *(Paññā)* | `Sammā-diṭṭhi` | Right View / Perspective | Understanding the 4 Noble Truths, karma, and the 3 characteristics |
| | `Sammā-saṅkappa` | Right Intention / Resolve | Intentions of renunciation (*nekkhamma*), non-ill-will, and harmlessness |
| **Ethics** *(Sīla)* | `Sammā-vācā` | Right Speech | Refraining from false speech, divisive speech, harsh speech, and idle chatter |
| | `Sammā-kammanta` | Right Action | Refraining from killing, stealing, and sexual misconduct |
| | `Sammā-ājīva` | Right Livelihood | Earning a living in ways that do not harm other living beings |
| **Meditation** *(Samādhi)* | `Sammā-vāyāma` | Right Effort | Preventing unarisen unwholesome states, abandoning arisen ones; cultivating unarisen wholesome states, maintaining arisen ones |
| | `Sammā-sati` | Right Mindfulness | Establishing the Four Foundations of Mindfulness (*Satipaṭṭhāna*) |
| | `Sammā-samādhi` | Right Concentration | Unification of mind culminating in the four meditative absorptions (*Jhānas*) |

## 3. The Five Aggregates *(Pañcakkhandha)*

| Aggregate | Pāli Term | Nature | Key Reflection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Form** | `Rūpa` | Physical body, four elements (earth, water, fire, wind), physical organs | *'This body is not me, not mine, not my self.'* |
| **2. Feeling** | `Vedanā` | Pleasant (*sukha*), unpleasant (*dukkha*), or neutral (*adukkhamasukha*) tone | *'Feelings arise, endure briefly, and dissolve without an owner.'* |
| **3. Perception** | `Saññā` | Recognition, labeling, memory, cognitive identification | *'Perception labels experience like a mirage.'* |
| **4. Formations** | `Saṅkhārā` | Volitional thoughts, intentions, emotions, karmic impulses | *'Thoughts and emotions are conditioned visitors in the mind.'* |
| **5. Consciousness** | `Viññāṇa` | Bare cognitive awareness at the 6 sense doors | *'Consciousness arises dependent on conditions.'* |

---

## 4. The Twelve Links of Dependent Origination *(Paṭiccasamuppāda)*

1. **Ignorance** (`Avijjā`) ➔
2. **Volitional Formations** (`Saṅkhārā`) ➔
3. **Consciousness** (`Viññāṇa`) ➔
4. **Name and Form / Mind & Matter** (`Nāmarūpa`) ➔
5. **Six Sense Bases** (`Saḷāyatana`) ➔
6. **Contact** (`Phassa`) ➔
7. **Feeling Tone** (`Vedanā`) ➔
8. **Craving / Thirst** (`Taṇhā`) ➔ *(Key point of mindfulness intervention)*
9. **Clinging / Grasping** (`Upādāna`) ➔
10. **Becoming / Existence** (`Bhava`) ➔
11. **Birth** (`Jāti`) ➔
12. **Aging, Death, Sorrow, Grief, & Despair** (`Jarāmaraṇa-soka-parideva-dukkha`)

## 5. The Five Hindrances & Their Specific Antidotes *(Pañca Nīvaraṇā)*

| Hindrance | Pāli Term | Simile of Water | Antidote & Remedy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Sensual Desire** | `Kāmacchanda` | Water mixed with bright dye | Contemplation of impermanence (*anicca*), body parts (*asubha*), contentment (*santuṭṭhi*) |
| **2. Ill-Will / Anger** | `Vyāpāda` | Boiling, turbulent water | Loving-kindness meditation (*mettā*), compassion (*karuṇā*), patient endurance (*khanti*) |
| **3. Sloth & Torpor** | `Thīna-middha` | Water covered in dense algae | Arousing energy (*viriya*), walking meditation (*caṅkama*), light visualization, splashing face |
| **4. Restlessness & Worry** | `Uddhacca-kukkucca` | Water whipped by wind | Steadying awareness on the breath (*samatha*), relaxing physical tension, forgiveness |
| **5. Skeptical Doubt** | `Vicikicchā` | Muddy water in deep darkness | Investigation of Dhamma (*dhammavicaya*), study with wise friends (*kalyāṇamitta*), practice |

## 7. The Four Sublime Abodes *(Brahmavihāra)*

| Virtue | Pāli Term | Core Radiance | Near Enemy (Impostor) | Far Enemy (Opposite) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Loving-Kindness** | `Mettā` | *'May all beings be well, happy, and peaceful.'* | Conditional affection / selfish attachment (*rāga*) | Hatred, anger, aversion (*dosa*) |
| **2. Compassion** | `Karuṇā` | *'May all beings in pain be free from suffering.'* | Pity / condescension | Cruelty, malice (*vihiṁsā*) |
| **3. Sympathetic Joy** | `Muditā` | *'May your happiness and good fortune never diminish.'* | Vicarious excitement / hypocrisy | Jealousy, envy (*issā*) |
| **4. Equanimity** | `Upekkhā` | *'All beings are owners of their karma.'* | Cold indifference / apathy | Craving & aversion (*rāga-dosa*) |

---

## 8. The Ten Perfections *(Pāramī)*

1. **Dāna** — Generosity & open-handed giving
2. **Sīla** — Virtue, ethical discipline, and harmlessness
3. **Nekkhamma** — Renunciation, simplicity, and letting go
4. **Paññā** — Wisdom, discernment, and direct insight
5. **Viriya** — Energy, vigor, and persistent courage
6. **Khanti** — Patient endurance, tolerance, and forbearance
7. **Sacca** — Truthfulness, honesty, and integrity
8. **Adhiṭṭhāna** — Resolute determination and unshakeable resolve
9. **Mettā** — Boundless loving-kindness and universal goodwill
10. **Upekkhā** — Serene equanimity, balance, and impartiality
