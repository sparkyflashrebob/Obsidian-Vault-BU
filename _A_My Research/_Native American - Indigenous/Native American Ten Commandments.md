The Native American 10 Commandments are a [[list of things to do]], rather than things not to do, and include:

- Care for the Earth, which is our Mother
- Honor all relations
- Open your heart and soul to the Great Spirit
- Treat all beings with respect, as all life is sacred
- Take only what is needed from the Earth
- Do what is necessary for the good of all
- Give constant thanks to the Great Spirit for each day
- Speak the truth, but only for the good of others
- Follow the rhythms of nature
- Enjoy life's journey, but leave no tracks
