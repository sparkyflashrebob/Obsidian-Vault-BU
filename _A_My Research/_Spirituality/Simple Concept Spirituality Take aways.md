# Stoicism
- logos, rational order to everything
- interconnected system
- everything occurs as it should
- cannot successfully resist change or will ourselves against it
- must live according to nature "[[as it is]]"
- What we can and cannot control
- aspects we are active participants in
	- choices, evaluations, perceptiions, how we respond to and consider what happens around us.
	- attention, efforts and concerns should focus on our inner domain
		- use our reason
	- useful worries vs wasteful worries
- Seneca
	- unexpected things happen
- what aspects we are passengers and can do nothing about
# Buddhism
- the world is a constant flux of interconnected events
- this is also within our inner domain
	- our thoughts, feelings and states of being
- we are empty points of awareness filled up with the phenomena of the world, our body and our mind
- peace thru non-attachment
- accept conditions of nature
- no resistance
- eliminate desire to control, cling and perpetuate things
# Taoism
- natural way and order of the universe is out of our control
- beyond our linguistic and rational comprehension
- live well by living in harmony with it
- being graceful
- passive to world events
- simply observing
# Antifragility
- with our problems we learn to strengthen our ability to withstand the misfortunes of life
- withstand the prospect of them happening
- better able to process them when they happen
- better prepared for the next
