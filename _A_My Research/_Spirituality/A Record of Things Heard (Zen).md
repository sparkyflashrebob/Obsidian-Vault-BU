a. Not worship relics. Not cling to such activities  
b. veneration of relics develops merit and brings happiness, good karma.  
c. Not cling to precepts - wrong to make them paramount. They are good but something to grasp. d. For attainment of the way, devote all effort to zazen (meditation) alone is what is stated.  
e. Everyone carries out the functions of their own occupation. when not suitable for their position this is called "putting world into disorder".  
f. when reigning in accordance within the will of the heavens, this is called a "well-governed world".  
g. Not wish to lead an easy life. Early it may be helpful but later it will cause great harm.  
h. great peace and joy, which is never lost when attained, depends only upon have the aspiration to make this phantom-like body follow the will of the Buddha in this lifetime.  
i. we should abandon any self-centered views we now have and follow the Buddha's precepts.
