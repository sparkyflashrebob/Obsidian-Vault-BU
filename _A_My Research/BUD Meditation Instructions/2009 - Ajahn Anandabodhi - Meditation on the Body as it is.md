## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ajahn Anandabodhi.

* **Contemplative Domain**: Body Contemplation (*Kāyagatāsati*) — The 32 Parts of the Body (*Dvattiṃsākāra* / *Paṭikūla-manasikāra*).

* **Circumstances**: Delivered during the morning sitting session on Day 5 of the Angela Center October Retreat.

* **Condensed Version of the Practice**: Following evening discussions on wholesome and unwholesome states of mind, Ajahn Anandabodhi guides practitioners into the traditional contemplation of the body 'as it is'—moving beyond superficial cosmetic identification. We habitually become mesmerized by external appearances, building pride, conceit, lust, or self-loathing based on skin-deep judgments. She methodically directs attention into the bodily elements: hair of the head, hair of the body, nails, teeth (viewed directly as part of the internal skeleton), skin, flesh, sinews, bones, bone marrow, kidneys, heart, liver, diaphragm, spleen, lungs, intestines, and digestive tract. Feeling the actual physical heart pulsing in the chest and the stomach processing food cuts through cognitive vanity. By recognizing the biological, conditional nature of the organism, the grip of *sakkāya-diṭṭhi* (personality view) relaxes, leaving the mind peaceful, grounded, and clear.

## 🧘 Experiential Guided Meditation Script

### 1. Settling Beneath the Surface [00:00–05:30]

We settle into our sitting posture. Allowing the eyes to close gently, bringing attention inward. We notice how much energy society expends obsessing over the exterior surface of the body—how it looks, whether it fits conventional standards of beauty or youth.


We give ourselves permission to let go of that cosmetic burden. If our stomach is tight or knotted up, we invite it to soften, feeling the body simply as an organic, living biological process.


`[Pause 45s]`


### 2. Contemplating the Fivefold Base & Skeleton [05:30–14:20]

We contemplate hair of the head (*kesā*)... hair of the body (*lomā*)... nails (*nakhā*)... teeth (*dantā*)... skin (*taco*).


`[Pause 45s]`


Notice the teeth in the mouth. Feel them against the tongue. The teeth are really the exposed part of the skeleton. If we don't clean them for just a few days, their biological nature becomes immediately apparent. We look at the skeleton supporting the flesh—skull, spine, ribcage, pelvis, arm and leg bones. Just calcium and minerals borrowed from the earth.


`[Pause 60s]`


### 3. Internal Organs, The Pulsing Heart & Healing Freedom [14:20–25:06]

We move deeper into the cavity of the body. Feeling the physical heart beating rhythmically in the chest. Pumping blood day and night, without asking for our opinion or praise.


`[Silence 90s]`


Noticing the lungs expanding, the diaphragm moving, the digestive tract doing its humble, continuous work in the abdomen. We don't have to define ourselves by this outer shell. We see the body as it actually is: conditioned, mortal, unowned nature. Knowing this, the heart rests in serene freedom.


`[Long Bell]`
