## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ajahn Anandabodhi (Co-founder of Aloka Earth Room / Saranaloka Foundation, then monastic senior at Abhayagiri).

* **Contemplative Domain**: Body Contemplation (*Kāyagatāsati*) — Head-to-toe sensory sweeping, posture alignment, somatic presence, walking meditation pause, and dedication of blessings.

* **Circumstances**: Delivered as a morning guided meditation during the 10-day Abhayagiri monastic retreat at the Angela Center in October 2009.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Anandabodhi guides practitioners to establish a stable, relaxed foundation by resting into the support of gravity where the body meets the seat. Moving systematically from the crown of the head, through the face, neck, shoulders, chest, belly, back, legs, and feet, awareness gently investigates sensations without demanding they be pleasant or different. She bridges sitting with walking meditation, explaining why walking paths have a defined length: arriving at the turning point acts as a sacred pause to 're-collect' ourselves. Whenever the mind drifts into past or future, each breath and step is a pristine invitation to begin anew. The practice concludes by dedicating the gathered spiritual energy (*pattidāna*), recognizing that sharing blessings never depletes our reserves but causes goodness to multiply.

## 🧘 Experiential Guided Meditation Script

### 1. Grounding in Gravity & Posture [00:00–04:15]

We bring attention to the body as it is sitting here right now. Particularly noticing the contact of the body with the seat, allowing ourselves to fully rest into gravity. There is no need to hold ourselves up with tension.


`[Pause 30s]`


Feeling the base of the spine, the pelvis, the thighs resting down. Allowing the upper body to lengthen naturally without strain. If our stomach is tight or knotted up, we invite it to soften, letting the breath move unimpeded into the belly.


`[Pause 45s]`


### 2. Systematic Head-to-Toe Sweeping [04:15–16:30]

We bring awareness to the crown of the head. Softening the scalp, the forehead, the tiny muscles around the eyes. Letting the jaw unhinge and relax.


`[Pause 30s]`


Moving down into the throat and neck. Letting the shoulders drop away from the ears. Sweeping awareness down through the arms, elbows, forearms, wrists, palms, and fingertips, feeling warmth, tingling, or coolness.


`[Pause 60s]`


Returning to the chest. Feeling the gentle expansion and contraction with each breath. Noticing sensations entering the senses. Sweeping down the spine, vertebra by vertebra, relaxing the lower back.


`[Pause 45s]`


Sweeping through the hips, the left knee, lower leg, ankle, and foot. Then the right leg, knee, calf, ankle, and foot. Resting as the whole body breathing, grounded and awake.


`[Silence 2 min]`


### 3. The Sacred Pause & The Field of Blessings [16:30–22:10]

We remember the rhythm of walking meditation. We have a limited length on the path so that we are not fooled into thinking we are going somewhere. When we reach the end and stop, we re-collect ourselves. If the mind wandered where our feet were not going, we start again.


`[Pause 30s]`


Each breath, each step, each morning is a chance to start fresh. There is never any need to despair. Whatever wholesome qualities we cultivate, we open our hearts to share with all beings. Just in the very act of giving, goodness increases like an infinite field of blessings.


`[Long Bell]`
