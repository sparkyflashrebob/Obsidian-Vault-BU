## 🧘 Practice Orientation & Somatic Staging

* **Contemplative Domain**: **Open Awareness & Wisdom (Tilakkhaṇa / Emptiness)**
* **Setting the Physical Base**: Settle the body erect upon the cushion or chair, allowing the sit-bones to anchor firmly into gravity. Soften the jaw, let the shoulders melt away from the ears, and allow the abdominal wall to expand and contract naturally with the breath.
* **Establishing the Anchor**: Receptive, non-demanding presence holding the primary meditation object with gentle, unwavering attention.
* **Working with Hindrances**: When thoughts, restlessness, or physical tightness arise, meet them with spacious, forgiving awareness without judgment.
