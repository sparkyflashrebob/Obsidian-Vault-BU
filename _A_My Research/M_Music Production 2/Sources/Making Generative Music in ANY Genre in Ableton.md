Creating generative music in Ableton Live doesn't require complex coding or experimental setups; you can achieve it by using Follow Actions and MIDI manipulation within the Session View. Here is the workflow demonstrated in the video:

1. Generative Drums and Rhythmic Remixing (0:41 - 2:27)
   Slice your loops: Drag a drum loop into a clip slot, then duplicate it (Ctrl/Cmd + D). Adjust the loop region for each clip to isolate different fragments of the beat. Enable Follow Actions: Select all the clips, navigate to the Launch tab, ensure Loop is enabled, and set Follow Action to Any. This forces Ableton to jump randomly between your clips, effectively creating a real-time, generative remix of your drum loop. 2. Generative Bass and MIDI Manipulation (4:11 - 6:41)
   Randomize MIDI: Create a MIDI bassline, then use the Transform tool (specifically the Recombine or Shuffle function) to generate variations of your melody. Scale Locking: Enable Scale Mode (e.g., C Minor) to ensure all generated notes stay musical and hit the correct intervals, preventing clashing notes while the pattern evolves. Apply Follow Actions: Just like the drums, set Follow Actions to Any across your various MIDI bass clips to keep the bassline evolving dynamically. 3. Harmonic Generation (7:01 - 8:45)
   Diatonic Chords: Build chords (like a 7th chord) and use the Scale Highlight mode to move those voicings up or down the scale. This keeps your harmonies sounding professional and coherent while still allowing for generative experimentation. 4. Capturing the Composition (9:28 - 10:41)
   Record into Arrangement: Because these generative techniques are running in the Session View, you can hit the Record button and let the project run. Ableton will capture the evolving performance into the Arrangement View, allowing you to go back later, pick the segments you like best, and discard the rest. By following this process, you shift your workflow from "manually composing every note" to "curating and arranging generative ideas," which works across virtually any genre.
