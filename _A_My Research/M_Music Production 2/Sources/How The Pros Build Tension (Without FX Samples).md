D.Va and what I've done is really simple the riff stays in tune but I've automated only one of the oscillators to drift upwards in pitch so instead of adding a separate riser sound on another track you make the synth itself rise and that movement makes the tension feel organic like the synth is physically straining upwards as the track progresses it's basically a secret riser hidden inside the sound itself kind of like a tensionbuilding Trojan horse kind of anyway have a listen to how it feels in the track okay let's move on to tension trick number two this one is inspired by a short video that I shared a while back so here I've loaded the track's vocal in a sampler and I've looped this section if I play this with the metronome you can hear it's looping a completely irregular length
