### 0 Skip to Content Sebastopol - Stage Specs

Thanks for your interest in our music venue. We like to call it (the Abbey). We thrive to provide top-shelf talent to a top shelf market and try our hardest to review each and every performance inquiry. While we wish we could provide a stage that serves to bands and audiences of all different levels and styles, we to are a business that has a refined program that consists many variables. We hope to reach everyone at some point or another with our format, but like the old industry sane goes, if we like it, we will contact you. Thanks for checking us out.
