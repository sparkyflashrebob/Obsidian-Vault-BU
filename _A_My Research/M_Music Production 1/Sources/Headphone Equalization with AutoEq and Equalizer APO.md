For optimal headphone sound with Equalizer APO, the combination of AutoEq presets and the Peace UI is often recommended. AutoEq provides settings tailored to specific headphone models, aiming for a flat frequency response, while Peace offers a user-friendly interface for applying these settings and managing multiple EQ profiles.

Here's a more detailed breakdown:

1. AutoEq:

Purpose:

AutoEq is a project that generates equalizer settings (like those found on oratory1990's Reddit thread) to correct the frequency response of headphones.

How it works:

It uses measurements of headphones and targets a specific frequency response, often based on the Harman curve, to make headphones sound more neutral and accurate.

Finding your preset:

AutoEq's website or GitHub repository (or similar sources like oratory1990 on Reddit) often have presets available for download. You can search for your specific headphone model.

File format:

AutoEq presets are often available in different formats, including graphic EQ (which is compatible with Equalizer APO).

2. Equalizer APO:

Function:

Equalizer APO is a system-wide equalizer for Windows that can be used to apply EQ settings to your headphones.

Peace UI:

Peace is a graphical user interface (GUI) that makes Equalizer APO much easier to use. It allows you to manage multiple EQ profiles, adjust settings, and automate different configurations.

Installation:

You'll need to install Equalizer APO first, then install Peace as a plugin.

Applying presets:

Once Peace is installed, you can import the AutoEq presets (e.g., from the github repository) into Peace and apply them to your headphones.

Preamp gain:

When applying presets, pay attention to the preamp gain setting to avoid clipping, especially if the preset is designed to boost certain frequencies.

Tweaking:

After applying the preset, you can further fine-tune the settings to your personal preference.

3. VST Plugins (Optional):

Electri-Q:

This is a well-regarded free VST plugin that can be used within Equalizer APO to apply more advanced equalization.

RoughRider3:

A free compressor VST plugin from Audio Damage that can help manage the dynamic range of your audio.

Other VSTs:

Various other free and paid VST plugins can be used with Equalizer APO for different effects.

4. Example Workflow:

Download and install Equalizer APO and Peace UI .

Download your headphone's AutoEq preset .

Open Peace and load the preset .

Adjust preamp gain: if necessary.

Save the preset with a descriptive name .

Optionally, add and configure VST plugins .

Enjoy your improved headphone sound!

By combining AutoEq presets with the user-friendly Peace interface, you can achieve excellent results with Equalizer APO, tailored to your specific headphones.
