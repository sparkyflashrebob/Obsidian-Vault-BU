we've all heard grooves that flow in a way you can't quite put your finger on where something feels strange and hypnotic but somehow it still works it's probably using one of a few techniques that mess with how you perceive the rhythm or time signature let's break these tricks down so you can easily use them to create mesmerizing grooves in your own tracks let's say you have a simple four to the floor beat that has a time signature of 44 but you have a vocal sample you want to use that has a time signature of 34 or 68 a good starting point would be to nudge your straight eighth note high hats so they swing enough to act as a triplet that reinforces the 68 groove of the vocal now even though you're still in 44 you have two rhythmic frameworks to pull from where you can easily create poly rhythms that work well with one another but things get really interesting when you add more uncommon time signatures into the mix like this baseline in 58 you could start by applying that same swung groove to match the vocal but the hypnotic feel comes from embracing the chaos of these time signatures interacting notice how the baseline doesn't fit neatly over the beat this is called a polymeter which is two different time signatures running side by side and results in each bar feeling different even though the baseline isn't changing it might be a bit too chaotic at times so try using it in short bursts to create tension and release this next one is something we've all heard before when a melody feels locked into a groove but when the rest of the track provides context it
