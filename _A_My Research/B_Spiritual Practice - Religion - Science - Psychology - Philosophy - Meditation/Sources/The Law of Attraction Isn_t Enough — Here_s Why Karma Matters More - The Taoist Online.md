Understanding why attraction works for some and fails for others.

The Law of Attraction

Why are some people happy in their lives, and others seem to miss any moment of happiness? To find that, many turn to the Law of Attraction for answers, believing it to be a Holy Grail. But is that the whole picture?

The Law of Attraction has become such an important law that many consider it essential, like the law of gravity, the laws of thermodynamics, or the law of energy conservation. People accept the Law of Attraction as something they can’t live without. In the early morning, as they wake up, they begin to think positively about their lives: they’ll win the lottery, get a new car, find a job, and everything they want will be available at a discount wherever they go.

However, they forget that other laws — perhaps even stronger — influence our lives more deeply than this law. One such law is

the law of karma

Belief in Reincarnation but Forgetting Karma
