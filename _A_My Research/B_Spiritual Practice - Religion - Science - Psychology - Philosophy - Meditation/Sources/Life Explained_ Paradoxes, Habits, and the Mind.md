RESEARCH PAPERS

1/ Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Relational Frame Theory, and the Third Wave of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapies by Steven C. Hayes

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science...

Explains the theoretical foundations behind the "backwards law," showing how over-effort and attachment to control can paradoxically inhibit well-being.

2/ The Glymphatic System: A Novel Component of Fundamental Neurobiology by LM Hablitz, MA Nedergaard

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34526...

Describes the brain's waste clearance system during sleep, supporting the discussion of nightly brain maintenance and its impact on cognition.

3/ Acceptance and Commitment Therapy and Contextual Behavioral Science: Examining the Progress of a Distinctive Model of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapy

by Steven C. Hayes

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles...

This article provides a detailed analysis of ACT’s development, core psychological processes, and how acceptance, letting go of control, and non-attachment to outcomes promote psychological flexibility.

4/ Creating (False) Memories With Elizabeth Loftus, PhD by Elizabeth Loftus

https://www.psichi.org/page/214EyeSum...

Details experiments demonstrating memory’s malleability, where recollections can be altered or fabricated by suggestion.

5/ Psychology of Habit by Wendy Wood, Dennis Rünger

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26361...

Reviews research on habit formation through cue–routine–reward loops and the role of conscious and unconscious processes in everyday behavior.
