**MIndfulness**
* Having followed our negative moods for so long and having not cultivated mindfulness for so long, then our defilements, our negative habits of mind, they have incredible momentum behind them.
* In our practice, we really heavily emphasize mindfulness of the body and mindfulness of breathing.
* That's the whole point of mindfulness, of breathing, and mindfulness of the body is learning how to pay attention to the breath, learning how to pay attention to the posture of the body in a way that is actually affecting our emotions.
* It's a great example of mindfulness, of breathing.
* The practice of mindfulness, and mindfulness of breathing is learning how to pay attention to the breath and the body, the feelings in the body, the posture of the body, instead of getting lost in our thoughts, instead of becoming completely overwhelmed by the pain of the particular emotion, how to direct our attention to our breathing or the sensations, other sensations in the posture of the body, to kind of push back against the tendency of the mind to be shaken and to be overwhelmed by these things.
* This is something that we're practicing when we sit in meditation, but it's also something that we practice all of the time, developing mindfulness in all postures, whether we're standing or we're walking lying down.
* This allows us to develop a consistent habit of mindfulness and helps us to start to change the course of the mind from being overwhelmed and being pulled into our thinking and our emotions, to starting to be more and more present, being able to be at ease in the midst of things that normally we can't be at ease.
* There is the potential for incredible power in the mindfulness of the body.
