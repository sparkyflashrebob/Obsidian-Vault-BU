# 🌲 Abhayagiri Monastic Retreats — Comprehensive Master Index
### *Annual Thanksgiving Monastic Retreats (2008–2023)*
#### *Lineage of Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery & Wat Nong Pah Pong*

---

## 🏛️ Retreat Container Architecture

The annual Thanksgiving Monastic Retreat is an intensive 10-day cloistered practice container held at the **Angela Center** in Santa Rosa, California (and historically at Applegate). Each day is anchored by the monastic cadence of mindfulness, restraint, and contemplation:

* **Morning Pūjā & Meditation (05:30 AM)**: Daily morning liturgical chanting (as cataloged in the [[_A_My Research/Abhayagiri/Abhayagiri Chanting Book#Morning Chanting (Pubbaṇha Pūjā)|Abhayagiri Chanting Book]]), opening the day in reverence and stillness.
* **Morning Reflection & Instructions (08:30 AM)**: Meditation guidance, body contemplation, breath techniques, and skillful framing for the day's sitting and walking periods.
* **Meal Offering & Reflection (11:00 AM)**: Monastic reflection on food and the requisites (*Dhātu-paccavekkhaṇa*), cultivating contentment, gratitude, and physical harmlessness.
* **Afternoon Dhamma Teaching (01:30 PM)**: In-depth canonical expositions and practical applications of the Four Noble Truths, Dependent Origination, and the Eightfold Path.
* **Evening Q&A & Pūjā (07:00 PM)**: Practitioners' inquiries clarified by senior teachers, followed by evening chanting (rotating parittas and reflections).
* **Closing Session & Re-determination of Precepts**: Formal transition from the 8 monastic precepts back to the 5 precepts of daily life, sealed with the *Pattidāna* (Sharing of Blessings).
