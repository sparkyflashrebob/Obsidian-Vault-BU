* **Lead Guide**: Joseph Bobrow Roshi (Opening guided sitting).

* **Structure**: 20-minute immersive guided meditation followed by 25 minutes of granular experiential Q&A resolving meditation hurdles.

* **Condensed Version of the Practice**: The sitting begins by establishing a comfortable, dignified posture and setting a curious, receptive intention. Attention rests gently on the somatic sensations of breathing in the body. Bobrow Roshi guides attention to open outwards to sounds—noticing the boundary between 'internal sensations' and 'external sounds'—and invites that boundary to dissolve. In the subsequent Q&A, practitioners ask about getting caught between techniques (counting breaths, noting) and resting in open presence. Ajahn Amaro uses the nautical analogy: during stormy mental states, do not be too proud to drop the anchor (counting breaths or body sweeping); when the mind settles into calm, lift the anchor and rest in the boundless sky of presence.

## 🧘 Experiential Guided Meditation Script

### 1. Posture & Somatic Grounding [00:00–05:00]

We can find a comfortable, upright posture. There is no need to stiffen or force ourselves into rigidity. Let our spine be naturally tall, our shoulders dropping away from our ears, and our hands resting softly on our lap or knees. We set our intention to enjoy this sitting, bringing a warm curiosity to whatever is present.


`[Pause 30s]`


We notice where the body meets the chair or the cushion. Feeling the support of gravity. If our stomach is tight or knotted up, we invite it to soften, letting the breath move unimpeded into the belly.


`[Pause 45s]`


### 2. Establishing the Anchor: Breath & Body Sensations [05:00–10:28]

We allow attention to settle on the natural flow of the breath. We are not manufacturing deep breaths or controlling the rhythm. Simply knowing breathing in, knowing breathing out. Feeling the tingling, warmth, coolness, or pressure as sensations enter the body.


`[Pause 60s]`


### 3. Expanding to Sounds & Dissolving Boundaries [10:28–20:00]

Now we can allow the field of awareness to expand to sounds. Sounds near and sounds far away. Notice: where does the sound end, and where does hearing begin? Can we find a boundary between 'me listening in here' and 'the sound out there'?


`[Silence 2 min]`


We simply let sensations and sounds enter the senses freely, without clinging, without pushing away. Resting as the space of knowing in which all things arise and pass.


`[Long Bell]`
