## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speakers**: Ajahn Amaro and Joseph Bobrow Roshi.

* **Theme**: The inquiry of 'Here' as a living practice; freedom unthreatened by material form; mechanical meditation vs. alive knowing; the realization of 'Nothing Missing'; closing reflections, community gratitude, and universal dedication of merit.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: In this concluding session, retreatants explore how to maintain non-dual realization beyond the retreat hall. Ajahn Amaro introduces the contemplative inquiry into the word 'Here'—noticing how mind habitually projects life into a distant future or elsewhere, when in fact awareness is always right here. He warns against 'mechanical meditation': one can become a flawless, clockwork breath-watcher and yet never awaken. Bobrow Roshi reminds practitioners that non-duality is not an elite attainment; the entire universe lacks nothing. The seminar concludes with a heartfelt expression of mutual respect between the Theravāda and Zen lineages and a shared dedication of merit, offering the day's fruits for the benefit of all living beings.

## 🌿 Contemplative Reflection & Everyday Integration

1. **The Gift of Presence**: In our families, workplaces, and daily encounters, remember that the greatest gift we offer is not our opinions, advice, or busy productivity, but our calm, undivided presence.

2. **Inhabiting 'Here'**: Whenever feeling overwhelmed or rushed, pause, take a breath into the belly, and quietly say to yourself: *"Here."* Feel awareness settle into its natural center.

3. **Releasing the Feeling of Lack**: Notice the subtle story that 'I am not spiritually advanced enough.' We can rest in the truth that the ground of knowing lacks nothing.
