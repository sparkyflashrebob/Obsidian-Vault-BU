## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Joseph Bobrow Roshi (Roshi in the Sanbo Kyodan Zen lineage, founding director of Deep Streams Zen Institute, clinical psychologist/psychoanalyst).

* **Dialogue Partner**: Ajahn Amaro (Co-Abbot of Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery).

* **Theme**: Non-Duality in Zen: Koan Practice, Form and Emptiness, Interpenetration, Wallace Stevens, Dōgen (*Genjōkōan*), and the Embodiment of Non-Duality in Everyday Relating and Action.

* **Circumstances**: Delivered as the Zen keynote counterpart following Ajahn Amaro's opening Theravāda presentation at the 2007 IMC daylong seminar.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: In Zen, non-duality is neither an intellectual abstraction nor a permanent retreat into a blank or pristine void. Drawing upon the *Heart Sutra* (*Form is Emptiness, Emptiness is Form*), Master Dōgen's *Genjōkōan*, and the poetry of Wallace Stevens (*The Snow Man*), Bobrow Roshi illustrates that true realization is an ongoing dynamic interpenetration (*jijimuge*). When we dissolve the rigid boundaries of self and other, the myriad dharmas step forward to illuminate us. Zen warns against 'clinging to emptiness'—the trap of becoming an aloof 'spiritual person' detached from genuine feeling and relational vulnerability. Awakening proves itself in how warmly and honestly we meet sorrow, anger, joy, and our fellow humans.

* **Expanded Version of the Teachings**: Koan practice functions as an experiential crowbar to dislodge conceptual duality. A koan is not a riddle to solve with the calculating mind; it is an intimate demand to embody the reality beyond subject and object here and now. Bobrow Roshi bridges classic Chan/Zen literature (*Sandōkai*, *Zhengdao Ge*) with modern psychoanalytic understanding (Donald Winnicott). He emphasizes that healthy realization does not bypass personality, emotional wounding, or ethical relational responsibility. When form and emptiness interpenetrate, we do not erase differences; rather, equality and difference hold hands. We listen to the snow, beholding 'nothing that is not there, and the nothing that is.'

## 🌿 Contemplative Reflection & Everyday Integration

1. **Stepping Off the Peak**: Notice when spiritual practice becomes a subtle refuge to hide from difficult relationships or daily responsibilities. True non-duality does not hide; it softens our belly, opens our chest, and engages life directly.

2. **Embodying Interpenetration**: Throughout daily tasks—washing dishes, typing emails, listening to a friend—notice that form is not obstructing emptiness, and emptiness does not obliterate form. Everything enters the senses in pristine openness.

3. **Meeting What Arises Without Defense**: When uncomfortable emotions surface (anxiety, grief, agitation), we can hold them without needing to reify a permanent 'damaged self' or forcefully suppress them with 'spiritual detachment.'
