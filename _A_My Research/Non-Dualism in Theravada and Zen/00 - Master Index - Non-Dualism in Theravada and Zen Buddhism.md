# 🏛️ Non-Dualism in Theravāda and Zen Buddhism — Master Seminar Index & Compendium
### *Ajahn Amaro & Joseph Bobrow Roshi — Insight Meditation Center (2007)*

> *"When Theravāda meets Zen at their contemplative depths, sectarian caricatures evaporate. In the Pāli suttas, the Buddha teaches: 'In the seen, only the seen... you are neither here, nor beyond, nor between the two.' In Zen, Master Dōgen proclaims: 'To forget the self is to be actualized by myriad things.' Non-duality is not an esoteric philosophy—it is the direct, unentangled reality of this very moment when grasping ceases."* — **Ajahn Amaro & Joseph Bobrow Roshi**

## 🧭 Comparative Theravāda & Zen Non-Dual Concordance

| Doctrinal Dimension | Theravāda Canonical Expression (Ajahn Amaro) | Zen & Chan Expression (Joseph Bobrow Roshi) | Living Contemplative Synthesis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Ground of Awakening** | *Asaṅkhata-dhātu* (The Unconditioned Element); *Viññāṇaṃ anidassanaṃ* (Consciousness without feature) | *Buddha-nature* (*Busshō*); The Original Face before your parents were born | Timeless, uncreated awareness already present; not manufactured by practice |
| **Sense Perception** | *Bāhiya Sutta*: In the seen, only the seen; in the heard, only the heard | Dōgen's *Genjōkōan*: Being actualized by the myriad things | Collapse of the manufactured subject-object divide upon sensory contact |
| **Relinquishing Identity** | *Atammayatā* ('Not made of that'); *Anattā* (Not self) | Forgetting the self; Dropping body and mind (*Shinjin datsuraku*) | Refusal of awareness to congeal into a fixed, defensive ego structure |
| **Ethics & Causality** | *Idappaccayatā* (Conditionality); Right Effort without clinging | Baizhang's Fox (*Fu mei yingu* - unobscured by cause and effect) | Freedom is not license; non-duality acts with immaculate ethical sensitivity |
| **Daily Ordinariness** | *Dhammatā* (*Tamadah* in Thai - ordinary, natural, the nature of Dhamma) | 'Everyday mind is the Way' (Mazu / Nanquan); Chopping wood, carrying water | The sacred is fully found in mundane daily activities, dishes, and emails |

## 📜 Master Mettā Compendium Entries Harvested

During this 7-part seminar, the following four master dedication and mettā/karuṇā passages were identified and logged for **Phase 4: Master Mettā Phrases Compendium**:

1. **Part 1 [76:54–77:44] — Non-Dual Wholeness & Boundless Sufficiency**:

   > *"May our hearts rest in the deep recognition that nothing is lacking in the unconditioned peace of this moment. Free from the compulsive grasping of self and other, may all beings abide in the spacious, open radiant awareness that is the true home of boundless kindness, safety, and peace."*

2. **Part 3 [32:36–33:17] — Ethical Harmony & Freedom from Obscuration**:

   > *"May we walk in complete harmony with all conditioned things, unobscured by cause and effect. Free from the blindness of self-interest, may all our actions be an offering of healing, patience, and boundless goodwill to every living being."*

3. **Part 5 [41:48–43:29] — Vast Wholeness & Healing Acceptance**:

   > *"May we hold all of our human feelings, memories, and vulnerabilities with infinite tenderness, knowing they do not define our true nature. Resting in the vast, unconditioned ocean of awareness, may all hearts find complete peace, freedom, and deep healing."*

4. **Part 7 [51:14–51:48] — Universal Dedication of Presence & Practice**:

   > *"What we give most of all to one another is our presence. We dedicate whatever fruits do unfold from this day, whatever seeds have been planted in our hearts, that they may sprout, blossom, and bear fruit for the healing, peace, and ultimate liberation of all living beings without exception."*
