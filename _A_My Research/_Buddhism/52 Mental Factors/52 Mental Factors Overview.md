# The 52 Mental Factors (*Cetasikas*)

In the **Abhidhamma** (Buddhist psychology and phenomenology), consciousness never arises in isolation. Every conscious moment consists of **citta** (bare knowing / consciousness) accompanied by a specific constellation of **cetasikas** (mental factors) that perform specialized cognitive, emotional, and volitional tasks.

There are **52 Cetasikas** in total, categorized into four broad divisions:

```
                          [ 52 CETASIKAS ]
                                  │
         ┌────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┐
         ▼                        ▼                        ▼
[ 13 Ethically Variable ]  [ 14 Unwholesome ]       [ 25 Beautiful ]
 (Aññasamāna)               (Akusala)                (Sobhana)
   ├── 7 Universals           ├── 4 Universals         ├── 19 Universals
   └── 6 Occasionals          ├── 3 Greed Group        ├── 3 Abstinences
                              ├── 4 Hatred Group       ├── 2 Immeasurables
                              └── 3 Sloth/Torpor/Doubt └── 1 Wisdom
```

## 2. The Functional Relationship Between Citta and Cetasika

Ancient masters illustrate the relationship between *citta* and *cetasika* with the metaphor of **water and medicine**:
* **Citta (Consciousness)** is like pure, transparent water. Its sole function is the bare awareness of an object (*vijānana*).
* **Cetasikas (Mental Factors)** are like dyes or herbs mixed into the water. If you mix in bitter poison (greed, anger, delusion), the water becomes toxic. If you mix in healing sweet medicine (loving-kindness, mindfulness, wisdom), the water becomes medicinal.

### The Four Characteristics of Co-Arising (*Ekuppāda-nirodhā*)
A mental factor is defined as a cetasika because it shares four identical attributes with consciousness:
1. **Arises together with citta** (*ekuppāda*)
2. **Ceases together with citta** (*ekanirodha*)
3. **Takes the identical object as citta** (*ekālambana*)
4. **Shares the identical physical base as citta** (*ekavatthuka*)

## 3. Directory of Study Guides in this Folder

* **[[7 Universal Mental Factors]]** (*Sabbacittasādhāraṇa*):
  * Contact, Feeling, Perception, Volition, One-pointedness, Life Faculty, Attention.
* **[[6 Occasional Mental Factors]]** (*Pakiṇṇaka*):
  * Initial Application (*Vitakka*), Sustained Application (*Vicāra*), Decision (*Adhimokkha*), Energy (*Viriya*), Rapture (*Pīti*), Zeal (*Chanda*).
* **[[14 Unwholesome Mental Factors]]** (*Akusala Cetasikas*):
  * Delusion group, Greed group, Hatred group, and Sloth/Torpor/Doubt.
* **[[25 Beautiful Mental Factors]]** (*Sobhana Cetasikas*):
  * 19 Beautiful Universals, the 3 Abstinences (*Sīla*), 2 Immeasurables (*Karuṇā & Muditā*), and Wisdom (*Paññā*).
