5 Courses of Speech
- timely or untimely, 
- true or untrue, 
- gentle or harsh, 
- connected with good or with harm, 
- spoken with a mind of loving-kindness or with inner hate.

> ‘Our minds will remain unaffected, and we shall utter no evil words ; we shall abide compassionate for their welfare, with a mind of loving-kindness, without inner hate. We shall abide pervading that person with a mind imbued with loving-kindness, and starting with him, we shall abide pervading the all-encompassing world with a mind imbued with loving-kindness, abundant, exalted, immeasurable, without hostility and without ill will.’ - Buddha
