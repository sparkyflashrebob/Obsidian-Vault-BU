The number of senses humans have is a complicated topic, and the answer
may vary depending on how you define a sense: [1, 2]
## Five classical senses
The most commonly recognized senses are sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch. [3]
## Sixth sense
Some say that proprioception, the sense of body awareness, is a sixth sense. For example, when you close your eyes and touch your nose, youre using proprioception. [1, 3, 4]
## Other senses
Other senses include balance, temperature, pain, and the ability to perceive heat
(thermoception). [1, 3]
## Chemical senses
Taste and smell are chemical senses that detect chemicals in food and the environment. Pain and itch are also chemical senses that are activated by irritants or broken-apart cells. [5]
## Number of senses
Depending on how senses are classified, the number of senses can range from five to 21. This is because there is some overlap between senses, and some physiological experiences, like hunger and thirst, aren&#39;t included in the count. [1]
