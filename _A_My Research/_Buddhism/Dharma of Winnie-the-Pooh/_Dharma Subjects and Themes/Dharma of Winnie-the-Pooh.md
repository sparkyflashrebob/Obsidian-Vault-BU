Winn the Pooh was sitting under a tree -
he hears a buzzing noise -
buzzing and buzzing (looking around)
And he thinks
there is only one thing that makes this buzzing -
and that’s bees!
And, of course the next thought is,
well bees - there is only one thing that bees do -
they make honey!
And he thinks again,
there is only one good thing honey is good for -
and that is for me to eat it!.
