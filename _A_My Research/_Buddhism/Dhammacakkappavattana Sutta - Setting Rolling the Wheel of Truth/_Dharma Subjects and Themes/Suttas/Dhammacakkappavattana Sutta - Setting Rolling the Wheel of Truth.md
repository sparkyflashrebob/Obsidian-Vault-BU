Buddha addressed the bhikkhus of the group of five 
		Benares 
		Deer Park at Isipatana
			Resort of Seers
	Start by talking about the two extremes ought not be cultivated (by those who have gone forth (Thanissaro))
		A. devotion to indulgence of pleasure in the objects of sensual desire 
			inferior, low, vulgar, ignoble, and leads to no good 
		B. devotion to self-torment
			painful, ignoble, and leads to no good 
	The middle way he discovered* avoids both of these extremes 
		gives vision, knowledge, and it leads to peace, to direct acquaintance**, to discovery, to [[nibbana]]. 
	* Reflected back on him meditating under a tree observing his father*
