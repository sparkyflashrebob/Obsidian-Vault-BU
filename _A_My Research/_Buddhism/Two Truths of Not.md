Two-Truth Theory or Not
This talk draws from one of Thanissaro Bhikkhu’s writing on the Buddha as a strategist, “The
Mirror of Insight.
He starts with a story of “A meditator in Singapore once wrote a letter to Ajaan Fuang,
describing how he applied the Buddha’s teachings to everyday life: Whatever his mind focused
on, he would try to see it as inconstant, stressful, and not-self. Ajaan Fuang had me write a
letter in response, saying, “Do things ever say that they’re inconstant, stressful, and not-self?
They never say it, so don’t go faulting them that way. Focus on what labels them, for that’s
where the fault lies.” — “Awareness Itself”.

theory of two levels of truth does not appear in the Pali suttas, or discourses, our most reliable
records of the Buddha’s own words. It’s a later addition to the tradition.
