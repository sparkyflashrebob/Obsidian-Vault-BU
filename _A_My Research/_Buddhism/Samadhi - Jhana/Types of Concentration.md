[[Neighborhood Concentration]] is attained as soon as the Hindrances disappear. Freedom from Hindrances is the only condition. 
Unsteady - collapses and arises

Concentration cannot be shifted from the Counterpart sign to the Jhana Factors

Full Concentration, on the other hand, is attained only with the full arising of the Jhàna Factors, in particular One-pointedness.
