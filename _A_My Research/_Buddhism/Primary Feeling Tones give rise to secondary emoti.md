Pleasant can give rise to either unhealthy emotions such as grasping, jealousy or clinging or to healthy states of joy, ease and happiness.
Neutral can give rise to unhealthy secondary such as boredom, lethargy and being spaced out or to healthy states such as peace, ease and contentment
Painful can give rise to unhealthy states of aversion, judgement, rigidity and fear or healthy states of clarity, steadfastness and wisdom
