The video, 5 Things You Should Never Share | Buddhist Teachings for a Peaceful Life, discusses five things that should be kept to oneself according to Buddhist wisdom. These include:

1. **Inner peace:** True peace comes from within and should not be disturbed by the opinions or reactions of others. Sharing inner thoughts and emotions can expose one's inner world to the "changing winds" of others, leading to doubt, criticism, or jealousy.
2. **Negative thoughts:** Complaining, criticizing, or dwelling on negativity can burden both the speaker and the listener. Words have power and can wound, so it's important to choose words carefully and only speak what is true, necessary, and kind.
3. **Personal vulnerabilities:** Sharing weaknesses, fears, and past mistakes with the wrong people can lead to pain and judgment. It's important to be mindful of who you share your vulnerabilities with and to only entrust them to those who will treat them with care and compassion.
4. **Future plans:** Sharing future goals can create expectations and pressure, and can invite doubt, distractions, and negative energy. It's better to let your actions speak for themselves and allow success to surprise the world.
5. **Acts of kindness:** True kindness comes from a place of love and compassion, not a desire for recognition or praise. Performing good deeds for the sake of approval can diminish the true reward of kindness, which is the joy it brings within.

The video emphasizes the importance of silence and mindfulness in cultivating inner peace and living a fulfilling life. It encourages viewers to choose their words wisely, guard their peace, and share only what nurtures growth.
