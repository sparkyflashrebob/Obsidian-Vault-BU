# Seven Factors of Enlightenment (*Satta Bojjhaṅgā*)

In Buddhism, the **Seven Factors of Enlightenment** (Pali: *satta bojjhaṅgā* or *satta sambojjhaṅgā*; Sanskrit: *sapta bodhyaṅga*) are seven wholesome mental factors (*cetasikas*) whose cultivation and harmonious balance lead directly to awakening (*bodhi*):

1. **Mindfulness** (*sati*) — Recognizing and maintaining clear presence with physical and mental phenomena (*dhammas*).
2. **Investigation of Phenomena** (*dhamma-vicaya*) — Direct, discerning inquiry into the nature, causes, and qualities of mental and physical states.
3. **Energy / Effort** (*viriya*) — Steadfast vigor, heroic determination, and courageous persistence without agitation.
4. **Joy / Rapture** (*pīti*) — Refreshing delight and spiritual zest arising from wholesome engagement and seclusion.
5. **Tranquility / Relaxation** (*passaddhi*) — Deep pacification, composure, and lightness of both bodily feelings and mental states.
6. **Concentration** (*samādhi*) — Calm, unified, one-pointed stillness of mind.
7. **Equanimity** (*upekkhā*) — Impartial, lucid balance of mind; unshakeable presence free from craving (*rāga*) and aversion (*dosa*).
