The Ten Paramis (Theravada Tradition)

- **Generosity (_Dana_):** Giving things, time, or help without clinging.

- **Morality (_Sila_):** Living an ethical life and following proper conduct.

- **Renunciation (_Nekkhamma_):** Letting go of greed and worldly attachments.

- **Wisdom (_Panna_):** Seeing the true nature of reality.

- **Energy (_Viriya_):** Applying persistent effort and diligence.

- **Patience (_Khanti_):** Practicing tolerance and endurance through hard times.

- **Truthfulness (_Sacca_):** Being honest and acting with integrity.

- **Determination (_Adhitthana_):** Staying firm and resolute on the spiritual path.

- **Loving-kindness (_Metta_):** Showing goodwill and compassion to all beings.

- **Equanimity (_Upekkha_):** Maintaining mental balance and peace amidst life's changes. The Six Perfections (Mahayana Tradition)

- **Morality** (_Sila_)

- **Patience** (_Khanti_)

- **Effort** (_Viriya_)
