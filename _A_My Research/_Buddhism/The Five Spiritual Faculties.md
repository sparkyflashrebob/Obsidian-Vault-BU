# The Five Spiritual Faculties (*Pañca Indriya*)

The **Five Spiritual Faculties** (*Pañca Indriya*) are the foundational spiritual faculties that govern the mind along the path to liberation:

1. **Saddhā** — Faith, Trust, Conviction
2. **Viriya** — Energy, Effort, Diligence
3. **Sati** — Mindfulness, Recollection
4. **Samādhi** — Concentration, Unification of Mind
5. **Paññā** — Wisdom, Discernment

> When these five faculties are cultivated, strengthened, and become **unshakable by their opposites** (doubt, laziness, heedlessness, distraction, and ignorance), they are known as the **Five Spiritual Powers (*Pañca Bala*)**.
