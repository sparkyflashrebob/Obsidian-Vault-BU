- not understanding the [[eight worldly winds]]

[[Radical Responsibility]]
- take responsibility for me wanting things to be other than they are
- when I don't this is the source of my suffering and also the cause of suffering for others
- inevitable to blame others
- take responsibility for the dissatisfaction that we feel
- this is starting to built our [[practice]]
- something to work with
- "if our suffering came from outside then there is nothing we can do about it"
- can't just change the world
- can't change other people
- We can change ourselves
- Realize the source of our suffering is inside/from the heart
- Not from the outside
- it is the way we are reacting
- lack of contentment or malcontent
