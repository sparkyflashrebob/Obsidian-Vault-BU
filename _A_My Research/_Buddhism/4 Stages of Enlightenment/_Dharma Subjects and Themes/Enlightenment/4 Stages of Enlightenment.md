The **four stages of awakening** in [Early Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Buddhist_schools "Early Buddhist schools") and [Theravada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theravada "Theravada") are four progressive stages culminating in full [awakening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightenment_in_Buddhism "Enlightenment in Buddhism") (_[Bodhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhi "Bodhi")_) as an [Arahant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arhat "Arhat").

1. [Sotāpanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sot%C4%81panna "Sotāpanna") (stream-enterer)
2. [Sakadāgāmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakadagami "Sakadagami") (once-returner)
3. [Anāgāmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C4%81g%C4%81mi "Anāgāmi") (non-returner)
4. Arahant 

## Path and Fruit[[edit source](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Four_stages_of_awakening&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Path and Fruit")]

A "Stream-enterer" (_[Sotāpanna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sot%C4%81panna "Sotāpanna")_) is free from:

-   1. Identity view (Pali: __sakkāya-diṭṭhi__), the belief that there is an unchanging self or soul in the five impermanent [skandhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skandhas "Skandhas")[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_stages_of_awakening#cite_note-4)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four_stages_of_awakening#cite_note-5)
-   2. Attachment to rites and rituals
-   3. Doubt about the teachings

A "Once-returner" (_[Sakadāgāmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sakadagami "Sakadagami")_) has greatly attenuated:

-   4. Sensual desire
-   5. Ill will

A "Non-returner" (_[Anāgāmi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An%C4%81g%C4%81mi "Anāgāmi")_) is free from:

-   4. Sensual desire
-   5. Ill will

An _[Arahant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arahant "Arahant")_ is free from all of the five lower fetters and the five higher fetters, which are:

-   6. Attachment to the four [meditative absorptions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhy%C4%81na_in_Buddhism#The_r%C5%ABpa_jh%C4%81nas "Dhyāna in Buddhism"), which have form (_rupa jhana_)
-   7. Attachment to the four [formless absorptions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhy%C4%81na_in_Buddhism#The_ar%C5%ABpa_%C4%81yatanas "Dhyāna in Buddhism") (_ārupa jhana_)
-   8. Conceit
-   9. Restlessness
-   10. Ignorance
