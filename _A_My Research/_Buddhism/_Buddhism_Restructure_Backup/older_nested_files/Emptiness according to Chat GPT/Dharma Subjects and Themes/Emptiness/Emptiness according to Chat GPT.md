Emptiness. What is it? 

Emptiness is an emotional state of being without hope or purpose. It can be a feeling of inner void, a sense of being lost, alone, or isolated. It can also be a feeling of despair and hopelessness, of not belonging or having any meaning in life. Emptiness can be caused by different kinds of experiences or life events, such as the death of a loved one, the end of a relationship, the loss of a job or career, or other traumatic life events. It can also be an ongoing feeling that may have developed from coping with stress and other difficult emotions over time.
