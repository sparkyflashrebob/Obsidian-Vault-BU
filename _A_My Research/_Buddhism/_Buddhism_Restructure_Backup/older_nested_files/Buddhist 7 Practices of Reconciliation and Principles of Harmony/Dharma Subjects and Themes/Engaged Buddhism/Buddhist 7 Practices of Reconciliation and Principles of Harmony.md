Practices of Reconciliation
1. Sammukha-vinaya = face to face sitting
2. smriti-vinaya = remembrance
3. amudha-vinaya = nonstubborness
4. tatsvahhavaishiya-vinaya=voluntary confession
5. pratijnakaraka-vinaka=accepting the verdict
6. yadbhuyasikiya-vinaya=decision by consensus
7. trinaprastaraka-vinaya=covering mud with straw


Details of Reconciliation
1. stated for all with both sides present - no private conversations
2. everything that led to the conflict
3. expectation to resolve the conflict - stubbornness is negative and counterproductive
4. admit each's own transgressions. begin the process of reconciliation. encourage like in the other
5. not challenge the verdict. trust communities decision
6. after wholehearted effort to reach settlement
7. elder decision - respect - overcome petty concerns

Principles of Harmony
1. Sharing a common space such as a forest or home
2. Sharing the essential of daily life together
3. Observing the precepts together
4. Using only words that contribute to harmony, avoiding all words that can cause the community to break
5. Sharing insights and understanding together
6. Respecting others viewpoints and not forcing another to follow your own viewpoint
