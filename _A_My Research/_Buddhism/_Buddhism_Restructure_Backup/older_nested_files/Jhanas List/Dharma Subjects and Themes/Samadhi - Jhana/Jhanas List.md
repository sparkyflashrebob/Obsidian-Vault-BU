The nine levels of jhana are:

1.  Delightful Sensations
2.  Joy
3.  Contentment
4.  Utter peacefulness
5.  Infinity of space
6.  Infinity of consciousness
7.  No-thingness
8.  Neither perception nor non-perception
9.  Cessation

(from Anguttara Nikaya 9.36)
