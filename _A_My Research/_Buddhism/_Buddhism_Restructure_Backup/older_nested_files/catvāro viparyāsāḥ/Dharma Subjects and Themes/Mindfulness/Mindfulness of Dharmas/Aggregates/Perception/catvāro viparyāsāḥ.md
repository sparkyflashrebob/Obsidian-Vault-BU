The phrase "catvāro viparyāsāḥ" is a Sanskrit term that can be translated to "four inverted perceptions." It is a concept in Indian philosophy, specifically in Samkhya and Yoga, that refers to four common errors or misconceptions that people have about the nature of reality. These misconceptions are:

1.  Mistaking impermanence for permanence
2.  Mistaking suffering for happiness
3.  Mistaking the impure for the pure
4.  Mistaking the not-self for the self

These four inverted perceptions can lead to ignorance and suffering, and the aim of philosophical inquiry and spiritual practice is to correct them and see the true nature of reality.
