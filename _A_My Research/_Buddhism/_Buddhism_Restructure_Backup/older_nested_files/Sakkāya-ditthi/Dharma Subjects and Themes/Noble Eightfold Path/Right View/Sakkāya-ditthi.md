[[Sakkāyaditthi]]: 'personality-belief', is the first of the 10 mental chains [[_A_My Research/_Buddhism/_Dharma Subjects and Themes/Fetters 1/samyojana]] It is entirely abandoned only on reaching the path of Stream-winning [[sotāpatti-magga ariya-puggala]] 

There are 20 kinds of personality-belief, which are obtained by applying 4 types of that belief to each of the 5 groups of existence khandha : 
1-5 Are the belief to be identical with materiality, feeling, perception, mental constructions or consciousness; 
6-10 Are to be contained in them; 
11-15 Are to be independent of them; 
16-20 Are to be the owner of them 

M. 44; S. XXII. 1.
