Majjhina 9

# What is Right View? 
**When one understands the unwholesome and the root of the unwholesome, the wholesome and the root of the wholesome.**

Wholesome and Unwholesome

## What is Unwholesome?
		Killing living beings 
		Taking what is not given 
		Misconduct in sensual pleasures
		False speech
		Malicious speech
		Harsh speech
		Gossip
		Covetousness 
		Ill-will
		Wrong view

## What is the Root of the Unwholesome
	Greed
	Hate
	Delusion

## What is wholesome?
	**Absention from** 
		killing living beings 
		Taking what is not given 
		Misconduct in sensual pleasures
		False speech
		Malicious speech
		Harsh speech
		Gossip
		unCovetousness 
		non-Ill will
		Wrong view

## What is the Root of the Wholesome 
	Non-Greed
	Non-Hatred
	Non-Delusion

### Result of Understanding the Wholesome and the Unwholesome
	When understood the unwholesome and the root of the unwholesome, the wholesome and the root of the wholesome 
		One entirely abandons the underlying tendency 
			to lust
			to aversion
			to the view and conceit 'I am'
			abandoning [[ignorance]]
			arousing true knowledge
			here and now make an end of suffering

# Nutriment
Another way one may understand Right View
	Understands [[Nutriment]]
		the origin of Nutriment 
		the cessation of Nutriment 
		the way leading to the cessation of Nutriment 

## 4 Kinds of Nutriment 
	1. Physical Food
	2. Contact
	3. Mental Volition
	4. Consciousness

### Origin of Nutriment 
	with the Arising of craving there is the arising of nutriment 
	with the cessation of craving there is the arising of nutriment 

### Cessation of Nutriment 
	The Noble Eightfold Path
		[[Right View]] 
		[[Right Intention]]
		[[Right Speech]]
		[[Right Action]]
		[[Right Livlihood]]
		[[Right Effort]]
		[[Right Mindfulness]]
		[[Right Concentration]] 


### Result of the cessation of Nutriment 
Same as understanding the wholesome and the unwholesome
	abandons the tendency to greed, aversion, to the view and conceit 'I am'
	abandoning ignorance and arousing true knowledge 
	here and now makes an end to suffering
