        

**[[Seven Factors of Enlightenment]]**

- **Mindfulness** (_[sati](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindfulness "Mindfulness")_)
- **Investigation** (_[dhamma vicaya](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhamma_vicaya "Dhamma vicaya")_)
- **Energy** (_[viriya](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viriya "Viriya")_)
- **Joy** or **rapture** (_[piti](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piti "Piti")_)
- **Relaxation** or **tranquillity** _[passaddhi](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passaddhi "Passaddhi")_
- **Concentration** _[samadhi](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samadhi_%28Buddhism%29 "Samadhi (Buddhism)")_
- **Equanimity** _[upekkha](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upekkha "Upekkha")_
