IMPERMANENCE - NOTION OR INSIGHT
Concentration on Impermanence - we have a notion - ready to accept that things are impermanent - but that does not help us - may know intellectually but we can thing that we and our loved ones will be around for a long time and will be the same - everything is changing every moment - like a river - yet we still think of ourselves we were many years ago - if we are unable to touch this in the present moment we will not be able to touch the truth of impermanence

We use mind consciousness to touch the true nature of impermanence - we need concentration - to gain insight of impermanence rather than the notion - this will liberate us.
This notion of it can help us to bring about insight though. In the same way a match is not a flame but a match can bring about a flame. When you have a flame it will consume the match.
What we need is a flame and not a match. The insight into impermanence and not the notion.

NOTIONS VS PRACTICE
In order to make skillful use of these teachings we have to practice. We need to learn how to make skillful use of these teachings in order to bring about insight.
While we are sitting, walking, reading, drinking we use the practice of concentration on the nature of impermanence - this is the only way to touch the insight of impermanence. Concentration means to keep that awareness alive - moment to moment and to maintain it for a long time. In this way concentration brings us insight and liberation.

TNH - gives us the example of a couple fighting. They are both suffering. Using the practice of concentration on impermanence we can visualize your beloved 300 years from now. Gain insight to how the reality of impermanence will manifest.  With this we would realize that we have a limited time together and are wasting it with our anger. See thru the delusion of our being angry.
All it takes is one breath to see thru the delusion of anger - how unwise it is for us to hold onto it. With this insight into impermanence, we are free from our anger. When we open our eyes all we want is to hold onto the world with love.

Impermanence is one awareness.  It is not a doctrine, a theory, or a notion. It is an instrument - a concentration - a deep awareness -  There are other concentrations - NO-SELF -
