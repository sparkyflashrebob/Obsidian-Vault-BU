When you are ready gently close your eyes or lower your gaze, bringing your awareness to the here and now.   You might take a few deep relaxing breaths, while setting the intention to be intimately present for your life for the next 20 minutes. Putting aside your current worries and concerns. 
(short pause)
Starting to bring in, your attention to your body.  Being aware of the posture of the body.  Noticing the feet on the floor, you butt on the cushion or chair. Letting go of any obvious tension that you are able to, especially in the face, neck & shoulders.
Finally coming to a position that is both relaxed and erect. Not so relaxed so drift off nor too rigid so as to cause any unnecessary tension.
(pause)   
Having placed both the mind and body in position - bringing your awareness to what is present here and now - Allowing the breath to come to the forefront of your awareness. This process of being aware of how the body is breathing.
(pause)  
Noticing breathing in..... 
Noticing breathing out...
Noticing breathing in and breathing out...
Noticing the quality of the breathing... noticing breathing in long or breathing in short....
Noticing breathing in short or breathing out short. or noticing breathing in long and breathing out short or noticing breathing in long and breathing out long...
Noticing just breathing.....
(long pause)
Noticing deep breathing....breathing that fills the whole body.... allowing the whole body to breathe... 
(short pause)
Experiencing the whole body breathing..... experiencing the whole body becoming calm and tranquil...
Becoming aware of your awareness in the body.... aware of this knowing quality.  The one who knows......
(long pause until the end)
Taking note of this quality of knowing. Taking note of this quality of awareness of the body....
As we end this meditation session setting the intention to carry this awareness into the period that follows
