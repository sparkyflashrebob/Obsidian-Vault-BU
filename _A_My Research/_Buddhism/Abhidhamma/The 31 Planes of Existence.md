These 31 planes are divided into three types of worlds: 
1. Kàma-loka or kàmabhava (the sensuous world) — 11 planes 
2. Råpa-loka or råpabhava (the world of form / fine material world) — 16 planes 
3. Aråpa-loka or aråpabhava (the formless world / immaterial world)

## Kàma-loka (The Sensuous World) 
The sensuous world is finely divided into the following planes: 
a. Kàmaduggati 
b. Kàmasugati

### A. Kàmaduggati Bhåmi — The Four States of Deprivation (apàya) are as follows:
1. Niraya (Hell) - See Majjhima Nikaya 129 & 130. Temporary. States of being. Torment. Devoid of hapiness. Only suffering. Not being punished by anyone. Cause and Effect. Like grains of sand form a beach or birds of a feather flocking together. Evil beings reside with other evil beings. This same natural law applies to all realms.
2. Tiracchàna Yoni (Animals). All non-human forms of life. Behaving like an animal will get you here. Not fun. Working. Always in fear. House pets dependent on us.
3. Peta Loka (Hungry ghosts). Wander hopelessly searching for fulfillment. Big fat stomachs and tiny little mouths. Never satisfied, always hungry. If greedy and don't practise [[_A_My Research/Dharma Subjects and Themes/Paramis/dana]] then might become one of these. Ghosts are humans who have strong attachment to human existence or a place. Can't leave. Need metta.
4. Asura (Demons) Titans. Powerful and warlike. Conflict with themselves.

Unhappy Dukka Realms. No change to be good or do good.  Our metta includes those in this realm.

### B. Kàmasugati Bhumi - Human plane and six planes of heavenly gods.
5 thru 11
1. Manussa Loka (Human beings) You are here (for now). Rebirth as a human being extraordinarily rare.  Precious.  unique mix of pleasure and pain.  Facilitiates the development of virtue and wisdom necessary to set one free from the cycle of rebirths.  [[stream entry]] [[sotapatti]]  Also balance. Neutrality. Equanimity. See page 35.
2. Càtumahàràjika Heaven - Devas of the 4 Gret Kings, gandhabbas (celestial musicians), and yakkas (tree spirits). 
3. Tàvatiüsa Heaven. 33 Gods of Sakka (Indra) as their King. Devotee of Buddha. Mansions in the air.
4. Yàma Heaven (Devas) Live in the air. Free of all difficulties.
5. Tusita Heaven (Contented devas) realm of pure delight and gaiety. Bodhisattas abide here prior to final human birth.
6. Nimmànarati Heaven - Devas delighting in creation.
7. Paranimmita-vasavatti Heaven (Devas wielding power over the creation of others) Sense please created by other for them.  Mara lives here.

Very little dukkha.
Beings not born as babies. Remain the same age until they die. See Page 37.

## Råpa-loka, Form Realms (The Fine Material World) 

The following are the different planes of devas:

1. Pàrisajja Brahma Retinue / servants of Mahà Brahma. 
2. Purohita Brahma Ministers / advisors of Mahà Brahma. Beings in these planes enjoy varying degrees of jhanic bliss. 
3. Mahà Brahma Great Brahmas.
