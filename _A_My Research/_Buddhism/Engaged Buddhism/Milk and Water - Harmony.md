[[Handful of Leaves]]
	Ghosita was filled with simsapa trees under which the Buddha liked to meditate during hot afternoons. One day after his meditation, he returned to the community holding a handful of simsapa leaves. He held them up and asked the bhikkhus, “Bhikkhus, which is greater—the number of leaves in my hand or the number of leaves in the forest?” 
	The bhikkhus answered, “The number of leaves in the forest.” 
	The Buddha answered, “Just so, what I see is much greater than what I teach. Why? Because I teach only those things that are truly necessary and helpful in attaining the Way.” 
	The Buddha said this because at Ghosita there were many bhikkhus who tended to lose themselves in philosophical speculation.
