The practice of mindfulness is an antidote to the feeling of disconnection that can arise as people try to leave their addiction behind. Its principles are intended to guide the mind to be more focused on the present. By grounding our attention in the here and now, we learn to acknowledge, accept, and let go of difficult emotions and thoughts. Instead of avoiding them, we handle them more intentionally, creating a healthy mind space that’s less likely to fall back into addictive behavior.
