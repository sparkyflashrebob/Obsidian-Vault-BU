# Ānāpānasati: The 16 Steps of Mindfulness of Breathing
**Canonical Reference**: *Ānāpānasati Sutta* (Majjhima Nikāya 118)  
**Doctrinal Framework**: The four tetrads developing the Four Foundations of Mindfulness (*Satipaṭṭhāna*).

---

## The Four Tetrads & 16 Breath Contemplations

16 Steps

"__\[1\]__ Breathing in long,One __discerns__, 'I am breathing in long'; or breathing out long,One discerns, 'I am breathing out long\.' 

__\[2\]__ Or breathing in short,One __discerns__, 'I am breathing in short'; or breathing out short,One discerns, 'I am breathing out short\.' 

__\[3\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in sensitive to the entire body\.'[\[2\]](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html#fn-2)One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out sensitive to the entire body\.' 

__\[4\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in calming bodily fabrication\.'[\[3\]](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html#fn-3)One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out calming bodily fabrication\.'

"__\[5\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in sensitive to rapture\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out sensitive to rapture\.' 

__\[6\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in sensitive to pleasure\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out sensitive to pleasure\.' 

__\[7\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in sensitive to mental fabrication\.'[\[4\]](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html#fn-4)One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out sensitive to mental fabrication\.' 

__\[8\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in calming mental fabrication\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out calming mental fabrication\.'

"__\[9\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in sensitive to the mind\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out sensitive to the mind\.' 

__\[10\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in satisfying the mind\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out satisfying the mind\.' 

__\[11\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in steadying the mind\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out steadying the mind\.' 

__\[12\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in releasing the mind\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out releasing the mind\.'[\[5\]](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/mn/mn.118.than.html#fn-5)

"__\[13\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in focusing on inconstancy\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out focusing on inconstancy\.' 

__\[14\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in focusing on dispassion *\[literally,* fading\]\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out focusing on dispassion\.' 

__\[15\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in focusing on cessation\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out focusing on cessation\.' 

__\[16\]__One __trains__ oneself, 'I will breathe in focusing on relinquishment\.'One trains oneself, 'I will breathe out focusing on relinquishment\.'
