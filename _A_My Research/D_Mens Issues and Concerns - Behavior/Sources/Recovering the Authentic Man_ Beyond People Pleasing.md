Here are 10 very clear, actionable steps to become happier based on the video:

---

1. Stop performing — start showing up as your real self.

Let go of trying to look like a man and focus on being one. Ask yourself: “Am I doing this because it’s me, or because I want approval?”

---

2. Say what you actually think and feel.

Don’t nod along to keep the peace. Practice giving honest answers — even to simple questions like “What do you want for dinner?”

---

3. Express your needs clearly, without guilt.

You’re allowed to say you’re tired, frustrated, or in need of space. Use direct language instead of hints or silence.

---

4. Stop monitoring other people’s moods.

You can care about someone without taking responsibility for how they feel. Their happiness is not your job.

---

5. Set boundaries — and enforce them.

Practice saying “no” without long explanations. For example, “I can’t make it” is enough. Your time and energy matter.

---

6. Catch and challenge your inner critic.

If you hear “I’m such an idiot,” pause and ask: “Would I talk to a friend this way?” Replace self-judgment with respect.

---

7. Learn what you actually like.

Reconnect with your preferences — music, activities, opinions — especially when no one’s watching. Define who you are.

---

8. Be kind — but not to earn approval.

Give and help others because it aligns with your values, not because you want to be liked or rewarded.

---

9. Handle conflict honestly, not passively.

Don’t avoid tough conversations. Use calm but firm phrases like “That doesn’t work for me” or “I see it differently.”

---

10. Accept that some people won’t like the new you.

Expect pushback from those who benefited from your people-pleasing. Stay the course. Your peace is worth it.

---
