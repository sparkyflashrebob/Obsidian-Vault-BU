###### Outline

###### The Dominic Pellico Case and Its Broader Implications

```
- The case of Dominic Pellico, a French man who drugged his wife and invited dozens of men to rape her over nearly a decade, shocked the world and served as the catalyst for this investigation
- A months-long undercover investigation revealed that Pellico was far from unique, and that vast international communities exist which continue to exploit and build upon what happened in his case
- The investigation concluded that Pellico is not an outlier but rather representative of a widespread and organised pattern of abuse
```

###### Infiltration of an International Network of Perpetrators

```
- An undercover journalist posed as a man online to infiltrate a vast international network of perpetrators hiding in plain sight
- Users were contacted across the US, the UK, France, Brazil and beyond, with most communication conducted in English
- A private Telegram group dedicated to sharing so-called sleep content was accessed via a fake identity, with the group containing almost a thousand members
- Hundreds of users within the group were actively coaching one another on how to sedate their partners, sharing liquids, pills, step-by-step instructions, drug names and exact dosages
```

###### The Role of Motherless.com in Facilitating Abuse

```
- Motherless.com, a pornographic website receiving over 60 million visits per month, was identified as a platform profiting from non-consensual content
- The site hosts over 20,000 videos of so-called sleep content, accumulating hundreds of thousands of views, with popular hashtags such as iCheck used to verify that a woman is asleep
- A user on the platform was found to be selling liquid sedative, operating what he described as a worldwide shipping business, with a bottle priced at 175 dollars and accompanied by instructions on use
- US safe harbor laws largely protect the site from legal liability, and neither Motherless nor Telegram responded to requests for comment
```

###### Survivor Testimony - Zoe Watts

```
- Zoe Watts met her husband at the age of 17, and now believes she was raped by him for approximately ten years before he confessed
- Following a church service, her husband confessed to crushing pills into her nightly cup of tea, using their son's sleeping medication to sedate her, take photographs and rape her
- The confession was delivered in a matter-of-fact manner, described by Zoe as being recited like a shopping list
- Following the revelation, she became severely ill, lost weight and struggled to leave the house or be around other people
- Speaking out occurred only after a severe panic attack, at which point her mother contacted the police, and her husband was subsequently sentenced to 11 years for rape, sexual assault by penetration and drugging
- Zoe acknowledged struggling to use the word rape in relation to what happened to her, reflecting the profound psychological difficulty survivors face in naming their experiences
```

###### Survivor Testimony - Amanda Stanhope

```
- Amanda Stanhope did not know her partner had been abusing her for five years, with physical signs including waking on a towel that had not been there when she went to bed, missing clothing and unexplained bruises
- Police reviewed video evidence that clearly showed her former partner performing a sexual act on her face while she was unconscious
- Despite the clarity of the footage, police told Amanda it could not be used as evidence because it appeared she might be pretending to be asleep
- Her former partner was charged with multiple counts of rape and sexual assault but took his own life before the case could go to court
- Upon learning about the Telegram group chats where such abuse is glorified and taught to others, Amanda described an immediate physical reaction of shock and described the behaviour as beyond evil
```

###### Survivor Testimony - Valentina

```
- Valentina, a mother of two whose identity was protected through a pseudonym and voice distortion, described witnessing her husband inject a syringe into her glass of Montenegro liqueur
- Her husband was sentenced to eight years in prison for multiple aggravated sexual assaults
- Valentina described the lasting psychological impact of the abuse, stating that something had definitively changed in her and that she continues to live with the presence of her trauma even when appearing happy on the outside
```

###### Challenges in Prosecuting Drug-Facilitated Sexual Abuse

```
- Many drugs used in these assaults leave the body within 12 hours, making forensic evidence extremely difficult to obtain
- Survivors frequently only realise what has happened to them much later, and even when they do come forward, they face significant roadblocks in reporting to police or pursuing legal cases
- In England, nearly a quarter of sexual assault victims are assaulted while unconscious or asleep, a figure that has risen over the last decade
```

###### Psychological Motivations Behind the Abuse

```
- A psychologist who assessed dozens of defendants in the Pellico case concluded that perpetrators were motivated by fantasies of domination
- These fantasies are fuelled by a broader culture that continues to treat many women as objects to be controlled
- The immense trauma inflicted on survivors can cause their sense of identity to all but vanish, with what remains shaped almost entirely by the abuse, making the rebuilding of trust in themselves and others a long and difficult process
```

###### Undercover Encounter with Piotr

```
- A man referred to as Piotr, not his real name, was identified within the Telegram group and began directly messaging the undercover journalist
- Without encouragement, Piotr shared details of his activities, including a message stating he had put his wife to sleep after a doctor refused to prescribe further sleeping pills, and continued to send photos and videos
- The journalist determined he was based in Poland and, after he refused to meet directly, learned through a tip that he planned to attend a party
- At the venue, the journalist identified Piotr dancing with his wife, confirming that the person who had been messaging day and night was a real individual actively abusing a real person
- Approaching Piotr's wife was deemed too dangerous, and the matter was referred to police
```

###### Gisele Pellicot as a Symbol of Strength for Survivors

```
- Gisele Pellicot was described as having taken hold of her own harrowing experience with astonishing dignity, becoming a source of strength for survivors around the world
- Messages from survivors including Amanda Stanhope were read to Gisele during the interview, expressing gratitude for her courage in breaking the silence and shame surrounding this form of abuse
- Gisele responded by describing the testimonies as overwhelming and beautiful, framing her ability to pass on strength to other survivors as a victory
- She urged all women to speak out, acknowledging it may take months or even years but expressing confidence that they will eventually get there
- Gisele also stated her belief that men can be allies and that society is capable of change, while warning perpetrators that survivors may one day wake up and denounce them
```

##### Investigation Background

* The investigation was prompted by the high-profile case of Dominic Pellicot, a French man who drugged his wife and invited dozens of men to rape her over nearly a decade
* The reporter spent months undercover, posing as a man, messaging members of online communities dedicated to drugging and raping partners
* The investigation revealed Pellicot was not an isolated case but part of a vast international network
* Communities were found operating across the US, UK, France, Brazil, and beyond, mostly communicating in English

##### Online Networks and Platforms

* The site motherless.com receives over 60 million visits per month and hosts over 20,000 videos of so-called "sleep content" with hundreds of thousands of views
* A popular hashtag #iCheck was used to prove a woman is asleep in videos
* A fake account was created to gain access to a private Telegram group with almost 1,000 members dedicated to sharing sleep content
* Users in the Telegram group coached one another on sedating partners, sharing liquids, pills, step-by-step instructions, drug names, and exact dosages
* One user on Motherless was found selling liquid sedative, shipping it worldwide for $175 per bottle, claiming it had no taste and the victim would not remember anything
* Men in the Telegram groups were making money selling clips and live streams for as little as $20
* The Telegram group eventually disappeared, but authorities note this is a recurring pattern — one goes offline and another resurfaces
* Motherless and Telegram did not respond to requests for comment
* US safe harbor laws largely protect sites like Motherless from liability
* German journalists have been campaigning to shut down Motherless and strengthen moderation on similar platforms
