*Quickly brainstorm creative action ideas by flipping over 3 cards at a time.*
MATERIALS 3 piles of cards: Tactics, Principles, Theories • Pen & paper
PLAY 1. Pick an issue you’re facing — say, your town is threatened by a fracking
pipeline & the CEO is coming to town. 2. Flip over one card from each pile. 3. Brainstorm . Generate action ideas. Consider how the cards might combine
to make a great action. 4. Record the best ideas.
OPTIONS ● Play in teams (Remember when 3 teams are generating ideas, there are 3
times as many ideas!) ● Keep one card constant , say, “Tactic: Flash mob,” while flipping over cards
from the other piles ● Use a timer. Time restrictions will prevent getting stuck too deep in the
practical details of a specific idea and, instead, help you focus on generating as many ideas as possible in the time allowed.
● Include Stories &/or Methodologies *● Get more advanced. Think this is too simple for your group? See Design a*
*Creative Action for a challenge toward building effective actions.*
TIPS FOR FACILITATORS ● Get creatively unstuck! This is a simple activity for exploring the possibilities
of creative action. (And having fun doing it.) ● Remember, “yes, and...” is the attitude that makes brainstorming flow. Try
not to shoot down or critique your teammate’s ideas. Instead, build on them, or just keep adding more ideas to the pile. There’s no wrong action here! This *game is about generating as many ideas as you can that might be possible.*
● Encourage participants to learn more. Each card includes a unique “pop-up code.” Turn your smartphone camera on and hover over the card. See what happens?! Your smartphone automatically pops up a link to the full version of that card in the Beautiful Trouble online toolbox.
● Don’t forget to capture your best ideas. For all of these activities, you may want to designate a notetaker who can write down — or quickly take pictures with their phone — any ideas your team generates during the game.
