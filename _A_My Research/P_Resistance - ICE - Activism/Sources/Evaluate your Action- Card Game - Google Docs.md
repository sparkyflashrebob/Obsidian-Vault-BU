Module 11
*Debrief after an action. Analyse an historic action. Test-flight an action that’s still in the works.*
MATERIALS 2 piles of cards: Principles, Theories • Pen & paper
PLAY 1. Choose an action to evaluate. 2. Draw a Principle or Theory card. Read it out loud. 3. Evaluate: Did this Principle/Theory play a role in your action? If so, how? If
not, did its absence impact what happened? How? 4. Imagine your action happening again. This time, informed by the
Theory/Principle you picked. What could you do differently to achieve a different or better outcome?
5. Curate a list of 3-5 Principles/Theories that you can use to guide future action planning.
TIPS FOR FACILITATORS *● Use Methodology: Action Star to evaluate the action in light of 8 critical*
questions every action planner should consider. ● Encourage participants to learn more. Each card includes a unique
“pop-up code.” Turn your smartphone camera on and hover over the card. See what happens?! Your smartphone automatically pops up a link to the full version of that card in the Beautiful Trouble online toolbox.
● Don’t forget to capture your best ideas. For all of these activities, you may want to designate a notetaker who can write down — or quickly take pictures with their phone — any ideas your team generates during the game.
