| Path Factor | Pāḷi Term | Classification | Description | Categories or Divisions | Purpose or Effect | Source |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| Right View | Sammā-diṭṭhi | Wisdom (Paññā) | The knowledge of the Four Noble Truths and the correct understanding of kamma, which serves as the foundation for the path. | 1. Right View Regarding Kamma (Kammassakatā), 2. Right View in Ten Matters (Dasavatthuka), 3. Right View of the Four Truths (Catusacca) | Destroys the latent stage of personality belief (sakkāyadiṭṭhi) and leads to the first nibbāna. | [1] |
| Right Thought | Sammā-saṅkappa | Wisdom (Paññā) | Mental application aimed at escaping the round of rebirth and directed toward the welfare of others. | 1. Thoughts of Renunciation (Nekkhamma), 2. Thoughts of Loving-kindness (Abyāpāda), 3. Thoughts of Compassion (Avihiṃsa) | Destroys the latent stage of personality belief and directs the mind toward the cessation of suffering. | [1] |
| Right Speech | Sammā-vācā | Morality (Sīla) | Abstinence from harmful verbal conduct. | 1. Abstinence from Falsehood, 2. Abstinence from Back-biting, 3. Abstinence from Abusive Speech, 4. Abstinence from Frivolous Talk | Destroys the transgression stage of personality belief and purifies verbal kamma. | [1] |
| Right Action | Sammā-kammantā | Morality (Sīla) | Abstinence from harmful physical conduct. | 1. Abstinence from Killing, 2. Abstinence from Stealing, 3. Abstinence from Sensual Misconduct | Destroys the transgression stage of personality belief and purifies bodily kamma. | [1] |
| Right Livelihood | Sammā-ājīva | Morality (Sīla) | Earning a living in a way that is free from immoral physical and verbal actions. | 1. Abstention from Wrong Livelihood, 2. Improper Livelihood, 3. Dishonest Livelihood, 4. Low Arts | Destroys the transgression stage of personality belief and ensures ethical survival. | [1] |
| Right Effort | Sammā-vāyāma | Concentration (Samādhi) | The exertion of will to prevent unwholesome states and develop wholesome ones. | 1. To prevent unarisen evil, 2. To abandon arisen evil, 3. To develop unarisen wholesome states, 4. To maintain arisen wholesome states | Destroys the active stage of personality belief and provides the power for path development. | [1] |
| Right Mindfulness | Sammā-sati | Concentration (Samādhi) | The constant focusing of the mind on physical and mental phenomena to eliminate restlessness. | 1. Mindfulness of Body, 2. Mindfulness of Feelings, 3. Mindfulness of Consciousness, 4. Mindfulness of Mental States | Destroys the active stage of personality belief and gains control over the 'mad' or fleeting mind. | [1] |
| Right Concentration | Sammā-samādhi | Concentration (Samādhi) | One-pointedness of mind achieved through fixing attention on a meditation object. | 1. First Jhāna, 2. Second Jhāna, 3. Third Jhāna, 4. Fourth Jhāna | Destroys the active stage of personality belief and eradicates mental restlessness. | [1] |
