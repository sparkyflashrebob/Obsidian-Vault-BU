| Stage of Purification | Pali Name | Primary Function | Associated Insight Knowledges | Obstacles or Defilements Removed | Source |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| 1. Purification of Virtue | Sīlavisuddhi | Establishing bodily and verbal restraint through four types of restraint: precepts, sense-door guarding, righteous livelihood, and wise reflection on requisites. | Not in source (Forms the foundation for insight) | Bodily and verbal misconduct; wrong livelihood. | [1] |
| 2. Purification of Mind | Cittavisuddhi | Developing concentration (access, absorption, or momentary) through serenity (samatha) or insight (vipassana) vehicles. | Not in source (Forms the second root for insight) | Five hindrances: sensual desire, ill will, sloth and torpor, agitation and remorse, and doubt. | [1] |
| 3. Purification of View | Diṭṭhivisuddhi | Arousing insight into the distinction between mind (nāma) and matter (rūpa). | 1. Knowledge of Delimitation of Mind-and-Matter (nāmarūpapariccheda-ñāṇa). | Personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi); the perception of a compact unit or soul. | [1] |
| 4. Purification by Overcoming Doubt | Kankhāvitaraṇavisuddhi | Discerning the law of cause and effect (causality) and the relationship between phenomena. | 2. Knowledge of Discerning Cause and Condition (paccayapariggaha-ñāṇa). | Skeptical doubts concerning past, future, and present existence; views denying causality. | [1] |
| 5. Purification by Knowledge and Vision of What is Path and Not-Path | Maggāmaggañāṇadassanavisuddhi | Distinguishing between true insight and the alluring distractions of the ten imperfections of insight. | 3. Knowledge by Comprehension (sammasana-ñāṇa); 4. Knowledge of Arising and Passing Away (udayabbaya-ñāṇa - undeveloped phase). | Ten imperfections of insight (illumination, rapturous delight, etc.) and the misconception that they constitute the goal. | [1] |
| 6. Purification by Knowledge and Vision of the Way | Paṭipadāñāṇadassanavisuddhi | Progressing through the mature sequence of insight knowledges focused on the three characteristics of existence. | 4. Knowledge of Arising and Passing Away (mature); 5. Dissolution; 6. Appearance as Terror; 7. Danger; 8. Disenchantment; 9. Desire for Deliverance; 10. Reflection; 11. Equanimity about Formations; 12. Conformity Knowledge. | Defilements that obstruct the path; perception of permanence, pleasure, and self. | [1] |
| 7. Purification by Knowledge and Vision | Ñāṇadassanavisuddhi | Realizing the four supramundane paths and fruits. | 13. Change-of-Lineage (gotrabhū); 14. Path Knowledge (magga-ñāṇa); 15. Fruit Knowledge (phala-ñāṇa); 16. Reviewing Knowledge (paccavekkhaṇa-ñāṇa). | Eradication of defilements and fetters (personality view, doubt, etc., up to ignorance) by means of the four supramundane paths. | [1] |
