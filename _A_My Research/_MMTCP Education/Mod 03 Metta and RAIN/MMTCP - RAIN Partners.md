

This is a guided meditation and sharing session designed for "RAIN partners." RAIN is an acronym for Recognize, Allow, Investigate, and Nurture, a mindfulness practice often taught by Tara Brach (though she's not the speaker here) for working with difficult emotions.

Here's a breakdown of the subjects and instructions in this session:

1.  **Introduction and Setup (0:00 - 0:24):**
    *   **Purpose:** A guided session for RAIN partners.
    *   **Role Assignment:** Before starting, partners need to decide who will be "Speaker Number 1" and "Listener Number 1" for the two sharing periods. This implies there will be a reciprocal sharing process.
    *   **Optional Longer Meditation:** Partners can agree to pause the recording near the beginning to have a longer opening meditation on their own if desired.

2.  **Beginning the Meditation - Stillness and Relaxation (0:41 - 1:14):**
    *   **Posture and Eyes:** Sit comfortably, close eyes, and come into stillness.
    *   **Body Scan and Relaxation:** Scan the body and consciously relax obvious areas of tension.
    *   **Resting Attention:** Once ready, rest attention on the senses – either the breath, body sensations, or sounds.
    *   **Goal:** To further quiet and settle the mind.

3.  **Inquiry - Identifying What's Between You and Well-being (1:14 - 2:43):**
    *   **Core Question:** "What is between me and true well-being?"
    *   **Areas of Exploration:** Participants are prompted to consider if this "block" relates to:
        *   Relationships
        *   Work
        *   Health
    *   **Identifying "Stuckness":** The aim is to find an area where they feel emotionally stuck and would like to experience insight, compassion, healing, and freedom.
    *   **Connecting with the Circumstances:** Take a few moments to get in touch with the specific circumstances of this emotional "tangle."

4.  **The "R" of RAIN - Recognize (for Speaker 1) (3:26 - 3:53):**
    *   **Instruction for Speaker 1:** Open eyes and share the chosen situation with Listener 1. Name the primary emotions that have become activated.
    *   **Instruction for Listener 1:** Offer an accepting and open-hearted presence to the speaker.
    *   **Time Limit:** Three minutes for this sharing.

5.  **Pause and Allowing (for both partners) (3:53 - 7:18, with period of silence from 3:53-6:50):**
    *   **Transition to Silence:** After Speaker 1 shares, both partners come into silence and close their eyes.
    *   **Purpose of Pause:** To simply pause and "hold together" what has been named by Speaker 1.
    *   **The "A" of RAIN - Allowing:** This pausing and giving space to what's been shared is described as the beginning of *allowing*.

6.  **The "R" of RAIN - Recognize (for Speaker 2) (7:18 - 7:46):**
    *   **Instruction for Speaker 2:** Open eyes and share their chosen situation with Listener 2 (who was previously Speaker 1). Name the primary emotions.
    *   **Instruction for Listener 2:** Offer an accepting and open-hearted presence.
    *   **Time Limit:** Three minutes for this sharing.

7.  **Pause and Allowing (for both partners) (7:46 - 10:44, with period of silence from 7:46-10:39):**
    *   Similar to the previous pause, both partners close their eyes and silently hold what Speaker 2 has shared, practicing allowing.

8.  **Deepening RAIN - Investigation and Nurturing (Individually) (10:44 - 11:19):**
    *   **Instruction:** Participants will now continue and deepen the RAIN practice *on their own*.
    *   **Posture Adjustment:** Attend to posture and make any adjustments for comfort.

9.  **The "I" of RAIN - Investigate (11:19 - 12:32, with period of silence from 11:19-11:54):**
    *   **Focus:** Inquiring into the *felt sense* of the experience, rather than a cognitive or analytical process.
    *   **Returning to the Body:** While stories and beliefs can be a portal, continually return attention to the body – to the felt sense and sensations of where one feels most vulnerable.
    *   **Qualities for Investigation:** Gentleness and genuine curiosity.
    *   **Inquiry Questions:**
        *   "What is the worst part of this?"
        *   "What most wants my attention?"
        *   "What's the most difficult or painful thing I'm believing?" (e.g., "I'm unlovable," "I'm unworthy," "I'll always fail").
        *   Sense if any of these core beliefs are present.

10. **Connecting Beliefs to Emotions and Sensations (12:32 - 17:11, with period of silence from 12:32-15:18):**
    *   **Moving to the Body:** Notice what emotions the identified belief brings up (e.g., fear, anger, grief, hurt).
    *   **Locating Sensations:** "Where do I feel these emotions inside?" "What do they feel like right now?" (e.g., clenched, raw, hot, sore, aching, empty, squeezed).
    *   **Embodying the Emotion:** Assume the facial expression and body posture that best reflect these feelings and emotions. Then ask again, "What do I notice?"
    *   **Transition to Nurturing (N of RAIN):** These next questions are a transition into nurturing.
        *   Stay connected with the vulnerable experience.
        *   Ask: "If the most vulnerable, hurting part of me could communicate, what would it express?" (Notice words, feelings, images).
        *   Ask: "How does this part want me to be with it?"
        *   Ask: "What does this part most need from me or from some larger source of love and wisdom?"

11. **The "N" of RAIN - Nurture (17:11 - 22:00, with period of silence from 17:11-21:42):**
    *   **Posture for Nurturing:** Breathe consciously, adjust posture to get in touch with one's "most awake experience of heart," the "most awake experience of mind." This is seen as the most evolved expression of who we are, our "loving presence," our "essence."
    *   **Calling on Inner Resources:** Call on one's most wise and compassionate self. If difficult, one can call in another being (friend, family, pet, teacher, spiritual figure) whose wisdom and love is trusted.
    *   **Offering What's Needed:** From this place of compassionate presence, inwardly offer the love, acceptance, forgiveness, compassion, or protection that the vulnerable part most needs.
    *   **Methods of Nurturing:**
        *   **Words:** Offer messages like "You're here," "You're not going away," "You care."
        *   **Touch:** Placing a hand on the heart gently and tenderly.
        *   **Imagery:** Seeing the inner child embraced or surrounded by light.
    *   **Energy Flow:** Let the nurturing energy wash through, permeating the most vulnerable part. Be the "holder and the held," offering and receiving loving presence.

12. **After RAIN - Resting in Presence (22:00 - 22:41):**
    *   **Cessation of Doing:** Cease any "doing" and rest in the presence and heart space that has emerged.
    *   **Familiarization:** Get familiar with this presence.
    *   **Integrating Residual Difficulty:** If some new or residual difficulty is felt, offer this too one's acknowledgment and care.
    *   **Inquiry into Being:** "In these moments, what is the sense of my being? Of who I am?"

13. **Reflecting on the Shift (22:41 - 23:40):**
    *   **Comparison:** "How has this shifted from when I began the meditation?"
    *   **Transition:** Prepare to move into the next sharing with the partner. Feel free to stretch, write notes, or drink water.

14. **Reflective Sharing (23:40 - 26:08):**
    *   **Speaker 1's Turn (again, as roles reverse for the second round of deeper sharing):** Respond to the following questions while the partner listens silently:
        *   "What was challenging about this practice?"
        *   "What did I experience or learn about myself that I want to remember?"
    *   **Time Limit:** Three minutes.

15. **Final Silence and Gratitude (26:08 - 33:20, with period of silence from 26:08-32:36):**
    *   **Instruction for Both:** Come into silence, close eyes, sit quietly.
    *   **Speaker 2's Turn (after silence):** Respond to the same reflective questions.
    *   **Time Limit:** Three minutes.
    *   **Concluding the Session:** After Speaker 2, maintain a safe and respectful space. Share anything else about the experience that feels important. This might include what it was like to listen and share with the partner.
    *   **Express Gratitude:** Express gratitude to each other in any way that resonates.

This session provides a structured way for partners to individually engage with the RAIN practice and then share their experiences in a supportive dyad, fostering both personal insight and connection.
