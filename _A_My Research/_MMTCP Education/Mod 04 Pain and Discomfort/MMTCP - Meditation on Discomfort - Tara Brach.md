**Summary:**

Tara Brach leads a guided meditation focused on mindfully engaging with physical discomfort. The practice begins with settling the body and breath. Participants are then guided to scan their bodies for areas of discomfort, and if found and tolerable, to bring a receptive, allowing attention to those sensations. The instruction emphasizes noticing any physical tensing or emotional/mental resistance (pushing away, pulling away, trying to "do" something with the pain).

Instead of resisting, Brach encourages simply being present with the sensations as they are, using soft mental labels like "burning," "aching," or "throbbing" to help anchor attention. The core of the practice is to cultivate an attitude of "you belong" or "this too" towards the unpleasant sensations, allowing them to be part of the present moment experience. She also guides participants to explore pendulation – gently shifting attention between the discomfort and a neutral or pleasant anchor in the body (like the hands or breath) to build resilience and prevent overwhelm. The meditation aims to foster a spacious, accepting awareness where sensations can arise, change, and pass, ultimately reducing the suffering associated with physical discomfort.

**Detailed Breakdown:**

**0:06 - 0:35: Settling In**
    *   **Posture:** Sit in a way that's comfortable.
    *   **Eyes:** Close your eyes.
    *   **Collecting Attention:** Gather your attention with a few nice, full breaths.
    *   *(Pause for silent breathing and settling)*

**0:36 - 0:50: Scanning the Body for Discomfort**
    *   **Body Scan:** Take some moments to let the attention scan the body.
    *   **Noticing Discomfort:** Notice if there are any areas of particular discomfort.

**0:51 - 1:43: Initial Engagement with Discomfort**
    *   **Receptive Attention:** If there is an area where unpleasant sensations are calling your attention, bring a receptivity there.
    *   **Observing Initial Reactions:** Notice what happens as you *begin* to be present with those sensations.
        *   Is there any attempt to push the pain away?
        *   Or to pull away from it?
        *   Or to *do* something with the pain?
        *   Notice any tensing in the body or emotions.

**1:44 - 2:29: Staying Present & Allowing (if tolerable)**
    *   **Tolerability Check:** If the area or constellation of unpleasantness feels *tolerable* (this is the inquiry), let your intention be to remain present, to *stay*.
    *   **Message of Allowing:** Perhaps send the message "you belong," or "this too," or "yes." Some message of allowing.

**2:30 - 3:02: Observing Sensations as They Are**
    *   **Being With:** Allow unpleasant sensations to be *as they are*.
    *   **Disregarding with Interest & Gentleness:** Disregard any judgment or story, and meet the sensations with interest and gentleness.
    *   **Noticing Change:** What do you notice?
        *   Is there change?
        *   Is there movement?
        *   More intensity? Less intensity?

**3:03 - 4:21: Labeling and Continued Observation**
    *   **Helpful Labeling:** See if naming is helpful. Offer some words that might describe the sensation (e.g., burning, aching, twisting, tearing, stabbing, throbbing).
    *   **Checking the Label:** Then sense, "Is that it?"
    *   **Continue Noticing Change:** Continue to notice how experience changes.

**4:22 - 5:18: Pendulation - Using a Resource Anchor**
    *   **If Sensations are Strong/Intolerable:** If the sensations are strong, towards the "intolerable zone," take some moments to explore establishing another place for attention – a *resource place* that feels neutral or pleasant.
        *   Examples: hands, lips, eyes, feet.
        *   If breath helps, breathe and rest in a place that has more ease.
    *   **Building Resilience:** This gives resilience, helps regain some balance and perspective.

**5:18 - 6:12: Practicing Pendulation (Back and Forth)**
    *   **Shifting Attention:** You might explore going back and forth some.
        *   Sensing the zone that feels like a resource.
        *   Then gently easing into a bit of where it's unpleasant.
        *   Then back again (to the resource).
        *   Then pendulating back into the difficult area and sensing again a bit of the discomfort.
    *   **Giving Space:** This back and forth gives some space.

**6:13 - 7:38: Space Around and Within Sensations**
    *   **Space Around:** You might also explore sensing into the space more immediately *around* the area of discomfort.
        *   See if you can let sensations float in a larger space.
        *   A soft attention, noticing their natural dance of change.
    *   **Space Within:** Perhaps you can sense the space around and also the *interior space* (as in the space in the nucleus of an atom).
        *   The space that sensations arise out of and dissolve into.
        *   Letting the body become like open space, plenty of room for unpleasant sensations to arise, and then fade, or intensify, and then dissolve, to move, to change.
        *   No holding, no tensing.

**7:39 - 9:42: Infusing with Awareness**
    *   **Wakefulness & Awareness:** Letting this space be infused with a wakefulness, an awareness.
    *   **Sea of Awareness:** Inhabiting a sea of awareness.
    *   **Sensations Floating:** Letting unpleasant sensations float, unfold, move in an accepting openness.
    *   *(Extended silent practice)*

**9:43 - End: Concluding Sound**
    *   *(Sound of a chime to end the meditation)*
