Ways to help students approach to Practice.

  

Communicate it is not personal - when people find it difficult - busy mind - all of us - Suzuki Roshi - “I have many difficulties in my practice, so I think you, too, will find it difficult to sit good zazen.” - Value of group sharing - it is not personal - everyone the same challenge - doesn’t reflect badly on me. - Buy a Community Buddha - leaning buddha - the cast is off and okay - “...not perfect, not permanent, and not personal.” - Ruth King.

  

Share that awareness and awakening is our very nature. I would not teach this if it is not possible. New habit. Trust that it is possible. Positive habit - this has to be believed. 100,000 prostrations - way to collect ourselves - laughed - more simply - trees vibrant with their story - anything we give ourselves fully too - we need to trust that it is part of our path -

  

Share the qualities of making an effort. Tuning of a musical instrument - not too loose and not too tight.

  

  

Ways to Help Students in Meditation Practice (Particulars - Logistical Elements)

  

Encourage a regular meditation practice. Regular but with flexibility. Made a commitment but with a back door how long or what posture or the style not fixed. Nature loves rhythm. “Make regular visits to yourself” - Rumi. A bit of relief and then you love it. A bone shaped necklace. Sit, stay, heal. Portal - Entry to waking up.

  

Explore the content of practice. Elements - collecting makes a difference - widen out all of life. How might we describe practice PRESENT HEART. Some collecting, some loving-kindness, some to move, how to be awake in the movement. Let it be an experiment. No two body/minds alike. There own experience. Everyone to customize the practice. Spirit of interest. Inner Listening. Listen to what opens your heart the most.

  

Explore the reward of practice. Gratification. Rid of stress. Clarity. They give way to home coming, be the awareness. He to be there. What works for them. What brings calm. Balancing.

  

Invite people toward their edge. If love peaceful but bad at being open what is difficult. Ease into that. Interest. Alive. Safe. Balancing.

  

Give and receive feedback. Not practice in a vacuum. Encourage students and ourselves to share with our practice.
