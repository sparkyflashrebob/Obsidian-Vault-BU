Exteroception-- what's happening in the space around my body, so moving objects, stationary objects, just the environment, whether you're-- I mean, if you're outside, whether you're going to be on pavement, whether you're in the field, whether you're going to fall into the rice paddy. You really have to be aware of what's happening in the environment.

And then proprioception-- the relationship of my body to objects in the environment like people, furniture, trees, rocks, animals, as they're moving and I'm moving. That is all very, very important and embedded in the practice. 

Manual Work Work is extremely important. Working with our hands is very highly prized as a very good practice. And collective living-- eating, sleeping, working, playing, living, and dying together. 

If you do these practices, you cannot be unaware of physical sensations, pain, thoughts,
