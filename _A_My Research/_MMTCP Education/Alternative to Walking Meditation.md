Simple and repetitive actions can develop calm and a greater sense of here and now

Seated or lying down
eyes closed or open

hands as primary anchor
mindful of movement and sensations
to start conscious breathing with movement
can practice with breath free and unrestricted

hands on thighs
eyes close or soften gaze - relax
open awareness to the sounds around you, sense of smell and touch

Feel your hands from inside right now
backs, palms, fingers, and thumbs.
Feel space between the fingers
you may be able to sense the pulse, tingling or subtle sensations

Bring attention to the right hand
gently turn the palm face up

gently inhale
Inhale and lift right hand 5 or 6 inches off your thigh
note "lifting"

turn palm face down
exhale and gently lower your hand to your palm
noting "placing"

Repeat with left hand

Alternatively repeat

As you continue you might release the control of the breath.  Focus on the movement.  Noting what is happening as it is happening
