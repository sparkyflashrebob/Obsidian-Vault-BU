## 1. Teacher Profile & Teaching Scope

Ven. Ajahn Punnadhammo was ordained in Thailand in 1991 under Ven. Somdet Phra Buddhaghosacariya (Ñāṇavara) and trained in the Thai Forest tradition. He is the founder and abbot of Arrow River Forest Hermitage in Thunder Bay, Ontario, Canada, and author of *The Buddhist Cosmos*. Renowned for his deep canonical scholarship, wit, and psychological clarity, his teachings bridge the cosmological and psychological dimensions of the Dhamma.
