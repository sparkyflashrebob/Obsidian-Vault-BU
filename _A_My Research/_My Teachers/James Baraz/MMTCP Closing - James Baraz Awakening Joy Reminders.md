Exploration of the teachings - how applied for meditative ease.
Shared 3 principles:
1. Important to know where happiness lies. #wise_effort 4 Wise Efforts. Make the effort to wake up. 2 deal with unhealthy states, states like greed, hatred and delusion, jealousy, fear, etc.  #akusala. Unhealthy states of mind. Parts of being human. Recommend  A) Guard against unwholesome states to prevent them from arising, B) and when they do arise, learn how to over come them (part of our training, bringing selfcompassion, mindfulness, kindness, patience, all of the beautiful teachings). And then there are the kusula states, the healthy states, like kindness, generosity, compassion, joy, equanimity, patience,  etc. C) Cultivate these wholesome states, which we are what we are doing when we are mindful. This is the best of all of cultivations because all of the other wholesome states follow from it. And D) when a wholesome state arises to maintain and increase that wholesome state is a good thing. This usually does not get a lot of play compared to the overcoming the unwholesome states or cultivate the wholesome. How might we do that? We naturally thing to increase it is to capture and hold onto it. However there is a problem.  All of the unwholesome states are states of contraction, while wholesome states are an expansion - an opening up. When we are trying to hold onto it you have just turned into an unwholesome state because you are grasping.  So how to turn it into a wholesome state? PAY ATTENTION TO IT.   LET IT BE HERE. DON'T MISS IT.
2. The Buddha asks us to notice how well these wholesome states [[feel]]. MN99. Subha Sutta. To Subha. He says when you are in the middle of a generous act, think to yourself that you are being generous now. That is a good thing. Not, I hope everybody sees  how generous I am.  All that is more Ego. He says notice how good it feels for a wholesome state to move through me. Generosity feels so good. There is a gladness that is connected to that wholesome state. He calls that gladness that is connected with the wholesome equipment of the mind. So this is a key, the Buddha says, to know that gladness and know how it feels in the body - in your mind. If you pay attention to it, and one of the beautiful qualities of mindfulness, is not just to know that there is calm here, or joy here, but bring your mindful attention to it. And really take it in. Marinate in it. Not with grasping. But just appreciating it.  And when we do this we give it much for life, to the wholesome. It is one thing to know a beautiful sunset - and then think - what is for dinner - and different to know a beautiful and let it sink in. And register inside. And knowing this is what it feels like to feel good. [[Rick Hansen]] is know for saying that we are velcro for negativity and Teflon for positivity. He has this formula - For positivity to sink in, we need to let it do so for at least 15 seconds. And if we can do that 6 times in a day, 90 seconds of wellbeing, if you can handle it. And if over a 2 week period, you will start to notice the effect (deepening the neuropathways, those grooves) and you will begin to notice the start of #habitually looking for the good. Usually we are on the lookout for the danger - amygdala - dangers - missing all of the good - #confirmation_bias - we find what we look for - how people are going to disappoint you - or how the world is going down the tubes - If instead of looking for the good in people, how amazing it is to be alive. this will become your #confirmation bias. Reorienting yourself to look for the good. Turning habit of the mind to being velcro for the positive and teflon for the negative. Not pretending. [[_A_My Research/_Buddhism/_Dharma Subjects and Themes/Brahmavihara 1/Sympathetic Joy or Mudita/Joy Practice]] Invite us to think of something that brings us joy.  Did anyone mention, their jewelry?  We get the message from society that material things bring us joy. Part 2: Describe what it felt like? This is evidence.  You do not have to manufacturer feeling good. When you notice doing something that makes you feel good, it is right inside of you. THE KEY IS TO DON'T MISS IT. #mindfulness Bring a mindful attention to what is inside of you.
