In this Dhamma talk from *Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery* (recorded July 25, 2026), *Ajahn Nyaniko* reflects on his priorities as an abbot and the importance of **Dhamma-Vinaya** (the teachings and the discipline) in monastic life. 

[Dhamma-Vinaya is the Priority \| Ajahn Nyaniko - YouTube](https://youtu.be/YUFjOvOb7CE?si=S-usqsoeV6VZUEDW)


**Key Takeaways:**
* **Dhamma-Vinaya as the Priority:** *Ajahn Nyaniko* emphasizes that his highest priority is not completing projects or maintaining monastery facilities, but rather upholding the *Dhamma-Vinaya*—the training of body, speech, and mind (7:13 - 10:01).
* **The Abbot’s Priority List:** He outlines his top five priorities (10:01 - 14:56):
    1. Upholding **Dhamma-Vinaya**.
    2. Maintaining his own personal practice.
    3. Ensuring **communal harmony**.
    4. Providing **encouragement** to others.
    5. Getting practical tasks done.
* **The "Rock Tumbler" Analogy:** He describes living in a community as being like a rock tumbler, where the friction of living together wears away one's rough edges, aiding in maturation and the development of wisdom (12:41 - 13:00).
* **The Nature of Practice:** *Ajahn Nyaniko* notes that monastic life is not always serene; it often involves "dredging the depths of hell," meaning it brings up old defilements and baggage to be faced and resolved. He highlights the importance of taking personal responsibility for one’s own suffering rather than blaming external circumstances (18:52 - 20:39).
* **Skillful Response:** He advises that rather than forcing an end to suffering through sheer willpower, practitioners should develop wisdom and mindfulness to respond skillfully to unpleasant situations (21:02 - 22:50).
