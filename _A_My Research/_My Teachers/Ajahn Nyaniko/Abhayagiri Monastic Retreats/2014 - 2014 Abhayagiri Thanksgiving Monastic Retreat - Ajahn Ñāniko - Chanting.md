## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata

### Canonical & Contemporary Setting
* **Canonical Setting**: Reflects the ancient tradition of chanting parittas and verses of protection to generate spiritual power and dedicate merit to those suffering from acute illness or grieving the loss of loved ones.
* **Contemporary Recording Venue**: Angela Center sanctuary, Santa Rosa, California. Delivered during the 2014 Abhayagiri Monastic Retreat.
* **Circumstances & Occasion**: Retreat reflection offering profound contemplative tools to understand defilement, cultivate noble character, and dedicate practice to the welfare of all beings.
* **People Involved**: Ajahn Ñāṇiko (speaker), resident monastics, and lay retreatants.

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **Dedications from the Heart**: Before beginning the evening chanting, Ajahn Ñāṇiko invites the assembly to hold Janice Nickel—struggling for breath in the intensive care unit while awaiting lungs—and Thomas Law in their hearts. Chanting ceases to be ritual and becomes an active transmission of loving-kindness and spiritual refuge.
