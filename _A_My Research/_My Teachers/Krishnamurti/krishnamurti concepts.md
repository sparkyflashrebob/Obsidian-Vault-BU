While Krishnamurti didn't write a specific book solely titled "Choiceless Awareness," this concept is a central theme throughout many of his works.
Here are some of his books where choiceless awareness is extensively discussed:
 * "Freedom from the Known": This book delves deeply into how the mind, with its accumulated knowledge and conditioning, limits our perception of reality. Choiceless awareness is presented as a way to transcend these limitations.
 * "The First and Last Freedom": This book explores the nature of freedom and how it can only be found through a deep understanding of the mind and its patterns. Choiceless awareness is presented as a crucial step in this process.
 * "Truth is a Pathless Land": This book emphasizes that truth cannot be found through any system of belief or dogma. Choiceless awareness is presented as a way to directly experience reality without the interference of preconceived notions.
It's important to note that Krishnamurti often conveyed his teachings through dialogues and informal talks. Many of these are available in book form and provide valuable insights into the concept of choiceless awareness.
I recommend exploring these books and other resources by Krishnamurti to gain a deeper understanding of this profound concept.
