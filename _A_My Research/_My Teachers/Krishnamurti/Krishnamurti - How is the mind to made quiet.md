The video is titled How is the mind to be made quiet? | Krishnamurti and was uploaded by Krishnamurti Foundation Trust on April 10, 2023. It has 320882 views and 5753 likes.

The key points of the video are:

- The question of how to make the mind quiet is a wrong question.
- The mind is naturally quiet when we are in the presence of something beautiful, such as a mountain, a tree, or a bird.
- When we try to make the mind quiet through meditation or other methods, we are actually hypnotizing ourselves.
- This is because we are projecting an end goal, such as enlightenment or God, and then trying to achieve it through a system of practice.
- This can lead to a waste of time and energy, as the end goal is often not what we actually want.

The video concludes by saying that the only way to make the mind quiet is to be present in the moment and to see things as they are.
