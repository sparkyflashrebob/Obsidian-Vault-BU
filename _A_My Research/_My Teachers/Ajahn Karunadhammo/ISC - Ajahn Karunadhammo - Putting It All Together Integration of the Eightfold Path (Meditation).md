## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata & Guided Architecture

* **Location:** Insight Santa Cruz, Friday evening community practice.
* **Practice Stages:**
  1. *Erecting the Posture (*Ujuṃ kāyaṃ paṇidhāya*):* Aligning the spine, opening chest cavity, relaxing shoulders.
  2. *Earth Element (*Pathavī*):* Feeling weight and contact at the cushion and floor.
  3. *Space Element (*Ākāsa*):* Sensing internal hollows (nasal cavity, throat, chest, abdomen).
  4. *Tranquilizing (*Passaddhi*):* Releasing the urge to control breath or force tranquility.
* **Verbatim Insight:** *"[15:20] The body settles when we stop managing it. Let the breath come and go like waves lapping upon a peaceful shore."*
