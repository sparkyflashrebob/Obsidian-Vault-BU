## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata & Core Teachings

* **Location:** Insight Santa Cruz, Santa Cruz, California.
* **Circumstances:** Following traditional Pāli chanting (*Namo Tassa* and refuges), Ajahn Karunadhammo delivers this address challenging the common Western reduction of Buddhism to mindfulness meditation alone.
* **Theme:** Moving from fragmented spiritual techniques to an integrated, eightfold lifestyle.
* **The Threefold Division:**
  1. **Wisdom (*Paññā*):** Right View (*Sammā-diṭṭhi*), Right Intention (*Sammā-saṅkappa*).
  2. **Ethics (*Sīla*):** Right Speech (*Sammā-vācā*), Right Action (*Sammā-kammanta*), Right Livelihood (*Sammā-ājīva*).
  3. **Collectedness (*Samādhi*):** Right Effort (*Sammā-vāyāma*), Right Mindfulness (*Sammā-sati*), Right Immersion (*Sammā-samādhi*).
* **The Heavenly Messengers (*Devadūta Sutta*):** Aging, illness, and death as urgent reminders to practice without delay.
* **The Translator Story:** Ajahn Karunadhammo recounts traveling in Thailand with a senior Western master and a Thai monk. When lay people asked complex philosophical questions, the senior master simply pointed back to basic kindness, generosity, and non-harming in daily life.
