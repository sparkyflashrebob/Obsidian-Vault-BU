## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Body as the First Foundation
The physical organism is our most honest, accessible anchor. Mental states fluctuate unpredictably, but physical sensations of posture, pressure, and warmth are undeniably real in the present moment.

### 2. Systematic Somatic Alignment
- **The Base**: Ensuring the knees and hips form a stable tripod.
- **The Spine**: Lengthening upward without rigid tension, like a stack of golden coins.
- **The Soft Front**: Softening the belly and diaphragm so the natural breath can move unimpeded.

### 3. Receptive Wakefulness
When the body is properly balanced, it requires minimal muscular effort to remain erect. This effortless alignment prevents physical fatigue and allows awareness to become luminous and calm.

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **The Weight of the Head**: Ajahn Karuṇadhammo points out that the human head weighs around ten pounds. If it tips forward even an inch, the neck muscles must work continuously like crane cables. Bringing the ears in line with the shoulders releases all neck strain.
