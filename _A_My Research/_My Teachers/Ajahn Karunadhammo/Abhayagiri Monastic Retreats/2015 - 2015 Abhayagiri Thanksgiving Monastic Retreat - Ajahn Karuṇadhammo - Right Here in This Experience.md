## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Delusion of Elsewhere
Spiritual seekers often carry an unconscious assumption: *'Dhamma is happening somewhere else—in a cave in Thailand, in a monastery, or after twenty years of retreat.'* This creates chronic restlessness. The Buddha taught that Dhamma is *sanditthiko* (immediately visible) and *akāliko* (timeless).

### 2. Deconstructing Narrative into Raw Experience
Experience consists of simple components: sensations in the body, feeling tones, auditory perceptions, and mental knowing. When we peel back our personal opinions, judgments, and stories about these sensations, reality is revealed as pure, unentangled flow.

### 3. In the Seen, Only the Seen
Resting back in the space of awareness, we stop creating an 'I' who is experiencing the breath. There is simply breathing, simply hearing, simply knowing. In that stillness of non-interference, peace is already present.

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **The Bāhiya Awakening**: Ajahn Karuṇadhammo recounts the urgent enlightenment of Bāhiya Dārucīriya, who begged the Buddha for Dhamma while the Buddha was on almsround. The Buddha gave him a few terse lines about seeing only the seen and hearing only the heard, and Bāhiya was completely liberated on the spot.
