## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata

### Canonical & Contemporary Setting
* **Canonical Setting**: Reflects the Pabhassara Sutta (AN 1.49-52), where the Buddha declares: 'Luminous, monastics, is this mind (pabhassaramidaṃ cittaṃ), but it is defiled by visiting defilements (āgantukehi upakkilesehi upakkiliṭṭhaṃ).' Mental development (bhāvanā) is the purification that frees the knowing heart.
* **Contemporary Recording Venue**: Angela Center sanctuary, Santa Rosa, California. Delivered during the 2014 Abhayagiri Monastic Retreat.
* **Circumstances & Occasion**: Retreat discourse offering practical contemplative tools to deepen presence, understand mental conditioning, and integrate practice into everyday life.
* **People Involved**: Ajahn Karuṇadhammo (speaker), monastic sangha, and lay retreatants.

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **The Visiting Guests at the Hermitage**: Ajahn Karuṇadhammo uses the simile of living in a secluded monastic kutī. When someone knocks on the door—whether a beloved friend or an obnoxious stranger—the monk opens the door, greets them, but doesn't invite them to move into the bedroom and take over the house. In meditation, restless thoughts and irritations are just visiting guests knocking at the senses. Acknowledge them at the door, but let them continue on their way without giving them the keys to the heart.
