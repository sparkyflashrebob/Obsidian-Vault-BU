* **Contemporary Recording Venue**: Angela Center sanctuary, Santa Rosa, California. Delivered during the 2014 Abhayagiri Monastic Retreat.
* **Circumstances & Occasion**: Retreat discourse offering practical contemplative tools to deepen presence, understand mental conditioning, and integrate practice into everyday life.

## 📊 Topic Breakdown & Supporting Practices

| Dimension | Left-Brain Analytical Straining | Right-Brain Somatic Receptivity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cognitive Mode** | Dissecting, evaluating, labeling, judging progress. | Intuitive, holistic, listening, feeling, allowing. |
| **Somatic Atmosphere** | Furrowed brow, tight eyes, clenched jaw, tense breathing. | Soft belly, relaxed shoulders, open ribcage, natural rhythm. |
| **Approach to Sutta Study** | Treating the Dhamma as a scholastic puzzle. | Meeting the Four Truths as visceral experiential realities. |
| **Spiritual Fruit** | Cerebral fatigue, conceptual arrogance, doubt. | Deep somatic ease (*passaddhi*), peace, and intuitive insight. |

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **Walking Off the Heavy Meal**: Describing his walk after the midday meal, Ajahn Karuṇadhammo notes how meditators walking on the paths sometimes look intensely serious, marching with grim determination as if trying to conquer enlightenment through sheer velocity. He smiles and reminds practitioners that the body relaxes and becomes alert not through grim force, but by allowing attention to soften into the felt rhythm of each step.
