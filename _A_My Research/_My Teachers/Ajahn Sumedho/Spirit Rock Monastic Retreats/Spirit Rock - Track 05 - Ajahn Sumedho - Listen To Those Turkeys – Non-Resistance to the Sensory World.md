### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transforming auditory irritation into Dhamma reflection; meeting the sounds of wildlife and human disturbance with equanimity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
During a quiet meditation period at Spirit Rock, the loud gobbling of wild turkeys outside the meditation hall disrupts the meditators' silence. Luang Por Sumedho seizes the moment to offer a hilarious and profound discourse on sensory irritation. Suffering is not caused by the turkeys making noise, but by our silent inner demand: 'Turkeys shouldn't gobble while I am achieving enlightenment!'

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Source of Irritation**: Why external sounds never disturb us; it is our aversion to sounds that disturbs us.

**The Bāhiya Instruction**: 'In the heard, let there be only the heard.' Hearing sound as vibration in empty space.

**Coexisting with Nature**: Welcoming the messy, uncontrollable symphony of the natural world.
