### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The absurdity of identifying with days of the week, social roles, and temporal moods; waking up to the timeless nature of conscious being.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a humorous and illuminating reflection titled 'I'm Saturday Morning.' He dissects how human beings unconsciously assume collective psychological identities tied to the calendar: 'Monday blues,' 'TGIF excitement,' or 'Saturday morning relaxation.' When we inspect reality, Saturday morning does not exist in nature—it is a collective social convention. Luang Por urges meditators to peel back every temporal mask until only timeless, unconditioned reality remains.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Fiction of the Calendar**: Recognizing how cultural conventions dictate emotional states without our consent.

**The Construction of the Ego Mask**: How we assemble an identity out of transient social roles and expectations.

**Timeless Abiding**: Discovering that conscious awareness is neither Monday nor Saturday, young nor old, living nor dying.
