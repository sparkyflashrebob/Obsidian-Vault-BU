### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Developing unshakeable trust in 'The Knowing' (Buddho) rather than seeking security in changing psychological conditions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered at Spirit Rock in April 2001, Luang Por Sumedho points to the bedrock of practice: 'Trust in Being the Knowing.' Worldly human beings place their trust in transient shelters: bank accounts, youth, health, relationships, and intellectual reputations. When these inevitably crumble, panic ensues. Luang Por shows that the only infallible refuge is the knowing itself (*Buddho*)—the pure conscious presence that witnesses all change without changing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The False Refuges of Samsara**: Why no conditioned shelter can ever provide permanent security.

**Trusting the Knowing**: Cultivating the confidence to rest as awareness rather than micromanage thoughts.

**The Infallible Sanctuary**: Experiencing the uncreated peace that remains intact through all life crises.
