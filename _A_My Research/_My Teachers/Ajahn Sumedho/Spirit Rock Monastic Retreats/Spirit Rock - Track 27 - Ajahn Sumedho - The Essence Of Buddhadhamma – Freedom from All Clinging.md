### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Buddha's ultimate summary to Sakka: 'Nothing whatsoever should be clung to' (Sabbe dhammā nālaṁ abhinivesāya).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho opens the 2008 Spirit Rock retreat by cutting directly to the core: 'The Essence of Buddhadhamma.' When the King of Gods asked the Buddha how to summarize the entire teaching in a single sentence, the Buddha answered: *'Sabbe dhammā nālaṁ abhinivesāya'*—Nothing whatsoever is worth clinging to as 'me' or 'mine.' Luang Por shows how this single teaching dismantles every spiritual complication, theology, and meditative technique into pure, unburdened freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Single Sentence Summary**: Contemplating *Sabbe dhammā nālaṁ abhinivesāya* in all situations.

**The Art of Non-Clinging**: How letting go differs fundamentally from pushing away or annihilating experience.

**The Peace of Non-Grasping**: Resting in the spacious Deathless sanctuary that remains when all clinging ceases.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls asking Ajahn Chah for the supreme secret of meditation, expecting an esoteric technique, only to have Ajahn Chah pick up a teacup and say: 'If you hold it tightly, your hand aches. If you put it down, there is no pain. That is all of Buddhism.'
