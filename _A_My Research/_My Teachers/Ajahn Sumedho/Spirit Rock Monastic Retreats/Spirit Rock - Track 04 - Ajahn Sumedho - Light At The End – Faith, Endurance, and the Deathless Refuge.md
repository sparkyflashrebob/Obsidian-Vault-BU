### Circumstances
Monastic retreat gathering and public discourses delivered at Spirit Rock Meditation Center in Marin County, California, addressing Western monastic candidates, senior lay practitioners, and Vipassanā students.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating periods of spiritual dryness, despondency, and doubt by anchoring in unshakeable faith (saddhā) and the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this evening retreat talk, Luang Por Sumedho offers deep encouragement to meditators passing through spiritual gloom, boredom, and fatigue. The Buddha's awakening brought 'light arisen' (*āloko udapādi*). Luang Por explains that the 'light at the end of the tunnel' is not a worldly reward waiting in the future, but the uncreated luminous nature of awareness that is already present right inside the darkness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**Working with Spiritual Despondency**: Understanding that darkness and resistance are natural phases of purification.

**The Arising of Inner Light**: Recognizing *Āloko Udapādi*—the knowing that illumines all mental weather.

**The Power of Patient Endurance (*Khanti*)**: Sitting through emotional storms without looking for emergency exits.
