Monastic retreat gathering and public discourses delivered at Spirit Rock Meditation Center in Marin County, California, addressing Western monastic candidates, senior lay practitioners, and Vipassanā students.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating diṭṭhi-upādāna (clinging to opinions) and asmimāna (the conceit 'I am'); dropping the compulsion to be morally and intellectually right.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho takes aim at one of humanity's most toxic addictions: the feeling of 'I'm Right, and You're Wrong.' Drawing from the Aṭṭhakavagga of the Sutta Nipāta, Luang Por illuminates how attachment to opinions, politics, philosophies, and even Dhamma views causes endless bloodshed and domestic misery. The spiritual goal is not to amass an impenetrable fortress of correct opinions, but to realize freedom from clinging to all opinions whatsoever (*diṭṭhi-visuddhi*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Drug of Self-Righteousness**: Why the ego loves feeling morally superior and outraged.

**The Sutta Nipāta on Disputes**: How philosophers and zealots grasp their views like shields and spears.

**Knowing Views as Views**: Holding opinions lightly as functional tools without making them an identity.
