### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Bridging Theravāda insight with the universal mystical yearning for the Divine; understanding the Deathless (Amata) beyond anthropomorphic gods.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this provocative and profound discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses 'The God of Deathlessness.' Many Westerners are repelled by religious theism or dogmatic atheism. Luang Por bridges the gap by showing that what theistic traditions call 'God'—the eternal, unborn, uncreated, all-loving ground of reality—is precisely what the Buddha termed the *Amata-dhātu* (the Deathless Realm) or *Asaṅkhata Dhamma*. Rather than arguing about theological dogma, we can directly experience this unborn sanctuary right here in conscious stillness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**Beyond Atheism and Theism**: Transcending theological concepts through direct contemplative realization.

**The Unborn and Uncreated**: Contemplating Udāna 8.3: 'There is an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned...'

**The True Divine Sanctuary**: Living from the Deathless rather than praying to an external heavenly monarch.
