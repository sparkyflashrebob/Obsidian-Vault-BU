### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The path leading to the cessation of suffering; developing Right View and integrating the eight factors into a unified way of living.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a masterclass on the Fourth Noble Truth: the Noble Eightfold Path (*Ariya Aṭṭhaṅgika Magga*). The path is not a sequential ladder of academic virtues, but an interconnected organism of wisdom (*paññā*), virtue (*sīla*), and mental cultivation (*samādhi*). Its duty is *bhāvetabba*—to be cultivated and developed in every situation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**Right View (*Sammā-diṭṭhi*) as the Fore-runner**: Seeing all phenomena in terms of causes, conditions, and cessation.

**The Threefold Division**: Harmonizing Paññā, Sīla, and Samādhi so wisdom guides effort and stillness.

**Bhāvetabba (To Be Cultivated)**: Living the path at the dining table, in traffic, and on the meditation cushion.
