## 1. Archival & Historical Overview

From 1995 through 2008, **Venerable Ajahn Sumedho** made historic return visits to his home state of California, conducting intensive monastic and silent contemplative retreats at **Spirit Rock Meditation Center** in Woodacre, Marin County. Recorded live across four distinct milestones—**1995**, **1999**, **2001**, **2005**, and **2008**—this collection represents a watershed moment in the transmission of the Thai Forest Tradition to the Western world.

In these thirty archival discourses, Luang Por Sumedho addresses American Vipassanā students, monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and seasoned lay practitioners. Moving beyond academic Theravāda doctrine, he directly introduces the hallmarks of his radical contemplative teaching: **The Sound of Silence (*Nāda-yoga*)**, **The Knowing (*Buddho / Phū Rū*)**, **Tathatā ("The Way It Is")**, **Space & Consciousness (*Ākāsa / Viññāṇa*)**, and the **Unconditioned Deathless (*Asaṅkhata Dhamma*)**.
