### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Extending mettā to pleasant, unpleasant, painful, and messy conditions without discrimination; cultivating an indestructible refuge in kindness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho unpacks 'Mettā for All Conditions.' Most people practice loving-kindness selectively—wishing well to sweet puppies, dear friends, and serene meditation sessions, while cursing mosquitoes, difficult colleagues, and restless mental states. Luang Por teaches that genuine mettā must embrace the bad weather as well as the sunshine. When we offer loving goodwill to our own anger, pain, and confusion, their demonic power vanishes.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**Universal Hospitality**: Greeting difficult moods, bodily ailments, and external irritants like distinguished guests.

**Disarming Inner Warfare**: How fighting against defilements only fuels them with negative attention.

**The Karaṇīya Mettā Path**: Cultivating the motherly heart that cherishes all living beings as an only child.
