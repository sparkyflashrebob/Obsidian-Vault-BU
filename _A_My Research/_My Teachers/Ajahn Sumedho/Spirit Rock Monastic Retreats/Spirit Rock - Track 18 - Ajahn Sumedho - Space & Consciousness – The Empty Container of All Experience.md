Monastic retreat gathering and public discourses delivered at Spirit Rock Meditation Center in Marin County, California, addressing Western monastic candidates, senior lay practitioners, and Vipassanā students.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating space (ākāsa) and consciousness (viññāṇa); recognizing that all material forms and mental objects exist in empty space.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides meditators through a deep contemplative investigation of 'Space and Consciousness.' Most humans fixate exclusively on objects—material possessions, physical bodies, and discursive thoughts. Luang Por directs attention to the space *between* and *around* objects. Space cannot be broken, painted, or destroyed; similarly, conscious awareness (*viññāṇa*) provides the transparent, immaculate space in which all experiences come and go without leaving a mark.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**Contemplating Space (*Ākāsa-dhātu*)**: How tuning into the empty space in the room instantly tranquilizes mental chatter.

**Consciousness as the Space of Mind**: Recognizing that thoughts of greed, anger, or worry arise inside vast empty awareness.

**Suññatā (Emptiness)**: The liberating realization that the heart is inherently spacious, boundaryless, and immaculate.
