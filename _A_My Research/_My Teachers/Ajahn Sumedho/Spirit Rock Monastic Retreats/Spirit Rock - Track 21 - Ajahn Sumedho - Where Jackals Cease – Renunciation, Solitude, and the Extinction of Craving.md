### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The cessation of mental howling (craving, obsession, doubt); finding the sacred solitude where the jackals of desire cease their cries.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a poetic and mystical retreat discourse titled 'Where Jackals Cease.' Borrowing the ancient canonical image of the jackal prowling the cremation grounds and howling for carrion, Luang Por portrays the restless, ravenous nature of the unawakened human mind. Worldly desire continually howls for sensory food. Through patient monastic discipline, stillness, and insight, we arrive at the place of holy quietude—the sanctuary where the jackals cease to howl and the Deathless shines forth.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Howling Jackal of Desire**: Recognizing the endless, hungry complaints of the ego in meditation.

**The Power of Holy Renunciation (*Nekkhamma*)**: Stepping off the treadmill of feeding desires to discover natural satisfaction.

**The Silence of Nibbāna**: Resting in the pristine extinction of lust, hatred, and delusion (*rāgakkhaya, dosakkhaya, mohakkhaya*).
