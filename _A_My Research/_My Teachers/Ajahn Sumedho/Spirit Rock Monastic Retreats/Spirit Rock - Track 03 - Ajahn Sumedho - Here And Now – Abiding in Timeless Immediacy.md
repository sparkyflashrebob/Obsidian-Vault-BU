### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The simplicity and depth of the present moment; abandoning the fictional realms of 'then' and 'when'.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the radical immediacy of the 'Here and Now.' Most human suffering occurs in psychological time—replaying ancient memories or dreading imagined futures. Yet consciousness itself is always here and now. When we cease fleeing the present experience, even discomfort reveals its empty, transient nature.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Fiction of Future**: How spiritual ambition postpones awakening to some non-existent future date.

**The Timeless Gate**: The present moment is not a slice of clock time, but the timeless opening into unconditioned awareness.

**Resting in the Knowing**: Experiencing bodily sensations, sounds, and moods as they are right now.
