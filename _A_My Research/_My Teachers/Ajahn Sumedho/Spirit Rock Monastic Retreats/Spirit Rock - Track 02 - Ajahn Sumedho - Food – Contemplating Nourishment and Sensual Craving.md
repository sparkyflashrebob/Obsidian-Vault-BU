### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deep investigation into physical, sensory, and mental food (āhāra); disarming the compulsive hunger of sensual desire.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
During the 1999 Spirit Rock Monastic Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho addresses 'Food'—not merely physical eating, but the mind's voracious hunger for sensory stimulation, contact, and mental entertainment. The Buddha taught that all beings subsist on food (*sabbe sattā āhāraṭṭhitikā*). By examining physical appetite and emotional hunger with patient awareness, we discover the peace of true sufficiency.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Four Nutriments (*Āhāra*)**: Physical food, sensory contact, mental volition, and consciousness.

**Investigating the Hunger**: Noticing the impulse to snack on thoughts, news, and sensory treats to avoid emptiness.

**The Spiritual Feast**: Feeding the heart on mindfulness, virtue, and tranquility.
