### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The mathematical precision of enlightenment: four truths through three turnings equals twelve insights (tiparivaṭṭaṁ dvādasākāraṁ).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho presents his signature mathematical exposition of Dhamma: 'Four Times Three Equals Twelve.' In the first discourse at Isipatana, the Buddha declared that only when his vision of the Four Noble Truths in their three turnings and twelve aspects (*tiparivaṭṭaṁ dvādasākāraṁ*) was completely pure did he claim unexcelled awakening. Luang Por methodically unpacks the three phases—theoretical acknowledgement (*sacca-ñāṇa*), active duty (*kicca-ñāṇa*), and final accomplishment (*kata-ñāṇa*)—for each of the Four Truths, turning ancient doctrine into a living practical diagnostic.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Twelve Facets of Awakening**: How the 3x4 matrix covers every possible movement of spiritual life.

**From Belief to Realization**: Why studying doctrine without fulfilling the duty (*kicca*) leaves defilements untouched.

**The Unshakeable Vision**: Experiencing the final completion (*kata-ñāṇa*) where suffering has ceased forever.
