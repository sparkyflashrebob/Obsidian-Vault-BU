### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical surrender to suchness (Tathatā); abandoning idealistic demands and finding peace in the imperfect present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho returns to his crowning teaching of 'The Way It Is' during the 2005 monastic retreat. He shows how our western idealistic culture trains us to be perpetual critics—demanding that society, politicians, monasteries, spouses, and bodies match our mental ideals. When reality falls short, we suffer. Luang Por reveals that true peace is not finding an ideal world, but dropping our childish resistance and realizing Tathatā: things are the way they are.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Tyranny of the Ideal**: How comparing what is with what 'ought to be' poisons the heart with resentment.

**Tathatā as Awakening**: Embracing suchness as the profound cessation of spiritual and worldly argument.

**Compassionate Engagement**: Why accepting 'the way it is' empowers wise, effective action without anger.
