### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Samsāra is fundamentally imperfect, fragile, and flawed; awakening means ending our futile crusade to force it to be ideal.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a compassionate and freeing talk titled 'This Is Not The Ideal.' Humanity suffers from chronic existential disappointment because we carry an unconscious blueprint of how the world, our bodies, our relationships, and our practice *should* look. When plumbing breaks, bodies ache, or political turmoil erupts, we lament. Luang Por reminds us that by definition, conditioned existence (*saṅkhāra-loka*) is imperfect (*anicca, dukkha, anattā*). Peace begins the moment we stop demanding perfection from a world whose very nature is change.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Flaw in the Matrix**: Understanding that samsāra is designed to be unsatisfactory (*dukkha*).

**Ending the Grievance**: How our sense of entitlement to a painless life tortures the heart.

**Finding the Real Jewel**: Discovering the immaculate peace of Dhamma right in the middle of a broken world.
