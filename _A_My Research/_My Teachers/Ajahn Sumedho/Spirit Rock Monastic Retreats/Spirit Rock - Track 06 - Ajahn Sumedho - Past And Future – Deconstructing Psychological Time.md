### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how the twin shadows of memory and fantasy create the illusion of a personal ego bound in time.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates the mental constructs of 'Past' and 'Future.' Memories of who we used to be, childhood wounds, past achievements, and anxiety about aging and death are all transient thoughts arising *now*. When we recognize memories as current mental objects (*dhamma-saṅkhāra*) rather than historical realities, the burden of the personal past instantly drops away.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Trap of Memory**: How identifying with our past history keeps us imprisoned in old trauma and guilt.

**The Mirage of Tomorrow**: How future projections create unnecessary worry and existential tension.

**The Eternal Now**: Awakening to the unconditioned stillness in which time itself is witnessed.
