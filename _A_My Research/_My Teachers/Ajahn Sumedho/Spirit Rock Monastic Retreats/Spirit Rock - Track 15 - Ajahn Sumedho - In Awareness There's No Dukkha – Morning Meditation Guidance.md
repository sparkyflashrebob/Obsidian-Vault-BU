### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning meditation guidance; directly experiencing that while conditions are dukkha, pure awareness itself contains zero suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this foundational morning meditation address, Luang Por Sumedho offers practical guidance on the central axiom of his Dhamma: 'In Awareness There's No Dukkha.' Pain, fear, sleepiness, doubt, and physical aches are all conditions (*saṅkhāras*), and by their very nature they are dukkha. However, the awareness that knows the ache is not aching; the awareness that knows fear is not afraid. By shifting identity from the condition to the knowing, suffering dissolves on the spot.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Crucial Distinction**: Disentangling the conditioned object (*saṅkhāra*) from the unconditioned knowing (*paññā*).

**Somatic Pain on the Cushion**: Practical instructions for observing knee and back pain without generating aversion.

**Resting in the Sanctuary**: Experiencing the untouched peace that coexists with physical discomfort.
