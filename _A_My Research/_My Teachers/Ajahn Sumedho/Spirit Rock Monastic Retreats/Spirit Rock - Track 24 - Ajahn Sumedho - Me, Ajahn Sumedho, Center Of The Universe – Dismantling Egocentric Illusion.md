### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Spirit Rock resident teachers, visiting monastics from Abhayagiri and Amaravati, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The humorous and ruthless deconstruction of asmimāna (the conceit 'I am'); seeing through the lifelong fiction of being the hub of reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this masterwork of self-reflective humor, Luang Por Sumedho exposes the universal delusion: 'Me, Ajahn Sumedho, Center of the Universe.' Every unawakened human experiences life through a perceptual trick where they are the heroic central star of the movie and everyone else is merely background scenery or supporting actors. Luang Por demonstrates how practicing mindfulness turns the camera around, revealing that the personal ego is merely a fleeting blip inside vast impersonal nature.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Egocentric Distortion**: How our sensory nervous system naturally fools us into thinking we are the hub of creation.

**Asmimāna (The Scent of 'I Am')**: Why even advanced practitioners must dismantle the subtle feeling of being someone special.

**The Relief of Nobody-ness**: Experiencing the sheer, intoxicating joy of not having to protect an ego.
