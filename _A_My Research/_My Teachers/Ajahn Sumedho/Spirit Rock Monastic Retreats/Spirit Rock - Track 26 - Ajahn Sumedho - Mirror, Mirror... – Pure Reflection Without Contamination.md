### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The nature of consciousness as an unblemished mirror: reflecting beauty and ugliness with total clarity while remaining completely untainted.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho employs the timeless contemplative metaphor of the mirror in 'Mirror, Mirror...' When you hold a mirror before a garbage dump, does the mirror get dirty? When you hold it before a diamond, does the mirror become wealthy? Consciousness (*viññāṇa*) is precisely like a mirror: it flawlessly reflects anger, bliss, pain, music, boredom, and terror without ever being stained or improved by any of them. Resting as the mirror is the secret of supreme freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Pristine Nature of the Knower**: Understanding that awareness has no shape, color, gender, or defilement.

**The Delusion of Stained Awareness**: Why we mistakenly believe our pure consciousness has become dirty when negative thoughts arise.

**Living as the Mirror**: Responding to the world with crystal clarity and effortless compassion.
