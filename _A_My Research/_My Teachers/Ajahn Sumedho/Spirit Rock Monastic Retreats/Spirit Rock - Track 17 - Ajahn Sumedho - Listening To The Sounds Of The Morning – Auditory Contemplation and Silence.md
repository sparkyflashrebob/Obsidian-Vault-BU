### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Using the dawn soundscape—birds, breeze, distant traffic—to reveal the silent, empty background of consciousness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this morning discourse, Luang Por Sumedho invites meditators to listen attentively to the early morning sounds surrounding Spirit Rock. The morning chorus of birds, rustling eucalyptus trees, and distant highway murmurs are not interruptions to silence; they are the play of nature inside silence. By shifting attention from the sound itself to the silent space holding the sound, meditators gain direct entry into the unconditioned.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
**The Soundscape as Meditation**: Welcoming every bird call and mechanical rumble as a reflection of nature.

**The Background of Silence**: Noticing that sounds come and go, but the silent consciousness witnessing them is unbroken.

**The Sound of Silence (*Nāda*)**: Tuning in to the primordial inner vibration as a timeless contemplative anchor.
