### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in relaxed attention (paccuppanna-dhamma) rather than straining through the self-view of 'I have to attain something'.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho opens with the distinction between willful striving and receptive, relaxed attention. In meditation, the ego immediately assumes an active role: 'I must get concentrated, I must develop peace.' But peace is not an object to be manufactured by effort. By establishing receptive listening and open awareness, we recognize the present moment as it is, letting go of the illusion that we are helpless victims of our mental conditioning.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Relaxed Attention vs. Striving**: Shifting from the ego's ambition to quiet receptivity and spaciousness.
- **Resting in Paccuppanna-Dhamma**: Trusting the naturally aware quality of mind without creating a meditation persona.
- **Beyond Victimhood of Conditioning**: Seeing how conditioned emotional reactions dissolve when held in unconditioned knowing.
