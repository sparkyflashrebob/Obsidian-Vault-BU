### Circumstances
Monastic community retreat discourse delivered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery in Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, England, offering direct practice instructions to resident monastics, anagārikas, and international lay retreatants.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Amaravati monastic community, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating emotional contractions, somatic knots in the stomach, and realizing the emptiness of phenomena.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho answers in-depth questions from retreatants regarding stubborn emotional wounds, anger toward parents, and spiritual numbness. He explains how to investigate emotional contractions without getting lost in personal narrative. When emotional pain is felt somatically in the body and held in non-judgmental awareness, it reveals its essential nature: empty (*suñña*) of personal self.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Somatic Contractions in the Belly**: Softening physical knots of fear and rage rather than analyzing them.
- **The Myth of Spiritual Numbness**: Seeing numbness not as a failure, but as another conditioned state to be known.
- **Emptiness (*Suññatā*) as Living Truth**: Discovering that when clinging ceases, reality is inherently free and clear.
