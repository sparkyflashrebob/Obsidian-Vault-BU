Monastic community retreat discourse delivered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery in Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, England, offering direct practice instructions to resident monastics, anagārikas, and international lay retreatants.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Amaravati monastic community, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The balance between active social engagement and deep contemplative renunciation in serving humanity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
During the 1995 Winter Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a universal question among Western practitioners: 'How can I justify sitting in silent retreat while the world suffers?' Using the classic Sedaka Sutta parable of the acrobats, Luang Por demonstrates that taking care of one's own mind is the greatest service one can render the world. When we purify our hearts of greed and hatred, we cease poisoning the collective human stream.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Parable of the Acrobats**: Why inner balance is the indispensable prerequisite for helping others.
- **True Compassion vs. Anxious Rescuing**: Disentangling genuine *karuṇā* from neurotic codependency and savior complexes.
- **The Radiance of Inner Peace**: How an awakened, peaceful human being uplifts entire communities without saying a word.

| Method | Frantic social engineering, burnout, and spreading emotional stress | Purifying the mind first; acting from grounded wisdom and non-harming. |
