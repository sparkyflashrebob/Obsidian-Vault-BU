# Master Index: Amaravati Monastic Retreats & Archival Series
*Discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery (Hertfordshire, England)*

---

## 1. Archival Series Overview

The **Amaravati Monastic Retreats & Archival Series** preserves Venerable Ajahn Sumedho's deep contemplative guidance delivered to monastic residents, anagārikas, and international lay practitioners during intensive silent retreats at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery in Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, England.

Covering the landmark **May 2006 10-Day Retreat**, annual monastic **Vassa (Rains) Retreats**, deep **Winter Retreats (1989–2007)**, and archival thematic discourses, these teachings emphasize:
1. **Relaxed Attention (*Paccuppanna-Dhamma*)**: Moving beyond willpower-driven striving and resting in natural, receptive awareness.
2. **The Unconditioned Realm (*Asaṅkhata*)**: Disentangling knowing (*Buddho*) from the flux of temporary mental formations (*saṅkhāras*).
3. **Deconstructing Personality Belief (*Sakkāya-diṭṭhi*)**: Penetrating the emotional illusion of a separate, flawed ego.
4. **The Inner Sound of Silence (*Nāda*)**: Using subtle inner auditory vibration as a direct gateway into empty stillness.
5. **Living in Biological Conditionality (*Idappaccayatā*)**: Meeting mammalian threat responses, fear, and bodily pain with compassionate non-clinging.

Every talk in this collection has been ingested directly from primary master audio and processed through the authentic **Faster-Whisper (base.en, int8)** pipeline into full verbatim prose formatted into coherent paragraph units (~60–120 words) with responsive Markdown tables and dynamic Pāli doctrinal tagging.
