### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the Asaṅkhata-dhamma: understanding the unconditioned not as a distant ideal, but as present knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Closing this core retreat series during the 2006 Vassa, Luang Por Sumedho offers a penetrating reflection on the Unconditioned (*asaṅkhata*). Many meditators conceive of the Unconditioned as a transcendent blankness or an esoteric state achieved after lifetimes of hardship. Luang Por points out that the Unconditioned is the unentangled space of knowing that is present right now, aware of every sound, breath, and thought.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Sutta Proof of the Unborn**: Reading the Buddha's classic declaration in the Udāna.
- **Awareness as the Asaṅkhata**: How awareness itself is unconditioned by the objects it reflects.
- **Resting as the One Who Knows**: Finding permanent sanctuary in Buddho.
