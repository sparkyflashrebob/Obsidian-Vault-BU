### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating sense consciousness (viññāṇa) and discovering consciousness without surface (viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
During the 2001 Vassa Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho delves into the subtlety of consciousness. Sati allows us to see how sense consciousness arises through contact between sensory organs and objects. Rather than identifying with what is perceived, we learn to recognize the clear, luminous, reflective quality of consciousness itself—the silent canvas upon which all experiences arise and vanish.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Conditioned Consciousness vs. Pure Knowing**: Distinguishing sensory contact from unattached awareness.
- **Consciousness as a Mirror**: How the mirror reflects beautiful and ugly objects without being stained by either.
- **Resting in the Luminous Mind**: Experiencing the mind's natural radiance when freed from grasping.
