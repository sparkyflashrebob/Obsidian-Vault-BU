### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing suffering through the three stages of the Four Noble Truths and dependent origination.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich Q&A session, Luang Por Sumedho explores the classical Theravāda framework of the Four Noble Truths. He demonstrates how each truth has three stages of realization (Tiparivaṭṭaṁ Dvādasākāraṁ): understanding the statement, realizing the duty (understanding dukkha, abandoning craving, realizing cessation, cultivating the path), and knowing that the duty is accomplished.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Twelve Aspects of Wisdom**: Moving beyond intellectual theory to direct realization.
- **Cessation (*Nirodha*) as Reality**: Experiencing the actual ending of desire in this very life.
- **Dependent Origination in Daily Experience**: Tracing how contact (*phassa*) leads to feeling (*vedanā*) and craving (*taṇhā*).

| 1. Dukkha | There is suffering. | Suffering should be understood (*pariññeyya*). | Suffering has been understood (*pariññāta*). |
| 2. Samudaya | There is the origin of suffering. | Craving should be abandoned (*pahātabba*). | Craving has been abandoned (*pahīna*). |
| 3. Nirodha | There is the cessation of suffering. | Cessation should be realized (*sacchikātabba*). | Cessation has been realized (*sacchikata*). |
| 4. Magga | There is the Eightfold Path. | The path should be developed (*bhāvetabba*). | The path has been developed (*bhāvita*). |
