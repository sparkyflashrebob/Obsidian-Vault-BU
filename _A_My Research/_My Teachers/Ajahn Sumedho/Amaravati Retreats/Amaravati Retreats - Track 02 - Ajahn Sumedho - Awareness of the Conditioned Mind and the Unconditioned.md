### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Distinguishing between the fleeting world of conditions (saṅkhāras) and the unconditioned presence (asaṅkhata-dhamma) that knows them.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Reflecting on the nature of mental formations, Luang Por Sumedho guides retreatants to notice the difference between objects of mind and the awareness that holds them. Every thought, emotion, memory, and physical sensation is a condition—born of causes, enduring temporarily, and ceasing. The unconditioned is not a mysterious metaphysical state far away, but the simple, silent reality of knowing that is present right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Nature of Saṅkhāras**: Investigating how thoughts arise and pass without leaving a permanent core.
- **The Space of Non-Clinging**: Finding stability in the silent ground of knowing rather than within temporary emotional weather.
- **The Deathless Reality**: Realizing Nibbāna not as extinction, but as freedom from entanglement in conditions.
