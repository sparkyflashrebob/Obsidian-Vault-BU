### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct pointer to the unborn, uncreated, and deathless reality (amata-dhātu) available in every moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this inspiring and luminous retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho welcomes retreatants into the Deathless (*amata*). The conditioned mind is constantly chasing death—investing its love in things that must perish. But when awareness turns back upon itself, it discovers the dimension that was never born, that does not age, and that cannot die. This is the ultimate refuge taught by the Buddha.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Noble Search (*Ariyapariyesanā*)**: Moving from the pursuit of transient pleasures to seeking the Deathless.
- **The Unborn Dimension**: Realizing that pure awareness does not possess physical weight, color, or age.
- **Living from the Deathless**: Bringing transcendent peace into everyday speech, work, and community relationships.
