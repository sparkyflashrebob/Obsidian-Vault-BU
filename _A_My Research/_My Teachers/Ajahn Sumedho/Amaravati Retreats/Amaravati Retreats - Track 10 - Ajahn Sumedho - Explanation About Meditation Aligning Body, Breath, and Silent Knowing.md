### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Foundational somatic grounding, natural breath awareness, and tuning into the inner Sound of Silence (nāda).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivering clear and practical meditation instructions during the 2007 Vassa Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through posture, breathing, and inner listening. Rather than forcing the breath into unnatural rhythms, he advises relaxing the body, opening sensory awareness, and tuning into the primal inner frequency known as the Sound of Silence—an anchor that cuts through mental wandering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Somatic Alignment**: Establishing an upright, dignified, and relaxed physical posture.
- **Breath as Natural Rhythm**: Allowing the breath to be long, short, shallow, or deep without management.
- **The Nāda Sound as a Meditation Anchor**: Accessing the inner auditory hum to stabilize concentration.
