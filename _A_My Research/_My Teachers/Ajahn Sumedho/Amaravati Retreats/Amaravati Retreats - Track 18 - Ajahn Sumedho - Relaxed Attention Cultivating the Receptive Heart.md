### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Balancing effort and relaxation, opening the energetic center of the heart, and trusting the natural Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the subtle balance between energy and tranquility in Buddhist meditation. Western practitioners often suffer from an excess of willpower (*viriya*) that produces tight jaws, furrowed brows, and emotional exhaustion. Relaxed attention is not lazy sleepiness; it is an alert, receptive open-heartedness that allows reality to present itself without interference.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Disease of Over-Effort**: Recognizing how the ego attempts to dominate meditation through brute force.
- **Balancing the Spiritual Faculties**: Harmonizing faith with wisdom, and energy with samādhi.
- **Somatic Softening**: Allowing the chest, belly, and face to relax while maintaining upright posture.
