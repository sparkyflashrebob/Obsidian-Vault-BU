### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Amaravati monastic community, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay retreatants.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Simplicity, natural mindfulness, and bringing clear attention to the transitions of retreat life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this short, sparkling morning reflection near the close of the May 2006 10-Day Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho emphasizes that enlightenment is not found in complex philosophy, but in the ordinary act of paying attention. Whether putting on sandals, closing a door, or chewing food, attention brings us directly into communion with the unconditioned.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Simplicity of Sati**: Shifting attention from mental concepts to raw physical movement.
- **Transitions as Training**: Using the moments between meditation sittings as the core practice.
- **The Wonder of the Ordinary**: Finding boundless peace in simple everyday sensations.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Moment | Unconscious Inattention | Mindful Attention (*Sati*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Walking into a Hall | Mind lost in thoughts; body mechanically moving like an automaton | Feeling the weight of feet, the temperature of air, and sensory contact. |
| Opening a Door | Rushing through to get to the next goal with impatient haste | Turning the handle with care and quiet awareness of touch. |
| Waiting in Line | Restlessness, checking phone/clock, mental complaining | Using waiting as an opportunity to rest in breath and the Sound of Silence. |
