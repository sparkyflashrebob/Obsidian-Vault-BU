### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Liberating the mind from temporal delusion, past regrets, future anxieties, and resting in timeless presence (akāliko).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho exposes the pervasive illusion of time. The past has no objective existence right now—it exists entirely as a memory thought arising in the present moment. The future has not arrived—it exists solely as imagination and projection. Suffering only occurs when we invest belief in these mental phantoms. When we awaken to the here and now, we discover the timeless (*akāliko*) freedom of Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Fabricated Past**: Seeing guilt and nostalgia as transient thoughts occurring right now.
- **The Fabricated Future**: Recognizing planning and anxiety as present mental projections.
- **The Timeless Sanctuary**: Stepping out of linear psychological time into unconditioned presence.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Dimension of Time | Psychological Delusion (*Moha*) | Direct Dhamma Realization (*Akāliko*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The Past | Heavy identity built on childhood trauma, former glories, and regrets | A memory thought arising in awareness; completely gone upon cessation. |
| The Future | Constant anticipation, dread, planning, and goal-oriented anxiety | An imaginary concept; reality will always arrive only as the present. |
| The Present | A mere stepping stone or inconvenience on the way to the future | The only reality that actually exists; the direct portal to the Deathless. |
