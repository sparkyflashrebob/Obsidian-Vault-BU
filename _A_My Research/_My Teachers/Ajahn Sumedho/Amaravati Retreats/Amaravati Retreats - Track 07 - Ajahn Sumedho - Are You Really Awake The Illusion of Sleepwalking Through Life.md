### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Challenging the mechanical trance of cultural conditioning and discovering genuine awakened presence (Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho opens the 1999 Winter Retreat by asking retreatants whether they are truly awake or simply acting out deeply ingrained mental habits. Most human lives are lived in a reactive, hypnotic daydream ruled by social conditioning, memories, and anxiety about the future. Awakening is not an esoteric mystical trance, but the sharp, clear recognition of reality as it is right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Trance of Everyday Routine**: Seeing how easily the mind falls into mechanical thought patterns.
- **Buddho as the Awakened One**: Reconnecting with the unconditioned awareness that knows thinking without getting lost in it.
- **The Power of Winter Retreat**: Using monastic silence and cold weather as a crucible for genuine self-honesty.
