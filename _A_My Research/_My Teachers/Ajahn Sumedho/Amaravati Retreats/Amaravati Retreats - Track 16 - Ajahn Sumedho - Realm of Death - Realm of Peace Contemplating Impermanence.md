### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplation of mortality (maraṇānussati) and discovering the deathless realm of peace (amata-dhātu).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this early historical discourse from the 1989 Winter Retreat, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into the profound contemplation of death. Everything born into the world of conditions must decay and die. When we evade the reality of death, we live in constant subtle anxiety. By embracing mortality fully, the mind lets go of superficial attachments and awakens to the deathless realm of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Maraṇānussati as a Liberating Shield**: How recollecting death cuts through petty grievances and vanity.
- **The Realm of Death**: Understanding the totality of sensory existence as subject to cessation.
- **The Deathless Realm (*Amata-dhātu*)**: Experiencing the unconditioned ground that was never born and never dies.
