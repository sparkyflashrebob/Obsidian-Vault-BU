### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the illusion of self through the experiential investigation of the five aggregates (khandhas).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a deep sutta reflection on the Five Khandhas: form (*rūpa*), feeling (*vedanā*), perception (*saññā*), mental formations (*saṅkhāra*), and consciousness (*viññāṇa*). Rather than treating the aggregates as abstract scholastic categories, he invites practitioners to observe them directly in the body and mind. None of these five components can be owned, controlled permanently, or called 'I' or 'mine'.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Form and Somatic Reality**: Seeing the physical body as an element of nature rather than personal property.
- **Feeling as Bare Sensation**: Disentangling pleasant, unpleasant, and neutral feeling tones from craving and aversion.
- **Perception and Memory**: Recognizing labels and concepts as fleeting interpretations, not final reality.
- **Consciousness as Knowing Presence**: Distinguishing between sensory awareness and the unconditioned refuge.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Aggregate (*Khandha*) | Delusion of Self (*Upādāna*) | Wisdom Insight (*Vipassanā*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Form (*Rūpa*) | 'This body is me; its aging and illness are my fault.' | The physical organism is biological nature, subject to natural conditionality. |
| Feeling (*Vedanā*) | 'I am hurting; this pain is unbearable.' | Sensory tone is impermanent; unpleasant feeling is not suffering unless resisted. |
| Perception (*Saññā*) | 'My interpretations and labels are objective facts.' | Labels and memories are conditioned constructs created by the mind. |
| Formations (*Saṅkhāra*) | 'My opinions, moods, and judgments define my soul.' | Mental chatter and emotional impulses arise and vanish upon causes. |
| Consciousness (*Viññāṇa*) | 'I am the perceiver inside the head looking out.' | Sense consciousness arises in dependence upon sense organ and contact. |

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered
