### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Confronting deep somatic dread, the biological reflex for control, and resting in the fearlessness of awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In the shadow of global upheaval in late 2001, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the instinctual human obsession with control. Fear is a natural mammalian survival response, but when the thinking mind identifies with fear, it constructs elaborate systems of control, paranoia, and defensive hostility. By holding fear directly in the body with compassionate mindfulness, we discover an unshakeable peace that external circumstances cannot threaten.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Biology of Threat**: Recognizing belly tightness and rapid pulse as natural conditionality (*idappaccayatā*).
- **The Futility of Total Control**: Understanding that trying to control impermanent phenomena is the very essence of dukkha.
- **Resting in the Deathless**: Moving from the animal brain's dread to the Buddha's fearlessness (*abhaya*).
