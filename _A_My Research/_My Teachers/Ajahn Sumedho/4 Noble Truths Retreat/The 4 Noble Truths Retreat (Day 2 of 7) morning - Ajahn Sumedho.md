## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery, Bright Hill Road, Singapore, hosted by the Buddha Dhamma Foundation and Venerable Sik Kwang Sheng.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: SN 22.59 (Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta), AN 3.136 (Dhamma-niyāma Sutta).

### Circumstances
Morning discourse from the intensive 7-day Four Noble Truths retreat held in Singapore in 2006, guiding international and Singaporean lay practitioners into the experiential realization of non-attachment and unconditioned awareness.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Host**: Venerable Sik Kwang Sheng (Abbot of Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery) & The Buddha Dhamma Foundation.
- **Assembly**: Hundreds of Singaporean and international retreatants observing the Eight Precepts and noble silence.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the universal law of impermanence (anicca) across all physical and mental conditions (saṅkhāras); establishing the seat of the Knower (Phū Rū / Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the second morning, Luang Por Sumedho guides meditators to establish themselves as the silent witness (*the one who knows* / *Phū Rū*). Every physical sensation in the knees, back, and lungs, and every mental impulse, thought, or memory, is a condition (*saṅkhāra*). By their very nature, conditions arise, reach a peak, and cease. Suffering occurs only when we jump into conditions and claim them as 'me' or 'mine'. When we step back into the unconditioned space of awareness, we see that conditions come and go, while awareness remains pure, spacious, and unshakeable.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Law of Anicca**: Why every created, formed, and compounded thing is inherently transient.
- **The Witnessing Position (Buddho)**: Disentangling the pure conscious witness from the mental objects passing across the screen of the mind.
- **Space and Form**: Recognizing that physical sensations and emotional storms occur within vast, untroubled conscious space.
