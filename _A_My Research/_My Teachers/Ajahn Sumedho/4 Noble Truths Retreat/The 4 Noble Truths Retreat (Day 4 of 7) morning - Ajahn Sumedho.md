## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery, Bright Hill Road, Singapore, hosted by the Buddha Dhamma Foundation and Venerable Sik Kwang Sheng.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 10 (Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta - Postures & Full Awareness), SN 45.8 (Magga-vibhaṅga Sutta - Right Effort).

### Circumstances
Morning discourse from the intensive 7-day Four Noble Truths retreat held in Singapore in 2006, guiding international and Singaporean lay practitioners into the experiential realization of non-attachment and unconditioned awareness.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Host**: Venerable Sik Kwang Sheng (Abbot of Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery) & The Buddha Dhamma Foundation.
- **Assembly**: Hundreds of Singaporean and international retreatants observing the Eight Precepts and noble silence.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Integrating mindfulness across the four postures (iriyāpatha); redefining Right Effort (sammā-vāyāma) not as violent striving, but as relaxed, effortless presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On Day 4, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the common meditator trap of striving and spiritual aggression. Many practitioners believe that Right Effort means clenching the jaw, straining the will, and forcing the mind into mystical trances. Luang Por reveals that true Right Effort is often 'no effort'—the relaxed, gentle willingness to simply be here and now, sustaining receptive attention across sitting, standing, walking, and lying down. When tension is abandoned, natural mindfulness (*sati*) flowers effortlessly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Fallacy of Spiritual Aggression**: How ambitious ego-striving creates subtle aversion and exhaustion.
- **The Four Postures as Contemplative Arenas**: Maintaining continuous continuity of awareness through sitting, walking, standing, and eating.
- **Effortless Attentiveness**: Resting in the effortless alertness of the heart that needs no forceful manipulation.
