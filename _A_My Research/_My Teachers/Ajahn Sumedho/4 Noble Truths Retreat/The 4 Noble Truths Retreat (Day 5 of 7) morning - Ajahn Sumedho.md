### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Four Noble Truths as an action-oriented diagnostic signpost rather than a religious dogma; realizing the bliss of cessation (nirodha).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this pivotal Day 5 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the core methodology of the Buddha: the Four Noble Truths are a directional signpost, not a philosophical dogma. Religious people often make the mistake of worshipping the signpost, arguing about the words on the sign, and setting up doctrines around it, while completely failing to walk in the direction it points! The First Truth points to understanding suffering; the Second to letting go of craving; the Third to realizing cessation (*nirodha*); and the Fourth to cultivating the Path. Realizing cessation is uncovering the sublime, unconditioned bliss that exists right now when craving ceases.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Signpost vs. The Destination**: Worshipping Buddhist doctrines vs. applying the Four Noble Truths as immediate medicine.
- **Samatha vs. Vipassanā**: Why temporary concentration states cannot liberate the heart without direct insight into cessation.
- **The Bliss of Nirodha**: Experiencing the cool, unshakeable peace that reveals itself whenever grasping stops.
