Morning discourse from the intensive 7-day Four Noble Truths retreat held in Singapore in 2006, guiding international and Singaporean lay practitioners into the experiential realization of non-attachment and unconditioned awareness.

- **Assembly**: Hundreds of Singaporean and international retreatants observing the Eight Precepts and noble silence.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Trusting the natural, unadorned presence of conscious awareness; the transformative mantra 'It's like this' as the ultimate solvent of reactivity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
As the retreat enters its penultimate day, Luang Por Sumedho presents his signature contemplative key: trusting pure awareness through the phrase 'It's like this.' When pleasant conditions arise, *it's like this*; when painful feelings, doubt, noise, or grief arise, *it's like this*. This simple reflection disarms the judgmental ego, stops proliferating narratives (*papañca*), and returns attention directly to the unconditioned knowing. Aware presence is not something to be constructed or manufactured—it is already here, timeless, luminous, and free.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Simplicity of Pure Knowing**: Awakening is not producing an extraordinary state, but noticing the awareness that is already present.
- **The Power of 'It's Like This'**: How three simple words dissolve the compulsion to fix, manage, or fight reality.
- **Integrating Practice into Lay Life**: Carrying the sanctuary of mindful awareness back into the hustle and commerce of modern Singapore.
