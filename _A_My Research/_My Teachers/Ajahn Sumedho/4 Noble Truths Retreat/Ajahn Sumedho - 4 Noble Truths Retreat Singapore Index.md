# The 4 Noble Truths Retreat (Singapore, 2006) — Master Archival Index & Contemplative Compendium

> [!NOTE] Historical & Contemplative Significance
> Held in **2006** at the historic **Kong Meng San Phor Kark See Monastery** (*Bright Hill*) in Singapore, this intensive 7-day retreat represents Luang Por Sumedho’s most comprehensive and systematic pedagogical exposition of the Buddha’s First Discourse: the **Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta** (*SN 56.11*).
> 
> Moving far beyond intellectual theory or academic Buddhist terminology, Luang Por presents the **Four Noble Truths** in their three aspects and twelve insights (*tiparivaṭṭaṁ dvādasākāraṁ*) as an immediate, practical diagnostic medicine for daily modern life.

## 🏛️ The Three Aspects & Twelve Insights Matrix (*Tiparivaṭṭaṁ Dvādasākāraṁ*)

Luang Por Sumedho structures the entire retreat around the exact diagnostic schema taught by the Buddha to the five ascetics at the Deer Park in Sarnath:

| Noble Truth | 1. Statement of Truth (*Sacca-ñāṇa*) | 2. Required Duty / Task (*Kicca-ñāṇa*) | 3. Realization of Completion (*Kata-ñāṇa*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Dukkha (Suffering)** | *"There is suffering in conditioned life."* | It is to be **understood** (*pariññeyya*). | It has been **fully understood** (*pariññāta*). |
| **2. Samudaya (Origin)** | *"Craving (taṇhā) is the origin of suffering."* | It is to be **abandoned** (*pahātabba*). | It has been **fully abandoned** (*pahīna*). |
| **3. Nirodha (Cessation)** | *"There is the cessation of suffering (cooling)."* | It is to be **realized** (*sacchikātabba*). | It has been **directly realized** (*sacchikata*). |
| **4. Magga (The Path)** | *"There is the Noble Eightfold Path."* | It is to be **cultivated** (*bhāvetabba*). | It has been **fully cultivated** (*bhāvita*). |
