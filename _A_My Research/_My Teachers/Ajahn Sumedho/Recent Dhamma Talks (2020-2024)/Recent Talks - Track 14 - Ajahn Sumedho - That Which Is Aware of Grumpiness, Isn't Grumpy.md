### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Liberating emotional states through the realization that awareness does not share the qualities of what it knows.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
With profound warmth and gentle humor, Luang Por Sumedho offers a liberating contemplative principle: 'That which is aware of grumpiness isn't grumpy.' When irritability, pettiness, or bad moods arise, we usually identify with them: 'I am so grumpy today.' But awareness is never grumpy. Awareness of greed is not greedy; awareness of sadness is not sad. Resting in awareness dissolves the tyranny of moods.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Impersonal Mirror**: Awareness reflects dirty water without becoming dirty.
- **Meeting Irritability Without Judgment**: Allowing bad temper to reveal its conditional, transient nature.
- **The Joy of Cittānupassanā**: Mindful investigation of emotional weather without despair.
