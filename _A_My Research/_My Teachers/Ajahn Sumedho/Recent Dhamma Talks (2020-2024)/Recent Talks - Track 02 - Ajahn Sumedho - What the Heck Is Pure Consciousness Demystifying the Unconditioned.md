### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying 'pure consciousness' and 'unentangled knowing' from high-flown mystical abstractions into immediate sensory reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
With his characteristic earthy humor, Luang Por Sumedho tackles the grand spiritual term 'pure consciousness'. Meditators often turn it into a holy, elusive peak experience. Luang Por brings it right down to earth: pure consciousness is simply the open, non-seeking awareness that is already present before you attach a name or judgment to what you hear, see, or feel.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Stripping Away Mystical Clichés**: Moving from romantic ideals of enlightenment to direct present knowing.
- **The Pre-Conceptual Space**: Noticing the gap between sensory contact and the arising of labels.
- **The Deathless Available Right Now**: Experiencing Nibbāna as the unconditioned ground beneath daily experience.

| Category | Conceptual / Mystical Fantasy | Direct Realization (*Ehipassiko*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pure Consciousness | A magical trance with flashing lights, celestial visions, and ecstasy | The ordinary, quiet, luminous knowing of sensations and silence. |
| Requirement | Years of torturous asceticism and superhuman mental gymnastics | Simply pausing and noticing what remains when grasping ceases. |
| Refuge | A temporary altered state that inevitably fades away | The uncreated reality (*asaṅkhata*) that never changes or decays. |
