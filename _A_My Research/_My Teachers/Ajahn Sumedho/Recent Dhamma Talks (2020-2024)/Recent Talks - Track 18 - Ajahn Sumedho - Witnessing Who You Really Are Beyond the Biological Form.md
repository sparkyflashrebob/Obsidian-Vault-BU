## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location
Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Hertfordshire, England.

### Canonical Setting
SN 22.59 (Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta), MN 10 (Kāyagatāsati)

### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct inquiry into identity: moving beyond the biological organism to recognize the deathless knowing presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this tender and uncompromising discourse, Luang Por Sumedho leads retreatants through the ultimate self-inquiry: 'Who are you really?' We naturally assume we are our physical body, our medical diagnoses, our personality quirks, and our age. But when we look into the nature of awareness, we see that what is knowing the body has no age, no gender, and no physical boundaries.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Biological Organism as Nature**: Seeing the body as four elements (*dhātu*) borrowed from the earth.
- **The Witnessing Presence**: Realizing that the awareness of aging is not aging.
- **The Deathless Identity**: Taking our stand in the unconditioned rather than the mortal vessel.
