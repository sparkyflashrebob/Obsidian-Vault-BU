### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Stepping back from the compulsive habit of analyzing, interpreting, and thinking about thoughts.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses one of the most stubborn traps in meditation: trying to solve thinking with more thinking. When an unwanted thought arises, the mind immediately comments on it, judges it, and invents strategies to get rid of it. Awakening occurs when we realize that the awareness of a thought is not thinking. We can simply let thoughts be as they are, without taking them personally.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Meta-Thinking Trap**: Noticing how the ego manufactures a 'thinker' to manage thinking.
- **The Silence Behind the Words**: Resting in the vast, unformed space that precedes and outlasts every concept.
- **Direct Disidentification**: Recognizing that thoughts are natural weather patterns of consciousness.
