### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The immediate realization of the timeless (akāliko) Dhamma beyond the linear tyranny of psychological time.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in late 2023, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the fundamental Buddhist concept of timelessness (*akāliko*). Psychological time is a mental fabrication: the past is a memory thought, the future is an anticipation thought. When awareness recognizes thinking as thoughts arising in the present moment, linear time collapses, revealing the timeless, spacious realm of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Hall of Mirrors of Past and Future**: Seeing how identity requires psychological time to survive.
- **Akāliko as Instantaneous Refuge**: Why awakening does not require time or evolutionary stages.
- **Living in the Eternal Present**: Bringing timeless awareness into elderly physical aging and bodily decline.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Dimension | Linear Psychological Time (*Kāla*) | The Timeless Dhamma (*Akāliko*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Structure | Past (regret/nostalgia) → Present (inconvenience) → Future (hope/fear) | The undivided, spacious immediacy of the present moment (*paccuppanna*). |
| Egoic Function | Feeds the narrative of 'becoming someone better in the future' | Reveals that there is no 'someone' to become; awareness is already whole. |
| Liberation | Always deferred to a future retreat, lifetime, or enlightened state | Discovered immediately upon the cessation of mental grasping right now. |
