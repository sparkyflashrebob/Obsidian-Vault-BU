Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reframing existential purpose: from worldly achievement to contemplative awakening and service.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho tackles the existential question that haunts modern society: 'What is the purpose of life?' Conventional society defines purpose through career advancement, financial accumulation, and family lineage. But because all conditioned things end in death, worldly purpose inevitably leads to despair. Luang Por reveals that the true purpose of human life is awakening—using our human faculties to transcend suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mirage of Worldly Success**: Why fame, money, and status leave the human heart hollow.
- **The Human Realm as the Rare Opportunity**: Understanding *manussatta-paṭilābho*—the rare blessing of human birth.
- **Awakening as the Ultimate Telos**: Using sensory life as a classroom for liberation.
