### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Overcoming Cartesian rationalism ('I think, therefore I am') and recognizing doubt (vicikicchā) as an impermanent condition.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this profound philosophical reflection, Luang Por Sumedho flips René Descartes' famous dictum: instead of 'I think, therefore I am', in Dhamma we see 'I think, therefore I doubt'. Thinking by its very nature fragments reality into subject and object, creating endless uncertainty. Liberation is found not by discovering the perfect intellectual theory, but by resting in the knowing that holds doubt without being shaken.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Western Obsession with Rationality**: Exposing the limitations of intellectual analysis in spiritual life.
- **The Nature of Vicikicchā**: Recognizing skeptical doubt as an energy pattern, not an objective truth.
- **Beyond the Divided Mind**: Resting in intuitive, non-dual awareness (*paññā*).
