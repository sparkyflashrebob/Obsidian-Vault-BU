Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contrasting external auditory noise and internal mental noise with the eternal, primordial Sound of Silence (nāda).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into deep auditory contemplation. The world is full of noise—traffic, news, social media, and internal mental chatter. But behind all noise is silence. By tuning into the inner Sound of Silence (*nāda*), we access an immediate anchor of spacious peace that no external cacophony can disturb.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Acoustic vs. The Primordial**: Distinguishing sensory sound waves from the inner frequency of awareness.
- **Nāda as a Samādhi Portal**: Using the inner listening tone to steady concentration and cut off obsessive thoughts.
- **The Silence of Nibbāna**: Experiencing the unconditioned stillness that holds all transient sounds.
