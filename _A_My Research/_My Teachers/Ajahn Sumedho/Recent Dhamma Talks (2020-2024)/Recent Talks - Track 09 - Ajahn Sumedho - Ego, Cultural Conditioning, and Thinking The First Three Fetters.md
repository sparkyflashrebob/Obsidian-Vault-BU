## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location
Amaravati Buddhist Monastery / Wat Ratanawan.

### Canonical Setting
SN 55.1 (Rāja Sutta), MN 22 (Alagaddūpama Sutta)

### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound morning Q&A dismantling personality belief (sakkāya-diṭṭhi), cultural habits, and the first three fetters.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this lucid dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the first three fetters that bind human consciousness. Stream-entry (*sotāpatti*) is not an unattainable myth; it begins with the experiential insight that our personality, cultural conditioning, and thoughts are not 'who we are'. When personality belief is dropped, skeptical doubt and obsessive adherence to outer rituals vanish naturally.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Cultural Masks as Saṅkhāras**: Seeing American, British, or Thai identity as conditioned programs.
- **Sakkāya-diṭṭhi in Plain Language**: The simple realization that the body and thinking mind are unowned nature.
- **The First Glimpse of Nibbāna**: How stream-entry permanently reorients the heart toward awakening.
