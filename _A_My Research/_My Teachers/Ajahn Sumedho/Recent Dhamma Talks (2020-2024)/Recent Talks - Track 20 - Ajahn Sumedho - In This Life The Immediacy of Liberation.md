## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location
Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Hertfordshire, England.

### Canonical Setting
SN 35.80 (Avijjāpahāna Sutta), AN 4.41 (Samādhi Sutta)

### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Realizing the fruits of practice in this very life (diṭṭhadhamme) rather than deferring freedom to future lifetimes.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on 30 May 2024 just before his 90th birthday, Luang Por Sumedho gives a ringing, joyful declaration: liberation is possible *in this very life* (*diṭṭhe va dhamme*). So many traditions postpone awakening to future existences or celestial realms. But the Buddha emphasized immediate verification. When craving stops, Nibbāna is experienced right here, right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Urgent Now**: Overcoming the procrastinating habit of deferring awakening.
- **Diṭṭhadhamma-Nibbāna**: The direct taste of cessation in the midst of daily activity.
- **A Lifetime of Verification**: Luang Por's retrospective on 58 years in the monastic robes.
