### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The crucial distinction between religious beliefs (diṭṭhi) and liberating experiential wisdom (paññā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in early 2024, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the role of beliefs in spiritual practice. Religions often devolve into tribal warfare over beliefs, dogmas, and creeds. The Buddha never asked anyone to blindly believe his words; he taught *ehipassiko*—come and see for yourself. Beliefs are mental constructs; wisdom is the direct seeing of things as they are (*yathābhūta-ñāṇadassana*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Kālāma Sutta Standard**: Not relying on hearsay, tradition, scripture, or charisma.
- **The Raft Metaphor**: Using Buddhist concepts to cross the river, not to carry on one's head.
- **From Belief to Knowing**: Transforming philosophical conviction into real-time awakening.
