### Canonical Setting

### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The culmination of practice in perfect understanding (sammā-diṭṭhi): seeing that all conditioned things are not self.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this culminating archival talk from 2024, Luang Por Sumedho offers a serene synthesis of his entire teaching career: 'Perfect Understanding'. Perfect understanding is not knowing everything about the universe; it is the simple, complete understanding of the Four Noble Truths. When we understand suffering, its origin, its cessation, and the path, the heart is completely liberated.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Consummation of Sammā-Diṭṭhi**: Moving from mundane right view to transcendent insight.
- **The Simplicity of the End**: Discovering that the end of the spiritual path is return to ordinary innocence.
- **The Legacy of Practice**: Leaving behind a clear compass for future generations of seekers.
