### Location

### Canonical Setting

### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Re-examining our core motivation: moving beyond spiritual consumerism to the noble quest for the cessation of suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho invites meditators to look deeply into their fundamental motivation for spiritual practice. Many come to meditation seeking relaxation, stress reduction, or personal enhancement. While those are natural benefits, the true aim of the Buddha's path is far more radical: the complete dismantling of craving, self-delusion, and the realization of unshakeable liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Beyond Spiritual Tourism**: Transitioning from superficial curiosity to resolute monastic dedication.
- **The Noble Search (*Ariyapariyesanā*)**: Choosing the pursuit of the Deathless over temporary worldly treats.
- **Sammā-Diṭṭhi as Compass**: Aligning every action with the Four Noble Truths.
