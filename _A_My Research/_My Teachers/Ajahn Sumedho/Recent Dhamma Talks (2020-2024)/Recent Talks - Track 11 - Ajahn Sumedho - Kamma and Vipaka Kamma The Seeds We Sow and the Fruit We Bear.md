### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The mechanics of moral intentionality (kamma) versus experiential result (vipāka) in daily life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this short and potent reflection, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the core Buddhist doctrine of action (*kamma*) and its result (*vipāka*). Meditators often misunderstand kamma as deterministic fatalism. Luang Por shows that vipāka is what enters our senses right now—the sights, sounds, bodily aches, and events we cannot control. Kamma is our present mental reaction: will we react with greed and hatred, or with mindful wisdom?

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Intention Is Kamma**: Cetanāahaṁ bhikkhave kammaṁ vadāmi (Intention, monastics, is what I call action).
- **Vipāka as What Enters the Senses**: Recognizing past fruit without grievance or victimization.
- **Creating Freedom Right Now**: How wise reflection (*yoniso manasikāra*) transforms painful vipāka into liberating wisdom.
