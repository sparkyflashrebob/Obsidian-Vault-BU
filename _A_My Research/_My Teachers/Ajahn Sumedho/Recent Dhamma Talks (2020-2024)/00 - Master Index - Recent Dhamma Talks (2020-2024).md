# Master Index: Recent Dhamma Talks (2020–2024)
*Late-stage contemplative discourses and dialogues delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho in his late 80s and 90th year*

---

## 1. Archival Series Overview

The **Recent Dhamma Talks (2020–2024)** collection captures the radical simplicity, profound depth, and luminous clarity of Venerable Ajahn Sumedho's culminating teachings delivered between the ages of 86 and 90.

Delivered during worldwide pandemic disruptions and into his 90th year (May 2024), these 21 discourses strip away all spiritual pretense, complex doctrinal scholasticism, and ascetic strain. They emphasize:
1. **The Knowing Beyond Thought (*Buddho*)**: Awakening from the cognitive loop of "thinking about thinking" into immediate presence.
2. **Transcending Cartesian Rationalism**: Realizing that "I think, therefore I doubt" is the nature of the conditioned intellect, while awareness is inherently unshakeable.
3. **Ideals Don't Feel**: Embracing somatic vulnerability (*vedanā*) with compassionate allowance rather than using rigid spiritual ideals as weapons of self-condemnation.
4. **Effortless Right Effort**: Recognizing that the cessation of craving (*nirodha*) requires no aggressive ego-willpower, only the relaxed release of grasping.
5. **Liberation In This Very Life (*Diṭṭhe Va Dhamme*)**: Declaring with total conviction that awakening is available in this living moment.

Every talk has been ingested from master audio and transcribed through the authentic **Faster-Whisper (base.en, int8)** pipeline into full verbatim prose formatted into natural paragraphs (~60–120 words) with responsive Markdown tables and dynamic Pāli doctrinal tagging.
