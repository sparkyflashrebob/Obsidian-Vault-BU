### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Monastic caregiving, nursing sick teachers, communal service, and dissolving self-centeredness through loving-kindness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Reflecting on his decades of monastic life and his experience caring for the ailing Ajahn Chah for a decade, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the spiritual gold hidden in caregiving. Caring for elderly parents, sick community members, or challenging people is hard on the ego. Yet it is the ultimate training ground for purifying selfishness and cultivating boundless mettā and karuṇā.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **'Whoever Would Tend to Me, Let Him Tend to the Sick'**: Living the Buddha's direct commandment.
- **Egoic Resentment in Service**: Observing the natural friction between personal convenience and selfless duty.
- **The Joy of Selfless Service**: How caring for another dissolves loneliness and personal self-pity.
