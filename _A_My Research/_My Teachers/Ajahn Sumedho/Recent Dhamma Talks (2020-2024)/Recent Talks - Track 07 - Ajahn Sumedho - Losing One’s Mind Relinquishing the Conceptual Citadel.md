### Circumstances
Late-stage contemplative reflections and public discourses delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho during his late 80s and 90th year, offering radical, direct pointers to awareness, the cessation of grasping, and living from the Deathless.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic disciples, resident Sangha, visiting monastics, and lay community.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The deliberate, wise surrender of the conceptual intellectual apparatus to discover transcendent wakefulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho playfully uses the phrase 'losing one's mind' to describe the transition from intellectual control to unconditioned wisdom. Conventional culture is terrified of losing its sanity, clutching tightly to memories, opinions, and concepts. But spiritual awakening requires 'losing' the small, neurotic conceptual mind so that the unconditioned, luminous knowing can shine forth.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Fragile Mental Fortress**: How we construct conceptual fortresses to shield against reality.
- **Sanity vs. Awakening**: Recognizing that conventional sanity often consists in well-adjusted collective delusion.
- **Trusting the Unformed**: Resting in the boundless space of knowing that remains when ideas are dropped.
