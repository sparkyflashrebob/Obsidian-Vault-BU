### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplation of bereavement, loss, and the inevitable parting from everything we cherish in life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this tender and sobering 54-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on one of the Five Daily Recollections: 'All that is dear and beloved to me is subject to change and separation.' We spend our lives gathering beloved possessions, friends, status, and health, yet conditionality guarantees their departure. Luang Por shows how deep contemplation of loss frees the heart from clingy attachment and unlocks unconditional gratitude.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Five Daily Recollections (Upajjhaṭṭhana) as essential daily medicine.
- Working through grief, sorrow, and lamentation without denial or despair.
- How holding conditions lightly actually increases our love and appreciation for them.
- Nibbāna as the unconditioned realm that can never be lost, separated, or broken.
