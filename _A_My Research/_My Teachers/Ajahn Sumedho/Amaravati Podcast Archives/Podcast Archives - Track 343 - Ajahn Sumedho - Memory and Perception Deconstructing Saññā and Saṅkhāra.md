### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Technical psychological exploration of how perception (saññā) and mental proliferation (papañca) construct illusory realities.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this brilliant 57-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the precise psychological mechanics of the Buddhist cognitive model found in the Honeyball Sutta (MN 18). Contact gives rise to feeling; what one feels, one perceives (*saññā*); what one perceives, one thinks about; what one thinks about, one mentally proliferates (*papañca*). Luang Por demonstrates how waking up at the level of perception halts the entire runaway freight train of psychological suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cognitive chain of Dependent Origination in sense perception.
- The Honeyball Sutta (MN 18): from bare sensory contact to obsessive papañca.
- How memory tags every incoming sight and sound with conditioned prejudices.
- Catching the mind between perception and proliferation: resting in the unelaborated.

| Stage of Perception | Cognitive Mechanism | Psychological Manifestation | Awakened Intervention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sense Contact (Phassa) | Eye meets form; ear meets sound | Raw acoustic or visual vibration | Bare awareness registers contact |
| Feeling (Vedanā) | Pleasant, painful, or neutral valence | Instant visceral reaction in the nervous system | Accepting feeling without craving or aversion |
| Perception (Saññā) | Memory labels: 'friend', 'enemy', 'good', 'bad' | Applying past conditioning to present reality | Recognizing labels as conditioned constructs |
| Proliferation (Papañca) | Runaway storytelling, paranoia, and obsessing | Creation of elaborate fantasies and identity battles | Cessation: resting as the knowing space |
