### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Three Universal Characteristics (tilakkhaṇa) as the supreme practical tools for experiential liberation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a profound 64-minute masterclass on the three marks of existence: impermanence (anicca), unsatisfactoriness (dukkha), and not-self (anattā). Rather than treating them as dry philosophical propositions, he demonstrates how applying them to real-time physical discomfort, emotional moods, and wandering thoughts instantly dissolves grasping and reveals unconditioned peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating Anicca not as pessimistic tragedy, but as the liberating fluidity that allows suffering to cease.
- Understanding Dukkha as the inherent limitation of all conditioned phenomena to deliver permanent satisfaction.
- Realizing Anattā as the direct perception that nothing in the five aggregates belongs to an ego-entity.
- Using the three characteristics as reflective mirrors to disarm emotional clinging.

| Universal Mark | Deluded Ego Reaction | Contemplative Insight | Liberating Fruit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Anicca (Impermanence) | Struggling to freeze pleasant states | All conditions naturally arise and pass away | Effortless letting go (Vāyāma) |
| Dukkha (Unsatisfactoriness) | Blaming external conditions for unhappiness | Conditions cannot provide absolute fulfillment | Freedom from unrealistic expectations |
| Anattā (Not-Self) | Taking thoughts and feelings as 'me' or 'mine' | Observing sensations as impersonal nature | Transcendence of identity view (Sakkāya-diṭṭhi) |
