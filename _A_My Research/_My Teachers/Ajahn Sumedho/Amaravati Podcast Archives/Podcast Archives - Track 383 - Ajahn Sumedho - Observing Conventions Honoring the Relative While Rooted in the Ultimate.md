### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Harmonizing conventional truth (sammuti-sacca)—monastic etiquette, language, social norms—with ultimate reality (paramattha-sacca).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 50-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a crucial philosophical balance: conventional truth (*sammuti-sacca*) versus ultimate truth (*paramattha-sacca*). Immature practitioners often discard manners, etiquette, and monastic rank under the guise that 'it's all an illusion; there is no self.' Luang Por explains that an enlightened being respects conventions scrupulously while never being fooled by them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The two truths in Buddhist epistemology: conventional reality and ultimate reality.
- The error of pseudo-enlightened nihilism: treating conventions with contempt.
- How monastic protocol (Vinaya, bowing, vassa seniority) serves as a vehicle of humility.
- Using conventions as skillful means (upāya) to live in harmony with the world.
