### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Clear seeing discrimination between fabricated impermanent phenomena (saṅkhata) and unoriginated deathless awareness (asaṅkhata).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a profound doctrinal map of Buddhist realization. Meditators often exhaust themselves trying to make the conditioned world perfect—calming every thought, perfecting every posture, eradicating every flaw. Luang Por reveals that true freedom comes not from refining conditions, but from discerning the Unconditioned that is already present. The born, created, and formed arises within that which is unborn and uncreated.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The classical distinction between Saṅkhata (the conditioned) and Asaṅkhata (the unconditioned).
- The futility of searching for lasting security within impermanent mental and physical aggregates.
- How pure awareness (Buddho) effortlessly accommodates all sensory turbulence without being disturbed.
- The realization of Nibbāna as the immediate, unfabricated peace prior to thought.
