### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute public question and answer session, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the challenges of lay life in the modern world. Participants ask about surviving toxic corporate workplaces, ethical dilemmas in finance and technology, how to maintain loving-kindness when working with cutthroat colleagues, and how to raise children mindfully. Luang Por provides practical, grounded, non-dogmatic guidance.

- Sammā-ājīva (Right Livelihood) in a complex globalized economy.
- How to work with integrity without becoming self-righteous or judgmental.
- Setting energetic and emotional boundaries in high-stress jobs.
- Parenting from presence: giving children mindful attention rather than material gadgets.
