### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The contemplative art of investigating the Unconditioned (Asaṅkhata): shifting attention from transient mental forms to the deathless background.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides meditators to the supreme destination of Buddhist inquiry: the Unconditioned (*Asaṅkhata*). Most meditators spend decades investigating conditioned things—breath sensations, emotions, thoughts, neuroses. Luang Por points out that conditions will never be satisfactory. True awakening requires turning attention 180 degrees to investigate that which is *not* conditioned: the silent space of awareness in which conditions appear.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Asaṅkhata Saṁyutta: the 33 synonyms of the Unconditioned in the Pāli Canon.
- The Udāna declaration: 'There is, monks, an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unfabricated...'
- Why refining conditioned states will never yield permanent liberation.
- The shift in identity: from the mortal form to the deathless Knower.
