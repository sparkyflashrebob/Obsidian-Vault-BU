### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning reflection on bare awareness: resting in the unelaborated space where in the seen there is only the seen.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 33-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho invokes the core teaching given to Bāhiya Dārucīriya: 'In the seen, let there be only the seen; in the heard, only the heard.' Luang Por guides monastics into direct perception. When sensory contact occurs, suffering only begins when the mind adds 'me' and 'mine'. Abiding as the unshaken witness reveals the intrinsic purity of consciousness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Bāhiya Sutta: the Buddha's most concise and radical instruction on liberation.
- How ego adds layers of interpretation, preference, and identity to bare sensory data.
- Morning silence in the monastery as the perfect canvas for observing bare perception.
- The effortless peace of living without constructing a self.
