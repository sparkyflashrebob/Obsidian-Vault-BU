### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Distinguishing between conceptual mental gymnastics and pure intuitive awareness in spiritual practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute Q&A, practitioners question Luang Por about decision-making, ethical dilemmas, and trusting intuition. Luang Por explains that intuitive intelligence (ñāṇa / intuitive knowing) operates prior to thinking. Conceptual thought divides reality into binary conflicts, whereas intuitive awareness sees holistically and responds naturally from wisdom and compassion without the interference of a self-serving ego.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The trap of endless intellectual over-analysis and spiritual doubt.
- Intuition as the direct, quiet registering of conditionality without ego distortion.
- How to distinguish healthy intuition from emotional impulses and biases.
- Trusting the silence: allowing skillful action to arise from a quiet mind.
