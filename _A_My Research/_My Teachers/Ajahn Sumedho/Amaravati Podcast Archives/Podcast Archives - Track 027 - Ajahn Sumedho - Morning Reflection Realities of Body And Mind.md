### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Somatic investigation of the physical form and mental moods through bare attention and non-resistance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A morning reflection focusing on the physiological realities of aging, sensation, and emotional moods. Luang Por emphasizes meeting bodily stiffness and fatigue not with aversion or self-pity, but as natural conditionality (idappaccayatā) that serves as the perfect contemplation for awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the body as a collection of physical elements (dhātu) subject to wear, tear, and impermanence.
- Distinguishing between physical discomfort (First Arrow) and mental resistance or complaint (Second Arrow).
- Observing mental moods as atmospheric weather that sweeps through consciousness without defining who we are.
- Establishing the 'one who knows' (Buddho) as the unshakeable refuge amidst somatic fluctuations.
