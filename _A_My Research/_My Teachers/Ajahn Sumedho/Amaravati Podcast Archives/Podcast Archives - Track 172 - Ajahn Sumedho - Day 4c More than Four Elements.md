### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Evening retreat exposition: moving beyond physical materialism by exploring space (ākāsa) and consciousness (viññāṇa).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of Day 4 of a 2017 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho explores why materialism fails to explain human reality. Science and materialism focus exclusively on the four physical elements; the Buddha recognized that space and consciousness are equally fundamental. When we include space and consciousness in our contemplation, the claustrophobic trap of physical mortality dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The limitations of scientific materialism in explaining conscious subjective experience.
- Contemplating space (ākāsa-dhātu): noticing the space in the hall, between trees, and between thoughts.
- Contemplating consciousness (viññāṇa-dhātu): noticing that which knows the space.
- Resting in the unconditioned realm that transcends all elemental composites.
