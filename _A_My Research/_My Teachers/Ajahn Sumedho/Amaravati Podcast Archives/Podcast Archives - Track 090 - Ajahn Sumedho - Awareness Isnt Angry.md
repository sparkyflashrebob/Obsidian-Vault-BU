### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Luang Por Sumedho's signature teaching: 'That which is aware of anger is not angry.'

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic 65-minute masterpiece, Luang Por Sumedho reveals his famous liberating insight: awareness is never angry, impatient, depressed, or sick. When anger surges through the nervous system, if we identify with it ('I am furious'), we create suffering; but if we notice that awareness itself is cool, spacious, and unperturbed, anger dissolves harmlessly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between emotional energy (saṅkhāra) and the unconditioned awareness that knows it.
- The physical sensation of anger: adrenaline surge, tightness in throat and solar plexus.
- How the witnessing position (Buddho) provides an immediate sanctuary during intense emotional provocations.
- Responding to insults and injustice with calm, resolute moral clarity rather than blind rage.
