### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Grounding Dependent Origination in daily contemplative practice: tracking how sensory contact sparks craving and how mindfulness breaks the chain.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho rescues Paṭiccasamuppāda from dry scholastic abstraction. Rather than analyzing twelve cosmological links spanning three lifetimes, Luang Por demonstrates how Dependent Origination operates right now across the six sense bases. When pleasant or painful feeling arises, we have a split-second opportunity to apply mindfulness. If we register feeling with bare awareness, craving cannot ignite, and the entire cycle of suffering terminates.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- De-mystifying Paṭiccasamuppāda: seeing the links in immediate moment-to-moment experience.
- The critical junction between Feeling (Vedanā) and Craving (Taṇhā).
- How winter retreat silence slows down the nervous system so mental links become visible.
- Dependent Cessation (Nirodha): tasting the peace of non-arising.
