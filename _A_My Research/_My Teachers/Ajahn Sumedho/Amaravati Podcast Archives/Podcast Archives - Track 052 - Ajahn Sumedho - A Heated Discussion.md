### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transforming verbal arguments, ideological heat, and defensive pride through patient endurance (khanti) and equanimity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por examines why human beings become so emotionally agitated during intellectual and philosophical arguments. He shows that debates rarely revolve around factual truth; they are defensive territorial battles of the ego protecting its cherished opinions (diṭṭhi). By noticing the somatic contraction and physical heat of anger, we can step out of the fray into unshakeable silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Investigating the physical sensations of heat, clenched jaw, and chest tightness during disagreements.
- Recognizing attachment to views and opinions (diṭṭhi-upādāna) as the root engine of interpersonal strife.
- Cultivating patient endurance (khanti) and the willingness to let others have the last word.
- Abiding as the peaceful awareness that understands both sides without identifying with either.
