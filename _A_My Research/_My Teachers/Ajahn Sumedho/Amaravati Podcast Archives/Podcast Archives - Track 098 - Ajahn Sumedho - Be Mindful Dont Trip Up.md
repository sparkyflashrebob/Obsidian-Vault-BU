### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Everyday physical and psychological mindfulness: avoiding the pitfalls of heedlessness (pamāda).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A delightfully practical and humorous 65-minute discourse on mindfulness of daily movement. Luang Por reminds the assembly that spiritual practice is not an ethereal flight into the clouds; it begins with watching where we put our feet, closing doors gently, and handling crockery with care. When physical heedfulness is cultivated, the mind naturally stops tripping over emotional obstacles.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The profound link between physical clumsiness and mental distraction.
- Practicing moving slowly, opening and closing doors silently, and placing items down with care.
- How physical care reflects gratitude and respect for the monastery and the donors.
- Transferring physical mindfulness into awareness of conversational traps and emotional reactivity.
