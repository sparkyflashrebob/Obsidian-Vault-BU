### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deep doctrinal analysis of the Ten Fetters (Saṁyojana) and how unexamined ego-assumptions enslave human consciousness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a masterclass on the Ten Fetters (Saṁyojana) that bind beings to the wheel of saṁsāra. Most people believe their oppressors are bad bosses, governments, or economic systems. Luang Por demonstrates that the ultimate oppressor is our own unexamined sense of self. From coarse identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) down to subtle conceit (asmimāna) and ignorance (avijjā), the fetters crumble when exposed to luminous awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Ten Fetters: five lower fetters (orambhāgiya) and five higher fetters (uddhambhāgiya).
- How identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) functions as the master architect of psychological misery.
- The subtle higher fetters: craving for form/formless existence, conceit, restlessness, ignorance.
- The method of liberation: seeing the fetters as impermanent mental fabrications.
