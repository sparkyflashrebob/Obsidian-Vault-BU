### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the Divine Messengers (Devadūta)—old age, sickness, and death—as urgent spiritual warnings to awaken.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given during morning rice gruel reflection, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the classical teaching of the Devadūtas: the heavenly messengers of aging, illness, death, and the monastic seeker. Worldly culture views aging and disease as medical failures to be concealed. In Dhamma, they are sacred messengers awakening us from the intoxicating stupor of youth, health, and life. By witnessing them as the Buddha, fear transforms into liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Three Divine Messengers in the Suttas: the frail elder, the diseased invalid, the decaying corpse.
- The Three Intoxications: intoxication with youth (yobbana-mada), health (ārogya-mada), and life (jīvita-mada).
- Witnessing the messengers through the eyes of the Buddha rather than a terrified ego.
- Gruel time contemplation: taking nourishment with reverence, humility, and gratitude.
