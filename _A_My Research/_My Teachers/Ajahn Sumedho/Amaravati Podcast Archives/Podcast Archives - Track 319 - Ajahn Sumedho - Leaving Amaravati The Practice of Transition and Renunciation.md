### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A historic, moving discourse reflecting on non-attachment to institutions, the impermanence of leadership, and relinquishing ownership.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deeply touching 35-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on transitions, departures, and non-clinging to monastery leadership. Having spent over two decades establishing Chithurst, Amaravati, and branch monasteries across the Western world, Luang Por reminds the community that even beloved spiritual monasteries are impermanent conventions. Monastics must never become institutional landlords. True monastic freedom is having empty hands and a heart ready to walk away at any moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The danger of institutional attachment: turning a monastery into personal territory.
- The monastic principle of renunciation applied to senior leadership and abbotship.
- Passing responsibility to the next generation of monastics with total trust and blessing.
- The universal truth of parting: all meetings inevitably end in separation.
