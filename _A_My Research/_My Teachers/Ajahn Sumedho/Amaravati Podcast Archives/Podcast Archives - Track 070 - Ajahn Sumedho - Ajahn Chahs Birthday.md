### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Celebrating the life, teachings, and global legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah on the anniversary of his birth.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute celebratory discourse delivered on June 17, Luang Por Sumedho leads the Sangha and lay assembly in commemorating the birth of their root teacher, Venerable Ajahn Chah. He shares vivid, humorous, and deeply moving stories of training in the remote Thai wilderness, distilling Ajahn Chah's master teachings on impermanence, endurance, and selfless love.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Recounting the austere beginnings of Wat Pah Nanachat (the International Forest Monastery) in 1975.
- Ajahn Chah's brilliance in using everyday farm analogies, buffaloes, and broken tea cups to teach profound Dhamma.
- The virtue of patient endurance (ot-thon) as the bedrock of monastic longevity.
- Expressing true gratitude to the teacher by practicing the Dhamma in accordance with the Dhamma (dhammānudhamma-paṭipatti).
