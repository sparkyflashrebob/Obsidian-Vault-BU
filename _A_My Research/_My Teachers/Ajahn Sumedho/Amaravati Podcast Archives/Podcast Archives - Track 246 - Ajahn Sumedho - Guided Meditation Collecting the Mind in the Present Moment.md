### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this experiential 45-minute guided meditation, Luang Por Sumedho gently walks the practitioner into the meditative sanctuary. He begins with physical posture—settling into the sitting base, relaxing the shoulders, unclamping the jaw, and allowing the belly to soften. He then guides attention to the natural rhythm of the in-and-out breath and the inner sound of silence, demonstrating how to rest as the effortless witness of whatever arises.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Setting up the physical base: balanced spine, softened pelvis, grounded sit-bones.
- Releasing chronic tension held in the face, eyes, neck, and chest.
- Establishing the breath anchor: letting the breath breathe itself without force.
- Timed pauses and spacious observational cues for sustained tranquility.
