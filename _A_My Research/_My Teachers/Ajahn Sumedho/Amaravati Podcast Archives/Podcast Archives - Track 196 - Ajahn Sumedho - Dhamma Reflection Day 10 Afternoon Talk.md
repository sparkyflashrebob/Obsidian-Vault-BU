### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Closing retreat reflection: carrying the sanctuary of stillness into the noisy marketplace of ordinary life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the final afternoon of an intensive 10-day retreat, Luang Por Sumedho prepares retreatants for re-entry into daily life. Practitioners often fear losing their peace when leaving the quiet cocoon of the monastery. Luang Por reminds them that stillness is not confined to meditation halls; it is the natural space of awareness that holds traffic, meetings, family friction, and daily tasks. If we remain as the knower, we never leave retreat.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The anxiety of retreat re-entry: anticipating the shock of email, phones, and crowds.
- How to hold sensory overwhelm in spacious, unentangled awareness.
- The Sound of Silence as a portable, instantaneous anchor in noisy environments.
- Treating everyday domestic responsibilities as continuous meditation practice.
