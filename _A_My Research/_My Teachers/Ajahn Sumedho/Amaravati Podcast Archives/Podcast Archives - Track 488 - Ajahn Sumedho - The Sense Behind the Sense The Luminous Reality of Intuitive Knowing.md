### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Discovering the subtle intuitive faculty: the knowing awareness (Buddho) that operates behind and through the six sensory organs.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho investigates 'the sense behind the senses'. Our five physical organs register sight, sound, smell, taste, and touch, while the intellect registers thought. But what is the knowing presence that registers all six? Luang Por reveals that consciousness itself is the primal sense—the uncreated, luminous knowing of Buddho. By tuning into the sense behind the senses, we step out of sensory slavery into sovereign freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The six sense doors (Salāyatana) and their corresponding consciousnesses.
- The uncreated Knower (Buddho) operating behind sensory cognition.
- Listening to the Sound of Silence as tuning into the matrix of awareness.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that is never stained by sensory contact.
