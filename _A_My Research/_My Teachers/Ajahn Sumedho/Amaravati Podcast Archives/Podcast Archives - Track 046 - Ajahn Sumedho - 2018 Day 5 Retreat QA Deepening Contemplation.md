### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating mid-retreat subtle hindrances, emotional releases, and energetic shifts.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
As the retreat deepens into its fifth day, retreatants ask about deep emotional grief surfacing from the past, subtle attachments to calm, energetic vibrations in the head, and maintaining mindfulness when returning to the world.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Holding old childhood trauma and grief in the vast, non-judgmental space of awareness.
- Understanding energetic sensations (pīti, vibrations, light) as natural side effects of concentration that must not be clung to.
- Avoiding spiritual materialism: desiring to sustain retreat tranquility indefinitely in daily household life.
- Practicing wise discernment regarding speech, work, and ethical livelihood.
