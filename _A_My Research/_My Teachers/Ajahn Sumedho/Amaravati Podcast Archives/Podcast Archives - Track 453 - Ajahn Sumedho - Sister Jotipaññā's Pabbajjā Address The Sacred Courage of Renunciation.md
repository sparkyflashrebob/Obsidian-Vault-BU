### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this inspiring 61-minute ordination address, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the going forth (*pabbajjā*) of Sister Jotipaññā into the Siladhārā monastic community. Shaving the head and taking the brown robes and ten precepts represents radical spiritual bravery. Luang Por invokes the ancient Therīgāthā (Verses of the Elder Nuns), celebrating how the monastic container provides women with an inviolable spiritual sanctuary to realize full awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The establishment and significance of the Siladhārā community at Chithurst and Amaravati.
- The Therīgāthā: the ancient lineage of enlightened Buddhist women.
- Shaving the head and wearing the robes as cutting through gender conditioning and social vanity.
- The ten precepts and monastic protocol as tools for psychological sovereignty and peace.
