### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct empirical examination of real-time suffering (dukkha) versus theoretical Buddhist study.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho asks the fundamental diagnostic question: 'Are you suffering today?' He cuts through abstract Buddhist scholarship to point directly at our daily experience of friction, annoyance, impatience, and anxiety. Dukkha is not a cosmic abstraction; it is the real-time tension we create when we resist the way life is.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why the First Noble Truth must be recognized and understood, rather than merely debated.
- Investigating the Two Arrows: the raw biological sensation (First Arrow) versus the mental grievance (Second Arrow).
- Observing how daily domestic inconveniences (rain, chores, transport delays) become vehicles for suffering.
- The profound relief of ceasing to create suffering right now.
