### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Stepping out of the compulsive cognitive cacophony of discursive thought into the silence of bare knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the silent winter retreat of January 2021, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the chronic suffering of compulsive thinking. Modern humans suffer not from a lack of thoughts, but from an addiction to thinking. Thoughts are like wandering stray dogs barking in the night. By tuning to the inner sound of silence, we discover the vast peace that lies beyond cognitive proliferation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the process of papañca: how a simple sensory perception explodes into a catastrophic mental saga.
- The physical exhaustion of being dragged along by obsessive thoughts and worry.
- Using the inner sound of silence (nāda) as a direct auditory bridge out of the thinking realm.
- Observing thoughts not as enemies to be killed, but as ephemeral sound ripples in empty space.
