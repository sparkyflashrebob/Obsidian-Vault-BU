### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in relaxed ease, somatic peace, and mental contentment in the living present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 53-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho offers a soothing antidote to modern chronic stress and urgency. We are conditioned to treat the present moment as an obstacle to be rushed through on our way to a future goal. Luang Por reminds us that liberation is not waiting in the future; it is the natural ease of a relaxed body and unhurried mind right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the teaching of the Bhaddekaratta Sutta on unshakeable present-moment abiding.
- Releasing the chronic physical habit of leaning forward, rushing, and bracing for impact.
- The somatic markers of ease: slow rhythmic breathing, soft belly, relaxed gaze.
- Discovering that when urgency stops, natural wisdom and responsiveness flourish.
