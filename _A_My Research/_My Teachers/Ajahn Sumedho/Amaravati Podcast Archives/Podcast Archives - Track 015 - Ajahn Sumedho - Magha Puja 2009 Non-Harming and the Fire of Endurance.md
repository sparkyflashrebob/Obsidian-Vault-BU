### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deepening the Ovāda-Pātimokkha: exploring non-harming (ahiṁsā) and patient endurance as the supreme spiritual power.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking on Māgha Pūjā in 2009, Luang Por Sumedho delves into the radical principle of non-harming (*ahiṁsā*). In a world that glorifies aggressive retribution, legal warfare, and revenge, the Buddha taught that true strength lies in refusing to strike back. When we practice patient endurance (*khanti*), we absorb the fires of worldly ill-will without passing them on, acting as universal emotional transformers of peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Ahiṁsā as Radical Power**: Why harmlessness is the highest form of spiritual bravery.
- **The Transformer Metaphor**: How the awakened heart absorbs negative energy and outputs metta.
- **Nibbāna as Supreme Freedom**: Realizing *Nibbānaṁ paramaṁ vadanti buddhā*.
