### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The core practice of the Thai Forest Tradition: resting in the unentangled position of 'The One Who Knows' (Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho presents the quintessential instruction of his root teacher Ajahn Chah: 'Poo Roo'—the one who knows (Buddho). Rather than getting lost in the content of experience, we step back into the knowing of experience. The knower is not an entity with an address or a personality; it is the natural capacity of consciousness to be aware.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Defining 'The One Who Knows' as a practical pointing instruction rather than an eternal soul.
- How the knower remains cool and untouched regardless of whether pleasant or unpleasant objects arise.
- Using the mantra 'Buddho' with the in-breath and out-breath to anchor in the knower.
- The realization that when the knower is recognized, fear and loneliness evaporate.
