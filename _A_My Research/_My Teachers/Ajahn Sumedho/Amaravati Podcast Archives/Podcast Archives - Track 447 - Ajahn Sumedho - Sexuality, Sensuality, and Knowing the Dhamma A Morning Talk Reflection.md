### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mature morning reflection on sexual desire and sensory craving: moving beyond puritanical repression and hedonistic indulgence into mindful knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this frank and compassionate 54-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho addresses sexuality and sensuality. In religious circles, sex is often shrouded in taboo, guilt, and puritanical shame. In modern culture, it is commercialized and treated as the supreme meaning of life. Luang Por articulates the Middle Way: sexual desire is a natural, powerful biological wave of kāma-taṇhā. When we observe it with bare awareness rather than acting it out or repressing it, sexual energy is transformed into spiritual vitality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Buddhist Middle Way regarding sexuality: neither hedonism nor ascetic shame.
- The Magandiya Sutta: the simile of the leper cauterizing wounds with burning coals.
- Celibacy (brahmacariya) in monastic life: voluntary conservation of vital spiritual energy.
- Integrating sexuality ethically in lay life through fidelity, mutual respect, and Mettā.
