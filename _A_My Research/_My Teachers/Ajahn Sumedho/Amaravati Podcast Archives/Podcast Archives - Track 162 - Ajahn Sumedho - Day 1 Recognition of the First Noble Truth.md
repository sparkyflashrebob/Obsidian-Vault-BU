### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Opening retreat discourse: directly recognizing the First Noble Truth of Dukkha in living experience.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this opening retreat discourse from May 2017, Luang Por Sumedho introduces the foundational starting point of all Buddhist practice: recognizing Dukkha. Dukkha is not a cosmic tragedy or personal curse; it is the natural friction of conditioned life. When we acknowledge suffering with openhearted honesty rather than dodging it, we take the essential first step toward total liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why the First Noble Truth is a profound relief: acknowledging that life is inherently imperfect.
- Differentiating between physical discomfort and the psychological aversion that creates Dukkha.
- Setting up the silent retreat container as an intentional laboratory to observe the mind.
- Establishing the refuge of the 'one who knows' (Buddho) right from Day 1.
