### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how the insecure ego projects threat and manufactures 'enemies' out of ordinary people, institutions, and sensations.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the deep winter retreat at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho shines a spotlight on the mind's chronic habit of manufacturing enemies. Whether it is an annoying monk clearing his throat across the hall, a political party, a spouse, or a bodily pain, the ego instinctively divides reality into 'me' versus 'the enemy'. Luang Por shows how otherness is an internal mental projection. When we meet threat with dispassionate awareness and Mettā, the enemy dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Kalahavivāda Sutta: the roots of conflict, quarrels, and disputes.
- How internal somatic tension projects threat onto harmless external targets.
- Deconstructing the archetype of the 'enemy' into impermanent causes and conditions.
- Resting in the unconditioned heart that has no enemies in the entire universe.
