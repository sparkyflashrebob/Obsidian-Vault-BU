### Theme of the Dharma Talk
True peace is not a fragile meditative trance or peaceful external environment, but the complete cessation of craving in the heart.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this sublime 69-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the ultimate goal of Buddhist practice: Peace (*Santi*). People often confuse peace with quietness—wanting a quiet monastery, peaceful music, or calm feelings. But as soon as a dog barks or an argument begins, that fragile peace collapses. Luang Por reveals true peace: it is not the absence of noise or conflict, but the absence of grasping. Real peace can hold the storm.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Fragile conditional peace (samatha) versus unshakeable transcendent peace (Nibbāna).
- Why seeking peace through controlling external conditions always fails.
- Peace as the nature of the unconditioned knowing (Buddho).
- Abiding in peace amidst sensory noise, sickness, and worldly chaos.
