### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A momentous 53-minute historical discourse delivered at the official consecration and opening of the newly constructed Dhamma Hall at Cittaviveka. Luang Por Sumedho reflects on twenty-five years of monastic dedication in West Sussex and dedicates the new building not as an architectural monument, but as a living temple of mindfulness, ethical conduct, and awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical significance of consecrating a permanent Buddhist temple in the English countryside.
- Explaining that the true temple is not brick, slate, and mortar, but the pure human heart abiding in Dhamma.
- Expressing heartfelt gratitude to the architects, craftsmen, donors, and resident monastics who labored for years.
- Invoking the blessings of the Triple Gem for all future generations who will seek peace within its walls.
