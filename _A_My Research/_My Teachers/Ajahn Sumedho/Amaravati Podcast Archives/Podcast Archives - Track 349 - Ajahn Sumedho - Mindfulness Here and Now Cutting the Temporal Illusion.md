### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct exploration of present-moment awareness: shattering the mental illusions of past and future to abide in the eternal Now.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho guides meditators directly into the present moment. The human mind is chronically addicted to temporal wandering—rehashing yesterday's interactions or anxiously rehearsing tomorrow's responsibilities. Luang Por unpacks the Bhaddekaratta Sutta, demonstrating that the only place where awakening can ever take place is here and now. The present is not a tiny slice of time between past and future; it is timeless reality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: 'The past is gone, the future yet unreached; whoever sees the Dhamma clearly in the present lives an auspicious day.'
- The anxiety inherent in psychological time and future projection.
- How sensory contact only ever occurs in the immediate present.
- Abiding as the timeless witness (Akāliko Dhamma) through all activities.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por describes sitting in silent retreat waiting for the clock to strike midnight, realizing that when midnight came it was just 'now', and that he had spent hours waiting for an imaginary future that never actually arrives.
