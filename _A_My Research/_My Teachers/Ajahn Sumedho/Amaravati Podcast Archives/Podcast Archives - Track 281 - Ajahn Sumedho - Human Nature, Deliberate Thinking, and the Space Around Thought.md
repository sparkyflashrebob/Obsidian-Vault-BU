### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Distinguishing between compulsive mental chatter and deliberate functional thought, and resting in the spacious silence around thinking.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a masterclass on the nature of the human thinking faculty. Meditators often make the mistake of trying to violently obliterate thinking, treating thoughts as sinful enemies. Luang Por clarifies that thinking is a natural human biological function. The problem is not thinking; it is compulsive proliferation (*papañca*) and identifying with thoughts. By noticing the vast silent space *around* thought, thinking subsides naturally.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between reactive neurotic rumination and deliberate, skillful thought.
- The Dvedhāvitakka Sutta: sorting thoughts into wholesome vs. unwholesome streams.
- Noticing the silent space before a thought arises and after it dissolves.
- Resting as the empty sky of consciousness rather than the weather of thinking.
