### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The expansive Thai Forest paradigm: understanding that interruptions, illnesses, and crises are not distractions from practice, but practice itself.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 67-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho presents the master perspective that liberates meditators from frustration: 'It's all part of practice.' When we believe meditation is only sitting quietly in a hall, then telephone calls, family duties, plumbing emergencies, and physical pain are experienced as hostile interruptions. Luang Por shows that there are no interruptions in Dhamma: whatever arises is the curriculum of awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dismantling the false barrier between 'pure meditation' and 'secular interference'.
- The Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta: mindfulness encompassing all bodily movements and mental states.
- How emotional flare-ups and logistical breakdowns serve as prime diagnostic mirrors.
- Resting in the unconditioned awareness that effortlessly accommodates all circumstances.
