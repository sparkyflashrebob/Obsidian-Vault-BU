### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating an unbroken stream of mindfulness (satatavihārī) across all four postures and daily transitions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
An expansive 80-minute masterclass on the power of continuous mindfulness. Luang Por Sumedho explains that the true power of meditation does not come from isolated bursts of heroic sitting, but from maintaining a connected, unbroken flow of gentle presence throughout the day: waking, stepping, washing, eating, speaking, and lying down. Like drops of water falling steadily into a bucket, continuous mindfulness overflows into spontaneous awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The classic Buddhist teaching on Satatavihārī: one who abides continuously mindful in all activities.
- Why fragmented, intermittent mindfulness produces slow progress and frequent emotional relapses.
- Using the transitions between postures as bridges of mindfulness rather than breaks in practice.
- Developing effortless momentum where mindfulness becomes the default setting of the mind.
