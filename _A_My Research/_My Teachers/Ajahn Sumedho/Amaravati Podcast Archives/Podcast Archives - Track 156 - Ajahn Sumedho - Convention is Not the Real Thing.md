### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Piercing the veil of societal conventions, religious rituals, and linguistic labels to encounter naked reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a sharp, clarifying 48-minute exposition on the difference between convention and reality. Words, currencies, passports, national flags, and religious ceremonies are conventional agreements. They are useful for organizing human society, but they are not the real thing. When we mistake the menu for the meal, we live in continuous spiritual starvation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The menu vs. the meal analogy: why reading Buddhist books is not tasting awakening.
- The chariot simile of Sister Vajirā: an assembly of parts conventionally called a 'chariot' or a 'person'.
- How language creates artificial boundaries where none exist in nature.
- Resting in the unnamable reality that is directly known by silent awareness.
