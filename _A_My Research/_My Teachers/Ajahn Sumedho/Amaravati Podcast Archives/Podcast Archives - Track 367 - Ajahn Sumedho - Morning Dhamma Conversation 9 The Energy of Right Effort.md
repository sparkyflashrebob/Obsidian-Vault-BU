### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat dialogue on balancing energy and relaxation: tuning the strings of Right Effort without excessive striving or laxity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 55-minute morning retreat dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sixth factor of the Eightfold Path: Right Effort (*Sammā-Vāyāma*). Western meditators constantly swing between two extremes: aggressive, hyper-disciplined striving that leads to exhaustion, and lazy, drowsy daydreaming. Luang Por invokes the Buddha's Simile of the Lute Strings: tuning practice neither too tight nor too loose produces the sublime music of liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The four aspects of Right Effort: preventing, abandoning, cultivating, and maintaining.
- The Simile of the Lute Strings (Soṇa Sutta): finding dynamic middle-way equilibrium.
- Why will-power and aggressive effort fail to produce lasting meditation peace.
- Right Effort as relaxed, vigilant perseverance rather than grim combat.
