### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical spiritual maturity: dismantling the psychological habit of blaming parents, partners, or society for internal Dukkha.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this uncompromising and empowering 60-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho confronts the blame reflex. Modern culture teaches us to be professional victims—blaming our childhood, economic circumstances, politicians, or rude companions for our misery. Quoting Dhammapada 165: 'By oneself is evil done, by oneself is one defiled; by oneself is evil left undone, by oneself is one purified.' Luang Por shows that others may cause physical pain, but only our own grasping creates suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The blame game: how victimhood keeps the ego in perpetual infancy.
- Dhammapada 165: the law of individual spiritual responsibility.
- Distinguishing between external provocation and internal reactivity.
- Taking total ownership of our kamma as the ultimate gateway to freedom.
