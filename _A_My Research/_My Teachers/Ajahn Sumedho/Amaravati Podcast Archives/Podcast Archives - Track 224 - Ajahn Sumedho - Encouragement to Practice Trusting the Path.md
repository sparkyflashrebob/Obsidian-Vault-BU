### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Warm, inspiring spiritual encouragement: sustaining daily practice through dry spells, doubt, and life transitions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a comforting and inspiring 59-minute Dhamma reflection. Every sincere meditator inevitably encounters spiritual plateaus—periods where sitting feels dry, inspiration wanes, and worldly distractions pull hard. Luang Por reminds the community that spiritual growth is like the growth of a great banyan tree: slow, invisible beneath the bark, yet rooted deep into the earth. Trust the process, maintain simple presence, and never give up.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Navigating the inevitable dry spells and spiritual deserts on the path.
- Why lack of blissful feelings does not mean practice has failed.
- The power of Appamāda (heedfulness) as the root of all wholesome states.
- The blessing of Kalyāṇa-mittatā (spiritual friendship) in sustaining lifelong practice.
