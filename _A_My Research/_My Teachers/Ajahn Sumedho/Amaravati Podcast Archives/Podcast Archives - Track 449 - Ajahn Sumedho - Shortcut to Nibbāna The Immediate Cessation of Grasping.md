### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The ultimate spiritual direct route: realizing Nibbāna immediately by dropping attachment to the six senses rather than climbing endless ladders.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this provocative and liberating 46-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reveals the 'shortcut' to Nibbāna. Scholastic Buddhism outlines complex maps of multi-lifetime progress, thirty-one planes of existence, and intricate jhāna levels. But the Buddha gave Bāhiya Dārucīriya the ultimate shortcut: in the seen only the seen, in the heard only the heard. Luang Por shows that the shortcut is simply letting go right now: when grasping ceases, Nibbāna is already here.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The long, scenic scholastic route versus the direct contemplative shortcut.
- The Bāhiya Sutta: the shortest discourse leading to immediate Arahantship.
- Why the ego fears the shortcut: it wants a dramatic hero's journey with badges.
- Tasting the unconditioned peace of cessation in this very moment.
