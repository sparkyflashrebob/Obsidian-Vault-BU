### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Culminating retreat instructions on integrating the Sound of Silence into worldly life, walking, working, and relationships.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the penultimate afternoon of an intensive retreat, Luang Por Sumedho guides retreatants on how to carry the Sound of Silence out of the quiet retreat hall into noisy daily life. Retreatants often fear losing their peace when they return to families and jobs. Luang Por explains that outer noise does not destroy the Sound of Silence—car horns, airport announcements, and human chatter all arise within the spacious silence without diminishing it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Carrying the Sound of Silence from cushion meditation into four daily postures.
- Why outer worldly noise cannot destroy inner contemplative silence.
- Using the nāda sound as an emergency mindfulness anchor during acute stress.
- Resting in the unconditioned background while actively functioning in the foreground.
