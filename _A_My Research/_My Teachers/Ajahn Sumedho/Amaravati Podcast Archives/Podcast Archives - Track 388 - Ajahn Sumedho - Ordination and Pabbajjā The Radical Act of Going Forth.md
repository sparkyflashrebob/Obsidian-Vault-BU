### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reflections on monastic ordination: the spiritual significance of pabbajja (going forth from home to homelessness) in the modern world.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the eve of monastic ordinations at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the radical ancient act of *pabbajjā*—going forth from the household life into homelessness. In a consumerist society that measures worth by earning power and family reproduction, putting on the ochre robe and shaving one's head is a revolutionary declaration of spiritual independence. Luang Por explores how ordination creates an inviolable sanctuary of renunciation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical meaning of Pabbajjā: following the footsteps of Prince Siddhattha.
- Shaving the head and taking the triple robe as shedding worldly identity.
- The four requisites (paccaya): robes, almsfood, lodging, and medicine.
- The lifelong commitment to wakefulness and communal service in the Sangha.
