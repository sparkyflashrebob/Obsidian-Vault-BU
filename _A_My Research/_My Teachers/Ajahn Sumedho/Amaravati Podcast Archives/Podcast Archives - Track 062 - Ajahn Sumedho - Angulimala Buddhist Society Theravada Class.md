### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme power of spiritual transformation, moral redemption, and the non-violent heart of the Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered to the Theravāda Class at the historic Buddhist Society in London, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the legendary story of Aṅgulimāla—the ruthless bandit who collected the fingers of his victims, encountered the Buddha, became an arahant, and embodied unconditional non-harming (ahiṁsā).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the Buddha's famous utterance to Aṅgulimāla: 'I have stopped, Aṅgulimāla; it is you who have not stopped.'
- Exploring the psychological meaning of 'stopping': halting the violent momentum of craving, aversion, and delusion.
- The Buddhist doctrine of kamma and redemption: no being is beyond the pale of spiritual awakening.
- The Aṅgulimāla Paritta as an enduring blessing for childbirth and healing throughout Southeast Asia.
