### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deep winter retreat inquiry into the root root-verb 'to be': deconstructing the psychological reflex of becoming (bhava).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during an intensive monastic winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho leads senior monastics into radical ontological inquiry. The mind is constantly fabricating 'becoming' (*bhava*)—becoming an arahant, becoming a better meditator, becoming worthy of respect. Luang Por challenges the assembly: 'What is this *I am*? Where does it reside?' By investigating the root assertion of selfhood, the entire cycle of Dependent Origination halts.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Mūlapariyāya Sutta: conceiving oneself in terms of earth, water, fire, air, and consciousness.
- The process of Bhava (becoming): how craving fuels the desire to be somebody.
- Winter retreat silence as the ideal laboratory for observing the arising of ego thoughts.
- Resting in the unoriginated knowing that neither becomes nor ceases.
