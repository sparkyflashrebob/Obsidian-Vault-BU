### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The foundational Thai monastic training admonition 'Rawang' (be careful / heedful / alert): safeguarding sense doors against unconscious slips.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Thai monastic command he heard thousands of times from Ajahn Chah: 'Rawang!' (ระวัง - 'Be careful! Watch out!'). In the West, 'be careful' sounds like anxious maternal overprotection. But in the Thai Forest Tradition, Rawang is the roaring battle-cry of mindfulness: pay attention to your feet, watch your robe, guard your eyes, take care of your speech. Rawang is vigilant, protective love in action.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Thai idiom 'Rawang' (ระวัง): heedfulness, alertness, and meticulous care.
- Safeguarding the six sense doors against the entry of defilements.
- Carefulness as an expression of reverence for life, the Sangha, and the requisites.
- How physical carefulness in monastery routines translates into internal mental stillness.
