### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic contemplation on the Three Refuges (Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha): moving from ritual recitation to internal sanctuary.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 47-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the profound act of taking refuge. People take refuge in money, romance, career status, or alcohol, yet all worldly refuges are impermanent and betray us in the end. Luang Por explores the Three Jewels—Buddha (the knowing awareness), Dhamma (the truth of things as they are), and Sangha (the community of virtue)—as the only secure, unshakeable sanctuary in existence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- False worldly refuges versus the Triple Gem (Tiratana).
- Buddho as the refuge of present awareness, not an ancient bronze statue.
- Dhamma as the refuge of conditionality and cessation.
- Sangha as the refuge of spiritual friendship and moral accountability.
