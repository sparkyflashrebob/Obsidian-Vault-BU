### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The limitless reach of mindfulness: how sati embraces the ugly, the beautiful, the noisy, and the silent without boundary.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho liberates mindfulness from artificial limitations. Practitioners often think mindfulness is only for quiet rooms, sitting cross-legged, or watching the breath. Luang Por proclaims: 'Sati has no limitations!' Sati can be aware of traffic jams, dental drills, panic attacks, depression, washing dishes, and aging. Mindfulness is not a delicate flower; it is boundless open space that can hold the entire universe.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dismantling the artificial boundaries placed on mindfulness.
- Sati in the four postures: sitting, standing, walking, lying down.
- How mindfulness embraces the unpleasant, painful, and terrifying without collapsing.
- The unconditioned nature of awareness: boundlessness as its inherent state.
