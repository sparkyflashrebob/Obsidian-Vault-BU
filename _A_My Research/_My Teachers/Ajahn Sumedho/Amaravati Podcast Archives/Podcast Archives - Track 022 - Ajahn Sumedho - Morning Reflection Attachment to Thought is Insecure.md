### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Why finding psychological security in thoughts, memories, and opinions is inherently unstable and anxiety-inducing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this early morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho reveals why human beings live in chronic anxiety: 'Attachment to thought is insecure.' Thoughts are by their nature impermanent, fluctuating, and conditional. When we build our identity, self-worth, and security on mental narratives, we are building a house on shifting sand. True security is found only in the silent space that holds thinking.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Fragility of Mental Constructs**: Seeing that opinions change with hormones and weather.
- **The Mirage of Self-Worth**: Why thinking cannot give you permanent psychological security.
- **Resting in the Silence**: Finding immovable sanctuary in unconditioned awareness.
