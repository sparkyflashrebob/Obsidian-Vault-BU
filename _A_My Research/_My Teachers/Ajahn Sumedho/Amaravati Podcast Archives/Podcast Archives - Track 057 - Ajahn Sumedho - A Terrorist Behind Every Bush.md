### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing cultural paranoia, mass media panic, and the primitive biological instinct of hypervigilance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this sharp and perceptive 63-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho analyzes the modern epidemic of anxiety and collective paranoia. Driven by sensationalist news cycles, society cultivates a state of perpetual terror—imagining threats behind every bush. Through the Dhamma, we dismantle this survival trance and reclaim our sovereign sanity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding how commercial media profits by weaponizing biological threat detection systems.
- The Buddhist teaching on overcoming fear and dread (Bhayabherava Sutta) through ethical purity and presence.
- Observing fear as a physical contraction in the gut rather than identifying with the catastrophic storyline.
- Cultivating trust in goodness and abiding as a calm, stabilizing influence in a panicked world.
