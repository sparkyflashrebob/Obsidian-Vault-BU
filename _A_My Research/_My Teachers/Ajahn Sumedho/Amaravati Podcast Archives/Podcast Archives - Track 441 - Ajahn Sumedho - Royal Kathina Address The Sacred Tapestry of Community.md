### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Royal Kathina festival address: reflecting on international Buddhist solidarity, the historic offering of the Royal Robe from Thailand, and communal harmony.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the auspicious Royal Kathina ceremony at Amaravati, where the Royal Thai Embassy presented the royal robe on behalf of His Majesty the King of Thailand, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the deeper meaning of Kathina. Beyond the pageantry and diplomatic celebration, Kathina represents the sacred interdependence of monastic and lay communities. Luang Por explores how the seamless cloth represents the unfrayed tapestry of the Dhamma preserved across 2,500 years.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical tradition of Royal Kathina (Kathina Luang) in Thai Theravāda.
- The reciprocal relationship: monastics offering spiritual guidance, lay community offering physical requisites.
- Dāna (generosity) as the foundation that dismantles selfishness and greed.
- The unity of diverse international cultures under the universal umbrella of Dhamma.
