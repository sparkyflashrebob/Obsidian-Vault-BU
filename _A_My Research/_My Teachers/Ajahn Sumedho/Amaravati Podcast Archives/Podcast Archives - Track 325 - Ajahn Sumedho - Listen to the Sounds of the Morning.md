### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning contemplation on receptive listening: attuning to dawn birdsong, distant traffic, and the inner Sound of Silence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered at daybreak in the Amaravati Temple, Luang Por Sumedho opens morning meditation with the practice of open auditory listening. Instead of treating sounds—chirping birds, wind in the trees, a distant airplane, someone coughing—as intrusive disturbances, Luang Por guides the assembly to open auditory awareness. Sounds come and go in the vastness of space. Behind all outer sounds is the ever-present Sound of Silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The contemplative practice of open listening: moving from selective grasping to receptive openness.
- Ending the war with auditory distraction: sounds are not bothering you; you are bothering the sounds!
- Tuning the inner ear to the subtle high-pitched vibration of the Sound of Silence (nāda).
- Resting as the vast acoustic space that accommodates all sounds without resistance.
