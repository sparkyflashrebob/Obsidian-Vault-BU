### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Overcoming the anxious, managing ego in meditation: addressing the neurotic questions of technique, duration, and progress.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the common anxiety of meditators: 'What do I do? And for how long do I have to do it?' This relentless calculating mind reflects our Western conditioning toward productivity, time management, and measurable goals. Luang Por explains that meditation is not a task to finish or an hourly job to complete. It is the radical cessation of doing—learning to simply be, awake and at peace, with whatever is occurring right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The corporate mindset applied to spiritual practice: goals, deadlines, and metrics.
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: the one fine night of dwelling peacefully in the present.
- The trap of technique hopping: endlessly switching methods in search of faster results.
- Cultivating timeless patience: letting meditation unfold according to nature, not ego schedules.
