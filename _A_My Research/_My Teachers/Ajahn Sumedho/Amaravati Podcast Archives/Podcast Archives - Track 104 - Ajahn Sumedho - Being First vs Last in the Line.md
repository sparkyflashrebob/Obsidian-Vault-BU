### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing hierarchy, social comparison, and the conceit of seniority through monastic etiquette.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this candid and humorous 52-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the monastic convention of seniority: lining up in order of rains (vassa) for almsround, meals, and ceremonies. He explores how the ego obsessively measures its position—whether being first at the head of the line or last at the tail. Both positions are traps if taken personally; true freedom is transcending comparison altogether.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding monastic seniority as an orderly convention rather than an endorsement of personal spiritual superiority.
- The three modes of conceit (māna): 'I am superior', 'I am inferior', and 'I am equal'.
- The hidden pride of false modesty: wanting to be the 'most humble' or last in line.
- Abiding in the selfless space where rank and status have zero meaning.
