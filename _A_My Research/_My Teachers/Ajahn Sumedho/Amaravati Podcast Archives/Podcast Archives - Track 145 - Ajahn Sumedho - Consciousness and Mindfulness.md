### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Harmonizing bare sensory consciousness (viññāṇa) with reflective, intentional mindfulness (sati).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this expansive 82-minute masterwork from February 2021, Luang Por Sumedho explores the precise psychological relationship between consciousness and mindfulness. Consciousness is the natural receptive mirror that registers sensory contact; mindfulness is the reflective wisdom that knows whether the mind is clinging, craving, or free. Together, they create an unshakeable foundation for awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between bare cognitive registration (viññāṇa) and moral/spiritual recollection (sati).
- Why having acute sensory consciousness without mindfulness leads to emotional reactivity.
- The reflective function of sati: stepping back to see the conditionality of mental states.
- Cultivating continuous, effortless awareness that requires no strained manipulation.
