### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Returning to the radical simplicity of awareness, cutting through academic over-complication and conceptual labyrinths.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 62-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on a passage from page 147 of his monastic writings, emphasizing the virtue of simplicity. The intellectual mind thrives on complication, generating intricate philosophical models, subtle stages of attainment, and endless debates. Yet the Buddha's core teaching is breathtakingly simple: suffering arises from clinging to conditions, and freedom is the cessation of clinging. By trusting simplicity, we step out of conceptual traps into genuine liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between complex scholarly Buddhist theory and living Dhamma.
- The spiritual arrogance of intellectualism versus humble present-moment awareness.
- How the five aggregates (khandhas) create false complexity when grasped.
- The sound of silence and the breath as effortless, simple gateways to peace.
