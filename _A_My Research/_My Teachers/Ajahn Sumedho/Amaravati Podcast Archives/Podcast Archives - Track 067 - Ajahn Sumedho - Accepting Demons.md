### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Spiritual shadow work: meeting inner demons of rage, lust, jealousy, and fear with fearless compassion.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this bold 56-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores how spiritual seekers try to repress their shadow side—their 'demons' of anger, sexual lust, jealousy, greed, and spite. Trying to kill or disown these demons only makes them stronger. Following the Buddha's example with Māra, we invite them to tea with wise, harmless hospitality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding Māra not as an external devil, but as the personification of unwholesome psychological impulses.
- The danger of spiritual bypass: pretending to be enlightened and serene while repressing dark human emotions.
- Applying the Buddha's greeting to the tempter: 'I see you, Māra; you are known.'
- Transforming the energy of defilements into fuel for wisdom and boundless compassion.
