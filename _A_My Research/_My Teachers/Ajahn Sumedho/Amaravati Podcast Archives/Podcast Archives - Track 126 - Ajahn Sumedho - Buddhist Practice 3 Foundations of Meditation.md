### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Establishing a stable daily practice: physical posture, shrine, breath anchor, and ethical alignment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides foundational, practical instructions on establishing a sustainable daily meditation practice at home. He covers setting up a quiet contemplative corner or shrine, establishing an upright yet relaxed physical posture, using the breath and the 'Buddho' mantra as gentle anchors, and maintaining moral precepts (sīla) to keep the mind free from agitation and remorse.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Setting up a dedicated sacred space at home as a physical reminder of spiritual aspiration.
- Finding an upright, balanced seated posture that avoids both rigid strain and sleepy slumping.
- Using the natural rhythm of the in-breath and out-breath without forcing or holding.
- How daily ethical conduct (sīla) protects the mind from remorse, creating natural tranquility.
