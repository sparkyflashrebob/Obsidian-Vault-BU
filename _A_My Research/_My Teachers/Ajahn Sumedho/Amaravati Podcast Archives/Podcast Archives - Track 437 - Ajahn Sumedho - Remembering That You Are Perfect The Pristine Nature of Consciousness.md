### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transcending the psychological wound of inadequacy: remembering that the unconditioned knowing is already whole, complete, and pristine.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this revolutionary 50-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho delivers a shocking statement to a room of earnest, self-critical meditators: 'Remember that you are perfect!' He clarifies that our conditioned personality, our physical bodies, and our emotional habits are obviously imperfect and flawed. But the awareness that knows these flaws—the uncreated space of Buddho—is completely whole, flawless, and pristine. Awakening is realizing our true identity as the mirror, not the mud.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cultural wound of 'original sin' and chronic human inadequacy.
- Distinguishing between the flawed personality (saṅkhāras) and pure awareness (Buddho).
- The Pabhassara Sutta: the mind is intrinsically radiant; defilements are merely visiting guests.
- Resting in the perfection of reality as it is (Tathatā).
