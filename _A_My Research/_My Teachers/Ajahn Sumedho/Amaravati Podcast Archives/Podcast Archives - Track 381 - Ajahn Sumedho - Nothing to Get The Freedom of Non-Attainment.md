### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Why the spiritual path is about subtracting delusions rather than acquiring attainments: the peace of having nothing to get.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 56-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho dismantles the consumerist spiritual mentality. In worldly life, success means getting things—getting degrees, getting money, getting status. Meditators unconsciously import this greed into Buddhism: wanting to get Stream-Entry, get samādhi, get enlightenment. Luang Por proclaims that the Dhamma is about getting rid of things, not getting anything. When there is nothing left to get, true freedom shines.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The consumerist approach to spirituality: collecting meditation attainments.
- The Heart Sūtra's declaration: 'With nothing to attain, the Bodhisattva relies on Prajñāpāramitā.'
- Subtracting defilements rather than adding spiritual badges.
- The profound contentment of the mind that no longer desires to get anything.
