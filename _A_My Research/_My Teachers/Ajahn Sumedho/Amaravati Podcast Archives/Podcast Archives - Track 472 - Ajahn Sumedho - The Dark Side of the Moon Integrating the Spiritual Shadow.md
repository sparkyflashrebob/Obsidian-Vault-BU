### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Facing the unacknowledged shadow self: bringing unconscious defilements, hidden malice, and dark impulses into the healing light of awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores 'the dark side of the moon'—the hidden shadow self. Religious people love presenting a luminous, saintly, serene face to the world. But behind that saintly facade lurks the dark side: hidden jealousy, irrational cruelty, petty malice, and sexual obsessions. Luang Por warns that repressing the shadow creates dangerous hypocrisy and spiritual neurosis. Only when the dark side is welcomed into awareness does true wholeness dawn.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Jungian shadow integrated with Buddhist psychology.
- The Upakkilesa Sutta: the sixteen corruptions of mind that obscure natural radiance.
- Why pretending to be holy is the greatest obstacle to enlightenment.
- Holding our ugly, petty, and foolish thoughts in the boundless, non-judgmental space of Buddho.
