### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how the thinking mind continuously manufactures and fabricates the illusion of a personal self.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the psychic machinery of self-creation (ahaṁkāra / mamaṁkāra). We are not born with a fixed ego; we manufacture ourselves moment by moment through memory, defensiveness, narrative, and social performance. When we see how exhausting and artificial this self-creation is, we simply stop doing it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding 'ahaṁkāra' (the 'I-maker') and 'mamaṁkāra' (the 'mine-maker') in Buddhist psychology.
- How we build an ego out of our wounds, achievements, and grievances.
- The physical exhaustion of maintaining the public persona.
- Discovering the relief of non-creation: resting as unconstructed awareness.
