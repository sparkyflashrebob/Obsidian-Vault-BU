### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Evening meditation instructions on gathering scattered mental energy and settling into deep samādhi at the close of retreat day.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in the quiet Temple at Amaravati during a monastic winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho guides monastics and lay residents into deep evening stillness. After hours of sitting and walking, the physical body carries fatigue and the mind may feel restless. Luang Por shows how to release physical tensions, forgive any unskillful reactions that arose during the day, and rest in the cool sanctuary of conscious awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Transitioning from daytime activity into evening contemplative silence.
- Releasing bodily fatigue: letting gravity hold the spine and shoulders.
- The practice of daily mental forgiveness: laying down burdens before sleep.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that neither sleeps nor tires.
