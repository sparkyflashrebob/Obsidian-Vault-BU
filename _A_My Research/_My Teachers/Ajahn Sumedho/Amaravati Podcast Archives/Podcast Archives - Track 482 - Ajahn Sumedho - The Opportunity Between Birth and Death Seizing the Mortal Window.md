### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the human lifespan as a finite, precious window between birth and death: utilizing our mortal existence to realize the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this sobering and urgent 51-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho contemplates mortality. From the moment of birth, every human being is marching toward physical death. Most people spend this precious window between birth and death accumulating toys, managing impressions, and distracting themselves from their mortality. Luang Por reminds us that this life is our supreme opportunity: we can use this body and mind to awaken to the Deathless before the biological vehicle expires.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Five Daily Recollections (Upajjhaṭṭhana): aging, illness, death, separation, kamma.
- Maraṇānussati (mindfulness of death) as the supreme generator of clarity and urgency.
- Why contemplating death increases our love and appreciation for each living moment.
- Realizing the Deathless (Amata) that was never born and can never die.
