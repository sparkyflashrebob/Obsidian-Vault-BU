### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Opening morning tea dialogue of the winter retreat: establishing the foundation of practice, harmonizing calm and insight.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In the inaugural 69-minute session of the 2003 winter retreat morning conversations, Luang Por Sumedho sits with monastics and long-term lay practitioners to establish the baseline of the retreat. He outlines how tranquility (*samatha*) and insight (*vipassanā*) develop together in tandem (*yuganaddha*). Luang Por invites practitioners to drop all preconceived spiritual formulas and begin from bare, unadorned present-moment experience.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Yuganaddha Sutta: developing tranquility leading to insight, and insight leading to tranquility.
- Creating a sacred, safe retreat container: noble silence and sensory moderation.
- Dropping the baggage of previous retreats and spiritual accomplishments.
- The baseline of practice: returning repeatedly to the simplicity of the body sitting here.
