### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Observing the lunar Uposatha observance: aligning monastic rhythm with cosmic cycles and investigating the waxing and waning of mental states.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on a rare 'Blue Moon' Uposatha evening, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on natural rhythms, monastic discipline, and the lunar calendar. Modern industrial culture has severed human life from nature with electric lights and 24/7 schedules. Monastic tradition preserves the ancient lunar rhythm of Uposatha—all-night meditation vigils, Pātimokkha recitations, and fasting. Luang Por reminds us that mental states wax and wane exactly like the moon.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The sacred tradition of the lunar Uposatha (observance day) in the Sangha.
- How physical and psychological energy fluctuates with lunar tides.
- The practice of all-night sittings: transcending physical fatigue through wakefulness.
- Resting as the vast sky that remains unaffected whether the moon is full or dark.
