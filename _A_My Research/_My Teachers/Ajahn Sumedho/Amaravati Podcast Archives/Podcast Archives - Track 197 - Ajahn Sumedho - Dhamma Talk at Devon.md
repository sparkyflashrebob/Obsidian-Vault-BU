### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Visiting the secluded branch monastery in Devon, Luang Por Sumedho delivers a 66-minute heart discourse on rural simplicity, nature contemplation, and somatic grounding. Away from the bustle of large centers, Devon offers quiet lanes and woodland silence. Luang Por reminds the community that external quiet is a supportive tool, but internal quiet requires relinquishing our mental resistance to life as it presents itself.
