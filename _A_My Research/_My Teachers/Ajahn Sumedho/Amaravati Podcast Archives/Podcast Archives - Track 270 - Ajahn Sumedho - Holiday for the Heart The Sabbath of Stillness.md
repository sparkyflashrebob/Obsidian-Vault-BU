### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Meditation as the ultimate inner vacation: letting go of all mental duties, striving, and self-improvement.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers an enchanting 81-minute Sunday talk comparing meditation to a 'holiday for the heart'. People spend thousands traveling to tropical islands seeking relaxation, only to carry their anxious, complaining minds in their luggage. Luang Por shows that true rest requires no airfare: it is simply giving the heart a holiday from the exhausting job of being 'somebody'. When we stop improving ourselves and rest in awareness, true rejuvenation occurs.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The futility of external holidays when the internal complainer travels along.
- Giving the mind a complete Sabbath: no projects, no duties, no spiritual goals.
- The restorative power of Passaddhi (bodily and mental tranquility).
- Resting in the unconditioned silence where the heart is permanently at home.
