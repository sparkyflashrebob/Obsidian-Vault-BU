### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the fundamental human terror of death as the fear of the unknown, and taking refuge in the Deathless (Amata).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a 56-minute masterclass on death and dying. People claim they are afraid of death, but Luang Por asks: 'What is death? Have you ever experienced it?' What we actually fear is our own conceptual projections: fear of pain, fear of losing our loved ones, fear of vanishing into nothingness. When we admit 'I don't know', fear transforms into profound wonder, opening the door to the Unborn and Deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Abhaya Sutta: the four kinds of people who fear death vs. the one who is fearless.
- Deconstructing the fear of death into biological survival reflexes and mental storytelling.
- The spiritual power of embracing 'Don't Know Mind': stepping into the great mystery.
- Resting as the Deathless awareness that knows the body's dissolution without dying.
