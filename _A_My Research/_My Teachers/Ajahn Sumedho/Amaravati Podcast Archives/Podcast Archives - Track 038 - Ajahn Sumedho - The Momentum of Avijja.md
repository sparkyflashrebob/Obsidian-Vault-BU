### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Interrupting the habitual momentum of spiritual ignorance (avijjā) through direct mindfulness of feeling.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the deep momentum of spiritual ignorance (avijjā) that propels the twelve links of Dependent Origination. Ignorance is not a lack of academic knowledge, but the fundamental failure to recognize awareness in the present moment, allowing raw sensory feelings to instantly trigger craving and clinging.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Defining Avijjā as 'not knowing' the Four Noble Truths in real-time experience.
- How sensory contact (phassa) gives rise to feeling (vedanā), and how ignorance turns feeling into craving (taṇhā).
- Interrupting the cycle at the link between feeling and craving by resting as the unentangled knower.
- Rewiring the chronic habit of anxiety and reaction into the stable momentum of peace.
