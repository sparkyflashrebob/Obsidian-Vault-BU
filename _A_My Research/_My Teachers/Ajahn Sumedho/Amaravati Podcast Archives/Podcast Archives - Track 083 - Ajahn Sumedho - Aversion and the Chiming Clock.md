### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating irritation, resentment, and sensory aversion through everyday monastic triggers.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic 69-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores how the mind manufactures suffering out of innocent external sounds. He relates his experience with an antique chiming clock in the monastery that initially drove him crazy with annoyance. By investigating the aversion rather than the clock, the sound transformed into a beloved Dhamma teacher.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding that aversion (dosa) is an internal emotional reaction, not an intrinsic property of the sound.
- Examining the physical symptoms of irritation: tightening jaw, constricted breath, racing angry thoughts.
- Using irritating sounds (chiming clocks, lawnmowers, coughing) as immediate bells of mindfulness.
- Cultivating unconditional patience (khanti) that remains serene in any sonic environment.
