### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme gift we can offer to ourselves and others: the unconditional, healing presence of awake, undivided attention.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this tender and expansive 68-minute discourse celebrating the 475th track milestone, Luang Por Sumedho explores 'the gift of presence'. In our hurried, distracted civilization, human beings offer each other material gifts, advice, opinions, and solutions, but rarely offer pure presence. Luang Por demonstrates that undivided, spacious, non-judgmental presence is the most healing power in the universe. When we offer presence to our own aching bodies and to suffering companions, all wounds begin to heal.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The crisis of attention in modern life: fractured minds, digital distraction, and loneliness.
- Presence as the living embodiment of Sati-sampajañña.
- How giving 100% presence to another person communicates unconditional worth and love.
- Resting in the eternal divine presence of the unconditioned (Asaṅkhata).
