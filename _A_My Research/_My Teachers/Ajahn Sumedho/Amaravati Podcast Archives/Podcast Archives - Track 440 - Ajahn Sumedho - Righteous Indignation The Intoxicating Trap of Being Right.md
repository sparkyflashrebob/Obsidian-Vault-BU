### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling the most insidious form of anger: moralistic self-righteousness, righteous anger, and ideological outrage.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this incisive and humorous 52-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho exposes one of the ego's favorite intoxicants: righteous indignation. Unlike crude animal rage, righteous indignation disguises itself as noble virtue. We feel completely justified in hating, canceling, or condemning others because 'they are wrong and we are right.' Luang Por warns that anger with a noble reason is still anger—it burns the heart and breeds conflict. True Dhamma stands for justice without a single drop of hatred.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychology of righteous indignation: why moral outrage feels so addictive.
- The Kodhana Sutta: the seven ways anger harms the angry person long before harming the enemy.
- The difference between clear ethical discernment and vindictive outrage.
- Embodying fearless truth while maintaining boundless Mettā.
