### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical morning monastic reflection: realizing that the conditioned personality cannot meditate or be mindful; awareness alone is awake.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through one of the most stubborn misconceptions in meditation: the belief that 'my personality' is going to become enlightened. We try to train our nervous, introverted, or ambitious personality to be calm and mindful. Luang Por reveals: 'The personality cannot be mindful!' Personality is merely a conditioned collection of habits, memories, and defensive masks. Awareness is mindful; awareness is already awake. Drop the personality and rest as Buddho.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The delusion of the 'mindful personality': trying to build an enlightened ego.
- Personality as conditioned saṅkhāras: cultural conditioning, language, and trauma.
- The shift from trying to fix personality to resting as the knowing witness.
- The effortless spaciousness that arises when personality is recognized as Anattā.
