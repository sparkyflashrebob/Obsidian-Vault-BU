### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the nature of conditioned phenomena (saṅkhāras) and resting in the unconditioned (asaṅkhata).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 63-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the comprehensive Buddhist concept of saṅkhāras (conditioned phenomena). Everything in the sensory world—bodies, thoughts, societies, solar systems, and emotions—is conditioned: brought into being by causes, altering as it stands, and inevitably ceasing. True peace is not found by rearranging conditions, but by awakening to the Unconditioned that holds them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding the Pāli term 'saṅkhāra': that which is put together, compounded, and formed.
- The universal tragedy of expecting conditioned things to provide permanent, unconditioned happiness.
- How winter retreat strips away external conditions to reveal the internal machinery of thought.
- Abiding in the Deathless (Amata) that was never put together and can never fall apart.
