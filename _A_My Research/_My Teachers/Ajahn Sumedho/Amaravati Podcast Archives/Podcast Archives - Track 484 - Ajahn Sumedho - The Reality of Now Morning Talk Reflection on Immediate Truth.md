### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic reflection on the concrete reality of the present moment: stripping away mental overlays to experience Now as it is.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho guides the Sangha into the concrete reality of Now. When we wake up, our minds immediately invent a complex virtual reality: schedules, problems, memories of yesterday, and worries about tomorrow. Luang Por reminds monastics to look at what is actually real right now: the body sitting on the floor, the temperature of the air, the sound of the bell. Now is the only reality there is.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Virtual mental reality versus concrete present reality.
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: making the present moment our stable sanctuary.
- Using morning Temple reflection to dismantle mental projections before work begins.
- Resting in the unconditioned truth that is sandiṭṭhiko (directly visible).
