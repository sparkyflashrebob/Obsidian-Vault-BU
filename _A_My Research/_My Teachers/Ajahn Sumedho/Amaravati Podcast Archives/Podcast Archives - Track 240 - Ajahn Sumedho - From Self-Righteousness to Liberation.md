### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling the corrosive defilement of self-righteousness, moral superiority, and spiritual conceit (māna).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this powerful 2021 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho tackles one of the most insidious obstacles in spiritual practice: self-righteousness. Meditators often become vegetarians, environmentalists, or disciplined monks, only to develop a toxic arrogance that judges meat-eaters, polluters, and ordinary people as inferior. Luang Por reveals that self-righteousness is merely ego in holy disguise. Real liberation begins when we let go of being 'right'.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Self-righteousness as the armor of spiritual pride and conceit (māna).
- The Three Modes of Conceit: 'I am better', 'I am equal', 'I am worse'.
- How moral superiority alienates friends, family, and Sangha members.
- True holiness as humble, harmless, non-judgmental Mettā.
