### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Talk 224: Discovering the realm beyond disappointment: releasing rigid expectations and taking refuge in the reality of suchness (tathatā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 46-minute discourse delivered in January 2021 (Talk 224), Luang Por Sumedho explores the root of human disappointment. Disappointment is not caused by external events; it is the inevitable collision between our rigid expectations and the flowing reality of life. Whenever we demand that conditions, people, monasteries, or our own minds be flawless, disappointment is guaranteed. Luang Por points to 'Where There Is No Disappointment': the unconditioned space of awareness that expects nothing and welcomes everything.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The anatomy of disappointment: expectations versus reality.
- How romanticizing spiritual communities leads to bitter cynicism.
- The practice of meeting conditions without pre-programmed demands.
- Resting in the unconditioned (asaṅkhata) as the only place where disappointment cannot exist.
