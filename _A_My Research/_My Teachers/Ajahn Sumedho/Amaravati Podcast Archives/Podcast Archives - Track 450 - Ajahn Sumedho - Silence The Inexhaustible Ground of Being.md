### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this landmark 54-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the ultimate mystery and refuge: Silence. We spend our lives bombarded by sound—speech, machines, traffic, music, and the endless internal commentary of thought. Luang Por guides practitioners into the primal silence within which all sounds appear and vanish. By tuning into the inner Sound of Silence (*nāda*), we anchor consciousness directly in the unconditioned peace of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Outer silence versus inner contemplative silence.
- The Sound of Silence (nāda) as the audible vibration of the unconditioned.
- How sounds arise out of silence and return to silence without breaking it.
- Resting in the eternal, inexhaustible ground of being.
