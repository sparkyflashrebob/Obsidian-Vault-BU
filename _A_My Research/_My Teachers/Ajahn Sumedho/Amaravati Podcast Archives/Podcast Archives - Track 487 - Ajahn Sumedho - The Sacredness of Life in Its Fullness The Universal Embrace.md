### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Honoring the total fullness of human existence: welcoming birth, death, joy, and sorrow into the sacred embrace of the Brahmavihāras.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this expansive 42-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sacredness of life. People often try to edit life—wanting only the sunny, peaceful, pleasant moments, while rejecting sickness, grief, and conflict. Luang Por teaches that true sacredness is the capacity to embrace life in its entirety. The four Brahmavihāras—Mettā, Karuṇā, Muditā, Upekkhā—are immeasurable because they have room for the whole human comedy and tragedy.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Editing life versus embracing life in its fullness.
- The four Brahmavihāras as the universal divine abodes of consciousness.
- Finding sacredness not in church rituals, but in present-moment reverence.
- The unconditioned heart as the infinite ocean that holds all waves.
