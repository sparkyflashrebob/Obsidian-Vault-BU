### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The nature of consciousness (viññāṇa) versus the unconditioned luminous knowing (viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute contemplation, Luang Por Sumedho explores the subtle distinctions between sensory consciousness that grasps objects and the unmanifest, luminous consciousness (viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ) that transcends time and space. When consciousness stops grasping at its own contents, it awakens to its true, pristine nature.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the famous Kevatta Sutta riddle: 'Where do earth, water, fire, and wind find no footing?'
- The distinction between sense-door viññāṇa (eye-consciousness, etc.) and pure unentangled knowing.
- How identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) attempts to make consciousness into an immortal personal soul.
- Resting in the natural radiance of the mind when defilements subside.
