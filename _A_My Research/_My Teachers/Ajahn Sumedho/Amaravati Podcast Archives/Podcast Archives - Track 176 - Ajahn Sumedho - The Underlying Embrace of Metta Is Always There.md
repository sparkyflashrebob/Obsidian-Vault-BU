### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in boundless loving-kindness (mettā) not as an effortful sentimental projection, but as the natural spacious embrace of unentangled awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this retreat discourse from 2020, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the profound nature of Mettā. Often practitioners treat loving-kindness as an emotional exercise where one tries to manufacture pleasant warmth toward difficult people. Luang Por reveals that true Mettā is non-resistance: the unconditional, patient acceptance of reality as it is. When the ego steps back, the natural warmth of consciousness embraces all experience without boundary.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing Mettā as an emotional performance vs. natural spacious acceptance.
- Holding self-criticism, irritation, and physical discomfort in the warm container of patience.
- The Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta: radiating goodwill like a mother cherishing her only child.
- Resting in the unconditioned heart that neither clings to favor nor retaliates against blame.
