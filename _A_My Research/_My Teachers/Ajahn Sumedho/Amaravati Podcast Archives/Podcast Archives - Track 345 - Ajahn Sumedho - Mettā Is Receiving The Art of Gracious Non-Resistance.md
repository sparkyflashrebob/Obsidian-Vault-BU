### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mettā understood as receiving reality: cultivating total non-resistant receptivity to whatever enters the senses.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep and evocative 67-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho offers an illuminating perspective: 'Mettā is receiving.' We often think of loving-kindness as an active, outgoing radiation or energy projection. But the deeper, more transformative aspect of Mettā is receptive: receiving the moment, receiving bodily aches, receiving difficult personalities, and receiving our own moods without resistance or complaint.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Active radiating Mettā versus receptive welcoming Mettā.
- How emotional suffering is caused by resisting what has already arrived.
- Receiving physical discomfort and illness with gracious patience.
- The unconditioned heart as the open sky that receives clouds, storms, and sunshine alike.
