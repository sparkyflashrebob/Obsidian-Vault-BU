### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the purpose of daily Pūjā: how chanting, bowing, and communal stillness cultivate reverence, humility, and Sangha harmony.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a question often asked by modern Western practitioners: 'Why come to Pūjā?' In individualistic cultures, daily religious liturgy is frequently viewed as a tedious chore or mindless ritual. Luang Por explains that Pūjā is a profound contemplative discipline. Bowing to the Triple Gem deflates ego conceit; chanting sacred suttas attunes the voice and heart to timeless truth; and sitting together in communal silence generates a protective canopy of collective mindfulness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychological and contemplative function of ritual, bowing, and chanting.
- Overcoming individualistic resistance to communal monastic schedules.
- The sound of chanting as an embodied meditation that calms the nervous system.
- Cultivating reverence (Gārava) and humility as antidotes to cynicism.
