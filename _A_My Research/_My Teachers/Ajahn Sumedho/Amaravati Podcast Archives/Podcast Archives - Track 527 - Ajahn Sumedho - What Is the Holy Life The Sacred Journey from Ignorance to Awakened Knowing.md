### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the Noble Search (Ariyapariyesanā): dedicating our brief human lifespan to that which is beyond aging, sickness, and death.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 73-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the Buddha's Noble Search. The ignoble search is seeking fulfillment in things that are themselves subject to birth, aging, sickness, and death (possessions, partners, reputation). The noble search is dedicating oneself to that which is unconditioned and deathless. Luang Por shows how the Holy Life is accessible to every sincere practitioner—monastic or lay—who chooses awareness over heedlessness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The distinction between the Noble Search (Ariya-pariyesanā) and the Ignoble Search (Anariya-pariyesanā).
- Waking up to the stark realities of aging, illness, and mortality.
- The refuge of the Dhamma as a living current guiding the contemplative heart.
- Moving beyond religious formalism into direct experiential knowing.
