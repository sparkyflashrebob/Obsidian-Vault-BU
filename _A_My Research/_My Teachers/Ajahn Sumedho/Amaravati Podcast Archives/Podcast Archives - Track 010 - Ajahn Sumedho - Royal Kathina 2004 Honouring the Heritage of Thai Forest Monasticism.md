The ceremonial presentation of the Royal Kathina Robe graciously bestowed by H.M. the King of Thailand.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the historic 2004 Royal Kathina gathering at Amaravati, where a royal robe was bestowed on behalf of King Bhumibol Adulyadej (Rama IX) of Thailand. Luang Por reflects on the cultural bridge connecting ancient Thai Theravāda monasticism with Western society. He emphasizes that while royal ceremonies carry majestic dignity, the heart of the occasion is universal: honoring moral virtue, selfless giving, and the Triple Gem.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Royal Gift**: The significance of the Thai crown supporting Western monasticism.
- **Tradition and Transience**: Honoring formal cultural forms without getting trapped in attachment.
- **The Unbroken Lineage**: How the forest tradition survived for 2,500 years through voluntary dāna.
