### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning retreat reflection examining political and ideological polarization: stepping out of binary tribalism into non-contentious Dhamma peace.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 51-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the pervasive cultural disease of binary thinking: 'You are either with us, or you are against us.' Whether in global politics, monastery disputes, or domestic arguments, the unawakened mind divides the world into rigid tribes: allies and enemies, good guys and bad guys. Luang Por guides practitioners into the Kalahavivāda Sutta (Sutta on Quarrels and Disputes), showing how awakening dissolves ideological camps and reveals the universal dignity of all beings.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychology of political and religious tribalism: us versus them.
- The Sutta Nipāta teachings on non-contention (Kalahavivāda and Cūḷaviyūha Suttas).
- How identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) feeds righteous outrage and warfare.
- Practicing the Middle Way: standing in clear, compassionate awareness beyond polarization.
