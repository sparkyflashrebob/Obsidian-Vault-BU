### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Debunking the myth that Nibbāna is millions of lifetimes away: realizing cessation immediately in the present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho directly challenges the popular Theravāda misconception that enlightenment requires eons of merit-making and is practically impossible in this life. Luang Por reminds the assembly of the Dhamma's core attributes: sandiṭṭhiko (directly visible here and now) and akāliko (timeless). Nibbāna is not a distant golden city; it is the immediate cessation of greed, hatred, and delusion right here.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cultural myth of spiritual postponement: waiting for the future Buddha Metteyya.
- The six qualities of the Dhamma: Svākkhato, Sandiṭṭhiko, Akāliko, Ehipassiko, Opanayiko, Paccattaṃ veditabbo.
- Nibbāna defined as cooling and cessation: noticing the spaces between thoughts.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that is intrinsically free from defilement.
