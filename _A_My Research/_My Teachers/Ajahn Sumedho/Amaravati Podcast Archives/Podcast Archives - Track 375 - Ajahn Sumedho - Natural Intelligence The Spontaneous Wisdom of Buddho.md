### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contrasting artificial intellectual cleverness with natural Dhamma intelligence: the intuitive knowing of the unconditioned heart.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the nature of true intelligence. Modern civilization idolizes artificial intellectual cleverness—accumulating data, winning academic debates, and calculating advantages. But clever people often live miserable lives because intellectual knowledge cannot conquer greed, anger, or existential terror. Luang Por reveals 'Natural Intelligence': the intuitive, unconditioned wisdom of Buddho that responds to life with spontaneous skill and compassion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Intellectual cleverness versus natural Dhamma intelligence (*paññā*).
- Why academic mastery of Buddhist texts often increases spiritual arrogance.
- The intuitive knowing of the unconditioned heart (*citta* / *Buddho*).
- Living with natural intelligence: spontaneous, uncalculated Right Action in daily life.
