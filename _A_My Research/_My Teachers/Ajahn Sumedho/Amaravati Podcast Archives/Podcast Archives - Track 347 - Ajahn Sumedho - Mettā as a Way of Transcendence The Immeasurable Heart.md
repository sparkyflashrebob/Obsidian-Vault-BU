### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Brahmavihāras as an elevator out of narrow egoic consciousness into cosmic, immeasurable liberation (Appamaññā Ceto-vimutti).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this expansive 83-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Tevijja Sutta, where the Buddha teaches the practice of radiating the four Brahmavihāras—loving-kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, and equanimity—in all directions: above, below, around, everywhere. Luang Por shows that when Mettā becomes immeasurable (*appamaññā*), it transcends all personal boundaries, dissolving identity view and leading directly to liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Tevijja Sutta: the ancient Vedic search for union with Brahma reinterpreted by the Buddha.
- The four directional radiation of Mettā: breaking out of the localized 'me' box.
- Appamaññā Ceto-vimutti: liberation of the heart through immeasurable goodwill.
- How cosmic love systematically burns away the five lower fetters.
