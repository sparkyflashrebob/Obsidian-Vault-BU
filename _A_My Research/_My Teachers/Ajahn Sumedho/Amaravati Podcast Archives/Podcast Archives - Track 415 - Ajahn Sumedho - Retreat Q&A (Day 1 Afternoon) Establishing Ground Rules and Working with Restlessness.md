### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute afternoon session on the first day of an intensive retreat, Luang Por Sumedho takes questions from practitioners who have just plunged into noble silence. The opening days of a retreat are notoriously turbulent: the nervous system is still vibrating with worldly momentum, smartphones have been surrendered, and the mind is wildly restless. Luang Por explains how to settle into the retreat container with patience and kindness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The initial shock of silence: transitioning from worldly overstimulation into monastery quiet.
- Working with Uddhacca (restlessness): observing mental speed without panic.
- Posture fundamentals: chairs versus meditation cushions; releasing physical performance pressure.
- Using the retreat schedule as a supportive womb rather than an oppressive prison.
