### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Disentangling modern slogans of 'following your heart' from authentic Buddhist intuitive awareness (ñāṇa-dassana).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 2021 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho critiques popular New Age advice: 'Just follow your heart!' Luang Por warns that the human 'heart' is often full of greed, romantic delusion, and neurotic impulse. If you follow an unpurified heart, you will follow it straight into suffering! True guidance comes not from following emotions, but from trusting awareness—the silent knowing that sees feelings, impulses, and wisdom with total clarity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The popular romantic myth of 'following your feelings' vs. Buddhist discernment.
- How unpurified desire masquerades as 'spiritual intuition'.
- The cultivation of pure awareness prior to emotional preference.
- Right Action (sammā-kammanta) arising naturally from stillness rather than emotional reaction.
