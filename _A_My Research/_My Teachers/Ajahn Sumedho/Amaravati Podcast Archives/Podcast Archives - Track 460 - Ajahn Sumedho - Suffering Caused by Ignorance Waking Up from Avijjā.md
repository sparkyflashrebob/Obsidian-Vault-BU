### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic reflection on the root cause of all human anguish: exploring how ignorance (*avijjā*) fabricates unnecessary suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 66-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho traces human suffering back to its root source: ignorance (*avijjā*). Ignorance in Buddhism is not a lack of academic knowledge, technological skill, or general intelligence. Ignorance is simply not knowing the Four Noble Truths in real-time experience. Luang Por shows how waking up to our daily ignorance instantly dismantles unnecessary mental anguish.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Defining Avijjā: spiritual blindness to the Four Noble Truths.
- How highly intelligent, educated people live in acute spiritual ignorance.
- The mechanics of Avijjā in daily life: projecting permanency onto changing forms.
- Mindfulness as the direct dawn of wisdom (Vijjā) that banishes ignorance.
