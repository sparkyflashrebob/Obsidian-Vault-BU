### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Expressing authentic love and spiritual devotion through selfless service, deep listening, and moral virtue.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the deeper spiritual dimensions of love beyond romantic projection or possessive craving. Authentic love in the Buddhist tradition is expressed through the four Brahmavihāras: unconditional benevolence (mettā), active compassion (karuṇā), joy in others' success (muditā), and grounded equanimity (upekkhā).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contrasting possessive craving (taṇhā) with pure, unentangled love (mettā).
- The practice of deep listening: offering an undivided receptive presence without interrupting or judging.
- How keeping moral precepts (sīla) is the highest form of love we can offer to our families and community.
- Dissolving barriers of self and other in the boundless ocean of loving presence.
