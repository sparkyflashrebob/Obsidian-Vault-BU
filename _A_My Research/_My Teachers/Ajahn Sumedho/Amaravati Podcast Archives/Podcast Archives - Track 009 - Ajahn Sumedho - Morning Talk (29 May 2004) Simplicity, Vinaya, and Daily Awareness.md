### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Living the noble lineage (ariya-vaṁsa): contentment with simple food, robes, lodging, and delight in meditation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive morning discourse delivered to the monastic community and guests in May 2004, Luang Por Sumedho expounds the ancient Ariyavaṁsa Sutta—the Noble Lineage. Monastic life is designed around intentional simplicity: contentment with whatever food, robes, and lodging are provided, and finding delight in meditation (*bhāvanārāmatā*). When we stop demanding that life cater to our desires, the heart rests in boundless contentment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Four Traditions of the Noble Ones**: Contentment with food, robes, shelter, and meditation.
- **The Friction of Communal Work**: Using daily chores and kitchen duties as vehicles of awareness.
- **Simplicity as Inner Luxury**: Discovering that true wealth is wanting very little.
