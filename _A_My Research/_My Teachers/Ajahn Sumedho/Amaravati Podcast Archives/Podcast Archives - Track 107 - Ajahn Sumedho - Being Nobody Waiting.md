### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The profound practice of being an anonymous 'nobody' and abiding comfortably in empty waiting.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates the modern dread of waiting and the terror of being an anonymous nobody. In a hyper-connected world where everyone is driven to establish an online brand and stay perpetually occupied, sitting quietly with nowhere to go and nothing to prove is a revolutionary act of spiritual sovereignty.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The anxiety that surfaces when there is nothing to do, nothing to read, and no one to impress.
- How waiting at bus stops, clinics, or meditation halls can become the supreme temple of presence.
- Dropping the heavy psychological armor of being an important professional or abbot.
- The supreme relief of discovering that being 'nobody' is the gateway to the Deathless.
