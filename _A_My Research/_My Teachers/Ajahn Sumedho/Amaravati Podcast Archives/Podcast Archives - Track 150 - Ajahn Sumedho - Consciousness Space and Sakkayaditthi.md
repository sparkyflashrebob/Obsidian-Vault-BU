### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) through the twin contemplations of space and unconditioned consciousness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 62-minute late-period discourse from May 2021, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the root fetter that binds beings to saṁsāra: personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi). By expanding consciousness into the boundless contemplation of space, the tight, defensive knots of selfhood dissolve. When we see that personality is merely a collection of conditioned memories and habits, the heart rests in unshakeable freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Sakkāya-diṭṭhi as the first of the ten fetters to be severed upon stream-entry (sotāpatti).
- How society brainwashes humans to identify with nationality, gender, career, and trauma.
- Using space meditation to pop the bubble of self-referential claustrophobia.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that was never a mortal personality.
