### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute Q&A session on the second afternoon of retreat, the questions shift from physical posture to emotional turmoil. As coarse mental chatter subsides, unresolved personal baggage surfaces: guilt about aging parents, anxiety over teenage children, and brutal self-criticism. Luang Por Sumedho demonstrates how to radiate Mettā to our loved ones from a distance, and how to disarm the inner judge with humorous compassion.
