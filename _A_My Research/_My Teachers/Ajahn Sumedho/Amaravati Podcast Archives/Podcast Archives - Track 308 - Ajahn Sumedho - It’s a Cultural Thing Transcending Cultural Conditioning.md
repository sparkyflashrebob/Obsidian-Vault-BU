### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A humorous and profound exploration of cultural conditioning: separating authentic universal Dhamma from Thai, British, and American cultural habits.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
With brilliant wit and deep discernment, Luang Por Sumedho tackles cultural conditioning in spiritual communities. Amaravati is a vibrant crossroad of Thai monastic customs, British reserve, American assertiveness, and European seekers. Luang Por reminds the assembly: 'Don't confuse culture with Dhamma!' Eating sticky rice, wearing Thai robes, or drinking afternoon tea are cultural conventions. True Dhamma is unconditioned and universal, belonging to no nation or era.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between universal Dhamma truth and Asian cultural forms.
- The clash of cultural expectations in international monastic communities.
- How people mistake cultural conformity for spiritual attainment.
- Taking refuge in the unconditioned heart that transcends all cultural identities.
