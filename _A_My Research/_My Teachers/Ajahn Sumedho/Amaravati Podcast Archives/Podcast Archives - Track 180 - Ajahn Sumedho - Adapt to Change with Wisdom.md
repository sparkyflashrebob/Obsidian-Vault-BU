### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating global uncertainty, biological aging, and societal upheaval through the wisdom of impermanence (anicca).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given during the global pandemic in 2020, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on adaptability and change. Human beings crave permanent security in a world whose fundamental nature is flux. When plans collapse and institutions shake, panic arises from arguing with reality. Luang Por guides the Sangha to align with impermanence rather than fighting it, finding the only real security in the unshakeable knowing that observes change without fear.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The suffering of demanding constancy from an inherently changing universe.
- Wise adaptation vs. resigned cynicism: meeting crises with calm, grounded intelligence.
- Contemplating biological aging, frailty, and world events as impersonal natural law.
- Resting in the unconditioned refuge (Buddho) amidst external instability.
