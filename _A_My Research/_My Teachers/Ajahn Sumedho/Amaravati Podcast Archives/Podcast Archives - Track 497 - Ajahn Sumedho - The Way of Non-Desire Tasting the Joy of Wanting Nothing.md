### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Walking the path of non-desire: discovering the sublime bliss (*niramisa-sukha*) of wanting nothing and having nothing to defend.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 67-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho turns modern consumer psychology upside down. The modern world teaches that happiness comes from acquiring the objects of our desire—more wealth, better food, exciting sex, higher status. Luang Por reveals that sensory gratification is fleeting and exhausting. The supreme happiness taught by the Buddha is *Taṇhākkhaya*—the destruction and cessation of desire. Wanting nothing is the ultimate freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Sensual happiness (Sāmisa-sukha) versus the bliss of non-desire (Nirāmisa-sukha).
- The exhaustion of feeding the hungry ghost of craving (Taṇhā).
- How renunciation (Nekkhamma) frees up vital energy for awakening.
- Resting in the complete, uncaused fullness of the present moment.
