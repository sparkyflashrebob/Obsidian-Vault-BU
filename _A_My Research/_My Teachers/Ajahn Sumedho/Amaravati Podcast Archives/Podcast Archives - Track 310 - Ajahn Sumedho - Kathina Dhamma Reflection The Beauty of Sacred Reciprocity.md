### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The annual Kathina robe-offering festival: exploring the symbiotic bond of mutual support between the monastic Sangha and the lay community.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the vibrant annual Kathina celebration at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the sacred institution of Kathina established by the Buddha. Monastics spend three months in retreat cultivating stillness and wisdom; at the retreat's conclusion, lay disciples gather to offer cloth and requisites. Luang Por illuminates the beauty of this 2,500-year-old relationship of mutual trust, generosity, and spiritual friendship.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The ancient historical origin of Kathina in the Vinaya Piṭaka.
- The symbiosis of the Buddhist community: monastics offering Dhamma; lay supporters offering material shelter.
- Dāna (generosity) not as a financial transaction, but as the supreme practice of loosening ego.
- The sewing of the Kathina robe on the same day as a living symbol of communal harmony.
