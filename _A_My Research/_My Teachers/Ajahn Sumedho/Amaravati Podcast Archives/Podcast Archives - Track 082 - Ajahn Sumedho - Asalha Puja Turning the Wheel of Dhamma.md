### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The revolutionary Middle Way (Majjhimā Paṭipadā) between sensory indulgence and self-mortification.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A magnificent 38-minute exposition on the core message of the Buddha's First Discourse. Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the Middle Way—steering clear of both the trap of sensory indulgence (kāmasukhallikānuyoga) and the trap of ascetic self-punishment (attakilamathānuyoga). The Middle Way is the receptive, mindful presence that knows both extremes without being trapped by either.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- How modern culture ricochets between hedonistic consumerism and neurotic puritanical diets.
- The Middle Way as a dynamic posture of conscious awareness rather than a bland compromise.
- Understanding the Three Turnings and Twelve Modes of the Four Noble Truths.
- Invoking the blessing of the Dhamma Wheel to guide our daily choices.
