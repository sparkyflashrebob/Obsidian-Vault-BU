### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating existence (jāti) and becoming (bhava) through silent retreat contemplation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 48-minute evening monastic discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the link between craving (taṇhā), clinging (upādāna), becoming (bhava), and birth (jāti). He explains how consciousness takes birth in a mood, an identity, or an argument, creating a whole world of suffering. By witnessing the arising of craving with dispassionate awareness, the cycle of birth and death is broken on the spot.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Tracing how subtle craving gives birth to the psychological sense of 'me' in real-time.
- Investigating how becoming someone is always accompanied by the fear of non-existence or loss.
- The realization of the Unborn (Ajāta) that stands outside the cycle of birth and death.
- Deep retreat silence as the sanctuary where the momentum of becoming winds down.
