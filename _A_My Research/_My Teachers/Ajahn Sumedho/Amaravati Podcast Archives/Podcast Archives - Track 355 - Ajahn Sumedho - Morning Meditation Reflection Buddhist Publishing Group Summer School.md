### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Practical morning meditation instructions for lay practitioners: somatic relaxation, establishing the breath anchor, and letting go of goals.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered to a large gathering of lay meditators at the annual Buddhist Publishing Group (BPG) Summer School in Leicester, Luang Por Sumedho offers an expansive 84-minute guide to morning meditation. He debunks the modern obsession with meditation techniques, goals, and spiritual ambition. Luang Por shows how true meditation is the radical cessation of ambition: resting as the knowing presence right here and now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Setting up the body: the importance of an erect, balanced spine without military rigidity.
- Releasing the spiritual ambition of 'getting enlightened by next Tuesday'.
- Ānāpānasati: watching the natural breath flow without controlling inhalation or exhalation.
- The Sound of Silence as a universal, non-sectarian contemplative refuge.
