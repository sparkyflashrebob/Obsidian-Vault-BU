### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning reflection on washing the mind clean of residual memory stains, grudges, and subconscious mental agitation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 43-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho uses the Buddha's Simile of the Cloth (Vatthūpama Sutta) to address how daily memories stain the mind. Just as a cloth covered in dust and stains cannot take dye evenly, a mind cluttered with memories and emotional grudges cannot reflect the Dhamma clearly. Luang Por guides monastics in washing the cloth of consciousness in the pure water of mindfulness each morning.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Simile of the Cloth: cleaning defilements of mind before dyeing with virtue.
- How waking up with yesterday's emotional momentum blinds us to the fresh day.
- The monastic practice of daily morning forgiveness and mutual reconciliation.
- Abiding in the fresh, unwritten space of bare awareness.
