### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The fundamental paradigm shift from seeking objects of awareness to recognizing the subjectless, unconditioned nature of knowing itself.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a razor-sharp exposition on the nature of consciousness. In meditation, the habit of the conditioned mind is to endlessly look for an object—a perfect breath, a light, a sensation, or a blissful state. Luang Por points out that awareness itself cannot be objectified. Like space, it has no color, shape, or weight, yet it contains all phenomena. In the seen, there is only the seen; in the known, only the known.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The seeker's dilemma: trying to grasp the Knower as if it were a thing to be possessed.
- The Bāhiya Sutta teaching: in the seen, merely the seen; no 'you' in terms of that.
- Resting as the empty sky of awareness through which sensory clouds pass without leaving a trace.
- Recognizing that peace is not an experience created by technique, but the natural ground of mind.
