### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Non-dual realization: recognizing that the physical body and universe appear within conscious space.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this radical 66-minute non-dual exposition, Luang Por Sumedho reverses the standard materialistic assumption about reality. Materialism claims that consciousness is a tiny physical byproduct produced inside a mortal skull. Through direct contemplative inquiry, Luang Por demonstrates that the body, the room, the sky, and the entire universe appear inside consciousness. Consciousness is the vast, boundaryless container.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Inverting the materialist paradigm: from 'consciousness inside the brain' to 'the body inside consciousness'.
- Contemplating the physical sensations of the body appearing as sensations inside conscious space.
- Dissolving the claustrophobia of bodily identification: we are not trapped in a biological cage.
- Resting in the unconditioned space of awareness that remains unconfined when the physical form dissolves.
