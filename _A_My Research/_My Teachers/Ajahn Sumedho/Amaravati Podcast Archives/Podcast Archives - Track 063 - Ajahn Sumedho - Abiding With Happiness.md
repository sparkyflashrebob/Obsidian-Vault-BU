### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating the pure, unconditioned happiness (sukha) born of renunciation, simplicity, and inner peace.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the true nature of happiness (sukha). While the world chases transient sensory excitement that inevitably collapses into exhaustion, the Buddha pointed to a deep, serene happiness that arises naturally when the grasping mind relaxes into contentment and ethical innocence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between worldly sensory excitement (kāmasukha) and spiritual peace (vūpasamasukha).
- Contemplating the Dhammapada verse: 'Happy indeed are we who own nothing; feeders on joy shall we be, like the Radiant Gods.'
- The joy of blamelessness (anavajjasukha) that flows from pure moral conduct (sīla).
- Learning to abide comfortably in simple moments of quiet without demanding external entertainment.
