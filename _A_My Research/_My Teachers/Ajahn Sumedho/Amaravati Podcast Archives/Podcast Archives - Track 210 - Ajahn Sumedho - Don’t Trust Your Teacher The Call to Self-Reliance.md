### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The radical Buddhist doctrine of self-reliance: taking refuge in verified direct experience rather than guru worship.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
With provocative humor and deep wisdom, Luang Por Sumedho tells his disciples: 'Don't trust me!' In an age of spiritual cults and guru scandals, Luang Por warns against the danger of projecting one's enlightenment onto an external master. The Buddha never demanded blind obedience; in the Kālāma Sutta, he insisted that we verify truth in our own direct experience. The teacher is merely a signpost pointing to the Dhamma within.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The spiritual trap of idealizing the teacher: guru devotion turning into childish dependence.
- The Kālāma Sutta: ten unreliable criteria (hearsay, lineage, charisma, scripture).
- The Buddha's final instruction: 'Be an island unto yourselves; take refuge in Dhamma.'
- Taking full adult responsibility for your own awakening, faults, and virtues.
