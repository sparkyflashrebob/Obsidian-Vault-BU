### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Shining the light of mindfulness on the chronic complaining mind: how the ego uses fault-finding to maintain a false sense of superiority.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this hilariously honest and penetrating 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho targets one of humanity's most cherished addictions: complaining. The tea is too cold, the room is too hot, the chant was too fast, the abbot's talk was too long! Luang Por demonstrates that complaining is not a reaction to bad conditions; it is a conditioned habit of the ego. The complaining mind will find fault in Paradise. Waking up to the complaining habit unlocks immediate, overflowing gratitude.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychology of the fault-finder: how complaining inflates the ego's sense of standards.
- Why fixing external conditions never cures the complaining habit.
- Mindfulness of the complaint: noticing the physical tightness in the throat and jaw.
- Transforming chronic complaint into joyful gratitude (Kataññutā) and contentment (Santosa).
