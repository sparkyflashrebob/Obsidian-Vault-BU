### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Making the radical intuitive shift from effortful meditation techniques to immediate resting in natural awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this profound late-period discourse from December 2021, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through what he terms a 'quantum leap' in practice. Instead of endlessly trying to build enlightenment through progressive meditation steps, one directly recognizes the unconditioned awareness that is already witnessing every step.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The limitation of treating meditation as a linear manufacturing process to produce a future enlightened ego.
- Direct realization of the Bāhiya Sutta instruction: 'In the seen, there is only the seen; in the heard, only the heard.'
- Recognizing that awareness is unconditioned, unconstructed, and effortlessly present right now.
- Resting as the knowing presence rather than manipulating physical or mental phenomena.
