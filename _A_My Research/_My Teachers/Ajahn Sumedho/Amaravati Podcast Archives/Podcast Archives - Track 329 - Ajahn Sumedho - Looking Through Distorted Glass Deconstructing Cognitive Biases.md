### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating cognitive distortions, emotional filters, and conditioned biases that distort perception of reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 71-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho compares the untrained mind to looking at the world through thick, wavy, distorted glass. Our perceptions are continually warped by four primary distortions (vipallāsa): taking the impermanent to be permanent, the painful to be pleasurable, the non-self to be self, and the foul to be beautiful. Through mindful inquiry, we clean the glass and perceive things as they truly are.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The four vipallāsas: perversions of perception, thought, and view.
- How emotional wounds and childhood conditioning warp adult interpretations.
- Projection: seeing our own unacknowledged anger and greed in other people.
- The practice of bare attention: perceiving sensory reality before concepts interfere.
