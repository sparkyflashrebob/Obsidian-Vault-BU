### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating what is truly trustworthy: examining the inherent instability of worldly security and finding refuge in the unconditioned.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 43-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho raises a fundamental existential question: 'What can you trust?' We place our trust in money, health, youthful vitality, social status, romantic relationships, and political institutions—yet every single one of these is subject to change, decay, and disappointment. Luang Por shows that true peace cannot be found by trying to stabilize unstable conditions. The only trustworthy refuge is the unconditioned awareness that knows conditions without clinging to them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The inherent betrayal of placing trust in impermanent conditions (anicca).
- The anxiety generated by trying to construct a risk-free worldly life.
- The deathless, uncreated nature of awareness as the only safe harbor.
- Shifting from panic-driven control to wise, serene relinquishment.
