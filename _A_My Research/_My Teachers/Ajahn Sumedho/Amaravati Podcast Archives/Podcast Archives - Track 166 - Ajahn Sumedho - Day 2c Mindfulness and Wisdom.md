### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The sacred marriage of mindfulness (sati) and wisdom (paññā): transforming bare attention into liberating insight.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of Day 2 of a 2017 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho explores the essential partnership of mindfulness and wisdom. Mindfulness establishes receptive presence and keeps the doors of perception open; wisdom investigates the Three Characteristics of whatever enters those doors. When sati and paññā work as one, the heart is protected from delusion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why bare mindfulness without wisdom can degenerate into passive daydreaming or amoral surveillance.
- How wisdom (paññā) applies the reflection: 'This is impermanent, unsatisfactory, and not-self.'
- The role of sati-paññā as an internal spiritual immune system defending against defilements.
- Resting in the luminous space where mindfulness and wisdom merge into effortless knowing.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sati (Mindfulness) | Receptive noticing, presence, remembrance | Notices sensations but may still identify with them | Maintains clear, unclouded presence |
| Paññā (Wisdom) | Discernment of causality, conditionality, Anattā | Can degenerate into dry intellectual debate | Instantly releases clinging |
| The Combined Power | Seeing what is arising and knowing it as empty | Vulnerable to emotional hijacking | Immediate cessation of suffering |
