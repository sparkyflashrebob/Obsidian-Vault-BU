### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning contemplation on the nature of the conditioned world (saṅkhāra-loka): investigating the arising, changing, and ceasing of all physical and mental phenomena.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during morning reflection in the Temple, Luang Por Sumedho invites monastics and lay residents to look directly at the conditioned world. Everything we experience through the five senses and the intellect is formed, born, compounded, and decaying. When we demand that the conditioned realm provide unconditioned security, we suffer. But when we investigate conditionality with dispassionate clarity, consciousness rests in the unconditioned ground.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The nature of saṅkhāras: all compounded phenomena are unstable and impermanent.
- The spiritual error of seeking lasting happiness within fleeting sensory objects.
- How morning monastic reflections establish a stable framework of Right View for the day.
- Resting as the unmoving knowing witness (Buddho) through all sensory encounters.
