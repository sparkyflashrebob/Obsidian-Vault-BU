### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The existential search for true rest: why neither worldly achievements nor altered meditative states provide lasting sanctuary.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this concentrated 24-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho asks the ultimate human question: 'Is there any place to rest?' In worldly life, we search for rest in relationships, vacations, homes, and financial retirement, yet rest remains elusive. Even in meditation, seeking rest in blissful absorption (*jhāna*) proves temporary. Luang Por reveals that true rest is not a place or an experience; it is the cessation of striving.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The exhaustion of chronic psychological wandering and seeking.
- The Ogha-taraṇa Sutta: crossing the flood by neither pausing nor straining.
- Why spiritual ambition and striving for enlightenment prevent true rest.
- Nibbāna as the unconditioned peace where the heart permanently lays down its burden.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls wandering as a solitary monk across the hills of England looking for a 'perfect quiet hermitage', until he realized the restlessness was inside his own feet, and that true rest was sitting right where he was.
