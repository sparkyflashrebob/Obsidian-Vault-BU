### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Experiencing Dhamma not as a philosophical dogma, but as the unshakeable reality of things as they are (Tathatā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 47-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through religious jargon. People often ask: 'What is Dhamma? Is it a philosophy, a religion, or a psychological system?' Luang Por states plainly: Dhamma is the Real. Whether Buddhas arise or do not arise, the law of nature remains: all conditioned things are impermanent, all conditioned things are unsatisfactory, all phenomena are non-self. Realizing Dhamma is waking up to what is.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Dhammaniyāma Sutta: the fixed natural order of Dhamma independent of opinion.
- How human cultures construct artificial realities that clash with the cosmic law.
- Truth as a living experience rather than an intellectual thesis.
- Surrendering to the Real: ending our lifelong argument with how things are.
