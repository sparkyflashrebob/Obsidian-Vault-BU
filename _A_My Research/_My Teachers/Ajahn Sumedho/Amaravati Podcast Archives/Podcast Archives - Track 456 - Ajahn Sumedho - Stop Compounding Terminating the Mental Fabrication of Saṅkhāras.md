### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Halting the runaway compounding of mental fabrications (saṅkhāras): how simple awareness stops psychological proliferation in its tracks.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this incisive 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the meaning of the word *saṅkhāra*—that which is compounded, fabricated, and concocted. Human suffering consists of compulsive compounding: a simple knee ache arises, and the mind immediately compounds it with irritation, medical anxiety, resentment, and identity. Luang Por gives the supreme Dhamma command: 'Stop compounding!' Learn to leave things as they are.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The definition of Saṅkhāra: mental concoctions, fabrications, and stories.
- Compounding interest on suffering: how an unexamined thought creates an emotional avalanche.
- The practice of bare awareness: witnessing sensory contact without adding mental dough.
- Resting in the uncompounded realm (Asaṅkhata Dhamma).
