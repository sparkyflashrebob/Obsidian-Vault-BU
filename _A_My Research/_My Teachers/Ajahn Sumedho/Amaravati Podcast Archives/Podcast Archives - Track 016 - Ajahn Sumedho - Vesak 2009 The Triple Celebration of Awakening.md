### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The triple commemoration of the Buddha's Birth, Supreme Enlightenment, and Final Parinibbāna under the May full moon.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the sacred full moon of Vesak, Luang Por Sumedho addresses thousands of pilgrims celebrating the Buddha's birth, awakening, and passing away. Luang Por shows that these three events are not ancient history; they reflect our own spiritual trajectory: being born into ignorance, awakening through the Four Noble Truths, and the final peaceful cessation of all craving and grasping in the heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Birth of the Seeker**: Honoring the rare blessing of human birth (*manussatta-paṭilābho*).
- **The Enlightenment at Bodh Gaya**: Discovering the Unconditioned beneath the Bodhi tree.
- **The Parinibbāna at Kusinara**: Learning the final lesson of impermanence: *vayadhammā saṅkhārā appamādena sampādetha*.
