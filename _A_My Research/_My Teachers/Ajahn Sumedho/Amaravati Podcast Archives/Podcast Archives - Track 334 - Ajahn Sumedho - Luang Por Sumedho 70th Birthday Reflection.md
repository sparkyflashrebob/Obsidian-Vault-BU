### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the occasion of his 70th birthday at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on his life and the remarkable growth of the Thai Forest lineage across Britain and Europe. Arriving at Hampstead Vihara in 1977 with four monks, nobody imagined branch monasteries would flourish. Luang Por reminds the assembly that institutional success is empty; the only true success is each person awakening to the unconditioned truth in their own heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Reflections on turning 70: entering the elder phase of monastic leadership.
- The history of the English Sangha: from Hampstead to Chithurst, Harnham, and Amaravati.
- The danger of institutional complacency: keeping the fire of meditation practice bright.
- Reaffirming the Four Noble Truths as the ultimate map of liberation.
