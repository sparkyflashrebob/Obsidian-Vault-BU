## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location
Amaravati Retreat Centre, UK.

### Canonical Setting
Dīpadasutta (SN 45.139) & Mahāparinibbāna Sutta (DN 16)

### Circumstances
Podcast archival discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, preserving foundational teachings of the Thai Forest Tradition for monastic and lay disciples worldwide.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Taking genuine refuge in awareness (Buddho) rather than external religious ceremonies, amulets, or dogmas.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A concentrated 20-minute retreat reflection delivered on Day 12. Luang Por reminds retreatants that taking refuge in the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha is not a superstitious ritual or cultural performance; it is taking refuge in the living, indestructible quality of receptive awareness that is present right now in your own heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Buddha's dying words: 'Be an island unto yourselves, an island of Dhamma, with no external refuge.'
- Moving beyond religious idolatry: Buddha is not a golden statue, but the knower.
- Dhamma as the ultimate truth of the way things are (Tathatā).
- Sangha as the noble quality of practicing in harmony with the Dhamma.

| Sangha | A club of ordained monks wearing robes | Those who live in alignment with ethical truth | Taking refuge in noble conduct |
