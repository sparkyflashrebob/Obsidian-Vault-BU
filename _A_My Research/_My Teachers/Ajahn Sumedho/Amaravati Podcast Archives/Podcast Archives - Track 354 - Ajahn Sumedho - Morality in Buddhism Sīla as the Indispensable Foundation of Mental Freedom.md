### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Why moral integrity (sīla) is not an arbitrary religious dogma, but the indispensable foundation of non-remorse and mental stillness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 50-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho rescues Buddhist morality (*sīla*) from conventional Western misunderstandings of sin and commandment. Sīla is not about pleasing an external deity or avoiding divine punishment. It is practical spiritual hygiene: living in a way that generates non-remorse (*avippatisāra*). When our speech and actions harm nobody, the mind naturally settles into effortless tranquility.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Five Precepts (Pañcasīla) as tools of freedom and self-protection.
- The causal progression: Sīla leads to non-remorse, joy, tranquility, and samādhi.
- Why attempting deep meditation without morality is like building a castle on sand.
- Monastic Vinaya as the ultimate container of communal safety and mutual trust.
