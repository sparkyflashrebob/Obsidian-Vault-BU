### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying spiritual awakening: why Dhamma is completely natural, direct, and obvious once we stop looking through conceptual filters.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho dispels the myth that enlightenment is an exotic, occult mystery imported from the Orient. The Dhamma is completely natural and obvious. Right now, hearing is occurring; breath is flowing; space is present; thoughts arise and fade. Truth is not hidden behind secret passwords; it is the immediate obvious reality of things as they are. Our only problem is that we ignore the obvious in search of the spectacular.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The tragedy of overlooking the obvious present moment in search of esoteric thrills.
- The Sandiṭṭhika (immediately visible) and Ehipassiko (inviting verification) nature of Dhamma.
- Ajahn Chah's simple, earthy metaphors cutting through academic mysticism.
- Resting in the unforced, natural awareness that is already awake.
