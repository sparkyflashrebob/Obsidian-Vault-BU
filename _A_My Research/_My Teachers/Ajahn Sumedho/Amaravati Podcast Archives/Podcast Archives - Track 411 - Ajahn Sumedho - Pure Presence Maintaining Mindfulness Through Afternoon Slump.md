### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating unbroken mindfulness during the challenging afternoon hours: working with physical fatigue, digestion, and routine.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this practical 45-minute afternoon discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the notorious 'afternoon slump' in monastic retreats. After lunch, the body is heavy with digestion, eyelids droop, and the morning's bright clarity fades. Luang Por teaches that spiritual practice is not only for bright, energized mornings; it is about bringing pure presence to dullness, heaviness, and lethargy. Being aware of sluggishness is itself pure wakefulness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Working skillfully with Thina-middha (sloth and torpor) in the afternoon.
- Walking meditation (caṅkama) as a powerful antidote to bodily sluggishness.
- Somatic investigation: feeling the heavy eyelids and sluggish pulse without despair.
- Maintaining the thread of pure presence through all four physical postures.
