### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Culminating elder reflections on five decades of monastic life, gratitude, and the enduring fruit of peace.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this concise and deeply moving 20-minute address from May 2017 during his 83rd year, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on five decades in the yellow robes. He expresses profound gratitude to the Sangha, to the Thai teachers, and to the lay supporters who made this monastic journey possible, declaring that the Buddha's path delivers exactly what it promises: unshakeable, indestructible peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Reflecting on entering the monastic order in 1966 and the miraculous flourishing of the Sangha in the West.
- The transformation of a restless, critical American seeker into a peaceful contemplative elder.
- Affirming that old age, sickness, and impending death hold no terror when resting in the Deathless (Amata).
- A final, tender blessing of mettā and gratitude to all companions on the holy path.
