### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the height of Cold War nuclear tensions in 1983, Luang Por Sumedho offers a profound and historic 89-minute reflection on peace. Surrounded by anti-nuclear protests, political paranoia, and existential dread of atomic annihilation, Luang Por reminds listeners that nuclear weapons are not the root problem—human hatred, greed, and fear are the root problem. Disarming missiles without disarming the human heart will never bring peace. Quoting Dhammapada 5: 'Hatred is never appeased by hatred; by love alone is hatred stilled.'

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychological roots of the nuclear arms race: paranoia, fear, and projection.
- Dhammapada 5: non-hatred as the eternal cosmic law.
- Why external political disarmament must be anchored in personal spiritual disarmament.
- Taking personal responsibility for not adding anger or hatred to a terrified world.
