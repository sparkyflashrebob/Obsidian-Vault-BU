### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating verified spiritual confidence (Saddhā) to overcome chronic self-doubt, timidity, and intellectual skepticism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the essential role of confidence (*saddhā / pasāda*) on the spiritual journey. Modern Westerners are conditioned by intellectual skepticism and psychological imposter syndrome, constantly feeling unworthy of enlightenment. Luang Por reminds the assembly that the Buddha's teachings were designed for flawed human beings. When we verify through our own experience that mindfulness brings real peace, confidence naturally blossoms into unshakable strength.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between blind dogmatic belief and verified experiential confidence (aveccappasāda).
- Overcoming the paralysis of chronic spiritual self-doubt (vicikicchā).
- The Five Spiritual Faculties (Indriya): Faith working in harmony with Wisdom.
- Stepping forward with noble monastic and lay dignity: trusting the Buddha within.
