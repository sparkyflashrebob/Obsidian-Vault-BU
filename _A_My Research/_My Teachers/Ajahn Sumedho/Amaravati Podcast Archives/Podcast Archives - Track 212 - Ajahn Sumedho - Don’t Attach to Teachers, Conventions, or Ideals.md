### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Raft Parable: releasing spiritual attachment even to exalted Buddhist traditions, monastic ideals, and holy dogmas.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through religious self-righteousness. Monastics and lay meditators often cling tenaciously to their tradition—believing Thai Forest Buddhism is the only pure lineage, or their monastic code is superior to all others. Luang Por invokes the Buddha's famous Raft Parable: the Dhamma is a vehicle to cross the flood of suffering, not a sacred monument to carry around on your head.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Raft Parable (Alagaddūpama Sutta): abandoning the raft upon reaching the farther shore.
- The danger of spiritual pride: looking down on other traditions and lay practitioners.
- Distinguishing between respecting traditions and clinging to them (sīlabbata-parāmāsa).
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that transcends Buddhist labels and cultural forms.
