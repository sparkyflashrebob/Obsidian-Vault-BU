### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Untangling the dense thicket of opinions, ideological arguments, and dogmatic views through the dispassionate sword of non-attachment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 43-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho tackles the human addiction to opinions. Quoting the Brahmajāla Sutta, where the Buddha enumerates sixty-two speculative philosophical views, Luang Por demonstrates that ideological debate is a tangled jungle (*diṭṭhigahana*) where humanity strangles itself. An opinion is merely a conditioned thought file. True wisdom does not consist of having the 'best' opinion; true wisdom is knowing that all opinions are empty saṅkhāras.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Brahmajāla Sutta: the 62 speculative nets of philosophical entanglement.
- Diṭṭhigahana: the thicket and wilderness of fixed opinions.
- Why human beings kill and wage war over abstract concepts.
- The freedom of non-attachment to views: resting in unconditioned knowing.
