### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning reflection on the subtle temptations of the contemplative life: spiritual pride, ascetic vanity, craving for solitude, and praise.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 53-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho explores the subtle temptations that ambush meditators on the spiritual path. Gross sensual temptations (wealth, hedonism) are easy to recognize. But the real dangers are subtle: the temptation of spiritual conceit, falling in love with deep meditative trances, self-righteous asceticism, and craving for disciples' praise. Luang Por outlines the ten armies of Māra and how to navigate them with unbending humility.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Padhāna Sutta: the ten armies of Māra arrayed against the Bodhisatta.
- Coarse worldly temptations versus subtle spiritual traps.
- The temptation of 'holy pride': looking down on non-meditators and laypeople.
- Using relentless self-honesty and Vinaya to stay protected on the path.
