### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Receptively feeling the raw somatic texture of life and starting fresh in the unburdened present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners to stop numbing themselves to experience and to bravely feel life as it is. We spend lifetimes dodging unpleasant sensations through distraction, workaholism, and mental commentary. By dropping mental resistance and feeling the raw somatic textures of warmth, cold, tension, and ease, we begin anew each moment with pristine clarity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why modern human beings are terrified of feeling their physical and emotional vulnerability.
- Mindfulness of feeling (vedanānupassanā): discerning pleasant, unpleasant, and neutral feeling without grasping.
- The practice of 'beginning anew'—dropping all baggage at the end of each breath.
- Opening the heart to the somatic richness of human embodiment.
