### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound 2021 inquiry into the nature of the knower: asking 'Who knows?' to dismantle the subtle illusion of an internal observer.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute discourse from August 2021, Luang Por Sumedho leads monastics into deep contemplative self-inquiry: 'Who knows?' While we easily recognize thoughts and sensations as objects, we intuitively assume there is a solid, enduring 'me' sitting in the center of the brain who is looking, hearing, and knowing. Luang Por shows that when we turn attention around to find the knower, no entity can be found. There is simply knowing—empty, luminous, uncreated, and free of self.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The inquiry into the knower: shifting from known objects to the nature of knowing.
- Dismantling the subtle conceit of the 'spiritual observer' or 'witness ego'.
- The Kevaṭṭa Sutta on consciousness that is signless, boundless, and luminous.
- Resting in selfless, unconditioned awareness (Buddho beyond personhood).
