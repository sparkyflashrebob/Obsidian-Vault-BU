### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating parting, endings, and saying farewell with an awakened, unentangled heart.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered as Luang Por Sumedho prepared for his retirement as abbot of Amaravati, this poignant 62-minute discourse addresses the art of saying goodbye. Human life is a continuous stream of arrivals and departures, hellos and farewells. Through awakened awareness, we can love and honor the Sangha and our friends fully without clinging to their physical presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The universal truth of separation (nānābhāva) and parting from all that we love.
- Distinguishing between cold, aloof detachment and warm, spacious non-attachment.
- Expressing heartfelt gratitude to the monastic and lay community for decades of shared spiritual life.
- Resting in the unconditioned refuge where no being is ever truly separated or lost.
