### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the eve of the year 2000, amidst global panic over the 'Y2K bug' and millennial apocalyptic hysteria, Luang Por Sumedho addresses an all-night peace vigil at Amaravati. He reminds listeners that the millennium is a human calendar convention, not an objective cosmic event. While the world frets over computer glitches and doomsday predictions, the awakened heart takes refuge in the timeless (*akāliko*) Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Millennium Myth**: Seeing how the human mind creates hysteria out of calendar numbers.
- **The Y2K Paranoia**: An amusing case study in collective mammalian fear.
- **The Timeless Sanctuary**: Stepping out of linear time into unconditioned wakefulness.
