### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Expanding gratitude beyond personal benefactors into a universal orientation toward every breath, element, and condition of life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Continuing his exploration of gratitude, Luang Por Sumedho deepens the contemplation into an unconditional orientation toward existence. Beyond feeling thankful when things go well, can we feel gratitude for difficult people, for winter cold, and for physical sickness that teaches us patience? Luang Por shows how universal gratitude (*katavedī*) melts the complaining ego, opening an inexhaustible spring of joy.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Moving from transactional gratitude to unconditional, non-dual appreciation.
- Using difficult personalities and physical ailments as supreme spiritual benefactors.
- The monastic virtue of Appicchatā (having few wishes) as the doorway to true peace.
- Recollecting the kindness of the four requisites: robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicine.
