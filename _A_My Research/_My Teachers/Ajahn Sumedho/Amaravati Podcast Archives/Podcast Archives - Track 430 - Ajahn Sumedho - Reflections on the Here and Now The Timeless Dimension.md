### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deep contemplative reflections on the present moment: liberating consciousness from the psychological tyranny of linear time.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this expansive 70-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the profound metaphysics of 'here and now'. We treat the present moment as a razor-thin transition between the past we regret and the future we fear. But Luang Por reveals that past and future are merely thoughts arising in the present. There is only ever Now. When we rest fully in the present, linear time collapses into timeless eternity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The illusion of psychological time: past as memory, future as anticipation.
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: cultivating auspicious presence in every posture.
- How anxiety and depression require psychological time to survive.
- The timeless reality (Akāliko) of awakened knowing.
