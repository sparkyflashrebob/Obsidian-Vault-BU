### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The subtle tyranny of spiritual idealism and the danger of weaponizing Buddhist doctrines against human reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho warns against the seductive trap of idealism. Seekers fall in love with romanticized ideals of the enlightened master, the pure forest monastery, and the perfect meditator. When mortal human beings fail to meet these impossible standards, idealism turns into bitter cynicism. Wisdom lies in honoring ideals without demanding that reality be other than it is.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychological mechanism of idealism: projecting abstract conceptual perfection onto messy reality.
- How idealism produces severe self-hatred when our meditation falls short of our textbook expectations.
- The wisdom of accepting human limitation while holding spiritual aspiration with kindness.
- Recognizing that Dhamma is discovered precisely in the imperfect, unpolished moments of daily life.
