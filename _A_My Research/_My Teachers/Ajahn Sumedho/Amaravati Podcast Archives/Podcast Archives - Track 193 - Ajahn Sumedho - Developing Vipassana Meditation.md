### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating Vipassanā (clear seeing insight) as a natural unfolding of mindful presence rather than a rigid technical exercise.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this comprehensive 62-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho demystifies Vipassanā meditation. Often taught as complex computational noting techniques or intense scanning protocols, Luang Por returns insight to its luminous, simple essence: seeing things as they actually are (yathā-bhūta-ñāṇa-dassana). By resting in quiet awareness, we directly witness that all phenomena—sensations, emotions, thoughts—are impermanent, unsatisfactory, and non-self.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The organic unity of Samatha (calm) and Vipassanā (insight) as two wings of one bird.
- Investigating the Three Characteristics (Tilakkhaṇa) in real time: Anicca, Dukkha, Anattā.
- Releasing the urge to manage, judge, or manipulate mental phenomena.
- The profound silence that opens when the mind ceases projecting a personal manager.
