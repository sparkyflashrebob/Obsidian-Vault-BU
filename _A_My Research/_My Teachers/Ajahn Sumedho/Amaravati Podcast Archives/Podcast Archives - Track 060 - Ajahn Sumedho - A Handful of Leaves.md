### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Buddha's essential teaching: prioritizing what directly leads to the cessation of suffering over speculative curiosity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Reflecting on the celebrated Siṁsapā Sutta, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Buddha's parable of holding a handful of leaves in a vast forest. What the Buddha knew was like the leaves in the entire forest, but what he taught was only the handful—because only this handful leads to dispassion, cessation, and liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The distinction between endless academic/scientific speculation and practical soteriological medicine.
- Why the Buddha consistently refused to answer speculative metaphysical riddles (the 14 unanswered questions).
- Focusing one's contemplative life entirely on the Four Noble Truths and the release of dukkha.
- Simplicity of the Forest Tradition: taking the handful of leaves and applying it directly to our daily suffering.
