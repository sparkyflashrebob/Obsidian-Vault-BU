### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 55-minute discourse delivered on Āsāḷhā Pūjā 2021, Luang Por Sumedho commemorates the historic anniversary of the Buddha's first sermon at the Deer Park in Sārnāth. Luang Por provides a masterful exposition of the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta, focusing on the three turnings (sacca-ñāṇa, kicca-ñāṇa, kata-ñāṇa) across each of the Four Noble Truths. He demonstrates that the Four Noble Truths are not abstract dogmatic propositions, but a practical scientific method for discovering unshakeable peace in this very life.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical setting of Āsāḷhā Pūjā: the Deer Park at Isipatana and the group of five ascetics.
- The Three Turnings and Twelve Modes (Tiparivaṭṭa Dvādasākāra) of the Four Noble Truths.
- The Middle Way (Majjhimā Paṭipadā) between sensory indulgence and self-mortification.
- The arising of the Dhamma Eye (Dhamma-cakkhu) in Añña Kondañña: 'Whatever is subject to origination is subject to cessation.'
