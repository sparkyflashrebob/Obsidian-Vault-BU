### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho speaks to a gathering of lay retreatants at Amaravati, answering the central question: 'How do we actually practice?' Lay practitioners often suffer from feeling spiritually inferior to monks, worrying that their busy family lives, jobs, and worldly obligations make enlightenment impossible. Luang Por dismantles this myth: Dhamma is not about monk versus lay; it is about mindfulness versus heedlessness. He offers four practical anchors for daily practice.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Overcoming the lay inferiority complex: Dhamma belongs to anyone who is awake.
- The four foundational postures: walking, sitting, standing, and lying down.
- Working with mental agitation, family worries, and work stress during sittings.
- Establishing an unshakeable five-minute daily mindfulness pause at home.
