### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extraordinary archival recording from April 20, 1978, a youthful Venerable Ajahn Sumedho speaks at the historic Hampstead Buddhist Vihara in London. Only a year after arriving in England with Venerable Ajahn Chah, Luang Por expounds on the timeless essence of Dhamma to a pioneering group of British seekers. Despite audio patina, his voice rings with prophetic clarity: the Dhamma is not an Eastern cultural import, but the universal human discovery of freedom from suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historic dawn of the Thai Forest Tradition in the West at Hampstead Vihara.
- Transplanting monastic Dhamma into urban London: damp basements, traffic noise, and walking almsround.
- The direct confrontation with suffering (Dukkha) as the starting point of wisdom.
- Ajahn Chah's core instruction: everything is uncertain (anicca); don't cling to anything.
