### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct contemplation of the Uncompounded (*Asaṅkhata*): the unborn, uncreated sanctuary that exists right here beyond all mental fabrication.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this profound 48-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the famous declaration from Udāna 8.3: 'There is, monks, an unborn, unbecome, unmade, uncompounded. If there were not that unborn, unbecome, unmade, uncompounded, there would be no escape from the born, become, made, compounded.' Luang Por guides meditators into direct realization of this uncompounded state: noticing the unmoving, uncreated awareness within which all compounded thoughts appear and cease.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Udāna 8.3: the philosophical foundation of Nibbāna as the uncompounded.
- The nature of compounded phenomena (saṅkhāras): formed, unstable, born, decaying.
- How to locate the uncompounded: resting as the knowing space, not the thoughts.
- Living in the uncompounded while gracefully operating in the conditioned world.
