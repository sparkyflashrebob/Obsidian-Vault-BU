### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Surrendering resistance to the natural river of conditionality: the contemplative meaning of entering the stream of Dhamma (Sotāpanna).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 49-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho explores the metaphor of the river of Dhamma (*dhammasota*). Most human suffering consists of swimming frantically upstream—fighting aging, fighting weather, fighting the behavior of others. Stream-entry (*sotāpatti*) is the profound moment of letting go of the oars and relaxing into the stream. When we surrender our personal resistance, the natural flow of Dhamma carries the heart directly to the ocean of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The metaphor of the stream: swimming upstream (craving) versus flowing downstream (wisdom).
- Sotāpanna (Stream-Enterer): abandoning the first three fetters through surrender.
- Relaxing into the flow of daily monastery duties and changing conditions.
- Trusting the Dhamma rather than personal ego manipulation.
