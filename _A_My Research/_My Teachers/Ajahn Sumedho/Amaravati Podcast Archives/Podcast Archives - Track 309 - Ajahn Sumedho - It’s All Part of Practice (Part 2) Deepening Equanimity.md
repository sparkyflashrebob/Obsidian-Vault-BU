### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deepening the 'all part of practice' paradigm into radical non-contention with all internal and external weather.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 66-minute continuation, Luang Por Sumedho explores radical non-contention. We suffer because we believe life should follow our personal script: we want constant good health, praise, agreeable companions, and smooth weather. Luang Por reminds us of the Eight Worldly Winds. When loss, blame, pain, and disrepute arrive, they are not errors in the universe; they are the natural landscape of saṁsāra. Meet them with an open heart: it's all part of practice.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Eight Worldly Winds as the ultimate testing ground for contemplative practice.
- Ending the lifelong war with reality: surrendering our childish demands on life.
- How to hold severe physical illness and aging within the 'all part of practice' framework.
- The unshakeable mountain of equanimity (upekkhā) that remains still in all winds.
