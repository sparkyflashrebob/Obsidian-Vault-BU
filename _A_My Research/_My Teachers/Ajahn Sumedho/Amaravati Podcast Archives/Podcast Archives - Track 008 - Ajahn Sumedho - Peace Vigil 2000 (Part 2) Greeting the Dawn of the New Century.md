The concluding dawn reflection of the Millennium Peace Vigil: meeting the new era with resolute loving-kindness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
As the first light of January 1, 2000 illuminates the temple, Luang Por Sumedho concludes the all-night vigil. Having sat through the night in unbroken mindfulness, the community greets the new century not with anxious anticipation, but with grounded resolve. Luang Por emphasizes that whatever the 21st century brings—technological revolutions, political upheavals, or environmental challenges—the Four Noble Truths remain our permanent compass.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Greeting the Dawn**: Meeting the new era with fresh, innocent presence.
- **The 21st Century Challenge**: Why technological power without wisdom is dangerous.
- **The Eternal Relevance of Dhamma**: Why the Buddha's truths will never become obsolete.
