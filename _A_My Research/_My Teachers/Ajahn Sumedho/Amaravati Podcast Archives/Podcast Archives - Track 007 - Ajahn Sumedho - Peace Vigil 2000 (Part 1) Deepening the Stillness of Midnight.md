### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Continuing the all-night millennium meditation vigil: investigating the stillness of midnight and resting in Buddho.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this short, sparkling midnight meditation reflection during the Peace Vigil 2000, Luang Por Sumedho leads the assembly into deep silence. As the clock passes midnight, the manufactured milestone evaporates, leaving only the pristine, timeless presence of awareness. Luang Por points directly to the unconditioned knowing that was here a thousand years ago and will be here a thousand years hence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Midnight Silence**: Tuning to the natural quiet when the outside world pauses.
- **Buddho as the Timeless Witness**: The knowing that never ages and never changes.
- **Aspiration for Universal Peace**: Dedicating the energy of the vigil to all beings.
