### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the third morning of retreat, the quiet mind often encounters deep emotional catharsis. In this 68-minute session, Luang Por Sumedho responds to meditators experiencing spontaneous tears, past grief, and intense energetic releases. Luang Por explains that tears on retreat are not setbacks; they are the unfreezing of emotionally armored hearts. By allowing grief to wash through without grasping a story, consciousness enters radiant samādhi.
