### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat Day 3 guidance on recursive awareness: recognizing that knowing is self-luminous and requires no personal manager.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
As the retreat enters its third day, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into the subtle practice of recursive awareness. Instead of focusing outward on objects (breath, pain, sound), we turn attention inward to recognize awareness itself. Awareness is aware of awareness. It requires no effort, no technique, and no ego-agent to sustain it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dropping the exhausting struggle of the 'meditator' trying to manage experience.
- Recognizing that awareness is already pure, luminous, and present right now.
- The realization that all thoughts, emotions, and physical sensations appear inside awareness.
- Resting in the unconditioned space of non-dual knowing.
