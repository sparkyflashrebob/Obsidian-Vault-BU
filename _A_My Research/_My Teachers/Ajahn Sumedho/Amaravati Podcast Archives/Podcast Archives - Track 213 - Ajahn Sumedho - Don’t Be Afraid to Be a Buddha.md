### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Overcoming spiritual timidity and stepping boldly into the natural dignity of awakened awareness (Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers an inspiring, electrifying winter retreat call to courage. Meditators often trap themselves in perpetual spiritual adolescence, believing that 'Buddha' is a mythical golden god from ancient India that an ordinary flawed person could never resemble. Luang Por reminds the Sangha that 'Buddha' simply means 'the one who is awake'. Stop hiding behind neuroses and unworthiness; have the audacity to be the Buddha right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The neurosis of spiritual unworthiness: identifying as a permanent sinner or victim.
- The true meaning of Buddho: the natural, awake quality of conscious knowing.
- Stepping beyond false modesty and cowardly hesitation in practice.
- Claiming your spiritual birthright: resting in the deathless here and now.
