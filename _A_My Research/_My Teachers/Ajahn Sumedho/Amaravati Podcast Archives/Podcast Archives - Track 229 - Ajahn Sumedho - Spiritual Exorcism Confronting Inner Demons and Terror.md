### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying inner ghosts, deep-seated terror, and psychiatric distress through Buddhist compassion and fearless presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this provocative, gripping 86-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the phenomenon of 'demons', supernatural terror, and psychological panic. In both Asian folklore and Western psychology, people are haunted by terrifying visions, paranoia, and oppressive mental states. Luang Por reveals that true 'exorcism' is not magical ritual, but the radical courage to face fear directly without running away. When you look the demon in the eye with Mettā, the demon dissolves into empty air.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Bhayabherava Sutta: the Buddha confronting fear and terror in dark charnel grounds.
- Deconstructing psychological 'demons': repressed guilt, trauma, and biological terror.
- Why resisting fear magnifies it into monstrous supernatural proportions.
- Using Mettā and radiant presence as the ultimate psychic protection.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recounts sitting alone in a haunted cremation ground in Thailand as a young monk, hearing footsteps and guttural breathing approach his mosquito net, sitting frozen in terror until he surrendered to death—only to discover the 'ghost' was a stray dog sniffing for food scraps.
