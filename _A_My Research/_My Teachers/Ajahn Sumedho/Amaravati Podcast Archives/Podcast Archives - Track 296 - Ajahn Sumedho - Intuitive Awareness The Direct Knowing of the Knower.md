### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Developing intuitive awareness (ñāṇa-dassana): cultivating direct, holistic knowing prior to the interference of discursive thinking.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 68-minute masterwork, Luang Por Sumedho explores the faculty of intuitive awareness. Modern education trains the linear, analytical intellect to dissect reality into pieces, leaving us paralyzed by doubt and endless pros-and-cons lists. Luang Por points out that intuitive awareness operates prior to thought: it registers conditions holistically, knows things as they are without words, and responds spontaneously with wisdom and compassion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between linear conceptual intellect and holistic intuitive awareness.
- The Bāhiya Sutta: in the seen, only the seen; in the known, only the known.
- How to access intuitive knowing: listening to the silence beneath the thinking mind.
- Trusting the unconditioned Knower (Buddho) to navigate complex life choices.
