### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The subtle tyranny of spiritual ideals and perfectionism contrasted with the living reality of human embodiment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates how high ideals—ideal monks, ideal teachers, ideal communities, and ideal enlightenment—often become brutal instruments of self-torture and cynicism. Ideals are conceptual creations; they don't have feelings. Wisdom lies in acknowledging ideals without demanding that reality conform to them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the phrase: 'Ideals don't feel; human beings feel.'
- How holding rigid romantic ideals about Buddhist monasteries leads inevitably to bitter disillusionment.
- Using ideals as inspiring compass bearings rather than rigid measuring sticks to beat ourselves with.
- Cultivating compassion for our own and others' inevitable human shortcomings.
