### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The self-luminous nature of the radiant mind (pabhassara citta) as the unconditioned light of reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this luminous 60-minute contemplation, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the Buddha's teaching on the radiant mind: 'Pabhassaramidaṁ bhikkhave cittaṁ' (Luminous, monks, is this mind, but it is defiled by visiting defilements). Consciousness is not dark, gloomy, or stained; its intrinsic nature is pure, shining light. Emotional defilements are merely temporary visiting guests.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the famous canonical teaching on the naturally luminous mind (pabhassara citta).
- Understanding defilements (kilesas) as passing visitors (āgantukā) rather than permanent personal flaws.
- How sunlight illuminates both beautiful flowers and rotten garbage without becoming tainted by either.
- Resting as the light of awareness that shines through joy, grief, health, and sickness.
