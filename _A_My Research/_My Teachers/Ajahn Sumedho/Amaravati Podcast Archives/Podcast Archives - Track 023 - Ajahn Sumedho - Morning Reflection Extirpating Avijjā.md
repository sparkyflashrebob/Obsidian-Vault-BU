### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The methodical eradication of fundamental ignorance (avijjā) through real-time mindfulness and Right View.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho examines the root of all existential delusion: *avijjā* (fundamental ignorance). Ignorance is not a permanent stain on the soul; it is simply the habit of overlooking reality in favor of mental projections. By bringing alert mindfulness to sensory contact (*phassa*), we sever the arising of craving (*taṇhā*) and extirpate ignorance at its very root.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mechanics of Avijjā**: How ignorance conditions mental formations (*saṅkhāra*).
- **Catching Dependent Origination in Real Time**: Severing the link between feeling and craving.
- **The Liberation of Vijjā**: Stepping into clear seeing (*yathābhūta-ñāṇadassana*).
