### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Directly observing the immediate painful fruit of grasping: learning through somatic feedback that attachment is suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through theoretical debates by pointing to direct somatic experience: 'See the result of attachment!' You don't need a PhD in Buddhist studies to understand suffering; you simply have to notice what happens in your body and mind the moment you grasp. Tightness in the throat, knotted belly, racing thoughts, panic—these are the immediate biological results of attachment. When we see this clearly, the hand naturally lets go of the hot coal.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The four types of clinging (Upādāna): sensuality, views, rules/rituals, self-doctrine.
- The immediate somatic signature of attachment: physical armoring and distress.
- Why intellectual understanding alone cannot stop clinging.
- Letting go as a natural reflex when the pain of holding is directly perceived.
