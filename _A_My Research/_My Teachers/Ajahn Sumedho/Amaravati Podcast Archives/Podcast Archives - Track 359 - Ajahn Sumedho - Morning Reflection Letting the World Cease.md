### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Buddha's teaching to Rohitassa: the end of the world is not reached by traveling across galaxies, but by realizing cessation within this body.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 40-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Rohitassa Sutta. The celestial deity Rohitassa possessed psychic speed and ran across cosmic systems trying to find the end of the world, dying before reaching it. The Buddha declared: 'In this very fathom-long body, with its perceptions and mind, I declare the world, the origin of the world, the cessation of the world, and the path to cessation.' Luang Por guides the Sangha to find the end of the world within.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Rohitassa Sutta: the futility of external wandering to find peace.
- Defining 'the world' (*loka*) in Buddhist psychology: the six sense bases and their objects.
- Cessation (*nirodha*): witnessing the vanishing of desire, thought, and selfhood.
- The profound stillness when the external world ceases to dictate internal reality.
