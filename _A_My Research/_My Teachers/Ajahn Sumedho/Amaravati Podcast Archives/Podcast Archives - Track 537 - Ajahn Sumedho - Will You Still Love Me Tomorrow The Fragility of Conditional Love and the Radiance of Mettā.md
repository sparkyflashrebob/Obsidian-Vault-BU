### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating human romantic longing, insecurity, and conditional attachment, and transcending them through unconditional Loving-Kindness (Mettā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 69-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho takes the title of the famous pop song—'Will You Still Love Me Tomorrow?'—to examine the universal ache of conditional human affection. Romantic love and worldly attachment are haunted by chronic anxiety: 'Will they change their mind? Will they leave me? Am I lovable?' Luang Por shows that conditional affection is rooted in craving and fear. In contrast, the Buddha's path cultivates Mettā—an unconditioned, boundless goodwill that demands nothing in return, radiating freely to all beings without fear of tomorrow.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The neurotic insecurity inherent in conditional worldly relationships.
- The distinction between romantic possessiveness (Pema/Rāga) and pure goodwill (Mettā).
- The Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta: cultivating a mother's heart toward all living creatures.
- Developing self-compassion: loving oneself without demanding perfection.
