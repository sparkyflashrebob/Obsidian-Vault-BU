### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transcending karmic conditioning: moving beyond good and bad karma into the unconditioned Dhamma of non-self (Anattā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this late-period discourse from 2021, Venerable Ajahn Sumedho cuts through the popular fascination with karma. While karma (intentional action) explains the conditioned patterns of body and personality, Dhamma is beyond karma. Good karma leads to pleasant rebirth; bad karma leads to pain. But Nibbāna is the cessation of karma—the realization of that which was never born, has no karmic debt, and is entirely free of self.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Understanding karma: intentional action and its conditioned fruits (vipāka).
- The fourth kind of karma: action that is neither dark nor bright, leading to the end of karma.
- Dhamma as the uncreated, unconditioned reality that knows karmic patterns without being stained.
- Resting in Anattā: realizing that past mistakes and personality flaws are not 'me' or 'mine'.
