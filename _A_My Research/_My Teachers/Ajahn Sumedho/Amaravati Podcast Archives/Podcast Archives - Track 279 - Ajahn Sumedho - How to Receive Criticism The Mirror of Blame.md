### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transforming insults, unfair criticism, and blame into supreme spiritual nourishment through non-reactivity and introspection.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this practical 55-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses how to handle criticism. Worldly people either erupt in defensive rage when criticized or collapse into shame and depression. Luang Por unpacks the Akkosa Sutta, where the Buddha compares an insult to an offered meal: if you do not accept the food, to whom does it belong? It remains with the giver! When criticism is valid, learn from it humbly; when invalid, let the words pass like wind through an open window.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Akkosa Sutta: refusing to accept the gift of anger.
- The Eight Worldly Winds: understanding blame as the inevitable counterpart of praise.
- Using criticism as an honest diagnostic mirror to uncover hidden blind spots.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that cannot be diminished by words.
