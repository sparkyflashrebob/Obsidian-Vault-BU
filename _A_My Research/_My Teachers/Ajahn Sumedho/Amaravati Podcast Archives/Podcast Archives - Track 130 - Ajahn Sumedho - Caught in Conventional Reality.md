### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How human consciousness becomes hypnotized and imprisoned by language, concepts, and societal games.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 73-minute deep exploration, Luang Por Sumedho investigates how human beings get caught in conventional reality. Words, clocks, money, titles, and social roles are useful practical agreements; but when we forget they are conventions and mistake them for ultimate reality, we enter a self-constructed prison of anxiety and competition.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The hypnotic power of nouns and language: turning dynamic natural processes into solid entities.
- How taking money, prestige, and institutional titles dead seriously creates existential dread.
- The Buddhist art of playing the conventional game skillfully without being fooled by it.
- Resting in the unnamable reality that exists prior to all words and conceptual descriptions.
