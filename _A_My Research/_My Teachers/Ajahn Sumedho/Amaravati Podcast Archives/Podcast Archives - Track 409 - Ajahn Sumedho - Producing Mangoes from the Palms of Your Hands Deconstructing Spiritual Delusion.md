### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Ajahn Chah's classic metaphor: deconstructing the absurd expectation of demanding unconditioned fruits from conditioned causes.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this lively and insightful 76-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks one of Ajahn Chah's most famous and humorous similes: expecting to produce mangoes from the palms of your hands. When people want lasting peace from marriage, wealth, status, or praise, they are like someone holding out their bare palm and demanding sweet ripe mangoes sprout instantly. Luang Por shows that if we want sweet fruit, we must plant the tree of Sīla, water it with Samādhi, and nurture it with Paññā.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Ajahn Chah's teaching: cause and effect versus magical spiritual thinking.
- Why wanting happiness without moral cultivation is demanding mangoes from your palm.
- The natural law of conditionality (idappaccayatā): fruits require proper soil and seasons.
- Patience (khanti) in meditation: allowing the spiritual garden to mature naturally.
