### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing social, family, and monastic archetypes: recognizing roles as temporary theater costumes rather than ultimate identity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this insightful 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho examines 'the roles we play'. From childhood to old age, we inhabit social roles: the son, the daughter, the husband, the wife, the professional, the rebel, the abbot, or the pious monk. Luang Por explains that roles are necessary conventional tools for social harmony, but suffering begins the moment we mistake the role for who we are. Awakening means taking off the costume and resting in pure awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Social roles as conventional theater masks (persona).
- The trap of the 'holy monk' role: spiritual vanity and public performance pressure.
- How to play conventional roles responsibly without taking them personally.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that has no role, no script, and no audience.
