### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the cyclic nature of time, saṁsāra, and resting in that which has no beginning and no end.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this expansive 74-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho investigates how the human intellect is obsessed with linear beginnings and endings—who started the universe, how will life end, and where do we go after death. The Buddha taught that in saṁsāra, no first beginning is discoverable; true liberation is stepping off the wheel of time into the timeless unconditioned (akāliko dhamma).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Buddha's Anamatagga teaching on the vast, beginningless wandering through saṁsāra.
- How the thinking mind creates linear time through memory (past) and expectation (future).
- The exhaustion of constantly starting new projects, relationships, and spiritual diets hoping for permanence.
- Resting in the Deathless (Amata) that was never born and will never die.
