### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the phrase 'I believe': how personal beliefs create mental prisons and divide humanity into warring camps.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during morning monastic reflection, Luang Por Sumedho investigates the psychology of belief. Notice how passionately people say: 'I believe this!' and 'I don't believe that!' Luang Por points out that belief is merely an opinion held by the intellect. People kill and go to war over beliefs, yet a belief is not reality. The Buddha did not ask us to believe him; he asked us to cultivate wakeful presence and see for ourselves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between dogmatic belief and direct experiential verification (*ehipassiko*).
- How beliefs become rigid identity armor that prevents genuine learning.
- The Canki Sutta: safeguarding the truth by acknowledging 'this is my opinion' rather than 'this alone is true'.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that transcends all belief systems.
