### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplative shadow work: meeting repressed anger, jealousy, shame, and terror with unshakeable Buddhist compassion and presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the difficult terrain of psychological shadow work. Religious practitioners often fall into 'spiritual bypassing'—using meditation to suppress unpleasant dark emotions like rage, lust, and shame behind a mask of serene holiness. Luang Por insists that true practice requires embracing the shadow. When we allow our darkest, most embarrassing thoughts into conscious awareness without judging them, their toxic charge is neutralized.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The trap of spiritual perfectionism and repression in monastic and lay practice.
- Welcoming the outcast: holding rage, petty jealousy, and terror in spacious Mettā.
- The Sabbāsava Sutta: defilements that are abandoned by enduring and by observing.
- Resting as the pristine mirror of awareness that reflects mud without becoming dirty.
