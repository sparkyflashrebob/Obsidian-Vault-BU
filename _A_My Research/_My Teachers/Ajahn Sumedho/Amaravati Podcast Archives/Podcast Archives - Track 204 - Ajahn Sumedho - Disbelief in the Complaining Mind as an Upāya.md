### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Using healthy skepticism toward one's own negative thought streams as a skillful means (upāya) for mental liberation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the deep winter retreat at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho offers a masterclass in Upāya (skillful means). When meditation sits get cold and physical energy drops, the mind launches into hyper-critical complaint. Luang Por presents a simple, revolutionary practice: simply refuse to believe the thoughts. You don't have to fight them or suppress them; just recognize them as unreliable conditioned noise.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Upāya (skillful means): pragmatic tools tailored to dismantle specific defilements.
- The Vitakkasaṇṭhāna Sutta: relaxing the thought-fabrication (saṅkhāra) by seeing its danger.
- Practicing absolute disbelief in negative self-narratives, remorse, and critical evaluations.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that remains unbothered by internal complaining.
