### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Exposing the Four Vipallāsas (perversions of perception) and returning to the pristine realization of things as they are.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the classical teaching of the Vipallāsas: the distortions of perception (saññā-vipallāsa), thought (citta-vipallāsa), and view (diṭṭhi-vipallāsa). Unawakened human beings routinely perceive the impermanent as permanent, the painful as pleasurable, the non-self as self, and the foul as beautiful. Luang Por demonstrates how mindfulness untangles these cognitive distortions, restoring direct realization of Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Four Perversions of Perception: mistaking anicca for nicca, dukkha for sukha, anattā for attā, asubha for subha.
- How media, advertising, and culture systematically exploit and reinforce these distortions.
- The process of papañca: mental proliferation turning simple sensory input into emotional drama.
- Right View (sammā-diṭṭhi) as the radical correction of perceptual distortion.
