### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The symbolic journey from 'Māravati' (the realm of death, temptation, and reactive delusion) to 'Amaravati' (the Deathless sanctuary of awakening).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this brilliant 45-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho plays on the name of the monastery: Amaravati. In Pāli, 'Amaravati' means 'abode of the deathless' (a-mara-vati). But Luang Por notes that before we awaken, we all live in 'Māravati'—the realm of Māra, characterized by fear, desire, conflict, and mortality. Luang Por charts the internal contemplative road that transports the practitioner from the prison of Māra to the boundless freedom of the Deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The symbolic geography: Māravati (realm of defilements) versus Amaravati (abode of the deathless).
- The four Māras in Buddhist doctrine: Khandha-māra, Kilesa-māra, Abhisaṅkhāra-māra, Maccu-māra.
- How Amaravati Buddhist Monastery was founded to be an external physical reflection of the Deathless.
- Taking refuge in the Deathless (Amata) within our own hearts.
