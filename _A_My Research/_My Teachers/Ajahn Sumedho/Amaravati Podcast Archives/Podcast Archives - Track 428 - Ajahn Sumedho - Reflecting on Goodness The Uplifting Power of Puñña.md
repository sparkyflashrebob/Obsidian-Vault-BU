### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The contemplative recollection of goodness, generosity, and virtue: countering modern cynicism and depression through joyful merit.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this authentic, unedited 45-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a pervasive modern malady: cynicism and self-deprecation. People dwell endlessly on their faults, trauma, and failures, completely ignoring their inherent goodness. In classical Buddhism, recollecting one's virtue (*sīlānussati*) and generosity (*cāgānussati*) is an essential meditative practice. Luang Por reminds us that taking joy in goodness gladdens the mind (*pāmojja*), creating the fertile soil for deep meditation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Buddhist concept of Puñña (spiritual merit / wholesome energy).
- Why modern Western culture confuses healthy recollection of virtue with arrogance.
- Cāgānussati: gladdening the heart through memories of selfless generosity.
- The causal ladder: Goodness -> Gladness -> Rapture -> Calm -> Samādhi.
