### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating memory (saññā) as an impermanent mental aggregate rather than an absolute historical identity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates the illusory nature of memory. Human beings spend lifetimes imprisoned by old memories of regret, trauma, nostalgia, and injustice. By seeing memory as a conditioned mental impression (saññā-khandha) arising in the present moment, we break its spell and step into timeless freedom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the classic teaching of the Bhaddekaratta Sutta: 'Do not chase after the past, do not place expectations on the future.'
- Recognizing that memories are not solid physical objects, but ephemeral thoughts occurring right now.
- Healing old childhood trauma not by endless psychological analysis, but by witnessing the emotional echo with compassion.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that was never tainted by past events.
