### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The contemplative art of listening to Dhamma: shifting from intellectual critique to direct somatic absorption of truth.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the spiritual mechanics of listening to a Dhamma talk (*dhammassavana*). In worldly education, lectures are evaluated analytically: agreeing, disagreeing, cataloging data, and critiquing style. Luang Por explains that a Dhamma talk is not academic information; it is a musical mirror designed to tune consciousness to the Unconditioned. By listening with an open, quiet heart, the listener's mind enters samādhi in real time.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The five blessings of listening to Dhamma (Dhammassavana Sutta): hearing what is new, clarifying what is known, dispelling doubts, straightening view, and calming the mind.
- Moving beyond intellectual critique to receptive auditory presence.
- A Dhamma talk as a spoken meditation: using the speaker's stillness to stabilize one's own.
- Resting in the unconditioned silence between spoken words.
