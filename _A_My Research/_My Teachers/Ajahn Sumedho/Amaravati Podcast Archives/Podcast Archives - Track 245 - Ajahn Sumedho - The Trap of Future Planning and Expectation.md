### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the chronic addiction to future anticipation, obsessive scheduling, and the anxiety of unmet expectations.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses one of the primary neuroses of modern society: obsessive future planning. We spend our lives preparing for a future that never arrives as imagined. When reality deviates from our meticulously crafted expectations, we erupt in anger or sink into depression. Luang Por teaches how to plan responsibly for practical needs without becoming emotionally entangled in the fantasy of future outcomes.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between practical functional planning and obsessive emotional anticipation.
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: 'Do not build hope on what has not yet come.'
- Why expectations are pre-packaged disappointments waiting to happen.
- The supreme freedom of showing up to the present moment without a script.
