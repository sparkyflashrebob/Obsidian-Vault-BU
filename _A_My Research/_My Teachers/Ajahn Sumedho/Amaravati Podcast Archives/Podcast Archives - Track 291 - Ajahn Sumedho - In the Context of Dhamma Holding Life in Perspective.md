### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Framing everyday emotional crises and worldly tragedies within the cosmic perspective of the Three Marks of Existence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this concentrated 28-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho shows how viewing life 'in the context of Dhamma' instantly neutralizes despair. When viewed through the narrow lens of ego, a broken marriage, lost job, or failing health looks like an absolute catastrophe. But when placed in the context of Dhamma—recognizing that all conditioned things are impermanent, unsatisfactory, and non-self—the drama shrinks and wisdom restores balance.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The tunnel vision of personal drama vs. the expansive context of Dhamma.
- The Dhammaniyāma Sutta: the fixed cosmic order that remains true regardless of personal feelings.
- How contextual perspective transforms victimhood into mature contemplation.
- Abiding in the unshakeable knowing that remains whole through every worldly storm.
