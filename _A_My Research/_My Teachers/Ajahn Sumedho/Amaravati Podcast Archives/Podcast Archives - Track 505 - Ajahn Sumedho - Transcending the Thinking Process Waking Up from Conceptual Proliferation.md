### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Recognizing the limitations of thought, stepping back from mental proliferation (papañca), and resting in the knowing space of silence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 62-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the path of transcending obsessive thinking. In modern culture, thought is glorified as the ultimate reality, trapping human beings in compulsive mental narration, anxiety, and judgment. Luang Por teaches that thoughts are merely conditioned objects arising and passing away within conscious awareness. By listening to the Nada sound (sound of silence) and observing thoughts without getting entangled, the mind discovers its natural, unconditioned peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The mechanics of papañca (conceptual proliferation) as analyzed in the Madhupiṇḍika Sutta.
- The distinction between intellectual understanding and intuitive awakening.
- The practice of 'thinking deliberately' to expose the empty nature of thoughts.
- Resting in the unconditioned sound of silence as a bridge beyond mental chatter.
