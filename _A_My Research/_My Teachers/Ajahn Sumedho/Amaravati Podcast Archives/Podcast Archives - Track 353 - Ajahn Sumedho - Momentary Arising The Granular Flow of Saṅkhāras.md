### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct microscopic observation of the momentary arising, persisting, and ceasing of physical and mental phenomena.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 41-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho takes meditators into direct microscopic observation of reality. Conventional thinking perceives solid objects: 'my body', 'my personality', 'the monastery building'. But when mindfulness sharpens, we discover that what seems solid is actually a rapid succession of momentary arisings and cessations. By seeing this momentary flow, clinging loses its foundation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The illusion of solid continuity (santati) created by rapid mental conceptualization.
- Direct observation of sensory flashes: sounds arising and vanishing in silence.
- Deconstructing physical pain: seeing an ache as tingling, vibrating micro-sensations.
- Resting in the unmoving space within which all momentary events flash.
