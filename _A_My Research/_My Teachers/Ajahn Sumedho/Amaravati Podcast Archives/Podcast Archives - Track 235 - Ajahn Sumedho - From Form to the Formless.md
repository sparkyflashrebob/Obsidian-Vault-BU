### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Progressive meditative refinement: transitioning from physical forms and boundaries into the boundless space of the formless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through the stages of meditative subtlety outlined in the Cūḷasuññata Sutta (The Lesser Discourse on Emptiness). Starting with coarse physical forms—the monastery hall, the forest, the body—mindfulness progressively steps back, recognizing the space around forms, and finally resting in the formless realms of infinite space, infinite consciousness, and emptiness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Cūḷasuññata Sutta: the progressive contemplation of emptiness.
- Moving attention from material objects to the space that accommodates them.
- Investigating the boundaries of the physical body: where does the body end and space begin?
- Resting in the unconditioned awareness that transcends both form and formlessness.
