### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme attribute of Dhamma: Sandiṭṭhiko—immediately apparent, directly visible here and now, verified for oneself.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho unpacks one of the primary chanted qualities of the Dhamma: *Sandiṭṭhiko*. Religion is often marketed as a future payoff—obey the rules now and you will go to heaven after you die. Luang Por emphasizes that the Buddha's Dhamma is immediate. When greed arises and you let it go, peace is realized *right now*. When anger is met with mindfulness, cooling occurs *right now*. Dhamma is verified here and now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The chanted formula: *Svākkhato Bhagavatā Dhammo, Sandiṭṭhiko, Akāliko, Ehipassiko...*
- Moving beyond religious promises of posthumous rewards into immediate verification.
- How recognizing craving and its cessation provides instantaneous real-time relief.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that exists right here, right now.
