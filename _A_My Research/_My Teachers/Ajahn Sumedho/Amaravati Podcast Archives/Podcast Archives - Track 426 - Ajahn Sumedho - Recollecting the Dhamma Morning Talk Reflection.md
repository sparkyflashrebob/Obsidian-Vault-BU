### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic contemplation on the six supreme qualities of the Dhamma: using Dhammānussati as an unshakeable inner refuge.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 48-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho leads the Sangha into deep contemplation of the Dhamma. Reciting the traditional Pāli formula—Svākkhāto, Sandiṭṭhiko, Akāliko, Ehipassiko, Opanayiko, Paccattaṃ veditabbo viññūhi—is not dogmatic worship. It is a precise contemplative checklist for verifying that the Dhamma is directly visible, timeless, and immediately accessible in the present moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The six attributes of the Dhamma unpacked in practical meditation.
- Dhammānussati as an immediate antidote to existential fear and insecurity.
- How theoretical scripture becomes living reality through personal verification.
- Resting in the timeless (Akāliko) nature of consciousness.
