### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Celebrating the Kathina robe offering at Chithurst—the sacred historical birthplace of the English Forest Sangha.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered at Cittaviveka (Chithurst Buddhist Monastery) during the annual Kathina festival, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the extraordinary journey of establishing the first forest monastery in Britain in 1979. He praises the enduring generosity of the lay community and highlights the symbiotic interdependence between the monastic order and society.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The canonical origin of the Kathina robe offering instituted by the Buddha for monks concluding the Vassa.
- Reminiscing on the austere, freezing early days of renovating Chithurst House with volunteer labor.
- The mutual support: monastics offer the gift of Dhamma and ethical inspiration; lay disciples offer food, shelter, and robes.
- The vibrant flourishing of Cittaviveka and the Hammer Wood sanctuary as enduring spiritual refuges.
