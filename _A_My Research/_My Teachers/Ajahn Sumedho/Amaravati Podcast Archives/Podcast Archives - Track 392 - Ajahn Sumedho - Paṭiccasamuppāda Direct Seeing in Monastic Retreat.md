### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Intensive winter retreat exploration of the twelve links: catching craving and clinging in real-time meditation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep 57-minute retreat talk, Luang Por Sumedho transitions Paṭiccasamuppāda from textbook diagrams into living meditation experience. In retreat silence, we can watch the links form in real time: a knee ache arises (contact and feeling); the mind instantly whispers 'I hate this' (craving); the hand tenses and posture shifts (clinging and becoming); frustration explodes (birth of suffering). By seeing this chain in real-time, the link shatters.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Watching Dependent Origination in the microcosm of a single meditation sitting.
- Catching the spark before it becomes a forest fire: mindfulness at the sense doors.
- The birth (*jāti*) of the ego: how every moment of clinging births a new suffering self.
- Resting in the unconditioned space where no links can connect.
