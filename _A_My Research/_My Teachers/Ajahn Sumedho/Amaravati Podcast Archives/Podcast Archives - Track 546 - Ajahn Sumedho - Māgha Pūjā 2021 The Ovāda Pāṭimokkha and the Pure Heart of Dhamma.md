### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 48-minute discourse delivered on Māgha Pūjā 2021—marking the final talk of the Amaravati Podcast Archives—Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the fundamental charter of the Buddhist monastic tradition: the Ovāda Pāṭimokkha. Commemorating the miraculous gathering of 1,250 enlightened monks without summons at Veluvana Bamboo Grove, Luang Por expounds the immortal verses spoken by the Buddha: 'To avoid all evil, to cultivate the good, to purify one's mind—this is the teaching of the Buddhas.' Luang Por concludes this monumental archival legacy with a tender, urgent reminder that the holy life is not an ancient legend, but a living, luminous reality waiting to be realized in every awake heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical miracle of Māgha Pūjā: the spontaneous gathering of 1,250 Ehi-bhikkhu Arahants.
- Exposition of the Ovāda Pāṭimokkha: non-violence, patient endurance (Khanti), and mind purification.
- The supreme declaration: 'Khantī paramaṃ tapo titikkhā' (Patient endurance is the highest austerity).
- Luang Por's culminating message: resting in the unconditioned knowing (Buddho) as our eternal home.
