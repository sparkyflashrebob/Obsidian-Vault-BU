### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Thorough contemplative exposition on the Three Turnings and Twelve Modes (Tiparivaṭṭaṁ Dvādasākāraṁ) of the Four Noble Truths.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 68-minute masterwork, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Four Noble Truths not as dry philosophical dogmas, but as a three-dimensional framework for direct liberation. He methodically guides the listener through the Three Turnings (sacca-ñāṇa, kicca-ñāṇa, kata-ñāṇa) across all four truths, showing how suffering is understood, craving let go of, cessation realized, and the path cultivated.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Three Turnings: understanding the statement, the duty/prescription, and the final realization.
- First Truth: Dukkha is to be fully understood, not fled from or suppressed.
- Second Truth: Craving (taṇhā) is to be abandoned by seeing its conditioned nature.
- Third Truth: Nirodha (cessation) is to be realized in the space of silence.
- Fourth Truth: The Eightfold Path is to be cultivated through continuous mindful presence.
