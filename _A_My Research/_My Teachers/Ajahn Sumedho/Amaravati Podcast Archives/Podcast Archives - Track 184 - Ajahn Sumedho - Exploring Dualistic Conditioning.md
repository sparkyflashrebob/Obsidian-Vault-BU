### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how language and conceptual polarity create the illusion of suffering and how non-dual awareness resolves conflict.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich Q&A dialogue, retreatants probe the mechanisms of mental division: good vs. bad, sacred vs. profane, self vs. other. Luang Por unpacks the Kaccānagotta Sutta, where the Buddha warns that the world largely depends on a dualism: existence and non-existence. By seeing the arising and cessation of conditions as they are with Right View, all dualistic battles subside into the peaceful stillness of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- How language inherently divides seamless reality into opposing polarities.
- The suffering of picking and choosing: craving the pleasant, resisting the unpleasant.
- The Middle Way: neither annihilationism nor eternalism, but seeing conditionality.
- Holding opposites in the non-reactive field of pure conscious awareness.
