### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Strengthening the three spiritual pillars: Saddhā (verified faith), Saṅkappa (Right Intention), and Adhiṭṭhāna (firm determination).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho inspires the monastic and lay assembly to cultivate the foundational triad of spiritual practice: Faith (*Saddhā*), Intention (*Saṅkappa*), and Commitment (*Adhiṭṭhāna*). Without faith, the mind remains cynical; without intention, energy wanders aimlessly; without commitment, practice collapses at the first hint of difficulty. Luang Por shows how these three faculties kindle an inexhaustible flame of wakeful vitality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Saddhā: verified confidence in the human potential for complete awakening.
- Sammā-saṅkappa: Right Intention oriented toward renunciation, harmlessness, and goodwill.
- Adhiṭṭhāna: firm spiritual determination that endures through dry spells and physical fatigue.
- Balancing the spiritual faculties (Indriya) to achieve unshakable stability.
