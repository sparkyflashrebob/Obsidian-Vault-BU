### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The harmonious synthesis of listening to the Sound of Silence (inner listening) and radiating universal loving-kindness (mettā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reveals the intimate synergy between the Sound of Silence and Mettā. When we listen inward to the high-pitched background sound of silence, discursive thinking falls quiet. In that spacious silence, the heart naturally softens. Mettā is not an effortful sentiment manufactured by thought; it is the natural warmth and unconditional friendliness of silent awareness itself.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Inner listening as the immediate gateway to mental quietude and non-interference.
- The natural marriage between wisdom (emptiness/silence) and compassion (mettā).
- Holding self-criticism, bodily aches, and difficult emotions in the warm field of silence.
- The Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta: radiating boundless goodwill to all realms without boundary.
