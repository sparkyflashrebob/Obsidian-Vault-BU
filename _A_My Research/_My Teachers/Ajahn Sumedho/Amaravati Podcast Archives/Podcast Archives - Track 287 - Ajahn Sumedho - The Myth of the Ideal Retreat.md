### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Shattering the romantic fantasy of the 'perfect retreat' and embracing the gritty reality of actual contemplative practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho dismantles the myth of the 'ideal retreat'. Meditators arrive with romantic daydreams: perfect silence, sweet incense, celestial bliss, and harmonious companions. Instead, they get coughing neighbors, squeaky floorboards, backaches, and internal mental chatter. Luang Por reminds practitioners that a retreat that frustrates your ego is far more valuable than one that feeds your spiritual fantasies.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The spiritual fantasy of an ideal hermitage vs. the gritty reality of human conditioning.
- How disruptions, noises, and irritations serve as high-grade meditation teachers.
- The Bhayabherava Sutta: the Buddha meditating in terrifying wilderness, not luxury spas.
- Resting as the unshakeable awareness that holds both pleasant and unpleasant conditions.
