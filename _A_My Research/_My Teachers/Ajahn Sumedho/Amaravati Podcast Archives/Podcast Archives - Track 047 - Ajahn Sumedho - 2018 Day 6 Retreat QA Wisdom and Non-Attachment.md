### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Advanced questions on anattā (not-self), subtle conceit, and the nature of conscious knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Retreatants inquire into the profound paradoxes of Buddhist philosophy: If there is no self, who is meditating? Who attains enlightenment? Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the doctrine of Anattā with pristine clarity, distinguishing between the delusion of a personal manager and the natural function of unconditioned knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Explaining Anattā not as a nihilistic dogma of annihilation, but as the liberating realization that nothing is 'me' or 'mine'.
- Clarifying the role of the 'knower' (Buddho): a conventional pointer to pure receptive awareness.
- Working with subtle arrogance and spiritual conceit as meditation deepens.
- Grounding high philosophical insights in simple kindness, patience, and humility.
