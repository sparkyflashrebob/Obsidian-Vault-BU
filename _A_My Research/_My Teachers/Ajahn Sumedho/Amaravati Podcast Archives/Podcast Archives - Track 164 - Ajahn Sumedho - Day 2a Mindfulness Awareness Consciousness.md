### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Clarifying the three foundational cognitive faculties: sensory consciousness (viññāṇa), awareness (sampajañña), and mindfulness (sati).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute masterclass from Day 2 of a 2020 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho provides pristine definitions for three terms often confused in spiritual literature: consciousness, awareness, and mindfulness. Consciousness registers sensory impressions; awareness is the receptive open space holding them; mindfulness is the reflective wisdom that remembers to let go of clinging.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Mapping the mind: distinguishing between viññāṇa, sampajañña, and sati.
- How sense consciousness can operate blindly under the influence of defilements without mindfulness.
- The reflective quality of Sati-Sampajañña as the steering wheel of the spiritual vehicle.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that integrates all three faculties into luminous presence.
