### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Guided somatic meditation sweeping receptive awareness through the anatomical structures and subtle energy fields of the body.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A practical 30-minute guided somatic contemplation. Luang Por Sumedho guides retreatants step-by-step through a methodical body sweeping meditation: feeling sensations from the crown of the head, softening the forehead and jaw, releasing tension in the throat and shoulders, opening the chest and abdomen, and grounding down to the soles of the feet.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Methodical somatic scanning from the crown of the head to the tips of the toes.
- Releasing accumulated emotional contractions held in the facial muscles and belly.
- Experiencing the body as elemental flow: earth (solidity), water (fluidity), fire (temperature), and air (motion).
- Resting in the unconditioned stillness that holds the physical organism.
