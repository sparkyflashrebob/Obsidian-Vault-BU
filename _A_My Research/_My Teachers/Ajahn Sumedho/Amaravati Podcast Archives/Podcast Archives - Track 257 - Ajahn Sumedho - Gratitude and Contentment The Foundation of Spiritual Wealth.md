### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The twin virtues of Kataññū-katavedī (gratitude and reciprocity) and Santuṭṭhi (contentment) as the supreme antidote to consumerist greed.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this warm, uplifting 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores gratitude (*kataññū*) and contentment (*santuṭṭhi*). Modern culture is organized around manufacturing artificial discontent—convincing us that we are inadequate and that happiness requires buying something new. Luang Por reveals that true wealth is not having what you want, but being content with what you have. When gratitude fills the heart, the fever of wanting subsides instantly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Buddha's teaching on the rarity of two people: the one who does a favor, and the one who is grateful.
- Practicing deep gratitude for parents, spiritual teachers, food, and the Sangha.
- Contentment (santuṭṭhi) as one of the supreme blessings in the Maṅgala Sutta.
- How gratitude transforms ordinary monastic chores into joyful acts of worship.
