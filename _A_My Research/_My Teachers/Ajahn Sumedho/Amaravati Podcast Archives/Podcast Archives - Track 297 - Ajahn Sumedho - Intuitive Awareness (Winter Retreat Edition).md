### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Winter retreat instructions on stabilizing intuitive awareness amidst physical discomfort, mental dullness, and emotional waves.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the deep silence of winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners on stabilizing intuitive awareness when practice gets tough. When knees ache, the hall is freezing, and the mind is dull or restless, the intellect wants to fight or flee. Luang Por instructs the assembly to simply register discomfort intuitively without building a story around it. The knowing of cold is not cold; the knowing of pain is not in pain.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Using winter retreat conditions to sharpen intuitive discernment.
- Separating bare sensory knowing from psychological commentary.
- The painless nature of the Knower: awareness cannot be bruised or frozen.
- Resting in unshakeable intuitive presence through long winter sittings.
