### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling guru worship: why spiritual teachers are finger-pointers, not ultimate refuges, and how to take refuge in your own awakened knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this refreshingly honest 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the perennial human tendency toward guru worship and teacher-dependency. Disciples often want an enlightened master to save them, validate them, or do the spiritual work on their behalf. Quoting the Buddha's dying words to Ānanda: 'Dwell with yourselves as an island, with yourselves as your refuge, with the Dhamma as your island.' Luang Por reminds everyone that even the greatest teacher is merely a signpost.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Attadīpa Sutta: 'Be an island unto yourself; take no external refuge.'
- The psychology of guru dependency: infantile longing for a perfect spiritual parent.
- Why great teachers like Ajahn Chah deliberately frustrated their disciples' dependencies.
- Taking refuge in the living Dhamma within your own heart.
