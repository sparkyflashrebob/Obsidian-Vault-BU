### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Shifting from effortful attention management to resting as pure receptive attention itself.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 51-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies a subtle contemplative distinction: instead of trying to force attention onto an object, we discover that we are the attention itself. When attention stops grasping outward at stimuli or collapsing inward into dullness, it rests in its own luminous nature.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The common mistake of treating meditation as a mental muscle to squeeze attention onto a spot.
- Relaxing the tension behind the eyes and in the forehead caused by forced focusing.
- Resting in receptive, panoramic attention that knows sound, breath, and posture simultaneously.
- Using the morning monastic silence to settle into this effortless abiding.
