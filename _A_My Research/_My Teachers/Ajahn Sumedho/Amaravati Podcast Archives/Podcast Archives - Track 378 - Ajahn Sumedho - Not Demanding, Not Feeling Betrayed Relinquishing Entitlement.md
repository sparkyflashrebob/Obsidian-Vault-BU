### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How subtle entitlement and demanding expectations set us up for heartbreak, and how letting go of demands heals relationships.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep 78-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho examines the psychological roots of feeling betrayed. We suffer feelings of betrayal because we unconsciously place impossible demands on other human beings—expecting them to be loyal, fair, consistent, and enlightened. When they inevitably fail our expectations, we feel betrayed. Luang Por shows that when we stop demanding perfection from a conditioned world, our hearts can never be betrayed.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The eight worldly winds (aṭṭha lokadhamma): gain and loss, praise and blame, fame and disrepute, pleasure and pain.
- The illusion of emotional entitlement: 'You owe me happiness and loyalty.'
- Why expecting conditioned beings to be unconditioned guarantees heartbreak.
- The beauty of relational Mettā: loving people without holding demands over them.
