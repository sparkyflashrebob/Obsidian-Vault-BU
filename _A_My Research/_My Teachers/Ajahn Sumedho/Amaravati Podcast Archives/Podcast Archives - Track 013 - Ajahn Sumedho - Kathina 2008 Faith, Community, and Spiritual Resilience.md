The grounding power of faith (saddhā) amidst global financial crises and economic upheaval.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in the autumn of 2008 as the global financial crisis was shaking the world's banking systems, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the annual Kathina gathering at Amaravati. While newspapers screamed about economic collapse and panic, Luang Por reminded the community of what cannot collapse: moral virtue, mutual generosity, and inner peace. True wealth is not numbers on a computer screen; it is a heart free from greed.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The 2008 Financial Meltdown**: Seeing how global greed creates economic devastation.
- **True Security in the Dhamma**: Taking refuge in what cannot be devalued or stolen.
- **The Resilience of Voluntary Simplicity**: How monastic living proves that human beings need very little to be happy.
