### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Ending the spiritual habit of geographical escapism and awakening directly inside the present human predicament.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the fantasy of spiritual escape. Meditators often believe enlightenment awaits them in an idyllic Himalayan cave or secluded Thai jungle, imagining that if they could only leave their family, job, or climate behind, suffering would end. Luang Por cites the Rohitassa Sutta: the end of the world cannot be reached by traveling. The end of the world is found right here in this fathom-long body equipped with mind.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Geographical escapism: running from relationship stress and emotional baggage.
- The Rohitassa Sutta: the celestial traveler who died wandering through galaxies searching for peace.
- Facing difficult emotions and boredom directly rather than fleeing into distraction.
- Awakening is not traveling to a new place; it is seeing the present place with new eyes.
