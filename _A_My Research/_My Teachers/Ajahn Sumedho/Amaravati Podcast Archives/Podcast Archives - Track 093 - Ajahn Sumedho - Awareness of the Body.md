### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mindfulness of the body (kāyagatāsati) as the foundational anchor for psychological stability and insight.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the somatic foundations of meditation. Human beings live largely in their heads—lost in a swirl of concepts, worries, and fantasies. By grounding awareness in the physical body—feeling the touch of the feet, the breath in the chest, and releasing knots in the belly—the mind regains natural equilibrium.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Buddha's praise of Kāyagatāsati as the supreme protector of the mind.
- Scanning and relaxing the areas of chronic tension: jaw, shoulders, throat, and solar plexus.
- Observing physical sensations (tingling, warmth, pressure) as bare elements (dhātu) without ownership.
- Using walking meditation (caṅkama) to integrate bodily awareness with movement.
