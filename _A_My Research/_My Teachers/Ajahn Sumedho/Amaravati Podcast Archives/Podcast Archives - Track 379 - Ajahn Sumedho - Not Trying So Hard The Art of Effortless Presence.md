### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Late-period Thailand discourse: relaxing obsessive spiritual ambition and resting into natural, effortless wakefulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this serene 32-minute reflection delivered during his later years at Wat Ratanawan, Luang Por Sumedho delivers an essential corrective for serious meditators: 'Stop trying so hard!' Western practitioners approach meditation with the same relentless work ethic they use in corporate careers—straining, forcing, and demanding results. Luang Por explains that meditation is not another job; it is the art of relaxing into awareness that is already present.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The spiritual ambition trap: trying to conquer the mind through sheer willpower.
- The difference between lazy indulgence and relaxed alert presence.
- Listening to the Sound of Silence as an effortless, non-straining anchor.
- The wisdom of elderly practice: letting go of spiritual heroics.
