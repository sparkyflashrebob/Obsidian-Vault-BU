### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Catching sudden flashes of unconditioned clarity (tathatā) amidst the routine busyness of daily life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this sparkling spring reflection from 2010, Luang Por Sumedho invites practitioners to notice the sudden 'flashes of reality' that occur throughout the day. When thinking briefly stops—between sentences, while drinking tea, or glancing at a flower—there is a sudden glimpse of reality as it is (*tathatā*). Meditation is simply learning to recognize and trust these spontaneous flashes of unconditioned presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Gap Between Thoughts**: Catching the silence that precedes mental commentary.
- **Tathatā in Plain Sight**: Experiencing 'suchness' without needing altered states.
- **Cultivating Familiarity**: Learning to abide longer in the space of knowing.
