### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Recursive awareness: recognizing that knowing is self-luminous and requires no secondary observer or ego.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A luminous, subtle 46-minute late-life teaching from 2020 on recursive awareness. Luang Por explains that awareness does not need an 'I' to make it aware; knowing is the innate quality of consciousness itself. By turning attention back upon awareness itself, all dualistic struggle between subject and object evaporates.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the subtle illusion of a 'watcher' standing behind awareness.
- Recognizing that awareness is naturally luminous (pabhassara) and unconditioned.
- The effortless peace of resting as awareness rather than trying to do awareness.
- The sound of silence (nāda) as a direct auditory reflection of self-luminous knowing.
