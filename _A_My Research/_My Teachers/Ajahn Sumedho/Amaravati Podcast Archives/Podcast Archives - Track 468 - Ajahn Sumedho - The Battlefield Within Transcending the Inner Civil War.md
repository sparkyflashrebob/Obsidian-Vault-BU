### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Conquering oneself: ending the internal civil war between the ideal spiritual self and the flawed human shadow.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 53-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks Dhammapada 103: 'Though one should conquer a thousand men in battle a thousand times, one who conquers oneself is truly the greatest conqueror.' Most meditators turn meditation into another war—fighting their thoughts, fighting their sexual desires, fighting their anger. Luang Por shows that self-conquest is not winning an inner civil war; it is declaring peace! True victory is embracing the entire battlefield in boundless compassion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dhammapada 103: the supreme victory of conquering one's own mind.
- The trap of spiritual self-aggression: trying to conquer defilements with anger.
- Declaring an inner ceasefire: making peace with the flawed human personality.
- Mettā as the ultimate weapon that disarms the inner enemy without bloodshed.
