### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Why the Dhamma is breathtakingly simple: cutting through scholarly jargon, complicated meditation systems, and resting in the present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho rescues meditation from over-complication. Human beings love complexity—we buy thick scholastic books, draw elaborate charts of mental states, and seek exotic multi-stage meditation systems. Luang Por reminds us that liberation is breathtakingly simple: it is Now. This breath, this step, this hearing, this knowing. Lay down the heavy pack of spiritual scholarship and enjoy the simplicity of Now.

- The intellectual addiction to complexity and academic jargon.
