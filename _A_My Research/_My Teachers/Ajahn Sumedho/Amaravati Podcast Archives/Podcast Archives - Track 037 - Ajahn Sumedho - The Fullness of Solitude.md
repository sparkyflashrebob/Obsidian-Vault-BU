### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Solitude (viveka) not as lonely isolation, but as the rich completeness of unentangled presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A poetic and deep reflection on the true meaning of solitude (kāya-viveka and citta-viveka). Luang Por explains that genuine solitude is not merely being physically alone in a forest hut; it is the freedom of the mind that no longer clings to companions, approval, or sensory stimulation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between painful loneliness (craving for external company) and sacred solitude (contentment in presence).
- The three types of seclusion: physical bodily seclusion, mental seclusion from defilements, and unconditioned seclusion.
- How modern entertainment, communication, and digital noise are designed to prevent us from ever facing ourselves.
- Discovering the richness of silence, where one feels connected to the entire universe without needing to grasp.
