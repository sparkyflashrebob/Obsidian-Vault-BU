### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical spiritual authenticity: dropping the pretense, perfectionism, and masks we hide behind.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this humorous and deeply liberating 54-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho uses the popular phrase 'coming out of the closet' in a universal spiritual sense: having the courage to step out of the psychological closet of pretending to be what we are not. We hide our insecurities, doubts, and human quirks behind respectable religious masks. True Dhamma practice begins when we dare to be honest with ourselves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The exhaustion of upholding a false persona of holiness, perfection, or unflappable calm.
- The psychological liberation of admitting: 'I am afraid, I am jealous, I am confused.'
- Why spiritual communities wither when members hide behind polite hypocrisy.
- Cultivating moral shame (hiri) and moral dread (ottappa) rooted in wisdom rather than neurotic guilt.
