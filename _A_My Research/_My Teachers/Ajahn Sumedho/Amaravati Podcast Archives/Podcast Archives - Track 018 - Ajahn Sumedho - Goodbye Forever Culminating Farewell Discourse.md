### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On 21 November 2010, Luang Por Sumedho delivers his historic final farewell sermon before an assembly of thousands of monastics and lay disciples at Amaravati. Stepping down after 33 years of building monasteries and guiding the Western Sangha, Luang Por bids 'Goodbye Forever' to his role as abbot. He leaves the community with one ultimate message: do not cling to his physical personality; trust the Dhamma, trust awareness, and take refuge in the Deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Final Farewell**: Formally handing over leadership to Ajahn Amaro and the resident Sangha.
- **Thirty-Three Years of Service**: Reflecting on the miracle of the English Sangha from 1977 to 2010.
- **The Supreme Bequest**: Leaving behind not material monuments, but the living practice of Buddho.
