### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Concise morning instructions for establishing the meditation anchor, relaxing physical tension, and settling into receptive stillness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this intimate 31-minute morning reflection delivered in the Amaravati Temple, Luang Por Sumedho guides the community in establishing an authentic meditation practice for the day ahead. He covers the physical posture, aligning the spine, softening the belly, and using the natural rhythm of breathing without forcing. Luang Por reminds practitioners that meditation is not a task to finish, but a way of resting awake.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Setting up the physical base: grounding into the earth and releasing facial tension.
- The natural breath: allowing the body to breathe itself without manipulation.
- Using the mantra 'Bud-dho' to synchronize breath with alert knowing.
- Opening the peripheral field of awareness to the Sound of Silence.
