### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning reflection on *Paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhi*—the Dhamma to be individually realized by the wise in their own hearts.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during morning reflection in the Temple, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the final chanted quality of the Dhamma: *Paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhi*. You can memorize the entire Tipitaka, write doctoral dissertations on Buddhist philosophy, and debate scholars, but none of that is 'knowing the Dhamma'. Knowing the Dhamma is when anger arises, and you know it without reacting; when pain arises, and you relax around it. Dhamma is verified personally within.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The chanted phrase: *Paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhi* (to be experienced individually by the wise).
- The radical difference between secondhand academic knowledge and firsthand realization.
- Why spiritual realization cannot be borrowed, inherited, or purchased.
- The simple, luminous presence of knowing (Buddho) available in every waking breath.
