### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound meditation on mortality (Maraṇānussati) and the realization of the Deathless (Amata-dhamma) right at the boundary of physical life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sacred intersection where death meets deathlessness. Everything that is born—this physical body, our personality, sensory faculties, and mental impressions—must inevitably die. By practicing mindfulness of death (maraṇānussati), we strip away superficial worldly vanity. Right at the threshold of acknowledging our absolute physical mortality, we discover that which cannot die: the unborn, unconditioned awareness that knows mortality without being subject to it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Mindfulness of death (Maraṇānussati) as the supreme antidote to heedlessness.
- Differentiating between the mortal body and the deathless space of knowing (Amata).
- How fear of death drives human neurosis, consumerism, and control.
- Resting in the unconditioned refuge that remains untouched when the body falls away.
