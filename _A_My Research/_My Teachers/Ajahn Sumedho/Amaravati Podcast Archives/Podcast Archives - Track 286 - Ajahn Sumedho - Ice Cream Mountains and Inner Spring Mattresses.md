### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A hilarious and profound critique of consumer culture's fantasy of a physical heaven of perpetual comfort and indulgence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho uses vivid, humorous imagery to satirize humanity's childish dream of paradise: 'Ice cream mountains and inner-spring mattresses!' Modern consumer society promises that if we could only surround ourselves with endless luxury, comfortable beds, gourmet food, and climate control, we would finally be happy. Luang Por reveals that sensory comfort without spiritual wisdom only breeds boredom, neurosis, and existential despair.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Sukhumāla Sutta: Prince Siddhattha's three luxurious palaces and the discovery that pleasure cannot prevent decay.
- The law of diminishing returns in sensual indulgence (kāmasukha).
- Why affluent societies suffer from the highest rates of depression and anxiety.
- Discovering the joy of renunciation (*nekkhammasukha*): lightness of heart without baggage.
