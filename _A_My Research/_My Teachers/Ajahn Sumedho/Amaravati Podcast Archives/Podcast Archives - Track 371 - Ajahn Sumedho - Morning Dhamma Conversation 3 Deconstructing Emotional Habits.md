### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat inquiry into latent emotional tendencies: bringing unconscious anger, defensive pride, and insecurity to light.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute morning retreat conversation, Luang Por Sumedho explores the subtle realm of underlying emotional tendencies (*anusaya*). Long retreats strip away external distractions, allowing deep-seated emotional patterns—irrational irritability, fear of inadequacy, hidden conceit—to bubble to the surface. Luang Por guides practitioners not to suppress these ugly emotions or analyze their childhood origins, but to hold them in spacious, non-judgmental awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The seven underlying tendencies (anusaya): sensual desire, irritation, views, doubt, conceit, craving for being, ignorance.
- Why intellectual analysis of psychological trauma cannot heal spiritual suffering.
- The somatic signature of defilements: feeling emotional contractions in the chest and belly.
- Allowing conditioned emotional energies to burn themselves out in the fire of awareness.
