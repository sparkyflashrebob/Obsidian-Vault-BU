### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this landmark 68-minute discourse celebrating the monumental 500th track milestone, Luang Por Sumedho provides a comprehensive synthesis of the three key mental faculties: Thinking (*vitakka*), Concentration (*samādhi*), and Wisdom (*paññā*). Meditators often treat thinking as an evil enemy to be destroyed and samādhi as the ultimate goal. Luang Por shows how each faculty plays an essential role: thinking investigates, concentration steadies attention, and wisdom liberates the heart into the Deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The proper role of conceptual thought: Yoniso-manasikāra (wise reflection) versus Papañca.
- The four developments of concentration in the Samādhi-bhāvanā Sutta.
- Why samādhi without paññā is merely temporary tranquility.
- The culmination of the three faculties: resting in the unconditioned knowing of Buddho.
