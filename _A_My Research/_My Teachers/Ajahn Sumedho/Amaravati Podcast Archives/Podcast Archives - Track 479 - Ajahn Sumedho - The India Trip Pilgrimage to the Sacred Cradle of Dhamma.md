### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Expansive 85-minute travelogue and spiritual reflection on pilgrimaging to Buddhist sacred sites across India: Lumbini, Bodh Gaya, Sarnath, and Kushinagar.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich and evocative 85-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on leading a monastic and lay pilgrimage to India. Drawing directly on the Mahāparinibbāna Sutta, where the Buddha urged disciples to visit the four places that inspire spiritual urgency (*saṃvega*), Luang Por shares vivid experiences at Lumbini (Birth), Bodh Gaya (Enlightenment), Sarnath (First Discourse), and Kushinagar (Parinibbāna). Amidst India's sensory chaos, poverty, and heat, the timeless peace of the Buddha shines with piercing brilliance.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The four sacred pilgrimage sites specified by the Buddha in the Mahāparinibbāna Sutta.
- The spiritual shock of India: confronting raw birth, disease, cremation fires, and beggars.
- Sitting beneath the Bodhi Tree at Bodh Gaya: connecting with the root of awakening.
- Turning outward physical pilgrimage into the inward journey toward the Deathless.
