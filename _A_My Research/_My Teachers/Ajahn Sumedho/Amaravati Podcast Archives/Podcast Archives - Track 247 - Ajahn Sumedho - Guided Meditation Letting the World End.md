### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound transcribed guided meditation on letting go of external responsibility, world concerns, and allowing the world to cease.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 54-minute guided sitting, Luang Por Sumedho invites meditators to practice the ultimate renunciation: 'Let the world end.' During meditation, there are no problems to solve, no wars to stop, no bills to pay, and no identity to maintain. Luang Por leads practitioners step by step into the cessation of the world (loka-nirodha) within the silence of consciousness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The conscious decision to put down all worldly roles and concerns at the door.
- The Rohitassa Sutta: realizing the cessation of the world in the fathom-long body.
- Allowing past and future to vanish into spacious present-moment awareness.
- Resting in the unconditioned silence where nothing is missing and nothing is needed.
