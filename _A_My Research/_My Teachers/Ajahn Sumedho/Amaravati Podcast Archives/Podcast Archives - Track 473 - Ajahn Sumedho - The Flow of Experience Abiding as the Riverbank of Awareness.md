### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Observing the continuous dynamic flow of experience without building dams of attachment or diving in headfirst.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 61-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the river of conscious experience. Thoughts, emotions, sights, sounds, and physical sensations are constantly flowing like a swift mountain river. Most humans make one of two mistakes: they jump into the torrent and get swept away by drama, or they build rigid mental dams trying to stop the flow. Luang Por teaches how to sit quietly on the riverbank: watching the water rush by without getting wet.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The river of experience (nadīsota): continuous flux and impermanence (Anicca).
- The mistake of grasping: trying to capture a bucket of river water and keep it still.
- Sitting on the riverbank: the contemplative posture of unentangled witnessing.
- Resting as the unmoving space through which all experience flows.
