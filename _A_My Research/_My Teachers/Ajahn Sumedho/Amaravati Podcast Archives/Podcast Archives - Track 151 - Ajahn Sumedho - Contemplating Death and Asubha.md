### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mindfulness of death (maraṇānussati) and contemplation of the thirty-two parts of the body (asubha) to sever physical vanity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this epic 102-minute masterclass, Luang Por Sumedho leads the assembly into one of the most powerful contemplations in Theravāda Buddhism: death and bodily unattractiveness (asubha). Modern society spends fortunes glamorizing and cosmeticizing the flesh. By contemplating the body as skin, bones, intestines, bile, and inevitable corpse, vanity collapses and consciousness awakens to the Deathless (Amata).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the 32 parts of the body (kesā, lomā, nakhā, dantā, taco...) as impersonal natural biology.
- Deconstructing physical vanity and sexual obsession by investigating beneath the surface skin.
- The practice of mindfulness of death (maraṇānussati) as the supreme antidote to laziness and heedlessness.
- Resting in the Deathless awareness that knows the body's decay without participating in it.
