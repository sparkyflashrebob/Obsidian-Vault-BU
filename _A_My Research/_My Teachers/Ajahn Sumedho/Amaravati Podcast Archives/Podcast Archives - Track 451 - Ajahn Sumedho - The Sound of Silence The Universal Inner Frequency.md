### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Comprehensive masterclass on tuning into the primal inner Sound of Silence (nāda sound) as an immediate, effortless anchor into unconditioned stillness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho delivers one of his most beloved core teachings: listening to the Sound of Silence. Neither an external acoustic noise nor physical ear damage, the nāda sound is the subtle energetic humming resonance of consciousness itself. Luang Por demonstrates how shifting attention from mental thoughts to this inner frequency instantly dissolves mental proliferation (papañca) and reveals the spacious unconditioned nature of mind.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- How to locate the Sound of Silence: listening into the space between words.
- The nāda sound as a non-sectarian, portable meditation anchor accessible anywhere.
- Using inner listening to cut through panic, anxiety, and obsessive thinking.
- The relationship between the Sound of Silence, emptiness (Suññatā), and Nibbāna.
