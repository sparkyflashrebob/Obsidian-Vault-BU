### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling sectarian conceit and spiritual elitism: freeing the heart from the seductive trap of believing 'our tradition is the best.'

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 51-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho confronts one of the most toxic traps in religious life: sectarian arrogance. Whether it is national patriotism, monastic elitism ('we are the authentic Forest Tradition'), or sectarian pride ('Theravāda is the original teaching'), the conceit of 'We Are the Best' (māna) is pure ego proliferation. Luang Por reminds monastics that conceit is an obstacle up to the stage of Arahantship. True Dhamma practice leads not to superiority, but to humble, spacious egolessness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The nine kinds of conceit (māna): superior, equal, or inferior.
- How religious communities co-opt spiritual practice into tribal chauvinism.
- Ajahn Chah's relentless testing and deflation of monastic pride.
- Cultivating humble openness, non-contention, and universal goodwill.
