### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme spiritual medicine of Anattā: stopping the exhausting habit of taking your body, emotions, and life circumstances personally.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Visiting Sharpham House in Devon, Luang Por Sumedho delivers one of his most beloved teachings: 'Don't take your life personally!' We suffer because we believe life is happening *to me*—that bad weather, difficult partners, bodily aches, and psychological moods are personal affronts. Luang Por shows that life is simply nature flowing according to causes and conditions. When you stop personalizing phenomena, peace is effortless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The root of Dukkha: personalized appropriation of impersonal natural phenomena.
- Investigating the five aggregates (khandhas) as nature, not 'me' or 'mine'.
- The Sharpham community setting: integrating contemplative freedom into lay rural life.
- Resting as the spacious witness who observes life without becoming a victim.
