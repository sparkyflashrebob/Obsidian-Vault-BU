### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the celestial realms (devaloka): exploring heavenly rebirth, blissful meditation states, and why even the highest heavens fall short of Nibbāna.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this evocative 59-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Buddhist cosmology of celestial heavens (*sagga*). In traditional teaching, virtuous living leads to rebirth in sublime celestial gardens of radiant beauty and sensual delight. Luang Por explains that while heavenly rebirth is real and praiseworthy, heavens are still conditioned and impermanent. Even devas must die when their good kamma is exhausted. True liberation is not ascending to heaven, but realizing the Deathless Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Buddhist cosmology: the sensual heavens, the Brahma realms, and their limitations.
- How deep meditative bliss mimics celestial realms within the human mind.
- The tragedy of celestial complacency: devas enjoying bliss until falling into lower realms.
- Nibbāna as the unconditioned reality transcending all thirty-one planes of existence.
