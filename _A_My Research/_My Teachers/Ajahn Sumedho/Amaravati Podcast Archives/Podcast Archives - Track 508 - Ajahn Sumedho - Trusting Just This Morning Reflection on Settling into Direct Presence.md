Morning retreat reflection on trusting the present moment as it is, without embellishment, expectation, or demand.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 74-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into the profound simplicity of 'Trusting Just This.' At the start of a retreat day, the mind typically rushes ahead with ambition, anxiety about sitting through long sessions, or complaints about cold weather and tired limbs. Luang Por gently points practitioners back to direct sensory reality: the breath entering the nostrils, the contact of the body with the floor, and the unblemished knowing that contains it all.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Morning meditation psychology: releasing anticipatory tension and mental projections.
- Somatic grounding: welcoming bodily aches and fatigue without resistance.
- The transformative power of the phrase 'Just This' as an anchor of mindfulness.
- Cultivating noble silence and spacious community harmony.
