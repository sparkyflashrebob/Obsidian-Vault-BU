### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Meeting severe physical and emotional pain from the unshakeable perspective of Buddha Mind (Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho provides master-level guidance on working with physical pain. Meditators routinely fight pain—tensing muscles, screaming internally with frustration, and spinning narratives of self-pity. Luang Por teaches how to enter the pain, separate the raw physical sensation from the mental aversion, and discover that Buddha Mind is completely untouched by the agony.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing physical pain into its elemental components: heat, throbbing, pressure, vibration.
- Recognizing how mental aversion (dosa) magnifies physical discomfort tenfold (The Second Arrow).
- The contemplative experiment: 'Can awareness itself feel pain?'
- Finding compassionate stability when facing chronic illness, severe headaches, or aging joints.
