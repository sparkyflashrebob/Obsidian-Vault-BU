### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deep relational peace: allowing human beings, physical conditions, and emotional moods to be what they are without demanding they be otherwise.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 79-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the root friction in human relationships: our relentless demand that people change. We demand that our parents be more loving, our partners more understanding, our children more compliant, and our colleagues more competent. Luang Por delivers a liberating truth: 'They are what they are!' Human beings act according to their conditioning. When we stop trying to remodel others, our hearts open in unconditional Mettā.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The relational lawsuit: demanding other conditioned beings become unconditioned.
- The law of conditionality: people behave according to their kamma and conditioning.
- How accepting others as they are paradoxically creates the safety for them to change.
- Resting in boundless equanimity and loving-kindness.
