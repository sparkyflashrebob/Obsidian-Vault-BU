### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Directing unwavering faith (saddhā) away from external doctrines into immediate, present-moment conscious awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this powerful 53-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho redefines religious faith (*saddhā*). Faith in Buddhism is not blind belief in historical myths, theological dogmas, or promises of heaven. Authentic faith is placing total, unshakeable trust in conscious awareness itself. Luang Por urges practitioners: 'Release your faith in this awareness!' When you trust the unconditioned knower rather than your neurotic thoughts, all fear dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Blind faith (amūlikā saddhā) versus grounded, verified faith (ākāravatī saddhā).
- Why placing faith in thoughts, political systems, or people always ends in betrayal.
- Awareness as the only infallible, inviolable refuge in existence.
- How resting in awareness effortlessly dissolves the hindrances.
