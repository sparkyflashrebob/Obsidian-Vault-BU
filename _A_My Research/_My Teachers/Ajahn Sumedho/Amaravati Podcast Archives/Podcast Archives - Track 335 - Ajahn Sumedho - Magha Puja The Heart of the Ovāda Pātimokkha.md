### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Commemorating Magha Puja: unpacking the fundamental charter of Buddhism delivered to the spontaneous gathering of 1,250 Arahants.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the full moon of Magha, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the famous Ovāda Pātimokkha given at the Bamboo Grove in Rājagaha: 'To avoid all evil, to cultivate good, to purify one's mind—this is the teaching of the Buddhas.' Luang Por explains that these three sentences summarize the entire spiritual journey from coarse behavioral discipline (sīla) to subtle heart purification (samādhi/paññā).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical miracle of Magha Puja: 1,250 enlightened monks assembling without prior summons.
- Sabba-pāpassa akaraṇaṃ: abstaining from harming self and others.
- Kusalassa upasampadā: actively developing wholesome qualities of generosity and kindness.
- Sacitta-pariyodapanaṃ: purifying consciousness of greed, aversion, and delusion.
