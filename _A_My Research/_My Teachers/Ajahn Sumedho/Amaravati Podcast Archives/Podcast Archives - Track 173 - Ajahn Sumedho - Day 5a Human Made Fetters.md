### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat Day 5 morning instruction: dismantling self-constructed psychological fetters, guilt, and dogmatic opinions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute retreat talk from 2020, Luang Por Sumedho investigates the subtle fetters that human beings manufacture to enslave themselves. We are not trapped by iron chains; we are bound by our cherished opinions, self-righteous judgments, lingering guilt, and attachment to rituals. By examining these fetters with clear seeing, we realize the cage doors have been unlocked all along.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Investigating the first three fetters: personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi), skeptical doubt (vicikicchā), and attachment to rules (sīlabbata-parāmāsa).
- How guilt over past failures becomes a chronic, self-torturing mental fetter.
- The freedom of dropping dogmatic opinions and political righteousness.
- Stepping through the open door of awareness into stream-entry (sotāpatti).
