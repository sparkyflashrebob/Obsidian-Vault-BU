### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic contemplation on Suchness (*Tathatā*): greeting the unfolding day with the peaceful recognition that 'it is like this'.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 43-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sublime teaching of *Tathatā*—Suchness, thusness, or 'just the way it is'. Human minds are chronically dissatisfied, constantly projecting how things ought to be. Luang Por reminds monastics as they begin their daily chores: reality is not an enemy to be conquered; reality is Suchness. Greeting every task, every ache, and every person with 'It's like this' brings immediate peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Tathatā as the unshakeable ground of Buddhist contemplation.
- The difference between passive resignation and active, wise acceptance.
- Using morning reflection to calibrate the mind to Suchness before duties begin.
- The Tathāgata: one who has realized Suchness and dwells beyond suffering.
