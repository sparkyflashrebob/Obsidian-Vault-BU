### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Māgha Pūjā reflection on the Ovāda-Pātimokkha: withdrawing our unconscious commitment to delusion and suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the auspicious full moon of Māgha Pūjā, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the Ovāda-Pātimokkha—the Buddha's fundamental ethical and meditative summary given to 1,250 enlightened monks. He examines how human beings make an unconscious, stubborn commitment to their own delusions: defending grievances, nursing grudges, and obsessing over self. Spiritual practice is withdrawing that commitment and dedicating the heart to purity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The three foundational verses of the Ovāda-Pātimokkha: non-harming, cultivating good, and purifying the mind.
- Examining how we commit to our suffering: replaying old arguments and nursing historical bitterness.
- The monastic virtue of patient endurance (khantī paramaṁ tapo titikkhā) as supreme purifier.
- Radiating the blessing of Māgha Pūjā to illuminate the darkness of worldly confusion.
