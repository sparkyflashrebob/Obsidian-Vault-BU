### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme culmination of the Buddha's charter: purifying the heart of greed, hatred, and delusion through non-attachment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 31-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the third and highest line of the Ovāda Pātimokkha: *Sacitta-pariyodapanaṃ*—the purification of one's own mind. While moral restraint (*sīla*) stops external harm and meditation (*samādhi*) steadies attention, true purification is the work of wisdom (*paññā*). Luang Por explains that purification does not mean scrubbing defilements with brute force; it means clearly seeing their impermanent, non-self nature so the heart ceases to grasp them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The threefold Buddhist charter: Sabbapāpassa akaraṇaṃ, kusalassa upasampadā, sacittapariyodapanaṃ.
- Why suppression is not purification: the danger of repressing shadow defilements.
- Purification through seeing: the transformative alchemy of bare awareness.
- The natural luminosity of the unburdened mind.
