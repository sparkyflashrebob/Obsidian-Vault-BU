### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme universal law of impermanence (anicca): how resting in the arising and ceasing of conditions ends suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho returns to Ajahn Chah's master teaching: 'It's uncertain. Everything that arises passes away.' We create suffering because we try to freeze pleasant moments into permanence and push away unpleasant moments. Luang Por reminds us that whether an experience is ecstatic bliss or excruciating grief, its nature is identical: it arises, changes, and ceases. When we allow things to come and go without interference, the mind abides in unshakeable peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Ajahn Chah's central instruction: *Mai nae* ('It's uncertain / not sure').
- The absurdity of arguing with the universal law of flux.
- Observing physical sensations, breath cycles, and moods as passing weather.
- The Unchanging Knower (Buddho) that witnesses all coming and going.
