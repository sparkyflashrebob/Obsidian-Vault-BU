### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Evening retreat discourse: pointing directly to the living texture of awakened awareness in the immediate present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of Day 5 of a 2017 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho offers a direct-pointing transmission of awakened presence. Awakening is not an exotic mountain peak to be conquered in the future; it is the natural quality of conscious presence right now: 'It's like this.' In the seen, there is just the seen; in the heard, just the heard; in the felt, just the felt. Resting here, there is no suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The direct pointing formula: 'It's like this' (Tathatā) as the ultimate liberating mantra.
- Unpacking the Bāhiya Sutta instruction: stopping the projection of 'me' into sensory experience.
- Observing the evening silence of the retreat hall as a living temple of peace.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that requires no further spiritual seeking.
