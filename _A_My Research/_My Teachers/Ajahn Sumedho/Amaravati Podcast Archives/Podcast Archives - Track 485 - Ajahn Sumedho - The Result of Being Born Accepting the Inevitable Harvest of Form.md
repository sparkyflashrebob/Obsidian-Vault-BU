### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The biological consequence of physical birth: understanding that aging, sickness, and death are not personal tragedies, but the natural results of being born.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the fundamental truth of biological existence: 'the result of being born'. In modern culture, we treat aging, illness, and physical decline as personal failures or medical anomalies that shouldn't be happening to us. Luang Por points out that whoever is born must age, must get sick, and must die. The only way to avoid the result of being born is to awaken to the Unborn (Asaṅkhata).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The causal link: Jāti (birth) inevitably conditions Jarā-maraṇa (aging and death).
- Why human beings take biological decay personally and feel betrayed by their bodies.
- Wise acceptance of the physical harvest: making peace with wrinkles, gray hair, and aches.
- Resting in the Unborn (Ajāta), Unoriginated (Abhūta) reality of Nibbāna.
