### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Practicing from intuitive heart wisdom rather than cold intellectual analysis: softening the inner critic with warmth and compassion.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this tender 48-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the common Western spiritual illness of living entirely in the head. Meditators analyze their hindrances like cold forensic scientists, creating emotional disconnection and self-judgment. Luang Por reminds us that Buddhist meditation is *citta-bhāvanā*—cultivation of the heart. True wisdom is not clever; it is warm, spacious, forgiving, and deeply kind.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The intellect versus the heart: why book knowledge cannot conquer emotional grief.
- Citta-bhāvanā: training the emotional and spiritual center of consciousness.
- Softening around physical and psychological pain with maternal tenderness.
- Resting in the unconditioned heart that has room for all human imperfections.
