### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Applying Buddhist teachings contextually as practical medicine rather than rigid, inflexible fundamentalist dogmas.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute discourse from December 2018, Luang Por Sumedho explores the contextual nature of the Buddha's teachings. The Buddha was not a dogmatic ideologue laying down eternal commandments; he was a master physician prescribing specific remedies for specific ailments. When we understand Dhamma contextually, practice becomes dynamic, compassionate, and wise.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why treating spiritual pointers as absolute dogmas causes sectarian arguments and spiritual stagnation.
- The Raft Simile: using teachings to cross over the river of suffering, not carrying the raft on our head.
- Balancing monastic form and cultural adaptability when establishing Dhamma in the West.
- Developing intuitive discernment (yoniso manasikāra) that knows what is skillful in each unique context.
