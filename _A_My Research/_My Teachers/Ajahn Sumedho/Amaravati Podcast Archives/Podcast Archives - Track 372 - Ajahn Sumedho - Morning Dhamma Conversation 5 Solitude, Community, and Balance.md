### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Balancing solitary kutī meditation with communal responsibilities, chanting, and kitchen work in a large monastery.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 82-minute morning dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a perennial monastic tension: the desire for solitary hermitage versus the demand of communal responsibilities. Many monastics yearn to lock themselves in their kutī and meditate all day, feeling irritated by monastery chores. Luang Por unpacks the Migajāla Sutta, where the Buddha defines true solitude not as physical isolation, but as freedom from craving. Communal work is where that freedom is embodied.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Migajāla Sutta: dwelling alone versus dwelling with a companion (craving).
- The danger of 'spiritual narcissism' when meditation is divorced from communal service.
- Transforming kitchen chores, sweeping, and administrative tasks into continuous bhāvanā.
- Harmonizing introverted contemplation with warm spiritual fellowship (kalyāṇa-mittatā).
