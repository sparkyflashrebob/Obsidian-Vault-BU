### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating conventional truth (sammuti-sacca) and ultimate reality (paramattha-sacca) with masterly balance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
An expansive 79-minute tour de force on Buddhist ontology. Luang Por Sumedho explores the two levels of truth: conventional truth (sammuti-sacca), which includes names, languages, social rules, monastic hierarchies, and money; and ultimate truth (paramattha-sacca), the unconditioned reality of things as they are. Wisdom uses convention without being fooled by it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between conventional reality (words, concepts, roles) and ultimate reality (bare sensation and knowing).
- The danger of 'spiritual anarchy': rejecting rules, manners, and ethics under the guise of high ultimate truth.
- How to honor conventions (monastic robes, bowing, passports) while remaining internally completely free.
- Resting in the unconditioned truth that transcends all linguistic and cultural boundaries.
