### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The living art of the Middle Way: navigating between indulgence and self-punishment with intuitive presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides a practical 43-minute guide to embodying the Middle Way (Majjhimā Paṭipadā). The Middle Way is not a rigid philosophical compromise or lukewarm moderation; it is the dynamic, sensitive awareness that steers clear of both sensual indulgence (kāmasukhallikānuyoga) and puritanical self-mortification (attakilamathānuyoga). It is resting in receptive knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why the human mind habitually swings like a pendulum between hedonism and harsh puritanism.
- The Middle Way as a somatic, intuitive balance: listening to the body's natural needs without greed.
- Using mindfulness of breath and posture to find the center of gravity.
- Resting in the unconditioned center where extreme views cannot take root.
