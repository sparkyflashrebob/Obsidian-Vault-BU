### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deepening experiential grounding in the present moment as the only invulnerable refuge against psychological anxiety.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 38-minute continuation, Luang Por Sumedho shows why the 'here and now' is not merely a philosophical concept, but the only invulnerable fortress of the human spirit. All suffering—regret, remorse, anxiety, fear of failure—requires mental projection into the non-existent past or future. When you pull attention fully into this immediate room, this immediate breath, this immediate sound of silence, anxiety is physically impossible.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The physical impossibility of anxiety in the naked present moment.
- How thoughts of past and future generate somatic contractions in the belly and chest.
- Using sensory anchors—feet on the floor, breath at the nostrils, inner sound—to stabilize.
- Abiding in the sanctuary of Buddho where no worldly harm can touch the mind.
