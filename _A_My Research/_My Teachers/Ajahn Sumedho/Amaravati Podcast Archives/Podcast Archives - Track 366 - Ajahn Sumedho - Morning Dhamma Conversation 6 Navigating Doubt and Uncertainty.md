### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Informal morning tea dialogue during winter retreat: working skillfully with doubt, skepticism, and intellectual confusion.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this lively and candid 51-minute morning retreat dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho sits with monastics and retreatants around the morning tea table. A practitioner raises deep doubts about whether enlightenment is really possible. Luang Por unpacks doubt (*vicikicchā*) not as a sign of spiritual failure, but as a mental state to be mindfully investigated. When we feel the contraction of doubt without believing its claims, doubt naturally evaporates.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The fifth hindrance: Vicikicchā (doubt and wavering indecision).
- Why intellectual skepticism cannot be resolved through more thinking or reading books.
- The Kālāma Sutta: knowing for oneself through direct experiential verification.
- Recognizing doubt as a passing weather front in the open sky of awareness.
