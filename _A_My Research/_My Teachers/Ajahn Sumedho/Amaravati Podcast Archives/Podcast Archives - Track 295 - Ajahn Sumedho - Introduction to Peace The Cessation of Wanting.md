### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A distilled, luminous 30-minute introduction to authentic Buddhist peace: not an emotional high, but the total cessation of wanting.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho cuts straight to the heart of what Buddhism means by 'peace'. In worldly culture, peace is thought of as an emotional high, a blissful state of relaxation, or an exciting spiritual trance. Luang Por clarifies that true peace (*santi*) is the cessation of wanting (*nirodha*). It is the unconditioned stillness that opens when craving, resistance, and grasping completely stop. Peace is always right here, waiting beneath our desires.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The radical definition of peace: the absence of disturbance, not the presence of a thrill.
- How wanting peace creates agitation and prevents the realization of stillness.
- The Third Noble Truth: Nirodha (cessation) as an immediately accessible reality.
- Resting in the unconditioned space of knowing prior to the arising of craving.
