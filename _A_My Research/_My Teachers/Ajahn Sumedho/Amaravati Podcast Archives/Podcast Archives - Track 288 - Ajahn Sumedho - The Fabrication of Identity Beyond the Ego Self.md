### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The deconstruction of identity: investigating how culture, language, and memory manufacture the illusion of a solid personality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a 59-minute investigation into identity. From the moment we are born, society stamps us with labels: a name, a gender, a nationality, a social class, a psychological diagnosis. We spend decades defending and promoting this composite brand. Luang Por shows how the five aggregates (khandhas) are completely impersonal natural phenomena. When we stop identifying with our labels, the immense burden of ego vanishes.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Sakkāya Sutta: identity view as the primary fetter binding beings to saṁsāra.
- How language forces reality into artificial subject-verb-object polarities.
- Deconstructing the five aggregates: form, feeling, perception, fabrications, consciousness.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that has no nationality, gender, or age.
