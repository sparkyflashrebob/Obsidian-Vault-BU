### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The art of non-judgmental awareness: recognizing that our moralistic, critical judgment is the primary source of the Second Arrow.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 50-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores 'the non-judgmental way'. The untrained mind is a chronic judging machine—measuring, comparing, criticizing, and finding fault with self, others, and the weather. Luang Por explains that non-judgmental awareness is not moral indifference; it is the radical spiritual capacity to register reality without the poisoning filter of personal aversion. Bare perception heals the mind's chronic wounds.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The judging mind as the primary manufacturer of the Second Arrow of Dukkha.
- Non-judgment versus moral apathy: clarity of discernment without malice.
- The Bāhiya Sutta standard: in the seen only the seen, in the judged only the judged.
- The immense relief and spacious freedom of retiring from the judge's bench.
