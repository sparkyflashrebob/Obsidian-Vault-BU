### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning reflection on weathering internal emotional typhoons: remaining rooted in the unshakeable eye of the hurricane through bare awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 55-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho uses the metaphor of an atmospheric storm to describe sudden emotional turbulence. Out of nowhere, the mind can be hit by a hurricane of rage, sorrow, or irrational panic. Immature meditators try to fight the storm or run from it. Luang Por shows how to enter the eye of the hurricane: resting in the unmoving knowing center where awareness remains perfectly still while the emotional winds roar harmlessly around it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The nature of emotional storms: sudden atmospheric changes in consciousness.
- The eye of the hurricane: discovering the unmoving knowing center (Buddho).
- Why arguing with emotional turbulence only fuels the storm.
- Somatic grounding: dropping awareness down into the feet and breath until the gale passes.
