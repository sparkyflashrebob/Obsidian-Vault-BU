### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Bringing awakened presence into afternoon activities, physical chores, and monastery maintenance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A grounded 46-minute afternoon reflection addressed to the resident monastic community and visiting guests. Luang Por reminds practitioners that meditation is not confined to the formal cushion in the temple; it must be brought into the afternoon work period—washing dishes, chopping vegetables, cleaning toilets, and speaking mindfully.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Overcoming the false split between 'holy meditation time' and 'unholy work time'.
- Practicing clear comprehension (sampajañña) in all four postures: walking, standing, sitting, and lying down.
- Using physical labor to burn up restless mental agitation and ground energy in the body.
- The communal beauty of work done as a selfless offering of service (veyyāvacca).
