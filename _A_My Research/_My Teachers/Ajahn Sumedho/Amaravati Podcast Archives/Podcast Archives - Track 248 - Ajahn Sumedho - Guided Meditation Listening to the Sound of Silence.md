### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho leads a landmark 54-minute guided meditation dedicated entirely to the Sound of Silence. He instructs practitioners how to gently turn auditory attention inward, attuning to the continuous high-pitched hum or cosmic vibration behind all sounds. He demonstrates how this sound cuts discursive thoughts instantly, providing an effortless anchor for calming the nervous system and touching Emptiness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Attuning the inner ear to the high-frequency background tone (nāda).
- Using the sound of silence to dissolve obsessive verbal thinking loops.
- Expanding auditory awareness to include both outer environmental sounds and inner stillness.
- Resting in the changeless knowing that listens to the sound.
