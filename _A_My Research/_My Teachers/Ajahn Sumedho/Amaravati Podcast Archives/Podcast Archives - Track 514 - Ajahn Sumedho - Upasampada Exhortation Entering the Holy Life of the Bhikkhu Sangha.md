### Theme of the Dharma Talk
High ordination ceremony address to newly ordained Bhikkhus Kassapo and Ñāṇadassano: the duties, protections, and noble calling of monastic life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 41-minute historical ordination address delivered at Chithurst (Talk 196), Luang Por Sumedho gives the traditional post-ordination exhortation to newly ordained Bhikkhus Kassapo and Ñāṇadassano. Luang Por reviews the four requisites (clothing, food, shelter, medicine), the four defeats (Pārājika), and the sacred commitment to living within the Dhamma-Vinaya. He reminds the new monks that ordination is not a promotion or social honor, but a total surrender of worldly ambition in order to realize the deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The traditional Upasampadā procedure and the heritage of the Theravāda Forest Lineage.
- The four resources (Nissaya) and four unmentionables/defeats (Pārājika).
- The Vinaya as a vehicle for mindfulness, restraint, and communal harmony.
- The relationship of devotion and obedience to spiritual teachers (Upajjhāya and Ācariya).
