### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Somatic investigation of chronic physical discomfort: separating raw feeling from psychological aversion.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 80-minute comprehensive meditation discourse, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through the crucible of physical pain. Pain is an unavoidable condition of owning a nervous system. The suffering is not the pain itself; it is the mental screaming, tension, and victimhood we add to the sensation. By relaxing around pain and observing it with dispassionate curiosity, pain loses its power to torment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Two Arrows: raw biological sensation (First Arrow) vs. mental resistance and storytelling (Second Arrow).
- Somatic softening: releasing contractions in the belly, jaw, and shoulders while feeling painful knees.
- Deconstructing the solid lump of pain into shifting currents of heat, tingling, and pressure.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that observes pain without experiencing agony.
