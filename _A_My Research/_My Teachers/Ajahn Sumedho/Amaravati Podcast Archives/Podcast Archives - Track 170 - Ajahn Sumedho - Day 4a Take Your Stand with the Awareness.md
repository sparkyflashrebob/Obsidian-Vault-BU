### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat Day 4 morning instruction: taking an uncompromising stand as the unshakeable witness amidst emotional storms.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 49-minute morning retreat discourse from 2020, Luang Por Sumedho exhorts practitioners to 'take their stand' with awareness. As retreat fatigue, emotional turbulence, and old memories arise, meditators habitually collapse into victimhood. Luang Por shows how taking an uncompromising stand as the witness (Buddho) provides an indestructible fortress that no emotional storm can conquer.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why mid-retreat emotional storms must be met with resolute presence rather than retreat.
- Taking our stand: deciding firmly not to be a victim of conditioned thoughts and moods.
- Using physical posture—upright spine, grounded pelvis—to support mental stability.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that observes the storm without participating in it.
