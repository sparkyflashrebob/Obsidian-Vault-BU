### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 45-minute Kaṭhina discourse, Luang Por Sumedho expresses heartfelt words of gratitude to the lay community and monastic Sangha following the annual three-month Rains Retreat (Vassa). Luang Por reflects on the ancient tradition established by the Buddha, where the lay community offers the robe of raw cloth (Kaṭhina) and essential requisites. He explains that Kaṭhina is not a commercial fundraiser; it is a celebration of pure generosity (Dāna), mutual respect, and the living reality of spiritual community.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical origin of the Kaṭhina ceremony in the Vinaya Piṭaka.
- The completion of the Vassa (three-month rains retreat) and monastic renewal.
- Gratitude (Kataññū-katavedī) as a supreme spiritual virtue and foundation of civilization.
- The symbiotic relationship between the lay stewards and the monastic Sangha.
