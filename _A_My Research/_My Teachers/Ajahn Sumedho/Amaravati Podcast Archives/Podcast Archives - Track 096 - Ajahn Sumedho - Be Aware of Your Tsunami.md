### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the devastating 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami as a mirror for natural impermanence and inner emotional tidal waves.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered shortly after the catastrophic 2004 Asian Tsunami, Luang Por Sumedho reflects with deep compassion and profound Dhamma insight on the tragedy. He uses the colossal natural wave to contemplate the fragility of human existence, the terrifying power of physical nature, and the psychological tsunamis of grief, anger, and panic that periodically sweep through the human heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Honoring the hundreds of thousands of lives lost in Southeast Asia with profound prayer and merit dedication.
- Understanding natural disasters not as divine punishment, but as the raw, indifferent forces of nature.
- Observing psychological tsunamis: overwhelming surges of grief, terror, or rage.
- Anchoring consciousness in the unshakeable bedrock of Dhamma that no tidal wave can ever wash away.
