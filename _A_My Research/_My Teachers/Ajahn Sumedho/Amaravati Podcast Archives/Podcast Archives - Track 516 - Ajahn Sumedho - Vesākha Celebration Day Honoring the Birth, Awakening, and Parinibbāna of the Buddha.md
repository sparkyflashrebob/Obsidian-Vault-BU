### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 51-minute discourse delivered on Vesākha Day 2021, Luang Por Sumedho commemorates the Triple Festival of Wesak: the Buddha's birth, supreme awakening, and final passing into Parinibbāna. Luang Por points out that Wesak is not merely a historical memorial service for an ancient Indian sage. Its true meaning is realized when we discover the Buddha within—the timeless, unconditioned awareness that knows suffering and its cessation in this very moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical significance of Vesākha in the Theravāda tradition.
- The three milestones: birth (human potential), enlightenment (truth realized), Parinibbāna (final peace).
- The practice of circumambulation (Padakkhiṇā) as an embodied meditation of reverence.
- Realizing Buddho: the inner knowing that is unblemished by worldly birth and death.
