### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 56-minute retreat Q&A session, Luang Por Sumedho answers deep questions on the Dhātuvibhaṅga Sutta. Beyond the four physical elements (earth, water, fire, air), the Buddha identified space (ākāsa) and consciousness (viññāṇa) as the subtlest elements. Luang Por explains how investigating consciousness as an impersonal element frees the mind from identifying as an ego.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The six canonical elements: paṭhavī, āpo, tejo, vāyo, ākāsa, and viññāṇa.
- Why identifying consciousness as 'my soul' is the most insidious delusion of all.
- Space as the element that contains all physical forms without being altered by them.
- Consciousness as the element that knows all phenomena without being tainted by them.
