### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The profound relief of halting psychological busyness: abiding in the unfabricated awareness where there is nothing to do.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this radical 56-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the ultimate monastic paradox: 'There is nothing to do.' The worldly mind is obsessed with doing, producing, improving, and fixing. Even meditators turn meditation into a frantic project of doing. Luang Por guides practitioners into the sublime stillness of non-doing: stepping out of the creator/doer identity and resting as bare, unmoving consciousness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The compulsion to do: how the ego equates non-doing with death.
- Kamma-nirodha: the cessation of intentional action driven by ignorance.
- The difference between physical monastery service and internal mental doing.
- Resting in the unconditioned ground where nothing needs to be added or removed.
