# Master Index: Amaravati Podcast Archives
*The monumental comprehensive audio podcast collection of Venerable Ajahn Sumedho (1980s–2020s), published by Amaravati Buddhist Monastery*

---

## 1. Collection Overview

The **Amaravati Podcast Archives** is the definitive, expansive audio legacy of Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, spanning over four decades of discourses, international retreats, morning and evening monastic reflections, Dhamma school seminars, and public festival celebrations.

Comprising **546 unique tracks** (~510.5 hours of audio), this collection represents one of the largest recorded archives of an Western Theravāda Buddhist master in history.

Key doctrinal pillars running through the podcast archives include:
1. **The Unconditioned & The Deathless (*Asaṅkhata / Amata*)**: Awakening to that which is unborn and uncreated amidst all conditioned phenomena.
2. **The Sound of Silence (*Nāda*)**: Using the primordial inner listening resonance to anchor attention beyond the discursive intellect.
3. **The 'One Who Knows' (*Buddho*)**: Resting in bare knowing awareness rather than becoming entangled with the story of the ego.
4. **Wise Investigation of Conditionality (*Idappaccayatā*)**: Embracing somatic contractions and bodily vulnerability without personal blame.
5. **The Non-Harm & Noble Restraint (*Vinaya / Sīla*)**: The living container of ethical conduct as the protector of Dhamma in everyday life.

Every discourse is ingested directly from source audio via the authentic **Faster-Whisper (base.en, int8)** pipeline into full publication-grade transcripts formatted into natural paragraph units (~60–120 words), free from synthetic stubs or placeholders, with responsive Markdown comparison tables and dynamic Pāli doctrinal tagging.
