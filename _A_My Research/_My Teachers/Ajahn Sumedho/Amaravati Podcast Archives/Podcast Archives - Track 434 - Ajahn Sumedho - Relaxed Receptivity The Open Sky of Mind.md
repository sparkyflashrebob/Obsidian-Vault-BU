### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating relaxed receptivity: shifting from aggressive doing to receptive listening, opening to the Sound of Silence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho guides meditators into 'relaxed receptivity'. Conventional spiritual practice is active and aggressive—seeking to attain, produce, or eliminate states. Relaxed receptivity, however, is feminine and welcoming: it allows reality to show itself. Luang Por demonstrates how tuning into the Sound of Silence naturally dissolves mental agitation and establishes effortless, receptive peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Active striving versus receptive presence: the shift from doing to being.
- The Sound of Silence (nāda sound) as the quintessential object of relaxed receptivity.
- How receptivity heals emotional armoring and psychological trauma.
- Abiding as the spacious sky within which thoughts, sounds, and pains pass freely.
