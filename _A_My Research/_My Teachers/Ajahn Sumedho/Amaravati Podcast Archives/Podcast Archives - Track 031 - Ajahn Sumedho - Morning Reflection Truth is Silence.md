### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The primordial silence behind all verbal proliferation as the direct doorway to ultimate truth.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A deep reflection on the limitations of language, concepts, and theological definitions. Luang Por explains that ultimate truth cannot be captured in words or dogma; it reveals itself in the silent, receptive space that remains when thoughts and conceptual debates subside.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between conventional conceptual truth (sammuti-sacca) and ultimate truth (paramattha-sacca).
- Using the inner sound of silence (nāda) as a meditation object that cuts through internal verbal narration.
- Recognizing that silence is not merely the absence of sound, but the receptive ground of all experience.
- Developing confidence in knowing without needing to formulate conceptual conclusions.
