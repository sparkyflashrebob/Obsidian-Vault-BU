### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The universal, objective reality of Dhamma: exploring how the laws of impermanence, suffering, and non-self exist eternally whether Buddhas appear or not.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this majestic 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Dhammaniyāma Sutta: 'Whether Tathāgatas arise or do not arise, there remains this established condition, this cosmic order: all saṅkhāras are impermanent, all saṅkhāras are unsatisfactory, all dhammas are non-self.' The Buddha did not invent the truth; he discovered the eternal law of reality. Luang Por shows how aligning our hearts with the Dhamma brings absolute, unshakeable harmony.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Dhammaniyāma Sutta: the objective, cosmic reality of the Three Characteristics.
- The difference between sectarian human religions and universal Dhamma.
- Dhamma as natural law: gravity, biology, and the mechanics of consciousness.
- Surrendering personal preference to align with cosmic truth.
