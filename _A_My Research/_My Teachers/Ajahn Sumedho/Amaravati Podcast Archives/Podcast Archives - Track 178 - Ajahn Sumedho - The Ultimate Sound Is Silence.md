### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Q&A exploration of the 'sound of silence' (nāda sound) as an immediate somatic and auditory gateway to the Unconditioned.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive Q&A session, retreatants ask Luang Por about working with internal and external sensory phenomena, leading to an in-depth dialogue on the Sound of Silence. Luang Por explains that this background cosmic hum or high-pitched vibration is not physical tinnitus, but an auditory manifestation of awareness itself. Listening to the sound of silence cuts through mental chatter and centers attention instantly in the present moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between physical ear ringing and the contemplative inner sound (nāda).
- Using the sound of silence as an effortless anchor during walking, sitting, and daily work.
- How attending to background silence dissolves foreground obsessive thought spirals.
- Integrating the sound of silence into contemplation of emptiness (suññatā) and non-self.
