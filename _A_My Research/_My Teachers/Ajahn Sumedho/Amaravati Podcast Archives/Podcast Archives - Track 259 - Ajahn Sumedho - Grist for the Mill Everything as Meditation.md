### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Ajahn Chah's master axiom: using all life's crises, annoyances, and interruptions as high-grade meditation fuel.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho unpacks one of the Thai Forest Tradition's most beloved metaphors: 'Grist for the mill.' An industrial water mill grinds wheat, barley, corn, and rye without complaining; whatever goes into the hopper is transformed into flour. In the same way, the awakened mind does not demand only sweet experiences. Traffic jams, boiler breakdowns, rude words, and physical illness are all grist for the meditative mill of awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Ajahn Chah's teaching: no experience is wasted if met with mindfulness.
- Ending the childish fantasy of an ideal, uninterrupted meditation hermitage.
- How daily monastery chores and domestic crises sharpen spiritual discernment.
- Resting as the unshakeable mill of Buddho that grinds all conditions into wisdom.
