### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The timeless, immediately visible Dhamma: why the past and future are pure illusions and life exists only in the present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a 70-minute master exposition on the Bhaddekaratta Sutta ('A Single Excellent Night'). The human mind lives almost entirely in time—regretting past mistakes or rehearsing future possibilities. Luang Por points out that all memories and all anticipations occur exclusively in the present moment. There is nowhere else to awaken except right now. By stepping out of the timeline, we enter the timeless (akāliko).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: 'Do not pursue the past, do not build hope on the future.'
- The tyranny of time: how psychological time manufactures worry, regret, and depression.
- Recognizing memory as an image appearing right now in consciousness.
- The Sandiṭṭhika (apparent here and now) and Akāliko (timeless) nature of Dhamma.
