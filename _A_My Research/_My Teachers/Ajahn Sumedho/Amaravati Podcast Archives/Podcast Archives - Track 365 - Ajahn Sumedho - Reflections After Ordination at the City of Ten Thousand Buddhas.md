In this historic 51-minute discourse delivered at the City of Ten Thousand Buddhas in California, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the groundbreaking ordination ceremonies conducted with Venerable Master Hsuan Hua. Standing before a massive assembly of Mahāyāna and Theravāda monastics, Luang Por celebrates the fundamental unity of the Sangha. Regardless of northern or southern traditions, all monks and nuns share the common heritage of the Vinaya and the singular quest to end suffering.
