### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The spiritual power of selfless service (veyyāvacca): cleaning toilets, cooking, and administrative labor as profound tools of ego-dissolution.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this practical and inspiring 59-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the spiritual practice of serving the Sangha (*veyyāvacca*). In Western individualism, work is viewed as a burden to be avoided, while meditation is viewed as 'real' practice. Luang Por invokes the Venerable Ānanda, whose total selfless service to the Buddha was his direct vehicle to awakening. Serving the monastic community breaks the backbone of self-importance and transforms mundane labor into pure worship.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Veyyāvacca (selfless service) as one of the ten meritorious deeds (puññakiriya-vatthu).
- The Venerable Ānanda as the supreme exemplar of joyful spiritual service.
- Cleaning toilets, washing dishes, and sweeping as profound meditations on non-self.
- How communal service protects the meditator from spiritual narcissism.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls being assigned by Ajahn Chah to clean the spittoons and toilets of the senior monks, feeling initial fury at doing such filthy work, until he realized that the fury was his defilement and cleaning toilets washed his soul clean.
