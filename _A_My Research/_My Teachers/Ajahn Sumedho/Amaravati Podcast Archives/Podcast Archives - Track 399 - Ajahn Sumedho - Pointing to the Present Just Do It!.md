### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cutting through endless theoretical postponement: establishing immediate presence without hesitation or procrastination.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this crisp and lively 33-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through spiritual procrastination: 'Just do it!' We read hundreds of books about meditation, attend seminars, and plan to meditate when we retire, when the weather improves, or when our schedule clears up. Luang Por reminds us that mindfulness requires no preparation, no special equipment, and no future time. Just be present right here and now!

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Spiritual procrastination: how the intellect uses 'preparation' to avoid presence.
- The simplicity of mindfulness: being aware of the body sitting here right now.
- Why waiting for ideal conditions ensures one will never awaken.
- The Dhamma as Ehipassiko: inviting direct, immediate investigation.
