### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A mature Buddhist inquiry into romantic craving, sexual desire, celibacy (brahmacariya), and pure unconditional affection.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this candid and compassionate 38-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho addresses romantic love and sexuality from a monastic and Dhamma perspective. Rather than adopting puritanical repression or hedonistic indulgence, Buddhism approaches sexual desire as a powerful natural biological impulse that creates intense attachment and vulnerability. Luang Por explores how monks practice celibacy to conserve energy for spiritual awakening, and how laypeople can transform possessive passion into respectful spiritual partnership.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Buddhist middle way: neither repression of sex nor blind indulgence.
- The biological drive of kāma-taṇhā: understanding its evolutionary purpose without being enslaved by it.
- Brahmacariya (holy life / celibacy) as a voluntary tool for inner tranquility.
- Transforming possessive romantic grasping into unconditioned Mettā and mutual respect.
