### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The famous metaphor of eating cookbooks instead of tasting food: why academic Buddhist study without practice leaves the heart starved.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this delightful, razor-sharp 48-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho uses one of his signature analogies: eating cookbooks. You can read a cookbook for fifty years, memorize every gourmet recipe, and analyze the chemistry of spices, but if you never cook and eat, you will die of starvation! In the same way, reading Buddhist scriptures without practicing mindfulness leaves the soul completely starved of peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The addiction to spiritual reading as a sophisticated defense against meditation.
- Dhammapada verse 19: the cowherd counting another's cattle without tasting the milk.
- How intellect creates the illusion of attainment while defilements remain untouched.
- Putting down the book and tasting the actual flavor of the present moment.
