### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A practical 41-minute transcribed guided sitting investigating the Three Characteristics (Anicca, Dukkha, Anattā) across body and mind.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this accessible, deeply rooted guided meditation, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through the direct contemplation of Vipassanā (insight). Beginning with physical sensations—pulses of tingling, pressure, temperature—he guides attention to notice that no physical sensation remains static even for a second (Anicca). He then expands awareness to mental moods and thoughts, revealing that whatever is impermanent is not-self (Anattā).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Posture and physical alignment: grounding the sit-bones, lifting the crown.
- Scanning physical sensations with dispassionate, gentle curiosity.
- Direct observation of Anicca: watching sensations arise, peak, and dissolve.
- Timed pauses and spacious silent prompts for sustained inquiry.
