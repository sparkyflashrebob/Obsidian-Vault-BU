### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contentment (santuṭṭhi) as the supreme spiritual wealth in a world consumed by insatiable craving.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 71-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the famous Dhammapada verse: 'Santuṭṭhi paramaṁ dhanaṁ' (Contentment is the supreme wealth). Modern consumerism is engineered to make human beings feel permanently dissatisfied, ugly, and lacking. Cultivating contentment is a radical spiritual antidote that instantly heals the disease of wanting more, revealing the inexhaustible wealth of an unclenched heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Analyzing how commercial marketing systematically destroys human contentment to drive consumer spending.
- Defining Santuṭṭhi: being satisfied with what one has, where one is, and who one is right now.
- The difference between lazy apathy and dynamic, peaceful contentment.
- Discovering that true wealth is not having immense possessions, but having very few desires.
