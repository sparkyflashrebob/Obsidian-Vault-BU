### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplative presence during the English monsoon, resting as receptive awareness without preference.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the Rains Retreat (Vassa) amidst a steady downpour, Luang Por uses the sound and visual beauty of falling rain to teach choiceless awareness. When we stop demanding that weather, health, or emotions be other than they are, the entire world reveals the Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the elemental interplay of water, earth, and wind as impersonal natural phenomena.
- Observing the mind's perpetual preference for sunshine over rain, comfort over discomfort, and pleasure over pain.
- Allowing the continuous sound of rain to absorb discursive thinking into natural tranquility.
- Resting in the unconditioned presence that remains dry and unaffected no matter how heavily the rain falls.
