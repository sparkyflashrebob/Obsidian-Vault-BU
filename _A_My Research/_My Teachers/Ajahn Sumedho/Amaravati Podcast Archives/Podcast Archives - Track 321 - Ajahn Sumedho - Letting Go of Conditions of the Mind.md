### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The art of non-clinging (cāga): releasing psychological identification with passing thoughts, emotional moods, and mental conditions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners through the mechanics of letting go (*cāga* / *vossagga*). People often treat 'letting go' as an aggressive mental act, trying to throw thoughts away or violently eject defilements. Luang Por clarifies that true letting go is completely passive: it is simply ceasing to pick things up! When we stop grabbing passing thoughts, they naturally cease on their own.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between violent suppression and natural release (cāga).
- Why thoughts and emotions have no power to torment unless we appropriate them.
- The Sabbāsava Sutta: defilements abandoned by avoiding, enduring, and seeing.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that remains clean and clear through all conditions.
