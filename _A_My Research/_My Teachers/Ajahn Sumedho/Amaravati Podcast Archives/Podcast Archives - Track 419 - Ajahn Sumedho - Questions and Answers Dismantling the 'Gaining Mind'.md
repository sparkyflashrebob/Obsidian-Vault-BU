### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Targeted Q&A session investigating 'gaining mind': how spiritual ambition and desire for attainments subtly sabotage meditation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 51-minute dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho explores the subtle trap of 'gaining mind' (*gaining idea*). A meditator asks why their practice feels stagnant despite strenuous effort. Luang Por reveals that the very effort to *gain* enlightenment is the primary obstacle. In meditation, whenever there is an 'I' trying to get a 'state', duality and craving are reinforced. True awakening occurs when the gaining mind is completely abandoned.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Defining the 'gaining mind': the subtle spiritual ambition that corrupts practice.
- The Zen critique of attainment: 'No gaining idea.'
- Why wanting peace creates restlessness; why wanting stillness creates agitation.
- The relief of non-attainment: resting as awareness that has nothing to prove or gain.
