### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Nurturing Saddhā (wise spiritual faith) balanced with Paññā (discerning wisdom) to anchor the mind in the Triple Gem.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the essential role of Saddhā (faith/confidence). In Western intellectual circles, faith is often equated with blind dogmatic belief. Luang Por clarifies that Buddhist faith is verified confidence: an intuitive trust that blossoms from testing the Dhamma in one's own direct experience. When balanced with discernment, faith protects the heart from the paralytic cynicism of intellectual skepticism.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Saddhā as the opening door to spiritual life: trust in human potential for awakening.
- Balancing the Five Spiritual Faculties (Indriya-samatta): Faith balanced with Wisdom.
- Moving beyond blind belief and intellectual cynicism into verified experiential confidence.
- Taking heartfelt refuge in the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha as living realities.
