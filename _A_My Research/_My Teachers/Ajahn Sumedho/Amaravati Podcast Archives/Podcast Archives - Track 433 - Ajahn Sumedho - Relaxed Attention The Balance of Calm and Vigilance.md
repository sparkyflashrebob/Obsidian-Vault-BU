### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mastering the subtle art of relaxed attention: harmonizing physical relaxation with crisp, alert mental wakefulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the quintessential balance of Buddhist meditation: relaxed attention. Meditators constantly veer between hyper-tense concentration that causes headaches and drowsy relaxation that leads to sleep. Luang Por shows how to tune the mind like a musical instrument: relaxing all muscular and psychological contraction while maintaining crystal-clear alert presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The false dichotomy: tension (trying hard) versus laxity (giving up).
- The Soṇa Sutta: the simile of the lute strings tuned to perfection.
- Softening the physical base: releasing tension in the eyes, jaw, and belly.
- Resting in alert, weightless presence: the natural state of consciousness.
