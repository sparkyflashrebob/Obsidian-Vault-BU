### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The concluding discourse of the 1989 BPG seminar: resting in the unconditioned heart of Dhamma beyond sectarian labels.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers the closing address at the 1989 Buddhist Publishing Group seminar. He urges practitioners to transcend scholarly debates and sectarian labels. Dhamma is not an intellectual hobby or an exotic Eastern philosophy; it is the immediate, living art of knowing the way things are (*yathābhūta*) and finding freedom right in the midst of worldly life.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Beyond Sectarianism**: Moving from Theravāda vs. Mahāyāna debates to universal awakening.
- **The Living Heart of Dhamma**: Recognizing truth in the ordinary sensory world.
- **Taking Refuge**: Establishing an unshakeable inner sanctuary in the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha.
