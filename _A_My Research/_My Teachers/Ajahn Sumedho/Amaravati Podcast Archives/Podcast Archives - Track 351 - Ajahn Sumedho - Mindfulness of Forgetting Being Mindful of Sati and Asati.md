### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating lapses of mindfulness: how recognizing that we have forgotten is itself the immediate return of mindfulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this insightful 61-minute talk from Wat Ratanawan, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a universal dilemma in meditation: forgetting. Practitioners constantly judge themselves for losing mindfulness and wandering into fantasy. Luang Por points out the profound Dhamma paradox: the moment you realize you have forgotten, you are mindful! If we meet forgetfulness with curiosity rather than guilt, mindfulness becomes unbroken.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cycle of remembering and forgetting in daily contemplative practice.
- Why self-blame and guilt over losing focus actually reinforce ego identity.
- Mindfulness of asati (unmindfulness): observing the gap between awareness and distraction.
- Resting in the unconditioned witness that neither forgets nor remembers.
