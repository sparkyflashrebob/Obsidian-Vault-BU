### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The definitive map of non-self: deconstructing experience into the five aggregates (khandhas) and distinguishing them from pure awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute masterwork, Luang Por Sumedho provides a crystal-clear doctrinal and experiential guide to the Five Aggregates (*khandhas*): Rūpa (form), Vedanā (feeling), Saññā (perception), Saṅkhāra (mental fabrications), and Viññāṇa (sensory consciousness). He explains how unawakened human beings mistakenly seize one or all of the khandhas as 'me' or 'mine'. Luang Por shows that conscious awareness (Buddho) is not trapped inside the khandhas; it is the spacious knowing that holds them.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The five aggregates systematically dissected: body, sensations, memories, thoughts, and sensory knowing.
- The Phenapiṇḍūpama Sutta similes: foam, bubble, mirage, plantain trunk, magic trick.
- The crucial distinction between the fabricated khandhas and unconditioned knowing.
- Resting in the unentangled freedom of the one who knows (Phū Rū).

| Aggregate (Khandha) | Classical Sutta Simile | Deluded Identity Claim | Awakened Insight (Anattā) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Form (Rūpa) | A lump of foam | 'This body is my authentic self' | Impersonal biological elements borrowed from nature |
| 2. Feeling (Vedanā) | A water bubble | 'I am my pleasure, pain, and depression' | Transient sensations bursting upon contact |
| 3. Perception (Saññā) | A desert mirage | 'I am my memories, name, and identity' | Conditioned labels projected by the intellect |
| 4. Fabrications (Saṅkhāra) | A hollow plantain trunk | 'I am my desires, habits, and personality' | Empty mental momentum without an enduring core |
| 5. Consciousness (Viññāṇa) | A magician's illusion | 'I am the immortal soul who perceives' | Transient sensory awareness arising from causes |
