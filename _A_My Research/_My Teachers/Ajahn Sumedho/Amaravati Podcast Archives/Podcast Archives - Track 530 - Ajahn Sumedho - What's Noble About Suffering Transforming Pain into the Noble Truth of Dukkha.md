### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Historical discourse (Talk 107) on the First Noble Truth: why suffering is designated as 'Noble' when understood with wisdom rather than endured with misery.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute historical discourse (Talk 107), Luang Por Sumedho investigates the First Noble Truth: Dukkha Ariyasacca. Suffering itself is not noble; what is noble is the wise, unshrinking contemplation of suffering. When suffering is met with ignorance, it generates bitterness, panic, and revenge. But when suffering is recognized as a noble truth—to be understood, held in awareness, and penetrated with wisdom—it becomes the very vehicle that dismantles ego and opens the heart to total liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why the Buddha called suffering a 'Noble Truth' (Ariya-sacca).
- The difference between suffering blindly versus understanding suffering with wisdom.
- The three aspects of the First Noble Truth (statement, duty, accomplishment).
- Somatic exploration: holding emotional grief and physical pain in non-judgmental awareness.
