### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Setting in Motion of the Wheel of Dhamma: unpacking the Three Turnings and Twelve Aspects across the Four Noble Truths.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the foundational discourse of the Buddha delivered at the Deer Park in Sarnath. He explains why the Buddha taught the Four Noble Truths not as philosophical dogma, but as an experiential science of liberation. By understanding the three turnings (sacca-ñāṇa, kicca-ñāṇa, kata-ñāṇa), suffering ceases to be a personal tragedy and becomes the supreme vehicle for awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical setting of the Deer Park and the first turning of the Wheel of Dhamma.
- The Middle Way avoiding the dual traps of sensual indulgence and harsh self-mortification.
- The Three Turnings and Twelve Modes (Tiparivaṭṭaṁ Dvādasākāraṁ) as practical contemplative duties.
- Awakening to the Deathless (Amata) through direct understanding of the cessation of craving.
