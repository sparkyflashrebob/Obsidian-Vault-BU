### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Standing firm amidst worldly winds: how wisdom remains unshaken by flattery or condemnation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 29-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates Dhammapada 81: 'Just as a solid rock is not shaken by the storm, so the wise are not moved by praise or blame.' Worldly foolishness consists of being an emotional puppet—swelling with pride when praised, and collapsing in depression when blamed. Luang Por teaches that when we see praise and blame as mere acoustic sounds, our hearts achieve noble invulnerability.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Eight Worldly Winds (Lokadhamma): praise, blame, gain, loss, fame, disrepute, pleasure, pain.
- Dhammapada 81: the simile of the solid mountain rock.
- The foolishness of seeking identity in the fickle opinions of other people.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that is beyond all human evaluation.
