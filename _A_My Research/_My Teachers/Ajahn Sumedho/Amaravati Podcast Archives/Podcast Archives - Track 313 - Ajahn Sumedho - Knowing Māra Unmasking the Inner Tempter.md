### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the myth of Māra (the Evil One): recognizing temptation, doubt, guilt, and sloth as internal psychological forces.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this powerful 74-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the archetype of *Māra*—the personification of delusion, temptation, and death in Buddhism. Religious lore portrays Māra as a terrifying demon with armies of monsters. Luang Por strips away superstitious folklore: Māra is the doubtful voice in your own head whispering: 'You can't do this; you are a failure; give up and eat some chocolate.' By recognizing Māra with clarity—'I see you, Māra!'—his power collapses.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The five manifestations of Māra: Khandha-māra, Kilesa-māra, Abhisaṅkhāra-māra, Maccu-māra, Devaputta-māra.
- How the Buddha defeated Māra beneath the Bodhi tree: not through violence, but through touching the earth with truth.
- The modern guises of Māra: cynicism, boredom, sexual obsession, spiritual defeatism.
- The power of recognition: unmasking negative self-talk robs it of authority.
