### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning monastic reflection on living without creating unnecessary psychological suffering: the immediate reality of Nirodha (Cessation).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho explores the third Noble Truth: *Dukkha-nirodha* (the cessation of suffering). Many people think Buddhism is an endless discussion of misery. Luang Por reminds monastics that the Buddha taught the end of suffering! Non-suffering is not an impossible dream; it occurs naturally every time grasping ceases. By noticing the moments of non-suffering throughout the day, the mind becomes confident in the reality of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Third Noble Truth: Nirodha as the direct cessation of grasping.
- Noticing the spaces of non-suffering: the calm between emotional waves.
- How morning monastic reflections establish a stable container of peace for the day.
- Resting as the unconditioned knowing that is intrinsically free from Dukkha.
