### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying enlightenment: clarifying awakening as the absence of delusion, clinging, and ignorance rather than an exotic mystical trance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 50-minute discourse from April 2021, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through mystical, esoteric fantasies of enlightenment. Many imagine awakening as experiencing fireworks, lightning bolts, and psychic superpowers. Luang Por explains that enlightenment is simply seeing things as they actually are (yathā-bhūta-ñāṇa-dassana)—the total cessation of craving, aversion, and delusion. It is not an exotic trance, but our ordinary, natural mind when free from grasping: peaceful, awake, clear, and compassionate.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dismantling esoteric, Hollywood fantasies of spiritual awakening.
- Enlightenment as an 'apophasis'—the absence of suffering rather than an added experience.
- The Rohitassa Sutta: finding the end of the world within this fathom-long body.
- Living awakening in the ordinary moments of washing dishes, walking, and breathing.
