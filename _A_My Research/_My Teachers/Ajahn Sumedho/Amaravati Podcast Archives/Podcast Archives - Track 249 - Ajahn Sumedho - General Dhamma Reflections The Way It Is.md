### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A comprehensive 78-minute Sunday reflection on the foundational Thai Forest Tradition approach to living in harmony with nature.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a vast, sweeping 78-minute Sunday Dhamma exposition. Weaving together personal anecdotes, monastic history, and canonical core teachings, Luang Por covers the full spectrum of spiritual life. From dealing with rude interactions at the checkout counter and utility bills to the high peaks of samādhi and Nibbāna, everything is brought back to Ajahn Chah's master phrase: *Tathatā*—the way it is. When we stop demanding that life be different, peace is right here.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Grounding Dhamma in ordinary domestic life: work, family, finances, and bodily aches.
- Ajahn Chah's legacy: simple, earthy wisdom cutting through complex intellectualism.
- The Three Characteristics (Tilakkhaṇa) as immediate living realities, not abstract theory.
- Living as the unconditioned witness (Buddho) in an unpredictable world.
