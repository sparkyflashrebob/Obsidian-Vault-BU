### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound doctrinal and experiential investigation into the fifth aggregate: Viññāṇa (consciousness) and its dependent origination.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 45-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho investigates the fifth aggregate: Consciousness (*Viññāṇa*). He cites the Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta, where the monk Sāti wrongly believed that consciousness is an immortal soul that transmigrates unchanged from life to life. Luang Por shows that sensory consciousness arises dependently: eye and forms produce eye-consciousness; ear and sounds produce ear-consciousness. When sensory conditions cease, sensory consciousness ceases, revealing unconditioned knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta: the Buddha's refutation of Sāti's soul-heresy.
- The six classes of sensory consciousness: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
- The Kevaṭṭa Sutta verse: 'Consciousness unmanifest, boundless, luminous on all sides...'
- The radical distinction between transient sensory consciousness and unconditioned knowing.
