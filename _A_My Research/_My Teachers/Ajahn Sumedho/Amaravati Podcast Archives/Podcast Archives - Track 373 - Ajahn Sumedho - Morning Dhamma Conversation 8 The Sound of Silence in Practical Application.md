### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Comprehensive conversational instruction on tuning into the inner Sound of Silence (nāda sound) across all daily postures and situations.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this remarkable 100-minute morning session, Luang Por Sumedho provides his most detailed conversational masterclass on the Sound of Silence (*nāda*). Retreatants ask nuanced questions about how to locate the sound, whether it is physical tinnitus or psychic vibration, and how to use it when distracted by heavy thinking or sensory storms. Luang Por demonstrates that the Sound of Silence is an immediate, portable, effortless gateway into unconditioned emptiness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- How to locate the inner sound: listening to the space between sounds.
- Tinnitus versus the nāda sound: physical ear pathology versus conscious acoustic resonance.
- Using the Sound of Silence to cut through runaway thinking and emotional panic.
- The nāda sound as the acoustic manifestation of emptiness (*suññatā*).
