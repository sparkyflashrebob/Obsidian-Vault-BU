### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Day 3 retreat morning exploration of consciousness (viññāṇa): deconstructing Sāti's heresy and discovering the unconditioned nature of awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the third morning of an intensive retreat, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the nature of consciousness (*viññāṇa*). He examines the famous Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta, where the monk Sāti erroneously believed that consciousness is an unchanging soul that transmigrates from life to life. The Buddha refuted this, showing that sensory consciousness arises dependently on conditions. Luang Por guides retreatants to notice the dependent nature of consciousness while resting in the unconditioned ground.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta: dismantling the soul-view of consciousness.
- The six sensory consciousnesses (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind) as conditioned events.
- Viññāṇaṃ anidassanaṃ: consciousness without surface, infinite and luminous.
- Resting as the knowing witness (Buddho) without grasping consciousness as 'me'.
