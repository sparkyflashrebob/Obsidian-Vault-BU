### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Shelley's poetic vision of the white radiance of Eternity stained by the dome of life: transcending the colorful fragmented forms of saṁsāra.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this magnificent 78-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho draws upon Percy Bysshe Shelley's famous elegy *Adonaïs*: *'Life, like a dome of many-coloured glass, stains the white radiance of Eternity, until Death tramples it to fragments.'* Luang Por shows how worldly experiences, personality quirks, and sensory desires are the colorful stained-glass dome. When we break through our fixation with the colorful fragments, we awaken to the pristine white radiance of pure Unconditioned awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Shelley's poetry as a sublime Western resonance of Buddhist Emptiness and the Unconditioned.
- How sensory perceptions and cultural identities stain the pure radiance of conscious knowing.
- The Kevaṭṭa Sutta: 'Consciousness unmanifest, boundless, luminous on all sides...'
- Awakening to Eternity before physical death tramples the mortal dome to fragments.
