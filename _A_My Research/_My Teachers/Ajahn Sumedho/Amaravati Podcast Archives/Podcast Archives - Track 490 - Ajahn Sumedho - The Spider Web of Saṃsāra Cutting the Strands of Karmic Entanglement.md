### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How craving and defensive grasping weave an intricate, sticky spider web of saṃsāra, and how mindfulness cuts through the web.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this incisive 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the metaphor of the spider web found in the Jālinī Sutta. Like a spider spinning silk out of its own belly and getting entangled in its own creation, the human mind spins desires, grudges, identities, and financial obligations, becoming trapped in its own web. Luang Por shows how the sharp razor of mindfulness cuts the strands of the web, setting consciousness free.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Jālinī Sutta: the 108 strands of craving that entangle beings.
- How we trap ourselves in our own mental webs: grudges, loans, status, expectations.
- The illusion of external imprisonment: recognizing that we spin our own prison.
- The sword of wisdom (*paññā-asi*): slicing through the net of delusion in a single breath.
