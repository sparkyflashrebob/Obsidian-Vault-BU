### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Classic Luang Por Sumedho discourse on fear and mental projection: how an untrained mind turns rustling leaves into a lurking terrorist.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this gripping and humorous 46-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho illustrates the psychology of projection through one of his most celebrated stories: the 'terrorist in the bushes'. Walking back to his kutī on a dark English night, he hears a rustling sound in the rhododendron bushes. In a split second, the mind hallucinates a bloodthirsty terrorist with a gun, triggering adrenaline and terror. When he shines his flashlight, it turns out to be a tiny, harmless hedgehog. Luang Por shows how 99% of human suffering is fabricated by hedgehogs in our minds.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The mechanics of fear projection: how imagination amplifies minor sensory cues.
- The Bhayabherava Sutta: meeting primal terror in the forest without running.
- Distinguishing between raw sensory sound and the horror movie invented by thought.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that observes panic without buying into it.
