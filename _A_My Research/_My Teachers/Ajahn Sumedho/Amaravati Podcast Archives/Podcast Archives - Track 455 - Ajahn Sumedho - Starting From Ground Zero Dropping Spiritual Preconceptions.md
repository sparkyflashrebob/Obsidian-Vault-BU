### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Returning to 'Ground Zero': stripping away academic Buddhist jargon, spiritual attainment stories, and beginning anew in bare presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this refreshing 67-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho invites meditators to return to 'Ground Zero'. Experienced practitioners often carry heavy baggage of past meditation successes, theoretical knowledge, and subtle conceit about how many years they have practiced. Luang Por demonstrates that real Dhamma practice always starts from Ground Zero: right here, right now, in the humble reality of this very breath.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The trap of the 'experienced meditator': spiritual pride and rigid expectations.
- Returning to beginner's mind: dropping all theoretical assumptions about Nibbāna.
- Ground Zero as the unconditioned present where nothing has been fabricated.
- The radical freshness of ordinary sensory perception when untainted by memory.
