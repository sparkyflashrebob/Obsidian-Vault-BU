### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Carrying meditation beyond the cushion into family life, workplace relationships, and ethical livelihood.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Continuing the foundational series, Luang Por Sumedho explains how to integrate Dhamma into ordinary daily routines. Meditators often experience a painful split between their peaceful morning sitting and their stressful workday. Luang Por shows how every telephone call, commuting traffic jam, and family disagreement is an immediate opportunity for Right Mindfulness and Right Action.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Overcoming the false dichotomy between 'sacred meditation' and 'profane secular work'.
- Using daily mundane transitions (doorways, escalators, washing hands) as mindfulness bells.
- Practicing Right Speech (sammā-vācā): pausing before speaking; avoiding gossip and cutting sarcasm.
- Living with family members with patience, generosity, and forgiveness.
