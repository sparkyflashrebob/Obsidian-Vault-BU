### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reflections on Wan Phra (the lunar Uposatha day): all-night meditation, communal renewal, and sharpening mindfulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the tradition of Wan Phra—the Buddhist holy day aligned with the four moon phases. In forest monasteries, Wan Phra is marked by chanting, recitation of the Pāṭimokkha, and sitting in all-night vigil (nesejjika). Luang Por explains that aligning spiritual practice with the lunar rhythm creates an intentional sanctuary in time, helping monastics and lay devotees alike step out of mundane busyness to dedicate themselves to wholehearted awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The ancient roots of the Uposatha observance day in the Buddha's time.
- The eight precepts (Aṭṭha-sīla) and their power to simplify and purify the mind.
- Working with physical exhaustion, sleepiness, and restlessness during all-night vigils.
- The cyclical rhythm of monastic life as a natural counterweight to commercial calendars.
