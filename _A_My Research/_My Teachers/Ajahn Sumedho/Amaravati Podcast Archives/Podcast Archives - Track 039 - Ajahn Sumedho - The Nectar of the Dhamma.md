### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Tasting the supreme sweetness and nourishment of truth when worldly poisons are relinquished.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
An expansive 80-minute exposition on the transformative nourishment of the Dhamma. Worldly pleasures offer temporary stimulation followed by exhaustion and addiction, whereas the Dhamma is a sweet nectar that refreshes, heals, and satisfies the deepest longing of the human heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the classic Dhammapada teaching: 'Drinking the sweetness of seclusion and the sweetness of calm, one becomes free from distress.'
- Examining how modern commercial culture creates synthetic cravings that leave the soul empty and chronically unsatisfied.
- The joy of pure ethical conscience (anavajja-sukha) and the bliss of meditation (samādhi-sukha).
- Cultivating reverence and gratitude for the Buddha's timeless medicine in our troubled modern world.
