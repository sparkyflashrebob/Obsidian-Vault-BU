### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the cunning strategies of ego-conceit (māna) through humorous, honest self-investigation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this candid and insightful 68-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho exposes the absurdity of human egoism: feeling superior, inferior, or stubbornly equal to others. He shares how ego can hijack even spiritual attainments, vegetarianism, and ascetic discipline, and shows that the cure is laughing at ourselves with wise compassion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Investigating the three forms of conceit (māna): 'I am better', 'I am worse', and 'I am equal'.
- How the spiritual ego forms identities around being 'more mindful', 'more austere', or 'more pure' than others.
- The vulnerability of the ego to criticism, gossip, praise, and blame (lokadhamma).
- Transcending the trap of self-judgment by viewing personality traits as conditioned habits rather than personal soul.
