### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound monastic funeral discourse contemplating the inevitability of mortality, the sharing of merit, and taking refuge in the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute funeral discourse delivered in the Amaravati Temple, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a gathering of grieving family and Sangha members. A funeral is the supreme Dhamma mirror. Worldly distractions, petty grudges, and financial ambitions instantly shrink into triviality when standing before a coffin. Luang Por offers deep comfort through the Dhamma: bodies die, but awareness was never born. In loving gratitude, we dedicate merit to the departed.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Buddhist funeral as an occasion for profound gratitude rather than hopeless despair.
- Contemplating the coffin as our own future destiny: the universal democracy of death.
- The traditional practice of sharing merit (pattidāna) with departed loved ones.
- Resting in the Deathless (Amata) that survives the dissolution of flesh.
