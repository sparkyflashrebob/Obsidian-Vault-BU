### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the auspicious anniversary of Ajahn Chah's birth (June 17), Luang Por Sumedho reflects with deep filial gratitude and profound contemplative insight on his decade of intimate training under the master at Wat Pah Pong. He highlights Ajahn Chah's direct, humorous, and uncompromising pointing to the unconditioned Dhamma beyond religious ritual.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Recollections of Ajahn Chah's skillful means in demolishing Western cultural pride and intellectual conceit.
- Understanding Ajahn Chah's classic pointers: 'Is it impermanent? Then let it go', and 'If you let go a little, you have a little peace.'
- The transmission of the Thai Forest Tradition to the West as an act of selfless compassionate offering.
- Realizing that the true teacher is not a mortal personality, but the living Dhamma present in our own awareness.
