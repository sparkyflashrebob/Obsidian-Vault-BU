### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Exposing the Western addiction to intellectual rationality, logical argumentation, and mental conceptualization.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the 2010 monastic retreat, Luang Por Sumedho diagnoses the primary neurosis of Western civilization: 'Addiction to Rationality'. We believe that every feeling, dilemma, and spiritual reality must be rationally explained, debated, and analyzed. But life is not a math equation. True peace is found only when we step beyond the rational mind into intuitive, somatic knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Tyranny of the Logical Brain**: Why intellectual brilliance cannot cure emotional suffering.
- **The Limits of Analysis**: Seeing that rational thinking can only create concepts about reality.
- **The Intuitive Sanctuary**: Trusting the non-verbal knowing of the heart (*citta*).
