### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Waking up from automatic, mechanical living: bringing crisp, affectionate attention to every moment of our existence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this urgent and compassionate 68-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho sounds the alarm on human unconsciousness. Most people sleepwalk through their lives—eating without tasting, speaking without listening, driving without seeing, lost in daydreaming. Luang Por urges: 'Pay attention to your life!' Life is not happening in our memories or fantasies; it is happening right here. Paying attention is the supreme act of love and wisdom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The trance of daily unconsciousness: living on automatic pilot.
- Paying attention as the foundational definition of Buddhist mindfulness (sati).
- Bringing presence to mundane activities: walking, eating, opening doors, dressing.
- The extraordinary beauty and freshness of ordinary reality when seen with bare attention.
