### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Using cultural superstitions around Friday the 13th to illuminate the existential fear of uncertainty (anicca) and finding refuge in Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given on Friday the 13th in October 2000, Luang Por Sumedho playfully uses Western superstitions (black cats, lucky charms, Friday the 13th) to expose humanity's terror of uncertainty. We invent rituals and charms hoping to guarantee good luck and ward off misfortune. Luang Por reminds us that uncertainty is not bad luck; it is the fundamental law of the cosmos (*anicca*). When we make peace with uncertainty, fear dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Superstition as an unconscious psychological defense against impermanence.
- Ajahn Chah's master teaching: *Aniccam vata sankhara* ('Uncertain indeed are all conditions').
- How the desire for certainty drives anxiety, control issues, and compulsive behavior.
- Taking refuge in the unshakeable Triple Gem rather than worldly charms.
