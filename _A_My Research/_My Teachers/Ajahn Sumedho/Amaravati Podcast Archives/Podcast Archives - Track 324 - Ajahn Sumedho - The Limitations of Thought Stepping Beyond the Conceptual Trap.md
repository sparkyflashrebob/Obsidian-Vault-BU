### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Recognizing the inherent boundaries of the conceptual intellect: why thinking can never touch the Unconditioned.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 54-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the boundaries of the human intellect. Thought is a linear, binary instrument: it can divide, measure, calculate, and compare, but it can never touch the seamless whole of reality. Meditators often exhaust themselves trying to 'think their way to enlightenment'. Luang Por shows how to use thought skillfully for practical daily tasks, while knowing when to put thought down and rest in silent awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Madhupiṇḍika Sutta: the proliferation of thoughts (*papañca*) as the architect of mental conflict.
- The limits of conceptual mapping: confusing the menu with the banquet.
- How thinking chops reality into artificial opposites: good vs. bad, self vs. other.
- Resting in the silent space that holds thought without being trapped by it.
