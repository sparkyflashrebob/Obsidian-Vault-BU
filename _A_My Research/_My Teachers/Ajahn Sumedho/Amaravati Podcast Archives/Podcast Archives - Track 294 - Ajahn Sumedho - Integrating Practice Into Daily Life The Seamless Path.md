### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Breaking the artificial boundary between 'meditation time' and 'ordinary life': living every moment as continuous practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the perennial struggle of lay meditators: how to integrate meditation into a busy secular life. People compartmentalize their existence: forty minutes of holy meditation in the morning, followed by fourteen hours of stress, email panic, road rage, and family arguments. Luang Por shows how every mundane moment—brushing teeth, answering the phone, standing in line—is an immediate opportunity for Right Mindfulness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Overcoming the false split between 'formal practice' and 'daily reality'.
- The Sampajañña (clear comprehension) section of the Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta: mindfulness in moving, eating, bending, and stretching.
- Using daily mundane transitions (doorways, traffic lights, kettle boiling) as mindfulness bells.
- Right Speech (sammā-vācā) as the frontline of spiritual practice in the workplace.
