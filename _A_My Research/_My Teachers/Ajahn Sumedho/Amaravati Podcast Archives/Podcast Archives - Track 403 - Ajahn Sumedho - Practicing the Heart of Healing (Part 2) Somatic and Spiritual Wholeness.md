### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The contemplative healing of chronic physical sickness and emotional trauma through mindfulness, acceptance, and the Seven Factors of Awakening.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this profound 56-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the spiritual dimension of healing. While medicine treats the biological hardware of the body, spiritual healing heals our relationship to sickness, aging, and death. Unpacking the Girimānanda Sutta, Luang Por shows how contemplating impermanence, non-self, and cessation releases the heart from fear, allowing deep psychosomatic harmony and resilience to emerge.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Girimānanda Sutta: the ten contemplations that heal the sick elder.
- Curing the body versus healing the relationship to the body.
- The Seven Factors of Awakening (Bojjhaṅga) as energetic medicine for physical distress.
- Resting in the unconditioned awareness that is never sick, injured, or broken.
