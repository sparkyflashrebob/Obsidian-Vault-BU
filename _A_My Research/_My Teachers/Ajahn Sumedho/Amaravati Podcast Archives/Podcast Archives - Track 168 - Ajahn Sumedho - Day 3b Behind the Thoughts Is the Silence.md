### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Attuning to the inner sound of silence (nāda) as the primordial unconditioned backdrop of all thought.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 46-minute afternoon retreat instruction, Luang Por Sumedho guides retreatants to attune to the 'sound of silence' (nāda). Behind the noisy clatter of internal verbal chatter, there is a subtle, high-pitched background sound present in consciousness. By using this sound as an object of contemplation, discursive thinking loses its grip and the mind settles into vast stillness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- How to locate the inner sound of silence: turning attention inward to the high-pitched vibration.
- Using the nāda sound as an effortless contemplative anchor throughout the day.
- Recognizing that thoughts arise from silence, exist in silence, and return to silence.
- The sound of silence as an auditory reflection of the Unconditioned (Asaṅkhata).
