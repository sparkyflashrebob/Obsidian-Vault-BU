### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Facing the inevitable realities of bodily decay, aging, and mortality with heroic contemplative honesty.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this powerful 56-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the fundamental existential reality that every living organism must face: birth, aging, illness, and death. Instead of living in comfortable denial, the spiritual practitioner uses mortality as a supreme mirror that exposes the folly of worldly ambition and reveals the Deathless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the universal law of jarā-maraṇa (aging and death) as an undeniable biological fact.
- How modern commercial society is built on the futile project of attempting to disguise and outrun death.
- Using daily mindfulness of death (maraṇānussati) to dissolve petty grievances and clarify priorities.
- Discovering the unborn space of consciousness that remains untouched as the biological organism fades.
