### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Foundational cultivation of loving-kindness (mettā): radiating goodwill without limits, preferences, or conditions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 40-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the practice of Mettā (loving-kindness) not merely as a formal recitation, but as an attitude of boundless welcoming. Most human affection is conditional: 'I love you if you agree with me, compliment me, and behave yourself.' True Mettā, however, is unconditional benevolence directed equally toward the pleasant, the unpleasant, and the neutral. Luang Por outlines the eleven benefits of Mettā taught by the Buddha.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The difference between romantic/conditional love and Buddhist Mettā.
- The Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta: loving all beings like a mother protects her only child.
- The eleven benefits of Mettā: sleeping peacefully, waking happily, and being loved by humans and devas.
- Directing Mettā toward our own shadow, physical aches, and neurotic emotional habits.
