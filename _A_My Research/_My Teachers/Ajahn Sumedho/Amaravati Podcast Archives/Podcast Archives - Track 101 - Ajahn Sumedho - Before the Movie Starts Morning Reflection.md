### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in the unconditioned stillness before the projection of mental movies, dramas, and self-identities begins.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho uses the evocative metaphor of sitting in an empty cinema before the projector starts running. The white screen represents pure unconditioned awareness; the dramatic movie represents our thoughts, memories, worries, and identities. When we remember the screen, we can enjoy or witness the film without being traumatized by the plot.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the difference between the luminous projection screen (awareness) and the flickering images (mental content).
- How we become hypnotized by our personal psychological dramas, taking fiction for absolute reality.
- The morning reflection practice: noticing the silence and stillness before thought proliferation (papañca) begins.
- Freedom from emotional entanglement while remaining fully responsive in the world.
