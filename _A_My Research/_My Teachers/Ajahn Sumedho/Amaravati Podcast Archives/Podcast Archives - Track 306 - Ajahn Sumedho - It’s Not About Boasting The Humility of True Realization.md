### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Exposing the vulgarity of spiritual boasting and commercial self-promotion: why authentic Dhamma embodies absolute humility.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the danger of spiritual ambition and bragging. In modern culture, people brag about their wealth, connections, and spiritual attainments—proclaiming their jhānas, their psychic insights, or their awakening. Luang Por reminds the community that genuine spiritual realization has nothing to boast about. Realization is not gaining a trophy; it is the vanishing of the one who wants to brag.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Vinaya prohibition against monastics claiming superhuman states (uttarimanussadhamma).
- How spiritual boasting reveals the survival of ego in holy disguise.
- The Three Modes of Conceit (māna): 'I am superior', 'I am equal', 'I am inferior'.
- True holiness as modest, quiet, harmless, and entirely devoid of self-promotion.
