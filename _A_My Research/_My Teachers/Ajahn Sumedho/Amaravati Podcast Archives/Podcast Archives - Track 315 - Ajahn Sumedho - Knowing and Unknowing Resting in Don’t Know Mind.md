### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The profound spiritual discipline of 'unknowing': stripping away accumulated opinions, concepts, and labels to touch pristine naked awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 67-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sacred practice of 'unknowing'. The intellectual mind is obsessed with knowing: naming, categorizing, diagnosing, and asserting opinions about everything. Luang Por shows that this constant labeling clutters consciousness and separates us from direct reality. True wisdom requires the humility of 'unknowing'—dropping our ideas about ourselves and the world, and resting in pristine, wordless presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The burden of conceptual knowing: how labels deaden direct sensory experience.
- The practice of 'Don't Know Mind': embracing open, spacious, childlike wonder.
- The Bāhiya Sutta: in the seen, only the seen; in the heard, only the heard.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that knows without using words or concepts.
