### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Extending mindfulness beyond formal cushion sitting into daily work, chores, sensory interactions, and interpersonal relationships.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this lively and practical 65-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho tackles the common spiritual split where people meditate diligently on their cushions for an hour, only to lose all mindfulness the moment they stand up. 'Just sitting is not enough!' Luang Por insists. Real Dhamma practice must permeate washing dishes, answering emails, driving cars, and interacting with difficult personalities.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The trap of the 'cushion specialist': high samādhi on the cushion, utter reactivity off it.
- The four postures (iriyāpatha): sitting, standing, walking, and lying down.
- Clear comprehension (sampajañña) in daily actions: eating, drinking, cleaning, and speaking.
- Transforming monastery work periods and household chores into profound Dhamma laboratories.
