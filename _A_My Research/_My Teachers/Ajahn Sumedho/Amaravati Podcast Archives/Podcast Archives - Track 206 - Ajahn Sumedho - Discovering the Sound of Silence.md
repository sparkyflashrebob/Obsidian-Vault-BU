### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The foundational history and contemplative instructions for using the inner cosmic sound (nāda) as a direct portal to Emptiness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this milestone discourse, Luang Por Sumedho shares the intimate story of how he discovered the 'Sound of Silence' (the nāda sound) and how it became his lifelong meditative anchor. He addresses common skepticism about whether this sound is merely physical ear noise or a genuine spiritual sign. By attending to this ever-present background vibration, the wandering attention is instantly pulled out of discursive conceptual thinking into luminous spaciousness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Luang Por's personal breakthrough with the nāda sound during solitary practice.
- How to distinguish the inner sound from neurotic anxiety or ear damage.
- Using the sound of silence across all four postures: sitting, standing, walking, lying down.
- The sound of silence as the sonic signature of conscious space and Suññatā (Emptiness).
