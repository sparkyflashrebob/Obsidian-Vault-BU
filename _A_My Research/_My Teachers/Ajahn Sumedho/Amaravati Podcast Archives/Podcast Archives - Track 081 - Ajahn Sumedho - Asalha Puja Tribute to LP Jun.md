### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the holy festival of Asāḷha Pūjā, Luang Por Sumedho commemorates the Buddha's First Turning of the Dhamma Wheel and pays an affectionate, reverent tribute to the newly deceased elder forest monk, Luang Por Jun. He draws on Luang Por Jun's life of radical simplicity, solitary forest dwelling, and uncompromising devotion to the Vinaya.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The historical significance of Asāḷha Pūjā: the birth of the Sangha and the revelation of the Middle Way.
- Recollections of Luang Por Jun's austere practice in the remote jungles of Thailand.
- Contemplating death not as an end, but as the final exam of a life devoted to Dhamma.
- Inspiring the Western monastic community to maintain the uncompromising standard of the Forest Tradition.
