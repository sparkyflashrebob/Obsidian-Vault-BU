### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Buddhist analysis of creation: the world is created not by an external deity, but through the six sense doors.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates the origin of the world. Religions routinely debate who created the universe, often blaming God for war, disease, and suffering. Luang Por points to the Buddha's revolutionary teaching in the Rohitassa Sutta: 'In this fathom-long body with its perceptions and intellect, I declare the world, the arising of the world, the cessation of the world, and the path.' We create the world through our sensory reactions.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Rohitassa Sutta: the cosmos created within the fathom-long living body.
- How the world of desire, terror, and conflict arises when sensory contact triggers unmindful craving.
- Reframing the problem of evil: not blaming external deities, but taking ownership of our own greed and hate.
- The cessation of the world (loka-nirodha) when consciousness ceases to grasp at sensory appearances.
