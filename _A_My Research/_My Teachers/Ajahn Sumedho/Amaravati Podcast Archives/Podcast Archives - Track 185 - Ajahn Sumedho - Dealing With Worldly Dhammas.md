### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating unshakable equanimity (upekkhā) amidst the Eight Worldly Winds: gain and loss, praise and blame, fame and disrepute, pleasure and pain.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 24-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the inevitable rollercoaster of worldly existence. The uninstructed worldling is overjoyed by gain, praise, and pleasure, and crushed by loss, blame, and pain. Luang Por reminds practitioners that these eight conditions fluctuate endlessly according to natural law. By recognizing them as impermanent worldly dhammas, the mind remains centered, dignified, and serene through every storm.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Eight Worldly Dhammas: gain/loss, status/disgrace, praise/blame, pleasure/pain.
- Why seeking constant praise and approval is a guaranteed formula for anxiety.
- Equanimity (upekkhā) as the unshakeable mountain that remains unmoved by winds.
- Treating criticism and setbacks as meditation teachers rather than personal injuries.
