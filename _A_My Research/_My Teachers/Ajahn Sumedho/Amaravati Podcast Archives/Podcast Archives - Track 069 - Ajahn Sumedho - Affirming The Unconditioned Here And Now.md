### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Ceasing the endless search for enlightenment in future time and claiming the unconditioned presence right now.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A powerhouse 59-minute discourse declaring the immediacy of the Deathless (Amata). Luang Por Sumedho challenges the ingrained habit of relegating Nibbāna to a distant lifetime or an impossible future attainment. The Unconditioned (Asaṅkhata) is the timeless background in which this very moment appears.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Exposing the delusion of spiritual postponement: 'I will be enlightened when I retire, or on my next retreat.'
- The timeless nature of Dhamma (akāliko): accessible immediately to the wise.
- How the thinking mind can only conceive of conditioned objects in linear time.
- Stepping out of the dimension of time into the eternal present of receptive knowing.
