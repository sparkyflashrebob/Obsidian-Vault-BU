### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating the perception of a renunciant mendicant (*samaṇa-saññā*): living with humility, vulnerability, and radical contentment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 69-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the mental perception of a religious seeker (*samaṇa-saññā*). In the Dasadhamma Sutta, the Buddha instructs monastics to constantly reflect: 'I am now living a life different from the layperson; my livelihood depends on others.' Luang Por discusses how cultivating this perception dismantles entitlement, establishes profound gratitude for the requisites, and keeps the flame of renunciation burning bright.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Samaṇasaññā Sutta and the Ten Reflections for Monastics (Dasadhamma).
- Living on alms: the spiritual psychology of eating food offered by others.
- How samaṇa-saññā protects the monastic from slipping into bourgeois complacency.
- Applying the renunciant perspective to lay practice: living simply on earth.
