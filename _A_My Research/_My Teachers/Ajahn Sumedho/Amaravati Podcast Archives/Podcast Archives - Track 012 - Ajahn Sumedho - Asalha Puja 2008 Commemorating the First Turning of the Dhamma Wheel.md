### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Commemorating the Buddha's first sermon, the arising of the First Noble Truth, and the birth of the Sangha.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the full moon of Āsāḷha, Luang Por Sumedho delivers a majestic sermon commemorating the First Turning of the Wheel of Dhamma at Sarnath. He recounts how the Buddha taught the five ascetics the Middle Way between sensual indulgence and self-mortification. Luang Por explores the twelve modes of the Four Noble Truths, emphasizing that when Kondañña realized 'whatever is subject to origination is subject to cessation', the light of the Sangha was born in the world.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Middle Way (*Majjhimā Paṭipadā*)**: Avoiding the extremes of hedonism and ascetic self-hatred.
- **The 12 Modes (*Tiparivaṭṭaṁ Dvādasākāraṁ*)**: Understanding the three turnings of the Four Noble Truths.
- **Kondañña's Breakthrough**: The birth of stream-entry through seeing *yaṅkiñci samudayadhammaṁ sabpantaṁ nirodhadhamman'ti*.
