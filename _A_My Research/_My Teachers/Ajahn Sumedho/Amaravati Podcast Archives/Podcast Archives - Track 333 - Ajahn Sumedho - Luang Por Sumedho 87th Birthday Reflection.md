### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Celebrating his 87th birthday at Wat Ratanawan at the foothills of Khao Yai, Luang Por Sumedho offers a serene and joyful Dhamma reflection. Having ordained in 1966 under Luang Por Chah, he looks back over 55 years as a Buddhist monk. With physical hearing, eyesight, and joint strength declining, Luang Por expresses radiant gratitude for the Sound of Silence and the realization that consciousness is not born and does not die.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the body at age 87: aches, pains, and biological impermanence.
- Fifty-five years in the Sangha: gratitude for Luang Por Chah and the monastic tradition.
- The Sound of Silence (nāda sound) as an immediate, effortless anchor for elderly practice.
- Awareness itself as the uncreated, deathless refuge (Asaṅkhata Dhamma).
