### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Developing contemplative discernment (dhamma-vicaya): distinguishing between wholesome states, unwholesome reactions, and unconditioned awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the second factor of awakening: *Dhamma-vicaya* (investigation of reality / discernment). Discernment is not academic intellect or clever argument. It is the intuitive capacity of the awakened heart to investigate phenomena: 'Is this wholesome or unwholesome? Does this lead to suffering or peace?' Luang Por demonstrates how sharp discernment cuts through emotional confusion without creating judgment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Dhamma-vicaya as an active, luminous factor of awakening.
- Discernment versus self-righteous moral judgment.
- Using wise inquiry to investigate somatic sensations and mental moods.
- The clarity of mind that emerges when discernment operates without an ego owner.
