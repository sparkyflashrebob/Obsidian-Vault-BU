### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct contemplation of the 32 parts of the body (Dvattiṃsākāra): dismantling cosmetic vanity, lust, and identity view through somatic anatomy.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic 74-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores one of the most powerful and confronting meditations in Buddhist monasticism: the contemplation of the 32 parts of the body (*dvattiṃsākāra*). Modern society spends billions on cosmetics, gym memberships, and fashion to sustain the illusion that the body is glamorous and immortal. By systematically contemplating head hair, body hair, nails, teeth, skin, liver, lungs, intestine, and bile, the myth of the physical self collapses into profound tranquility.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Dvattiṃsākāra (32 parts of the body) in the Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta.
- Overcoming sensual obsession and body dysmorphia through realistic anatomy.
- The five initial parts given at ordination: kesā, lomā, nakhā, dantā, taco.
- How body contemplation produces spontaneous dispassion (virāga) and peace.
