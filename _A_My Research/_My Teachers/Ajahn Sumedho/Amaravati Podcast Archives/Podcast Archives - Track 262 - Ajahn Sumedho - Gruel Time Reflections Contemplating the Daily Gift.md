### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Morning meal reflections on moderation in eating (bhojane mattaññutā), bodily elements, and receiving the food of donors with profound gratitude.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the traditional early morning rice gruel gathering at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho leads the resident community in contemplative reflection. Receiving nourishment in a Buddhist monastery is a sacred act: the food is offered freely by lay disciples with prayers of faith. Luang Por reminds the monastics to eat with mindfulness of elements (dhātu), moderation, and reverent dedication of merit to all living beings.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The four traditional reflections on food: not for play, intoxication, or vanity, but for sustaining life.
- Bhojane mattaññutā: knowing the right measure in eating to keep the body light for meditation.
- Contemplating food as bare elements: earth, water, fire, and air returning to nature.
- The profound karmic debt of eating the food of faith (saddhā-deyya) with a pure heart.
