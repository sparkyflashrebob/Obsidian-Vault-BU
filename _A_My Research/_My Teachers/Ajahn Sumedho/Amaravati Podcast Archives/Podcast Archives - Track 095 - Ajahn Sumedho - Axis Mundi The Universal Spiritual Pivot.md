### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The timeless spiritual axis (Axis Mundi) at the center of consciousness, around which the entire world revolves.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
An expansive, majestic 84-minute masterpiece. Luang Por Sumedho explores the ancient mythic concept of the 'Axis Mundi'—the world pillar connecting earth and sky. In direct Buddhist contemplation, this axis is the unshakeable stillness at the center of conscious awareness, remaining unmoved while the wheel of saṁsāra spins furiously around it.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the mythic Axis Mundi as a symbol for the unmoving center of pure knowing.
- How the spinning world of politics, media, and drama sweeps consciousness into dizziness.
- Finding the still center of the hurricane: resting in Buddho amidst intense crises.
- The integration of heavenly spiritual aspiration and grounded earthly embodiment.
