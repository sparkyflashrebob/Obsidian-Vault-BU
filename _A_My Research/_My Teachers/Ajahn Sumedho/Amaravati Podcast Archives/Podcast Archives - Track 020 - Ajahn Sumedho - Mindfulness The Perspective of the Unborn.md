### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating mindfulness not from the perspective of an anxious ego, but from the unconditioned perspective of the Unborn (ajāta).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep autumn discourse, Luang Por Sumedho presents a radical reorientation of mindfulness (*sati*). Usually, we practice mindfulness from the perspective of the born: 'I am a stressed person trying to become mindful.' Luang Por invites practitioners to take their stand in the Unborn (*ajāta*). Awareness was never born, does not age, and does not die. When mindfulness is viewed from the Unborn, all striving ends.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Perspective of the Born vs. The Unborn**: Flipping the spiritual equation.
- **The Sutta Proof of the Ajāta**: Reading the Buddha's declaration in Udāna 8.3.
- **Living as the Deathless**: Resting in unconditioned wakefulness in the midst of daily activity.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Identity | 'I am a mortal, anxious human being trying to practice mindfulness.' | Awareness is unborn, unconditioned, and already whole. |
| Method | Straining to control the mind, fighting distractions, feeling inadequate | Effortlessly holding all changing conditions in the spacious field of knowing. |
| Refuge | A fragile, temporary state of concentration that easily shatters | The deathless sanctuary of Asaṅkhata Dhamma that was never broken. |
