### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reflective Q&A: how to use wise reflection (yoniso-manasikāra) to review practice, forgive daily slips, and cultivate continuous mindfulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 44-minute session, Luang Por Sumedho answers nuanced questions about the balance between silent absorption and reflective contemplation (*yoniso-manasikāra*). Meditators often believe thinking is always bad and must be completely stopped. Luang Por explains that while obsessive proliferation (*papañca*) causes suffering, wise reflection is an essential limb of the path. Using conscious questions to investigate conditionality opens the door to wisdom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Papañca (unwholesome mental proliferation) versus Yoniso-manasikāra (wise reflection).
- Using gentle, targeted questions in meditation: 'What is this? Is this permanent? Is this self?'
- Evening review of the day: practicing self-forgiveness without moralistic guilt.
- Developing intuitive discernment that operates faster than conceptual thought.
