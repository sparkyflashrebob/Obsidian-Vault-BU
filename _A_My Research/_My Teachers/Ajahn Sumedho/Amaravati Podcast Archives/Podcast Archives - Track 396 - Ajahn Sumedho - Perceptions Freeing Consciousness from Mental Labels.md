### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how perceptions (*saññā*) filter sensory reality, project illusory meanings, and entrap consciousness in suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho conducts an in-depth investigation into perception (*saññā*). Perception is the mental faculty that recognizes, names, and labels incoming sensory impressions. Without mindfulness, we mistake our labels for reality—we mistake the label 'enemy' for the human being, or the label 'hopeless' for the situation. Luang Por shows how stepping back into pure knowing frees the heart from the prison of perceptions.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The nature and function of saññā (perception/recognition) in the five aggregates.
- How cultural conditioning and past traumas warp our perceptual lenses.
- Recognizing that labels are merely mental symbols, not ultimate truths.
- Resting in bare perception: seeing things as they are (Yathābhūta-ñāṇadassana).
