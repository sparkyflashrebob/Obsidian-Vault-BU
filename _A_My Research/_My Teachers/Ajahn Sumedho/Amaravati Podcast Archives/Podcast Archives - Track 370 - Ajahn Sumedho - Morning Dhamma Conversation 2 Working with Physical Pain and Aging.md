### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Intimate dialogue on working with physical pain, chronic knee injuries, and biological aging: separating sensation from suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this poignant and practical 95-minute morning dialogue, Luang Por Sumedho engages monastics who are struggling with long hours of cross-legged sitting, aching backs, and stiff joints. Drawing directly from the Sallatha Sutta, Luang Por deconstructs the two darts: the first dart is the raw physical sensation of pain; the second dart is the mental resistance, anger, and anxiety we shoot into ourselves. Luang Por demonstrates how pain softens when held in compassionate space.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Sallatha Sutta: distinguishing between the first physical arrow and the second mental arrow.
- Deconstructing the concept 'pain' into bare sensations of heat, pressure, and tingling.
- How fear and anticipatory tension amplify physical suffering ten-fold.
- Using physical pain as an uncompromising teacher of impermanence and non-self.
