### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Realizing the Third Noble Truth: cessation (nirodha) as a real-time, empirical refuge rather than an abstract theory.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the silent winter retreat of January 2004, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Third Noble Truth: Nirodha Sacca (the cessation of suffering). Meditators endlessly study suffering and analyze its causes, but often treat cessation as a distant, theoretical promised land. Luang Por shows that cessation is experienced every time a thought ends, an emotion subsides, and the mind relaxes into silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why Western practitioners often understand Dukkha thoroughly but remain skeptical of Nirodha.
- The Three Turnings of the Third Noble Truth: statement, prescription (cessation is to be realized), and realization.
- Noticing the empty pause between thoughts and the silent stillness after sound ceases.
- Abiding in Nirodha as the direct, living taste of Nibbāna in this very life.
