### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical reframing of Dukkha: why suffering is not a moral failure or a curse, but the supreme spiritual teacher that must be welcomed and understood.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the precise duty associated with the First Noble Truth. The Buddha did not say that suffering should be run away from, suppressed, or cured with alcohol. In the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta, the Buddha declared: 'This Noble Truth of Suffering is to be fully understood (*pariññeyya*).' Luang Por boldly proclaims: 'Suffering should be welcomed!' When we welcome Dukkha as an honored teacher, it guides us directly to Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The three turnings and twelve aspects of the Four Noble Truths.
- Pariññeyya: the sacred duty of fully understanding Dukkha.
- Why running from suffering guarantees its perpetual return.
- Welcoming grief, physical pain, and disappointment into the vast heart of compassion.
