### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Discerning the fundamental dividing line of Buddhist metaphysics: distinguishing between transient conditioned phenomena and the timeless Unconditioned.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 62-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho articulates the master key of the Buddha's teaching: recognizing the difference between conditions (*saṅkhata*) and the Unconditioned (*asaṅkhata*). Everything we can name, think, feel, or see is a condition—it arises, changes, and ceases. The tragedy of humanity is expecting conditions to be stable and eternal. When we stop demanding that conditions satisfy the heart, awareness naturally falls back into the unconditioned ground.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The three marks of the conditioned (Saṅkhata-lakkhaṇa): arising, ceasing, and changing while standing.
- The unconditioned (Asaṅkhata): unoriginated, unmade, uncompounded, deathless.
- How meditators confuse pleasant meditative conditions with the Unconditioned.
- Resting as the knowing witness that bridges the relative and the ultimate.
