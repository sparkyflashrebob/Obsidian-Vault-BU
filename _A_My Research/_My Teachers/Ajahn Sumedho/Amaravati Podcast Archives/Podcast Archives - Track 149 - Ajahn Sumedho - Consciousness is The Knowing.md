### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in pure knowing as the ultimate uncreated sanctuary: the essence of the Forest Tradition.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 71-minute masterwork, Luang Por Sumedho explores the simplicity and profundity of knowing. When all meditation techniques, philosophies, and doctrines are put aside, what remains is the naked fact of knowing. Consciousness is that which knows. Knowing requires no effort, no education, and no special status; it is immediately present, timeless, and completely unburdened.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The radical simplicity of 'just knowing': moving beyond complicated meditative gymnastics.
- How the knowing faculty remains unsoiled by ugly thoughts or painful physical sensations.
- Recognizing that we do not need to create peace; we simply rest in the knowing of whatever is present.
- The realization that this pure knowing is the living reality of the Third Noble Truth (Nirodha).
