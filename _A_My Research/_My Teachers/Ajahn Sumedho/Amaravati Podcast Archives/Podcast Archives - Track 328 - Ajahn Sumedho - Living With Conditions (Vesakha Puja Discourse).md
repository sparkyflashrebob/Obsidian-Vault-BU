### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Vesakha Puja reflections on the birth, enlightenment, and parinibbāna of the Buddha: dwelling peacefully in the conditioned world through wisdom.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
On the holy festival of Vesakha Puja, commemorating the Buddha's birth, awakening, and passing, Luang Por Sumedho explores how an enlightened being lives among conditions. The Buddha did not escape into a distant heaven; he lived for 45 years amidst hot weather, backaches, challenging disciples, and political turmoil. Luang Por emphasizes that freedom is not found by annihilating conditions, but by understanding their dependent origination.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The three great milestones of the Buddha's life commemorated at Vesak.
- Living with conditions: the bodily reality of the Buddha after awakening.
- Conditionality (idappaccayatā): understanding that whatever arises must cease.
- Resting in the unconditioned silence while functioning gracefully in the world.
