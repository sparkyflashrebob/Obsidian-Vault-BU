### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A moving memorial eulogy celebrating the life, scholarship, and spiritual dedication of Maurice Walshe (translator of the Dīgha Nikāya).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this intimate 18-minute memorial, Luang Por Sumedho pays tribute to Maurice O'Connell Walshe (1911–1998), the eminent British scholar and translator who gave the English-speaking world the authoritative translation of the Dīgha Nikāya (*Thus Have I Heard: The Long Discourses of the Buddha*). Luang Por honors Walshe not only for his monumental academic translation, but for his profound humility, gentle humor, and lifelong lay devotion to the Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Honoring Maurice Walshe's monumental contribution to Buddhist scholarship in the West.
- The historic importance of the Dīgha Nikāya translation for modern practitioners.
- Walshe's long friendship with the English Sangha and his regular visits to Chithurst and Amaravati.
- The noble transition of a senior Dhamma disciple into peaceful passing.
