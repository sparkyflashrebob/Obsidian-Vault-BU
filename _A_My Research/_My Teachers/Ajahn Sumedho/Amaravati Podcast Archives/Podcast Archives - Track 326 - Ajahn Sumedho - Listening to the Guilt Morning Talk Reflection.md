### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Working skillfully with unconscious guilt, shame, and self-blame through non-judgmental listening and compassionate acceptance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 56-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho addresses one of the most stubborn hindrances in Western practitioners: guilt and self-blame. Conditioned by cultural, religious, and familial expectations, the mind constantly generates feelings of inadequacy and unworthiness. Rather than believing the narrative of guilt or trying to suppress it, Luang Por guides the Sangha to listen to the feeling of guilt as a conditioned emotional vibration—holding it in spacious awareness until it dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cultural conditioning of guilt and sin in Western psychology.
- Distinguishing between moral shame (hiri-ottappa) and neurotic, chronic guilt.
- Listening to somatic contractions in the chest and stomach without buying into the story.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing (Buddho) that is intrinsically free from sin and blame.
