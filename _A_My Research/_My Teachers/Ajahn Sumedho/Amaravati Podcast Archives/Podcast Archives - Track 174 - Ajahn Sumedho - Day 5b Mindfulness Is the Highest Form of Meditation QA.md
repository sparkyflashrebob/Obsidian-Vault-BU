### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Retreat Q&A: clarifying why continuous mindfulness (sati) is superior to striving for temporary altered states of consciousness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute retreat Q&A session, Luang Por Sumedho responds to retreatants asking about deep trance states, altered perceptions, and mystical experiences. He clarifies with gentle authority that mindfulness is the highest form of meditation. Altered states and dramatic visions come and go; bare, wakeful mindfulness of conditions as they are is the only practice that permanently uproots delusion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why chasing mystical or ecstatic states creates spiritual materialism and addiction.
- Mindfulness as ordinary, sober, transparent presence that never decays.
- Handling doubts about whether one is 'making progress' in meditation.
- Reassuring practitioners that simple awareness of an ordinary breath contains the entire Dhamma.
