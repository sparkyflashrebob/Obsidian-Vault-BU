### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A mature 2021 reflection on human purpose, vocation, ethical integrity, and navigating worldly existence with Dhamma wisdom.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 50-minute discourse from July 2021, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a universal existential inquiry: 'What should I do with my life?' In modern culture, life is often measured purely by financial success, career prestige, and sensory consumption. Luang Por reminds practitioners that a genuinely meaningful human life is founded on three pillars: harmlessness (Sīla), generous service (Dāna), and inner awakening (Bhāvanā). When life is aligned with Dhamma, every circumstance becomes an opportunity for spiritual growth.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The emptiness of purely materialistic and career-driven life goals.
- The highest blessings (Mangala) as outlined by the Buddha.
- Cultivating right livelihood (Sammā-ājīva) and ethical integrity.
- Finding purpose in serving others while maintaining inner detachment.
