### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The spiritual power of dedicating merit (pattidāna), sacrificial devotion, and consecrating daily action to the Triple Gem.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this Kathina-season discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the ancient practice of offering and sharing merit. Modern rationalists often dismiss merit-making as primitive superstition. Luang Por reveals that true dedication transforms personal ambition into universal service. When we offer our energy, time, and meditative peace for the welfare of ancestors, living beings, and the Dhamma, the claustrophobic ego dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The psychology of offering: shifting from consumerist taking to devotional giving.
- The traditional practice of sharing merit (pattidāna) with departed relatives.
- Dedication of practice as a protective shield against spiritual pride and cynicism.
- Consecrating ordinary chores and monastery upkeep as sacred offerings.
