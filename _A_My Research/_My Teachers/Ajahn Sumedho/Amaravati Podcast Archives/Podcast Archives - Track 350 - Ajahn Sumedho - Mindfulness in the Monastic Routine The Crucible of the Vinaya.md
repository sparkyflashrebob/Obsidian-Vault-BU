### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How the strict daily schedule and communal monastic routine of the Thai Forest Tradition function as an external womb of mindfulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 32-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho discusses the spiritual genius of the monastic routine. From rising at 4:00 AM, chanting morning pūjā, sweeping the grounds, eating one meal a day from an almsbowl, to evening meditation, the routine strips away personal preferences and choice. Luang Por explains that rather than being an oppressive cage, the Vinaya and monastic discipline provide an external structure that frees the mind from egoic hesitation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Sekha Sutta: the comprehensive training of the disciple in morality, sensory restraint, and wakefulness.
- Surrendering personal preference: why freedom is not having endless choices.
- The daily rhythm of Amaravati: bells, chanting, almsround, work periods, and silent sittings.
- How routine eliminates neurotic decision fatigue and reveals the stillness within structure.
