### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the morning of Day 4 during a silent meditation retreat, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the classic mid-retreat obstacles: restlessness, emotional memories, physical stiffness, and subtle doubt. He instructs retreatants to cut through mental fatigue by taking refuge directly in the unshakeable posture of Buddho—the wakeful knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why Day 4 is often the emotional turning point where subconscious material breaks through.
- Using the mantra 'Bud-dho' coordinated with in-and-out breath to steady a wandering mind.
- Meeting physical knee and back pain as bare physical sensation rather than an emotional enemy.
- Reassuring retreatants that noticing one's chaos is the proof of awakening, not failure.
