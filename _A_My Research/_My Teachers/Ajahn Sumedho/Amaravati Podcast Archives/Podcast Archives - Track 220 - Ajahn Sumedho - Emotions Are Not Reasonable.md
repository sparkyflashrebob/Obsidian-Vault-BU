### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Understanding the fundamentally non-rational nature of emotions and ceasing the futile demand that feelings make logical sense.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho offers liberating insight into the emotional life. Intellectual people suffer tremendously because they demand that their emotions be reasonable. When sadness, anxiety, or irritation arises without a logical cause, they torture themselves trying to 'figure out why'. Luang Por points out that emotions are biological waves, not rational mathematical equations. Stop demanding that feelings make sense; simply feel them as they are and let them go.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The intellectual error of trying to solve emotional states with logical analysis.
- Emotions as saṅkhāras: conditioned, unpredictable weather patterns of the organism.
- The somatic approach: feeling emotional pressure in the throat, chest, and stomach.
- The relief of disidentification: you don't have to explain or justify what you feel.
