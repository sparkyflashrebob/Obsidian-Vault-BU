### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound monastic ordination discourse exploring Pabbajjā (Going Forth) as a deliberate, joyful embrace of radical uncertainty.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this inspiring ordination discourse, Luang Por Sumedho speaks to candidates taking the monastic vows of Pabbajjā (Going Forth from home to homelessness). In worldly life, every institution is designed to manufacture predictable security—pensions, mortgages, career ladders. Monastic ordination is the exact opposite: stepping voluntarily into absolute uncertainty, relying entirely on almsfood, simple robes, and the generosity of the world.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The meaning of Pabbajjā: going forth from conventional security into spiritual homelessness.
- Living on alms: total economic vulnerability as the foundation of inner sovereignty.
- The monastic precepts (Vinaya) as a vessel designed to sail on the open sea of uncertainty.
- Discovering the only real refuge: not a guaranteed future, but an awakened mind.
