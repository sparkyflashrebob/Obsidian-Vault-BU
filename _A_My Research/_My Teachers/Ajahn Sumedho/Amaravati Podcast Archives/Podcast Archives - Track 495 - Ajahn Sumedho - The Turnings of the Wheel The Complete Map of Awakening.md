### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Masterclass on the three turnings (tiparivaṭṭa) and twelve aspects of the Four Noble Truths: transforming theoretical doctrine into direct living liberation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 54-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho provides a brilliant exposition of the Buddha's first discourse: the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta. The Buddha did not merely list four truths; he turned the wheel of truth through three distinct stages (*tiparivaṭṭa*): theoretical recognition (*sacca-ñāṇa*), understanding the specific duty (*kicca-ñāṇa*), and direct completion of the duty (*kata-ñāṇa*). Luang Por demonstrates how practicing all twelve aspects leads to full, irreversible awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The three turnings (tiparivaṭṭa): Sacca-ñāṇa, Kicca-ñāṇa, Kata-ñāṇa.
- The twelve aspects applied to the Four Noble Truths.
- Why modern Buddhism gets stuck in the first turning (theoretical understanding).
- The completion of the third turning: 'Suffering has been fully understood; craving has been abandoned; cessation has been realized; the path has been developed.'
