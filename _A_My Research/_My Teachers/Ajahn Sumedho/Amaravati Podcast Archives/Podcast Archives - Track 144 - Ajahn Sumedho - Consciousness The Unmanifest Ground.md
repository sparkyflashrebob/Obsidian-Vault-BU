### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating consciousness (viññāṇa) beyond sensory objects: the unmanifest, infinite luminous knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 69-minute deep contemplation, Luang Por Sumedho explores the profound sutta teaching on 'viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ anantaṁ sabbato pabhaṁ' (consciousness that is unmanifest, infinite, and luminous in every direction). When consciousness is untethered from objects, thoughts, and self-referential narratives, it reveals itself as the vast, timeless ground of being.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the famous canonical riddle of unmanifest consciousness in MN 49 and DN 11.
- How sensory consciousness becomes imprisoned when it identifies exclusively with mortal bodily forms.
- The inner sound of silence (nāda) as a direct auditory indicator of this luminous space.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that was never born and cannot die.
