### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The famous canonical equation: 'He who sees the Dhamma sees me; he who sees me sees the Dhamma.'

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in February 2021 during his 86th year, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the profound declaration of the Buddha to the dying monk Vakkali. Awakening is not worshiping the mortal form of a teacher; it is directly seeing the conditionality of all things (Dhamma) and resting as the unconditioned knower (Buddha).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the story of Venerable Vakkali and the Buddha's admonition against bodily attachment.
- How 'Being the Buddha' means embodying the witnessing consciousness that knows conditions without grasping.
- How 'Seeing the Dhamma' means observing the arising and ceasing of the five aggregates.
- The timeless unity of Buddha and Dhamma in our immediate contemplative experience.
