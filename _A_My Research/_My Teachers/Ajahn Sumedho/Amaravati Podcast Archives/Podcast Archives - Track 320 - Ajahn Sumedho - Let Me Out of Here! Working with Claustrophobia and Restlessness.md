### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the desperate panic of 'Let me out of here!': working with physical confinement, emotional boredom, and retreat claustrophobia.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho shines a humorous, penetrating light on the universal panic cry: 'Let me out of here!' Whether sitting in an uncomfortable meditation hall, trapped in a difficult marriage, enduring a boring meeting, or feeling sick in bed, the ego screams to escape. Luang Por shows that running away to a new place never cures claustrophobia, because you take the running mind with you! The only real escape is turning around and relaxing directly into the confinement.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The anatomy of restlessness (uddhacca) and the panic impulse to flee.
- How running away from difficult situations creates an addictive habit of avoidance.
- Somatic softening: feeling the tight panic knot in the stomach and breathing through it.
- Discovering the boundless freedom of awareness that can never be imprisoned.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por tells of being in a tiny, windowless monk's kuti in Thailand during the monsoon season, feeling intense claustrophobic terror, wanting to smash the door down, until he sat on the floor, took a deep breath, and realized: 'The kuti isn't small; my clinging mind is small!'
