- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deepening meditative surrender: allowing the self-conscious identity to dissolve into pure spacious stillness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A deeply poetic and contemplative 52-minute discourse on total surrender. Luang Por Sumedho guides the assembly past the initial stages of concentration into the profound dissolution of ego boundaries. When we stop defending our self-image, asserting opinions, and managing experience, the entire fabricated structure of personality melts like a salt doll into the ocean of silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The fatigue of constantly maintaining the performance of being 'somebody'.
- The courage required to let go of control and dissolve into the unknown.
- Emptiness (Suññatā) not as bleak annihilation, but as radiant, unburdened freedom.
- Resting as the unconditioned silence in which birth and death are merely passing ripples.
