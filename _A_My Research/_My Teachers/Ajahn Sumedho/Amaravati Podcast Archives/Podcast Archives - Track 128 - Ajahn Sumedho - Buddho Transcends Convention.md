### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The witnessing position (Buddho) as the unconditioned reality transcending all religious and social conventions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 77-minute master discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores how the awakening consciousness (Buddho) transcends all conventional categories—gender, nationality, social status, and even Buddhism itself. Conventions are useful tools for living together in society, but the one who knows (Buddho) belongs to no race, no religion, and no time.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Distinguishing between the convention of 'Buddhism' and the living reality of 'Buddho'.
- How identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) attaches to religious labels, creating spiritual tribalism.
- The transcendent nature of the witnessing position: knowing conventions without being trapped by them.
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that was never born an American, a Briton, or a monk.
