### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Unmasking spiritual ignorance (avijjā) and delusion (moha) as the root architects of worldly suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a piercing investigation into delusion (moha). Greed and anger are easily recognized because of their fiery heat, but delusion is subtle, sleepy, and pervasive. It is the fundamental assumption that we are separate entities existing independently inside bodies. Luang Por exposes how delusion constructs elaborate narratives to maintain the ego, and how simple bare attention blows away the fog of ignorance.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Avijjā: not intellectual stupidity, but fundamental spiritual blindness to the Four Truths.
- How delusion masquerades as common sense and practical worldly ambition.
- The trance of identification: mistaking passing moods for one's authentic self.
- Waking up from the dream: using inquiry and alertness to pierce the illusion.
