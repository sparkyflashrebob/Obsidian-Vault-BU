### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the vast chasm between the conditioned realm of saṅkhāras and the deathless realm of Nibbāna.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of Day 3 of a 2017 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the Buddha's revelation of the Unborn, Uncreated, and Unformed (Udāna 8.3). Everything in the sensory realm is born, decays, and dies; seeking permanent security in bodies, relationships, or wealth is tragic folly. True refuge is found in the Deathless (Amata) that is immediately accessible in pure awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The famous Udāna passage: 'There is, monks, an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned...'
- Why the fear of death haunts human beings who identify exclusively with the physical form.
- The practice of inclining the mind toward Nibbāna (nibbāna-dhātu) in daily sitting.
- Discovering that what was never born can never die.
