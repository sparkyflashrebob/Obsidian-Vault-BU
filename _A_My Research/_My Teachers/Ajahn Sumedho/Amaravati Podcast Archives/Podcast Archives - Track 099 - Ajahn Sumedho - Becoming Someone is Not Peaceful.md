### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The chronic exhaustion of 'becoming somebody' (bhava) versus the supreme peace of non-becoming (bhava-nirodha).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the silent winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho presents an extraordinary contemplation on 'becoming' (bhava). Society conditions us from childhood to become somebody—a successful professional, an admired person, and even a 'great enlightened meditator'. Luang Por shows that all becoming is intrinsically fraught, stressful, and insecure. Freedom is discovering who we are when we stop trying to become anything.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the psychic mechanism of 'becoming' (bhava) in Dependent Origination.
- How spiritual ambition turns the Dhamma into another arena for ego-aggrandizement.
- The freedom of non-becoming (bhava-nirodha): resting in unconditioned awareness.
- Embracing the posture of the simple nobody who is content to sweep the floor.
