### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Realizing liberation right here and now: abandoning the spiritual future and resting in the timeless reality of present knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 73-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho delivers an uncompromising exhortation to stop postponing enlightenment to some distant future retreat or lifetime. Spiritual seeking often becomes another trap of becoming (bhava-taṇhā), where the mind is endlessly chasing future attainments. By waking up to 'This Is It!'—the sensory and mental immediacy of the present moment—we discover that Nibbāna is not a place to reach, but the unconditioned space of knowing within which all conditions arise and cease.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The spiritual trap of becoming (bhava): chasing future states of jhana and enlightenment.
- The Bāhiya Sutta instruction: in the seen, only the seen; in the heard, only the heard.
- The unconditioned presence of the 'One Who Knows' (Buddho).
- Resting in the sound of silence as an immediate gateway out of conceptual entrapment.
