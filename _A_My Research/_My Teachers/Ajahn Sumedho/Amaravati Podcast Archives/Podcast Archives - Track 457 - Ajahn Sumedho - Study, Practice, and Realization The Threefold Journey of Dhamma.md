### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Synthesizing the three foundational pillars of the Buddhist path: theoretical study (pariyatti), meditative practice (paṭipatti), and direct realization (paṭivedha).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this comprehensive 62-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the harmonious balance between the three stages of Dhamma: *Pariyatti* (scriptural learning), *Paṭipatti* (meditative practice), and *Paṭivedha* (direct realization). Scholastic academics often get trapped in study without practice, while anti-intellectual meditators wander blindly without scripture. Luang Por shows how wise study maps the terrain, faithful practice walks the path, and direct realization tastes the fruit.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The classic threefold division: Pariyatti, Paṭipatti, Paṭivedha.
- The trap of scholasticism: mistaking the menu for the meal.
- The trap of blind practice: meditating without Right View or ethical foundations.
- Direct realization: when the truths of the suttas are verified in one's own living heart.
