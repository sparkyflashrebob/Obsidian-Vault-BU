### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating self-constructed psychological traps of guilt, perfectionism, and ideological certainty.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho investigates the unconscious traps through which practitioners torture themselves: impossible standards of spiritual perfection, lingering guilt over past mistakes, and rigid dogmatism. Through gentle humorous self-observation, he shows how seeing these tendencies clearly releases their grip.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the inner critic and the habit of measuring oneself against idealized images of enlightenment.
- Examining how monastic rules (Vinaya) can be distorted into tools for judging others or feeling inadequate.
- Recognizing that feelings of unworthiness or superiority are both rooted in the illusion of self (sakkāya-diṭṭhi).
- Resting in the unconditioned knowing that requires no validation from perfectionist thought.
