### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The anatomy of the awakened mind: effortless clarity, unentangled knowing, and natural compassion.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute masterwork, Luang Por Sumedho provides a comprehensive exposition on the awakened mind (Buddho). Awakening is not an esoteric, hypnotic trance state; it is the natural, sober, and transparent functioning of consciousness when freed from the distorting filters of self-interest, fear, and delusion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Demystifying enlightenment: awakening is ordinary presence purified of grasping.
- How the awakened mind relates to physical pain: feeling sensation without creating an owner of the pain.
- The spontaneous emergence of compassion and moral clarity when the ego-defense mechanisms collapse.
- Practicing living from the awakened position throughout ordinary daily activities.
