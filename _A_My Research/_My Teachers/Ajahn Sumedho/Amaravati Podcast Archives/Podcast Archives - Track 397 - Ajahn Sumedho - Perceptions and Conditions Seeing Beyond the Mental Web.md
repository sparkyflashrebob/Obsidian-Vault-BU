### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How conditioned perceptions bind consciousness, and how resting in bare awareness dissolves the web of suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the interconnected web of perceptions and conditions. Every perception we have is conditioned by our biological senses, language, memories, and emotions. When we cling to these conditioned perceptions as absolute truth, we clash with everyone whose conditions differ. Luang Por demonstrates how waking up to the conditioned nature of all views unlocks universal compassion and peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The relativity of conditioned perceptions: no two human beings perceive the same world.
- The tragedy of ideological and religious warfare: fighting over conditioned concepts.
- Using the Three Characteristics (Tilakkhaṇa) to see through all perceptual illusions.
- Abiding as the unconditioned witness beyond all conditioned mental webs.
