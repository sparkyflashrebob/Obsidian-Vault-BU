### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Death contemplation (maraṇānussati) as the most potent wake-up call from worldly complacency.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
A piercing Sunday evening discourse reminding practitioners that every worldly attachment carries within it the seed of death. Everything created, bought, loved, or pursued in the worldly realm ends in dissolution. Embracing this truth does not induce morbid depression, but wakes the mind up to the deathless Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating mindfulness of death (maraṇānussati) as a supreme spiritual priority recommended by the Buddha.
- Examining how modern culture goes to absurd lengths to conceal aging, decay, and death behind cosmetic illusions.
- Seeing that worldly pleasure is fundamentally tragic because it cannot be sustained.
- Discovering the fearless joy of the Deathless (Amata) when identification with the mortal vehicle is abandoned.
