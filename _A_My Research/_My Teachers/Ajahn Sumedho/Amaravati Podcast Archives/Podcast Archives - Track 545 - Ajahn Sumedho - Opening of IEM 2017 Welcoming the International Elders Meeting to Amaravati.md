### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this concise 5-minute archival address from May 2017, Luang Por Sumedho opens the International Elders Meeting (IEM) at Amaravati. Gathering senior abbots, elders, and monastics from branch monasteries across Europe, the Americas, Australasia, and Thailand, Luang Por expresses deep joy and reverence for the enduring vitality of the Forest Tradition. He invokes the Buddha's teachings on Sangha harmony (Aparihāniya-dhamma), celebrating the spirit of mutual counsel, shared Vinaya, and spiritual friendship that preserves the Dhamma across generations.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The significance of the International Elders Meeting in the Ajahn Chah lineage.
- The Aparihāniya-dhamma: meeting frequently and departing in harmony.
- Reflecting on the global expansion of the Forest Tradition over forty years.
- The enduring bond of spiritual brotherhood and mutual support among monastic elders.
