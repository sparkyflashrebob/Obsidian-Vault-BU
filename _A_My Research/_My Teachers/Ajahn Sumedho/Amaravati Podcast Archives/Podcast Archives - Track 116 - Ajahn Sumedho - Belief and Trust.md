### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The crucial distinction between rigid dogmatic belief and open, intuitive spiritual trust (saddhā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 60-minute discourse from June 2021, Luang Por Sumedho investigates the vital difference between belief and trust. Belief is of the head: a rigid set of opinions, doctrines, and dogmas that people fight and die for. Trust (saddhā) is of the heart: an intuitive, courageous openness to reality, trusting the unconditioned truth even when the intellect cannot formulate answers.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Why dogmatic religious belief often breeds intolerance, fanaticism, and sectarian conflict.
- The Kālāma Sutta teaching: do not go upon hearsay, tradition, or authority, but investigate for yourself.
- Cultivating Saddhā: trust in the capacity of awareness to know truth and navigate life skillfully.
- The courage to rest in 'not-knowing' without needing intellectual crutches.
