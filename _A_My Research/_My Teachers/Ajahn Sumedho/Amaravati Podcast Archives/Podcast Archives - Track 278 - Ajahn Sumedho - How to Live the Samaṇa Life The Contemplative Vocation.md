### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The noble fruits and daily discipline of the Samaṇa (spiritual wanderer): living lightly upon the earth with contentment and wakefulness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho expounds upon the life of the *Samaṇa*—the quiet seeker who renounces worldly competition to cultivate the heart. Grounded in the Samaṇaphala Sutta (The Fruits of the Contemplative Life), Luang Por explains that a true samaṇa is not defined merely by shaved heads or robes, but by an internal posture of peaceful non-contention, gentle restraint, and boundless contentment with few requisites.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Samaṇaphala Sutta: visible fruits of monastic life from blamelessness to liberation.
- The meaning of Samaṇa: one who calms the defilements and lives in harmlessness (ahiṁsā).
- Living on the offerings of others with radical humility, gratitude, and purity.
- How lay practitioners can embody the samaṇa spirit within domestic life.
