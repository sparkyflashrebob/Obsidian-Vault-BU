### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The seamless harmony of concentration (samatha) and insight (vipassanā): how wise seeing generates profound stillness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 57-minute retreat talk, Luang Por Sumedho heals the artificial divide between Samatha (tranquility) and Vipassanā (insight). Many meditators believe they must first attain deep trance-like concentration before they can begin insight practice. Luang Por shows that when we observe impermanence and not-self directly, the mind naturally settles into effortless, unshakeable samādhi.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Yuganaddha Sutta: developing tranquility and insight in tandem (yuganaddha).
- Why forced concentration often creates rigid suppression, headaches, and rebound restlessness.
- The natural stillness that arises spontaneously when grasping and aversion cease.
- Vipassanā-samādhi: the stable, luminous calm that can operate amidst daily sounds and movement.
