### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The annual robe-offering celebration as the joyful heartbeat of monastic-lay interdependence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the sacred meaning of Kathina. The festival marks the conclusion of the three-month Rains Retreat (Vassa). Lay supporters offer cloth to the Sangha, and the monks sew and dye the robe on the same day. Luang Por reminds the community that Kathina is not a commercial ritual, but an ancient celebration of selflessness, trust, and loving-kindness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Kathina Cloth**: The symbolic simplicity of the single sewn robe.
- **Interdependence of Lay and Monastic**: How material support and spiritual guidance nourish each other.
- **Dāna as Joy**: Experiencing the light, unburdened heart of generosity.
