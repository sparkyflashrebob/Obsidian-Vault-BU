### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A profound 2021 discourse exposing the fabricated nature of identity: realizing that our personality and self-concept are purely imagination.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute discourse from March 2021, Luang Por Sumedho delivers a luminous teaching on non-self (anattā). We spend decades defending, evaluating, and suffering over our 'self'—our personality traits, our past successes, our failures, our self-worth. Yet when we look closely with quiet mindfulness, we discover that this 'self' is entirely a product of mental imagination (saññā and saṅkhāra). It has no independent reality. Waking up from this imagined character is the greatest relief a human being can experience.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The five aggregates (khandhas) as the building blocks of imagined identity.
- How memory and imagination weave the continuous illusion of an enduring ego.
- The freedom of realizing: 'I am not my personality, my history, or my thoughts.'
- Resting in the unconditioned space of Buddho where identity dissolves into peace.
