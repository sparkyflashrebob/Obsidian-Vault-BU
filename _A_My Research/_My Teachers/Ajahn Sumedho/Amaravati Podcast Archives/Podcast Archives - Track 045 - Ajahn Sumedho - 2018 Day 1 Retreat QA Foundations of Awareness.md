### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this opening Q&A session of a 2018 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho answers intimate practice questions from retreatants struggling with restlessness, obsessive thoughts, posture difficulties, and anxiety about whether they are meditating correctly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the common mistake of trying to empty the mind of thoughts by brute willpower.
- Working skillfully with painful knees, backaches, and posture fatigue during long retreat sittings.
- Using the mantra 'Buddho' and the inner sound of silence as practical, gentle anchors.
- Reassuring retreatants that noticing one's monkey mind is the beginning of wisdom, not a failure.
