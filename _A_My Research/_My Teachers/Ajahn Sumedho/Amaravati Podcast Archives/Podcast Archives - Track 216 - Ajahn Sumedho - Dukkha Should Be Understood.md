### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The foundational duty of the First Noble Truth: pariññeyya—dukkha is to be fully comprehended, not suppressed or fled from.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 52-minute master exposition, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the precise imperative of the First Noble Truth. The Buddha did not say 'suffering should be avoided', 'suffering should be cured with drugs', or 'suffering should be sugar-coated with positive thinking'. He said: *Pariññeyyaṁ*—dukkha is to be fully understood. When we turn around and welcome suffering with compassionate presence, its underlying delusion dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The instinctual human reflex to flee, numb, or suppress unpleasant experiences.
- The First Turning: Dukkha as a noble truth, not an embarrassing personal failure.
- The duty of Pariññeyya: turning toward discomfort with radical somatic curiosity.
- How fully comprehending suffering naturally severs the craving that creates it.
