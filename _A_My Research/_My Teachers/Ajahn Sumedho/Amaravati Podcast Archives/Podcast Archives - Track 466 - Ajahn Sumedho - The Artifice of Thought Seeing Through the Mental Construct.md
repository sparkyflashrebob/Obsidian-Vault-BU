### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the artificial, fabricated nature of conceptual thought: liberating consciousness from the hypnotic spell of words and concepts.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this brilliant 66-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the artificial nature of thought. Human beings mistake thoughts for reality: we believe that our words, judgments, theories, and concepts ARE the world. Luang Por points out that thought is merely an evolutionary biological tool—a useful artificial construct like a hammer or a fork. When we see the artificial nature of thought, its power to torment us vanishes instantly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Vitakkasaṇṭhāna Sutta: five skillful strategies for stilling distracting thoughts.
- The difference between reality itself and our conceptual mental models of reality.
- How words create artificial dualisms: self vs. other, good vs. bad, success vs. failure.
- Resting in the unarticulated knowing space that precedes thought.
