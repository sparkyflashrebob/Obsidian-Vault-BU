### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in October 2001 shortly after the terrorist attacks of September 11, Luang Por Sumedho addresses a stunned global community. With world leaders beating the drums of war and public discourse engulfed in terror and vengeance, Luang Por provides an unshakeable moral anchor. He points out that hatred cannot cease through hatred; escalating retaliation only multiplies grief. In the midst of collective shock, the only true sanctuary is the Ground Zero of our own unconditioned awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Dhammapada verse: 'Hatred is never appeased by hatred; through non-hatred alone is hatred appeased.'
- Deconstructing the collective cycle of fear, vengeance, and geopolitical escalation.
- How to hold immense grief and national shock without succumbing to demonic hatred.
- Ground Zero as the zero point of consciousness: returning to the stillness prior to political division.
