### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The prime link of Dependent Origination: 'With ignorance as condition, mental formations arise.'

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this deep 57-minute late-period exposition from July 2021, Luang Por Sumedho examines the root of the twelve links of Dependent Origination: 'Avijjā paccayā saṅkhārā' (Dependent on spiritual ignorance, karmic formations arise). By replacing ignorance with real-time awareness (vijjā), the entire causal chain of suffering is dismantled at its foundation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Defining Avijjā: not a lack of worldly information, but ignorance of the unconditioned space of knowing.
- How unconscious beliefs and karmic habits (saṅkhāras) drive compulsive emotional reactions.
- The power of mindful presence in interrupting the automatic birth of suffering.
- Abiding as the pure, unconditioned knowing that was never tainted by ignorance.
