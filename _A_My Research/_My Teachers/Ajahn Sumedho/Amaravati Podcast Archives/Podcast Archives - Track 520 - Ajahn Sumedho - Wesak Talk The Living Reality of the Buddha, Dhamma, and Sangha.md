### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Traditional Wesak discourse exploring the Triple Gem not as abstract dogmatic symbols, but as living, experiential realities.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 59-minute Wesak talk, Luang Por Sumedho explores the profound meaning of taking refuge in the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha. Taking refuge is not an act of blind religious conversion; it is an intelligent, deliberate realignment of consciousness. The Buddha is our capacity for awareness; the Dhamma is the truth of things as they are; the Sangha is the lineage of those who live skillfully. In honoring Wesak, we recommit ourselves to this tripartite refuge of liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The Triple Gem (Ti-ratana) as an experiential reality rather than religious dogma.
- The Buddha as the unconditioned 'One Who Knows' (Buddho).
- The Dhamma as the natural law of impermanence, suffering, and non-self.
- The Sangha as the harmonious field of virtuous and awakened beings.
