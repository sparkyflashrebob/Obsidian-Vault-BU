### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Evening meditation instructions: releasing the momentum of the day into the stillness of awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during a retreat in September 1998, Luang Por Sumedho provides evening guidance for weary meditators. After a long day of sitting and walking, physical fatigue and mental restlessness often peak in the evening. Luang Por invites practitioners to drop all strivings to achieve something special, allowing the body and mind to completely rest in the unconditioned ground.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Releasing Evening Fatigue**: Allowing tiredness to be an object of awareness without complaint.
- **The Cessation of Seeking**: Letting go of the ambition to reach a high meditative state before bed.
- **The Sound of Silence in the Dark**: Using the inner listening tone to steady consciousness.
