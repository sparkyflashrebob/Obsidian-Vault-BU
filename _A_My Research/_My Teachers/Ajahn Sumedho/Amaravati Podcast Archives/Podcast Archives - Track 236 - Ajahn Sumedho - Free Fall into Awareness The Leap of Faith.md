### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The exhilarating spiritual surrender of letting go of control and free-falling into pure unconditioned presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this dynamic 52-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho compares meditation to skydiving. Meditators cling desperately to the airplane door of their thoughts, neuroses, and identities, terrified of the drop. Luang Por urges practitioners to take the leap: let go of trying to control your mind, let go of holding yourself together, and free-fall into bare awareness. Awareness is the parachute that is already open; it catch you completely.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The exhaustion of chronic psychic clutching: holding yourself together.
- The Ogha-taraṇa Sutta: crossing the flood by neither standing still nor straining.
- The radical leap of faith (Saddhā) from conceptual control into naked knowing.
- Discovering that awareness cannot fall, crash, or be broken.
