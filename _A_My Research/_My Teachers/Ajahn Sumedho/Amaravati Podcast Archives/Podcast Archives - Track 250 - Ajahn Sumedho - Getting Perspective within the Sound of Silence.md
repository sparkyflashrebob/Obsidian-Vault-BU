### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Using the Sound of Silence to gain immediate psychological perspective on emotional entanglements, worries, and crises.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 41-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho demonstrates how the Sound of Silence serves as a supreme perspective-giver. When we are caught in an emotional crisis or financial worry, our consciousness contracts until the problem seems to fill the entire universe. Luang Por shows how tuning into the nāda sound instantly restores cosmic perspective: the problem shrinks into a tiny ripple in the vast ocean of silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The tunnel vision of emotional obsession: mistaking a temporary dilemma for total reality.
- Tuning into the sound of silence to expand conscious aperture and restore perspective.
- How background stillness contextualizes and neutralizes foreground panic.
- Skillful problem-solving arising from spacious calm rather than desperate urgency.
