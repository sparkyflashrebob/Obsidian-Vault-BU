### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How the mind weaves a chronic waking dream of ego identity, and how mindfulness serves as the morning alarm waking us to reality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 70-minute morning reflection, Luang Por Sumedho compares ordinary human life to a continuous waking dream. We dream that we are important, flawed, victimized, or superior. When night dreams occur, they feel completely real until we wake up. In the same way, spiritual awakening (*bodhi*) is waking up from the dream of personality into the light of unconditioned awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The waking dream: how thinking fabricates an ongoing narrative of selfhood.
- Personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) as the central character in the psychological dream.
- Using daily mindfulness as the gentle awakening bell in the Temple.
- Resting as the awakened one (Buddho) who knows the dream without being trapped in it.
