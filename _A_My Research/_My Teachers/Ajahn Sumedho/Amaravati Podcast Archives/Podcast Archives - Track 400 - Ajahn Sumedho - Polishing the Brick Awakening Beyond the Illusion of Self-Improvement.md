### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Masterful 80-minute discourse on the famous Zen koan of polishing a brick to make a mirror: why meditation cannot manufacture Buddhahood because awareness is already pure.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this landmark 80-minute discourse celebrating the 400th track milestone, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the famous story of Master Mazu and his teacher Nanyue Huairang. Mazu was meditating cross-legged day and night to become a Buddha. Huairang picked up a roof tile and began vigorously polishing it with a stone. When Mazu asked what he was doing, Huairang replied: 'I am polishing this tile to make a mirror.' Mazu exclaimed: 'No matter how much you polish a tile, it will never become a mirror!' Huairang replied: 'And no matter how much you sit cross-legged, you will never become a Buddha!' Luang Por reveals that meditation does not create awakening; it reveals the uncreated mirror of knowing that is already pristine.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The famous Chan dialogue between Mazu Daoyi and Nanyue Huairang.
- The delusion of spiritual manufacturing: trying to polish a defiled ego into an enlightened Buddha.
- Why sitting cross-legged without wisdom is just 'polishing the brick'.
- Awakening as resting in the intrinsically pure, unconditioned nature of consciousness (Pabhassara Citta).
