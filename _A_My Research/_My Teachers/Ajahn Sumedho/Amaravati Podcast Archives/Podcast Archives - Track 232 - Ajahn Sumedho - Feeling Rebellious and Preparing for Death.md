### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this candid, delightful reflection from 2020, Venerable Ajahn Sumedho looks back over his life at age 86. He reflects on his early American rebellious nature—rebelling against social conformity, the military, and expectations—and how that rebellious energy was transformed into monastic renunciation. Now in the twilight of life, Luang Por prepares for death with radiant cheerfulness, resting in the Deathless awareness that was never born.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Transforming rebellious ego defiance into noble monastic renunciation.
- Maraṇānussati (mindfulness of death) as the supreme meditation practice for elders.
- Letting go of physical beauty, physical agility, and worldly reputation.
- The joy of having nothing left to prove: resting in the Unborn.
