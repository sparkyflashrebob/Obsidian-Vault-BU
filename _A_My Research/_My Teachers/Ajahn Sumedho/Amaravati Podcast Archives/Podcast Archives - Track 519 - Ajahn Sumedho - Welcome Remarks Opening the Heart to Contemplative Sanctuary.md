### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In these 5-minute welcome remarks from May 2017, Luang Por Sumedho warmly greets retreatants arriving at Amaravati. He welcomes everyone into the peaceful sanctuary of the monastery, encouraging them to lay down the heavy luggage of their worldly concerns, identities, and anxieties at the monastery door. By setting a pure intention for self-discovery and kindness, the gathering becomes a fertile field for the realization of Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The monastery as a sacred sanctuary of peace and refuge.
- Laying down the burden of worldly roles, careers, and responsibilities.
- Establishing the foundational aspiration of harmlessness and wakefulness.
- The blessing of practicing together in spiritual community (Sangha).
