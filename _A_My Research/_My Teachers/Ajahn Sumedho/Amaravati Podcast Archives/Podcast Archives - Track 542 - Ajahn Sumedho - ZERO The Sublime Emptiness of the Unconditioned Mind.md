### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A radical discourse on Emptiness (Suññatā): reducing the ego to ZERO and discovering the boundless fullness of unconditioned peace.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 64-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho presents one of his most striking and memorable metaphors: 'ZERO.' The world teaches us to be a 'Number One'—a winner, an achiever, a famous somebody. In contrast, the spiritual path of the Buddha is the art of becoming ZERO. Zero has no weight; zero cannot be insulted; zero cannot be defeated; zero takes up no room and yet contains everything. When the mind surrenders all pretensions of becoming and returns to zero, it enters the sublime peace of Suññatā (Emptiness).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The cultural obsession with becoming 'Number One' versus the peace of becoming 'Zero'.
- The profound teachings of the Cūḷasuññata Sutta on gradual entry into emptiness.
- How becoming Zero immunizes the heart against worldly praise and blame.
- Resting in the unconditioned void where all suffering naturally ceases.
