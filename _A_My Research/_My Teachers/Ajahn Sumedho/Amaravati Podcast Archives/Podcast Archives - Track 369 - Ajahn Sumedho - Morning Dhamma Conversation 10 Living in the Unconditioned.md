### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Concluding winter retreat dialogue: stepping out of intensive retreat into daily monastery life while anchored in the Unconditioned.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this culminating 44-minute dialogue concluding the winter retreat series, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the transition back into active monastic and daily responsibilities. The true test of retreat practice is not whether we can sit silently for two hours, but whether awareness remains rooted in the Unconditioned (*Asaṅkhata*) when phones ring, visitors arrive, and duties resume. Luang Por reminds everyone that the Unconditioned has no boundary.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Bridging the divide between formal retreat sanctuary and everyday activity.
- The Unconditioned (Asaṅkhata) as ever-present reality that is not dependent on quiet rooms.
- Using daily chores and interpersonal encounters to test the stability of mindfulness.
- Carrying the perfume of winter silence into service for the community.
