### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The contemplative spirit of inquiry: awakening the bojjhaṅga of Dhamma investigation (dhammavicaya).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this concentrated 24-minute opening retreat talk from 2020, Luang Por Sumedho sets the spiritual tone for the retreat. Meditation is not a passive hypnotic trance or an endurance test of sitting rigid; it is an active, joyful endeavor to investigate reality. By activating Dhammavicaya (investigation of Dhamma), every breath, sound, and mood becomes a fascinating field of discovery.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Activating Dhammavicaya: the second factor of enlightenment.
- Approaching meditation with the curiosity of an explorer rather than the grim duty of a soldier.
- Investigating how thoughts appear out of silence, linger, and dissolve.
- The liberating joy of discovering the selfless nature of all phenomena.
