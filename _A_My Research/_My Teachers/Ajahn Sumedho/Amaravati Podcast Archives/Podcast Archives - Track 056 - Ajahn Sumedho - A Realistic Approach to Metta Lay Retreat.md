### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, retreatants, and podcast listeners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying loving-kindness (mettā) as unconditional non-aversion and spacious acceptance rather than forced sentimentality.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during an autumn lay retreat, Luang Por Sumedho offers a refreshing, grounded perspective on Mettā. Loving-kindness is not about manufacturing artificial romantic affection for enemies or suppressing irritation; it is the courageous willingness to allow people, bodily aches, and emotional flaws to exist without projecting malice.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Debunking the guilt meditators feel when they cannot generate warm feelings during loving-kindness practice.
- Defining Mettā as radical patience and non-aversion: allowing reality to be as it is without ill will.
- Starting with oneself: forgiving our personal neurotic habits, physical stiffness, and lingering memories.
- Extending spacious non-resistance outward to difficult colleagues, annoying sounds, and global turmoil.
