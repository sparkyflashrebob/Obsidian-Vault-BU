### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A landmark milestone discourse investigating the illusion of self (Anattā): hunting for the ego and discovering pure emptiness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this monumental 60-minute milestone talk (Track 300), Venerable Ajahn Sumedho leads an uncompromising inquiry into the nature of self. Where is this 'I' that suffers, worries, and seeks enlightenment? Look for it! Is it the body? The body is skin and bone. Is it feelings? Feelings arise and fade. Is it thoughts? Thoughts come and go like chatter. Is it memory? Memory is fiction. When we search with laser-sharp inquiry, no solid self can ever be found: there is only the vast, radiant silence of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The great contemplative hunt: searching directly for the owner of experience.
- The Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta: five aggregates systematically investigated and relinquished.
- The relief of non-self: you don't have to improve, protect, or enlighten an ego that never existed.
- Resting in the unconditioned truth: 'Form is like a lump of foam; feeling like a bubble; awareness is free.'
