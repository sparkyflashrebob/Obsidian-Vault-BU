### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Winter retreat guidance on releasing the grim burden of spiritual work and treating meditation as a joyful inner sanctuary.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the austere cold of winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho softens the monastic intensity with his teaching of the 'Holiday for the Heart'. Retreatants often approach meditation like coal miners going down into a dark pit—clenching teeth and laboring under a grim burden of spiritual ambition. Luang Por reminds them that meditation is a sanctuary of release. Let the retreat be a holiday where you lay down all your heavy baggage.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Overcoming the grim asceticism that turns meditation into painful drudgery.
- Releasing the burden of trying to 'get' samādhi or 'attain' Nibbāna.
- The healing power of somatic softening in cold weather: relaxing the spine and breath.
- Resting in the painless Knower that needs nothing to be complete.
