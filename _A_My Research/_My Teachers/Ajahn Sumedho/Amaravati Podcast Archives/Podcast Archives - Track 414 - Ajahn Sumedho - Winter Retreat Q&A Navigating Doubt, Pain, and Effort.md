### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Intimate winter retreat question and answer session: resolving doubts about progress, handling severe back pain, and tuning Right Effort.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this lively 55-minute Q&A session from the 2002 winter retreat, Luang Por Sumedho answers candid inquiries from resident monastics and retreatants. Topics include what to do when meditation feels like a complete failure, how to manage acute physical pain during long sittings without injuring the body, and how to stop doubting whether one has the spiritual capacity to awaken. Luang Por offers grounded, humorous, and deeply compassionate remedies.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Deconstructing the feeling that 'my meditation is going backwards'.
- Somatic strategies for acute back and knee pain: posture adjustment versus mindful endurance.
- Working with intellectual doubt: recognizing doubt as a mental state rather than ultimate truth.
- Balancing monastic communal duties with solitary retreat focus.
