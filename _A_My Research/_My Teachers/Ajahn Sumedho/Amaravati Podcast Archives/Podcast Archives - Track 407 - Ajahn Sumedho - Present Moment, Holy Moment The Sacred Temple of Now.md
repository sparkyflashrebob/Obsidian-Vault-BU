### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transforming mundane daily existence into sacred presence by consecrating the immediate present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this evocative 42-minute winter retreat discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores the holiness of the present moment. In conventional religion, holiness is confined to special buildings, holy relics, or consecrated days. But the Buddha revealed that true holiness is not a place; it is an awakened mind. When we meet the present moment with pure, non-grasping awareness, every breath, every step, and every sound becomes holy.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The sacred versus the profane: dismantling religious dualism.
- The Bhaddekaratta Sutta: 'The one who sees the Dhamma clearly in the present lives an auspicious day.'
- How time-bound thinking creates mundane depression and spiritual thirst.
- Abiding in the timeless holy sanctuary of bare awareness (Akāliko).
