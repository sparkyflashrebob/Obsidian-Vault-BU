### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Meditation on space (ākāsa) as a profound doorway to emptiness, detachment, and emotional freedom.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this captivating 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho teaches space contemplation. Most people spend their lives fixated on solid objects, thoughts, and emotional crises. By shifting attention to the vast space that surrounds and contains all objects, the mind experiences immediate spaciousness, cooling, and release from claustrophobic self-obsession.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- Contemplating the Buddha's advice to Rāhula: 'Develop meditation like space; for space does not cling to anything.'
- Noticing the physical space between objects in the meditation hall, and the space between thoughts in the mind.
- How resting in space provides room for painful emotions to arise and dissolve without overwhelming us.
- Transitioning from space contemplation into direct awareness of unconditioned emptiness (suññatā).
