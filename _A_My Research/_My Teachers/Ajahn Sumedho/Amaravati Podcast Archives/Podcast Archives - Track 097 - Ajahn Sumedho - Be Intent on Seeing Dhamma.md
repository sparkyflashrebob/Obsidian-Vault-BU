### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating sharp, unwavering contemplative intention (yoniso manasikāra) throughout the monastic day.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 65-minute monastic discourse, Luang Por Sumedho challenges practitioners to be uncompromisingly intent on seeing the Dhamma. It is extraordinarily easy to drift into mechanical habit, treating monastic life as a comfortable routine. Luang Por shows that every sensory encounter is an urgent invitation to observe conditionality and awaken.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- The crucial role of wise reflection (yoniso manasikāra) as the forerunner of the Noble Eightfold Path.
- Guarding against monastic complacency: mistaking robes and ceremonies for true liberation.
- Using daily friction and interpersonal annoyances as primary lenses for seeing the Four Noble Truths.
- Maintaining the continuous inner intention: 'What is the Dhamma of this moment?'
