### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Systematic exploration of the Noble Eightfold Path as an organic, unified vehicle of wisdom (paññā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho presents a thorough exposition of the Noble Eightfold Path (*ariya aṭṭhaṅgika magga*). Rather than seeing the path as eight sequential steps completed one after another, he demonstrates that all eight factors function simultaneously like eight strands of a single rope. When anchored in Right View (*sammā-diṭṭhi*), every aspect of thought, speech, and action becomes an expression of liberated wisdom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Organic Unity of the Path**: Why morality, meditation, and wisdom are mutually supportive.
- **Right View as the North Star**: Aligning the entire life with the reality of conditionality.
- **From Effort to Wisdom**: How paññā matures into effortless liberation.

| Right Effort / Mindfulness / Samādhi | Straining to concentrate on objects during meditation | Continuous, relaxed awareness anchored in the unconditioned ground. |
