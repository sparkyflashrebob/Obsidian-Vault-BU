### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A landmark farewell discourse before retirement: tuning into the unconditioned reality of the present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on 26 July 2010 just months before stepping down as abbot of Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho offers a majestic summing up of his teaching career. Like tuning a radio from static to a crystal-clear broadcast, spiritual practice is simply tuning our attention from the neurotic static of thoughts to the silent broadcast of reality. When we tune into the Dhamma, suffering ceases instantly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Radio Dial Metaphor**: Moving attention from thought-static to silent presence.
- **Thirty-Three Years in England**: Reflecting on the flowering of the Dhamma in the West from 1977 to 2010.
- **The Final Refuge**: Trusting the Unconditioned as the only reliable sanctuary in life and death.
