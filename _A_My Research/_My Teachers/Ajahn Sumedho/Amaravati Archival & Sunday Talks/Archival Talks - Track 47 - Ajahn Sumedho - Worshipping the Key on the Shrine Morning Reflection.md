### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The famous parable: why we must use the key to unlock the door rather than placing the key on the altar to worship it.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this humorous and razor-sharp morning reflection at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho delivers his iconic parable of 'worshipping the key on the shrine'. If someone gives you a golden key to unlock a treasure chest, you don't build an elaborate shrine, light incense, bow three times, and worship the key—you put the key in the lock and open the chest! Similarly, Buddhist doctrines, chants, and robes are keys designed to unlock liberation, not idols to be blindly worshipped.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Parable of the Golden Key**: Distinguishing between the instrument and the fruit.
- **The Trap of Religious Idolatry**: How veneration of scriptures and rituals replaces actual meditation.
- **Unlocking the Treasure Chest**: Opening the heart to the unconditioned wealth of Nibbāna.
