### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Turning the finger of blame around to inspect one's own mind: the radical art of self-investigation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho reflects on our reflex to point fingers outward. When life is uncomfortable, we blame the weather, the government, our spouse, our monastery abbot, or the tea. But the Dhamma finger always points directly back at you. Other people do not create our suffering; our grasping (*upādāna*) and aversion (*dosa*) create our suffering. Awakening begins the moment we stop looking outside for scapegoats.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Finger Pointing Inward**: Transforming every irritation into a direct mirror of attachment.
- **The Easy Faults of Others**: Why seeing other people's defects is easy, while seeing our own requires courage.
- **The Sovereignty of Self-Responsibility**: Reclaiming the power to liberate oneself from all victimization.
