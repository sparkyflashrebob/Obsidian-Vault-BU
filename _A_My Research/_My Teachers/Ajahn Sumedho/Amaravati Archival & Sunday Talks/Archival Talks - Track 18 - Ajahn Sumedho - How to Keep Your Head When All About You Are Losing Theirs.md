### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplative sovereignty: remaining centered and unshakeable amidst mass panic, hysteria, and scapegoating.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Drawing inspiration from Rudyard Kipling's poem 'If' and the Buddha's teachings on equanimity, Luang Por Sumedho delivers a tour de force on psychological sovereignty. When social hysteria, institutional collapse, or community gossip breaks out, human beings naturally stampede like frightened animals, looking for someone to blame. Luang Por shows how the meditator remains like a stone mountain—firm, unshakeable, and full of compassion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Stampede Reflex**: Understanding collective fear and mass hysteria as primitive biology.
- **The Mountain Metaphor**: Standing firm against the eight worldly winds of praise and blame.
- **Taking Blame with Dignity**: How accepting unfair criticism without retaliation conquers the world.
