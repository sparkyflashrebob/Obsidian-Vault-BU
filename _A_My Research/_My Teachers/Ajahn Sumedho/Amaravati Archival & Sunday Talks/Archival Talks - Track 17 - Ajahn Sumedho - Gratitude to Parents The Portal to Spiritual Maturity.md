### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deepening the practice of gratitude as an antidote to entitlement, alienation, and spiritual narcissism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Revisiting the theme of filial gratitude eight years later, Luang Por Sumedho explores how cultivating gratitude toward parents dissolves spiritual narcissism. When we feel entitled, we constantly measure life by what it fails to give us. When we practice gratitude for the simple fact of being alive and having had parents who fed and protected us as helpless infants, the heart opens to universal humility.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Entitlement vs. Gratitude**: The root cause of modern chronic depression and dissatisfaction.
- **The Helpless Infant**: Remembering the biological vulnerability that required parental sacrifice.
- **Gratitude as a Samādhi Portal**: How a grateful heart effortlessly settles into deep meditation.
