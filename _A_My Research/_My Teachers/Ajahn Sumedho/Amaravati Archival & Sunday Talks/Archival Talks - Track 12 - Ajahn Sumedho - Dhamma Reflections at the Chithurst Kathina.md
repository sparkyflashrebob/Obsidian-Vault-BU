### Circumstances
Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The reciprocal sacred contract between monastics and lay community at the mother monastery of Chithurst.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking in the historic shrine room of Cittaviveka (Chithurst Buddhist Monastery) during the 1995 Kathina, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the foundation of the Thai forest lineage in Britain. Chithurst was the crucible where Western monastics first learned to live dependent upon the almsbowl. Luang Por celebrates the beauty of reciprocal faith—lay people providing requisites, monastics providing living Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Chithurst Legacy**: Remembering the grueling early days clearing Hammer Wood and renovating the derelict house.
- **The Almsbowl as Equalizer**: How begging for daily food demolishes the American ideal of rugged individualism.
- **The Fragrance of Generosity**: How voluntary dāna creates an oasis of peace in a commercial world.
