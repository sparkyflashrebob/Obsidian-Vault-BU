### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The transformative momentum of spiritual merit (puñña), ethical integrity, and generosity as the ocean of good action.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich Sunday talk, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Buddhist concept of *puñña* (wholesome merit / spiritual power). Modern intellectuals often dismiss merit as superstitious folk Buddhism. Luang Por demonstrates that merit is simply the accumulated momentum of selfless generosity, pure conduct, and meditative cultivation. Just as countless drops of rain fill a vast ocean, small daily wholesome choices create an ocean of protective peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Demystifying Puñña**: Understanding merit as positive karmic momentum and clean conscience.
- **The Inward Radiance of Dāna**: How voluntary giving washes away chronic existential scarcity.
- **The Ocean of Sīla**: How ethical restraint provides an unbreachable psychological fortress.
