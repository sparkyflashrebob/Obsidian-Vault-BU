### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Radical self-honesty (sacca) as the bedrock of Buddhist meditation and emotional maturity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores truthfulness (*sacca*) not merely as refraining from lies, but as radical emotional authenticity. We deceive ourselves constantly—pretending we are more patient, forgiving, or detached than we actually are. Meditation begins when we stop posing for the spiritual mirror and honestly acknowledge our greed, jealousy, fear, and pettiness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mask of the 'Good Buddhist'**: Dismantling spiritual hypocrisy and performance anxiety.
- **Sacca as the Primary Pāramī**: Why truthfulness is the indispensable engine of stream-entry.
- **Meeting Flaws Without Self-Hatred**: Cultivating the gentle honesty that heals rather than condemns.
