### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Examining external religious authority versus direct contemplative verification.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the 1989 Sunday talk series at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho investigates the concepts of religious authority and revelation. In the West, religion has historically been based on external dogmatic authority and divine revelation. The Buddha offered a radically different approach: *sandīṭṭhiko* and *ehipassiko*—truth that is directly visible and verifiable in one's own heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **External Authority vs. Direct Seeing**: Why the Buddha rejected blind obedience to scriptures or teachers.
- **Revelation in the Present Moment**: Understanding Dhamma revelation as the sudden lifting of delusion.
- **The Middle Way Between Dogma and Cynicism**: Avoiding both fundamentalist belief and cynical nihilism.
