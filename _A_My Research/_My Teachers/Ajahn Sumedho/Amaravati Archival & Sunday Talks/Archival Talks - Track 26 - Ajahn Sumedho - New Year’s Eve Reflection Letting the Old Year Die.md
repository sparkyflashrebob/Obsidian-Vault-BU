Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the passage of time on New Year's Eve, releasing past burdens, and greeting the unborn present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the traditional all-night New Year's Eve vigil at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho invites practitioners to observe how human beings use cultural milestones like New Year's to fabricate guilt or false hope. The old year does not die at midnight; it dies with every exhaled breath. By allowing the past to die completely, we enter the new moment without carrying heavy psychological luggage.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Myth of New Year Resolutions**: Why willpower-driven resolutions fail without mindfulness.
- **Letting the Dead Past Bury Its Dead**: Releasing ancient grievances, regrets, and self-blame.
- **The Unborn Now**: Awakening to the only moment where true life exists.
