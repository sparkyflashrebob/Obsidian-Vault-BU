### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating sensual desire (kāmacchanda), the limitations of worldly gratification, and the joy of renunciation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho examines humanity's relentless obsession with sensual gratification. Society teaches that satisfaction comes from finding the perfect sensory stimulation—delicious food, beautiful sights, romance, and luxury. Using the Buddha's vivid similes of the meat-hook and the leper cauterizing wounds with fire, Luang Por reveals that sensual pleasure is merely temporary relief from sensual craving. True bliss is the coolness of non-attachment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mirage of Sensory Satisfaction**: Why sensual pleasure by its nature can never be satisfied.
- **The Hook in the Bait**: Seeing how craving blinds us to the suffering inherent in grasping.
- **Nekkhamma-Sukha**: Discovering the refined, sovereign joy of renunciation and contentment.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por shares how before ordaining he had all the sensual pleasures of modern Western life—music, parties, good food, travels—and yet felt like a starving ghost, until he tasted the peace of a Thai forest monastery.
