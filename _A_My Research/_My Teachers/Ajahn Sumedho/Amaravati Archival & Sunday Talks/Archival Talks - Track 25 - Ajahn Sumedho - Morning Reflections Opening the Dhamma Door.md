Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The open doorway of Dhamma: opening the doors to the Deathless (apārutā tesaṁ amatassa dvārā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this short morning reflection at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho recites the Buddha's ancient declaration upon attaining enlightenment: 'Open are the doors to the Deathless for those with ears to hear.' The Dhamma door is not locked by an angry deity or guarded by elite priests; it is open right now. We simply have to turn our attention away from the worldly rat race and step through the portal of mindful knowing.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Open Door of the Deathless**: Awakening as an ever-present invitation.
- **Ears to Hear (*Sotāvanto*)**: Cultivating receptive, humble listening.
- **Stepping Through the Threshold**: Moving from worldly hesitation to resolute faith (*saddhā*).
