### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The mechanics of using the Sound of Silence (nāda) as a continuous meditation object during intensive retreat.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during a silent retreat in May 1998, Luang Por Sumedho offers practical guidance on anchoring awareness in the Sound of Silence (*nāda*). Meditators often struggle during retreats with endless mental turbulence and boredom. Luang Por demonstrates how tuning to the inner sound bypasses conceptual thinking immediately, providing a stable, joyful, and effortlessly spacious container for the entire retreat.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Nāda in All Four Postures**: Listening to the inner sound while sitting, walking, standing, and lying down.
- **The Antidote to Restlessness**: How inner listening starves discursive mental chatter of fuel.
- **The Sound of Nature**: Recognizing the inner hum as the vibrational frequency of universal nature.
