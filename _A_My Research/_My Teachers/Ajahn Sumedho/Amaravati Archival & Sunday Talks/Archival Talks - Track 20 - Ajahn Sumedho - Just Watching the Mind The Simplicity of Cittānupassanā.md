### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct, non-interfering contemplation of the mind (cittānupassanā) and observing the play of mental states.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic archival recording from Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho presents the radical simplicity of 'just watching the mind'. We spend our entire lives trying to control, decorate, and improve our minds. But mindfulness of mental states (*cittānupassanā*) does not require remodeling the mind; it simply asks us to observe mental states—greedy or generous, agitated or peaceful—without grasping or resistance.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Non-Interfering Observer**: Learning to watch without meddling or managing.
- **The Movie Screen Metaphor**: How the screen remains clean regardless of what movie is projected.
- **Resting as the Knower**: Abiding as Buddho while mental weather passes by.

| Peaceful / Blissful Mind | Clinging: 'Finally I got it! I must preserve this state forever.' | Knowing: 'A calm state of mind is present; it arises upon causes and will cease.' |

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por compares the mind to a wild monkey: if you chase it around the room trying to catch it, it screams and bites; if you sit still and watch it quietly, it eventually gets bored, sits down, and falls asleep.
