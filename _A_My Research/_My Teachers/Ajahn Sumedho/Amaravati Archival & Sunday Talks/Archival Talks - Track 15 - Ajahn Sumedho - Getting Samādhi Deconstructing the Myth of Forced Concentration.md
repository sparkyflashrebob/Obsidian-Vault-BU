### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Exposing the egoic trap of 'trying to get samādhi' through forceful suppression.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking to Western and Thai monastics in northeast Thailand, Luang Por Sumedho deconstructs the common obsession with 'getting samādhi'. Meditators turn samādhi into a prize to be won through aggression, gritting their teeth and trying to blast away stray thoughts. Luang Por points out that samādhi is not an acquisition; it is the natural collectedness of a mind that is free from remorse, grasping, and distraction.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Disease of 'Getting'**: How ambition destroys the receptive stillness needed for samādhi.
- **Natural Collectedness**: Allowing concentration to emerge as the natural consequence of moral purity.
- **The Space Around Distraction**: Instead of fighting noise, noticing the silence that holds the noise.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Method | Forceful Suppression (Wrong Samādhi) | Natural Collected Presence (*Sammā-Samādhi*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mental State | Strained, hyper-tense, irritable, terrified of any wandering thought | Relaxed, luminous, alert, comfortable in physical body. |
| Refuge | A fragile, temporary mental bubble that shatters at the first sound | The unconditioned knowing presence that effortlessly contains all sounds. |
| Long-term Fruit | Emotional instability and explosive anger when interrupted | Gentle patience, profound emotional resilience, and deep wisdom. |
