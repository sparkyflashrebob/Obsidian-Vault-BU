### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating communal living, monastic friction, and accepting unenlightened personalities with patient loving-kindness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this wonderfully candid and practical discourse, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the inevitable friction of living with other human beings. Spiritual communities often suffer from the naive expectation that everyone in robes or at retreats will be an enlightened saint. Luang Por reminds us that monasteries are hospitals for the sick, full of unenlightened personalities. True wisdom is learning to live gracefully with irritating people without adding ill-will.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Monastery as a Hospital**: Dispelling romantic illusions of saintly communal perfection.
- **The Five Ways of Removing Resentment**: Sutta tools for working with irritating individuals.
- **Holding Quirks in Mettā**: Expanding our capacity to forgive personal eccentricity.

| Reaction to Friction | Outrage, gossip, passive-aggressive complaining, leaving the community | Using interpersonal irritation as a sharp mirror to investigate our own self-will. |
| Atmosphere | Hyper-critical, tense, divided into factions, brittle | Humorous, forgiving, warm, resilient, and deeply harmonious. |
