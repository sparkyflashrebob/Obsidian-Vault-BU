### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Establishing the sovereign witnessing posture of Buddho to dismantle emotional entanglement.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this foundational monastic instruction, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into 'the witnessing position'. Whenever strong desires, rage, fear, or depressive moods flood consciousness, our habitual reflex is to either indulge them or fight them. The witnessing position offers the third way: stepping back into the one who knows (*Buddho*). From this vantage point, we see all emotions as impermanent weather.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Witnessing Posture**: Stepping out of the arena of mental combat into the observer's seat.
- **Commitment to Buddho**: Using the recollection of the Buddha as an unshakeable inner anchor.
- **The Dissolution of Grip**: Watching emotional storms dissipate when denied personal ownership.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por compares the witnessing position to standing on a high riverbank watching muddy debris, dead logs, and flotsam rush past in a flood: you don't jump into the river to fight the logs; you simply watch them float by from the dry bank.
