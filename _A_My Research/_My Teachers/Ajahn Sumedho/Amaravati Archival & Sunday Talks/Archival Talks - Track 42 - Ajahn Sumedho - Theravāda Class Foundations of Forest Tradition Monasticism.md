### Circumstances
Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A systematic instructional class on the history, Vinaya foundations, and contemplative ethos of the Theravāda Forest Tradition.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic instructional class recorded in 1988, Luang Por Sumedho introduces lay students to the foundations of the Theravāda Forest Tradition. He explains the lineage stretching from the Buddha through the forest masters of Thailand, the rigorous 227 rules of the Pātimokkha, the role of the wandering dhutaṅga ascetic practices, and why living simply under trees provides the ideal soil for enlightenment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Forest Tradition Lineage**: Ajahn Mun Bhūridatto and the revival of ancient ascetic practice.
- **The Living Vinaya**: Why ancient monastic rules remain razor-sharp instruments of mindfulness today.
- **Simplicity as Sovereignty**: The freedom of possessing only three robes and an almsbowl.
