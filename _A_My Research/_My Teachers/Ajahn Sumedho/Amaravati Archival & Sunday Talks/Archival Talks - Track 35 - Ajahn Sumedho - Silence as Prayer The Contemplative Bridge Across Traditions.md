### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dialogue with Western contemplative Christianity: discovering silence as the universal language of prayer and contemplation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic 1994 Sunday discourse, Luang Por Sumedho builds a profound bridge between Buddhist mindfulness and Western Christian mysticism. Many Westerners associate prayer with begging God for favors or reciting verbal petitions. Luang Por explores contemplative prayer as radical, silent surrender—the emptying of ego (*kenosis*) into the silent mystery of the Unconditioned. Silence is the ultimate prayer.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Petitional Prayer vs. Contemplative Silence**: Moving beyond begging God to resting in the Divine Ground.
- **The Christian Mystics**: Recognizing kinship with Meister Eckhart, St. John of the Cross, and The Cloud of Unknowing.
- **The Sound of Silence as Sacred Ground**: Meeting across religious boundaries in the sanctuary of still awareness.
