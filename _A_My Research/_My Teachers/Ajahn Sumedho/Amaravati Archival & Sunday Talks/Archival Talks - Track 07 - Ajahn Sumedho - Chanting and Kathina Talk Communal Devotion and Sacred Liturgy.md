Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The spiritual power of collective chanting (paritta) and the annual Kathina festival of robe-offering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explains the inner contemplative meaning of Buddhist liturgical chanting and the ancient Kathina ceremony. Chanting is not rote superstition; it is vibrational mindfulness that aligns speech with Dhamma truth. The Kathina festival represents the joyful reciprocal bond between lay supporters and the monastic Sangha, grounding communal life in mutual generosity (*dāna*) and gratitude (*kataññū*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Liturgy as Vibrational Sati**: Tuning into the physical resonance and meaning of sacred Pāli verses.
- **The Kathina Tradition**: The historical origin in the Buddha's lifetime and its preservation today.
- **The Chemistry of Dāna**: How selfless giving cleanses the heart of greed and builds spiritual community.
