Archival monastic discourse delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho, addressing resident monastics, visiting teachers, and the lay assembly on core principles of Theravāda Forest Tradition contemplation.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Waking up to the fresh simplicity of reality at dawn, before the momentum of egoic storytelling begins.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this intimate morning reflection delivered at dawn in the Amaravati temple, Luang Por Sumedho invites the community to appreciate the quiet purity of the early morning. Before the conditioned mind launches into its daily agendas, grievances, and ambitions, there is an open window of primordial wakefulness. Beginning the day in this open space colors every subsequent action with peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Dawn Window**: Capitalizing on the natural quiet of the nervous system before thinking starts.
- **Setting Noble Aspiration**: Dedicating the day to harmlessness, patience, and mindful presence.
- **The Rhythm of Monastery Life**: How routine and communal silence support the awakeness of each heart.
