### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Historical tribute to Ajahn Pannavaddho, monastic pioneer in the West and chief disciple of Ajahn Maha Boowa.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a moving historical tribute to Venerable Ajahn Pannavaddho (Peter Morgan), one of the foremost Western Buddhist monastics who ordained in London in 1957 and lived for decades alongside Ajahn Maha Boowa at Wat Pa Baan Taad. Luang Por recounts the early history of the English Sangha, celebrating Pannavaddho's brilliant engineering mind, profound samādhi, and uncompromising dedication to the Vinaya.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Pioneers of the Western Sangha**: The courageous English seekers of the 1950s and 60s.
- **Ajahn Pannavaddho's Genius**: Combining Oxford engineering acumen with deep Thai forest asceticism.
- **The Transmission of Vinaya**: How monastic discipline took root in British soil.
