### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reflecting on the death and legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah: discovering the master not in a memory, but in living Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho reflects on his beloved teacher Venerable Ajahn Chah several years after his passing. Disciples often grieve the loss of an enlightened master, asking: 'Where is Ajahn Chah now?' Drawing on the Yamaka Sutta, Luang Por explains that even when Ajahn Chah was alive, he could not be found within the five aggregates. Ajahn Chah was never a physical body or personality—he was the living embodiment of Dhamma. That Dhamma is alive in every moment of mindful presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Yamaka Sutta Deconstruction**: Why an enlightened being cannot be identified with form or mind.
- **Beyond Nostalgic Grief**: Honoring the teacher by practicing the teachings rather than building idols.
- **The Eternal Heritage**: Experiencing Ajahn Chah's living wisdom in our own awakening.
