### People Involved

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A definitive, uncompromising monastic discourse on personality view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi) as the foundational root of suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat in November 2012, this archival discourse is Luang Por Sumedho's definitive exposition on *sakkāya-diṭṭhi* (personality view). He methodically tears apart the belief that we are our thoughts, our monastic rank, our cultural background, or our physical bodies. When personality view is severed through clear seeing, stream-entry is attained and the heavy burden of being someone is permanently laid down.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Anatomy of Sakkāya-diṭṭhi**: Tracing how sensory contact gets claimed as 'me' and 'mine'.
- **Monastic Conceit**: Why even robes, precepts, and ascetic attainments can become dangerous ego props.
- **The Joy of Emptiness**: Experiencing the vast relief when there is no longer a personal self to defend.
