### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Buddha's epithet 'Lokavidū' (Knower of the Worlds) and discovering the cosmos within the fathom-long body.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores one of the supreme epithets of the Buddha: *Lokavidū*—the Knower of the Worlds. In the Rohitassa Sutta, the Buddha declared that the end of the world cannot be reached by traveling through outer space; rather, it is within this very fathom-long body, with its perceptions and intellect, that the world, its origin, its cessation, and the path to its cessation are found.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Cosmos Within**: Redefining 'the world' (*loka*) as sensory contact and mental projection.
- **The Rohitassa Sutta Benchmark**: Why space exploration cannot cure human existential suffering.
- **Resting as Lokavidū**: Embodying the knowing presence that transcends all cosmic realms.
