### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct retreat instructions on accessing, cultivating, and resting in the inner Sound of Silence (nāda).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this foundational guided discourse delivered at IMS in 1990, Luang Por Sumedho introduces the practice of 'inner listening' to the Sound of Silence (*nāda*). He explains how this high-pitched, steady vibrational frequency can be heard by anyone who turns attention inward. Unlike breath, which changes with emotional states, the Sound of Silence is unvarying, spacious, and immediately accessible as a portal to emptiness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Discovering the Nāda Sound**: Simple auditory cues to identify the inner hum.
- **Nāda as an Anchor of Emptiness**: Using inner listening to instantly dissolve conceptual chattering.
- **Listening from the Heart**: Receptive attention that effortlessly holds physical aches and sounds.
