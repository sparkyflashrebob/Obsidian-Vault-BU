### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The instantaneous nature of awareness: turning on the light of mindfulness to dispel centuries of mental darkness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered at IMS in 1990, Luang Por Sumedho uses the profound metaphor of light and darkness. If a room has been pitch black for a thousand years, you do not have to fight the darkness with a broom or shovel to get it out. You simply turn on the light, and centuries of darkness vanish in a split second. Similarly, no matter how much trauma, delusion, or confusion our past holds, the single moment of mindful presence instantly dispels all darkness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Instantaneous Light**: Why awakening does not require decades of digging through past memories.
- **Fighting the Dark**: Exposing the futility of battling psychological neuroses with willpower.
- **Sati as the Light Switch**: Accessing immediate clarity in this very moment.
