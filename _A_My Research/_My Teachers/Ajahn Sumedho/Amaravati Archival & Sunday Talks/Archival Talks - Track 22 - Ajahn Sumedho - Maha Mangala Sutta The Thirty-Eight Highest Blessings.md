### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Systematic exposition of the 38 blessings (maṅgala) from basic ethical living to complete enlightenment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking at the historic headquarters of the Buddhist Society in London, Luang Por Sumedho offers a luminous commentary on the Mahā Maṅgala Sutta. When a celestial deva asked the Buddha what constitutes the highest blessing in the universe, the Buddha did not point to astrological lucky omens or magical talismans. He outlined 38 practical, psychological, and spiritual practices—from avoiding fools to having a mind unshaken by worldly fortune.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **True Blessings vs. Superstition**: Moving from magical omens to ethical and mental cultivation.
- **The Graduated Path of Maṅgala**: From foundational association with the wise to supreme Nibbāna.
- **The Mind Unshaken by Worldly Winds**: Realizing *phuṭṭhassa lokadhammehi cittaṁ yassa na kampati*.
