### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha, resident community, visiting practitioners, and lay disciples.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The proper relationship between student and spiritual master: avoiding dependency while honoring lineage.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking in Thailand during the annual Ajahn Chah memorial gathering, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the role of the spiritual teacher (*ācariya*) and the monastic community (*Sangha*). Seekers often fall into emotional codependency, projecting god-like perfection onto teachers. Luang Por reminds monastics of the Buddha's final instruction: 'Be islands unto yourselves; take the Dhamma as your refuge.' A true teacher points you back to your own awakened nature.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Guru Trap**: Avoiding cultish idolization and emotional infantilization.
- **The Teacher as Mirror**: How a true teacher reflects your own delusions without enabling them.
- **Taking Refuge in Sangha**: Finding strength in the harmonious discipline of the community.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Dynamic | Unhealthy Teacher Worship (Cultic Guruism) | Noble Dhamma Mentorship (*Kalyāṇa-Mitta*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Student Posture | Blind obedience, emotional infantilization, demanding constant approval | Deep reverence paired with clear discernment and autonomous practice. |
| Teacher Role | Authoritarian figure holding secret power and personal salvation | Compassionate guide pointing the student to their own inner knowing. |
| Ultimate Refuge | The teacher's physical personality and charismatic presence | The Dhamma: the verified truth of the cessation of suffering. |
