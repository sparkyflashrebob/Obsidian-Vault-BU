# Master Index: Amaravati Archival & Sunday Talks
*Historical discourses, Sunday reflections, and monastic gatherings delivered by Venerable Ajahn Sumedho (1988–2019)*

---

## 1. Archival Series Overview

The **Amaravati Archival & Sunday Talks** collection unites 47 profound historical discourses delivered across four decades of Venerable Ajahn Sumedho's leadership in the West.

Covering landmark **Sunday Public Talks**, **Kathina & Robe Offerings**, **Theravāda classes**, **Interfaith & Funeral addresses**, and historical visits to **IMS, Cloud Mountain, and California**, these teachings emphasize:
1. **The Witnessing Posture (*Buddho*)**: Disentangling knowing awareness from the turbulence of emotional storms.
2. **Stress & The Two Arrows (*Sallatha Sutta*)**: Rewiring the chronic habit of stress into real-time peace and sovereign presence.
3. **The Sound of Silence (*Nāda*)**: Tuning to the inner primordial vibration as a continuous sanctuary.
4. **Gratitude as Spiritual Foundation (*Kataññū*)**: Healing childhood wounds, honoring parents, and overcoming entitlement.
5. **Dismantling Religious Idolatry**: Parables like "Worshipping the Key on the Shrine"—using Dhamma to unlock liberation rather than placing teachings on an altar of blind worship.

Every talk has been transcribed from master audio via the authentic **Faster-Whisper (base.en, int8)** pipeline into full verbatim prose formatted into natural paragraphs (~60–120 words) with responsive Markdown tables and dynamic Pāli doctrinal tagging.
