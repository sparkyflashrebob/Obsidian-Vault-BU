### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a deeply touching eulogy and Dhamma reflection at the funeral of Dr. Nina Coltart, eminent British psychoanalyst and devoted supporter of the English Sangha. Luang Por weaves together the clinical depth of psychoanalysis with the transcendent truth of Buddhist liberation, honoring Nina's fierce intellect, sharp humor, and courageous surrender to mortality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Psychotherapy vs. Dhamma**: Where psychological analysis ends and contemplative liberation begins.
- **The Great Equalizer**: How death strips away professional accolades and intellectual brilliance.
- **The Gift of a Well-Lived Life**: Rejoicing in goodness and dedicating merit (*patti-dāna*).
