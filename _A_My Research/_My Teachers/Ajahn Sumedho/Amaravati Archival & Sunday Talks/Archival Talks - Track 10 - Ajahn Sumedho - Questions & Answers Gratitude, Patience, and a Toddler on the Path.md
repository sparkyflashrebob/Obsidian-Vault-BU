### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this heartwarming and humorous Q&A session from a 2019 retreat, Luang Por Sumedho addresses lay practitioners balancing intense spiritual aspirations with the messy realities of raising young children and dealing with aging bodies. Using the metaphor of 'a toddler on the path', Luang Por shows how our neurotic spiritual ego behaves exactly like a demanding toddler—and needs patient, loving, firm boundaries.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Inner Toddler**: Meeting psychological tantrums with amusement rather than anger.
- **Parenting as Monastic Practice**: Seeing caregiving for toddlers as an intensive retreat in selflessness.
- **The Foundation of Kataññū**: Why gratitude toward parents and benefactors softens the hardest heart.
