### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Filial gratitude, healing childhood wounds, and honoring the maternal and paternal lineage through Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho gives a profoundly moving sermon on gratitude toward parents (*kataññū-katavedī*). In modern Western culture, psychology often focuses on childhood grievances, blaming parents for adult neuroses. Luang Por reorients practitioners to the classical Buddhist view: even if our parents were imperfect, they gave us the gift of human birth. Repaying this debt through gratitude and sharing Dhamma heals our deepest emotional rifts.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Irrepayable Debt**: The Buddha's declaration on carrying parents on one's shoulders.
- **Healing Grievance with Kataññū**: Replacing blame and resentment with profound compassion.
- **Establishing Parents in Dhamma**: The supreme gift of introducing parents to ethical virtue and mindfulness.
