### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Translating the ancient concept of dukkha into the modern vocabulary of chronic nervous system stress and somatic contraction.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this highly relevant Sunday talk, Luang Por Sumedho translates the Pāli term *dukkha* into the contemporary experience of stress. Stress is not caused by traffic, taxes, or computer screens; stress is the energetic friction generated when the mind resists reality. Using the classic Sallatha Sutta teachings on the Two Arrows, Luang Por demonstrates how holding biological tension in mindfulness rewires chronic stress into peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Two Arrows in Modern Life**: Sights, sounds, and rude encounters as the First Arrow; outrage as the Second Arrow.
- **Biological Survival Reflexes**: Seeing stomach knots as evolutionary mammalian responses (*idappaccayatā*).
- **Rewiring the Habit of Stress**: Freeing up vital energy so we can deal with life skillfully.
