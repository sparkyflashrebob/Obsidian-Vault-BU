#### The "So What?": Conventional vs. Ultimate Reality
Conventional reality (*sammuti*) provides language and concepts. Ultimate reality (*paramattha*) is the silent ground of awareness. Using conventional forms without clinging enables graceful participation in the world without identity view.

### 6. Final Synthesis: Implications for Mindfulness and Spiritual Practice
*The core paradigm shift, ultimate takeaway, and practical application for daily life, teaching, and future media/podcast/book content development.*

The core paradigm shift in *8 Precepts - 5 - 2006-05-20  AMV 10 Day Retreat http---www.amaravati.org* is stepping out of intellectual struggle into the simplicity of 'just knowing.'
