Listening
Open
Relaxed attention
Not doing - like you have to do this.
These are words of encouragement and to remind you.
Imperatives can affect negatively on the consciousness,
the thought I should be mindful - it is that that reinforces Sakkaya Ditthi
The idea that "I am somebody who should be mindful who is not and I should be."
That's Sakkaya Ditthi. It is not about the way things should be or what you want or not want things to be, what you like and dislike.
But in awareness there is this resting, observing, noticing - non-critical/not-judgemental.
It is like this.... dat-dadah .... the way it is


Sakkaya Ditti, your personality is a creation based on ideas, on memories, identities with the conditioned realm.
In Vipassana we investigate the conditions. Looking at it with the 3 characteristics - anchica, anatta, dukkha so that we can learn to see thru the unresolved assumptions and attitudes we operate from, from the conditioned perspective that we have acquired since birth. If we never see, we spend our lives operating from attachments, conditions. 
Sakkaya-ditthi, vicikiccha, silabbata-paramasa. Personality view, doubt or uncertainty, attachments to rites and rituals.  Our belief system.
These three fetters we keep talking about.
