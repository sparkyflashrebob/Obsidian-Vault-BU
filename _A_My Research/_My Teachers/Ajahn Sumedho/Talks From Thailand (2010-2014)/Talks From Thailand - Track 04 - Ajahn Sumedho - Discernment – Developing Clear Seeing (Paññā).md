## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 111 (Anupada Sutta), AN 2.30 (Vipassanā & Samatha).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating penetrative spiritual discernment (paññā) to differentiate between conditioned phenomena and the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the faculty of spiritual discernment (*paññā*). Discernment is not academic intellect, but the laser-like ability of mindful awareness to distinguish between what is born, changing, and stressful, and that which is unborn, changeless, and free. Without discernment, even spiritual experiences become traps of conceit.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Intellect vs. Discernment**: Differentiating between thinking about reality and seeing reality directly (*yathābhūta*).
- **The Knife of Wisdom**: Cutting through subtle spiritual pride, mystical visions, and emotional projections.
- **The Fruit of Clear Seeing**: Resting in unentangled freedom regardless of sensory circumstances.
