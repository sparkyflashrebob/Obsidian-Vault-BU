## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: AN 6.10 (Mahānāma Sutta - Recollection of Sīla), SN 55.3.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Skillfully navigating bodily illness and depressive mental states by recollecting one's own ethical integrity (sīlānussati).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho offers intimate, compassionate counsel on dealing with severe physical illness and dark emotional depressions. When the body is wracked by sickness and the brain chemistry is depressed, meditation feels impossible and negative self-talk multiplies. Luang Por teaches the classical practice of *Sīlānussati* (recollecting our own virtues): reminding the heart of years of non-harming, generosity, and ethical restraint. This pure recollection acts as a bright lighthouse that guides the heart through emotional darkness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Anatomy of Depression**: Recognizing that depression is biological mental weather, not a spiritual failure.
- **Sīlānussati (Recollection of Virtue)**: Reminding the heart: 'I have not intentionally harmed living beings; my intentions are pure.'
- **The Healing Light of Sīla**: How remembering ethical goodness revives confidence and cuts through despair.
