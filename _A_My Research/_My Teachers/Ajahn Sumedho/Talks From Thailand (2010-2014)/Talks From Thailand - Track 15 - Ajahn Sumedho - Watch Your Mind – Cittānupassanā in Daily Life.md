## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 10 (Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta - Cittānupassanā), Dhp 33–43 (Cittavagga).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The art of non-reactive mental observation (cittānupassanā); watching the changing moods and currents of the mind without taking the bait.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho offers a core masterclass on *Cittānupassanā* (mindfulness of mind states). The untrained mind is like a wild monkey, leaping from thought to thought, falling into depression, elation, grievance, and fantasy. The practice is not to violently suppress mental movement, but to watch your mind with spacious, dispassionate curiosity. When you watch the mind without fear, mental storms lose their power to disturb.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Wild Mind**: Acknowledging the natural biological turbulence of human thoughts.
- **Non-Interference**: Why trying to force the mind to be blank only creates deeper tension.
- **The Clear Mirror**: Becoming the immaculate mirror that reflects whatever passes without holding a single trace.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls Ajahn Chah comparing the mind to a wild water buffalo: if you tie it up with a short rope, it struggles and breaks the rope; if you give it a long rope in an open field, it grazes peacefully and eventually lies down.
