## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: Ud 8.1 (Nibbāna Sutta), SN 43 (Asaṅkhata Saṁyutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting as the unconditioned witness; distinguishing the formed, moving, transient universe from the silent backdrop of the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this subtle, luminous discourse, Luang Por Sumedho points attention directly to 'That Which Is Not a Condition.' Every thought, breath, sound, mood, pain, and joy is a condition (*saṅkhāra*). Conditions are born and must die. But what is it that knows the condition? The knowing itself was never born, has no shape, does not age, and can never die. By shifting identity from the condition to the knowing, we step out of mortality into the Unconditioned (*Asaṅkhata*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Formed vs. The Formless**: Looking past the parade of mental objects to the open space in which they march.
- **The Deathless Reality**: Why awareness does not need to be purified—it is already untouched by defilements.
- **The Simplicity of True Refuge**: Ceasing to search for peace in conditions and resting in unconditioned presence.

## 3. Topic Breakdown & Practical Frameworks

| Aspect        | A Condition                                  | That Which Is Not a Condition                             |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dimension** | A Condition (*Saṅkhāra*)                     | That Which Is Not a Condition (*Asaṅkhata*)               |
| **Arising**   | Has a beginning; caused by conditions        | Unborn, unoriginated, uncaused.                           |
| **Duration**  | Constantly changing, shifting, deteriorating | Changeless, constant, timeless (*akāliko*).               |
| **Ending**    | Must terminate; dissolves back into elements | Deathless (*amata*); never ceases because it never began. |

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por compares conditions to waves on the ocean and the unconditioned to the vast ocean depths: human beings spend their whole lives obsessing over whether the waves are big or small, forgetting that beneath the surface, the water is completely still.
