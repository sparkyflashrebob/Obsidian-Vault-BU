## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: Ud 8.1–3 (Nibbāna Suttas), SN 43 (Asaṅkhata Saṁyutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme destination of the Buddha's path: the Unborn, Uncreated, Unformed, and Unconditioned (Asaṅkhata Dhamma).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides attention to the heart of the Dhamma: the Unconditioned (*Asaṅkhata*). Everything in the sensory realm is conditioned (*saṅkhata*)—it is born, changes, and perishes. If there were only the conditioned, there would be no escape from suffering. But because there is the Unborn and Unconditioned, complete liberation is possible right now by stepping back from mental conditions.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Two Realms**: Distinguishing the realm of conditions (*saṅkhāra*) from the unconditioned presence (*asaṅkhata*).
- **Awareness as the Portal**: Why conscious presence is not a condition created by the brain, but the space in which conditions appear.
- **The Peace of Nibbāna**: Experiencing the cooling of craving in the unconditioned heart.
