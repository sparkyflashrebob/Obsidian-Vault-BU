## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: AN 4.242, Vinaya Piṭaka (Maha-khandhaka - Teacher & Student).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Historical memoirs and contemplative lessons from training under Venerable Ajahn Chah and visiting the great Forest Masters (Krooba Ajahns) of Isan.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this delightful, heartwarming 30-minute talk, Luang Por Sumedho reminisces about living among the legendary *Krooba Ajahns*—the enlightened elder masters of the Thai Forest Tradition. He describes the absolute reverence, simplicity, and raw spiritual power of teachers like Ajahn Chah, Ajahn Tate, Ajahn Fun, and Ajahn Khao. Their wisdom was not found in scholarly books, but in their eyes, their flawless ethical discipline, and their utter fearlessness of death.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Krooba Ajahn Tradition**: What it meant to sit at the feet of realized jungle masters in the 1960s and 70s.
- **Teaching Through Osmosis**: How wisdom was transmitted through chores, presence, and silence rather than lectures.
- **Preserving the Flame**: The duty of modern monastics to embody the uncompromising integrity of the lineage.
