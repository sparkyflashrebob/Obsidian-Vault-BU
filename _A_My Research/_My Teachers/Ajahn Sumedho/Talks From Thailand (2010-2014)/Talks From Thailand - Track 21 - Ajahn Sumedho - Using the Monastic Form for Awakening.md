## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: Vinaya Piṭaka (Pātimokkha), MN 53 (Sekha Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The monastic discipline (Vinaya) as a profound contemplative mirror rather than an oppressive legal code.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the monastic assembly on 'Using the Monastic Form for Awakening.' Modern people often view monastic rules and rituals as archaic, rigid, and restrictive. But the 227 rules of the Vinaya and the communal etiquette of the Forest Tradition are designed as an immaculate mirror. By surrendering personal preference to the communal form, self-will (*taṇhā*) is starved, and the heart finds supreme inner freedom within outer restraint.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Container of Discipline**: How the Vinaya creates a protected sanctuary from worldly distraction.
- **Surrendering Personal Will**: Giving up preferences regarding food, clothing, schedule, and companions.
- **The Freedom of Form**: Discovering that true spontaneity and liberation flourish within disciplined boundaries.
