## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: AN 3.53 (Sandiddiṭṭhika Sutta), SN 12.20 (Paccaya Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The timeless (akāliko) nature of Dhamma; stepping out of chronological anxiety into the eternal present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
As the year 2010 draws to a close, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on *Timelessness*. Human beings live in chronic temporal stress, driven by clocks, deadlines, aging, and the fear of running out of time. But the Dhamma is *akāliko*—immediately effective and timeless. Awareness does not exist inside time; time exists inside awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Trap of Chronological Time**: How the concept of time manufactures existential anxiety and haste.
- **The Akāliko Nature of Truth**: Why enlightenment does not require decades of waiting—it is realized the moment clinging stops.
- **Resting in the Timeless Heart**: Experiencing the unaging witness of this moment.
