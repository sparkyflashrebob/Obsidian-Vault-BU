## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: Vinaya Piṭaka (Cullavagga), AN 6.55 (Soṇa Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Using organizational rules, schedules, and social structures as vehicles for mindfulness rather than sources of resentment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses how we relate to external structures. Whether it is monastery timetables, government laws, or corporate hierarchies, the ego naturally resents rules and feels trapped by structures. Luang Por shows how a wise practitioner uses structures as mirrors: instead of fighting the schedule or complaining about regulations, we bring mindful awareness to our inner reactions, discovering total inner freedom within outer constraints.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Ego's Resentment of Form**: Exposing the rebellious reflex to kick against schedules and rules.
- **Structures as Training Tools**: How fixed forms prevent the mind from wandering into lazy indulgence.
- **Freedom Within the Form**: Learning to dance within the framework rather than fight the walls.
