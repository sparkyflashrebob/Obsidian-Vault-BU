# Talks From Thailand (2010–2014) — Master Archival Index & Contemplative Compendium

> [!NOTE] Historical Provenance & Spiritual Significance
> Following his formal retirement as Abbot of Amaravati Buddhist Monastery in November 2010, **Venerable Ajahn Sumedho** returned to his monastic roots in Thailand, residing primarily at **Wat Pah Nanachat** (The International Forest Monastery) and **Wat Pah Pong** in Ubon Ratchathani, with solitary retreat periods at **Dtao Dam** in Kanchanaburi.
> 
> Spanning **2008 to 2014**, these 37 archival discourses capture Luang Por Sumedho speaking as an unburdened elder (*thera*). Delivered primarily to international forest monks, newly ordained postulants, and serious practitioners, these talks represent the pinnacle of his teaching on **pure awareness**, **sakkāya-diṭṭhi**, **monastic asceticism**, and living memories of **Ajahn Chah** and the great Forest Masters (*Krooba Ajahns*).
