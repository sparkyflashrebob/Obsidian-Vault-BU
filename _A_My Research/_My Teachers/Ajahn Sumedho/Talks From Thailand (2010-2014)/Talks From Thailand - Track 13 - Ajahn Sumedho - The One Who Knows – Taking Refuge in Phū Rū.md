## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: AN 1.49 (Pabhassara Sutta), SN 22.89 (Khemaka Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The quintessential teaching of the Thai Forest Tradition: stepping out of thinking into 'The One Who Knows' (Phū Rū / Buddho).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic Thailand discourse, Luang Por Sumedho points directly to the foundational realization taught by Venerable Ajahn Chah: *Phū Rū*—The One Who Knows. We are endlessly lost in the drama of our thoughts, emotions, memories, and opinions. But who is the one that knows this thought? Who is the one that knows this emotion? The knower itself is not a thought, not a feeling, and not a person. It is pure, spacious, unconditioned awareness. Taking refuge in *Phū Rū* is immediate awakening.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Knower vs. The Known**: Disentangling the pure subject of awareness from all objective phenomena.
- **The Nature of Phū Rū**: Recognizing that the knower is unborn, deathless, and completely empty of self (*anattā*).
- **Abiding as Awareness**: Living daily monastic life grounded in the silent watcher.

| Aspect | The Known | The One Who Knows |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dimension** | The Known (*Saṅkhāras / Objects*) | The One Who Knows (*Phū Rū / Buddho*) |
| **Identity** | Thoughts, moods, memories, bodily aches, sounds | The pure silent consciousness in which objects appear and dissolve. |
| **Characteristics** | Formed, changing, unstable, impermanent (*anicca*) | Formless, changeless, constant, peaceful, deathless. |
| **Relationship** | Grasped as 'me' and 'mine' by worldly delusion | Witnessed with effortless, unattached clarity. |

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls sitting with Ajahn Chah on a wooden porch at Wat Pah Pong, listening to the wild jungle storms, when Ajahn Chah pointed to his chest and whispered: 'The rain falls, the wind blows, the trees bend—but the One Who Knows neither falls nor bends.'
