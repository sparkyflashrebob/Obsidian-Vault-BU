## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 131 (Bhaddekaratta Sutta - An Auspicious Day), SN 22.85.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The core Pāli concept of Paccuppanna Dhamma: anchoring consciousness in immediate reality rather than past memory or future anticipation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho expounds on *Paccuppanna Dhamma*—the truth of the present moment. Past memories and future projections are merely mental formations occurring right now in consciousness. When we awaken to Paccuppanna Dhamma, the psychological burden of past trauma and future worry vanishes into the pristine clarity of the living present.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mirage of Past and Future**: Seeing that memory and imagination are biological electrical currents in current consciousness.
- **Resting in Paccuppanna Dhamma**: Establishing the mind in the only moment that actually exists.
- **The Freedom of the Unburdened Heart**: Dropping personal history to discover pristine awake presence.
