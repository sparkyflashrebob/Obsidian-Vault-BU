## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 118 (Ānāpānasati Sutta), MN 10 (Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct instructions on mindfulness of breathing (ānāpānasati) and resting as the silent unattached observer in the heart.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered to the international monastic community at Wat Pah Nanachat, Luang Por Sumedho offers intimate, practical instructions on meditation. Breath meditation is not a mechanical drill to attain hypnotic states, but a mirror reflecting the condition of the heart. By letting the breath be as it is—long, short, coarse, or subtle—we settle into the unconditioned space that knows the breath.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Breath as Natural Anchor**: Following the natural rhythm of inhalation and exhalation without manipulation.
- **The Observer vs. The Striver**: Shifting from trying to become a successful meditator to resting as the knowing presence (*Buddho*).
- **Relaxed Vigilance**: Combining somatic ease with alert, clear-eyed attentiveness.
