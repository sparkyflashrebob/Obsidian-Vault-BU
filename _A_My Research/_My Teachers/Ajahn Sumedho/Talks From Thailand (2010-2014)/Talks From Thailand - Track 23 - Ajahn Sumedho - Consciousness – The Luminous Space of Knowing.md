## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 38 (Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya Sutta), DN 11 (Kevaṭṭa Sutta - Consciousness Without Surface).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The nature of consciousness (viññāṇa); distinguishing sensory cognitive consciousness from the unconditioned, boundless knowing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this profound 62-minute exploration, Luang Por Sumedho investigates consciousness. While science treats consciousness as an accidental byproduct of neural circuitry, the Buddha pointed to consciousness as an experiential dimension that can be awakened. Luang Por guides meditators past the transient objects of consciousness—sights, sounds, thoughts—to the boundless, unconditioned knowing itself (*viññāṇaṁ anidassanaṁ*), which has no boundaries, no death, and no suffering.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Objects vs. The Knowing**: Learning to step back from what is known to the knowing presence.
- **Consciousness Without Surface (*Viññāṇaṁ Anidassanaṁ*)**: The luminous space where the four elements find no footing.
- **The End of Duality**: Discovering that subject and object resolve into pure unconditioned awareness.
