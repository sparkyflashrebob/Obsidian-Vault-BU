## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 26 (Ariyapariyesanā Sutta), Sn 1.8 (Karaṇīya Mettā Sutta), Jātaka tales.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Reflections on the Bodhisattva ideal from the Theravāda Forest Tradition perspective; integrating selfless service with non-attachment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Concluding the Talks From Thailand archive, Luang Por Sumedho explores 'The Bodhisattva Ideal.' Within Buddhism, debates often arise between the Mahāyāna Bodhisattva path and the Theravāda Arahant path. Luang Por transcends sectarian divides by showing that true Bodhisattva compassion and true Theravāda non-clinging are identical in the awakened heart. A life dedicated to the welfare of all beings is the natural expression of a mind free from self-view.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Beyond Sectarian Divisions**: Why arguing over Mahāyāna vs. Theravāda is a manifestation of personality view.
- **The Essence of the Bodhisattva**: Boundless, selfless compassion (*karuṇā*) operating without ego-attachment.
- **The Complete Circle**: Awakening from self to serve the world with joyful humility and unshakeable peace.
