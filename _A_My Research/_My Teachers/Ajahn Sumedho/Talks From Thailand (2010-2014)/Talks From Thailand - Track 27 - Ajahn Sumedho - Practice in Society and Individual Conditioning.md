## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: DN 31 (Sigālovāda Sutta), MN 128 (Upakkilesa Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Navigating the clash between personal psychological conditioning, societal expectations, and transcendent Dhamma practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this wide-ranging 58-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho examines how individual cultural conditioning interacts with spiritual practice. Every human being carries an enormous backpack of social conditioning: national prejudices, family traumas, and societal definitions of success. When we try to practice Dhamma through the lens of conditioning, we create confusion. Luang Por teaches how to live harmoniously in society while remaining completely free inside.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Backpack of Conditioning**: Unpacking the subconscious programs of our cultural upbringing.
- **Living in the World, Not of It**: The Buddhist art of fulfilling social duties without spiritual attachment.
- **Transcendent Freedom**: Discovering the pure heart that has no nationality, race, or social class.
