## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: SN 56.11 (Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta - Third Truth), SN 22.85.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The deep contemplative investigation of Nirodha (Cessation); realizing that what begins must end, and resting in the peace of ending.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive 52-minute discourse delivered in early 2011 at Wat Pah Nanachat, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Third Noble Truth: *Nirodha-sacca* (The Truth of Cessation). Meditators often fixate on arising phenomena—thoughts, sensations, states—and miss their ceasing. Luang Por instructs the Sangha to deliberately contemplate cessation: watching anger end, watching anxiety end, watching the breath end. In the space where conditions cease, the unconditioned peace of Nibbāna reveals itself.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Contemplating the Ending**: Shifting attention from how things arise to how things naturally vanish.
- **The Third Truth as Experience**: Realizing that cessation is not physical annihilation, but the cessation of craving.
- **The Cool Peace of Nibbāna**: Resting in the spacious silence that remains when an emotional crisis ends.
