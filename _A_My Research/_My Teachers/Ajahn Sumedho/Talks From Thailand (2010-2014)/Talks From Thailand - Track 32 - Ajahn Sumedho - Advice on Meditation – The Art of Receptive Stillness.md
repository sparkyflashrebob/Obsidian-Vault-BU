## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 118 (Ānāpānasati Sutta), MN 121 (Cūḷasuññata Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Refined meditative pointers given to monastics; cultivating effortless stillness through patient non-doing.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho provides refined meditation guidance to the Sangha gathered at Wat Pah Pong. Meditators constantly struggle because they treat meditation as an aggressive chore—trying to conquer thoughts, force stillness, or attain special absorptions. Luang Por emphasizes receptive stillness: letting the body relax, dropping the ambition to become an enlightened being, and resting as the unentangled knower.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Trap of Doing**: How the habit of 'doing' prevents the mind from discovering stillness.
- **Somatic Relaxation**: Dropping tension in the shoulders, eyes, jaw, and solar plexus.
- **Resting in Suññatā (Emptiness)**: Realizing that silence and peace are already present when manipulation ceases.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por tells of a time he meditated in a hut next to a noisy water pump at Wat Pah Pong, getting furious at the machine, until he realized the pump was simply pumping water—only his ego was generating the suffering.
