## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 26 (Ariyapariyesanā Sutta - The Noble Search), SN 55.5.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Exhortations to postulants and newly ordained monks taking on the robes; understanding the gravity and beauty of the holy life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich 63-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho gives intimate counsel to men preparing for ordination (*pabbajjā*) at Wat Pah Nanachat. Taking on the yellow robes of the Buddha is not joining a career or escaping worldly problems; it is a total commitment to the Noble Search. Luang Por warns candidates against idealizing monastic life, reminding them that their own emotional conditioning and restlessness will accompany them into the monastery, where it must be faced with unwavering honesty and awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Noble Search (*Ariyapariyesanā*)**: Choosing the timeless path of non-attachment over worldly acquisition.
- **Disillusionment as Awakening**: Why facing monastic loneliness, boredom, and doubt is an essential passage.
- **The Sacred Trust**: Honoring the lay community whose daily alms support the contemplative life.
