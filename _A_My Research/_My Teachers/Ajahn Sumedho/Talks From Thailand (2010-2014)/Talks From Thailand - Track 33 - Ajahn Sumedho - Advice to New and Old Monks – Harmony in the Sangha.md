## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Pong, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: MN 31 (Cūḷagosiṅga Sutta - Living in Concord), AN 6.12.

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Sustaining communal harmony; mutual respect between junior and senior monastics; humility as the bedrock of monastic longevity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this frank, practical 19-minute address to the assembled Sangha, Luang Por Sumedho speaks to both junior and senior monks. Junior monks can easily fall into fault-finding, arrogance, and impatience with tradition, while senior monks can become complacent, rigid, and entitled. Luang Por reminds all monastics that the beauty of the Sangha is 'blending like milk and water' through mutual deference, humility, and genuine mettā.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Duties of Junior Monks**: Cultivating patience, service, humility, and willingness to learn.
- **The Pitfalls of Seniority**: Guarding against complacency, spiritual entitlement, and harshness.
- **Sangha Harmony**: How mutual kindness and respect make the monastery a living sanctuary of the Dhamma.
