## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Wat Pah Nanachat, Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand, recorded during Luang Por Sumedho's historic post-retirement residency in Thailand following his step-down as Abbot of Amaravati in 2010.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: AN 4.24 (Kāḷaka Sutta - Suchness / Tathatā), MN 1 (Mūlapariyāya Sutta).

### Circumstances
Intimate, advanced monastic instructions given to junior and senior forest monks at Wat Pah Nanachat, Wat Pah Pong, and Dtao Dam, focusing on pure unconditioned awareness, ascetic discipline, and the living legacy of Venerable Ajahn Chah.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha of Wat Pah Nanachat and Wat Pah Pong, international monks, and visiting lay practitioners.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The trap of demanding reality conform to our ideals; finding supreme peace in Tathatā (suchness) through the phrase 'It should be like this'.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses a universal cause of spiritual suffering: the tyranny of 'should'. We believe the monastery should be quieter, monks should be kinder, weather should be cooler, and our meditation should be deeper. This chronic friction is rooted in aversion to reality. Luang Por introduces a powerful inversion: 'It should be like this!' Whatever is happening right now is the inevitable result of prior conditions. When we stop arguing with conditions and accept their suchness (*tathatā*), the mind returns to profound stillness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Tyranny of 'Should'**: How idealistic expectations blind us to present reality.
- **Tathatā (Suchness)**: Understanding that conditions can only ever be exactly the way they are right now.
- **The Relief of Surrender**: Stepping out of chronic complaining into effortless peace.
