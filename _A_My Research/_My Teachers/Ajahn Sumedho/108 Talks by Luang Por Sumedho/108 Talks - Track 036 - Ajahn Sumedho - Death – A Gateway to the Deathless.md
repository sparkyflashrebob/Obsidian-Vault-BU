## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Canonical Setting

### Circumstances
Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplation of mortality (*maraṇānussati*) as the supreme doorway to unconditioned liberation rather than morbid despair.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during a 1993 retreat, this deep 90-minute discourse unpacks how the honest confrontation with physical death dismantles trivial superficialities. When we accept our inevitable passing, the mind stops grasping at temporary illusions and turns toward the unconditioned witness that is untouched by death.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Maraṇānussati as Daily Practice**: Why recollecting death brings profound clarity, tenderness, and urgency (*saṁvega*).
- **The Fear of Annihilation**: Investigating the primal terror that the observing mind will cease to exist.
- **The Gateway Experience**: Moving from dread of the grave into peaceful reverence for the Deathless realm.
