### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The grand finale of the 108 Talks; Dhamma practice as the ultimate voyage from worldly madness and neurosis into the sublime sanity of the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Concluding the monumental 108 Talks collection, Luang Por Sumedho delivers the crowning discourse of his abbotship: 'Moving Towards Sanity.' Worldly life, driven by craving, vanity, and fear, is a collective form of madness (vipallāsa). The Buddha did not teach Buddhism as a dogmatic belief system or an esoteric ritual cult; he offered a practical path of psychological and spiritual sanity. Awakening is simply coming to our senses—stepping out of neurotic delusion into the clear, luminous sanity of this living moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Universal Neurosis of Samsara**: Recognizing that clinging to the transient is literally insane.
- **The Dhamma as Ultimate Sanity**: How mindfulness (sati) restores equilibrium, clarity, and genuine mental health.
- **A Lifetime of Refuge**: Luang Por's parting testament after decades of monastic leadership: resting in the Unborn, Uncreated, and Deathless.
