### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Mettā as boundless universal goodwill; moving beyond personal affection to the unconditional acceptance of our messy human reality and the world.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the practice of loving-kindness (*mettā*). Mettā is not an emotional sentimentality or forcing ourselves to like everyone; it is the deliberate refusal to harbor malice, resentment, or ill-will toward any living being, beginning with our own flawed humanity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Mettā vs. Liking**: Why we do not have to approve of bad behavior to extend boundless goodwill.
- **Self-Compassion as the Foundation**: Healing the harsh inner judge that constantly berates our imperfections.
- **Radiating into Boundless Space**: The traditional Brahma-vihāra cultivation: wishing well to all beings without exception.
