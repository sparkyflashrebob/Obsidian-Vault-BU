### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Relational wisdom; deconstructing romantic projections and the demand for other people to complete us, relating instead from the sovereign wholeness of Dhamma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1989 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho addresses human relationships. Conventional romance teaches us to seek a partner who will fulfill all our desires and banish loneliness—a guaranteed recipe for resentment. The 'perfect relationship' is our relationship with Dhamma: understanding that wholeness is already present in awakened awareness, allowing us to love and support others without codependency.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Fantasy of Being Completed**: Projecting our yearning for the unconditioned onto imperfect human partners.
- **Kalyāṇamittatā (Noble Friendship)**: Walking together toward liberation based on shared virtue and truth.
- **The Relationship with the Present Moment**: Meeting experience with unconditional acceptance.
