### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating *ārammaṇa* (mental objects and sensory supports); seeing how consciousness clings to objects and discovering the space that knows them.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this technical yet profoundly accessible discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Pāli term *ārammaṇa* (the object of consciousness). The untrained mind is obsessed with its objects—chasing pleasant ārammaṇas and battling unpleasant ones. Liberation occurs when attention shifts from the object to the silent space of knowing in which all ārammaṇas appear and disappear.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **What Is an Ārammaṇa?**: Sights, sounds, smells, physical feelings, and thoughts as sensory objects of consciousness.
- **The Addiction to Stimulation**: Why the human mind panics when sensory objects quiet down in silent retreat.
- **Resting in the Knowing Space**: Turning attention from the content of experience to the context of awareness.
