### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Concluding Part 2 in the year of Luang Por Chah's passing (1992); the indestructible spiritual beauty of a life dedicated to selfless integrity and truth.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1992—the year Venerable Ajahn Chah passed away—Luang Por Sumedho reflects on 'Spiritual Beauty.' Physical beauty decays with age, but the beauty of an awakened heart—steeped in loving-kindness, patience, humility, and wisdom—only deepens with time. This talk stands as a luminous tribute to the lineage of Ajahn Chah and a call for practitioners to embody that beauty in daily life.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Physical Decay vs. Spiritual Radiance**: Why old masters look more beautiful than glamorous youth.
- **The Legacy of Luang Por Chah**: How an illiterate forest monk from Northeast Thailand inspired thousands worldwide through genuine virtue.
- **Embodying the Path**: Taking responsibility to carry the torch of Dhamma forward into the next generation.
