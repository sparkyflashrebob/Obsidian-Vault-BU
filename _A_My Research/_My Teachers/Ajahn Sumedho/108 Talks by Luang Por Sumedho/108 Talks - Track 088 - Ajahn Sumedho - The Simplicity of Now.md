### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in the uncluttered, spontaneous simplicity of the present moment; letting go of the burden of spiritual ambition.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Concluding the 2007 winter retreat teachings, Luang Por Sumedho returns to 'The Simplicity of Now.' The thinking mind is endlessly complicated, always preparing for a future that never arrives. The present moment, by contrast, is completely simple: the breath comes in, the breath goes out; the body sits, the ear hears. Resting in this simplicity is the heart of Dhamma.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Burden of Conceptual Planning**: The exhaustion of living in future projections and hypothetical scenarios.
- **The Refreshing Taste of Now**: Noticing how peaceful reality is when we drop our demands on it.
- **Living with Radical Trust**: Trusting that awakened awareness knows how to respond to life without anxious plotting.
