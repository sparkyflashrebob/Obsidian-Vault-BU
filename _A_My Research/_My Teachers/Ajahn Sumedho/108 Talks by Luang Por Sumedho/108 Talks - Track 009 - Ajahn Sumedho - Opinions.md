### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The intoxicating trap of opinions, views (diṭṭhi), and dogmatic self-righteousness; observing how the ego manufactures conflicts out of the desire to be right.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1981, Luang Por Sumedho exposes the compulsive addiction to opinions. Human beings fight wars, break friendships, and torture themselves over views. By recognizing an opinion simply as a transient mental formation (*sankhāra*) rather than absolute identity, we awaken to the silent freedom of non-attachment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Arrogance of 'I Am Right'**: How holding views creates immediate division into 'us' vs. 'them'.
- **Opinions vs. Direct Knowing**: An opinion is a concept about reality; Dhamma is direct seeing of reality.
- **Letting Go of Religious Dogma**: Even Buddhist concepts become obstacles when clung to as sectarian superiority.
