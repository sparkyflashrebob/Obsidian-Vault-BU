### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Taking refuge in the inviolable sanctuary of the present moment (attadīpa); standing firmly on the island of awareness amidst the flood of samsara.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Reaching the centenary milestone of the 108 Talks, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on the Buddha's dying instruction: 'Dwell with yourselves as an island, with yourselves as a refuge, with no other refuge; dwell with the Dhamma as an island, with the Dhamma as a refuge, with no other refuge.' The raging waters of world crises, emotional turmoil, and bodily decay flood around us. Yet in the exact center of this moment lies an unshakeable island—the silent, luminous space of here and now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Flood of Samsara (Ogha)**: The torrential currents of sensual desire, becoming, views, and ignorance that sweep worldly beings away.
- **The Island of Dhamma**: Understanding the Buddha's exhortation to be an island unto oneself as resting in the unconditioned nature of awareness.
- **Centering in the Storm**: How to remain firmly planted on the island while external storms and institutional duties rage.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered

Luang Por recalls the devastating monsoons in Northeast Thailand where swollen rivers swept away huts and bridges, and how the monastics took refuge on small dry earthen knolls, watching the raging waters pass safely beneath them.
