### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how the mind fabricates its perceived reality through the six sense doors and mental proliferation (papañca).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1990 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Buddhist psychological definition of 'the world' (*loka*). The world is not merely an external planet; it is the subjective reality manufactured moment-by-moment when sensory contact (*phassa*) sparks feeling, perception, and mental storytelling. By dismantling this fabrication in meditation, the fabricated world ceases, revealing peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The World in the Six Sense Bases**: How eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind create the experience of universe.
- **The Factory of Papañca**: How a single perception cascades into a vast internal drama.
- **The Cessation of the World**: Standing outside the sensory contact loop through mindful stillness.
