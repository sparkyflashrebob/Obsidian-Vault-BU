### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The radical simplicity of awakened mind; peeling away intellectual complexity, philosophical jargon, and meditative contrivance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 2003 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho points to the 'Ultimate Simplicity' of the Dhamma. The ego loves complexity because complicated doctrines give it something to analyze and master. But the Truth is astonishingly simple: what begins must end; what is aware of this breath is already peaceful. Awakening is returning to this radical simplicity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Ego's Love of Complexity**: Why intellectual seekers prefer complicated systems over direct looking.
- **The Essence of the Path**: Noticing the arising, changing, and vanishing of conditioned phenomena.
- **Resting in the Uncomplicated**: Living with an uncluttered mind that responds naturally to whatever arises.
