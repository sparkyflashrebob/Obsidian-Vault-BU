### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Discovering the radiant, untroubled constancy of pure conscious space that remains pristine through all emotional storms.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this subtle 2009 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho guides attention to 'The Constancy of the Supernal Mind.' Worldly emotional states are erratic, turbulent, and fragile. Yet beneath the transient weather of joy, depression, fear, and desire lies a luminous conscious substrate that is constant, unshakeable, and serene. Awakening is recognizing this natural luminosity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Turbulence of Mental Weather**: Acknowledging that thoughts and emotions are biological weather patterns.
- **The Supernal Substrate**: Tuning into the luminous, spacious knowing (*pabhassara citta*) that is never stained by defilements.
- **Constancy Amidst Change**: Resting in the uncreated presence that remains identical in youth, old age, health, and sickness.
