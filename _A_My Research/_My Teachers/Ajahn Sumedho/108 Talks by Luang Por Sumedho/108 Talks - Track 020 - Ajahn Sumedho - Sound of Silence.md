### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The historic, definitive introduction to the Sound of Silence (nāda)—the primordial inner background vibration of awareness used as an unshakeable contemplative anchor.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1986, this landmark discourse marks Luang Por Sumedho's definitive public introduction to the 'Sound of Silence' (*nāda*). When the attention turns inward, an subtle, high-pitched, ringing sound is audible in the background of consciousness. Unlike thoughts, moods, and sensory objects, this sound is constant, peaceful, and empty of self. By using it as a meditation object, the mind easily slips out of discursive thought into the stillness of the unconditioned.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Discovering the Inner Sound**: Tuning into the cosmic ringing sound present whenever listening attentively.
- **Not Tinnitus or Pathology**: An ancient contemplative anchor known in Indian tradition as *nāda* or the cosmic sound.
- **A Portable Anchor in Daily Life**: Listening to the sound while walking, working, or riding the subway to silence discursive commentary.
- **Gateway to the Unconditioned**: Using the sound to disengage from emotional dramas and rest in pure aware space.
