### Theme of the Dharma Talk
A masterly exposition of *Sammā-Sati*; separating genuine Dhamma mindfulness from modern superficial concentration techniques.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive 75-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho explores mindfulness (*sati*) as the supreme guardian of the spiritual heart. Mindfulness is not merely paying attention to sensory details; it is the reflective capacity to remember the Truth: knowing that all conditioned things are impermanent, unsatisfactory, and not-self.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The True Scope of Sati**: Moving beyond mechanical attention to reflective ethical mindfulness.
- **Guarding the Sense Doors**: How sati prevents sensory contact from exploding into lust or resentment.
- **The Continuity of Sati**: Weaving mindfulness throughout walking, eating, chores, and resting.
