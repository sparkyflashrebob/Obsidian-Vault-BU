- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the space between thoughts, words, breaths, and objects; resting in the background stillness that contains all form.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides attention to 'The Space Between Things.' We are trained to notice objects—the furniture in the room, the words in a sentence, the sensations in the body. But every object is surrounded by space. By consciously attending to the silent gaps between words and thoughts, the mind naturally enters stillness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Foreground vs. Background**: Shifting attention from foreground objects to background space.
- **The Space Between Thoughts**: Discovering that thoughts do not form a continuous wall; there is open silence between them.
- **Space as the Image of Nibbāna**: Space has no owner, cannot be burned, damaged, or stained.
