### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The radiant beauty and psychological safety of ethical restraint (sīla); how harmlessness purifies the heart and establishes unshakeable self-respect.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1987, Luang Por Sumedho illuminates the profound beauty of morality. Sīla is not a list of rigid religious prohibitions imposed by an external authority. It is the natural, graceful expression of an awakened heart. When we live without harming, stealing, or lying, the mind is free from guilt and fear, becoming a fragrant sanctuary of peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sīla as Inner Radiance**: Unlike physical cosmetics, virtue never ages or loses its charm.
- **The Blessing of Non-Remorse (Avippaṭisāra)**: How clean ethical conduct is the prerequisite for deep meditative absorption (*samādhi*).
- **Harmlessness as Global Responsibility**: The profound ecological and social healing born of voluntary ethical restraint.
