### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Historical discourse commemorating 25 years of Cittaviveka; the consecration of the newly built Dhamma Hall and the endurance of monastic lineage.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on a momentous occasion in 2004, Luang Por Sumedho consecrates the magnificent new Dhamma Hall at Cittaviveka (Chithurst). He looks back 25 years to 1979 when the Sangha first arrived at a ruined, overgrown Victorian house. This talk honors the faith of the lay supporters, the dedication of the monastics, and the living power of Ajahn Chah's lineage.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **25 Years of Cittaviveka**: Reflecting on the miraculous journey from a damp ruin to a flourishing monastic community.
- **The True Purpose of a Dhamma Hall**: A consecrated space dedicated to silence, reflection, and liberation.
- **Lineage as Living Fire**: Carrying forward the discipline (*Vinaya*) and teachings of the Forest Tradition for future generations.
