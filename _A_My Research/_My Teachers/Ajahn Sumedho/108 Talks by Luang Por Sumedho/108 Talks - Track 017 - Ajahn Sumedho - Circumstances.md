### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Meeting the inevitable fluctuations of human circumstances—praise, blame, gain, loss, pleasure, pain—with equanimity and wise perspective.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1986 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho addresses how practitioners react to external circumstances. Worldly life constantly swings between praise and blame, success and failure. If our peace depends on favorable conditions, we will remain emotional hostages to the world. True refuge is in the unshakeable knowing that observes all circumstances with spacious equanimity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Eight Worldly Dhammas**: Gain and loss, pleasure and pain, praise and blame, fame and disrepute.
- **Circumstances as Teachers**: Difficult people, cold weather, and illness seen as prime meditation objects.
- **The Unconditioned Eye**: Maintaining inner stillness regardless of whether worldly plans succeed or fail.
