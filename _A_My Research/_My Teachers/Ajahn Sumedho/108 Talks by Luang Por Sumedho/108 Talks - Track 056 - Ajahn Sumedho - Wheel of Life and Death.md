### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Bhavacakra (Wheel of Becoming); how craving and ignorance drive the cyclical momentum of birth and death, and how mindfulness halts the wheel.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive 80-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Buddhist teaching on Dependent Origination (*paṭiccasamuppāda*) and the Wheel of Life. We do not need to wait for physical rebirth to see the wheel in motion; every time contact leads to feeling, feeling leads to craving, and craving leads to becoming (*bhava*), a new birth in samsāra takes place.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mechanics of Samsāra**: Tracing the links from sensory contact (*phassa*) to emotional entanglement and identity birth.
- **Breaking the Chain at Vedanā**: Learning to feel pleasant or painful sensations without fueling them with craving or aversion.
- **Halting the Wheel**: How mindful awareness steps off the carousel of endless mental becoming.
