### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How unconditioned awareness intersects with the fleeting flow of human time and mortality; living as a conscious conduit of the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the mystical yet practical intersection between time and eternity. As human organisms, we are bound to time: our bodies age, the monastery clock ticks, and the seasons turn. Yet conscious awareness itself is timeless (*akāliko*). The spiritual life is the conscious poised balance right at this intersection—living responsibly in time while anchored in the Timeless.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Dual Citizenship of the Practitioner**: Living in conventional time (*sammuti*) while resting in ultimate reality (*paramattha*).
- **Aging as the Great Clock**: Using physical wrinkles and declining energy as relentless pointers to the Deathless.
- **The Timeless Witness of the Hour**: Noticing that the aware space knowing this exact minute has never aged.
