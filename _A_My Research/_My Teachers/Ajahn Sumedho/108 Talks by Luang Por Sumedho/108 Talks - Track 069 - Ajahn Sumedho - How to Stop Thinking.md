### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Practical skillful means for working with compulsive discursive thought; shifting from thought-combat to receptive listening.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this classic practical talk, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the most common meditation struggle: 'How to Stop Thinking.' Trying to stop thinking with willpower is like trying to smooth water with a flatiron—it only creates more turbulence. Luang Por reveals how listening to thought as inner sound naturally leads to effortless silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Futility of Thought-Combat**: Why fighting mental chatter only strengthens the ego's investment in thinking.
- **Listening to the Inner Voice**: Treating discursive thought as an auditory phenomenon in awareness.
- **The Sound of Silence as Anchor**: Tuning into the *nāda* pitch, where conceptual proliferation cannot survive.
