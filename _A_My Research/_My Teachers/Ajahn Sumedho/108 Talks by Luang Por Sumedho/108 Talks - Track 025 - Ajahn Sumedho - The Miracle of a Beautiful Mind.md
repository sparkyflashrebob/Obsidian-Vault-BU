### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Recognizing the innate luminosity and pristine beauty of consciousness (pabhassara-citta) when unburdened by adventitious defilements.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1989, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the Buddha's teaching that the mind is naturally luminous and pure, but obscured by visiting defilements (*upakkilesa*). A 'beautiful mind' is not one filled with clever philosophical arguments, but one rooted in gentleness, non-harming, and unentangled presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Primordial Ground of Consciousness**: Awareness itself has no gender, nationality, anger, or greed; it is pristine knowing.
- **Defilements as Visiting Guests**: Greed, hatred, and confusion arrive and depart like weather; they are not the home itself.
- **The Miracle of Non-Attachment**: Stepping back to let the natural luminosity of the heart shine forth.
