### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The threefold training (tisso sikkhā)—virtue (sīla), collected stillness (samādhi), and wisdom (paññā)—as the highest spiritual gift (dāna) offered to community and world.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1981 at Cittaviveka, Luang Por Sumedho reframes the threefold training. Rather than seeing ethical rules and meditation as grim monastic duties, he illuminates them as a radiant gift. Sīla gives fearlessness to all beings; samādhi brings unshakeable presence; paññā liberates the mind from greed, hatred, and delusion.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sīla as Fearlessness**: Living without killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying, or intoxication creates an aura of safety around oneself.
- **Samādhi as Emotional Sanctuary**: A collected mind does not pollute the environment with agitation, irritability, or demand.
- **Paññā as Final Refuge**: Wisdom sees through conditioned conventions without nihilism or despair.
