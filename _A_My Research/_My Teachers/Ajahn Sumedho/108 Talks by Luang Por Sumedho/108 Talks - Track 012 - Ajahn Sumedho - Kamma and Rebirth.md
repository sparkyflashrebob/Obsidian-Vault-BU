- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Chithurst Buddhist Monastery (Cittaviveka), West Sussex, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Understanding kamma and rebirth not as fatalistic speculation or metaphysical dogma, but as the living reality of intentional action and its psychological momentum.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1982, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the Dhamma teaching on kamma. Kamma is volition (*cetanā*). Every intentional act of body, speech, and mind conditions consciousness. Rebirth is not merely what happens after physical death; the sense of 'I am' is reborn thousands of times each day through grasping at thoughts and desires.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Kamma as Intention**: Action without volition carries no kammic weight; the quality of heart determines the result.
- **Moment-to-Moment Rebirth**: Being reborn as angry, jealous, greedy, or peaceful through identification with mind states.
- **Liberation from Kammic Momentum**: Resting in the Knower (*Buddho*) ends the cycle of kammic becoming.
