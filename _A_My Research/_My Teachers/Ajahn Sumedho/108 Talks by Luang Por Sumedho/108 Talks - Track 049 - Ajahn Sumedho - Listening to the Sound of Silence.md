### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Practical instruction for tuning into the inner cosmic pitch (*nāda* / sound of silence) as an unconditioned contemplative anchor.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho offers comprehensive guidance on his signature meditation practice: listening to the sound of silence (*nāda-sound*). This subtle, high-pitched inner vibration is not a physical ear disorder (tinnitus) or personal creation; it is an ever-present acoustic reflection of conscious space that immediately quietens discursive thought.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Finding the Sound of Silence**: How to listen inward to the high-frequency ringing present in background awareness.
- **An Object that Cuts Off Thinking**: Why discursive mental chatter cannot easily coexist with listening to the nāda sound.
- **The Sound of Nature**: Using the inner hum as a constant companion throughout the working day and sitting practice.
