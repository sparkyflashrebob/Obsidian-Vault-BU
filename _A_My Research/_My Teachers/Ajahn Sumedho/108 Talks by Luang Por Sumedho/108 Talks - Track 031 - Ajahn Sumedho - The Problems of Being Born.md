### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating physical birth (jāti) as the foundational vulnerability that conditions aging, sickness, physical frailty, and death.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1991, Luang Por Sumedho explores the sobering reality of physical birth (*jāti*). We celebrate birthdays with cakes and parties, but in Dhamma, birth is the entry into a sensitive, vulnerable biological vehicle subject to cold, heat, disease, and dissolution. By understanding that physical vulnerability is natural to birth, we stop taking bodily aches, sickness, and old age as personal grievances.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Birth as the Root Condition**: With birth (*jāti*) as condition, aging and death (*jarāmaraṇa*) inevitably follow.
- **The Body as Sensitive Organism**: Why bodily discomfort is natural, not a sign that something has gone wrong.
- **Finding the Unborn**: The ultimate spiritual escape from birth and death through awakening to the deathless.
