### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Noble Eightfold Path as a unified experiential reality; seeing how virtue, stillness, and wisdom interlock.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 62-minute exposition, Luang Por Sumedho walks practitioners through 'The Path to Liberation.' The Noble Eightfold Path is not an academic checklist or sequential obstacle course; it is an organic, interlocking whole. Right View inspires ethical non-harming (*sīla*), virtue steadies the mind (*samādhi*), and collected stillness uncovers liberating wisdom (*paññā*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Eightfold Path as an Organic Unity**: Understanding how each factor supports and feeds the others.
- **Right View as the North Star**: Perceiving that all conditions are impermanent, unsatisfactory, and not-self.
- **The Path as a Living Vehicle**: Riding the raft of practice across the flood of samsāra to the unconditioned shore.
