### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Personal memoir and historical reflections on training under Venerable Ajahn Chah in the late 1960s and 1970s; the rigorous beauty of the Forest Tradition.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich 72-minute historical retrospective, Luang Por Sumedho recounts his earliest days as the first Western monk at Wat Pah Pong in 1967. He describes the intense physical austerity: eating once a day out of an alms bowl, living with scorpions and poisonous centipedes, speaking no Thai, and having every Western preconception broken by Ajahn Chah's uncompromising wisdom and warmth.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Arrival in Ubon Ratchathani**: How an American ex-naval officer found himself in an austere jungle monastery in 1967.
- **The Crucible of Monastic Training**: Surviving malaria, language barriers, and exhausting daily physical labor.
- **The Genius of Ajahn Chah**: Remembering Ajahn Chah's boundless humor, laser-sharp intuition, and unconditional compassion.
