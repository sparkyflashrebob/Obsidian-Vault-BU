### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The wordless, ever-present reality of consciousness; that which cannot be spoken or described, yet is immediately known as our true refuge.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 78-minute contemplation, Luang Por Sumedho points to 'Unspoken Immanent Awareness.' Words, concepts, doctrines, and suttas are pointers; they can never capture the living reality of awareness. Awareness cannot be spoken because it is the witness of speech. By stepping back from the spoken into the unspoken, we touch the Unborn.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Limits of Language**: Why the profoundest truths of the Dhamma remain beyond the vocabulary of intellect.
- **Immanent and Transcendental**: Understanding how awareness is simultaneously right here in ordinary sensation and free from conditions.
- **Resting in the Unspoken**: Learning to abide in wordless presence without feeling the nervous urge to narrate our experience.
