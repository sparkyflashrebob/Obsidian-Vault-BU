### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Transcending identity view (sakkāya-diṭṭhi); recognizing that personality, body, and opinions are transient conditioned formations rather than an enduring self.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1985 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners to transcend the illusion of a personal self. The personality is a conventional social convenience, not an ultimate fact. By observing thoughts, physical sensations, and emotional patterns without claiming ownership, the mind awakens to unconditioned consciousness (*Buddho*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Convention of 'I Am'**: Using pronouns ('I', 'me', 'mine') skillfully for worldly communication without believing in them.
- **The Five Khandhas as Empty Phenomena**: Body, feeling, perception, mental fabrications, and sensory consciousness observed as impermanent ripples.
- **Freedom from Self-Improvement**: Liberation is not perfecting the ego, but awakening from the dream of ego entirely.
