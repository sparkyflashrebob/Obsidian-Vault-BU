### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Realizing the Third Noble Truth (*Nirodha* / Cessation) in the present moment rather than postponing liberation to an elusive future attainment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho challenges the subtle assumption that liberation is something to be achieved ten years from now after endless ascetic struggle. In the Buddha's Dhamma, whenever craving stops, suffering ceases right then and there. The end of suffering is not a distant goal; it is a present reality to be realized.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Postponing Enlightenment**: How spiritual ambition turns the path into a future fantasy that ignores current reality.
- **The Direct Cessation of Wanting**: Witnessing the peaceful stillness that arises the moment craving is dropped.
- **The Third Noble Truth as Direct Experience**: Realizing Nirodha not as an abstract dogma, but as the quiet heart here and now.
