### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Signs (*nimitta*) of the transcendent in ordinary worldly life; how disillusionment with samsāra becomes the seed of awakening.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 1998 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on 'Sign of Transcendence.' When the promises of worldly success, sensory pleasure, and material comfort inevitably fail to bring lasting peace, that disillusionment is not a catastrophe—it is the sacred sign pointing beyond samsāra.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Blessing of Disappointment**: Why feeling unsatisfied with ordinary worldly life is a sign of spiritual maturity.
- **The Divine Messengers**: Old age, sickness, and death as heralds reminding us of what is truly essential.
- **Awakening to the Transcendent**: Shifting loyalty from temporary worldly conditions to the Dhamma.
