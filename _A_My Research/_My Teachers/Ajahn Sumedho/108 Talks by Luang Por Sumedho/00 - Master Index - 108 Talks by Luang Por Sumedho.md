# 108 Talks by Luang Por Sumedho — Master Archival Index & Contemplative Compendium

> [!NOTE] Archival Provenance & Retrospective Significance
> Published by **Amaravati Buddhist Monastery** to mark Venerable Ajahn Sumedho's formal retirement as abbot in 2010, the **108 Talks** collection represents the definitive historical and contemplative retrospective of his 32 years of teaching in the West.
> 
> Spanning from **1978 to 2010**, these 108 discourses chronicle the pioneering establishment of the Thai Forest Tradition outside Southeast Asia: from the initial damp winter days at the Hampstead Buddhist Vihara, to the arduous physical renovation of Chithurst House (Cittaviveka), the consecration of Amaravati, and the universal pointing to the *Sound of Silence*, *Buddho*, and the *Unconditioned*.
