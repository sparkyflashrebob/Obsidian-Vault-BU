### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Sustaining resolute faith (saddhā) in the innate nobility and goodness of human beings despite global catastrophes, wars, and cynicism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
With the world reeling from economic crises and ongoing geopolitical conflicts in 2010, Luang Por Sumedho delivers an uplifting reflection on 'Faith in the Goodness of Humanity.' It is exceedingly easy to become cynical, misanthropic, and despairing when consuming daily news media. But Luang Por reminds the Sangha that the fundamental nature of the human heart (pabhassara citta) is pure, radiant, and drawn toward truth. Awakening restores our faith in each other and inspires tireless compassionate action.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Scourge of Worldly Cynicism**: How constant exposure to catastrophe tempts the mind into hopeless bitterness.
- **The Luminous Nature of the Heart**: Remembering that defilements are merely adventitious visitors (āgantukā kilesā).
- **Faith (Saddhā) as an Active Spiritual Power**: Channeling trust in human potential to build communities of peace and sanity.
