### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Experiential guided sitting: allowing the sensory constructs of 'the world' to cease within awareness, uncovering the uncreated peace beyond suffering.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered as the final discourse of the 20th century at Amaravati in late 1999, this guided meditation invites practitioners to 'let the world end.' In the Buddha's Dhamma, the world (*loka*) is whatever arises at the six sense doors. By stepping back and witnessing conditions cease, the world ends right here and now in the awakened heart.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Buddha's Definition of the World**: How 'the world' is constructed second by second through eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
- **Allowing Conditions to Die**: Releasing the nervous habit of constantly sustaining worldly worries and projections.
- **The Uncreated Dawn**: Resting in the unshakeable stillness that remains when the psychological world comes to an end.
