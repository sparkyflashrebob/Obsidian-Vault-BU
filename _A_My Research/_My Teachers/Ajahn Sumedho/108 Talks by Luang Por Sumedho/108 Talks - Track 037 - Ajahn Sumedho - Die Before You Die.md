### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Psychological ego-death: dying to entitlement, pride, identity, and personal history while the physical organism is still alive.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the ancient monastic maxim to 'die before you die.' When we willingly let our self-importance, reputation, and conditioned opinions die right now in meditation, physical death loses all power over us. True fearlessness arises when there is no self left to defend.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Burden of Self-Importance**: The exhausting labor required to sustain a personal image in the world.
- **Surrendering the Narrative**: Letting go of the compulsive need to justify past grievances or future ambitions.
- **The Joy of Emptiness**: Experiencing the boundless relief of being 'nobody special.'
