Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Heedfulness (*appamāda*) as the unfailing portal to the Deathless realm; dismantling complacency in practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho focuses on the opening verse of the Dhammapada's Appamādavagga: 'Heedfulness is the deathless path, heedlessness is the path to death.' Mindful attention is not a casual hobby; it is the active guardianship of the heart that opens the gate to liberation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Danger of Complacency**: How habitual routines and comfortable monastery life can lull the mind into heedlessness.
- **Appamāda as Active Vigilance**: Staying awake and aware in ordinary transitional moments.
- **Passing Through the Gate**: Recognizing that every single moment offers an open doorway to the Unconditioned.
