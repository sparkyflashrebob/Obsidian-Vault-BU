### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The true temple is within; crossing the threshold from worldly mental noise into the inner sanctuary of the Deathless.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho speaks on 'Entering the Sacred Place.' Human beings build stone temples, cathedrals, and stupas to honor the sacred. But the true holy temple is not built with hands; it is the silent, pure sanctuary of the awakened heart. Whenever we step back from self-centered desire and rest in stillness, we cross the threshold into the sacred place.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Outer Temple vs. The Inner Sanctuary**: Recognizing that buildings are symbols pointing to inner truth.
- **Crossing the Threshold**: Letting go of shoes, worldly concerns, and personal baggage as we enter practice.
- **Living from the Sacred Ground**: Bringing the reverence and silence of the shrine room into every daily interaction.
