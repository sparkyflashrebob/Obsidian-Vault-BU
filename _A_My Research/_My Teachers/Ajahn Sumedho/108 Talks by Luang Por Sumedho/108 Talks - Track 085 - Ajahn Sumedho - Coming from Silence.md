### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Acting, speaking, and teaching from the unconditioned silence of the heart rather than from neurotic self-will.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho reflects on 'Coming from Silence.' Most human speech and action comes from a restless urge to fill the void, defend an opinion, or prove self-worth. When we train the mind to dwell in inner silence (*nāda*), our words and actions emerge spontaneously from wisdom and kindness, unburdened by egoic calculation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Action from Ego vs. Action from Silence**: Discerning whether our words are driven by nervous tension or clear wisdom.
- **The Sound of Silence as Compass**: Tuning into the inner pitch before speaking or making decisions.
- **The Power of Stillness**: Why an awakened being transforms the room simply by being silent and present.
