### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The direct experiential realization of Cessation (Nirodha)—discovering the unconditioned peace that emerges when craving (taṇhā) is completely relinquished.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1985 during the founding year of Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Luang Por Sumedho explores the Third Noble Truth: Nirodha Sacca. Cessation is not an abstract metaphysical theory, but an immediately verifiable reality (*sandiṭṭhiko*). When the mind ceases chasing pleasant sensations and stops resisting unpleasant ones, the fever of craving cools down into the pristine peace of Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Cessation as Immediate Reality**: Nirodha is experienced whenever an emotional contraction, desire, or worry ceases in awareness.
- **The Three Forms of Craving**: Understanding sensual craving (*kāma-taṇhā*), craving to become (*bhava-taṇhā*), and craving to get rid of (*vibhava-taṇhā*).
- **Resting in the Coolness of Nibbāna**: Nibbāna literally means 'blown out' or 'extinguished'—the end of the fire of greed, hatred, and delusion.
