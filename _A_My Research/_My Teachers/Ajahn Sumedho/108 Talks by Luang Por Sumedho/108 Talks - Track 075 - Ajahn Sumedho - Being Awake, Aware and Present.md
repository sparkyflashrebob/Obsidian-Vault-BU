### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The triad of contemplative freedom: waking up from the trance of conditioning, knowing awareness, and grounding in the living present.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this rich 75-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho articulates the core practice of the awakened heart: being awake, aware, and present. We do not need special states of trance or esoteric initiations; we only need to wake up from our self-referential daydreams and inhabit the living truth of this very moment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Waking from the Daydream**: Recognizing how much of daily life is spent sleepwalking in memories and worries.
- **Awareness as Our True Nature**: Remembering that awareness is not something we have to manufacture; it is already awake.
- **Grounding in the Fathom-Long Body**: Reconnecting with the physical senses as the anchor of presence.
