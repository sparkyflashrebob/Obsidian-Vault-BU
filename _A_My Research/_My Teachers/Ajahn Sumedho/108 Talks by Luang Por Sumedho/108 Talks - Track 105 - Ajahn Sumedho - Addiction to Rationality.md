### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The limitations of the discursive intellect; transcending intellectual addiction to enter the intuitive wisdom of direct awareness (paññā).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses a defining malady of Western culture: 'Addiction to Rationality.' While the logical intellect is a superb tool for science, engineering, and daily logistics, it is completely incapable of freeing the human heart from suffering. The mind insists on figuring everything out, analyzing its neuroses, and demanding rational explanations for existence. Luang Por demonstrates that spiritual awakening is not an intellectual deduction, but an intuitive opening (ñāṇa-dassana) that transcends rational thought.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Western Rationalist Habit**: Why educated modern seekers struggle so intensely to silence analytical chatter.
- **The Limits of Deductive Logic**: Recognizing that existential peace cannot be argued or thought into existence.
- **The Leap into Intuitive Wisdom**: Cultivating the 'listening mind' that rests prior to logical categorization.
