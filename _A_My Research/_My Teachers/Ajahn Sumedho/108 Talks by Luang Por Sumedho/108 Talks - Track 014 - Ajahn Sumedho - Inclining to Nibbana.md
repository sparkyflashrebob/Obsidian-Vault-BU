### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Chithurst Buddhist Monastery (Cittaviveka), West Sussex, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Inclining the heart away from conditioned phenomena toward the peace of Nibbāna—the cool, unconditioned reality of cessation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1984 as the community prepared to establish Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho reflects on 'inclining toward Nibbāna.' Like the river Ganges naturally inclines, slopes, and flows toward the ocean, a mind that ceases clinging naturally flows into the unconditioned stillness of cessation (*nirodha*). Nibbāna is not a distant reward after death; it is the cool peace experienced whenever grasping ceases.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Metaphor of the Ganges**: Practice is not pushing a river uphill, but removing the dams that block the natural flow.
- **Nibbāna as Coolness**: Literal meaning of extinguished fire; the cooling of the fires of greed, hatred, and delusion.
- **Recognizing the Space Between Thoughts**: The unconditioned is present in every gap of stillness.
