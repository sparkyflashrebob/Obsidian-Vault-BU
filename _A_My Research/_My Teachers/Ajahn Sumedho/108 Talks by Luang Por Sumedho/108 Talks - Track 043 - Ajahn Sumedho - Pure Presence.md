## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance
Delivered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK, preserved in the historic *108 Talks by Luang Por Sumedho* retrospective collection published by the Amaravati Buddhist Monastery archive.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: Ud 1.10 (Bāhiya Sutta), SN 35.95 (Mālukyaputta Sutta).

### Circumstances
Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
'In the seen, only the seen'; abiding as raw, unentangled, pure awareness without generating secondary mental stories.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Reflecting on the famous instruction to Bāhiya Dārucīriya, Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into 'Pure Presence.' When seeing, there is just the seen; when hearing, just the heard. When no subjective 'I' is added onto sensory contact, the root of all suffering instantly dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Bāhiya Instruction**: Understanding how identity (*asmi-māna*) is artificially superimposed upon sensory data.
- **Stopping at the Senses**: Experiencing sights, sounds, smells, and sensations without spinning opinions.
- **Abiding as Unentangled Knowing**: Resting in the awareness that has no location, no boundary, and no gender.
