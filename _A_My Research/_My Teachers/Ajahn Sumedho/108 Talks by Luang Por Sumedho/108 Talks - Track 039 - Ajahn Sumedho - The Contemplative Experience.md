### Theme of the Dharma Talk
What distinguishes authentic contemplative inquiry (*yoniso manasikāra*) from academic philosophy or mere daydreaming.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 1994 discourse, Luang Por Sumedho clarifies the core of the contemplative life. Contemplation is not philosophizing about Dhamma concepts; it is turning attention inward to examine the raw texture of conscious experience as it actually unfolds in real time.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Reflective Investigation**: Using questions not to seek cerebral answers, but to open a silent space of listening.
- **Seeing Through Opinions**: Noticing how strongly we hold dogmatic positions without examining their emptiness.
- **Trusting Direct Knowing**: Relying on personal observation rather than secondhand spiritual authority.
