### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Somatic body contemplation combined with loving-kindness; dissolving knotting and physical contractions with warm, radiant acceptance.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 1999 meditation discourse, Luang Por Sumedho teaches a practical somatic approach to metta bhavana. Instead of repeating dry verbal formulas, we systematically sweep warm, benevolent awareness through the physical tissues, releasing chronic tightness in the belly, chest, face, and spine.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Somatic Contractions as Frozen Fear**: Recognizing that tension in our stomach or chest is natural bodily protection.
- **Sweeping Warm Awareness**: Bathing each muscle group in gentle, non-judgmental acceptance.
- **Radiating from the Center**: Allowing physical ease to expand outwards as boundless goodwill to all beings.
