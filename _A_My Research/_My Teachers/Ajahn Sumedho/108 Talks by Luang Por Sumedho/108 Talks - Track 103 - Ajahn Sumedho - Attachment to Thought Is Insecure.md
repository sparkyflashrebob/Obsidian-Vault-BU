### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The chronic instability of conceptual proliferation (papañca); stepping back from thoughts to the indestructible security of pure awareness.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this sharp and liberating discourse, Luang Por Sumedho diagnoses the core mechanism of existential anxiety: 'Attachment to thought is insecure.' Thoughts are inherently unstable, fragmentary, and fleeting; seeking lasting security in beliefs, opinions, philosophical formulations, or self-narratives is like building a castle on shifting sand. Luang Por directs us to investigate the space between thoughts—the silent, unattached witness that never fluctuates.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Nature of Thought (Saṅkhāras)**: Recognizing that every thought has a beginning, a middle, and an end.
- **The Castle on Sand**: Why no philosophy, theology, or political doctrine can ever provide existential security.
- **The Space Between Thoughts**: Tuning into the silent unconditioned consciousness that remains when thought ceases.
