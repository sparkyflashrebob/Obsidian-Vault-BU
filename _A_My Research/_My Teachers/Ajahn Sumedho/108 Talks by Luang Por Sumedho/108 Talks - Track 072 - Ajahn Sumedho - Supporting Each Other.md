### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Communal harmony and Sangha practice; reflecting on the Buddha's acrobat parable: 'By taking care of yourself, you take care of others.'

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho examines 'Supporting Each Other' through the lens of the famous Sedaka Sutta. An acrobat carrying an assistant on a bamboo pole tells the assistant: 'You watch me, and I'll watch you.' The assistant replies: 'No, master! You balance yourself, and I'll balance myself; thus protected, we perform safely.' Real communal support begins with internal mindfulness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Acrobat Parable**: Why inner balance is the greatest contribution we can make to our family and community.
- **Codependency vs. Spiritual Friendship**: Distinguishing neurotic enabling from wise, empowering companionship (*kalyāṇa-mittatā*).
- **Harmonious Communal Living**: Practicing in community as a spiritual crucible where personal preferences are surrendered.
