- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme canonical refuge of *Ajāta* (The Unborn); resting in the awareness that has never been born and therefore can never die.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho delivers a profound exposition on the famous Udāna declaration: 'There is, bhikkhus, an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned...' Everything in our sensory world—bodies, feelings, opinions, galaxies—is born and subject to death. But that which is aware of birth and death was never born. Awakening is shifting identity from what is born to the Unborn.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Realm of the Born (*Jāta*)**: Recognizing that all physical and mental phenomena have a beginning and an end.
- **The Canonical Reality of the Unborn**: Understanding why Nibbāna is not annihilation, but uncreated reality.
- **Resting as the Unborn**: Abiding as timeless awareness rather than struggling as a mortal being trying to become immortal.
