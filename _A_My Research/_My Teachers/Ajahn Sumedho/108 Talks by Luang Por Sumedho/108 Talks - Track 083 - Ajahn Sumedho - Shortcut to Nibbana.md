### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The direct, immediate shortcut to unconditioned peace: recognizing that which knows desire right now without becoming the desire.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho points to the direct 'Shortcut to Nibbāna.' Many believe Nibbāna requires decades of painful ascetic torment. But the Buddha showed that whenever there is seeing, there is just the seen; whenever there is desire, there is that which knows the desire. By stepping back from the wanting mind into the unconditioned witness, Nibbāna is touched right now.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Endless Scenic Route**: How the mind turns meditation into an elaborate, never-ending project of self-improvement.
- **The Direct Doorway**: Asking: 'What is aware of this thought right now?'
- **Resting in the Unborn**: Discovering that peace is not an accomplishment, but the natural state of awareness free from grasping.
