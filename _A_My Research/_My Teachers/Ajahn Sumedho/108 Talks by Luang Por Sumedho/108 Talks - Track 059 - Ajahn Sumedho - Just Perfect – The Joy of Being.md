### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in the intrinsic perfection of the present moment; discovering uncaused joy (*pīti* / *sukha*) that does not depend on pleasant sensory conditions.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores 'The Joy of Being.' The calculating intellect always finds something wrong: the room is too cold, the tea is too sweet, our meditation is too distracted. But from the perspective of awakened awareness, the present moment is already 'just perfect' because it is simply the way reality is (*tathatā*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Chronic Fault-Finder**: How the conditioned mind is biologically wired to spot defects and complain.
- **Suchness (*Tathatā*)**: Seeing that whatever is present is completely natural given its causes.
- **Uncaused Spiritual Joy**: Tasting the delight of existing without needing external entertainment.
