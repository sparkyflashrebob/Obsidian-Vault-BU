### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The noble task of the First Noble Truth: suffering is to be understood (pariññeyya), not resisted, condemned, or fled from.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1991 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho reiterates the central axiom of his life's teaching: Dukkha is to be understood. Most humans spend their lives running away from suffering through entertainment, food, romance, and busyness. By having the courage to turn toward discomfort, investigate its nature, and hold it in compassionate awareness, suffering reveals its own cessation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Task of the First Truth**: Suffering is not a failure; it is the textbook of awakening.
- **The Habit of Escape**: How unconscious aversion perpetuates secondary suffering (*dukkha-dukkha*).
- **Somatic Embracing of Pain**: Relaxing the belly and breathing with tension rather than fighting it.
