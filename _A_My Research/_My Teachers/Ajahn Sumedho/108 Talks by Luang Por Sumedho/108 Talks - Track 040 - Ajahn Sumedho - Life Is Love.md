### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Moving from needy sentimental attachment to the vast, unconditioned loving-kindness (*mettā*) that embraces all of life.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho addresses the true spiritual dimension of love. Worldly love is conditional and demanding: 'I love you because you satisfy me.' Universal Dhamma love (*mettā*) has no demands; it is the natural warmth of an unburdened heart that allows everything to be as it is without ill-will.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Conditional vs. Unconditional Love**: Disentangling loving-kindness from possessiveness and romantic fantasy.
- **Forgiving the Human Flaw**: Holding our own shortcomings and those of others in patient, spacious benevolence.
- **Mettā as an Ocean**: Extending goodwill to all beings—friends, strangers, and those who provoke us.
