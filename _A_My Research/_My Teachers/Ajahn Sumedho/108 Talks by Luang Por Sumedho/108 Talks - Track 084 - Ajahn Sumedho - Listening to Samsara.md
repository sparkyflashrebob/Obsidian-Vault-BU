### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Receptive inquiry into worldly turmoil; listening to samsāra's clamor as sound within awareness rather than feeling threatened by it.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this 2007 talk, Luang Por Sumedho explores 'Listening to Samsāra.' The worldly realm is full of noise, complaints, political panic, and neurotic demands. Rather than fighting samsāra or hiding behind monastery walls, the practitioner listens to the entire worldly clamor like sound in the room—witnessing its transient, hollow nature from the unshakeable stillness of awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Samsāra as Noise**: Viewing the frantic drama of human society as an acoustic event in consciousness.
- **The Armor of Non-Resistance**: Why feeling threatened by worldly chaos only proves that we are clinging to conditions.
- **The Great Listening Space**: Allowing samsāra to shout itself hoarse while resting in unentangled peace.
