### Circumstances
Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Grounding meditation in genuine sincerity, heart-warmth, and love of truth, preventing practice from degenerating into clinical intellectualism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1991 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho addresses the tendency of modern meditators to approach Dhamma with cold, analytical detachment. Genuine Buddhist practice is 'from the heart'—infused with devotion, compassion, and emotional honesty. When intellect is balanced with the heart's natural sensitivity, practice becomes a refuge of healing and deep peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Intellect vs. Heart**: The intellect dissects and divides; the heart integrates and feels.
- **Emotional Honesty on the Cushion**: Acknowledging grief, loneliness, and vulnerability without judgment.
- **The Warmth of Compassion**: Healing the chronic Western disease of self-criticism through tender acceptance.
