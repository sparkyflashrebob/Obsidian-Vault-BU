### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Direct contemplative pointers to the Unconditioned (Asankhata)—stepping beyond concepts, symbols, and forms into the silent reality of Nibbāna.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1990, Luang Por Sumedho provides direct experiential pointers to ultimate truth. Buddhist teachings, scriptures, and monastic robes are not the goal; they are road signs pointing to that which has no form, no boundaries, and no birth. By ceasing all grasping at symbols, the heart abides directly in the deathless peace of the unconditioned.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Teachings as Pointers, Not Destinations**: Don't eat the menu; eat the food. Dhamma concepts point to unconditioned reality.
- **There Is That Sphere (Atthi Bhikkhave)**: The Buddha's affirmative pointer to the unconditioned dimension beyond earth, water, fire, and wind.
- **The Stillness Behind All Movement**: Resting in that which knows movement without moving.
