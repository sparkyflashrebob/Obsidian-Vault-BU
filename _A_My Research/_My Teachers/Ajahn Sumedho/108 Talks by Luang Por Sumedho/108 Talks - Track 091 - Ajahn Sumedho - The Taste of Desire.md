### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating *Taṇhā* (Craving); feeling the somatic contraction of wanting without acting on it, uncovering the peace of non-attachment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho guides practitioners into investigating 'The Taste of Desire.' Desire is not something to be hated or violently repressed; it is a somatic contraction that has a distinct, recognizable taste—a restless, burning itch that promises satisfaction but delivers thirst. By tasting desire mindfully without feeding it, its hypnotic spell breaks.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Three Flavors of Craving**: Sensual desire (*kāma-taṇhā*), desire for becoming (*bhava-taṇhā*), and desire for annihilation (*vibhava-taṇhā*).
- **The Somatic Itch of Wanting**: Observing how desire manifests as tightness in the throat, chest, and stomach.
- **The Joy of Non-Grasping**: Tasting the deep, cool peace that appears when craving is allowed to subside on its own.

| Doctrinal Fruit | Fuels the wheel of becoming (*samsāra*) | Direct realization of the Third Noble Truth (*Nirodha*). |
