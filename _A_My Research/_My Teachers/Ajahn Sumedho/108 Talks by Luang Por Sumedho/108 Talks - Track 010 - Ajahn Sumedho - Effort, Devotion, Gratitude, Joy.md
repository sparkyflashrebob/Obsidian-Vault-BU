### People Involved

- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Chithurst Buddhist Monastery (Cittaviveka), West Sussex, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Balancing resolute spiritual effort with devotional warmth (bhatti), deep gratitude (kataññutā), and wholesome joy (pīti / pamojja); curing practice of grim stoicism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1981 at Chithurst, Luang Por Sumedho warns against the dry, grim stoicism that often afflicts serious Western meditators. Spiritual practice is not an endurance test of sheer willpower. When grounded in gratitude (*kataññutā*) for the Triple Gem and openhearted joy (*pamojja*), effort becomes buoyant, natural, and sustainable.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Pathology of Grim Meditation**: Meditating with clenched teeth produces irritability, headaches, and eventual burnout.
- **Gratitude as the Antidote to Cynicism**: Remembering the boundless generosity of teachers, parents, and benefactors.
- **Wholesome Joy as Fuel for Samādhi**: Joy gladdens the heart, relaxing physical tension so the mind settles into stillness.
