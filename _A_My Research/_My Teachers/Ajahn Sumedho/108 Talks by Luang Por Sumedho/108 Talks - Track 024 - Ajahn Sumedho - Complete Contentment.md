### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The supreme spiritual wealth of contentment (santuṭṭhī); finding peace with simple necessities and releasing the fever of consumer dissatisfaction.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1989 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho explores the traditional teaching that 'contentment is the highest wealth' (*santuṭṭhī paramaṃ dhanaṃ*). Western culture stimulates infinite desires for novelty and entertainment. True freedom begins when we cultivate contentment with what is present right now—food, shelter, health, and companions.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Contentment as Active Spiritual Faculty**: Not passive resignation, but conscious refusal to let craving dictate well-being.
- **The Noble Lineage (Ariyavaṃsa)**: Monastic joy in simple robes, almsfood, and modest shelter.
- **Overcoming the 'If Only' Mind**: Dropping the fantasy that happiness requires different conditions.
