### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplating the classical liturgical virtues of the Dhamma—svākkhāto, sandiṭṭhiko, akāliko, ehipassiko, opanayiko, paccattaṃ veditabbo viññūhi—as direct experiential realities rather than dogma.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1981 at Cittaviveka, Luang Por Sumedho unpacks the chanted Dhamma recollection (*Dhammānussati*). The Dhamma is not an esoteric book or Eastern belief; it is *sandiṭṭhiko* (directly visible here and now), *akāliko* (timeless), and *ehipassiko* (inviting all to come and see). By turning inward (*opanayiko*), anyone can directly verify the peace of non-clinging.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sandiṭṭhiko (Directly Visible)**: Truth is not hidden in the afterlife; it is apparent the moment the mind stops daydreaming.
- **Akāliko (Timeless)**: The laws of impermanence and non-self operate identically in 1st-century India, 20th-century England, or outer space.
- **Ehipassiko (Inviting Inspection)**: The Buddha invites open investigation, never demanding blind belief.
