### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Dismantling **sakkāya-diṭṭhi** (personality view / identity view), the first fetter (*saṁyojana*); recognizing that personality view is not innate but socially acquired, and discovering the unshakeable refuge of Paramattha Dhamma beyond self-criticism.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
One of Luang Por Sumedho's most famous and practical discourses, focusing directly on the eradication of **sakkāya-diṭṭhi** (personality view). Luang Por explains that we are not born with a personality view; we acquire it through cultural competition, comparison, and conditioned ideals. By taking our moods, health, Vinaya practice, and thoughts personally, we remain wide open to suffering. True freedom comes from shifting allegiance to Paramattha Dhamma and Buddho—the impersonal witness that does not take anything personally.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Anatomy of Sakkāya-Diṭṭhi**: We are not born with personality view; it is an acquired cultural habit of measuring our worth against ideals.
- **The Vulnerability of Identity**: When we operate from personality view, our boundaries are wide open to offense, hurt feelings, and resentment.
- **Paramattha Dhamma vs. Sakkāya-Diṭṭhi**: The personality view will never believe you are pure; only resting as the unconditioned knower (*Buddho*) reveals natural purity.
