### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Resting in Suchness (Tathatā)—the unalterable natural reality of things exactly as they are, dropping resistance and complaint.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1986 at Amaravati, Luang Por Sumedho explores the profound teaching of *Tathatā*—Suchness. Things are simply like this. The cold is like this, the aching knee is like this, the busy mind is like this. When we stop demanding that reality conform to our personal ideals, we step directly into peace.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Power of 'It's Like This'**: A single mantra that cuts through the habit of argument and complaint.
- **Conditioned Reality as Nature**: Everything in the universe arises due to causes; nothing is personal.
- **The Shift from Judge to Witness**: Dropping the role of internal critic to become pure, attentive presence.
