### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Three Aspects and Twelve Insights of the Four Noble Truths (*tiparivaṭṭaṁ dvādasākāraṁ*) as an immediate meditative protocol.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho offers a masterclass on 'Using the Dhammacakka Sutta for Awakening.' The Buddha's first discourse is not a philosophical lecture to be memorized; it is a practical diagnostic formula. Luang Por breaks down the three turns of each Noble Truth: recognizing the truth (*sacca-ñāṇa*), understanding what must be done (*kicca-ñāṇa*), and realizing the work is completed (*kata-ñāṇa*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The First Discourse as a Laboratory Manual**: Moving beyond chanting the sutta to living its twelve insights.
- **The Task Associated with Each Truth**: Dukkha is to be understood; craving is to be abandoned; cessation is to be realized; the path is to be cultivated.
- **The Infallible Compass**: Using the Four Noble Truths to navigate every modern emotional crisis and physical trial.
