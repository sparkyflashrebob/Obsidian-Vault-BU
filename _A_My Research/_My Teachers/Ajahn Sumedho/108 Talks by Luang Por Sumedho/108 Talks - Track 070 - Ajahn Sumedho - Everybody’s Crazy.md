### Location & Provenance

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Ajahn Chah's famous maxim: 'All unenlightened beings are mad'; developing boundless compassion by acknowledging the universal insanity of samsāra.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho unpacks one of Venerable Ajahn Chah's most radical and liberating observations: 'Unless you are an arahant, you are crazy.' Expecting unawakened human beings to be completely sane, rational, and fair is itself insanity. When we accept that everyone is conditioned by greed, hatred, and delusion, outrage ceases and boundless compassion arises.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Perversion of Perception (*Vipallāsa*)**: How delusion takes the impermanent to be permanent and not-self to be a self.
- **Dropping Moral Indignation**: Why being outraged at human foolishness is like being angry at poison ivy for being poisonous.
- **The Compassion of Sane Awareness**: Resting in awakened knowing as the only true island of sanity in a mad world.

## 4. Stories & Anecdotes Offered
