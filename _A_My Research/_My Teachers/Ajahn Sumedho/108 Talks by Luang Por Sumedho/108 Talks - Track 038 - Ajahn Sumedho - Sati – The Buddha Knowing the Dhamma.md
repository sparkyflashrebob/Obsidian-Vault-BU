### Circumstances
Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Demystifying mindfulness (*sati*); transitioning from mechanical concentration drills to the natural, intuitive awakened knowing of Buddho.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho reframes mindfulness not as an intense, strain-filled mental grip, but as simple, receptive recognition: the Buddha knowing the Dhamma. 'Buddho' is the one who knows, and 'Dhamma' is whatever is occurring right now—whether pleasant, unpleasant, peaceful, or agitated.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sati as Natural Presence**: Releasing the artificial striving that turns meditation into an aggressive task.
- **The Observer and the Observed**: Learning to trust the simple capacity to notice without taking sides.
- **Integrating Sati into Daily Chores**: Walking, sweeping, washing dishes with effortless presence.
