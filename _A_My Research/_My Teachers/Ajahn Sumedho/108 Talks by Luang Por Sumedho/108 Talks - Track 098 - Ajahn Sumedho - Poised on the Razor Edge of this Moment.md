### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Heedfulness on the knife-edge of now; balancing between past memory and future projection with majestic presence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this urgent 70-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho describes contemplative practice as being 'Poised on the Razor Edge of this Moment.' To tilt toward past memory is nostalgia or remorse; to tilt toward future imagination is worry or ambition. Freedom exists exclusively on the knife-edge of now, where past and future fall away and awakened presence stands immaculate.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Knife-Edge of Now**: Why the present moment has no width or duration—it is a timeless razor edge.
- **Falling into Past and Future**: Observing the chronic momentum of the mind to tumble into memory or fantasy.
- **The Balance of the Spiritual Warrior**: Remaining alert, poised, and utterly fearless in the living present.
