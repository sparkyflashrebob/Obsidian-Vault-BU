### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Deathless (*amata-dhātu*); investigating the illusion of mortality by shifting awareness from the dying body and ego to that which knows birth and death.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this foundational 1993 discourse following the passing of Luang Por Chah, Luang Por Sumedho explores the meaning of 'The End of Death.' Death belongs entirely to what is born—the physical organism, feelings, and thoughts. By resting in awakened knowing (Buddho), we touch the Unconditioned Deathless realm that was never born and therefore never dies.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **What Dies vs. What Knows**: Disentangling the physical senses and ego-narrative from unborn awareness.
- **The Mirage of Survival**: How spiritual ambition often secretly seeks personal immortality for the ego.
- **Resting in the Deathless**: Resting in timeless presence rather than striving to become enlightened in the future.
