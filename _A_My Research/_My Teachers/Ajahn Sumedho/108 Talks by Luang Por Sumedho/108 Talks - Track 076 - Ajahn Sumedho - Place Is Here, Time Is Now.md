### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The twin coordinates of liberation: 'Place is here, time is now.' Dismantling spatial and temporal delusions in practice.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this extensive 80-minute discourse, Luang Por Sumedho cuts through the illusion that freedom is found somewhere else or at some future time. 'Place is here; time is now.' There is nowhere else to go; you can never leave 'here.' You can never experience 'tomorrow.' Awakening is discovering total peace within these two timeless coordinates.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mirage of Somewhere Else**: Why traveling to exotic holy places or remote caves cannot grant peace if we take our grasping mind with us.
- **The Illusion of Tomorrow**: How the concept of time traps the spiritual seeker in endless becoming.
- **The Eternal Here and Now**: Settling into the only place and time that has ever actually existed.
