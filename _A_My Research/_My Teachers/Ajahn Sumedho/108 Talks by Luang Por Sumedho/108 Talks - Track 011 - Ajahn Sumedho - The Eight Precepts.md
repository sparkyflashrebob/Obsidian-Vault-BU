### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Chithurst Buddhist Monastery (Cittaviveka), West Sussex, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The Eight Precepts (aṭṭha-sīla) not as ascetic deprivation, but as a deliberate simplified container for awakening and noble silence.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in 1982, Luang Por Sumedho explores the spiritual power of taking the Eight Precepts on retreat and Uposatha days. By giving up evening meals, entertainment, bodily adornment, and luxurious beds, the mind steps off the treadmill of sensory gratification, discovering an unconditioned contentment that does not depend on stimulation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Precepts as Protectors**: Restraint protects the mind from falling into habitual reactivity.
- **Simplifying Sensory Input**: The 6th, 7th, and 8th precepts dismantle the entertainment habit.
- **Noble Silence as Sanctuary**: How speech restraint conserves mental vitality.
