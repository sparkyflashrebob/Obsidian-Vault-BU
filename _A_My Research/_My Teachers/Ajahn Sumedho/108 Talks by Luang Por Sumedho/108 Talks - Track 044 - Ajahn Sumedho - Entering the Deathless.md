- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The portal of non-attachment; how acknowledging the cessation of conditions directly reveals the unproduced element (*asaṅkhata-dhātu*).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho deepens his teachings on the Deathless (*amata-dhātu*). We do not create or enter the Deathless by doing something new; we realize it by ceasing to cling to conditioned phenomena. When grasping stops, what remains is the Deathless peace that has always been here.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Trap of Spiritual Attainment**: Recognizing that enlightenment cannot be attained by an ego looking for rewards.
- **Witnessing Cessation (*Nirodha*)**: Paying keen attention to the moment when a feeling or sound vanishes into silence.
- **Resting in What Remains**: The luminous, open stillness that is present when wanting and resisting fall silent.
