### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating *Suññatā* (Emptiness); perceiving that all sensory and mental phenomena are hollow of a separate, enduring self.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Luang Por Sumedho explores the profound teaching on Emptiness (*suññatā*). Emptiness is not nothingness or nihilistic void; it is the absence of a permanent self within conditions, and the spacious unconditioned reality in which all temporary forms appear and dissolve.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Misunderstanding of Emptiness**: Reassuring practitioners that emptiness is not a bleak void, but luminous freedom.
- **Empty of 'Me' and 'Mine'**: Seeing emotions, memories, and physical aches as ownerless natural events.
- **The Space Between Thoughts**: Tuning into the silent pause before and after a thought arises.
