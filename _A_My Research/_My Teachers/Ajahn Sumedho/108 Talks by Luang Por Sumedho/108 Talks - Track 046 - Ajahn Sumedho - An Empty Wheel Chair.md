## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location & Provenance

### Canonical Setting
Canonical reference: SN 22.85 (Yamaka Sutta), SN 22.1 (Nakulapita Sutta).

### Circumstances
Archival audio recording from the foundational years of the English Sangha, capturing Luang Por Sumedho's direct, practical, and humorously honest presentation of the Dhamma.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: [[Ajahn Sumedho]].
- **Assembly**: Monastic Sangha and lay community gathered at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery, Great Gaddesden, Hertfordshire, UK.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplation of paralysis, illness, and physical decline; honoring Ajahn Chah's decade of physical immobility with absolute mental freedom.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
One of Luang Por Sumedho's most poignant discourses. He recalls the final ten years of Venerable Ajahn Chah's life, when a stroke left him paralyzed, unable to speak, and seated in a wheelchair. Even as his physical body was utterly broken, his radiant presence showed that true Dhamma is completely independent of physical health.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Ajahn Chah's Final Teaching**: How a master who spoke no words for a decade taught the most profound lesson on anattā.
- **The Body as a Rental Vehicle**: Acknowledging that the physical organism belongs to nature and will break down.
- **Illness of Body vs. Illness of Mind**: 'Though my body is sick, my mind is not sick' (Nakulapitā Sutta).
