**Overview**

 In this meditation retreat session, Ajahn Sumedho emphasizes the importance of understanding suffering, or dukkha, as a fundamental aspect of human experience. He explains that suffering is not merely personal but a universal condition that everyone encounters, regardless of their circumstances. Through the lens of the Four Noble Truths, he encourages participants to reflect on their experiences of discomfort and pain without attaching them to the personal "I am" narrative, thereby fostering a more objective understanding of suffering. By recognizing suffering as a natural part of life, participants can cultivate acceptance and insight, ultimately leading to a more profound sense of peace and resilience. The session highlights the significance of meditation as a tool for exploring and understanding suffering rather than escaping it, advocating for a fearless engagement with life's challenges.
