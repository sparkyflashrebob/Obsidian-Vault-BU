## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. Celebrating the Fruit of Ten Days
Ten days of silent monastic practice represents hundreds of thousands of mindful breaths, miles of mindful walking, and countless victories over mental hindrances. The monastics rejoice in the immense spiritual merit (*puñña*) created by the community.

### 2. Practical Re-entry Wisdom
The teachers address the delicate art of breaking silence:
- Don't rush into frantic speech; speak with measured mindfulness.
- Expect sensory sensitivity when returning to traffic, phones, and grocery stores.
- Protect your daily morning sit as an essential anchor.

### 3. Pouring the Waters of Dedication
The ceremony concludes with the pouring of water, dedicating the blessings of the retreat to parents, teachers, and all beings in saṁsāra.
