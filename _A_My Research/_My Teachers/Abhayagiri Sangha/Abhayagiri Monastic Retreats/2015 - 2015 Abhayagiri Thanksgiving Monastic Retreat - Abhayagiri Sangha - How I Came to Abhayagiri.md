## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. A Thanksgiving Tradition of Storytelling
At the Angela Center Thanksgiving retreats, the Thursday evening session traditionally steps away from formal doctrinal exposition to offer living testimony. Hearing how ordinary men and women navigated careers, relationships, and modern American culture to embrace the ancient monastic code bridges the gap between ancient suttas and contemporary life.

### 2. Panelist Narratives:
- **Upāsikā Debbie Stamp**: Shares her early encounters with the Forest Tradition in the 1980s, supporting monastics when they first arrived in California without land or shelter, and the founding years of Abhayagiri.
- **Armand**: Reflects on his journey as an anagārika / lay steward, navigating the transition from professional responsibility to monastic renunciation.
- **Ajahn Karuṇadhammo**: Recounts his background in medicine and hospice care, confronting the raw reality of mortality, finding the teachings of Ajahn Chah, and taking ordination under Ajahn Pasanno.
- **Ajahn Ñāṇiko**: Describes his youth, intellectual searching, reading the suttas, visiting Abhayagiri, and recognizing the undeniable authenticity of the monastic training.
- **Tan Khantiko**: Shares his early experiences as a young monk, learning Pāli, adapting to monastic discipline, and finding peace in communal wilderness practice.

### 3. The Power of Living Examples
These personal odysseys demonstrate that renunciation is not an escape from reality, but an unflinching embrace of truth driven by faith (*saddhā*) and compassion.
