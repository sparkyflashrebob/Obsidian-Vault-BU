## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Ancient Blueprint of the Fourfold Assembly
In DN 16, the Buddha made it explicitly clear that the longevity and strength of the Sāsana relies upon four equal pillars: monks (*bhikkhus*), nuns (*bhikkhunīs*), laymen (*upāsakas*), and laywomen (*upāsikās*). When any pillar is weakened, the pavilion of Dhamma tilts.

### 2. The Birth of Saraṇaloka and Aloka Vihāra
Having trained for decades as senior monastics at Amaravati and Chithurst under Ajahn Sumedho, Ajahn Ānandabodhi and Ajahn Mettā felt the urgent calling to pioneer a permanent monastic residence for women in North America. Invited by California lay stewards, they founded the Saraṇaloka Foundation to support monastic training and eventual full ordination.

### 3. The Pioneering Spirit of Forest Nuns
The teachers recount the practical realities of launching a new vihāra: finding rental properties, surviving on alms, and creating an authentic forest training hermitage where future generations of women can dedicate their lives entirely to awakening.
