The [[Six Sense Bases]]
- complementary to [[The Five aggregates]] 
- Buddha used to explore the nature of experience
- uncover the nature of [[Google Keep/Dukkha]]
- Occur in Pairs
- internal and external
- six internal bases
- mind gain access to sense object - to the world

Since all conditioned experience is included in the noble truth of suffering, the six internal bases may be called "the channels through which suffering originates"

- [[Saccasamyutta (SN 56:14, at V 426)]] defines [[the noble truth of suffering]] as the six internal sense bases.
- The six internal bases each have a corresponding external base
- 12 Bases in All
	- the eye and visible forms
	- the ear and sounds
	- the nose and odors
	- the tongue and tastes
	- the body and tactile objects
	- the mind and mental objects
- The pairs are called "bases" (āyatana) because each serves as the platform for the arising of the corresponding type of consciousness. 
	- eye-consciousness arises in dependence on the eye and visible forms
	- ear-consciousness arises in dependence on the ear and sounds
	- each of other types of consciousness in dependence on its respective internal and external base
	- From this we can see that the principle of conditionality governs the arising of consciousness
	- this undercuts the notion of consciousness as a stable, autonomous subject of experience
	- This principle of conditionality also underlies the formula of dependent origination

The Mind-base ([[manāyatana]])
- identifies with consciousness entirely
- and as such with all six classes of consciousness
- appears to be an internal mental organ
- support for the arising of mind-consciousness ([[manoviññāṇa]])
- also the base of mental objects (dhammāyatana) to be the objective sphere of mind-consciousness
- based on this the mind-base might be taken as the passive subliminal flow of mind from which active reflective consciousness emerges (REFRASE THIS) and its corresponding external base to be the purely mental objects apprehended by acts of thoughts, introspection, imagination, reflection, and meditative contemplation.

Whereas the scheme of [[the 5 aggregates]] have been advances primarily to show the objective ground for the deluded notions of "mine", "I". and "self", [[six sense bases]]  have a closer connection with [[craving]].
- underscored by the classical formula for dependent origination which describes the progress from [[six sense bases]] to [[contact]] to [[feeling]], etc
- the meeting of the sense base, object, and consciousness is called "[[contact]]" ([[phassa]]) 
- with contact as a condition there arises [[feeling]] 
- with [[feeling]] there is [[craving]]

Since craving is the origin of [[Google Keep/Dukkha]], and craving is nurtured by the feelings originated through contact at the six sense bases, to eliminate craving we must change our perspective
- to regulate craving (and other unwholesome responses to feeling) requires us to control our reactions to the input of the senses
- to facilitate this, a practice called "[[restraint of the senses]]" ([[indriyasamvara]]) is inserted into training
- this serves as a bridge between ethical behavior and formal meditation

> On seeing a form with the eye, the monk does not grasp at its signs and features. Since, if he left the eye faculty unguarded, evil unwholesome states of longing and dejection might invade him, he practices the way of its restraint, he guards the eye faculty, he undertakes the restraint of the eye faculty. On hearing a sound with the ear. . . . On smelling an odor with the nose. . . . On tasting a flavor with the tongue. . . . On touching a tactile object with the body. . . . On cognizing a mental object with the mind, he does not grasp at its signs and features. Since, if he left the mind faculty unguarded, evil unwholesome states of longing and dejection might invade him, he practices the way of its restraint, he guards the mind faculty, he undertakes the restraint of the mind faculty.

Bodhi. Noble Truths, Noble Path: The Heart Essence of the Buddha's Original Teachings (p. 87). Wisdom Publications. Kindle Edition. 

Together with moderation in eating and devotion to wakefulness, restraint of the senses is said to constitute the foundation for the destruction of the influxes.  *****DIFFERENT THAN HAVING THEM REMOVED
- Important not to grasp at the signs and features of the object***
- WHAT IS THIS?
- When we grasp at these signs and features
	- the objects attractive and repulsive aspects
	- we begin to mentally proliferate the base input of sense data in ways that provoke our desires
	- entangling us more tightly in the web of craving and aversion.
- Instead of responding to feelings instinctively
	- towards the pleasant and away from the unpleasant
	- <u>the teachings instruct us to see how feeling is merely a conditioned state that arises thru a complex process involving the sense bases, consciousness, and contact.</u>
- On some occasions simply discerning the conditioned origin of feeling is sufficient to short-circuit the entire cascade of events by which feeling overwhelms the mind.
- Instead of letting the mind run out to the object redirect one redirects the [[beam of attention]] the process by which the experience is generated
- expose constructed nature & intrinsic impermanence of all factors in the process
- seeing how feeling arises based on contact one becomes disenchanted with the eye, with forms, with eye-contact, and with whatever feeling arises with eye-contact as a condition

FEELING
- pleseant, painful, or neutral
- each related to one of the 3 root defilements
	- pleasant = lust
	- painful = hatred
	- neutral = delusion
- as long as these consume the mind the whole experience - blazes with the fires of greed, hatred & delusion - with the fires of birth, old age and death
- [[The Fire Sermon]]
	- "everything is burning"
		-  fires of the defilements
		-  suffering of repeated birth, aging & death

Aggregates emphasis the absence of a self
- stress the contemplation of non-self

The [[Six Sense Bases]] emphasizes the contemplation of impermanence

Both actually imply and emphasis both.
[[The Five Aggregates]] = composite nature of experience
[[Six Sense Bases]] = noticing craving - higher stage of the path

Craving
- take enjoyable feelings as permanent
- taken rightly seen as fleeting and impermanent
- internal sense bases, their objects, type of consciousness, and contact with them must be seen as "impermanent, changing, and becoming otherwise"
- same for feeling, perception, and volition that arise thru contact
- when seen directly, feelings, the contact, the sense bases thru which those contacts originate, ignorance is abandoned and clear knowledge arises.
- Each sense faculty is naturally drawn to it corresponding object
	- happy when we gain experiences we desire
	- but set ourselves up to fall
	- when they cease or perish - our delight vanishes
	- dejection and anguish
- Delight in the senses is a hinderance/obstruction to [[nibbana]] (final goal of spiritual life)
- Agreeable sense objects are like bait
- six hook for the misery of being
- swallowed the bait of Mara
- under Mara's control

Bondage not in the sense bases
- in the [[craving]] that arises
- black and white oxes are yoked together by a single harness that binds them together
- eye is not fetter of forms
	- desire-and-lust that arises in dependence on them is the fetter
- Buddha eradicated craving
- Purpose of training is to remove craving
- Win liberation of mind

Six kinds of animals
- when bound together and released, each animal rushes to its familiar habitat
- if we do not exercise restraint and self-control - each of the senses run toward their object
- if tied to a post (mindfulness) - they will pull different directions but eventually become fatigued, settle down and become still
- This is the mind in meditation
