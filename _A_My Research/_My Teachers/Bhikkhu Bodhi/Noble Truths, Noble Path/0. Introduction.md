## Sutta Pitaka
Suttas = discourses
Pali Canon three major divisions
	baskets
		vinaya pitaka = monastic discipline 
		sutta pitaka = discourses 
		abhidhamma pitaka = systematic presentation constructed from the teachings 

5 Collections = Nikayas
Four Major Nikayas 
	1. Digha Nikaya = Long Discourses - 34 suttas - 3 vaggas/books
	2. Majjhima Nikaya = Middle Length Discourses - 152 suttas - 3 vaggas
	3. Samyutta Nikaya = Connected Discourses - approx 3000 short discourses - 56 chapters (samyuttas) - 5 vaggas 
	4. Anguttara Nikaya = Numerical Discourses - approx 2400 short suttas - 11 chapters (nipatas)

Digha = aimed at popular audience, inspire faith, devotion - attract converts
Majjhima = directed toward Buddhist community - doctrine and practices of th Dhamma 
Samyutta = organized by subject - advanced meditators
Anguttara =  easy to retain

Also the Khuddaka Nikaya = Minor Collection that has swelled
