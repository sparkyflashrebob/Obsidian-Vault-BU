[[Dealing With Anger]]
	Learning to Deal with Anger
		benefits of slaying anger
		[[3 kinds of persons with anger]]
			line etched in stone - long time - not easily erased
			line etched in dirt - gets angry - but does not persist
			line etched in water - even when spoken to harshly remains friendly
		[[4 kinds of vipers]]
			venom quick to come up but not virulent - anger does not linger
			virulent but not quick to come up - not often become angry but it lingers
			both quick and virulent - angry often and long lasting
			neither quick or virulent - not come up often and does not last
	 [[Dangers in Anger]]
			1. one is displeasing and disagreeable to many people
			2. lots of internal hostility -feelings of things being against you
			3. abundance of faults
			4. dies confused
			5. at breakup of body after death 
	[[Benefits of Patience]]
		1. Pleasing and agreeable
		2. Not violent
		3. without remorse
		4. dies unconfused
		5. born with good destination

[[Ways of Removing Anger]]
			thinking "They acted/act/will for my harm, but what can be done about it" one removes resentment, 
			   			same for those I love (past present and future)
			   			also for those I dislike (past present and future)
			   			finally, understanding that,
			   			one does not become angry without a reason
			   			these are the ten ways of removing resentment


[[Ways of Removing Resentment]]
1. develop loving-kindness for the person one resents
2. compassion
3. equanimity
4. disregard and not pay attention
5. apply the ownership of karma for the person one resents
	
‘This venerable one is the owner of his kamma, the heir of his kamma; he has kamma as his origin, kamma as his relative, kamma as his resort; he will be the heir of any kamma he does, good or bad.’
