## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. Combating Spiritual Poverty
The uninstructed mind easily falls into feelings of spiritual lack and unworthiness. We compare our turbulent minds to imagined ideals of serene saints and conclude we lack what it takes. Debbie reminds retreatants that just arriving at retreat is proof of immense spiritual wealth.

### 2. The Seven Noble Treasures (*Ariya-Dhana*)
Material wealth is vulnerable to market crashes, theft, and physical decay, and must be abandoned at death. Noble wealth—conviction, integrity, conscience, prudence, learning, generosity, and discerning wisdom—is portable, indestructible, and deepens with every act of mindfulness.

### 3. Activating Inner Resources Under Pressure
When hindrances arise on the cushion, we do not need to panic. We can draw upon our reservoir of resources: recollecting the goodness of our life, taking refuge in the breath, and resting in the presence of spiritual friends.
