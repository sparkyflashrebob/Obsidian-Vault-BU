## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **The Swept Path After the Autumn Rain**: Walking along the Angela Center paths following heavy night rain, Debbie observed how the dedicated cleaning of the retreatants had been completely covered over by yellow and red leaves. She reflects on how our lives are just like that path: we sweep the mind clean, conditions blow through, and leaves fall again. True wisdom doesn't curse the wind; it simply keeps sweeping with a joyful, generous heart.
