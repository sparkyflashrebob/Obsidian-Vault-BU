## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata & Meditative Guidance

* **Location:** Spirit Rock Meditation Center, Upper Hall, Woodacre, California.
* **Circumstances:** Morning guided sitting during the July 2024 residential retreat exploring the living relevance of the Four Noble Truths and Dependent Origination.
* **Core Practice Instructions:**
  1. *Settling the Sensorium:* Noticing how attention is habitually lured outward by sights, sounds, and thoughts.
  2. *The Object vs. The Space:* Turning attention around from the perceived object to the space of knowing (*citta*).
  3. *The Nāda Inner Sound:* Tuning into the subtle high-pitched inner vibration (the "sound of silence"), using it as a reliable, ever-present anchor that cuts through discursive mental proliferation.
* **Stories Offered:**
  * **Luang Por Sumedho’s Discovery of the Sound of Silence:** Ajahn Amaro recounts how Luang Por Sumedho discovered the *nāda* sound on his own while practicing in a remote forest monastery in Thailand, only to find years later that it is described in the ancient Indian Upanishads and Surangama Sutra.
  * **The Irritated Student and Persistent Sounds:** A retreatant complained that the constant hum of the ventilation fan was ruining their peace. Ajahn Amaro guided the student to notice that the sound does not create suffering; the suffering is born from the desire for the sound to stop.
