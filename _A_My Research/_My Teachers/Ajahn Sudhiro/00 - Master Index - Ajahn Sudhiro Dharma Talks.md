## 1. Teacher Profile & Teaching Scope

Ven. Ajahn Sudhiro is a senior Thai Forest monastic who trained extensively in Thailand and spent significant periods residing and teaching at Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery. His teachings combine gentle monastic humor, deep Vinaya discipline, and incisive analysis of the mental defilements, particularly ego, conceit (*māna*), and spiritual pride.
