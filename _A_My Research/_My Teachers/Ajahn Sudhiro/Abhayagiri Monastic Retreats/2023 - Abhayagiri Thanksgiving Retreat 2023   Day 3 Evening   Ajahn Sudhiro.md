## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Bojjhaṅgas as the Direct Vehicle to Enlightenment
The Seven Factors of Awakening are the specific mental qualities that, when matured together, directly trigger awakening (*bodhi*). They are not mystical gifts, but cultivated mental faculties.

### 2. The Two Wings of the Bojjhaṅgas
- **The Energizing Triad**:
  - Investigation (*Dhammavicaya*): Asking wise questions; examining mental states.
  - Energy (*Viriya*): Arousing physical wakefulness; steady application.
  - Rapture (*Pīti*): Cultivating wholesome joy and interest in the breath.
- **The Calming Triad**:
  - Tranquility (*Passaddhi*): Calming the nervous system and physical formations.
  - Concentration (*Samādhi*): Unifying awareness in still, unbroken presence.
  - Equanimity (*Upekkhā*): Resting in balanced, non-clinging neutrality.

### 3. Sati as the Universal Regulator
Mindfulness observes which wing is deficient and skillfully applies the appropriate nourishment, bringing the heart into harmonious equilibrium.
