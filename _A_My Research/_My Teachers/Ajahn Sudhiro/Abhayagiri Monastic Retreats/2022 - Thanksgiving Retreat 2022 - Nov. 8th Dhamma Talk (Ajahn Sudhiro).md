## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. Effort as Wise Gardening
Right effort is never about clenching fists or grinding teeth. A good gardener doesn't scream at weeds to die or pull flowers upward by force. They pull the weeds gently by the roots and ensure the soil has water and sunlight. Meditative effort is the intelligent encouragement of wholesome qualities.

### 2. The Four Great Strivings (*Sammappadhāna*)
- **Restraint (*Saṁvara-padhāna*)**: Guarding sense doors to prevent new hindrances.
- **Abandonment (*Pahāna-padhāna*)**: Letting go of arisen negativity through wise reflection.
- **Cultivation (*Bhāvanā-padhāna*)**: Developing the Seven Factors of Awakening.
- **Maintenance (*Anurakkhaṇā-padhāna*)**: Protecting and maturing wholesome states of samādhi.

### 3. Balancing the Awakening Factors (*Bojjhaṅga*)
When the mind is sluggish or depressed, we cultivate energizing factors: Dhamma investigation (*dhammavicaya*), energy (*viriya*), and rapture (*pīti*). When the mind is agitated or restless, we emphasize calming factors: tranquility (*passaddhi*), concentration (*samādhi*), and equanimity (*upekkhā*). Mindfulness (*sati*) oversees the balance at all times.
