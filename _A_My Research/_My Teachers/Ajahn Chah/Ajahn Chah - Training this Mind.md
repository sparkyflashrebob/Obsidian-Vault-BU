Mind
- not much too it
- radiant in and of itself
- naturally peaceful
- why it doesn't feel peaceful is because it is lost in its moods
- tricked by its moods
- tricked to feeling pleasure one minute and suffering the next
- these moods are not the actual mind itself
- but the mind gets lost and carried away by moods
- this is what it is like to have an untrained mind
- its natural state is not the moods - nor the happiness or the sadness
- we go around thinking we are suffering, our mind is suffering, but it is just lost in the various moods it is experiencing

Another way of looking at this:
- our mind is naturally peaceful
- it is still and calm like a leaf that is not being blown about by the wind
- yet if the wind blows its flutters the leaves that it contacts 
- the leaves flutter because of the wind
- likewise with the mind

Likewise with our thoughts
- this is a way we can understand the nature of thoughts
- and stay still
- which is the natural state of mind
- practice is to see if it is in its natural state
- these thoughts come and go and the mind gets caught up in them

Our practice is observe the mind
- to see this
- in order to grow wisdom
- observe the nature of thoughts 
- not just blindly follow them

The mind is naturally peaceful.
To understand and know this we come together for this practice of meditation.
