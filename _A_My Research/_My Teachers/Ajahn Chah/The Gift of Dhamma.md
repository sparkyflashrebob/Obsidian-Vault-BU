MY Notes:
The World (Paris) has many good things to offer (that please the senses) but people ignorant of dhamma only become confused by them.

Dhamma: that which cuts thru the difficulties and problems of humankind
- gradually reducing them to nothing
- when mental impressions arise in us we'll be able to deal with them and go beyond
- problems common to us all
- if we do no know how to solve them we'll always be subhect to suffering and distress

The Practice:
- it is not far
- right here in our body and mind
- a confused body and mind means a confused person
- peaceful body and mind, a peaceful person

The mind:
- like rain water
- pure in its natural state
- if we drop green coloring in clear water it would turn green
- if yellow it would turn yellow
- the same with the mind
	- when comfortable mental impressions drop in the mind is comfortable
	- when mental impressions drop in the mind that is uncomfortable the mind is uncomfortable
- mind become cloudy just like coloured water
- when clear water contacts yellow, it turns yellow
- when it contacts green, it turns green
- it will change color every time
- the water which is green or yellow is naturally clean clear
- this is its natural state
	- naturally clean and clear
- this is also the natural state of the mind
	- clean, clear, unconfused
- it becomes confused only because it pursues mental impressions
- it gets lost in its moods

A Peaceful forest:
- if there is no wind, the leaves remain still
- when the wind blows, they flap and flutter
- the mind is similar to the leaf
- when it contacts mental impressions, it flaps and flutters according to the nature of the mental impression
- the less we know about the dhamma, the more the mind will pursue mental impressions
- feeling happy, it succumbs to happiness
- feeling suffering, it succumbs to suffering

- when the mind has no one to look after it, it's like a child without a mother or father to take care of it

- without a refuge they are insecure
- likewise with the mind
	- if not looked after
		- or no training or maturation of character with right understanding

The Mind:
- What is it?
- It isn't really a thing
- it is that which feels or senses
- that which senses, received and experiences all mental impressions
- At this moment there is mind
- As I speak, mind acknowledges what I am saying
- Sounds enter the ear and you know what is being said
- that which experiences is called '[[mind]]'

The mind is mind
Mental Objects are mental objects
Mental Objects are not mind
Mind is not mental objects

To understand our minds and the mental objects in our minds, we say mind is that which receives the mental objects which pop into it

when these two things
- mind and it object
- come into contact
- give right to feelings
	- some good
	- some bad
	- some cold
	- some hot...
	- all kinds
- without wisdom to deal with these feelings, the mind will become troubled

Meditation is the way of developing the mind so that it may be a base for the arising of wisdom.

Breath is a physical foundation
- mindfulness of breathing
- breath as mental object
- take breath because it is the simplest and 
- has been the heart of mediation since ancient times

When the occasion arises for sitting meditation
- sit upright
- say "I will now let go of all my burdens and concerns"
- not want anything that will cause you worry
- Let go of all concerns for the time being
- Set you attention on the breath
- breathe in and out
- don't intentionally make the breath long or short
	- weak or strong
- let it flow naturally
- Mindfulnees and self-awareness, arising from the mind, will know the in-breath and the out-breath
- Nothing else to do but this!
- Keep your mindfulness fixed on the in-breath and out-breath as they occur
- Be aware of the beginning, middle and end of each breath
	- beginning of breath at the tip of the nose
	- middle at the heart
	- end at the abdomen
- On exhalation, its the reverse
	- abdomen
	- heart
	- tip of the nose
- Develop the awareness of the breath in this manner
- This will relieve all worries

Perhaps other thoughts will enter the mind and will take up other themes and distract you.
- Don't be concerned about them.
- Just take up the breathing again as you object of attention
- The mind may be get caught up in judging and investigating your moods but continue to practice. 
- being constantly aware of the beginning, middle and end of each breath.

Eventually Mind and body will get accustomed to the work
- fatigue will disappear ([[energy]])
- Body will feel lighter
- breath will become more more refined
- [[Readwise/Books/Mindfulness]] and [[self-awareness]] will protect the mind and watch over it

Continue practice like this until the mind is [[peaceful]] and [[calm]]
- until it is [[one]]
- [[one]] means mind will be completely [[absorbed in the breathing]]
- it doesn't separate from the [[breath]]
- [[mind unconfused and at ease]]

When the mind is peaceful, [[fix attention]] on the [[in-breath]] and [[out-breath]]
- at the tip of the nose only
- no longer need to follow the breath
- called [[calming the mind]]
- [[relaxed and peacefull]]
- when [[tranquillity]] arises, the mind stops
- stops with a single object
- known as making mind peaceful so [[wisdom]] may arise

This is beginning - the foundation of our practice
- should practice every single day
- wherever you may be
- whatever you are doing
- this is the "[[mental training]]"
