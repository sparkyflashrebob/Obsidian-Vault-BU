# 🏛️ Venerable Ajahn Chah — Master Historical Audio Recordings Index
### *Historic 1979 Visit to the Insight Meditation Society (Barre, MA) & Guided Instructions*

> *"The mind in its original nature is naturally pure and peaceful, like clear rainwater. It is only because of the passing visiting conditions that it appears troubled. When you let go of the wanting, the suffering dies. Die to the self before your physical body dies, and you will know the unconditioned peace of Nibbāna."* — **Ven. Ajahn Chah**

## 📜 Master Mettā Compendium Entries Harvested

During this processing of Ajahn Chah's historical audio recordings, the following master dedication and mettā phrases were captured for **Phase 4: Master Mettā Phrases Compendium**:

1. **Day Discussion [48:30–51:41] — Freedom from Fear & Peaceful Dying**:

   > *"May we meet the ending of all things with serene wisdom and fearless trust. Free from grasping and terror, may our hearts rest in the unconditioned deathless peace, wishing comfort, safety, and ultimate liberation to all beings without exception."*

2. **Evening Dharma Talk [42:47–47:21] — Historic Monastic Blessing & Forgiveness**:

   > *"May the blessings of the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha protect and guide you. Whatever wrong we have done to one another by body, speech, or mind, may we forgive and be forgiven. May your practice bear the fruit of deep peace, wisdom, and liberation for the benefit of all living beings."*

3. **With Mindfulness & Wisdom Together [09:10–11:24] — Natural Ease & Unentangled Wisdom**:

   > *"May our minds rest in natural ease, united with mindfulness and clear wisdom. Free from forcing and fear, may our hearts abide in serene balance, wishing peace, safety, and happiness to all beings."*
