## 1. Teacher Profile & Teaching Scope

Ven. Ajahn Sona is the founder and abbot of Birken Forest Buddhist Monastery ('Sītavana') in British Columbia, Canada. Ordained in 1989 under Ven. Ajahn Chah's senior disciples, he is renowned for his lucid, elegant, and deeply practical expositions of the Noble Eightfold Path, breath meditation (*ānāpānasati*), and the classical 37 Requisites of Enlightenment (*Bodhipakkhiyā Dhammā*).
