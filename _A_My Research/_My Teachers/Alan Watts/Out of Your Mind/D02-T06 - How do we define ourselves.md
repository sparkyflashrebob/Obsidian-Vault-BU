### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Addressing humanity's terror of being locked in eternal darkness after death, Watts guides listeners through his famous meditation: imagine going to sleep and never waking up, which inevitably flips into its twin mystery—what was it like to wake up without ever having gone to sleep? Because non-existence isn't an experience, life continually begins anew through every 'I' in the cosmos. Watts then exposes the 'ego' as simply conscious attention functioning like a ship's trouble-shooting radar: identifying wholly with your alarm system condemns you to perpetual anxiety, whereas sensing the whole organism uncovers harmony even inside conflict.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Tackling death anxiety head-on, Watts presents a remarkable contemplative yoga: sit quietly and genuinely imagine what it would be like to fall asleep and *never* wake up. Stay with that question long enough, he promises, and a startling reversal flashes open—what was it like to wake up at birth after *never* having gone to sleep? Since total vacuum cannot be experienced, you never endure everlasting darkness in a locked room; whenever and wherever new conscious beings wake up across galaxies, that awakening happens as *'I'*—without needing personal memory any more than you need memory to regulate your thyroid.

From there, Watts demystifies the 'ego' cleanly without invoking spooky mysticism. Conscious attention acts like a ship's radar: its biological job is troubleshooting, scanning the horizon for rocks, reefs, and hazards. Identifying your entire identity with the hazard-radar ensures chronic tension! Stepping back into whole-organism awareness reveals an extraordinary truth: just as white corpuscles battling bacteria in your bloodstream produce vibrant health at the level of your whole body, human discords and conflicts form living harmony at a deeper scale of cosmic process—not dead spirit versus dead matter, but rhythmic, unblemished pattern.
