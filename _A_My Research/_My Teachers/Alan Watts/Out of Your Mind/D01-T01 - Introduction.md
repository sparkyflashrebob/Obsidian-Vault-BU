### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Alan Watts defines awakening not as acquiring esoteric beliefs, but as de-hypnotization: dropping fixed mental abstractions so we can come back to our immediate sensory experience. The Sounds True narrator introduces the Sausalito houseboat seminar series on *The Nature of Consciousness*.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

This opening track frames the entire twelve-disc *Out of Your Mind* seminar series. Watts begins with a characteristically playful paradox: what spiritual traditions call awakening is fundamentally a process of de-hypnotization—coming back to one's immediate senses rather than remaining trapped inside verbal and social hypnosis. Paradoxically, recovering direct sensory aliveness requires going 'out of your mind,' out of obsessive conceptual chatter. The Sounds True archival narrator then situates the recordings in their historic late-1960s setting on Watts' converted ferryboat home in Sausalito, California, introducing the first session on *The Nature of Consciousness*.
