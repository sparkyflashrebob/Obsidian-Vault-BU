### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Watts explores the psychological oscillation from embarrassed rebellion against an all-seeing cosmic Father to bleak mechanical atheism, raising Albert Camus' question of suicide alongside his own five playful questions. Mechanistic tough-mindedness flattered colonial empires but traps intellectuals in 'prickly' precision versus vague 'goo.' Because prickles and goo mutually define each other—reality is 'gooey prickles and prickly goo'—neither reductionism nor escapism works; instead, Watts argues we must choose a cosmological premise joyful enough to make the game worth the candle.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Tracing the psychological swing from religion to atheism, Watts observes why the cosmic potter initially felt comforting—making the individual feel loved by a watchful Father—until that cosmic surveillance grew suffocating (*'quit bugging me!'*). Rejecting God left modern thinkers stranded inside a meaningless machine, bringing Albert Camus to declare suicide the sole serious philosophical question (to which Watts humorously adds four: *who started it, are we going to make it, where do we put it, who's going to clean up, and is it serious?*). Any viable hypothesis of the universe, Watts argues with pragmatic humor, ought at least to make continuing the game worth the candle—otherwise miserable adults merely breed anxious children who drag on out of fear.

Examining why academic culture insists on bleak materialism, Watts introduces his famous typology: *prickles* versus *goo*. Prickly thinkers prize crisp definitions, particles, and logical positivism; gooey thinkers savor waves, continuity, and idealism. Each camp dismisses the other—yet neither could even recognize its own stance without the contrast of the opposite pole! Reality itself is neither rigid spikes nor amorphous slush, but *'gooey prickles and prickly goo,'* dancing together like front and back. Philosophy thrives not by silencing dissenters, Watts notes, but by thanking opponents for helping clarify the polarity.

## Stories, Analogies & Anecdotes Offered
