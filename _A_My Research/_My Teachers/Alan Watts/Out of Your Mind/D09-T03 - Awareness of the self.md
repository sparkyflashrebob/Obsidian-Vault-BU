### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Distinguishing cosmic consciousness from narrow conscious spotlighting, Watts notes that you already beat your heart, grow your hair, and run your pineal gland effortlessly without knowing *how*. In the same unbroken rhythm, this cosmos 'peoples' galaxies just as an apple tree apples—though blind intervals between births hide the thread. Why don't we spot the Self? Because a knife cannot cut itself nor a light illuminate itself! Yet the *Upaniṣads* affirm *mokṣa* ('liberation' via *neti, neti*—neither this nor that idea): not a catatonic blackout, but returning with eyes wide open to behold mutual relatedness (*relativity*) everywhere. Through shifting magnification and time-scale—capable of fitting empires and orchestras inside the tip of a fingernail or seeing human aura-heat through infrared lenses—perception selects rhythmic patterns like fingers choosing chords on an endless piano.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Why does Vedānta insist we *are* supreme consciousness even when we feel small and confused? Because focused waking attention is only a narrow flashlight floating atop vast non-conscious responsiveness—the effortless intelligence steadily pulsing your heart, circulating blood, and adjusting glands without awaiting orders. Like fallen leaves yielding fresh foliage, this universe endlessly sprouts conscious beings across galaxies because *it is a peopling universe, just as an apple tree apples*; forgetting the blank intervals makes us forget our deep root.

A blade needs no edge against itself, yet Vedānta celebrates *mokṣa*: liberation from the hypnosis of being 'poor little me.' Applying *neti, neti* ('not this, not that' conceptual picture) clears the clutter—not to vanish into vacant trance, but to wake back up seeing fronts linked to backs, insides to outsides, brief mosquitoes equal to ancient galaxies. Once magnification shifts cleanly, entire empires, battleships, and orchestras could dance unseen inside a single fingernail tip, and infrared glasses reveal silent heat halos around strangers—reminding us perception is selective piano-playing atop an infinite keyboard belonging wholly to the Self.
