### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Bringing all twelve seminar discs to a radiant conclusion, Watts explains why Huìnéng and Mahāyāna masters equate *śūnyatā* with *mirror* and *space*: only an uncolored mirror reflects every hue, and visual, audible, temporal, and musical spaces *are* mind itself—just as Meister Eckhart noted an eye must remain colorless to behold color, and healthy brains function effortlessly by staying void to themselves! Showing why practical, family-loving Chinese Buddhists reformed ascetic celibacy and rallied around the Indian householder *sūtra* featuring wealthy merchant Vimalakīrti, Watts ends the collection with Vimalakīrti out-debating even Mañjuśrī (Bodhisattva of Wisdom) on how to define emptiness—winning total victory without speaking a single syllable: the thunderous silence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Rounding out the entire *Out of Your Mind* cycle, Watts brings *śūnyatā* to rest inside its most luminous symbol: a spotless mirror. Far from barren absence, empty space holds mountains, stars, oceans, saints, scoundrels, silence between sounds, and intervals between tones—because space *is* consciousness, viewed from countless living vantage points. Echoing Christian mystic Meister Eckhart—*'in order to see color, my eye has to be free from color'*—Watts remarks wryly that you notice your brain cells only when sick with a tumor; ordinary health operates by effortless invisibility, an 'empty head' receiving the whole cosmos cleanly.

No wonder earthy, family-centered Chinese culture embraced this non-dual *Mahāyāna* revelation over world-denying celibacy! Honoring household love and productive craft rather than empty-minded cave isolation, Chinese Zen treasured the *Vimalakīrti Nirdeśa Sūtra*, whose worldly lay hero calmly refuted the Buddha's elite monastic disciples one by one. Climaxing a philosophical contest on defining the Ultimate Void—after even Bodhisattva of Wisdom Mañjuśrī praised saying nothing as best—Vimalakīrti uttered not one single word, winning universal allegiance through Vimalakīrti's unforgettable *thunderous silence*.
