### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Why does Zen use sudden jolts or shouts? Because trying to travel toward eternal *now* resembles an English yokel telling an American tourist lost for Upper Tudnam: 'If I were you, I wouldn't start from here.' Warning against Carl Jung's 'inflation' (the 'Holy Man' delusion when mistaking non-dual flow for personal omnipotence), Watts reframes vision neurologically: every visible room takes place *inside* your optic nerves while your skull sits *inside* the world—mutual containment he calls transaction. Like electricity refusing to leave a battery until a global circuit closes, human intelligence isn't alien to barren rocks: rocks imply minds just as acorns imply oaks.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

In *satori*, Watts notes, this very moment—no matter how dull or slightly irritable—is eternity itself; attempting to *try not to try* traps you back in searching. Hence Zen masters spring sudden shocks solely to snap seekers awake right where they stand, exposing 'How do I attain enlightenment?' as comical nonsense—like the British countryman telling a lost tourist asking directions to Upper Tudnam, *'I wouldn't start from here!'* Simultaneously, Watts issues an essential psychiatric caution: once the felt wall between voluntary and involuntary melts, unstable minds risk what Carl Jung diagnosed as **inflation**—assuming one has become Yahweh-in-charge rather than realizing *everybody else* shares the same seamless ground.

Probing the illusion of 'behind the eyes,' Watts points out a stunning neurological paradox: the colors, benches, and faces you seem to see 'out there' are actually firing inside the occipital cortex at the back of your skull—yet that skull sits simultaneously inside the surrounding room! Organism and environment don't 'bump' like two billiard balls; they engage in mutual **transaction**, like buying and selling happening at once. Closing with the physics of an Earth-circling electric wire, Watts explains that current won't even depart the battery terminal until the return switch closes at the far end: similarly, from the Big Bang's very first millisecond, rocky worlds already implied mindful brains, just as an acorn carries the oak and fingernails belong to one unbroken body with solid stone.
