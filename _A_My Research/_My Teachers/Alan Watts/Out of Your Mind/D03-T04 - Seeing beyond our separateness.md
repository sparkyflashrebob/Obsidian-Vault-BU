### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Through a lucid optical simile, Watts asks us to picture reality as a glowing light enclosed inside a dark perforated sphere: each pinhole becomes an individual perspective ('John Jones') gazing outward—temporarily amusing itself by pretending to be solely that isolated speck while forgetting the undivided blaze inside. Holding both the practical social mask and the underlying cosmic light together yields extraordinary poise, humor, and tenderness toward others.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Offering a luminous visual metaphor for non-dual individuality in *The Web of Life*, Watts imagines a radiant lamp encased within a black sphere pierced by countless tiny pinholes. Each aperture functions as a distinct conscious viewpoint—your personal name, history, and sensory outlook—allowing the single source to behold the world from billions of angles at once. Ordinary social conditioning trains every aperture to believe it exists strictly inside its pinhole rim, forgetting the boundless luminosity shining through all apertures alike. Recognizing the whole radiant interior while still honoring the specific focus of one's everyday persona allows us to walk cleanly through practical life with unforced compassion and lightness.
