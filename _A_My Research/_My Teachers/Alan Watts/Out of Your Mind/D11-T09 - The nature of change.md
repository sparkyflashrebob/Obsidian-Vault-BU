### Condensed Version of the Teachings

Making *anitya* (constant flux) visceral, Watts likens human identity to a Niagara whirlpool or UC Berkeley: students, buildings, cells, and atoms rush steadily through, leaving only a recognizable *pattern of doing* rather than solid frozen stuff. Connecting modern quantum observation puzzles to ancient insight, he shifts attitude from Dylan Thomas's bitter 'rage at the dying of the light' to playful delight in vital tension—without slight resistance, form could not dance at all! Answering whether returning forms or lives are identical or separate with the Pāli maxim *na ca so, na ca añño* ('neither the same nor another'), Watts uses low vs. high organ pipes to show how lifespan gaps differ from moment-to-moment breathing only in slower vibrational frequency.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings

Bringing the Three Marks down from abstraction into everyday physics and biology, Watts demonstrates why fixed personal identity is an optical illusion. Just as a Niagara whirlpool never clutches permanent water droplets yet keeps a lively recognizable shape, or just as 'University of California' remains an evolving pattern while students, faculty, and concrete buildings continuously turn over, each human being is an uncatchable dance of atoms—far harder to band than migrating pigeons.

Facing the emotional sting of change—which tempts poets like Dylan Thomas to rage furiously against dying light—Watts notes a subtle physical secret: form exists solely through gentle interplay between flow and playful resistance, just as music or tender human touch requires lively springiness instead of shapeless mush. Addressing karma and rebirth without smuggled-in immortal souls (*ātman*), Watts quotes the Pāli formula *na ca so, na ca añño* ('not the same, yet not another'), comparing a melody replayed two days running or high organ pipes sounding continuous versus deep bass pipes revealing rhythmic pulses. Human birth and death simply pulse across wider wavelengths than sleeping and waking—the exact same shimmering vibration.
