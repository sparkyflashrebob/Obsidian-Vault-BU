## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ajahn Candasiri.

* **Theme**: The Five Daily Recollections (*Upajjhatthana Sutta*); confronting the cultural denial of mortality; aging and chronic physical decline as spiritual teachers; *Maraṇānussati* (Mindfulness of Death) as the wellspring of compassion and urgency (*saṃvega*).

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Candasiri delivers an unflinching, compassionate exposition on the three universal messengers: aging (*jarā*), sickness (*byādhi*), and death (*maraṇa*). Modern Western society expends immense resources attempting to deny impermanence—glorifying eternal youth and exiling the sick and dying to institutional corridors. Ajahn Candasiri reflects on her personal milestone of turning 50, recalling the profound admonition of her teachers that entering the autumn of life is the prime opportunity to awaken. Rather than being morbid or depressing, the daily contemplation of mortality cuts through trivial anxieties and vanity. When we accept that this breath could be our last, our relationships become tender, our grudges vanish, and we rest in that deathless awareness (*amata-dhātu*) which does not age, sicken, or die.
