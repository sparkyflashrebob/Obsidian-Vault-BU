## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker / Chanting Leader**: Ajahn Candasiri.

* **Theme**: The meditative power of English liturgical chanting; contemplating the meaning of the Triple Gem in one's native language; dedication of body and life to liberation.

* **Circumstances**: Conducted as the Saturday evening pūjā during the Spirit Rock Monastic Retreat, enabling English-speaking retreatants to absorb the profound doctrinal meaning behind the ancient Pāli cadences.

* **Condensed Version of the Practice**: While Pāli chanting connects practitioners with historical lineage and musical rhythm, chanting in English allows direct cognitive contemplation of the words. Ajahn Candasiri guides the retreatants through the English translation of the Evening Service: honoring the Blessed One who fully attained perfect enlightenment; recollecting the Dhamma as timeless (*akāliko*), encouraging investigation (*ehipassiko*), and leading inward; and honoring the Sangha as 'sorrow's destroyer.' Committing one's body and life to the Triple Gem reorients our existential priorities away from self-centered vanity toward selfless awakening.
