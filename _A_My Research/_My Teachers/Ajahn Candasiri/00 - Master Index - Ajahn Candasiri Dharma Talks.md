## 📜 Master Mettā Compendium Entries Harvested

During this comprehensive processing of Ajahn Candasiri's audio archive, the following master dedication and mettā phrases were captured for **Phase 4: Master Mettā Phrases Compendium**:

1. **Evening Chanting in Pāli [12:30–13:37] — Universal Salutation and Triple Gem Refuge**:

   > *"By the utterance of this truth, may I grow in the Master's way. By my devotion to the Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha, may all blessings be bestowed upon all living beings, freeing them from fear, suffering, and sorrow."*

2. **Challenging Conditions [56:47–58:47] — Courageous Equanimity in the Storm**:

   > *"May we meet all difficult conditions with patience, dignity, and an open heart. Untouched by praise or blame, pleasure or pain, may our hearts rest in unshakeable peace, wishing safety, healing, and freedom to all beings."*

3. **Evening Chanting in English [10:52–12:15] — English Dedication of Life & Sorrow's Destruction**:

   > *"The Sangha is sorrow's destroyer and bestows blessings on me. To the Triple Gem I dedicate this body and life. By the utterance of this truth, may I grow in the Master's way, and may all beings be blessed with peace, freedom, and liberation from all sorrow."*

4. **Living with Aging, Sickness, and Death [53:29–55:40] — Embrace of Mortality & Deathless Sanctuary**:

   > *"May we meet the reality of aging, sickness, and death with serene acceptance, courage, and radiant peace. Free from fear and attachment, may our hearts rest in the deathless sanctuary of awakening, and may all beings be free from sorrow, lamentation, and despair."*

5. **Social Thought, Speech & Action [58:10–60:15] — Social Harmony & Compassionate Action**:

   > *"May all our thoughts, words, and actions be guided by non-harming, humility, and boundless goodwill. Free from enmity and self-righteousness, may we be instruments of healing, reconciliation, and peace in a divided world."*
