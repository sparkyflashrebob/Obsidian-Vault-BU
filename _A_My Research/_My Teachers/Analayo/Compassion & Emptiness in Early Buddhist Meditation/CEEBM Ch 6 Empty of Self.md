# NOT-SELF
Teaching on Not-Self and how identity can arise in relation to the 5 Aggregates

1st Sermon by the Buddha
4 Noble Truths
2nd discourse to the same five former companions
teaching of not-self leading to their full awakening

> Bodily form is not-self. If bodily form were the self, then it should not happen that disease and pain arise in relation to bodily form, and one should not get the wish for bodily form to be in this way and not in that way. Because bodily form is not-self, there is the arising of disease and pain in relation to bodily form and one gets the wish for bodily form to be in this way and not to be in that way. Feeling ... perception ... formations ... consciousness is also like this.

Analayo. Compassion and Emptiness in Early Buddhist Meditation . Windhorse Publications Ltd. Kindle Edition. 

1. A self that is not in control and cannot have everything it wishes
2. Body can get sick
3. Feelings are sometimes painful
4. perceptions not always enjoyable
5. one decisions do not always lead to desired results
6. consciousness does not experience only what one wishes
7. can influence things to a certain extent, but they are clearly beyond one's complete control.

A. Aggregates impermanent and subject to change
B. Dukkha in that they cannot provide lasting satisfaction
C. Since they are dukkha, they are not fit to be regarded as a self

	Not a self that is permanent
	Not a source of unalloyed happiness (it is conditioned)(not  pure)

# REALIZATION OF [[Emptiness]] 

Teachings point towards understand what the signless concentration of the mind is rooted in
	rooted in formations 
	rooted in intentions
	as such, do not delight in that and seek it out 
	not establish awareness on that

When we "know" in this way we become liberated 
	liberated from the influx of [[sensual desire]] - craving for pleasure and possessions
	influx of [[existence]] - continued existence and be reborn/continue in another form
	influx of [[ignorance]] - lack of understanding of true nature of reality (annicca, anatta, dukkha)  
	The three primary causes of suffering

We know that we are free and therefore empty of the influx of sensual desire, existence & ignorance 

Whatever weariness we know is not the result of these [[asava]] but of this body of mind with its six sense spheres and the life faculty

> this is called truly dwelling in emptiness, without distortion, namely the eradication of the influxes, the influx-free and unconditioned liberation of the mind.

Analayo. Compassion and Emptiness in Early Buddhist Meditation . Windhorse Publications Ltd. Kindle Edition. 

The actual insight comes from directing attention to the [[Media - Unconditioning - Waking Up]]
	to the impermanent nature of the signless concentration of [[Mind]] 
	
Main point is the realization that the experiencing of signlessness is still conditioned 
	signlessness being not having any way of knowing it and free from any concepts 
	it is still something created in one's [[Mind]] 
	it is fabricated by its own intentions 
	being conditioned it is not something we can rely on - it is impermanent 
	dispassionate towards it
	becoming dispassionate to that which is impermanent and conditioned enables the breakthrough to full [[Liberation]] 

Whateve weariness that is left is simply the continuity of life - being in this body and having these senses 

The destruction of the influxes comes to be defined as the "unsurpassible" manifestation of emptiness 
	Emptiness is really about "voiding" the mind of defilements 

Chirping stops - the mind becomes silent

# SUMMARY EXPLORING EMPTINESS
1. Direct attention to our present situations 
2. Find something more stable than all the busyness (ie forest) invites seclusion
3. Find something solid/indisquishable character (i.e earth, nimitta) from here degrees of abstraction 
4. From solidity to spacious - immaterial spheres - no obstructions or limits - deconstruct our tendency to perceive objects as if they are solid and independently existing
5. From here turning to the mind itself - towards what we are conscious of and how this colors our experience - our mental projections and evaluations.
6. Seeing thru this as being conditioned - resulting in the notion that there is nothing. Empty of self and what can be called self. Nothing to be identified with. 
7. Turning to signlessness - not attending to the features and aspect of objects - letting go of concepts or notions in the mind. 
8. Knowing that this too is conditioned, one gives up holding to any experience of emptiness.  "The true realization of supreme emptiness dawns." One becomes aware of weariness due to [[Condtionality]] . One becomes aware of the [[Media - Unconditioning - Waking Up]]. And what remains is simply the continuity of life.   

1. Gradual refinement of experience - meditation leaves behind matter and it complement, space.
2. Letting go of identification with the experiencing [[Mind]] 
3. Letting go of the signs required for the formation of concepts
4. Letting go of the experience of emptiness experiences so far
5. Knowing they are all conditioned - arrive at the supreme emptiness of <u>liberating the mind of defilements</u>.
6. Letting go of experience itself.
