### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the afternoon session of the 'Recollections of Ajahn Chah' retreat at Spirit Rock on September 19, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno delves deeply into how Ajahn Chah trained senior disciples to lead communities. He reflects on the ten years Ajahn Chah spent bedridden and silent, teaching through his uncomplaining physical presence.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Furnace of Community Life**: Why Ajahn Chah prioritized living and working together in Sangha over isolated forest hermitages. Communal life rubs off our rough edges.
- **Skillful Means with Stubborn Students**: How Ajahn Chah used sudden reversals, appointing reluctant monks to positions of heavy responsibility to burn away their self-concern.
- **The Silent Sermon of Sickness**: Caring for the paralyzed master from 1982 to 1992, witnessing how total surrender to physical decay revealed absolute peace.
