### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Overcoming the subtle conceit of comparison (*māna*)—measuring oneself as superior, equal, or inferior to others.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on July 31, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno examines the three modes of conceit: 'I am better,' 'I am equal,' and 'I am worse.' Even 'spiritual low self-esteem' is a form of self-centered conceit. True liberation occurs when all measurement is dropped and the mind rests in unconditioned awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Three Conceits (Māna)**: Superiority breeds arrogance; equality breeds rivalry and defensiveness; inferiority breeds depression and self-loathing.
- **The Scent of 'I Am' (Khemaka Sutta)**: How even advanced practitioners retain a lingering residue of selfhood like soap scent on a freshly laundered cloth, until final release.
- **Resting Beyond Comparison**: Learning to celebrate the virtues of others (*muditā*) and accept our own present capacities without measurement.
