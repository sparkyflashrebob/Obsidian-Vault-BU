### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on August 20, 2011, Ajahn Pasanno provides comprehensive meditation instructions for Ānāpānasati. He emphasizes establishing physical comfort and posture, recognizing the rhythm of natural breathing, and using the breath to soothe autonomic nervous system activation.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Somatic Setup**: Aligning the spine, opening the chest, and releasing tension from the eyes, jaw, and belly before looking for the breath.
- **Receptive Observation**: Not interfering with the breath's speed or depth; allowing the body to breathe itself and resting as the witness.
- **Calming Bodily Formations**: Using the smooth flow of breath to iron out somatic agitation and invite gentle joy (*pīti-sukha*).
