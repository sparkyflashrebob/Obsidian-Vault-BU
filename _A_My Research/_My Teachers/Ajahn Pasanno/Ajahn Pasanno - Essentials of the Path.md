### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Returning to the bedrock essentials of Buddhist practice: the threefold training in virtue (*sīla*), concentration (*samādhi*), and wisdom (*paññā*).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on July 17, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno cuts through spiritual complexity by reaffirming the non-negotiables of the path. When practice feels stagnant, confusing, or overburdened, we return to the threefold training: purifying our conduct, steadying the attention, and examining attachments with clear wisdom.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sīla as Foundation**: Without clear ethical conduct and sensory restraint, concentration remains unstable and anxiety-ridden.
- **Samādhi as Gathering**: Collecting the scattered energies of the mind around an anchor (breath or body) to create a unified, peaceful instrument.
- **Paññā as Liberator**: Applying collected stillness to penetrate the three characteristics (impermanence, unsatisfactoriness, not-self).
