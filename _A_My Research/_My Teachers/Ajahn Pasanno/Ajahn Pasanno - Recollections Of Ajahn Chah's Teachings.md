### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Recorded on New Year's Day 1995, this historical discourse captures Ajahn Pasanno reflecting on the living Dhamma of Ajahn Chah as preparations began for founding Abhayagiri Monastery. He shares how Ajahn Chah established Wat Pah Nanachat and guided his Western disciples through the friction of cultural conditioning.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Spirit of Wat Pah Nanachat**: How Ajahn Chah created an international training ground where Western seekers learned to surrender pride and embrace ancient Vinaya standards.
- **Teaching Through Shock and Humor**: Ajahn Chah's ability to expose students' hidden hypocrisies with gentle mockery and radiant kindness.
- **Carrying the Dhamma to the West**: Ensuring that monastic branch monasteries in Europe and America preserved the raw ascetic simplicity of the Thai forest.
