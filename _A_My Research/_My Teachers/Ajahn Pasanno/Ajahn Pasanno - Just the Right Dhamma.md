### Circumstances
Delivered on Friday, May 17, 2024, at Amaravati Buddhist Monastery during the opening international gathering celebrating Luang Por Sumedho's 90th year. Introduced with warmth and personal gratitude by Abbot Ajahn Amaro, Ajahn Pasanno addresses a worldwide assembly of monastics and lay disciples on the Thai phrase *Dhamma kū phō dī*—'The Dhamma is that which is just right'—exploring the delicate balance of spiritual calibration.

### People Involved
- **Introducer**: Ajahn Amaro (Abbot, Amaravati Buddhist Monastery).

- **Assembly**: Luang Por Sumedho, senior international elders, resident Sangha, and lay disciples gathered from around the world.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Finding harmonious balance in Dhamma practice—not straining or over-tightening the mind, nor slacking into complacency, but discerning 'just the right Dhamma' for the present moment.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during a visit to Amaravati on May 17, 2024, Ajahn Pasanno reflects on the subtle art of spiritual calibration. Drawing on the classic lute-tuning simile from the Soṇa Sutta, he illuminates how spiritual practice is neither heroic self-mortification nor passive drifting. By developing sensitive awareness of our current bodily and mental state, we apply the specific skillful means needed to gladden, settle, or liberate the mind.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Tuning the Faculties (Indriya-Samatta)**: Harmonizing faith with wisdom, and energy with concentration. When energy exceeds stillness, agitation arises; when concentration lacks energy, the mind sinks into dullness.
- **Discerning Present Conditions**: Rather than mechanically applying the same technique, recognizing what is required right now—whether calming bodily agitation, brightening a foggy mind, or investigating attachment.
- **Monastic Lineage and Living Dhamma**: Reflections on the enduring spirit of the Amaravati and Chithurst communities, training under Luang Por Sumedho, and the timeless nature of the Dhamma.
