### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Saturday evening, September 2, 2023, Ajahn Pasanno guides practitioners to transform their emotional relationship with the meditation anchor. Instead of treating the breath as an inert target to be captured by will, we relate to it as an intimate, trustworthy friend. When friendliness infuses mindfulness, somatic holding dissolves naturally.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Aggression of Control**: How Western practitioners often approach meditation as an aggressive conquest, creating tension in the forehead, chest, and stomach.
- **Breathing with Kindness**: Inviting the breath to nourish the nervous system; allowing each exhalation to soothe bodily fatigue.
- **Establishing Natural Ease**: When the breath feels welcomed, the wandering mind willingly returns home to the body without being coerced.
