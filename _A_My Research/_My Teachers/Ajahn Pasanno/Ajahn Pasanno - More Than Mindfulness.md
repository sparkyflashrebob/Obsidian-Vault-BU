### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Why bare attention alone is insufficient for liberation—integrating mindfulness (*sati*) with clear comprehension (*sampajañña*), wise consideration (*yoniso manasikāra*), and right view (*sammā-diṭṭhi*).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on April 16, 2024, at Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery, Ajahn Pasanno critiques the modern commercial tendency to isolate 'mindfulness' as a standalone relaxation technique. In authentic early Buddhism, mindfulness is always accompanied by clear comprehension, moral discernment, and wise investigation. Without wisdom, bare attention can simply be used to be mindfully angry or mindfully greedy.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sati and Sampajañña**: Mindfulness remembers the object; clear comprehension knows the purpose, suitability, domain of practice, and underlying reality of non-delusion.
- **Yoniso Manasikāra (Wise Reflection)**: Looking deeply into the causal conditions of mental states rather than merely watching them passively.
- **The Noble Eightfold Path as an Organism**: Mindfulness is one limb of eight; without Right View, Right Intention, and Right Livelihood, practice remains stunted.
