### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The exhilarating release experienced when the Five Hindrances fall away, comparing the free mind to someone released from debt, prison, and slavery.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given in the summer of 2023, Ajahn Pasanno explores the five classic canonical similes for freedom from the hindrances. Overcoming mental obstacles is not grim drudgery; it is the joyful recovery of our birthright. Like a debtor paying his final bill or a traveler arriving safely through a wilderness, the settled heart breathes with relief.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Five Similes (MN 39)**: Sensual desire is like a loan; ill will is like a feverish illness; sloth-and-torpor is like imprisonment in a dungeon; restlessness-and-worry is like slavery; doubt is like lost travelers in a desert.
- **Real-Time Liberation**: Freedom is not a distant event after death; every time we let go of anger or craving, we experience a momentary taste of Nibbāna.
- **The Power of Gladness (*Pāmojja*)**: How acknowledging small victories in meditation generates the wholesome joy needed to sustain continuous practice.
