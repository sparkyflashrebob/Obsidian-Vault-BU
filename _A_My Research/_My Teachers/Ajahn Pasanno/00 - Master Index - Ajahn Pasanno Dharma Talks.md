# Venerable Ajahn Pasanno — Master Audio Recordings Index & Contemplative Compendium

> [!NOTE] Teacher Provenance & Archival Scope
> **Venerable Ajahn Pasanno** (born 1949 in Manitoba, Canada) is one of the most senior Western disciples of Venerable Ajahn Chah. Ordained in Thailand in 1974 at Wat Pleng Vipassana, he trained under Ajahn Chah at Wat Nong Pah Pong and served for 14 years as the abbot of Wat Pah Nanachat (International Forest Monastery). In 1997, he moved to California to co-found and lead Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery alongside Ajahn Amaro.
> 
> This Master Index catalogs **149 recorded discourses, retreats, and inquiries**, plus the complete **More Than Mindfulness** anthology, organized across five foundational modules:
> 1. **The 2013 Winter Retreat Monastic Study Curriculum** (33 talks on the Five Hindrances, Five Khandhas, Six Sense Bases, and Monastic Duty).
> 2. **The 2008 Abhayagiri Metta Retreat & Q&A Compendiums** (13 Core Talks + 5 Master Practitioner Inquiry Compendiums spanning 66 questions, Cazadero, CA).
> 3. **Standalone Dhamma Discourses & Historical Recollections** (26 discourses spanning 1995–2024).
> 4. **Anthologies & Publications**: *More Than Mindfulness* (18 Chapters and Master Index).
