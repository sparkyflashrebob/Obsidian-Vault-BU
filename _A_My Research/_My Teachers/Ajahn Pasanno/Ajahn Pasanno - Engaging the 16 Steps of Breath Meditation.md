### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given on Thanksgiving Day 2013, Ajahn Pasanno guides retreatants through the 16 steps of Ānāpānasati. Rather than treating the steps as a rigid, mechanical ladder, he presents them as a flexible landscape of exploration: training bodily calm, gladdening and steadying the mind, and culminating in natural relinquishment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **First Tetrad (Kāya)**: Experiencing long/short breath, experiencing the whole body (*sabbakāya*), and calming the bodily formation (*passambhayam kāyasaṅkhāraṃ*).
- **Second Tetrad (Vedanā)**: Experiencing rapture (*pīti*), pleasure (*sukha*), and calming mental formations (*cittasaṅkhāra*).
- **Third Tetrad (Citta)**: Experiencing the mind, gladdening the mind, concentrating the mind, and releasing the mind.
- **Fourth Tetrad (Dhammā)**: Contemplating inconstancy (*aniccānupassī*), dispassion (*virāgānupassī*), cessation (*nirodhānupassī*), and relinquishment (*paṭinissaggānupassī*).
