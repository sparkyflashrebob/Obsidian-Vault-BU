### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Practicing Dhamma means engaging with life, work, and community—how to ensure our engagement leads to liberation rather than entanglement.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Saturday, August 14, 2004, following two weeks of monastic quiet, Ajahn Pasanno examines the nature of spiritual work and community life. Spiritual practice is not passive withdrawal; it is active engagement. The crucial test is whether our interaction is driven by craving and defensiveness, or guided by wisdom, patience, and relinquishment.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Engagement without Entanglement**: Participating fully in duties—building huts, cooking, administrative tasks—without creating identity or suffering around the outcomes.
- **Two Sorts of Thinking (MN 19)**: Sorting mental motivations into those imbued with desire/ill will and those imbued with renunciation and harmlessness.
- **The Mirror of Sangha**: How living in community reflects back our attachments and provides real-time training in relinquishing self-will.
