### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on January 16, 2024, during the annual memorial gathering at Abhayagiri, Ajahn Pasanno reflects on the enduring presence of Ajahn Chah thirty-two years after his passing. Ajahn Chah's greatest gift was not personal charisma, but pointing disciples away from personality toward the unshakeable refuge of the Dhamma within.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Kataññū-Katavedī (Gratitude in Action)**: Honoring the teacher not merely through ritual incense and chanting, but through practicing the Dhamma in accordance with the Dhamma (*dhammānudhamma-paṭipatti*).
- **The Lion's Roar of Impermanence**: Ajahn Chah's constant refrain: 'Mai nae' (It's uncertain). How this simple phrase dissolves all philosophical confusion.
- **The Living Lineage**: How hundreds of branch monasteries worldwide continue to practice according to the same standards established in the Ubon jungle.
