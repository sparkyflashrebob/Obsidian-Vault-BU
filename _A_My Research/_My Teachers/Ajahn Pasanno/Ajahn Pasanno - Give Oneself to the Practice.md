### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on April 10, 2021, Ajahn Pasanno addresses the subtle ambivalence that keeps practitioners half-committed to practice. Drawing on the noble lineage of the disciples (*ariyavaṃsa*), he reminds us that the Dhamma carries the sole flavor of release. We cannot experience liberation while holding back; freedom unfolds when we surrender our preferences and give ourselves completely to the discipline.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Ambivalence of Modern Mind**: How intellectual hesitation, cynical doubt, and endless options prevent practitioners from tasting the fruit of stillness.
- **The Four Traditions of the Noble Ones (*Ariyavaṃsa*)**: Contentment with simple food, clothing, lodging, and delight in meditation and relinquishment.
- **Surrendering the Burden of Self**: Finding relief not by perfecting our ego-constructs, but by offering our life into the container of Dhamma.
