### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the opening Saturday of the 2022 Rains Retreat (Vassa), Ajahn Pasanno unpacks the four foundational contemplations: the preciousness of human birth, the reality of impermanence and death, the inviolability of kamma, and the pervasive unsatisfactory nature of saṃsāra.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Precious Human Birth**: Utilizing the canonical simile of the blind sea turtle surfacing once every century into a single floating yoke to appreciate the rarity of our spiritual opportunity.
- **Impermanence & Mortality**: Death is certain, the hour of death is uncertain. Allowing mortality contemplation (*maraṇānussati*) to burn away trivial preoccupations.
- **The Law of Kamma**: Actions have consequences. We are the heirs to our deeds; ethical conduct (*sīla*) provides the essential protective container.
- **Defects of Saṃsāra**: Recognizing that worldly pleasures cannot yield lasting fulfillment, motivating sincere renunciation.
