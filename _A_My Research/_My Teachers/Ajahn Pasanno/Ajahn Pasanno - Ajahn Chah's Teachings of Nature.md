### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on September 4, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno reflects on Ajahn Chah's profound communion with the natural world. Ajahn Chah rarely cited scholarly commentaries; instead, he pointed disciples to the forest around them: yellow leaves falling from trees, streams flowing around rocks, and animals foraging. Nature reveals impermanence, harmony, and effortless conditionality.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Sermon of Falling Leaves**: Seeing green leaves, yellow leaves, and dry decaying leaves on the same branch as an undeniable demonstration of human aging and mortality.
- **Trees Don't Grumble**: How trees accept sunlight, rain, storms, and winter without complaint, illustrating the virtue of patient endurance (*khanti*).
- **Forest Hermitage as Sanctuary**: Why solitude in nature naturally calms the human nervous system and makes the mind transparent to Dhamma.
