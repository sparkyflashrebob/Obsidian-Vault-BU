### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Deconstructing the ego's addiction to its personal problems and discovering the peace of not-self (*anattā*).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on August 7, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno investigates how the mind creates a solid 'me' out of its difficulties. Humans paradoxically cherish their personal problems because problems provide identity. By seeing difficulties as impersonal conditions (*idappaccayatā*) rather than personal dramas, the burden dissolves.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Manufacture of 'Me'**: How the sense of self is not a static thing, but an ongoing verb—'I-making' (*ahaṅkāra*) and 'mine-making' (*mamaṅkāra*).
- **Reframing Dukkha**: Shifting from 'Why is this happening to ME?' to 'This is the First Noble Truth entering the senses.'
- **Dropping Ownership**: Observing physical pain and emotional irritation without claiming them as property.
