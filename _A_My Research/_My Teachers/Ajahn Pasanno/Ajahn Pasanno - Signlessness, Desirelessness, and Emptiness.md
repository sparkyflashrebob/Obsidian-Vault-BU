### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The three classical doorways to liberation (*vimokkha-mukha*): signlessness (*animitta*), desirelessness (*appaṇihita*), and emptiness (*suññatā*).

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Labor Day, September 5, 2011, Ajahn Pasanno provides an advanced meditation exposition on the three liberative doors. When attention ceases identifying with sensory appearances (signs), relinquishes the impulse to grasp or direct experience (desirelessness), and sees the lack of substantial self (emptiness), consciousness opens directly into Nibbāna.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Signlessness (*Animitta*)**: Moving beyond conceptual tags and labels (visual forms, sounds, mental concepts) into the unconditioned field prior to signs.
- **Desirelessness (*Appaṇihita*)**: Abandoning all longing, projecting, and intention toward conditioned phenomena. When the mind wants nothing from reality, it becomes imperturbable.
- **Emptiness (*Suññatā*)**: Seeing all experiences—thoughts, physical pains, bliss—as empty of a core owner or permanent substance.
