### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating the four nutriments of life and guarding the mind against toxic sensory and psychological consumption.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered in the summer of 2010, Ajahn Pasanno examines the Buddha's radical teaching on the four nutriments: physical food (*kabaḷīkārāhāra*), sensory contact (*phassāhāra*), mental intention (*manosañcetanāhāra*), and consciousness (*viññāṇāhāra*). Just as spoiled food sickens the body, unexamined sensory stimulation poisons the heart. Wisdom lies in rigorous sense restraint.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Simile of the Son's Flesh (SN 12.63)**: Contemplating physical food with the solemn gravity of parents crossing a desert, eating only to sustain life rather than for entertainment.
- **Sensory Contact as Nutriment**: Recognizing how news, gossip, entertainment, and digital media feed greed, anger, and anxiety.
- **Nutriment of Volition & Consciousness**: How chronic desires for future becoming fuel existential exhaustion; learning to starve unwholesome desires by withholding attention.
