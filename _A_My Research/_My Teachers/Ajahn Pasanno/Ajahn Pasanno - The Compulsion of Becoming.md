### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating craving for becoming (*bhava-taṇhā*)—the relentless existential drive to be someone, go somewhere, or accomplish something.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on August 21, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno explores the mechanics of Dependent Origination, focusing on the link of becoming (*bhava*). Even on the meditation cushion, the mind constantly attempts to 'become' an enlightened person or enter a future state. Freedom is discovered by stopping all becoming and resting in uncreated stillness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Mechanism of Bhava**: How craving (*taṇhā*) leads to clinging (*upādāna*), which crystallizes into worlds of becoming (*bhava*) and subsequent birth (*jāti*) into suffering.
- **Spiritual Becoming**: How ambition for spiritual attainment recreates the very wheel of saṃsāra on the cushion.
- **Resting in the Unborn**: Stepping off the treadmill of becoming by fully inhabiting the present moment without demanding that it lead anywhere.

## 4. Notable Sutta Passages & Practical Anecdotes

Ajahn Pasanno recalls asking Ajahn Chah what monks should aim to become. Ajahn Chah laughed: 'Don't be a monk, don't be an abbot, don't be an arhat. Don't be anything at all! When you are nothing, you are free.'
