### Theme of the Dharma Talk

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Saturday, June 12, 2021, during Abhayagiri's 25th Anniversary Nine Days of Dhamma, Ajahn Pasanno investigates the compulsive hunger of the mind. Even on the spiritual path, the habit of 'seeking'—for better states, deeper samādhi, or greater recognition—perpetuates restlessness. Freedom is found when the seeking heart recognizes its own completeness in present awareness.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Perpetual Hunger of Taṇhā**: How craving subtly hijacks spiritual aspirations, transforming meditation into an endless consumer project.
- **Contentment (*Santuṭṭhī*) as Supreme Wealth**: Cultivating active contentment with simple food, shelter, clothing, and present mental conditions as the foundation for deep peace.
- **Honoring 25 Years of Abhayagiri**: Reflections on the founding of the monastery in 1996, the generosity of the community, and the timeless purpose of monastic sanctuaries.
