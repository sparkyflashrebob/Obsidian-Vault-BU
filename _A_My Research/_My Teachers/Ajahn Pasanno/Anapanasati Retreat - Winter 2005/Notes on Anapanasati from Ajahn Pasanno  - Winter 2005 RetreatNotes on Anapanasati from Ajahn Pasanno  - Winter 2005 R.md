Notes on Anapanasati from Ajahn Pasanno (Winter 2005 Retreat)

Use a particular practice to establish a basis of awareness and attention.
Recognize the gross aspects of what our mind tends to.
Learn how to give us a handle on sustaining attention.
MOB
POSTURE
Basics of paying attention to the posture.
Get a good feel for the body
recommend sitting on your sit bones and not your tailbone.
May mean leaning a bit forward
Not holding yourself with your back or shoulders
Can let the breath come in and be natural
may need to slide hands forward a bit
BREATHING
natural - not control
exercise of mindfulness and attentiveness to what the breath is doing
as the mind relaxes the body become more tranquil
natural for the breath to become longer
CLEARLY KNOWING
pajānāti
helpful to pay attention clearly to the beginning of the in-breath
aware of the sensation of breathing
cold/hot/does it feel restricted/flow freely
More interested in exploring the breath than to gain something out of it.
There is always this frantic quality that comes into the mind when we are trying to make the meditation be a particular way.
Part of the practice of meditation is recognizing the underlying assumptions that come with the simple act of attending to the breath coming in and going out.
Easy to overlay with our projections, assumptions, view and opinions without questioning.
They slip right in.
So as we take an interest we begin to see what hampers that clear knowledge of breathing. Insights into those particular tendencies.
Not get bogged down in that - just taking note - student

ATTEND TO THE RHYTHM OF THE BREATH - THE SENSATION OF BREATHING
Whole path - nose - throat - chest - abdomen
Body and breath together
An Anchor for the breath
If one feel more comfortable bring attention to the abdomen and use that as the main focal point.
Abdomen advantage of their being movement - more clear - not subtle

ENGAGING ATTENTION
Mind wanders and needs to be engaged in order to sustain attention
If not engaging awareness, attention, taking interest, assessing the result of that attention then the mind is going to start going dull very quickly.
Tendancy as we sit and pay attention to the breath for us to wait for the result to come - for the mind to be peaceful. - tendency for the mind to become dull and get sleepy/drowsy or one starts to daydream/poliferating.

The actual object - place where you pay attention is an aspect we need to be considering

OTHER MEDITATIONS
Walking Meditation
Normal activity and making it into meditation by creating awareness of what we are doing
20 - 25 paces
attention to physical act of walking
relaxing the body
aware of posture
movement
attention to sensations of walking
path flat even if possible so not worry about obstacles or tripping
if mind starts proliferating then stop (pause) in the middle of the path and bring the mind back, clear the mind, come back to the physical sensation of walking
at the end of each then asking if we are present
if not another opportunity to establish mindfulness
sustain the continuity of awareness
