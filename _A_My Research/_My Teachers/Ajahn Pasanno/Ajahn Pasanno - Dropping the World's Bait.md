### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Investigating how worldly pleasures, praise, and comfort act as hooks that ensnare the unwary heart, and finding the safety of renunciation.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered during the 2015 Thanksgiving Retreat, Ajahn Pasanno unpacks the classic Nivāpa Sutta where sensory objects are likened to crops planted by a deer hunter. Trappers do not set out bare hooks; they cover them with delicious bait. Wisdom is the capacity to perceive the hook beneath the sweetest worldly pleasures.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Four Herds of Deer (MN 25)**: The first herd eats carelessly and is destroyed; the second herd starves itself in fear and falls prey; the third herd becomes complacent in safe areas; the fourth herd establishes a secure sanctuary beyond the hunter's reach.
- **The Hook in the Bait**: How praise, social approval, and material comfort inevitably extract a price in anxiety and loss of integrity.
- **The True Sanctuary**: Entering the jhānās and cultivating insight into not-self as the only impenetrable fortress against Māra's snares.
