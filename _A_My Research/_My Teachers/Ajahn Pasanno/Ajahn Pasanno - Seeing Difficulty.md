### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on August 14, 2010, continuing reflections on the Buddha's First Sermon, Ajahn Pasanno explores the First Noble Truth. Suffering is not a failure; it is the primary noble truth to be fully understood (*pariññeyya*). True ease arises when we stop fleeing difficulty and hold it with courageous, somatic clarity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Two Extremes**: Investigating our habit of oscillating between seeking sensual comfort when stressed and imposing harsh self-judgment when meditation feels dry.
- **Understanding Dukkha**: Dukkha must be comprehended, craving abandoned, cessation realized, and the path cultivated. We cannot jump to peace without fully embracing the reality of stress.
- **The Knot in the Stomach**: How emotional friction immediately registers as tightness in the solar plexus or belly; meeting this contraction with spacious patience rather than aversion.
