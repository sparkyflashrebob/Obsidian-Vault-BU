### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Speaking at Amaravati on May 15, 2024, Ajahn Pasanno shares intimate memories and contemplative lessons drawn from nearly five decades of training alongside Luang Por Sumedho. He explores Luang Por's signature teachings: questioning who is suffering, using the inner 'Sound of Silence' (nāda) as a meditation refuge, and realizing that awareness is naturally unentangled.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Direct Pointing of Luang Por Sumedho**: Luang Por's constant inquiry: 'Who is suffering? What is this?' Cutting through elaborate psychological stories with direct existential awareness.
- **The Sound of Silence (Nāda Sound)**: Using the subtle high-pitched internal sound as an immediate anchor for consciousness, shifting attention from the contents of consciousness to the container of knowing.
- **Surrendering Self-Importance**: How Luang Por modeled humility, monastic duty, and the willingness to bear organizational burdens while maintaining unbroken inner spaciousness.
