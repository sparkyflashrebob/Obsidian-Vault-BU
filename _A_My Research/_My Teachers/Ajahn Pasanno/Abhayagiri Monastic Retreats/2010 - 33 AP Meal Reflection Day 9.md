## 📖 Executive Summary & Core Teachings

On the penultimate day, Ajahn Pasanno guides practitioners to observe how appetite and eating fuel the psychological momentum of becoming (bhava) and self-identity.

Eating is one of the most primal sensory activities of human life. By bringing the mindfulness cultivated on the cushion into the food line, eating ceases to be an unthinking biological indulgence and becomes a potent meditation on the four elements, impermanence, and moderation.
