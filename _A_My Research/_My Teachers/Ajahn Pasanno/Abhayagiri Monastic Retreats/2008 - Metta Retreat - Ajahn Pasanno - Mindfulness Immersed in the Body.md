### People Involved
- **Speaker**: Ajahn Pasanno.
- **Assembly**: Monastic and lay retreatants gathered at the Padmasambhava Peace Institute.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Establishing somatic presence as the indispensable physical container for loving-kindness; softening holding patterns in the belly, chest, and face.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of September 8, 2008, Ajahn Pasanno guides practitioners to ground mettā in the living body. Goodwill is not an abstract concept visualized in the intellect; it is felt somatically. When the body is tense or guarded, the heart contracts. By relaxing physical holding patterns and inhabiting the posture, mettā radiates effortlessly.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Body as the Altar of Practice**: Sinking attention out of the head into the torso, diaphragm, and pelvic floor.
- **Somatic Contractions as Messengers**: Meeting tightness in the chest or solar plexus not with irritation, but with welcoming warmth.
- **The Posture of Dignity**: Aligning the spine and settling into gravity, allowing the physical form to embody peace.
