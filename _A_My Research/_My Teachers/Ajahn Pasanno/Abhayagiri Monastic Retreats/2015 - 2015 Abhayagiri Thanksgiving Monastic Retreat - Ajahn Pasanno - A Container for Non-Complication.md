## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Burden of Worldly Complexity
Every day in the world requires endless negotiations: what to wear, what to eat, what to buy, how to project an identity. This habitual churning keeps the mental nervous system in chronic red alert. Ajahn Pasanno explains that retreat is an intentional shelter where all those unnecessary decisions are laid down.

### 2. Sīla as a Container of Non-Complication
The Eight Precepts (*aṭṭhaṅgasīla*) are not arbitrary religious rules designed to constrict us; they are boundaries of non-complication (*nippapañca*). They eliminate harmful speech, sexual tension, and sensual distractions, creating total emotional safety among fifty strangers practicing in the same room.

### 3. Creating the Space for Dhamma to Arise
When the external perimeter is sealed with virtue and silence, the mind can no longer distract itself with external drama. It is forced to look inward, discovering that peace is not something that must be acquired, but what naturally remains when complication ceases.
