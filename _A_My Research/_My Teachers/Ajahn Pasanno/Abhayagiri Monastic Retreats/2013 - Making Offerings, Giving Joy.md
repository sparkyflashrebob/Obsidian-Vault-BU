## 🏛️ Structured Contextual Metadata

### Canonical & Contemporary Setting
* **Canonical Setting**: Reflects the *Chalaṅgadāna Sutta* (AN 6.37) on the six factors of giving: glad before giving, bright during giving, and joyful after giving (*dakkhiṇā visujjhati*).
* **Contemporary Recording Venue**: Angela Center sanctuary, Santa Rosa, California. Delivered on the final morning during the formal offering of requisites to the monastic Sangha.
* **Circumstances & Occasion**: Retreatants conclude their 10-day retreat by offering requisites, food, and financial support to Abhayagiri Monastery for the upcoming winter retreat.
* **People Involved**: Ajahn Pasanno (speaker), monastic Sangha, retreat stewards, and lay practitioners.

---

## 💡 Teachings Overview

### Central Contemplative Theme
**The Joy of Generosity (*Dāna-Mayā Puñña*) and Dedication of Merit**. Making offerings at the end of a retreat is not a transactional fee; it is an expression of heartfelt appreciation (*anumodanā*). Ajahn Pasanno expresses gratitude on behalf of the monastic community, noting how lay generosity sustains the forest hermitage through the cold winter months, and reminds practitioners to dedicate the merit to all beings.
