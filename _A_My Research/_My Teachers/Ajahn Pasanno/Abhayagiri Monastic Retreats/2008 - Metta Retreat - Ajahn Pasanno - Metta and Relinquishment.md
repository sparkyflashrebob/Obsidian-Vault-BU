### Theme of the Dharma Talk
How loving-kindness naturally matures beyond emotional affection into deep relinquishment (*vossagga*), insight, and equanimity.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on September 12, 2008, Ajahn Pasanno explores the ultimate fruit of mettā. In early Buddhism, loving-kindness is not merely a mood booster; it is developed supported by relinquishment (*vossagga-pariṇāmiṃ*). When goodwill is free from personal grasping, it naturally ripens into compassion (*karuṇā*), appreciative joy (*muditā*), and sublime equanimity (*upekkhā*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Vossagga (Relinquishment)**: Mettā without letting go leads to emotional fatigue and codependency. Relinquishment allows love to be completely free.
- **The Four Brahmavihāras as an Ecosystem**: Mettā meets suffering as compassion; meets goodness as joy; and rests in equanimity when outcomes cannot be changed.
- **The Bridge to Nibbāna**: How boundless loving-kindness dissolves the rigid sense of 'self' versus 'other,' providing direct insight into anattā.
