## 📜 Full Talk Transcript

**Ajahn Pasanno:**

Beginning our morning, this is the official opening of our retreat and our orientation. 

I would like to introduce the teachers and monastics who are here supporting us for these ten days: Ajahn Karunadhammo, who is the vice-abbot at Abhayagiri; Ajahn Yatiko, one of our senior monks; and Upasika Debbie Stamp, who has been a pillar of the Abhayagiri lay community and steward for many years. 

Together, we are here to support your practice, offer reflections, answer questions, and walk this path alongside you.
