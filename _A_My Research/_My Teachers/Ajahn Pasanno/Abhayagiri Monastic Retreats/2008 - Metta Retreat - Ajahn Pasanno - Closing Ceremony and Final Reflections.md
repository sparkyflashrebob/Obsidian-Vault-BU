### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Sunday morning, September 14, 2008, Ajahn Pasanno concludes the retreat. The true retreat does not end when we pack our bags; it moves into the marketplace. He offers practical advice for protecting the quiet mind as retreatants return to families, technology, and responsibilities, concluding with the traditional dedication of merit (*pattidāna*).

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Threshold of Re-Entry**: Not rushing into sensory overload; moving gently into speech and external duties.
- **Protecting the Buffer of Peace**: Maintaining a daily morning and evening sitting routine to keep the channel of mettā open.
- **Dedication of Merit (*Pattānumodanā*)**: Sharing the spiritual fruits of our collective retreat with all ancestors, parents, benefactors, and troubled beings everywhere.
