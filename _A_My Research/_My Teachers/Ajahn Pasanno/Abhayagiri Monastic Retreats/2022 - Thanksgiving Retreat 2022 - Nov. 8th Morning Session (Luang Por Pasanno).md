## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Global Sangha in Real Time
Luang Por Pasanno notes the wondrous interplay of causes that allows monastics and laypeople across the globe to meditate as one body. He expresses deep gratitude to the organizers and technical stewards making the hybrid retreat possible.

### 2. Contemplating Worldly Turmoil (*Lokadhamma*)
The modern world experiences relentless waves of collective anxiety: pandemics, economic uncertainty, social polarization, and climate fears. Luang Por reminds practitioners that external conditions have never been secure or predictable. The Buddha did not teach us to wait for the world to become perfect before finding peace.

### 3. Deepening Contemplative Roots
Like a banyan tree that sends down aerial roots to brace itself against monsoon typhoons, a meditator sends roots into virtue (*sīla*), mindfulness (*sati*), and loving-kindness (*mettā*). These inner roots ensure that when the storms of life strike, our peace remains intact.
