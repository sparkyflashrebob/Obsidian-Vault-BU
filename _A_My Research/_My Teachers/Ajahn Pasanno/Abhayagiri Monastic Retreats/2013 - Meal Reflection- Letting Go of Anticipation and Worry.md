## 💡 Teachings Overview

### Central Contemplative Theme
**Meeting Food Desires and Worries with Simple Knowing and Letting Go**.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Mealtime often triggers intense mental proliferation: anticipatory excitement, sensory craving, or subtle anxiety over diet and digestion. Ajahn Pasanno encourages retreatants to use the meal as an immediate laboratory for practicing 'simple knowing and letting go.' Even when thoughts of food and preference are tenacious, holding them in spacious knowing dissolves their compulsive power.

## 📜 Full Talk Transcript

**Ajahn Pasanno:** Just breaking for the meal and using the meal as an opportunity for reflecting on the different thoughts that come up into the mind.

There are inevitable thoughts of looking at food with expectation, and sometimes with worry. The mind tends to look for an object for thinking, proliferating, and filling itself. We can give it that sense of just knowing and letting go—knowing and letting go.

Even with something as tenacious as thoughts of food, thoughts of desire, or thoughts of aversion, all of that can still be held in that way of simple knowing and letting go.
