## 2. Structured Contextual Metadata

### Location
Padmasambhava Peace Institute, Cazadero, California; delivered during the annual silent Abhayagiri Monastic Metta Retreat.

### Canonical Setting
Canonical foundation in DN 13 (Tevijja Sutta), AN 4.125 (Metta Sutta).

### Circumstances
The classical canonical practice of radiating an all-pervading, boundless mind of loving-kindness to the North, South, East, West, above, and below.

### People Involved
- **Speaker**: Ajahn Pasanno.
- **Assembly**: Monastic and lay retreatants gathered at the Padmasambhava Peace Institute.

### Theme of the Dharma Talk
The classical canonical practice of radiating an all-pervading, boundless mind of loving-kindness to the North, South, East, West, above, and below.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the evening of September 12, 2008, Ajahn Pasanno guides retreatants through the ancient canonical method of directional radiance (*appamāṇa-cetovimutti*). Like a powerful horn blower making himself heard in all directions without limitation, the practitioner radiates an abundant, exalted, unconfined heart of goodwill to all living beings.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Horn-Blower Simile (DN 13)**: The canonical image of a conch blower who easily makes himself heard in all four directions; so too, boundless mettā leaves no corner untouched.
- **Directional Progression**: Radiating East, West, North, South, upward, downward, and all around.
- **Dissolving Spatial Boundaries**: Moving from personal relationships to an environmental, planetary consciousness of goodwill.
