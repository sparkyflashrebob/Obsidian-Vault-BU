### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Contemplative readings from the Pali Canon highlighting the supreme power of loving-kindness as the ultimate cosmic and psychological protector.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the final full evening of the retreat (September 13, 2008), Ajahn Pasanno reads and comments on key canonical passages. He emphasizes Itivuttaka 27, where the Buddha declares that just as the radiance of the moon outshines all the stars, so too the liberation of heart through loving-kindness outshines all other meritorious works.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Moon Outshining Stars (Iti 27)**: All external religious rituals, ascetic austerities, and material offerings do not equal a sixteenth part of a heart cultivating boundless goodwill.
- **Mettā as Invulnerability**: Canonical stories of weapons, fire, and poison failing to harm those abiding in pure loving-kindness.
- **Integrating Canonical Wisdom into Daily Life**: Carrying the beauty of the suttas back into homes, workplaces, and families.
