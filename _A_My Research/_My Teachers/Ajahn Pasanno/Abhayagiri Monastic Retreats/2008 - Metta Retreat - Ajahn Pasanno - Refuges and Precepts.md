### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Establishing the ethical and devotional container for the retreat: taking refuge in Buddha, Dhamma, and Sangha, and committing to the Five Precepts as the bedrock of mettā.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on the opening evening of the retreat (September 7, 2008), Ajahn Pasanno establishes the foundations of practice. Mettā cannot flourish in an atmosphere of guilt or moral agitation. By taking refuge in the Triple Gem and undertaking the precepts, practitioners establish a protective boundary of harmlessness (*avera*) that provides the psychological security required for boundless goodwill.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Refuge as Active Orientation**: Taking refuge is not a passive ritual, but aligning consciousness with awakening (Buddha), the truth of nature (Dhamma), and the noble community (Sangha).
- **Precepts as the Gift of Safety**: Undertaking ethical restraint gives the gift of fearlessness to all living beings, liberating the mind from remorse.
- **Setting Retreat Aspiration**: Moving away from ambitious spiritual performance toward patient, humble attunement.
