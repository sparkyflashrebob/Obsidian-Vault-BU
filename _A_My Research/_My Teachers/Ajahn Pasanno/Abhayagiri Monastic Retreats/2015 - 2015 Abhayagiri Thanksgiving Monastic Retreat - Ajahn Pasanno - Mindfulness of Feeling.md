## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. Vedanā as the Turning Point of the Mind
In the chain of Dependent Origination, feeling (*vedanā*) is the decisive link where saṁsāra is either perpetuated or dismantled. Once craving (*taṇhā*) catches fire, suffering is virtually guaranteed. But between feeling and craving, there is an open window of choice. If mindfulness abides, feeling remains just a raw feeling tone—it does not become greed or hatred.

### 2. Dissecting the Three Tones
- **Pleasant Feeling (*Sukha-vedanā*)**: The instinct is to cling, prolong, and repeat. Skillful practice appreciates ease without grabbing.
- **Unpleasant Feeling (*Dukkha-vedanā*)**: Physical knee pain, stiffness, or emotional sorrow. The instinct is resistance, blame, and fidgeting. Skillful practice breathes through discomfort with patient composure.
- **Neutral Feeling (*Adukkhamasukha-vedanā*)**: Sensation without strong valence. The instinct is to space out or look for entertainment. Skillful practice anchors sharp vigilance.

### 3. Freedom from the Hedonic Treadmill
By observing feeling tones as fleeting, conditioned, and not-self (*anattā*), we step off the exhausting rollercoaster of chasing pleasure and fleeing discomfort.

## 📜 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

I hate this cushion!'* When we separate the physical sensation from the emotional storytelling, the ache is just burning heat and pressure, completely bearable.
