### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Offered on the afternoon of September 11, 2008, Ajahn Pasanno leads an extensive guided meditation. Starting with somatic grounding, posture alignment, and softening facial muscles, he guides practitioners through breathing with kindness, establishing self-forgiveness, wishing peace to loved ones, and opening boundless goodwill toward difficult beings and the wider world.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Paced Somatic Relaxation**: Releasing tension from the crown of the head down through the shoulders, ribs, and abdomen.
- **The Breath as a Messenger of Goodwill**: Inhaling peace, exhaling ease; letting each breath dissolve emotional contractions.
- **Boundless Expansion**: Radiating loving-kindness outward like the warm rays of the morning sun.
