### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Establishing receptive, clear presence through somatic mindfulness, breath contemplation, and collectedness of mind.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Meditation is an experiential investigation of the present moment. In this teaching, Ajahn Pasanno invites the community to step back from habituated mental reactions, establish anchored bodily presence, and cultivate an attitude of honest, non-judgmental curiosity. By recognizing how hindrances and sensory impingements operate, the mind releases compulsive grasping and rests in sovereign clarity.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Ecology of the Practice**: Ajahn Pasanno methodically demonstrates that formal sitting and daily duties are not two separate realms. Right effort is not a violent struggle against conditions, but a calm, steady realignment of attention toward the wholesome.
- **Meeting the Edges of Discomfort**: When physical fatigue or emotional resistance enters the senses, the instruction is to soften somatic tension (such as a tight or knotted belly, clenched jaw, or restricted breath) and hold the raw sensation in patient, compassionate awareness without spinning off into secondary storylines (*papañca*).
