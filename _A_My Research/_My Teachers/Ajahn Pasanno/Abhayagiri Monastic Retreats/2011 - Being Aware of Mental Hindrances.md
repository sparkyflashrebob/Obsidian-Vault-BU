## 📖 Executive Summary & Core Teachings

Ajahn Pasanno reminds retreatants that the five hindrances do not magically appear only on the meditation cushion. In truth, they operate continuously throughout the day—especially around food, appetite, and waiting in line. He encourages practitioners to notice greed, impatience, and personal investment as they transition into the meal offering.

---

## 📜 Full Talk Transcript

**Ajahn Pasanno:**

This is an opportunity to pay attention to the five hindrances outside the formal sitting periods. 

It isn't as if the five hindrances just arise in meditation and aren't there any other time! Use the circumstance of the meal—looking at food, waiting in line, smelling aromas—to observe how desire, irritation, or restlessness arise. 

Notice what personal investment we make in these hindrances, and simply recognize: *"Ah, this is a hindrance."* Maintain that clear awareness.
