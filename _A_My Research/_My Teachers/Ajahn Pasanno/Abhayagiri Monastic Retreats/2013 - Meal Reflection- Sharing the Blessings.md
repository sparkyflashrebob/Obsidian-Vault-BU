### Canonical & Contemporary Setting
* **Canonical Setting**: Reflects the ancient Theravāda liturgy of merit dedication (pattidāna), where the wholesome energy generated through spiritual practice and generosity is intentionally directed toward parents, teachers, departed loved ones, and all sentient beings.

## 💡 Teachings Overview

### Central Contemplative Theme
**Dedication of Merit and Thanksgiving Intentionality**.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on Thanksgiving Day, Ajahn Pasanno reminds the assembly that the traditional meal blessing chant is an opportunity for dedicating merit. By bringing specific loved ones, parents, and benefactors to mind and wishing that the goodness of our retreat practice supports their well-being, Thanksgiving becomes a genuine spiritual act of boundless goodwill.

## 📜 Full Talk Transcript

**Ajahn Pasanno:** One of the ways to use the meal blessing chant is as an opportunity for a dedication of blessings—a dedication of merit.

We bring to mind various people, setting an intention: 'Through the goodness of these actions, with the generosity and kindness that has been done, may these good actions be a source of well-being for that particular person.'

Bringing them up in mind, setting that image in the mind, and setting that intention of well-wishing is particularly appropriate on Thanksgiving Day.
