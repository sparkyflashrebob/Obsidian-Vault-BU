## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. Daily Chanted Recollection of the Requisites
In the Thai Forest Tradition, monastics and retreatants chant the reflection on the four requisites (clothing, food, shelter, medicine) every morning. Ajahn Pasanno highlights the pivotal phrase: *'Wisely reflecting, I use this almsfood, not for fun, not for intoxication, not for weight-gain, not for personal adornment, but only for the maintenance and nourishment of this body.'*

### 2. Sustaining the Body for the Path
When food is consumed with this orientation, it transforms from a sensual indulgence into a sacred fuel. It frees the practitioner from digestive heaviness, gluttony, and remorse, allowing the physical vehicle to support the arduous mental cultivation of samādhi and paññā.
