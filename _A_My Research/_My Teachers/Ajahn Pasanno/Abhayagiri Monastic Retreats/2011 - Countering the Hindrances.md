## 📖 Executive Summary & Core Teachings

Ajahn Pasanno explains how the initial absorption factors (*jhānaṅga*) function as practical tools in everyday meditation:
* **Vitakka (Applied Thought / Lifting)**: The intentional act of lifting the attention and placing it firmly upon the meditation object (the physical sensation of the breath).
* **Vicāra (Sustained Evaluation / Exploration)**: Looking at the breath from different perspectives—examining where it is comfortable, how it affects tension in the chest or abdomen, and adjusting the posture.

By engaging *vitakka* and *vicāra*, the mind is occupied with wholesome exploration, leaving no room for sensual fantasies or restless irritation to take root. Group chanting similarly operates as a powerful method for unifying energy and dispelling sluggishness.

## 📜 Full Talk Transcript

**Ajahn Pasanno:**

Recognizing how to integrate different routines—such as devotional chanting and reflection—helps channel our energy into mindful attention. 

When working with the breath, we utilize two classical faculties: *vitakka* and *vicāra*. 

*Vitakka* is applied attention: taking the mind and placing it on the physical sensation of the breath. 
*Vicāra* is evaluation: examining the breath from different angles. How does the breath feel in the chest? Is there tension in the shoulders? Can the breath permeate the whole spine? 

When you actively investigate the breath with *vicāra*, you are not daydreaming or fighting distractions; you are filling the space of consciousness with wholesome discernment. 

This is how the hindrances are starved and concentration naturally deepens.
