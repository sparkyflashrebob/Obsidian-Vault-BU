## 💡 Teachings Overview

### Central Contemplative Theme
**The Complete Architectural Map: Unfolding Ānāpānasati Through the Four Tetrads**.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
In this masterwork 55-minute morning discourse, Ajahn Pasanno provides a comprehensive, practical roadmap through all sixteen steps of breath meditation. Moving beyond cerebral maps, he emphasizes experiential sensitivity (*paṭisaṃvedī*): feeling the breath permeate the whole bodily matrix, gladdening the heart with wholesome joy (*pīti*) and ease (*sukha*), steadying the mind (*samādahaṃ cittaṃ*), and culminating in the contemplation of impermanence, dispassion, cessation, and complete relinquishment (*paṭinissagga*).

## 📊 Doctrinal Comparison & Analytical Framework

| Tetrad | Contemplative Focus | Progression of Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I. Body (*Kāya*)** | Steps 1–4: Long, short, whole breath-body, calming formations. | Establishes somatic presence and soothes physical tension. |
| **II. Feelings (*Vedanā*)** | Steps 5–8: Experiencing rapture (*pīti*), pleasure (*sukha*), mental formations. | Infuses the heart with wholesome joy and tranquilizes emotions. |
| **III. Mind (*Citta*)** | Steps 9–12: Experiencing the mind, gladdening, steadying, releasing. | Purifies mental states and stabilizes luminous concentration. |
| **IV. Dhammas** | Steps 13–16: Contemplating inconstancy (*anicca*), dispassion, cessation, release. | Deep insight culminating in total relinquishment (*paṭinissagga*). |

## 📖 Stories & Anecdotes Offered

* **The Breath as an Experiential Friend**: Ajahn Pasanno warns against treating the breath as an abstract visual object or an intellectual exercise. He highlights that the Pāli word *paṭisaṃvedī* shares its root with *vedanā*—feeling, sensing, tasting. We must learn to 'taste' and feel the breath with warmth and intimacy, letting it soothe every layer of our being.
