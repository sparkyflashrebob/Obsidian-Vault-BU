### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Given on August 28, 2010, to visiting youth from the Spirit Rock Teen Group, Ajahn Pasanno provides an accessible, lively exploration of ethics and kindness. Virtue is not a set of rigid religious taboos, but a protective shield that grants fearlessness, self-respect, and clear conscience.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Sīla as Fearlessness**: Living ethically gives the extraordinary gift of safety to all beings. An ethical person walks through the world without guilt or paranoia.
- **The Mirror of Intention (MN 61)**: Reflecting before, during, and after an action: 'Does this deed harm myself, others, or both?'
- **Active Kindness in Daily Encounters**: How simple warmth, patience, and non-judgmental listening transform schools, families, and peer relationships.
