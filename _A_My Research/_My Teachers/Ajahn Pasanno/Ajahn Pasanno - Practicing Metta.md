### Theme of the Dharma Talk
Cultivating loving-kindness (*mettā*) as an essential psychological refuge and boundless container during times of collective uncertainty and fear.

### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Delivered on March 20, 2020, as the monastery entered lockdown at the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, Ajahn Pasanno offers crucial guidance on using loving-kindness to overcome isolation and panic. Mettā is not superficial sweetness, but a robust, courageous stance of goodwill that refuses to give rise to ill will toward anyone.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **Mettā in Times of Crisis**: How fear contracts the heart and causes humans to view others as threats. Mettā consciously expands awareness to include all beings in protection.
- **The 11 Canonical Benefits**: Sleeping peacefully, waking happily, seeing pleasant dreams, being dear to humans and non-humans, mind easily concentrated, and passing away unconfused.
- **Starting Close and Radiating Out**: Grounding friendliness in our own physical body and mind first, softening inner harshness, then radiating boundless goodwill in all directions.
