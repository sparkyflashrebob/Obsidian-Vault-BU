### Condensed Version of the Teachings
Offered during the 'Recollections of Ajahn Chah' daylong retreat at Spirit Rock on September 19, 2010, Ajahn Pasanno unpacks 'Subduing the Busybody'. Through practical examples and personal memories from training in northeast Thailand, he demonstrates how Ajahn Chah used everyday monastic discipline to turn consciousness back on itself.

### Expanded Version of the Teachings
- **The Context of Subduing the Busybody**: Exploring how internal attachment manifests as emotional friction, and how Ajahn Chah intervened with timely, disarming wisdom.
- **Embodied Non-Grasping**: Grounding awareness in the body and breath to weather periods of doubt, fatigue, or restless mental proliferation.
