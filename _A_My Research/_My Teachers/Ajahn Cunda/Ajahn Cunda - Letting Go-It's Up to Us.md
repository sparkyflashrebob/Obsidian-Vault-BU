## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ajahn Cunda.

* **Theme**: Personal spiritual responsibility (*attano kamma*); moving beyond dependence on charismatic teachers; reflections on traveling with senior elders (Luang Por Pasanno / Luang Por Sumedho); the meaning of Uposatha (Wan Phra); community gratitude and self-reliance.

* **Circumstances**: Delivered during the 2023 Rains Retreat (Vassa) on an Uposatha evening at Abhayagiri, reflecting upon monastic training and the transition from reliance on mentors to direct internal mastery.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Cunda reflects on the special cadence of the three-month monastic Rains Retreat, where Saturday and Wan Phra (lunar observance day) coincide. Reflecting on his experiences accompanying senior teachers on international journeys, he shares a transformative realization: while having a wise mentor is an immense blessing, a great teacher will ultimately step back, letting the student know: *'You don't need my help anymore; you know what to do.'* Spiritual progress cannot be subcontracted. The Buddha famously declared in Dhammapada verse 276: *'You yourself must make the effort; the Tathāgatas are only teachers.'* True letting go happens in the quiet intimacy of one's own awareness. The talk concludes with a profound expression of gratitude to the Sangha and the lay supporters who make the holy life possible.
