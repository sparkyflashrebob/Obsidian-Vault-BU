# 🏛️ Ajahn Cunda — Master Dharma Talks Index & Compendium
### *Senior Monastic Teacher at Abhayagiri Buddhist Monastery*

> *"The Dhamma invites us to go against the grain of habitual worldly grasping. While the Buddha and our teachers can point the way with infinite compassion, the actual work of letting go is entirely up to us. When we embrace that responsibility with humility and dedication, the unconditioned peace of freedom is realized right here."* — **Ajahn Cunda**
