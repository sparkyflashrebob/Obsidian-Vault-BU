# Dependent Origination: Seeing the Dharma
**Teacher**: Gary Buck  
**Year**: 2015  
**Subject**: *Paṭiccasamuppāda* (Dependent Co-arising / Conditionality) in Meditative Inquiry and Daily Experience

---

## Course Overview

This 8-week course by Gary Buck offers a granular, experiential inquiry into the twelve links of Dependent Origination (*Paṭiccasamuppāda*). Rather than treating conditionality solely as an abstract metaphysical or philosophical theory, the curriculum systematically guides practitioners to observe the moment-to-moment arising and cessation of experience across sensory contact, feeling tone (*vedanā*), craving (*taṇhā*), clinging (*upādāna*), and the psychological construction of identity (*bhava* and *jāti*).
