## 📖 Expanded Version of the Teachings

### 1. The Bodily Texture of Ill Will vs. Mettā
Aversion, impatience, and irritation are somatic contractions: the stomach knots up, the throat tightens, the heart feels shielded and armored. Mettā is the intentional biological unwinding of this defensive armoring.

### 2. The Fourfold Diamond Architecture
- **Mettā (Good Will)**: The heartfelt wish: *'May you be happy and well.'* Meeting the world with unconditional friendliness.
- **Karuṇā (Compassion)**: The heart trembling in response to pain: *'May you be free from suffering.'* Refusing to turn away from difficulty.
- **Muditā (Sympathetic Joy)**: Celebrating the success and goodness of others: *'May your happiness continue.'* The ultimate antidote to envy.
- **Upekkhā (Equanimity)**: The unshakeable mountain: *'All beings are heirs to their own kamma.'* Compassion guided by wisdom.

### 3. Bringing the Heart Home
We cannot authentically love others if our internal relationship to our own body and mind is harsh or critical. Ajahn Mettā guides retreatants to make the present moment a home of warmth and forgiveness.
