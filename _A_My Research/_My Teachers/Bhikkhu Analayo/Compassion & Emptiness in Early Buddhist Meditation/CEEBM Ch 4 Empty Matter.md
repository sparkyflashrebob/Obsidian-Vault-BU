#### The empty nature of the six senses 
> The eye is empty; it is empty of being permanent, of being perpetual, and of having an unchanging nature, and it is empty of what belongs to a self. Why is that? That is its intrinsic nature. Forms ... eye-consciousness ... eye-contact ... feeling arisen in dependence on eye-contact that is painful, pleasant, or neutral, is also empty; it is empty of being permanent, of being perpetual, and of having an unchanging nature, and it is empty of what belongs to a self. Why is that? That is its intrinsic nature. The ear ... the nose ... the tongue ... the body ... the mind are also like that. This is [the implication] of the saying: “The world is empty.”3

### EARTH

Here cautioning one  wishes to dwell in emptiness 
	to not give attention to the perception of people 
	not give attention to the forest
	should give the unitary perception of earth

Further clarifying:
	if seeing earth as having hills and hollows, with animals and bushes,....things in it
	one should not attend to it so
	if instead on see this earth as level and flat like the palm of the hand, then one's manner of looking at it is beneficial and should be frequently attended to.

How one looks at things
Calls this non-emptiness: just the unitary perception of earth
	As a result one is not weary because of the perception of people (and I would assume because of proliferation) 
	same for the forest (not weary)
	only weariness because of this unitary perception of earth

Whatever is not present, one sees as empty
whatever else is present, one sees as truely present
	further clarifying what it mean to truely dwelling in emptiness, without distorion

Gradual entry into emptiness requires leaving behind earlier perceptions
having emptied one's perception of these objects one give attention to the next step
which in this case is perception of the earth element

The point is that we arrive at unitary perception
	disregarding all irregularities
	only perceive the basic notion of earth

The idea from here on out that the modes of perceiving being cultivated involves some degree of abstraction

Previously the instructions were to just direct attention to what would be visible.
This was refined so that one viewed the earth with no distinct qualities

...only weariness because of the unitary percpetion of the sphere of infinite space

truly dwelling in emptiness, without distortion

abandoning the notion of solidity manifest in any matter
leaving behind of all that is characteristic of matter

1st of the four immaterial attainments
	transcendence of all that is material

> Completely transcending perceptions of form, with the cessation of perceptions of resistance, not attending to perceptions of diversity, [being instead aware] of infinite space, one dwells having attained the sphere of infinite space.

Analayo. Compassion and Emptiness in Early Buddhist Meditation . Windhorse Publications Ltd. Kindle Edition. 

All perception of form are left behind
Matter no longer matters

This is a step in training perception
refining and deconstructing ordinary perception up to the point where meditation can culminate in the experience of Nirvana
supreme emptiness is attained
The issue at stake is to reveal the empty nature of matter, not proclaim its non-extence

> For one who rightly knows and sees as it really is the arising of the world, there will not be [the notion] that the world does not exist; for one who rightly knows and sees as it really is the cessation of the world, there will not be [the notion] that the world exists.

Analayo. Compassion and Emptiness in Early Buddhist Meditation . Windhorse Publications Ltd. Kindle Edition. 

The point here is 
that for one who sees the world that is so.
for one who sees cessation of the world then the world does not exist

Instead of falling prey to these two extremes
there is the middle way
recognizing the dependently arisen nature of things
