*Cittaviveka (Chithurst Buddhist Monastery) & Abhayagiri Audio Collections*

---

## 1. Teacher Profile & Teaching Scope

Ven. Ajahn Sucitto is one of the most senior Western disciples of Ven. Ajahn Chah. Ordained in Thailand in 1976, he served as the abbot of Cittaviveka (Chithurst Buddhist Monastery) in the UK from 1992 to 2014. Renowned for his exquisite integration of somatic embodiment, subtle energetic sensitivity (*prāṇa / vāyo*), and canonical Early Buddhist psychology, his teachings explore how liberation is realized directly through the lived, felt body.
