## Overview 

 In the meeting titled "Vedantic Self and Buddhist Non-Self," Swami Sarvapriyananda explored the contrasting concepts of self in Vedanta and Buddhism, particularly focusing on the notion of Atman (the eternal self) in Hindu philosophy versus the Buddhist idea of Anatman (no self). Through a detailed analysis, he presented the Seven Point Reasoning of Chandrakirti, a prominent Buddhist philosopher, which critiques the existence of an independent self. The discussion delved into the philosophical debates between Hindu and Buddhist schools, examining how both traditions articulate their understanding of reality, suffering, and liberation. Swami Sarvapriyananda concluded by reflecting on the deeper connections between these philosophies, suggesting that both ultimately point towards a non-dual understanding of existence.
