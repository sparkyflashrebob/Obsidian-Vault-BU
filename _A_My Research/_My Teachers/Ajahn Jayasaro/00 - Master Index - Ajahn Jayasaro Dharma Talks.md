# 🏛️ Ajahn Jayasaro — Master Dharma Talks Index & Compendium
### *Renowned Scholar, Biographer of Ajahn Chah (*Stillness Flowing*), and Senior Forest Monastic*

> *"Meditation is learning how to learn. When unbroken mindfulness stabilizes into samādhi, the knee-jerk reactions of greed and aversion are stilled. In that optimum learning capacity, the mind directly sees that thoughts, feelings, and sensations arise and pass away with no owner to be found. In that seeing, suffering dissolves."* — **Ajahn Jayasaro**

## 📜 Master Mettā Compendium Entries Harvested

During this comprehensive processing of Ajahn Jayasaro's audio archive, the following master dedication and mettā phrases were captured for **Phase 4: Master Mettā Phrases Compendium**:

1. **Faith [53:38–54:38] — Radiant Conviction & Inner Refuge**:

   > *"May our hearts be filled with unwavering faith in the Triple Gem, in our own capacity to awaken, and in the healing power of wholesome action. Abiding as our own true refuge, may all beings be blessed with clarity, courage, and lasting peace."*

2. **Gladden Your Own Mind [55:06–56:09] — Joyful Resilience & Communal Blessing**:

   > *"May we meet our practice with joyful patience, effort, and radiant appreciation. May the fruits of our wholesome deeds bring peace and happiness to ourselves, our families, and all communities, supporting the welfare, safety, and awakening of all beings."*

3. **Learning to Learn [71:32–72:55] — Optimum Clarity & Unowned Liberation**:

   > *"May our minds dwell in the pristine clarity of unbroken mindfulness and deep stillness. Free from the blindness of reactive grasping, may we see all conditioned things as unowned and impermanent, abiding in peace, wisdom, and liberation for all beings."*
