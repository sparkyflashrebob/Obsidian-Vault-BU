# 🏛️ Ven. Ajahn Brahm — Master Dharma Talks Index & Compendium
### *Abbot of Bodhinyana Monastery & Spiritual Director of the Buddhist Society of Western Australia*

> *"Buddhism is not a set of beliefs to defend; it is the art of letting go. When you make peace with the present moment, when you open the door of your heart to whatever arises—whether pain, grief, or joyous still absorption—you discover that there never was anything lacking in the unconditioned ground of freedom."* — **Ven. Ajahn Brahm**

## 📜 Master Mettā Compendium Entries Harvested

During this comprehensive processing of Ajahn Brahm's audio archive, the following master dedication and mettā phrases were captured for **Phase 4: Master Mettā Phrases Compendium**:

1. **The Dangers of Complaining [66:51–68:38] — Heart of Appreciation & Goodness**:

   > *"May we look upon ourselves, our loved ones, and all beings with eyes of appreciation and forgiving kindness. Free from the poison of fault-finding, may our hearts celebrate the 998 good bricks in every life, dwelling in harmony, peace, and boundless goodwill."*

2. **How to Deal with Pain [56:44–58:05] — Compassionate Embrace of Pain & Healing**:

   > *"May we meet all bodily suffering and illness with open-hearted kindness, fearlessness, and deep peace. Free from aversion, may our hearts be a sanctuary of comfort and healing for ourselves and all living beings."*

3. **Ending of All Life's Problems Pt 2 [60:35–61:43] — Universal Forgiveness Liturgy & Sādhu Dedication**:

   > *"If we have hurt someone today by word or deed, may we be more mindful the next time. May we forgive ourselves. Whatever wrong someone may do to us, may we be compassionate and forgive, and bear no hatred in our hearts. Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu! May all beings be well, happy, and peaceful."*

4. **The Four Jhānas [68:54–72:59] — Radiant Stillness & Unshakeable Peace**:

   > *"May our minds find their way into the deep, radiant stillness of samādhi. Dropping all burdens of doing and grasping, may we abide in the joyful peace of the unconditioned, and may all beings be blessed with tranquility, clarity, and liberation."*

5. **Non-Self [55:10–57:21] — Lightness of Not-Self & Universal Release**:

   > *"May we lay down the heavy burden of self-ownership, resting in the unentangled peace of anattā. Free from conceit and fear, may all beings experience the boundless joy of an unowned heart, living in harmony and freedom."*
