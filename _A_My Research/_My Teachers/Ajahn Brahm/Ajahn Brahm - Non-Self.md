## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ven. Ajahn Brahm.

* **Theme**: The radical teaching of *Anattā* (Not-Self); dismantling the illusion of the 'controller' or internal soul; the five aggregates (*khandhas*) as unowned nature; the driverless bus metaphor; liberation through relinquishing ownership.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Brahm tackles the most misunderstood and profound teaching in Buddhism: *Anattā*. The delusion of self (*sakkāya-diṭṭhi*) operates as an assumed central agent ('the controller') who owns thoughts, commands the body, and suffers emotions. Ajahn Brahm dismantles this illusion by analyzing human decision-making. Every choice—whether ordering food or choosing clothes—is an emergent result of causes, conditions, biological instincts, cultural conditioning, and sensory impressions. Just like a modern automated train or driverless bus, the vehicle moves smoothly without an internal driver. When we realize the body and mind are conditioned phenomena (*anattā*), we stop taking aging, sickness, and emotional weather personally. Realizing not-self is not frightening annihilation; it is the ultimate relief of dropping a 50-pound backpack.
