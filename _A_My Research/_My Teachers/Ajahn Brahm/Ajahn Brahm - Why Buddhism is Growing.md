# 🎙️ Ajahn Brahm - Why Buddhism is Growing
### *Ven. Ajahn Brahm — Dhammaloka Buddhist Centre, Perth (June 22, 2001)*

> *"Why is Buddhism growing so rapidly in the modern world? Because it does not demand that you believe anything on blind faith, it threatens no one with eternal damnation, and it never started a religious war. It offers practical tools for peace of mind right now. As the old saying goes: 'It is easy enough to be pleasant when life goes along like a song, but the one worthwhile is the one who can smile when everything goes dead wrong.' Buddhism gives you the art of smiling in the storm."* — **Ajahn Brahm [28:04 / 45:10 / 56:30]**
