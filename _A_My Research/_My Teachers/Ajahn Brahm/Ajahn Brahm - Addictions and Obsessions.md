### *Ven. Ajahn Brahm — Dhammaloka Buddhist Centre, Perth (June 27, 2003)*

## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ven. Ajahn Brahm.

* **Theme**: The root psychology of addictions and obsessive behavioral patterns; craving as an attempt to escape emotional emptiness; healing addiction through self-forgiveness and meditative joy.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Brahm examines the spectrum of addiction—from substance abuse (alcohol, narcotics, cigarettes) to behavioral addictions (gambling, workaholism, shopping) and psychological obsessions (compulsive thinking, worry, control). Addiction is driven by the fundamental inability to be at peace with oneself in the present moment. When emotional pain, boredom, or self-loathing arises, the mind frantically reaches for an external stimulant or distraction to mask the void. Ajahn Brahm reveals that willpower alone rarely sustains long-term recovery; what truly heals addiction is discovering a higher, unblemished source of inner joy (*niramisa sukha*) through meditation, coupled with radical self-forgiveness.
