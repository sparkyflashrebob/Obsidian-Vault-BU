# 🎙️ Ajahn Brahm - Dealing With Pain During Meditation
### *Ven. Ajahn Brahm — Dhammaloka Buddhist Centre, Perth*

> *"When physical pain or discomfort arises during meditation, can we just let it be? The moment we fight it, or worry that coughing or moving will disturb other people, we add anxiety and resistance on top of the physical sensation. Opening the heart and letting pain be is the first step toward true relief."* — **Ajahn Brahm [00:00–01:41]**

## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ven. Ajahn Brahm.

* **Theme**: Working skillfully with physical discomfort during meditation; ceasing social anxiety about coughing or moving; the power of radical acceptance.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Brahm addresses the common predicament of dealing with physical pain or the urge to cough during group sits. Retreatants often panic, thinking: *'Oh, I better not cough because it will disturb people!'* This mental anxiety creates immense somatic tension. By learning to soften, breathe, and simply let physical sensations be, the conflict dissolves.


---

## 💬 Impactful Quotes with Exact Timestamps

> *"I've seen this so often in meditation retreats—people think, 'Oh, I better not cough, it will disturb everyone,' and they choke themselves with tension! Just relax, let sensations enter, and let things be."* — **[01:41]**

## 🌿 Contemplative Reflection & Everyday Integration

Notice when discomfort arises during sitting. Instead of immediately bracing or generating anxiety, take a conscious breath. If our stomach is tight or knotted up, invite it to soften. Meeting sensation with ease prevents the Second Arrow from striking.
