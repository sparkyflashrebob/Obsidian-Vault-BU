## 🧭 Structured Contextual Metadata

* **Speaker**: Ven. Ajahn Brahm (Ajahn Brahmavamso).

* **Theme**: The illusion that happiness lies in changing outer circumstances; the universality of *dukkha* across all life roles; contentment with the present moment.

* **Condensed Version of the Teachings**: Ajahn Brahm playfully deconstructs the restlessness of the human condition. When people are single, they suffer from loneliness; when they marry, they suffer from relationship friction; when they divorce, they suffer from loneliness again; and if they ordain as monastics, they simply experience 'monk suffering.' The suffering does not originate in the status of being married, single, or monastic—it originates in the mind's chronic craving to be somewhere else. Peace is realized when we cease running away from our present circumstances and make peace with this moment.

## 🌿 Contemplative Reflection & Everyday Integration

Notice when the mind whispers: *'If only my situation were different—if only I had another job, another house, or more quiet—then I would finally be at peace.'* Recognize this as the classic 'somewhere else syndrome.' If our stomach is tight or knotted up, we invite it to soften. We can remind ourselves: peace is not at the next destination; peace is the stillness of this very step.
