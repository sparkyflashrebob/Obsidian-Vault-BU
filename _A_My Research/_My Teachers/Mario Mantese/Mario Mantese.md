The sources discuss Mario Mantese, often referred to as Master M, as a Universal Master who guides others on a spiritual journey dedicated to **liberation**. This path is characterized by uncompromising clarity, ego dissolution, and the direct realization of one's boundless, eternal nature.

### The Identity of the Universal Master

Mario Mantese is presented as a spiritual figure whose status as a Master stems from a profound personal transformation.

1.  **Awakening through Death:** In 1978, Mantese, a successful musician, was stabbed in the heart, resulting in clinical death followed by a five-week coma. During this intense "death" experience, he recognized that his true being—the person he knew as Mario Mantese—continued to live without the physical body. He realized that his existence is timeless, deathless, and limitless. This realization was a "true resurrection in divine light," where his personality was completely swallowed by the Infinite, and he became that which had swallowed him. Zen Master Shodo Harada Roshi described this ordeal as having the "good fortune to receive a completely new life" and realizing *satori*, or pure consciousness.
2.  **Nature of the Master:** Mantese is referred to as Master M and is viewed as standing "in line with Ramana Maharshi, Babaji, Yogananda, and perhaps other masters". He is described as a **cosmic master** and a **highly developed spiritual being**. While his physical body showed the effects of his accident (wobbly walk, sometimes unclear speech), observers noted he radiated serenity, contentment, and "tremendous light". Mantese himself states, **"I am nothing and no one"**.
3.  **Unbound and Multidimensional Being:** The Master's reality is described as **timeless, formless, without space, and imperceptible to the senses**. He embodies the **Totality** and possesses "multidimensional awareness". His existence transcends dualities: he is absent yet present, and his body can appear transparent or as vibrating light. He is seen as the "clear eye of creation" and "pure love itself".

### The Path to Liberation

Mario Mantese's teachings focus on the realization that the individual is already the absolute reality and that liberation involves dissolving the illusion of the separate ego.

1.  **The Goal: Not-I and Totality:** The ultimate objective is liberation, often referred to as the **"Path of Fire"**. This involves the **awakening, transformation, and inner resurrection of the Divine Being**. Mantese teaches that the "true human being is unbound and limitless". Liberation is achieved by discovering that the seeker already **is** that which is sought—the Totality, which is always attained. The true self is "unformed, unborn, uncreated, and unbecoming".
2.  **The Illusion of the Individual:** The core of suffering and bondage is the mistaken identification with the body and the mind, creating the illusory concept of an "I" or individual personality. Mantese maintains that the individual practitioner, the striver, is itself an illusion. He advises realizing that "the body is always sick, and in fact that is the only sickness". True being is deathless, and one should "die before your physical death" to clearly recognize what actually dies.
3.  **Not Striving, But Seeing:** Mantese rejects traditional paths, methods, practices, and techniques, calling them "delusive, doomed to betray those who follow them". These practices are bound to time and space and originate from the ego. He famously asks, **"I ask myself, does God have to do practices to be God?”**. Instead of striving, one should question where the will to achieve comes from. The true approach is **intuitive, direct seeing**. This profound clarity immediately dissolves misunderstandings and delusions, akin to turning on a light in a dark room.
4.  **Not-Doing (Non-Activity):** The wisdom of the path centers on Not-Being and Not-Acting. **True order only comes through Not-Doing**, which is not indifference, but an "enormous divine intensity". The awakened one remains silent and acts through non-action, allowing "everything that is temporal and entrapping [to be] dissolved".

### The Master’s Function in Transformation

Mantese's teaching is largely transmitted through his energetic presence and the unique power of his words, which facilitate purification and awakening for those ready to receive it.

1.  **Energy and Light-Force:** Mantese's presence is characterized by a radiating light-energy that is "uplifting, penetrating, and immensely powerful". This energy acts as a **powerful universal force** that releases and dissolves old obstacles and hindrances. When he initiated the **"Inner Circle"**—a deeper work of liberation—the force was so intense it was described as the "energy and intensity of the universe," causing core inner shifts, such as the instantaneous extinguishing of a smoking addiction.
2.  **Words as Deeds (Transformation):** Mario's words function as the "expression and activity of the universe". His words are described as "round and clear, born of essence and filled with essence". They are so potent that they are considered "deeds", setting things in motion and transforming them. He can provide a clear and profound answer to an unstated question simply by glancing at someone and adding an extra sentence to his current topic.
3.  **The Uncompromising Approach:** Mantese uses "uncompromising, ego-shattering methods". He focuses on bringing students out of self-pity and self-absorption, often emphasizing that **true spirituality must manifest in the practical matters of everyday life**. He encourages being **"normal"** and loving, stating, "To be normal is not something tedious or boring, but rather something extraordinarily profound". He takes away concepts and forces people to confront their inner workings directly.
4.  **Miracles and Healing:** Although Mantese performs healings and displays astonishing abilities (such as knowing events far away or affecting the weather), he maintains that this is **not the "real work"**. He says, "Things happen because they happen, not because someone wants them to happen". Healing is a natural outcome when individuals gain clarity and experience the light of presence. His primary function is the path of liberation and clarity.

***

The path to liberation offered by Mario Mantese is not a journey *to* a distant spiritual destination, but rather an internal transformation achieved through profound self-recognition. It is like realizing that the map you were consulting, the steps you were trying to take, and the goal you sought were all reflections of the same room you were standing in—and the Master’s role is simply the brilliant light that illuminates the room so clearly that the shadows and confusion dissolve.
