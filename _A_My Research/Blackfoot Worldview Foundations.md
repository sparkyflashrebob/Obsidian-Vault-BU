# **Blackfoot Worldview Foundations**

Western thought is based on matter that can be perceived through five senses and views the world as static and unchanging

Einstein's phrase "God does not play dice with the universe" exemplifies the Western belief in a fixed, predetermined reality

Western thinking uses a funnel approach that narrows from broad perspectives to singular points, leading to beliefs in one right answer and one true way

Blackfoot worldview centers on energy forces rather than matter, with everything in constant motion and transformation

Energy waves exist everywhere and humans act like radios filtering these waves rather than containing information within themselves

Everything in the Blackfoot world is animate including rocks, trees, sky, and clouds, whereas Western thought only considers humans and some animals as animate.

Dr. Leroy Little Bear, Indigenous Ways of Knowing and Western Science
