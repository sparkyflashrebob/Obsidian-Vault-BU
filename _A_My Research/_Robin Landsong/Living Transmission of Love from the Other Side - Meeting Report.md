#### Session Structure Overview

- The session was outlined to consist of four parts: an opening movement and stretching segment, a slideshow presentation, a 30-minute transmission meditation, and a closing group sharing period.
- Robin explained that the meditation would not be guided, meaning no verbal instruction would be provided during the 30-minute period, as she would be dropping into a meditative state herself.
- During the meditation, Robin described her role as energetically connecting with beings encountered during her near-death experience and transmitting that energy to participants.
- All experience levels were welcomed, from complete beginners to those with decades of meditation practice, with the expectation that repeated attendance would deepen the experience over time.

#### Movement and Breathwork Preparation

- Shoulder rolls combined with intentional breathing were introduced as a method to reduce stress and support nervous system regulation before entering the meditation.
- Participants were guided through spleen-opening side body stretches, explained through a Chinese medicine lens as a way to reduce excessive or looping thinking.
- A breathing technique focused on a full exhale followed by a natural inhale was taught as one of the most accessible and effective tools for nervous system regulation.
- Full diaphragmatic inhales were also practiced, with Robin noting that deep breathing can surface authentic emotion by shifting awareness out of the analytical mind and into the body.
- The overall purpose of the movement and breathwork segment was described as reducing the initial layers of stress so participants could enter the meditation without needing to work through tension first.

#### Slideshow on Self-Compassion and Meditation Preparation

- Self-compassion was presented as the foundation for discovering personal purpose and meaning, with the suggestion that reducing self-criticism opens the path to a more fulfilling life.
- Turning toward difficult emotions and sensations rather than avoiding them was identified as a key principle from Robin's Stanford compassion training and a core element of developing self-compassion.
- Belly breathing was described as a tool that shifts attention from thought into body, activates the parasympathetic nervous system, builds presence, and reconnects individuals to a sense of safety and life force.
- The phrase "this too belongs" was introduced as a compassionate internal response to any emotion, sensation, or experience that arises during the meditation.
- Healing in community was highlighted as an antidote to loneliness, with the group setting presented as an intentional container for shared experience.
- Robin's book "Loving Bravely" and her individual and five-person group sessions were briefly introduced as available offerings for those wishing to continue working with her.
- The Veterans Healing Trauma through Singing Medicine film was described as a deeply personal milestone representing a long-term vision to create healing films for diverse groups including children and various communities.

#### Meditation Instructions and Participant Guidance

- Participants were encouraged to find a comfortable position, lie down if preferred, and use earplugs to deepen their inward focus during the 30-minute meditation.
- Robin described her process of energetically gathering all participants at the start of the meditation before moving into the transmission, comparing it to a teacher counting students before a field trip.
- A request was made for participants with a strong sense of grounding and secure housing to contribute extra stabilizing energy to the group, as Robin was in the middle of a housing transition.
- Participants were advised to gently focus on any unmet physical, emotional, spiritual, or ancestral needs before the meditation began, as this intention could help draw in the healing beings being called upon.
- Robin clarified that the beings she calls in during the meditation are rooted in love, compassion, devotion, and healing, and that participants should maintain their own spiritual boundaries as appropriate to their values.
- A grounding technique called counting corners was offered as a practical tool for anyone who became overwhelmed during the meditation, involving opening the eyes and counting the corners of the room to return to the present moment.

#### Transmission Meditation Session

- A 30-minute silent transmission meditation was conducted, during which Robin dropped into connection with beings from her near-death experience to transmit healing energy to the group.
- Robin returned the group from the meditation using singing as an auditory signal to gently bring participants back to waking awareness.

#### Robin's Post-Meditation Reflection

- Robin shared that her experience during the meditation centered strongly on themes of confidence, personal power, and releasing patterns of self-doubt and second-guessing.
- A sense of embodiment in one's birthright as a healing presence for oneself and others was described as a central transmission received during the session.
- Robin described a felt presence she identified as the Grandmother of All, a figure from her near-death experience associated with the final veil between the living and the other side, who appeared to sing to the group.
- Shiva was described as a consistent presence throughout the meditation, repeatedly affirming the group's collective power and dissolving patterns of diminished self-expression.
- A strong physical organ-level healing was sensed toward the end of the meditation for at least one participant, prompting Robin to remain in the meditation longer than planned.
- An image of small men's pants arose as a symbolic representation of a diminished and colonialized concept of masculine power, which Shiva was described as discarding in favor of a model of power-with rather than power-over.
- The shift from power-over to power-with and power-within was framed as a significant cultural transition currently affecting masculine identity in Western society.

### Spiritual and Societal Insights

**The Transmission Meditation Model**

- Robin's meditation is not a guided meditation but a transmission — she drops into the beings from her near-death experience and transmits their presence to the group
- The beings she calls in are described as oriented entirely toward love, devotion, healing, kindness, compassion, and supporting authentic selfhood
- Robin acknowledged that some participants may have religious concerns about the beings she calls in and encouraged each person to act in alignment with their own values and spiritual boundaries
- She noted that across thousands of people she has brought these beings to, she has only received beneficial responses

**Collective Healing and Shared Vision**

- The dragon eye appearing independently to Patricia, Jazzy, and Joan during the same meditation was presented as evidence of collective wisdom and shared healing when people gather in this way
- Robin described the group as a community that meets monthly and deepens over time, encouraging new participants to return

**Surrender and Non-Grasping**

- Gisella shared that saying yes to her autoimmune illness — including saying yes to death — led to the resolution of many of her symptoms
- Robin affirmed that the non-grasping surrender state is the path toward healing more effectively than grasping for outcomes
- Robin reflected that fully surrendering before the meditation placed her in a much more peaceful state than trying to maintain control

**Masculine Identity and Cultural Shift**

- Robin described the masculine as being in crisis in Western culture due to a foundational shift away from "power over"
- The image of pants too small for any adult was used to represent the diminished, colonialized construct of masculine power
- Shiva's gesture of tossing the pants aside was described as symbolic of composting the old model and entering an era of power with, power within, and power with one another
- Robin suggested planting flowers on the grave of "power over" as a cultural metaphor

**Authenticity and Emotional Presence**

- Robin described a client who was speaking rapidly and not breathing, staying in her head to avoid emotion; when Robin slowed her down and introduced breath, the client began to cry and access authentic feeling
- Robin framed this as the necessity of increasing our own capacity to be with our authentic selves as the foundation for being with the authenticity of others

**Humor and Lightness in Healing**

- Gisella introduced the concept of bringing humor to everything that arises in life — smiling with difficulties rather than resisting them
- Robin affirmed this as a meaningful part of the healing orientation

**Singing Medicine as a Language of the Land**

- When asked what language she sings in, Robin explained it is not a specific African or other named language but her own language, arrived at by listening to the land where she is — Northern California
- She described it as influenced by but not derived from any particular tradition
