The video references a "worksheet" in the context of Byron Katie's method called "The Work." While the video itself doesn't explicitly show the worksheet, based on information about "The Work," it typically includes sections to:

Identify a stressful situation and the thought you have about it.

Inquire into that thought using four questions:

Is it true?

Can you absolutely know that it's true?

How do you react, what happens, when you believe that thought?

Who would you be without1 that thought?

Turn the thought around to find the opposite and see if that turnaround is as true or truer.

So, the worksheet guides you through a process of identifying a stressful thought and then questioning its validity and the impact it has on your life.
