i spent years getting better at sound design and it actually made my tracks worse not because the sounds were bad they were actually really good but I was focusing on the wrong thing on paper everything made sense the sounds were big the presets were solid the layers should have worked and yet my mix always sounded muddy crowded smaller than it should be and this is where most producers get stuck because you think the problem is the sound itself but the issue it's how they're working together sound design doesn't happen in isolation and once you understand this the goal stops being can I make this amazing sound and it becomes how is this sound different from what I already have because when sounds are too similar they don't fit they fight nothing's actually broken here they're just doing the same job when you layer sounds with similar characteristics same octave same tone same width they don't add together they compete this would be like five people talking on top of each other in a room at the same time nobody gets louder it just gets chaotic so what works better here is going to be forcing contrast between your layers instead of asking "Does this sound cool by itself?" The better question is how is this sound different from what's already there let me show you what I mean same sound same notes they just stopped competing now here's something important even when the nature of the sound what it sounds like again nothing is wrong here but they're similar in character they're trying to do the same thing when you stack sounds that have the same tambber same vibe
