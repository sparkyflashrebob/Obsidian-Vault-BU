Permaculture Design Principles

Thinking tools, when used together, allow us to creatively re-design our environment and our behaviour in a world of less energy and resources

The foundations of permaculture are the

https://permacultureprinciples.com/ethics/

(centre) which guide the use of the 12 design principles, ensuring that they are used in appropriate ways.

These principles are seen as universal, although the methods used to express them will vary greatly according to the place and situation. They are applicable to our personal, economic, social and political reorganisation as illustrated in the

permaculture flower

https://permacultureprinciples.com/flower/

Each principle can be thought of as a door that opens into whole systems thinking, providing a different perspective that can be understood at varying levels of depth and application.
