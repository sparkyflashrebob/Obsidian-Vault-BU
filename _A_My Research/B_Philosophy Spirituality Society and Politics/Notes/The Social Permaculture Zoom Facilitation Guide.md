Here is a structured agenda and a selection of ice-breakers designed to build trust and connection in your very first Zoom meeting. Because your goal is to lay the foundation for a cooperative culture based on Social Permaculture, this agenda prioritizes authentic human connection and emotional safety over logistics [1, 2].

### Proposed Zoom Meeting Agenda (60-90 Minutes)

**1. Welcome & Mindful Grounding (5-10 minutes)**

* Start by welcoming everyone and acknowledging that they took a leap of faith to join a new group.
* Guide the participants through a brief moment of silence or mindful breathing. This gives people a chance to transition from the stress of their daily lives and arrive fully present, immediately reinforcing your community's commitment to "Inner Transition" and emotional well-being [3, 4].

**2. Establishing Communication Agreements (10 minutes)**

* To create an environment where everyone feels "safe enough" to open up, introduce a few core communication agreements right from the start [5, 6].
* You can display these clearly on your screen:
  + **Mindful Listening:** Ask participants to listen with their whole selves, rather than mentally planning what they are going to say next [7].
  + **Move Up / Move Back:** Encourage those who tend to speak frequently to "move back" and listen, while warmly inviting quieter voices to "move up" and share [3].
  + **The Right to Pass:** Ensure everyone knows it is perfectly acceptable to simply say "I pass" if they prefer to just observe [7].

**3. Small Group Ice-Breakers (15-20 minutes)**

* Large Zoom galleries can be intimidating and hinder vulnerability. Use Zoom breakout rooms to divide participants into smaller, more intimate groups of four to six people [8].
* Give them one of these specific, connection-building ice-breakers to discuss:
  + **"No Place Like Home":** Ask each person to describe a specific dish or kitchen smell that reminds them of home. Ask the listeners to follow up with genuine questions about the memories associated with that dish [9].
  + **"Defining Moments":** Have participants take a few minutes to think of one or two defining moments in their life, and then take turns sharing those stories with their small group [10, 11].
  + **"Hidden Talents":** Ask each person to share a couple of things they know how to do that most people don't, while the rest of the group asks curious follow-up questions [9].

**4. Full Group Heart Sharing (20-30 minutes)**

* Bring everyone back to the main room. Open the floor for your very first "Heart Sharing" circle [12].
* Keep the focus away from business or logistics. Instead, ask a single guiding question related to the YouTube videos that brought them there, such as: *"What is one thing you are seeking in a community that you feel is currently missing in your life?"*
* Remind participants to practice "Self-Focus" by using "I" statements and speaking directly from their own personal experiences [13].

**5. Closing & Next Steps (5 minutes)**

* Thank everyone for their vulnerability and for taking the time to connect.
* Clearly state when the next virtual meeting will be and how they can stay in touch between meetings (e.g., joining an email list or a private forum).
