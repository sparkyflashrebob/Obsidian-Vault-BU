To attract the right people to your new community, your first YouTube videos should act as a beacon for your foundational values. Because you want to use the "chunking" method—sharing your vision in short, manageable pieces rather than overwhelming your audience all at once—you can break your message down into several focused videos.

Here are the specific core values to highlight and how you can translate them into engaging topics for your first few videos:

### Core Values to Broadcast

These are the foundational principles of Social Permaculture and community building that will resonate with the people you want to attract:

* **The "Partnership" Way over the "Dominator" Way:** Emphasize that your community is moving away from competition and power-over dynamics, and instead values cooperation, interconnectedness, compassion, mutual support, and kindness [1].
* **Inner Transition:** Highlight the Transition Towns principle that we cannot change our outer world without first transforming our inner attitudes, beliefs, and fears [2].
* **The "Beloved Community":** Share the vision of building a diverse, radically inclusive space based on love and justice, recognizing that we are all tied together in an "inescapable network of mutuality" [3].
* **Earth Care, People Care, and Fair Share:** The three core ethics of permaculture applied directly to human relationships and community structures [4, 5].

### Specific Topics for Your First Videos

You can use these topics to structure your first few uploads. Each one focuses on a single "chunk" of your vision:

**Video 1: The Antidote to Modern Isolation**

* **The Topic:** Talk about how modern society often forces us into small, isolated nuclear families where we might not even know our neighbors, leaving us feeling disconnected from each other and the natural world [6, 7].
* **The Hook:** Introduce your community as a deliberate response to this isolation. Explain that you are building a space where people can return to our ancestral roots of deep connection and mutual dependence [6, 8].

**Video 2: What is "Social Permaculture"?**

* **The Topic:** Most people think permaculture is just about gardening, but you can explain how you are applying it to human relationships.
* **The Hook:** Introduce the ethics of *Earth Care, People Care, and Fair Share*. Explain that before you can successfully manage land or projects together, you must first build the "invisible structures" of trust and communication, because "it's the people, not the land" that make a community thrive [2, 4, 9].

**Video 3: Why We Need "Inner Transition"**

* **The Topic:** Discuss the concept of "Zone 0" in permaculture—which represents the inner life and well-being of the community members [2, 4].
* **The Hook:** Share your belief that true systemic change requires inner work. Talk about how your community will prioritize "Heart Sharing" and emotional well-being just as much as practical tasks, creating a space for true authenticity rather than performing for others [2, 10, 11].

**Video 4: Building a "Beloved Community"**

* **The Topic:** Explain that you aren't just looking for people who look and think exactly like you. Share your vision for a "Beloved Community" that embraces diversity and recognizes that everyone’s experiences are invited and belong [3, 12].
* **The Hook:** Talk about how true peace and freedom are built by learning to stay in relationship and "break together" through conflicts, rather than running away when things get difficult [13-15].

**The Call to Action for Every Video:**
At the end of each video, remember to guide your viewers to the "safe enough" space. You can say something like: *"If these values resonate with you, true community can't be built in a YouTube comments section. I invite you to join our next virtual Heart Sharing meeting on Zoom to connect directly."*
