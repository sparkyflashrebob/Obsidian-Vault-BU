## 1. Executive Summary & Core Evacuation Decision

In the event of a **100-Year (1% annual chance)** or **500-Year (0.2% annual chance)** compound flood in Larkspur, all street-level and carport parking at 260 Larkspur Plaza Drive will experience severe, destructive inundation:

* **100-Year Flood (FEMA BFE = 10.0 ft NAVD88)**: Floodwaters submerge street parking by **+3.3 ft (39 inches)** and carport stalls (including the Prius stall) by **+2.25 ft (27 inches)**. Any vehicle remaining in the lower lot or on the street will suffer complete interior and mechanical destruction.
* **500-Year Flood (~11.0 to 11.5 ft NAVD88)**: Floodwaters rise to **+3.5 to +4.8 ft** across the entire parking complex, submerging vehicle roofs and overtopping the apartment ground landing by 8 to 14 inches.
* **The Solution (Immediate High Ground)**: Directly west of the complex, **Doherty Drive** climbs out of the flood plain into the foothills. Driving **0.25 miles (a 1-minute drive or 4-minute walk)** west on Doherty Drive brings vehicles to **80.6 ft NAVD88** at Magnolia Avenue—**70 feet above the 100-year flood line**.

## 5. Step-by-Step Evacuation Procedure

1. **Advance Timing**: Move vehicles **at least 2 hours before the projected astronomical high tide** or during the daylight lull preceding an atmospheric river landfall. Once water reaches the street crown (7.31 ft), low-clearance vehicles cannot safely drive through.
2. **Driving Route**:
   * Exit the parking stall and head south on **Larkspur Plaza Drive** toward Doherty Drive.
   * Turn **right (West)** onto **Doherty Drive**.
   * Continue west past Riviera Circle as Doherty Drive climbs out of the valley basin.
   * Park along the curb on **Upper Doherty Drive** or turn onto **Magnolia Avenue**.
3. **Pedestrian Return**:
   * Walk down Doherty Drive back to the complex (a gentle 4-minute downhill walk).
   * Even if low road dips pool water later, pedestrian sidewalks and elevated curbs remain passable much longer than driving lanes.
