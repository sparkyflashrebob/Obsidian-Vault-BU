## Metadata
- Author: [[Psychology Today]]
- Full Title: From Being Mindful to Mindful Living
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: The article discusses the importance of distinguishing between a diluted form of mindfulness and mindful living that motivates people to become agents of positive change. While being mindful in each moment is a useful coping mechanism, true mindful living requires people to get out into the world and do more. The article argues that society needs more mindful living to support economic and political futures, and that companies should recognize the importance of spirituality in the workplace beyond mindfulness and meditation. Mindful living involves cognitive operations that support goal-oriented behavior, and while mindfulness is a valuable tool for meaning, sometimes living demands more than just being.
