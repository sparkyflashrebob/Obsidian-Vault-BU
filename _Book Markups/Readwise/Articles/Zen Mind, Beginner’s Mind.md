Shunryū Suzuki taught that the beginner's mind is an "open and enthusiastic" mind with many possibilities, while an expert's mind has few. This beginner's mind is the mind of compassion and is boundless, allowing one to keep their own precepts. It is a state that can be developed through silent meditation and involves having no thought of achievement or self.
