- **The structure of the mind** 
  Buddhists describe the person as composed of five **skandhas** ("aggregates"): 
  1.  **The body (rupa)**, including the sense organs.
  2.  **Sensations and feelings (vedana)**, coming out of contact between sense organs and objects.
  3.  **Perceptions and ideas (samjña)**, especially manifest in our ability to recognize things and ideas. 
  4.  **Mental acts (samskara)**, especially will power and attention.
  5.  **Basic consciousness (vijñana)**. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gtssqek4fzwr7dhysx8fdxaw))
- The last four are called **naman**, name, meaning the psyche. **Namarupa** (name-form) is therefore the Buddhist term for the person, mental and physical, which is nevertheless anatman, without soul or essence. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01gtssqz9artpyj1kfs0eezaak))

- Buddhism also differentiates among six "fields" (**ayatana**) for the five skandhas:  sight, hearing, smelling, tasting, touching, and mind, as well as the objects of these six senses. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h2sj27g4b2245jfxgavg5pwk))
- Mahayana Buddhism adds **alaya-vijñana**, “storehouse” consciousness, to the skandhas.  This is similar to Jung’s idea of the collective unconscious.  What is stored there are called **bijas** or seeds, which are inborn tendencies to perceive the world in a certain way and result from our karmic history.  They combine with **manas** or ego to form the illusion that is ordinary existence.  By quieting this ego and becoming less self-centered, your mind realizes the "emptiness" (**sunyata**) of all things.  Then you have peace. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h2sj39tk2c1r8sqbdjcz1pvc))
- Life is at very least full of suffering, and it can easily be argued that suffering is an inevitable aspect of life. If I have senses, I can feel pain; if I have feelings, I can feel distress; if I have a capacity for love, I will have the capacity for grief. Such is life. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h2sj462gznhq556bvj93wt65))
