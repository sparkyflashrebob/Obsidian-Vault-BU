    - Note: The Buddhist view of hope is that it can be used as a tool to create a better future. Oren Jay Sofer states that there is a reliable way to release ourselves from suffering, to protect other beings, mitigate harm, and build a better world. Sister Clear Grace explains that hope can be a way of relating to suffering amidst continuity and change, while Ayya Yeshe suggests that hope can be used as a form of resilience, allowing us to develop intention in the face of obstacles and create a more just world. Ultimately, the Buddhist view of hope is a way of finding joy and freedom, and can keep us connected with the potential to awaken.
