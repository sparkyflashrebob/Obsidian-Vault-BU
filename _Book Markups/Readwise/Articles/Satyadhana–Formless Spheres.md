# Satyadhana–Formless Spheres

![rw-book-cover](https://readwise-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/static/images/article0.00998d930354.png)

## Metadata
- Author: [[Dhivan Thomas Jones]]
- Full Title: Satyadhana–Formless Spheres
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: Satyadhana–Formless spheres is a meditation practice that investigates the illusory nature of four aspects of experience. It involves a continual state of knowing what is perceived and what is not, and focuses on the subtler disturbances of perception to ultimately lead to the realization that there is no faculty of perception and that all entities that appear in one's field of perception are illusory. The practice culminates in liberation from the "intoxication of sense desire, better and unlimited existence, and ignorance" and leads to an establishment of emptiness in accordance with the way things really are.
- URL: https://thebuddhistcentre.com/system/files/groups/files/satyadhana-formless_spheres.pdf
