## Metadata
- Author: [[Ekua Hagan]]
- Full Title: Sugar: 5 Surprising New Findings From Neuroscience
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: Sugar has become an integral part of modern life, with the average American consuming over 30 teaspoons a day. Neuroscience research has revealed the potent brain effects of sugar, which help explain why it is so difficult to moderate consumption. Our sweet tooth can be made, not born, and can be quickly learned with regular exposure to high-sugar foods. Sugar tolerance can increase rapidly with high-sugar diets, and sugar can act as a pacifier for the mind. However, excess sugar consumption can have negative effects on brain function, such as interfering with memory and learning.
- URL: https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/the-healthy-journey/202303/sugar-5-surprising-new-findings-from-neuroscience
