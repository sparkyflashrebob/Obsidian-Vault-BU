- Document Note: The benefits of doing absolutely nothing are often overlooked. Taking a break to simply "be" can reduce stress, enhance creativity, serve as an emotional reset, and promote mindfulness. It can also improve focus and concentration, improve relationships, and restore physical energy. Being still and disengaging from distractions can help individuals become more aware of their surroundings and appreciate the little things in life.
