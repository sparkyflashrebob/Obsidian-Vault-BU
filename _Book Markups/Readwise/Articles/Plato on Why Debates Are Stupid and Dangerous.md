- Document Note: Plato distinguishes dialectic from debate, the latter being a sport where getting people to agree with you is paramount, regardless of whether your position is right or wrong. Debates can be dangerous, especially when the one doing the debating has a particular trick up their sleeve to manipulate people to side with them. Sophists, who were known to be money-hungry professional debaters in Plato’s Greece, excelled in eristic, an ability to win a debate by using deceit and tricky dialect. Nowadays, popularity means more than the truth; as such, debates are all about who is more entertaining and who has the louder voice.
