- Clinical depression had been slowly rising in the U.S. prior to the COVID-19 pandemic but has jumped notably in its wake. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p45077zhee371h9bpr5qe6))
- Social isolation, loneliness, fear of infection, psychological exhaustion (particularly among front-line responders such as healthcare workers), elevated substance abuse and disruptions in mental health services have all likely played a role ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p45adagdsfhv2gkszs4ez4))
- Among subgroups, women have historically reported substantially higher levels of depression than men. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p45wv23g4wcztr47ejwc11))
- women were disproportionately likely to lose their jobs or to exit the workforce altogether due in part to the pandemic driving children home from school or day care. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p46cgy537a5vv52axpvs50))
- Women also made up 78% of workers in all healthcare occupations in 2019, exposing them to enhanced emotional and psychological risk associated with the pandemic. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p46new4rg03htdx4bvph8c))
- Young adults, in turn, are more likely to be single and to report loneliness, particularly so during the pandemic. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p46y6g4ex8byztas1zvydt))
- They also [need more social time to boost their mood](https://news.gallup.com/poll/151457/seniors-maintain-happiness-highs-less-social-time.aspx) than older adults, something directly impacted by COVID-19. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p474anwx0s01csf5bgjpdc))
- Daily experiences of sadness, worry and anger -- all of which are closely related to depression -- are highest for those under 30 and those with lower income levels ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p47axyf1fd1cdajt4tvj00))
- And, like women, young adults and people of color were disproportionately likely to lose their jobs altogether due to the pandemic. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01h0p47gs3qt8eq0gnqb6r9fm6))
