## Metadata
- Author: [[Psychology Today]]
- Full Title: The Crucial Work of Facing Suffering
- Category: #articles
- Document Note: The article discusses the importance of therapists bearing witness to their clients' suffering, highlighting the difficulty of the task. It emphasizes the need to meet the pain with full presence and to not attempt to fix it or take it away. It also encourages therapists to look at their own resistance, hesitation, fear, and helplessness when faced with a client's pain, as this can help increase their tolerance for the client's anguish. Lastly, it stresses that encountering pain honestly is the gateway to true healing.
