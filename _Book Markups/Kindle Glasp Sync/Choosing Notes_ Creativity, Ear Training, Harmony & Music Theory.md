### Highlights & Notes

> Often times, the vocalist will sing a phrase and the guitar or piano will either echo that phrase or play a bit of a line that continues or compliments that phrase. Other times an instrument will harmonize with the vocal and play the exact same phrase at the same time.

> It is commonly understood that in vocal songs the sole purpose of each instrument is to simply support and highlight the vocal. Likewise, during an instrumental passage, whichever instrument is taking the lead role should be supported by the other players and not interfered with or "stepped on".
