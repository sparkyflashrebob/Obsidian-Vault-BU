#### Highlights & Notes

> Meditators can eventually attain access concentration using either type of momentary concentration practice&mdash;samatha or vipassanā. However, samatha practices are more likely to lead to access concentration because of their more stable nature. Access concentration is characterized by the significant reduction or complete dropping of the five hindrances and the arising and strengthening of the jhāna factors.
