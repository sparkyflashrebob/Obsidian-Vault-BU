Ardency means being intent on what you're doing, trying your best to do it skillfully. This doesn't mean that you have to keep straining and sweating all the time, just that you're continuous in developing skillful habits and abandoning unskillful ones. Mindfulness helps that effort along by reminding you to stick with it, so that you don't let it drop.
