#### Highlights & Notes

> ehipassiko

> We practice looking inward in ways that give rise to liberating insight.

> ajjhattanupassana:

> (anupassati)

> (ajjhatta),

> The Dhamma points to ease in ways of attention that are not dependent upon having ideal conditions in an unpredictable world.

> The wise ruler, said the Buddha, knows that safety lies not in building walls against danger but in removing fortifications of the heart, so that unwholesomeness cannot find any foothold in the first place.
