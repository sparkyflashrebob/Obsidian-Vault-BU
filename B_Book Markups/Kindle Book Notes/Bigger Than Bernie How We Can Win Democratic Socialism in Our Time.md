#### Highlights & Notes

> class struggle.

> Capitalism is an economic system in which a small group of people own things like factories, companies, and money itself, and everyone else has to sell their labor to them in exchange for a wage, which they use to buy

> what they need to survive. Through their labor, workers create a surplus that is funneled into the bosses&rsquo; pockets as profits rather than being used for the good of everyone.
