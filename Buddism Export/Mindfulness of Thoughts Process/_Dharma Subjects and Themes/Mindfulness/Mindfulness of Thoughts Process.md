1-minute practice (loving mindful awareness)
- Counting your thoughts
- picture thoughts
- word thoughts
- images & sound
- soft thoughts as comments in the background

We will now have a period of meditation where we can include mindfulness of thought
- follow the sensations of breathing
- sensations in the body
- stream of thoughts in the background

Let them be. Let them rise and fall like waves of the ocean around the breath

Check in periodically to see if a strong thought has arisen and carried away your attention

It can be helpful when you notice thoughts to gently name it.  Planning mind, judging mind, worrying mind, or happy mind, contented mind.  This simple naming and acknowledging thoughts are supportive of the witnessing quality of mindful loving awareness.

As you become aware of thinking, notice that these too are impermanent, arising and passing away just like a cloud in sunlight.

After the thought has diminished, simply return again to mindfulness of breath and body for a time until another strong experience—whether a thought or emotion or sound—pulls your attention from the breath.

Stepping out of the stories the thought tells, you can see the common patterns of thought without being so caught in them. And more importantly, you begin to rest in the field of mindful loving awareness rather than being repeatedly carried away by thought.

Now you can alternate mindfulness of breathing with mindfulness of other strong experiences when they arise. You become a steady, loving witness of all that arises and passes. You become a peaceful one sitting still amidst the rising and falling waves of experience.

Here are some further ways to investigate and learn about thought as you are growing in mindfulness.
● Notice how it feels, the very moment you shift from being absorbed or lost in thought, to simply name it without being swept into it.
● Notice which types of thought really have an effect on your body. What thoughts trigger you most? What is their bodily effect?
● Notice how some thoughts automatically bring strong emotions, and how some emotions automatically bring up certain thoughts. Learn to witness the interplay between thought and emotion.
● Notice the experience of the space between thoughts.
● Deliberately bring up a sad thought, an angry thought, a loving thought. Notice how
powerfully these thoughts and their connected emotions affect the whole state of your body and mind.
● Imagine you are the open sky and thoughts float through like  clouds. Sense what it is like to shift from thinking to lovingly noticing the clouds
