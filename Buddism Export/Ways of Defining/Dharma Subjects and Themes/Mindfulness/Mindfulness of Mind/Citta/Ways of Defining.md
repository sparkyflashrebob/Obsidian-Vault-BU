[[_A_My Research/_Buddhism/_Dharma Subjects and Themes/Mindfulness 1/Mindfulness of Mind/Citta/Citta]]
As an Agent = that which knows the object
As and Instrument = with the help of other concomitants know the object
By sheer activity = is a knowing of the the object

Definition as mere activity is the most adequate definition of these terms.
