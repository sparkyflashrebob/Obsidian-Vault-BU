
Here are the seven factors of awakening:

- Mindfulness
    
    A mental quality that is required in all wholesome states of mind. It is related to memory and can help recall activities more easily.
    
- Concentration
    
    One of the seven factors of enlightenment. It is a necessary condition to be cultivated by a disciple in order to attain awakening.
    
- Equanimity
    
    The highest of the seven awakening factors. When the seven factors are developed successfully, they lead to a direct spiritual experience, or penetration.
    
- Energy
    
    Can be strengthened by reflecting on the benefit of effort and the danger of indolence. When the mind and body are energized, the mind becomes clear and joy can naturally arise.
    
- Investigation
    
    The second factor that brings us closer to enlightenment. It is our search for truth, including the general study of reality and ourselves as we experience them.
    
- Tranquility
    
    Related to physical and mental calmness and is therefore a direct antidote to the hindrance restlessness-and-worry.
    
- Joy
    
    The spiritual path often begins when we realize that getting what we want doesn't make us joyful. It's what we do, not what we buy or are given, that builds joyfulness.
