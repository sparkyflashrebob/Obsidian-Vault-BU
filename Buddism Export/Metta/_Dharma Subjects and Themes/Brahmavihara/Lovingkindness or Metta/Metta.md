-   [[_unsorted from computer/Ebooks/Unknown/attitude (798)/Dharma Subjects and Themes/Brahmavihara/Lovingkindness or Metta/Metta]] If engaged in widely, practiced and carried out, one gains and give rise to wholesome endowments and there will be eleven result in turn, eleven fruits.
    -   What are the eleven? One sleeps peacefully (1), one wakes up peacefully (2), one does not encounter bad dreams (3), heavenly beings protect one (4), human beings love one (5), one is respected by non-human beings (6), one will not be harmed by poison (7), by weapons (8), by water or fire (9), nor be subjected to torturing (10), and with the breaking up of the body at death one will be reborn in a god realm, in a Brahmā heaven (11).
