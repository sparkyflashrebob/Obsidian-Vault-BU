On a side note: 
Jhana is deep concentration practice where one withdrawals the mind from automatic responses and leads to states of perfect equanimity and awareness.    
It is equated with the definition of "concentration" practice where one reaches a state of one-pointed absorption into an chosen object of meditation, and penetrates what is experienced to one-pointed equanimity. 
There are eight levels of jhana with the state of perfect equanimity being the 4th Jhana. These are called the Rupa Jhana's or Material Jhana's. 
Levels 5 thru 8 explore deeper states beyond that and are know as the immaterial Jhanas.

Just briefly I'll list them here:
1st Jhana is known for Pleasant Sensations, 
2nd Jhana for Joy, 
3rd Jhana for Contentment, 
4th Jhana for Utter Peacefulness, 
5th Jhana as Awareness of the Infinity of Space, 
6th Jhana of Infinite Consciousness, 
7th Jhana of No-thingness and 
8th Jhana as Neither perception nor non-perception.  
Some people refer to a 9th Jhana as Cessation or Nibanna or Enlightenment.
