Buddhism When This is, when this is not, That is not
Conditionality Dependent Origination
 
 The core teaching of Buddhism is the doctrine of Dependent Origination, which states that all phenomena are interdependent and arise in a conditioned way. This means that everything is determined by conditions and nothing arises or exists independently. 
 
 A central tenet of Buddhism is that this conditionality is pervasive, covering all aspects of life. This includes both the physical world and the psychological realm. 
 
 The principle of Conditionality or Dependent Origination can be summarized in two phrases: "When this is, that is; when this is not, that is not." This phrase emphasizes the idea that everything arises in dependence on other factors and can only exist in a particular combination of conditions. When these conditions are absent, the phenomenon also disappears.
