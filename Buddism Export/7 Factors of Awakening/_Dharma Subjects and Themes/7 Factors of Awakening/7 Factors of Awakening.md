#buddhism
#Factors_of_Awakening


In [Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhism "Buddhism"), the **Seven Factors of Awakening** ([Pali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pali "Pali"): _satta bojjha_ṅ_gā_ or _satta sambojjha_ṅ_gā_; [Skt.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit "Sanskrit"): _sapta bodhyanga_) are:

-   **Mindfulness** (_[sati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindfulness_(Buddhism) "Mindfulness (Buddhism)")_, Sanskrit _smṛti_). To maintain awareness of reality, in particular the teachings (_[dhamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhamma "Dhamma")_).
-   **Investigation** of the nature of reality (_[dhamma vicaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhamma_vicaya "Dhamma vicaya")_, Skt. _dharmapravicaya_).
-   **Energy** (_[viriya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C4%ABrya "Vīrya")_, Skt. _vīrya_) also determination, effort
-   **Joy** or **rapture** (_[pīti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%ABti "Pīti")_, Skt. _prīti_)
-   **Relaxation** or **tranquility** (_[passaddhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Passaddhi "Passaddhi")_, Skt. prashrabdhi) of both body and mind
-   **Concentration** (_[samādhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam%C4%81dhi_(Buddhism) "Samādhi (Buddhism)")_) a calm, one-pointed state of mind,[[_A_My Research/_Buddhism/_Dharma Subjects and Themes/Logic and Philosophy 1/Catuskoti]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Factors_of_Awakening#cite_note-1) or "bringing the buried latencies or _[samskaras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa%E1%B9%85kh%C4%81ra "Saṅkhāra")_ into full view"[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Factors_of_Awakening#cite_note-FOOTNOTELusthaus2014113-2)
-   **Equanimity** (_[upekkha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upekkha "Upekkha")_, Skt. _upekshā_). To accept reality as-it-is (_yathā-bhuta_) without craving or aversion.

This evaluation of seven awakening factors is one of the "Seven Sets" of "Awakening-related states" (_[bodhipakkhiyadhamma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhipakkhiyadhamma "Bodhipakkhiyadhamma")_).

The Pali word _bojjhanga_ is a compound of _[bodhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhi "Bodhi")_ ("awakening," "enlightenment") and _anga_ ("factor").[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Factors_of_Awakening#cite_note-3)
