|Anger|
|Give up anger; renounce pride;  <br>transcend all worldly attachments.  <br>No sufferings touch the person  <br>who is not attached to name and form,  <br>who calls nothing one's own.  <br>Whoever restrains rising anger like a chariot gone astray,  <br>that one I call a real driver;  <br>others merely hold the reins.<br><br>Overcome anger by love; overcome wrong by good;  <br>overcome the miserly by generosity, and the liar by truth.  <br>Speak the truth; do not yield to anger;  <br>give even if asked for a little.  <br>These three steps lead you to the gods.<br><br>The wise who hurt no one, who always control their body,  <br>go to the unchangeable place,  <br>where, once they have gone, they suffer no more.  <br>Those who are always aware, who study day and night,  <br>who aspire for nirvana, their passions will come to an end.<br><br>This is an old saying, Atula, not just from today:  <br>"They blame the person who is silent;  <br>they blame the person who talks much;  <br>they also blame the person who talks in moderation;  <br>there is no one on earth who is not blamed."  <br>There never was, nor ever will be, nor is there now  <br>anyone who is always blamed or anyone who is always praised.<br><br>But the one whom those who discriminate praise  <br>continually day after day as without fault,  <br>wise, rich in knowledge and virtue,  <br>who would dare to blame that person,  <br>who is like a gold coin from the Jambu river?  <br>That one is praised even by the gods, even by Brahma.<br><br>Be aware of bodily anger and control your body.  <br>Let go of the body's wrongs  <br>and practice virtue with your body.<br><br>Be aware of the tongue's anger and control your tongue.  <br>Let go of the tongue's wrongs  <br>and practice virtue with your tongue.<br><br>Be aware of the mind's anger and control your mind.  <br>Let go of the mind's wrongs  <br>and practice virtue with your mind.<br><br>The wise who control their body,  <br>who control their tongue,  <br>the wise who control their mind are truly well controlled.|
