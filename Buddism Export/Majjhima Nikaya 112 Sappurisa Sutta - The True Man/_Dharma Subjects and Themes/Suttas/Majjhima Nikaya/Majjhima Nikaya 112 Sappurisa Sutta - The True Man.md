Teach the character of a true man and an untrue man

Untrue Man
One comes from an aristocratic family
	lauds himself and disparages others

True man considers
	is is not because of one's aristrocratic family that states of greed, hatred, or delusion are destroyed.  
	Even though someone may not have come from ..., yet the they have entered upon "the way that accords with Dhamma, entered upon the proper way, and conducts themselves according to the dhamma, he should be honored for that, they should be praised for that.
	Neith lauds themself or disparages others.
