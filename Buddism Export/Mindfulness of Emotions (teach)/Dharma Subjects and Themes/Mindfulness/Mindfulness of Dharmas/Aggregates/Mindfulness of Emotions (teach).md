Base:
upright and yet comfortable
close your eyes or soften your gaze.
Take a few deeper cleansing breaths then let the breath settle into its natural rhythm.

Notice if there is any obvious tension that you can release easily.
Let the eyes and face be soft.
Relax the shoulders and let the arms and hands rest easily. 
And let the belly be soft and the breath natural.
Let the heart be soft as well - to receive whatever arises with kindness and compassion
----
Now with this embodied presence - bring a kind and gentle awareness to the breath.
You can notice it as coolness in the nostrils (cold air coming in and warm air going out), or as tingling in the back of the throat, or as the rise and fall of the chest or belly - wherever you find it easiest to be relaxed yet alert.
Feel each breath and as you allow it, invite a sense of calm and ease to grow
--
Return gently to the breath whenever you notice the attention has wandered - 
Wandering is a natural process - this is what the mind does - 
-- longer pause
For now let all other experiences, sounds, sensations, thoughts, and images rise and fall like waves of the ocean around the breath.
-------------------
START HERE START HERE START HERE
----
After a time, become mindful of any emotions that might call your attention - bringing the same mindful loving awareness to them as you have to the breath.
When an emotion arises, let go attending to the breath for the moment and receive the emotion kindly - with curiosity.
---
It can be helpful to name it softly - Sadness as, sad... joy, joy, ..boredom, bored whatever it is. Keep the noting soft so as to not take away from the emotion you are experiencing.
---
As you attend to the emotion, notice carefully where you feel it in your body. Maybe as the face being flushed, or tightness of the heart, a fluttering in the belly.  You may also notice it as a feeling that is a constellation of sensations.
[Pause]
How does it respond to your kind awareness? Does it dissolve or get stronger? Does it change into another emotion? Notice this too.
--
Know that you are the space of loving awareness that can kindly notice emotions as they come and go. Tune in to this knowing quality of presence whatever your experience is.
[Longer Pause]
If you feel resistance to contacting the emotion, or it feels very strong, notice any story that the mind tells along with the feelings in your body.
Acknowledge this resistance, if it is present, kindly. Then let it be in the background and again bring a gentle presence to the emotion itself.
--
If at any time the emotion dissolves, come back home - to presence - return back to awareness of the body or the breath. 
--
Now you can include emotions in your loving awareness, as well as attending to the breath and body. As they come and go, you are becoming the calm steady witness, acknowledging them without getting caught.
--
Remaining in mindful loving awareness, you become gradually more open, balanced, and free.
