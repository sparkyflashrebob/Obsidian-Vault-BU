**Seven stages of purification (_satta visuddhi_)** 

1. Purification of conduct (_sila-visuddhi_)
2. Purification of mind (_citta-visuddhi_)
3. Purification of view (_ditthi-visuddhi_)
4. Purification by overcoming doubt (_kankha-vitarana-visuddhi_)
5. Purification by knowledge and vision of what is path and not-path (_maggamagga-nanadassana-visuddhi_)
6. Purification by knowledge and vision of the course of practice (_patipada_–_nanadassana-visuddhi_)
7. Purification by knowledge and vision (_nanadassana-visuddhi_)
