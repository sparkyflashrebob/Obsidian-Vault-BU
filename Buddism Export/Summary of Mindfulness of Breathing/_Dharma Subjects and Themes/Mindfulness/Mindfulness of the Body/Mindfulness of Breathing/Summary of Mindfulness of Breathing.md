According to the Buddha, there are specific steps involved in practicing Mindfulness of Breathing. These steps are as follows:

1. Establishing a comfortable posture: Find a comfortable position, either sitting or lying down, where you can be relaxed and alert.

2. Bringing attention to the breath: Direct your attention to the natural rhythm of your breath. Observe the inhalation and exhalation without trying to control or manipulate it.

3. Being aware of the entire breath cycle: Pay attention to the entire breath cycle, from the beginning of the inhalation to the end of the exhalation. Notice the sensations and movements associated with each breath.

4. Cultivating a relaxed and focused mind: As you continue to observe the breath, let go of any distractions or wandering thoughts. Bring your attention back to the breath whenever the mind wanders.

5. Developing mindfulness and concentration: Deepen your awareness of the breath by focusing on specific aspects, such as the sensation of the breath at the nostrils or the rise and fall of the abdomen. This helps to cultivate mindfulness and concentration.

6. Expanding awareness to the body: Expand your awareness beyond the breath to include the sensations and movements in the rest of the body. Be fully present and attentive to the physical sensations as they arise and pass away.

7. Integrating mindfulness into daily life: As you become more skilled in mindfulness of breathing, aim to carry this awareness into your daily activities. Practice being fully present and attentive in all aspects of your life.

These steps help to develop mindfulness, concentration, and a deep understanding of the breath and the body, leading to a greater sense of calm, clarity, and insight.
