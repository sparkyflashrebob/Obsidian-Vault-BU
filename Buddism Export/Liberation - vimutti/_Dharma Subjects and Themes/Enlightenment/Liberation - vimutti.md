from Encyclopedia of Buddhism, Malalasekera, Weeraratne, Vol VIII, Dhammacammam Pavattitam appativattiyam
Vimutti covers both Final liberation thru destruction of all unwholesomeness and as well as liberation that falls short of being the ultimate goal of early Buddhism

Vimuttimagga
Gradiation of types of liberation
- vimokkha
	- samisa (worldly)
		- experiences of liberation related to the 4 jhanas
	- niramisa (unworldly)
		- immaterial spheres
	- niramisa niramisatara (more unworldly than unworldly)
		- retrospective knowledge of the successful deliverance of mind from lust, anger, & delusion

Liberation of Mind 
- divine abodes
	- cetovimutti
	- liberated from hostility and ill-will
		- avera and avyapajjha
	- each of the brahmaviharas
	- transcends the limitations imposed by hostility and ill-will
- Metta (loving-kindness)
	- the escape (nissarana) from ill-will (vyapada)
	- moon that outshines any star
	- rising sun that dispells all darkness
	- can reach non-returner
	- weaken the fetters
	- one will sleep well and wakeup well
	- not be disturbed by evil dreams
	- protected by hostile actions of others
	- find it easy to concentrate
	- have pleasant countenence
	- will pass away without confusion
	- will be reborn in a non-sensual heavenly world
	- pretection assistence when facing non-humans
	- deliverance through perceiving thing and beings as beautiful
	- even though liberation of mind falls short of the goal, the liberating effects are remarkable
		- mental freedom spacial and emotional sense
		- temp freedom from unwholesome karmic retribution
- Karuna (compassion)
	- same with vexation (vihesa) 
- Mudita (sympathetic joy)
	- liberation from discontent
	- related to communal harmony
- Upekkha (equanimity)
	- liberation from lust
- all of these boundless (mahaggata & appamana)
- boundless liberation of mind (appamana cetovimutti)
- trumpeter heard in all direction
- unlimited in a spacial sense and from karmic perspective
	- any limiting action cannot remain
- development temporarily overrule negative karmic influence of another
- need to be combined with development of the factors of awakening to progress to the highest liberation
