You can turn your camera off if you would like. MIndfulness is something you can
develop in any posture. But remember we are encouraging to take a posture that can
provide you with a sense of ease and also where you can maintain a sense of
wakefulness...presence.
Other than this, there are no special meditation instructions this week. We've talked a lot
about the various techniques and tools that are available for you to use. Such as the use
of an anchor. This is an embodied practice. So you can use the body, the posture, your
awareness of breathing, a sound such as the Nada sound, etc. These are meant to help
us us to be more aware and mindful and not get lost in the next series of thinking that
tends comes up. So there is a discipline to the practice.
But there is also an art to meditation. I would like to encourage you to use your intuition,
and your curiosity. So there is also this encouragement to think of this as a creative
exploration. You know best what you need.
As we sit on the cushion we start to notice some things about ourselves that we didn't
notice before. There can be a sense of grounding in the senses using the techniques
but there is also this groundlessness, an unfolding, a changingness. Where things are
not so sure. You can open to this "embodied truth practice".
So... you take your seat under the stars and your seat of enlightenment, or just simply be
present with what is here.....we begin....
