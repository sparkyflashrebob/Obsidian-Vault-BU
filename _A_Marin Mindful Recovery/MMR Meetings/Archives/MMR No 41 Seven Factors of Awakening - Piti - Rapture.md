Piti vs Sukha
● Sukkha belongs to the 2nd Aggregate of Feelings - namely pleasant ones
● Piti belongs to 4th Aggregate of Mental Formations
● Rapture has an intense - energetic quality of arousal and excited anticipation (like seeing
a mirage in the distance of something you want)
● Sukha is the softer and more refined experience of comfort and wellbeing (having arrived
at the oasis)
Worldly vs Unworldly Rapture
● Worldly associated with sense pleasures (things we like and make us feel good)
● Unworldly Rapture is associated with seclusion & renunciation both from external
conditions & from the hindrances!!!!
● Being freed of our burdens.
● A tast of complete freedom.
● As a factor of Enlightenment, we are looking at the unworldly type of rapture.
● Great interest being pleasurable.
33. “In one who has aroused energy, unworldly rapture arises. On whatever occasion unworldly

● Rapture from Tireless Energy - from Investigation & Inquiry - from discerning wisdom

FIVE GRADES OF RAPTURE
1. Minor Rapture - lifting of the spine - hold posture effortlessly - Extremely interested - one
might also experience goosebumps or a trembling of the body. You might also be able
to experience this as a worldy feeling
2. Momentary Rapture - jolt of energy - suspect this to be what I was experiencing prior to
my panic attack - lack of mindfulness, investigation, and too much energy
3. Wavelike or showering rapture - thrilling sensations coming over the body again and
again.
4. Uplifting Rapture - Whole Body risen in the air - careful to lose fact that this is
conditioned.
5. Pervading Rapture - sublime feeling of happiness and exhilaration filling the whole body
