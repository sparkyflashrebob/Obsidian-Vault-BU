In working with the next 4 steps we returned to our feelings
Develop joy and happiness and transform suffering
Our feelings are us and we need to learn to look after them
If we don’t who will?

Our body and our feelings are our territory and we are responsible for that.
As a result of conscious breathing and calming the body, joy, a pleasant feeling arises.
This feeling of joy makes us happy and brings about some peace.
And we are aware of it.
So we are now aware of feelings in the body and feelings in the mind, wherever they are
produced.
So the mind includes feelings and perceptions.
When we are aware of every bodily and every mental, we are aware of every feeling.
So we have been able to calm the body previously and now we are able to calm the mind to
make it peaceful.
As we focus on the mind in the third group of trainings we work with whatever mental formations
are present and learn to cultivate mental formations that are beneficial, and, being aware of
mental formations that are not beneficial, learning to transform them.
What is beneficial is what brings us happiness and what is unbeneficial is does not.
When we are happy we easily become concentrated,
There are a few ways of describing concentration
It can feel as if we are absorbing into the breath
Or we can experience being enveloped by the breath.
Either way, we are aware of the one that knows.
When we use the mind to observe the mind, this brings us to deep concentration.
Anything that comes into the mind can become an object of our concentration
The 12th exercise or training is where one releases the mind to freedom. It is not bound by past
or future. The knots that bind us are loosened, untied and let go.
We are aware of this too.
The mind is then left free to observe things as they arise and pass away.
We gain insight into the changing nature of all that exists.
This insight into impermanence opens the way for us to see our connected and selfless nature.
Nothing has a separate, independent existence
Seeing this we let go of the desire to hold on to any experience.
As this deepens and sinks in, we come to a place of great joy, a feeling of emancipation, and the
ending of illusion. Freeing our selves from the intention to grasp any notion.
And finally, the last step illuminates for us what is to let go of ourselves, give up the burdens of
ignorance and grasping. To arrive at final liberation.
So this is the training.
Each step building upon the other.
