Before we start - there was a question about desire being a good thing
And as I described, desire can be both skillful or unskillful.
I’d like to clarify that some.
The Buddha spoke of two kinds of desire:
The desire that arises from ignorance and delusion which is called taṇhā – or craving –
and desire that arises from wisdom and intelligence, which is called kusala-chanda, or
dhamma-chanda, or most simply chanda.
So thankfully my description was accurate.
One good way to look at it is as the transformation of tanha to chanda.
Chunda is defined in many ways - “an eagerness to offer, to commit, and to apply oneself”,
An aspiration
And intention
Or simply interest and zeal.
But as I mentioned, I think the easiest way is look at it as the desire that is either skillful or
unskillful. And to look at our intentions.
