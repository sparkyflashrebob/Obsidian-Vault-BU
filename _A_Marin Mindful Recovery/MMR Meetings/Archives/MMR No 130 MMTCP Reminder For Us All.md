1. Important to know where happiness lies. #wise_effort 4 Wise Efforts. Make the effort to wake up. 2 deal with unhealthy states, states like greed, hatred and delusion, jealousy, fear, etc.  #akusala. Unhealthy states of mind. Parts of being human. Recommend  A) Guard against unwholesome states to prevent them from arising, B) and when they do arise, learn how to over come them (part of our training, bringing selfcompassion, mindfulness, kindness, patience, all of the beautiful teachings). And then there are the kusula states, the healthy states, like kindness, generosity, compassion, joy, equanimity, patience,  etc. C) Cultivate these wholesome states, which we are what we are doing when we are mindful. This is the best of all of cultivations because all of the other wholesome states follow from it. And D) when a wholesome state arises to maintain and increase that wholesome state is a good thing. This usually does not get a lot of play compared to the overcoming the unwholesome states or cultivate the wholesome. How might we do that? We naturally thing to increase it is to capture and hold onto it. However there is a problem.  All of the unwholesome states are states of contraction, while wholesome states are an expansion - an opening up. When we are trying to hold onto it you have just turned into an unwholesome state because you are grasping.  So how to turn it into a wholesome state? PAY ATTENTION TO IT.   LET IT BE HERE. DON'T MISS IT.
2. The Buddha asks us to notice how well these wholesome states feel. MN99. Subha Sutta. To Subha. He says when you are in the middle of a generous act, think to yourself that you are being generous now. That is a good thing. Not, I hope everybody sees  how generous I am.  All that is more Ego. He says notice how good it feels for a wholesome state to move through me. Generosity feels so good. There is a gladness that is connected to that wholesome state. He calls that gladness that is connected with the wholesome equipment of the mind. So this is a key, the Buddha says, to know that gladness and know how it feels in the body - in your mind. If you pay attention to it, and one of the beautiful qualities of mindfulness, is not just to know that there is calm here, or joy here, but bring your mindful attention to it. And really take it in. Marinate in it. Not with grasping. But just appreciating it.  And when we do this we give it much for life, to the wholesome. ### Instructions from Atisha
7 Reminders 
1. Consider all phenomena to be like dreams. Nothing lasts. Y2K? 2022, Back with middle ages, your childhood, with the dinosaurs. Everything appears and dissolves. Nothing can be grasped and held on to. Who we are is the loving witness of it all.
2. Be grateful to everyone. The spirit of gratitude.  The spirit of heart. For the blessings life provides and to those we love, and for those who are difficult, those who teaching how to open your heart of compassion.
3. Don't be swayed by outer circumstances. The 10,000 joys and the 10,000 sorrows - they will come. You, are this loving awareness can say Yes, this too.
4. Don't brood over the faults of others. Don't get engaged in that judgemental mind. We are all imperfect. Perfectly imperfect. We are all our own unique selves in a marvelous way. And that is just reality. Perfection is an imagination.
5. Rest in loving awareness. 
6. At all times rely on a joyful mind. Being alive, joy is intrinsically in it. Even in the 10,000 joys and the 10,000 sorrows. There is a magic and a joyfulness that is always present, even in our difficulties. 
7. Don't expect a standing ovation. We do this out of love, not to get koodos.  We do this because we become this space of loving awareness, that is present with all things.
