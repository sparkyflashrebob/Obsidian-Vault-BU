<h2>TNH</h2>


Last time we spoke about home. 

And we know that once we are home, we no longer feel lonely. When I feel at home I am warm, comfortable, safe,.... fulfilled. 

So *Home is a place where loneliness disappears*. 

Where is home? 

The Buddha said it very clearly that *home is in us*. 

And there is an island that he said to go back to.

[[*the island*]] of self. 

This is our home. 

This is a practice, not a theory. 

Loneliness is about time, I feel very lonely. 

Even if we are surrounded by many people, we are lonely together.

And there is a vacuum inside of us. 

You don't feel comfortable with that kind of vacuum. 

So we try to fill it up by connecting with other people. 

We believe that when you can connect with other people, and then the feeling of loneliness will disappear. 

%%But this is not true because we are not at home within our selves.%%

And #technology supplies us with a lot of devices in order to connect. 

To stay connected.  

Always stay connected. 

But we feel lonely, we continue to feel lonely. 

We check email several times a day. 

We send email several times a day.

We post messages every time we want to share. 

We are busy all day in order to connect. 

But that does not help with loneliness - with reducing the amount of loneliness in us. 

This is what is happening in the present moment in our modern civilization.

Our relationship is not good. 

We are not in good relationship with our partners, with our brother, with our sister, with our parents, with our society. 

And we feel very lonely.

And we use technologies in order to try to dissipate and fill in the loneliness.

But we have not succeeded. 

Every time we sit down on a cushion that is to connect with ourselves. Because in our daily life, we are disconnected with ourselves. 

We walk and we don't know that we are walking. 

We are there but we do not know that we are there. We are live but we do not know that we are alive. 

We are losing ourselves. 

We are not ourselves. 

And that is happening almost all day long. 

So the act of sitting down is an act of revolution 

To sit down you cut off, your stop that state of losing yourself 

of not being yourself. 

When you sit down you connect to yourself 

and you don't need an iPhone or a computer to do that. 

You just need to sit down mindfully, and breathe mindfully. 

And in a few seconds, you connect with yourself.

You know what is going on? 

What is going on in your body? 

What is going on in your feelings, in your emotions, what is going on in your perceptions.

And you are home and ready to take care of home, 

You have left home for a long time. 

And home has become a mess. 

So going home means to sit down and be with yourself and connect with yourself and accept the situation as it is. 

It is a mess, yes, but I accept it. 

Because I have left home for a long time, I have allowed things to happen like that at home.

To allow this mess. 

So now I'm home. 

I will rearrange everything. 

And with my in breath, with my out breath, my mindful breathing, I begin to smile at everything. And I will tidy up my home. 

I allow my body to release the tension while I breathe in and breathe out. 

And the Buddha told me how to do that. 

How to breathe in, and how to release tension in my body. 

I'm aware of my feeling of loneliness, of sadness, of fear, of anxiety. 

I smile to the feeling of loneliness, of fear, my anxiety. 

I say my dear loneliness, I know you are there.  

I hope to take care of you. 

And you make peace with your loneliness. 

You make peace with your fear. 

There's a wounded child in you. You recognize her, you recognize him, 

And you embrace them tenderly in your arms. 

That is the act of going home and taking care of home.

Every time you take a step - whether you are breathing in or breathing -  you go back to yourself. 

every step bring you home to [[the here and now]]. 

So that you can connect with yourself, your body your feelings, and that is real connection. 

And you don't need a lot of technology to do that. 

This is the revolution 

This is the way to heal ourselves and our society. 

We are losing ourselves. 

We are lost. 

You have to find yourself again you have to go home.

So there is the illusion of connection. 

We think with that technology with these devices you can connect.

but you cannot really connect. 

How can you connect with another person when you cannot connect with yourself.?

So the teaching of the Buddha - going home to [[the island of self]] is the way to heal our society 

and to heal ourselves 

So with the act of breathing in, you go inside 

==**[[The way out is in]]**==

When you take a step, you go in yourself , 

because that step brings you home to the [[here and now]] 

It helps you to connect with your body. 

Your body is your breathing, 

your body is your feet, 

your body is your lungs, 

and you are connecting with body, with feet, with breath, with lungs. 

Your home, because body is part of your home. 

When you spend two hours with your computer, you forget entirely that you have a body

and without your body, how can you be alive?

So in Plum Village, many of us program a bell, mindfulness bell in our computer, 

and every 15 minutes you hear the bell 

and we stop working, stop thinking, 

go back to our in breath, we enjoy everything and reconnect with ourselves and we smile, we become alive again. 

We know that we have a body which is a wonder. 

Our body is a wonder 

Conducting is first of all, connecting with body.

And then there is a feeling whether that is a feeling of sadness, anger, loneliness, 

it is us 

connecting with our in breath and out-breath. 

And then with that mindful in breath, we connected our feelings. 

Smile to our feelings. 

We say don't worry. I am home.  I'll take care of you. 

And you embrace your feeling with tenderness 

whether it is fear or anger or loneliness and whatnot. 

%%We are with%% ourselves with that kind of practice. 

This is an act of going home and you don't need a technological device to do so. 

Be a home for yourself. 

The Buddha said "be an island unto yourself" 

Taking care 

Taking refuge in the island of self 

That is the practice recommended by the Buddha. 

So when we walk  from the parking lot to the office, why don't you go home with every step? 

Why would you continue to think,  to worry, to suffer? 

%%[[mindful in every moment]]%%

Every step can bring you home, you walk like a Buddha, 

you act like a free person 

and that is possible. 

You recover yourself 

you connect with yourself at every step

and freedom is possible. 

And you are able to compare it in yourself. 

You are not losing yourself anymore. 

You connect with your body. 

You connect with your feeling with every step 

and every step bring you freedom 

and if you can find a home for yourself, you can help your partner to find his or her self.

because she's lonely also.

He is lonely also.

And he's looking for for home 

For some warmth. For some safety. 

And that is why once you have got a home you can go to your partner. 

You can go to the other person 

and since you have a home you are in a position to help him to have a home also. 

And you are confident because you know how to 

how to connect with yourself 

how to have a home for yourself. 

So that confidence in you will inspire him or her to do the same. 

She can find a home in you. %%refuge for others%% 

And then she'll lean on that in order to build a home in her. 

Before you can go to him or to her to help, you have to have yourself.

You have to connect with yourself. 

And then when you have been able to connect with yourself, then the next step is possible 

You will be successful to connect with another person. 

Without the first step, the second step is not possible. 

And you don't really need iPhone or something like that. 

You need your eyes to look at him or her with compassion 

Even if they are not in the same town, you can connect with him with her easily. 

Because you are already yourself - already home.

And whatever you say, whatever you think, will help her 

will help him to recover themselves

And then, when she is able to go back to herself, your relationship becomes a real relationship because both of you have home. 

And that's why when you come together, you find home in each other at the same time as each of you. 

And this is a collective home for both of you. 

There's a home here, there's a home here. %%pointing to the head and the heart%% 

and there is a collective home here 

And it is the base of everything, 

if you want to help society, 

if you want to help community, 

if you want to help your country 

and then you have to base it on this. 

And when you have a true home you have happiness, 

you have safety, 

you have fulfillment 

and then you are in condition to go to the other 

whether they are an individual or a society, a group of people. 

So this is the way prescribed by the Buddha. 

Everything has to begin with yourself 

and the teaching 

the practice is very clear. 

Every breath 

every step, 

every sitting, 

every act like drinking, cleaning. 

If done in mindfulness you have a true home 

To enjoy the wonders of life around you and in you,
