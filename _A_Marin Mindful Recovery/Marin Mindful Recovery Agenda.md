- Welcome - Format - meditation /reading/comment & discussions/sharing the merit.
- The purpose of the Marin Mindful Recovery Group, is to deepening their spiritual connection thru exploration of mindfulness and meditation  or present awareness in every day life, opening the door to a greater peace and joy in our lives.
- We meet the first and third Fridays of every month, to meditate, to discuss the challenges of daily practice, both on and off the cushion as they relate to our recovery,  and to explore readings and other teachings from Buddhist and other contemplative practices.  
- All levels of meditation experience are welcome! As well as stages of recovery.

- Guidelines: For the stability of the group we ask that you not engage in cross-talk. Sticking to our direct experience.  At the end of the meeting we will open it up for a short period to allow for feedback. 
- Brief Introductions...for the benefit of new persons.
- Meditation 20 - 30 minutes -- Diligent, Clearing Knowing, Mindful, Free from Desires and Discontent in the world.
- Mindful talk.
- 8:20 reading.
- At 8:50 Questions/comments about this evenings session.  Anything need to be said.
- At 9:00pm - Sharing of merit. What insights we have gained from this evenings practice we give out freely to our fellow travellers.  May all beings, May Mother Earth, May the Universe gain benefit from our practice. 
- May all beings be happy and have the causes of happiness. May they be free from sorrow and the causes of sorrow. May they have that great happiness which is sorrowlessness. May they come to rest in the great equanimity, which is free from attachments and aversion.
