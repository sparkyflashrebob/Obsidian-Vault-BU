# Invitation

Welcome you to MMR for (date), 
I'd like us us to consider how blessed we are - to find ourselves 
Here - in this virtual space 
and Now - at this time 
Together - in fellowship, 
and to have these practices and these teachings. 

I encourage each of us to be open to our experience - 
to what we hear, 
and to what we feel, 
while keeping an open heart.  

Our purpose here is to explore this path that the Buddha's teachings point us towards 
- towards presence and wakefulness 
- towards freedom from suffering 
	- to the freedom that is available to us at any time 
	- and particularly right here in this present moment 
	- the only moment we have. 
Understanding that the path to our liberation is through this awareness.

By grounding our attention in the here and now, we learn to pause, relax & open what we are experiencing. And with that, connect with the flow of experience and the consciousness that is with that regardless of the conditions present.  

%% ALT%%
By grounding our attention in the here and now, we learn to acknowledge, accept, and let go of difficult emotions and thoughts. And, instead of embellishing or avoiding them, we work with them with more intention, creating a healthy space within us  -- for the benefit of ourselves and for others. 

  
In a minute we will sit together, then we will have a short break. 

This evening we will continuing with our discussion on the Mindfulness of Breathing,  venturing even deeper into the 4th tetrad or grouping of instructions and taking a look at dispassion or fading away.  The 14th of the 16 steps that is contained in the Anapansati Sutta.

PREMEDITATION INSTRUCTIONS

With that, we will now meditate for a period of 20 - 25 minutes.  

So I invite you to take your preferred meditation posture - one that both supports you so that you may be at ease but encourages a sense of wakefulness and alertness. 

  

Establishing a sense of awareness in the body (pause) 

knowing you are in a body - this quality of knowing what is present - here - in this moment. 

(pause)

  

In our practice today - and in following with this theme of MOB - I invite you to work with the breath or any one anchor that you choose. Be it with the breath, sensations in the body, or sounds. Resting in this anchor of awareness.  In the simple process of breathing ---- breathing in and breathing out. 

(pause)

And with the training - experiencing the whole body, calming body and mind, experiencing a sense of joy, a sense of pleasure, naturally allowing the process to deepen, returning to this awarenesss of breathing-in and breathing-out.

During the last few moments, I will offer some suggestions so that we may investigate how things are always changing. But for now attention to our chosen anchor - mindfule of breathing-in and breathing-out.
