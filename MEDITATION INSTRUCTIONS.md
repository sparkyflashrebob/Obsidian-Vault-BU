**MEDITATION INSTRUCTIONS**

SIT COMFORTABLY – BACK STRAIGHT – BUT NOT STIFF – NOTICE ANY TENSION AS YOU SIT AND BE AWARE THAT MOTHER EARTH SUPPORTS YOU SO THAT YOU CAN RELAX INTO HER.

LET GO OF ANY APPARENT TENSION – YOU CAN ROLL YOUR SHOULDERS AND FLEX YOUR NECK – BRINGING IT BACK INTO A RELAXED POSITION.

LET THE EYES CLOSE GENTLY – RELAXED SO THAT YOU ARE NOT SQUEEZING.

KEEP THE EYES SOFT – THE FACE SOFT – THE CHECK SOFT

TAKE A FEW DEEP BREATHS  - SETTLE THE AWARENESS INTO THE BODY

FEEL YOUR ENTIRE BODY   ALLOWING THE BREATH TO FLOW EASILY.

NOTICE WHERE YOU FEEL THE BREATH WITH THE GREATEST EASE.

WHETHER AT THE ABDOMAN, THE BACK OF THE THROAT OR AT THE TIP OF THE NOSE.

NOTICE EACH BREATH – THE IN BREATH ---- THE OUT BREATH –

NOTICE EACH BREATH AS IT COMES IN AND IT GOES OUT. …..

AS IT GOES IN SHORT AND IT GOES OUT SHORT….AS IN GOES IN LONG AND AS IT GOES OUT LONG.

FEELING THE WHOLE BREATH. FEELING THE WHOLE IN BREATH, FEELING THE WHOLE OUT BREATH.

FEELING THE WHOLE BREATH IN THE WHOLE BODY.

KEEP MINDFULNESS IN FRONT OF YOU.  STAY AWARE OF EACH BREATH.

SOUNDS WILL ARISE, FEELINGS, THOUGHTS OF PAST AND FUTURE. BE AWARE OF THEM AS THEY ARISE AND PASS AWAY, GENTLY RETURNING BACK TO THE BREATH.
