## The Sign
- [[nimitta]]
	- mark of things
	- signal to perceiver of what a thing is
	- enables [[recognition]]
	- causal nuance
		- causes the process of [[making sense of experience]] thru the operation of [[perception]]
- Hermann von Helmholtz
	- sign
		- represents external influences we experience
		- not completely accurate reproduction
		- okay for same circumstance to produce the same sign
			- not a different one
	- Buddhism
		- influenced by defilements
		- Can be misleading
	- says experience should not be seen as illusory
		- sign of something (existing or happening)
- Exploration
	- delayed recognition
		- image perceived in different ways based on viewing distance
	- also by narrative (thinking about the sign)
	- recognize by features that are less likely to change
		- voice for example
	- Working mechanism of recognition
		- lookout for what is relatively permanent
	- Perception is to grasp a sign of permanence in something... stands for permanence. Sign remains unchanged until one returns to recognize.
- Tend of overlook [[Impermanence]]
	- built into perception
		- [[change blindness]]
			- can impair the ability to perceive
	- Need to intentionally NOTICE THINGS CHANGING
		- counter balance tendency to ignore change
		- form of cultivating liberating insight
- [[Sustained Meditative Attention on Impermanence]]
	- can lead to gain [[signless liberation]]
