### **Meditation Experiences and Visions**
Participants frequently shared specific physical or visual sensations they experienced during the meditation sessions:
*   **Visual Hallucinations:** One participant described seeing colors and shapes during a session, specifically "grey triangles" that were "coming in and getting smaller going out like a tunnel" before turning into a "verbally blob". Another participant mentioned that they see colors when they lay in bed to breathe, noting they hadn't realized this could be considered meditation.
*   **Physical Distortions:** A participant shared a story about focusing on their breath but feeling their pulse so strongly that they became convinced their leg was "tweaked" and resting on the side of their foot. When they opened their eyes, they realized they had been sitting flat the whole time, noting it was the "strangest thing" that their brain was convinced of a position that wasn't real.
*   **Disassociation/Loss of Body:** One individual described a moment of deep focus where they suddenly felt like they didn't know if they had limbs, asking themselves, "do I have gloves?" and realizing the only thing they could feel was their "favorite things" or focus.
*   **The "Restart" Button:** A participant described the sensation of meditation as a "computer restarting," stating that about 15 minutes in, it feels like "no brain function" and "fucking amazing" because their racing mind finally clears.
*   **Synchronized Breathing:** One participant observed that during a deep meditation, the group’s breathing synchronized, describing it as "almost like music".

### **Personal History and Recovery**
Participants also offered glimpses into their personal lives and struggles with addiction:
*   **Jail and Reconnection:** One participant shared that they had been in jail for four months prior to coming to the center. They noted that watching TV was a positive experience for them because it allowed them to "take a peek at the outside world" and catch up on media they had missed,.
*   **Military and Substance Use:** A participant briefly shared their background, mentioning they were in the Marine Corps where their job was to "round up the truth," followed by living in New York City and struggling with meth and whiskey at the end of their run,.
*   **Trazodone Dreams:** A participant shared a humorous story about sharing a room with another patient. They mentioned taking Trazodone and waking up their roommate by talking in their sleep about "pumping oil" into a cooking pot that wasn't big enough.
*   **Using Nature to Cope:** A participant told a story about a previous Sunday when they were extremely stressed ("pressure was through the roof"). Instead of attending the group, they went down to the trees to listen to the sounds, which helped them calm down and drop the stress.

### **Daily Life and Struggles**
*   **Obsessive Thoughts:** One participant confessed that during meditation, their mind kept "rehearsing" how they come across to others, worrying that they are "pretty loud" and "pushing," and that this thought loop was "eating me up alive".
*   **Travel Memories:** A participant shared a memory of traveling to the Grand Tetons in 2018 and dropping into Idaho, recommending "Jenny Lake" where one can see the reflection of Mount Moran like a perfect mirror,.
*   **Financial Stress:** A participant shared a positive experience of mapping out their finances to handle debt, noting that "hedging my bet" and preparing allowed them to relax rather than living a "seat of my pants type of lifestyle".
