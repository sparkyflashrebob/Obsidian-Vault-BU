1Breathing in, I know I am
breathing in. (inhale)
Breathing out, I know I am
breathing out. (exhale)
In (inhale) / Out (exhale) 

2Breathing in, my breath grows deep.
Breathing out, my breath goes slowly.
Deep / Slow 

3Breathing in, I feel calm.
Breathing out, I feel ease.
Calm / Ease 

4Breathing in, I smile.
Breathing out, I release.
Smile / Release 

5Aware of my body, I breathe in.
Relaxing my body, I breathe out.
Aware of body / Relaxing body 

6Calming my body, I breathe in.
Caring for my body, I breathe out.
Calming / Caring 

7Smiling to my body, I breathe in.
Releasing the tension in my body,
I breathe out.
Smiling to body / Releasing tension 

8Breathing in, I calm my body.
Breathing out, I smile.
Calm / Smile

9Breathing in, I dwell in the present moment.
Breathing out, I know it is a wonderful moment.
Present moment / Wonderful moment


Hanh, Thich Nhat. How to Sit (Mindfulness Essentials) (pp. 98-100). Parallax Press. Kindle Edition.
