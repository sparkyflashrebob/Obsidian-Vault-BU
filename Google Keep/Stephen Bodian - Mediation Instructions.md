
Additional Meditation Instructions

Notice where you awareness is centered.

Allow this awareness to drop down into your body. 
Sense coming and going of your breath.
Sense your arms and legs.
Allow awareness to settle in sensation
richness and fullness of your sensation experience

if you find yourself separating out.
just come back to sensation.
If you are here, you can allow your awareness to open beyond the body.
To include all of experience. inside and outside.
MInd open like the sky including everything
Rest as this openness.
