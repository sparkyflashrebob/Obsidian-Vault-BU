Easy for us to get lost in wanting things to be a certain way.. This is called Dukkha - stress and suffering. When things aren't quite right.
And is dukkha?.
birth, aging, death, 
sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair.
Association with disliked.
Separation from the liked.
Not attaining ones wishes.

Namely the 5 focuses of identity:
Attachment to form (this body and the bodies of others), feelings, our perceptions, mental-formations(views, opinions, beliefs, intentions that our minds create), and consciousness (5 senses including the mind).

We can have negativity towards self.
We frequent narrow down what we experience to just few aspects of our lives and neglect others.
What we think and ponder upon becomes the inclination of the mind.
WHAT things should we think and ponder upon frequently and what do we forget or neglect?
This includes old age, sickness and death like I mentioned above. As we well as that all that is mine, beloved and pleasing will become otherwise, will become separated from me.
We are the owners of our karma, heirs to our karma, born of it, and abide supported by it. Whatever karma I shall do, for good or for ill, of that I will be the heir 
This is what we neglect to or forget to keep in mind. 
Intentionally turn to gratitude.
How?
We can reflect that:
We have been supported by others all of our lives. As Babies unable to care for ourselves. Our Parents raised us the best they could. But surely their have been others who have supported us in some way. 
Often we cannot pay this back, our passing was fleeting, or they have passed away.  So we have to learn to pay it forward.
How? By Being able to listen to and support others. Carry the message. By not taking things so personally that we let people slide when they hurt us, knowingly or unknowingly.  By taking the high road in our interactions. To be kind and forgiving. By staying sober and not making ourselves into a problem. 

There is a Dangers of taking these and other good teachings and the reflections above for granted.
In the world it appears that usually when we get feedback it is because of a perceived problem. We can see this in ourselves -  how we talk to ourselves, in our worries and fears.
Fault finding mind is destructive. 
We can and should purposely reflect on the good as it so easy to reflect on the bad. Our minds are velcro for the bad and Teflon to the good.  

We Forget that we are all in this together - relatives in old age, sickness and death. In our difficulties.
So we can turn inward and use contemplation as a means for connecting to our path. 
We are alive, breathing, we have life. 
How can we make best use of it?
Having a spiritual path can bring balance to our lives, give it meaning, make it meaningful,  give us purpose. 
To awaken ourselves.
To free ourselves from our own bandage.

We do this thru Mindfulness of the Body. Our experience right here and now.
Mindful of Feeling  Mindful of our Mind and of our state of mind.
Sotawanta- The Listener  The One Who Knows.
Knows things as they are.
Knows things as they arise 
Knows things as the pass away.
Without clinging.
Without attachment 
Without judgment.
Trusting our intuition of the felt sense of what we are experiencing to end suffering.
