This evening I will be leading us into the meditation with a couple of subtle changes, a little more guidance at the beginning, as it will connect with our topic today. 
So as we start I'd like you to first take a moment to just become aware of the heaviness of the body.  The heaviness of the arms, the heaviness of the head on the shoulders, aware of the heaviness of the whole body.

Likewise, I'd also like us to become aware of the feeling of touch, either the pressure of your feet resting on the floor, or your butt in contact with on the cushion of the chair, or, if you are laying down, the pressure on your backside.

Stay with this feeling of touch for a moment. This can help us get away from any thinking.

Now we turn our attention to following the breath. At this time we want to place our awareness on labeling the breath as it comes in and as it goes out. As we know the in-breath coming in - gently label it as "IN", and as we know each out-breath gently label each out-breath as "OUT". Noting each in-breath and each out-breath in this manner.


Staying with this noting of each in-breath and each out-breath. 
The purpose of this meditation in particular is to gently build up and develop concentration.
If you become distracted by a sound, come back to this knowing each in and out-breath. 
If you become distracted by a thought, come back to this knowing of each in and out-breath. 



If you find during the course of the meditation that you are able to stay with the knowing of each in-breath and each out-breath without any effort, then you can drop the noting and open your awareness to the sensation of breathing.
