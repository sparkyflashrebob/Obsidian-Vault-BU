1. Sensual desire - nurished by unwise attention on them - denurishing by a)learning to meditate on impure objects, b) devoting oneself to meditation on the impure, c) guarding the sense doors, d) moderation in eating, e) noble friendship*, f) suitable conversation**
*models for overcoming sensual desire ** conversation about overcoming sensual desire - especially about meditation on impurity
2. Ill-will - giving unwise attention to objects causing aversion - cultivation of loving-kindness & compassion & sympathetic joy & equanimity.
3. Sloth & torpor - listlessness, lazy- sluggish - unwise attention to it.  Rousing energy - exertion - continuous exertion (not overeating, change body posture, perception of light, staying in open air, noble friendship and conversation) Recollection of death - suffering of impermanance - sympathetic joy - contemplate the spiritual journey - masters greatness)
4. Restlessness and remorse - unrest of mind - quietude of mind - rapture - concentration - tranquility
5. Doubt - things causing doubt - wholesome & unwholesome, blameless & blameworthy, noble or low, contrasts to dark and bright
