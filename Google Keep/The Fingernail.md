The Buddha once asked his followers, as he scratched the surface of the dirt with his fingernail, would the amount of dirt under his fingernail be more or less than the great earth itself.  They answered that the amount of dirt under his fingernail is very small in comparision.  
Similarly, the amount of suffering for one who has reached stream-entry that they can expect to experience in the future is comparable to the amount of dirt under his fingernail.
The amount of suffering for a person who has not reached stream-entry is comparable to the great earth.
