Let yourself sit in an upright yet comfortable way.
When you are ready, close your eyes.
[Pause]
Take a few deeper cleansing breaths and then let the breath settle into its natural rhythm.
[Pause]
Notice if there is any obvious tension in the body that you can release easily and do so. Let the eyes and face be soft.
[Short pause]
Relax the shoulders and let the arms and hands rest easily.
Let the belly be soft and the breath natural.
[Short pause]
And let the heart be soft as well, to receive whatever arises with kindness and compassion.
[Pause]
Now with this embodied presence, bring a kind and gentle awareness to the breath.
You can notice it as coolness in the nostrils, swirling tingling in the throat, or the rise and fall of the chest or belly.
Feel each breath and as you allow it, invite a sense of calm and ease to grow.
[Pause]
Return gently to the next breath whenever you notice the attention has wandered. Wandering is a natural process . . . no judgement.
[Longer Pause]
Let all other experiences, sounds, sensations, thoughts, and images rise and fall like waves of the ocean around the breath.

[Pause]
After a time, become mindful of any emotions that call your attention.
You can bring the same mindful loving awareness to them as you have to the breath.
When an emotion arises, let go of attention to the breath and receive the emotion kindly. It can be
helpful to name it softly.
Sad, sad . . . or joy, joy . . . or bored, bored . . . or anxious, anxious . . . or excited, excited.
MINDFULNESS OF EMOTIONS
As you attend to the emotion, notice carefully where you feel it in your body. Also notice how it
feels as a constellation of sensations.
[Pause]
How does it respond to kind awareness? Does it dissolve or get stronger? Does it change into
another emotion? Notice this too.
You are the space of loving awareness that can kindly notice emotions as they come and go.
[Longer Pause]
If you feel resistance to contacting the emotion, or it feels very strong, notice the story that the
mind tells along with the feelings in your body.
Acknowledge it kindly. Then let this be in the background and again bring a gentle presence
to the emotion.
[Pause]
When the emotion has changed or dissolved, return back to awareness of the breath for a time.
[Pause]
Now you can include emotions in your loving awareness, as well as attending to the breath and
body. They come and go and you are becoming the calm steady witness, acknowledging them
without getting caught by each one.
[Pause]
Remaining in mindful loving awareness, you become gradually more open, balanced, and free.
