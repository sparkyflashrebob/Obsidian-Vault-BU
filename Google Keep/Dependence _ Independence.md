In this practice actually we are pointing to Independence from conditions.
Silence, stillness, 
Seeing ourselves from the perspective of conditions and learning to free ourselves from being attached to conditions

To paraphrase Ajahn Sumedho talking about peace but using independence- "Even independence is a condition. When look for independence as a condition we stop looking at the reality of here and now."

Peace and independence are always here in the here and now in the midst of any and all conditions. It is something to recognize. 
Any way that we limit ourselves with words and identities those are not peaceful. This limiting is not freedom but dependence.

The desire for independence has cause us suffering

Any of the ideas about independence I mentioned above when not ideal can cause us suffering.
Even if we were to get our docks a row and experience a period of freedom or peace based on conditions being in our favor, these things change. 
The conditions change, illness, and conflicts arising  and we can be limited in some way by in one's mind, or in the world, or in society. 
With avija conditions arise and we get lost in the resulting unsatisfactoryness. We build in these concepts of ways to be conscious of our reality. Biases. Seeing the world thru the conditions we hold in the mind but are not aware of. 
Keeping this awareness into mind

Are we truly free and independent when we have to strive for survival? 
Alan Watts tells the story of our striving, grade school to career and at some point we feel we have arrived. But when reflect we realize we are the same person before we even started the striving, the same one who was there all along.
Fooled into going somewhere where you aren't and thinking things should a certain way for you to be free. We are already free. It is our conceptualization that imprison us.
