In this play of life, whether you like it or not, you play a role that has been designated to play.. The role in itself you play is one thing, but you are to understand inside your being your true nature. 
Someone can be awake to thr true news of who they are and still be a shoeshine boy or a janitor , a doctor or a politician.. 
If there is a God who is going to judge you,, he is not to say listen I made you with so much potential you just end up in a supermarket.. What am I goibg to do with you.. That is a very human thing..  Your realization is what is important.. That you awaken to your true nature., wake up to the truth of who you are.. 
THAT IS YOUR WORK.
