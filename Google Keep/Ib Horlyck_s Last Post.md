Now that I come to die

Uddrag af Lonchenpa

Now that the connection with this life
has lost its karmic power,
do not lament about this beggar
who died happily and unattached
But constantly pray

All beings are transients, like past and future guest:
The have gone, the young will also go.
This generation won't even last a hundred years -
understand this thoroughly straight away

The time has come to go; like a traveler,
I must go my way.
My joy in dying has been well earned: It is
greater than all the wealth in the ocean
now that the connection with this life
has lost its karmic power,

Do not lament about this beggar
who died happily and unattached,
But constantly pray

Birgitte og Karidin
