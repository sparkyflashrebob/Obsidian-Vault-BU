
MEDITATION IS LIFE!

Don’t meditate in order to relax. Don’t meditate in order to silence your mind. Don’t meditate in order to get into a “meditative state”, to become enlightened, or to transcend mind and body.

These are all second-hand ideas about meditation.

True meditation is kinder, simpler, more heartfelt and without a goal, so it’s impossible to fail. 

Meditate for meditation’s sake. Meditate like a spontaneous dance, for the pure experience and joy of it. Taste your own taste, feel your own gentle being amidst the backdrop of a chaotic world. Meditate playfully, like a child who lives totally in the present. Let your meditation become a deep embrace of your flaws, your imperfections, and even your inability to meditate properly. Drench it all in fascination, and don’t even make fascination into a goal.

Meditate because it’s who you are. Meditate because you are in love with the fleeting moments of life. Meditate as gently or as fiercely as you want. Meditate on your relaxation and your struggle.

Meditate away your concepts of meditation, but love your concepts too.

Meditation is life, your true self.

- Jeff Foster
