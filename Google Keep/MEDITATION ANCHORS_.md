MEDITATION ANCHORS 

It is helpful to select a home base or anchorthat allows you to quiet and collectthe mind, as well as 
to deepen embodied presence. Useful anchors are: 
. The breath as it enters and leaves the nostrils, upper lip, and or throat 

. Other sensations during breathing, such as the rise and fall ofthe chest or belly 
. The whole body as it breathes 
. Whole-body awareness without focus on breath 
. The sensations of hands and feet 
. 4-6 touch points in the body, such as buttocks, hands, etc. 
. Seeing, hearing, sitting, touching 
. Sounds; listening to all sounds within or around you 
. Listening to and feeling one's entire experience (i.e., receiving sounds and sensations 
in awareness)
