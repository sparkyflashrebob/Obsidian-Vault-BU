Before we dedicate the merit of the practice to others, I invite you to look towards your own heart space - and to notice if there is anything that has or may need healing.  (pause)

With what is left, let's take a moment to reflect on whatever merit we have gained from this evening's practice and how we might support those around us with our attention, our compassion, and our good wishes. We give this out freely and willingly to all of our fellow travelers, be they near or far away. 

May all beings, May Mother Earth, May the Universe gain benefit from our practice.
