Attention emerges from deep within the body
the path to mindful life is not paved by taming and subduing but rather by surrender.

somatic mindfulness is the body's mindfulness, a relaxed, loving attention spontaneously experiencing the present moment through the bodymind.

The mind is distracted by the body is not.
The body is already mindful.
At the moment of distraction a practitioner of somatic mindfulness releases control and allows attention to be drawing back by the body or by feeling the breath....
