Ajahn Amaro - reminded me about us all being in the same boat of aging, sickness & death.  We all breathe, eat, sleep - we have bodies, we have minds. In this - we are unified. We are all part of a natural order. 
Also, remind us that so much of the Buddha's teachings are of letting go of a selfish view and turning to turn away from a life that is self-centered to one that is dhamma centered - one centered upon nature. 

The Sutta on the Wonderful and the Marvelous, Majjhima.

Pragmatic.  Gives us tools, teaches us how to use them.

Ability to watch our minds is enhanced with virtue and meditation.  To see things as they arise, abide and pass away.
Without this practice, if we experience anger or jealousy or hurt feeling we may act on them and hurt our loved ones.  Or we might say we shouldn't have these feelings and we suppress it. But with this ability to watch our minds, we are able to know that mood, know that feeling. Oh, this is an angry feeling, this is an irritating feeling. You watch the mood rather than follow the mood. Do-marome.
The more we are able to develop this skill then you are better able to live life harmoniously with others.  That benefits you and those you engage life with, you coworkers, your family, strangers on the road.

Shift from self centered to dhamma centered.

Recognize that feelings, moods, etc are part of nature. This is the result of being born in a human form....
if you are a human being you can experience anger, and jealousy as well as joy and love.
Normally when these things come up we say we have an anger problem or a jealousy problem, I'm a lustful person, a fearful person. We make it personal.
These things in the world, the trees the stones, etccc this is how they are. We did not invent them. I didn't invent kindness or wisdom.
We begin to see these things we experience as patterns of nature 
WE don't achievements in a conceited manner. We don't get self critical. 
We instead realize that if we follow that difficult feeling then us and others will suffer. If we follow generosity, and kindness we benefit and so do others.

We can look at our lives free of self view.
Don't take yourself personally book.
