
Faith and Wisdom balance each other, as do Energy and Concentration. 
The Five Faculties are ‘controlling' faculties because they control or 
master their opposites. The faculties and powers are two aspects of the 
same thing.

Faith (saddha) - controls doubt
Energy/Effort/Persistence (viriya) – controls laziness
Mindfulness (sati); - controls heedlessness
Concentration (samādhi) - controls distraction
Wisdom/Discernment (pañña, prajña) – controls ignorance
