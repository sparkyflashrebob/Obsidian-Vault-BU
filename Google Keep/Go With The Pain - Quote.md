Anne Morrow Lindbergh.
"Go with the pain. Let it take you. Open your palms and your body to the pain.
It comes in waves like a tide. And you must be open as a vessel lying on the beach, letting it fill you up and then retreating, leaving you empty and clear. With a deep breath, it has to be as deep as the pain. One reaches a kind of inner freedom from pain as though the pain were not yours, but your body's.
The spirit leaves the body on the altar."
