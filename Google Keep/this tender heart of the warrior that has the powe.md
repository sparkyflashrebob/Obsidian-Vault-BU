Chogyam Trungpa Rinpoche. "When you awaken your heart, you find to your surprise that your heart is empty. You find you're looking into space. Who are you? Where are you? What is your heart?
If you really look you don't find anything tangible and solid. If you search for the awakened heart, if you put your hand through your rib cage and feel for it, there's nothing there but tenderness. You feel sore and soft, and if you don't open your eyes to the rest of the world, you feel tremendous sadness.
This sadness doesn't come from being mistreated. You don't feel sad because someone has insulted you, or because you feel impoverished. Rather, this experience of sadness is unconditioned. It occurs because your heart is completely open and exposed like pure, raw meat.
Even if a mosquito lands on it, you feel so touched. And yet, it is this tender heart of the warrior that has the power to heal the world."
