Preparatory Stage
- Observe precepts if abstention from killing, stealing, sexual misconduct, lying and devisive speech, intoxicants, and if on retreat taking food afternoon, entertainment/perfumes, using luxurious beds.
4 Protections - think of enlightened one, loving-kindness, loaathsome aspects of the body and death.
Basic Exercise I:  keep your mind on your abdomen.  know the movements of rising and falling of it.  Make a mental note of rising for upward and falling for downward movement while it occurs.  This develops attention, concentration of mind abd ubsught ub contemplation.
Never verbally repeat the words rising and falling.  do not think of the words rising and falling. Make no effort make the abdominal movements more distinct, because this causes fatigue that interferes with the practice.  Just be totally aware of the movements of rising and falling as they occur in the course of normal breathing.
Basic Exercise II:  Other mental activities may occur.  Intentions, ideas, imaginings.  They cannot be disregarded.  A mention note must be made of each as it occurs.
If you imagine something, you must know that you have done so and make a mental note, imagining.   Reflecting, thinking, intending, wandering, going, arriving, meeting, speaking, arguing, seeing.  Until it passes away.  Then return to rise and fall of abdominen.  Likewise if you intend to bent the neck, intending.  if bending, bending.  If straightening the neck, straightening.  Then returning to the rise and fall of the abdominen.
Basic Exercise III:  After a while     you will likely experience the feeling of fatigue or stiffness in the body, arms or legs.  Should this happen, simply keep the knowing mind on that part of the body where such feelings occur and carry on contemplation, noting itred or stiff.  Do this naturally.  Gradually this will become fainter and finally cease altogether.  If this becomes unbbearable then change your position but do not forget to make a mental note of intending, before you proceed to change your psoition.    Each movement must be comtemplated in order and in detail.  Lifting the legs, stretching, bending, etc... as before.  Same with itching, painfull, aching, pressing, piercing, tired giddy.  
This practice is followed while not directly in meditation, doing worldly activities.
