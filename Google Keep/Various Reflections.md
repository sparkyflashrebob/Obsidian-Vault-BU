For Reflection What allows, invites, makes room for enough safety in your life? What does refuge mean to you? How do you feel it and embody it? How can refuge support you in your aspirations and actions? What could you do, who could you envision yourself to be, if you fully felt the benefits of refuge? How is your sense of refuge determined by your cultural experience, your culture of origin, or the cultures of your familial lineage?

Yang, Larry. Awakening Together: The Spiritual Practice of Inclusivity and Community (p. 82). Wisdom Publications. Kindle Edition.
