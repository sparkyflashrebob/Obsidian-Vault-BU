It is a sobering thought that the finest act of love you can perform is not an act of service but an act of contemplation, of seeing. When you serve people, you help, support, comfort, alleviate pain. When you see them in their inner beauty and goodness, you transform and create. • ANTHONY DE MELLO

Brach, Tara. Radical Compassion (p. 169). Penguin Publishing Group. Kindle Edition.
