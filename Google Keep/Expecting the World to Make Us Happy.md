Having the expectation for the world to make us happy.  For conditions to make us happy. People, places, life events. 
Conditionality. 

Where it doesn't work is expecting some ultimate fulfillment through whatever you achieve. If you expect some ultimate fulfillment - ultimate happiness - then you're telling the world - the world in whatever form it manifests to you - you're telling the world make me happy.  You place expectations upon the world that it cannot meet.
