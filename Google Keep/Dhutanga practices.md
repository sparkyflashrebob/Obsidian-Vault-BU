
The 13 Dhutanga practices are:
1. Wearing patched-up robes: pamsukulikanga,
2. Wearing only three robes: tecivarikanga,
3. Going barefeet alms-round: pindapatikanga,
4. Not omitting any house while going for alms: sapadanikanga,
5. Eating at one sitting only: ekasanikanga,
6. Eating only from the alms-bowl: pattapindikanga,
7. Refusing all further food: khalu-paccha-bhattikanga,
8. Living in the forest: Araññikanga,
9. Living at the root of a tree: rukkha-mulikanga,
10. Living in the open air: abbhokasikanga,
11. Living in a cemetery: susanikanga,
12. Being satisfied with whatever dwelling: yatha-santhatikanga,
13. Sleeping in the sitting position (and never lying down): nesajjikanga.
