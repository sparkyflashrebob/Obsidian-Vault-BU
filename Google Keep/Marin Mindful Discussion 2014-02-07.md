-Watchman named Mindfulness on the lookout for when we get carried away in Mindlessness.
- In Theravada called PROMPTED CONSCIOUSNESS

UNFABRICATED MINDFULNESS

- capacity of a mirror to reflect what comes before it.

- something we need to recognize and come back to.

- skillful means to free the mind.
- all these different aspects work in harmony.
