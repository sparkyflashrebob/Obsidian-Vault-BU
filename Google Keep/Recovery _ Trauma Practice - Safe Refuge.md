Recovery Trauma

from Peter Levine

_____Practice________ 

exercise you can do with a group or individual
1. (today or last day)
	think of a time today when you felt less pain or less anxiety
	or when they felt some pleasure or pleasurable
	or a time when you most felt like yourself
	or like yourself that you would like to feel


2. (back a bit further)
	next step
	a different  time in the last day or the last week when you felt the least pain, etc... even by the smallest amount
	this builds with a little pleasure

--------

can't fail with this exercise - you will always get a positive experience

no one feels the same about of pain or anxiety every single moment
aware of that moment that felt good and the moment they are having now

no longer than 2 weeks back
not separating meditation and trauma work - they feed into each other
as a meditative practice
