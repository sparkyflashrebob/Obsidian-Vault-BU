The Four Exertions
Another way we might practice right effort is by practice of the 
The 4 Right Exertions
Abandon the unskillful, develop the skillful

The four exertions (cattārimāni padhānāni) are summarized as:
Restraint (saṃvara padhāna) of the senses. Also known as Guarding the Senses. 
Abandonment (pahāna padhāna) of defilements.
Cultivation (bhāvanā padhāna) of Enlightenment Factors. Also Developing
Preservation (anurakkhaṇā padhāna) of concentration, for instance, using charnel-ground contemplations.   Also Maintaining.

“The Fourfold Right Diligence is nourished by joy and interest. If your practice does not bring you joy, you are not practicing correctly.” – Thich Nhat Hahn
5 Factors of Exertion (that determine our level of effort)
Conviction - how much do we believe in what we are doing
Free from Illness & discomfort and in good health - fit for exertion
Honest - vs avoiding to look at 
Persistent and steadfast
Discerning  - thinking of the lute - aware of suffering
