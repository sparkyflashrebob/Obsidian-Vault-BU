Simple repetitive action can develop calm and a greater sense of here and now
Be aware as you walk
Natural movement
stand firmly planted
hands rest easily
open to your sense of the surroundings
bring attention into the body
center yourself
feel standing on the earth
Walking can be normal based on your physical needs

Start
Begin by noticing the intention to move and then noticing how you move
which foot you start with (helpful to select either all the time)
Pay attention to either the movement, the pressure of feet, the posture, the body, the breathing.
At end of the path notice stopping
Collect your awareness noting if your mind went elsewhere
come back to presence before you intend to turn around
know the movement of turning around (which direction, speed, how body moves)
End

Before proceeding collect attention and start again. 

Addition from MMTCP Notes
Notice not trying to get somewhere or not - quality of mind - speeding
If the mind wanders note "wandering", "thinking", "hearing", "planning"
Come back to presence
Use walking meditation to calm and collect yourself and to live more wakefully in the body
Let it teach you to stay centered and steady as the waves of experience rise and fall with each step
