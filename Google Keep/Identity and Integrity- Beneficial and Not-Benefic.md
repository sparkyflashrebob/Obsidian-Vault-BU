Identity lies at the intersection of diverse forces that make up my life.
Integrity lies in relating to those forces in ways that bring me wholeness and life rather than fragmentation and death.

Identity and integrity can never be fully named or known by anyone, including the person who bears them.

Palmer, Parker J.. The Courage to Teach (Kindle Location 618). Wiley. Kindle Edition.
