Reflect on a "stuck place" where we are getting caught in difficult emotions

The Practice
5 - 10 min of quiet meditation
"R" 
1st Person Reflects on their situation and names the primary emotions
3 minutes
Pause for silence and then Switch

"A" "I" "N"
Individually for the next 10 minutes
"letting be" - willingness to pause and stay present as it is

Allowing - "this too" - "I can hold this"

"I" Felt sense of the experience other than the cognitive or analytical process

What is the worst part of this? What most wants my attention?
• What is the most difficult /painful thing I am believing?
• What emotions does this bring up (fear, anger, grief?)
• Where do I feel these emotions inside?
• What is the felt sense of these emotions as sensations (i.e. clenched, raw, hot, sore,
aching, empty, squeezed)?
• What do I notice when I assume the facial expression and body posture that best reflect these feelings and emotions?

Transition to Nurturing
If the most vulnerable hurting part of me could communicate, what would it express
(words, feelings, images)?
• How does this part want me to be with it?
• What does this part most need (from me or from some larger source of love and
wisdom)?

Nurturing
Breath consciously and adjust posture to fully meet my most awake experience of heart and mind

AFTER THE RAIN
Cease any "doing" and rest in the presence and the hearthspace
