Avoid these 17 mistakes
1- Not mastering the essense of public speaking (tell a story and sell a point)
2- They do not sell. Every speech is a sales presentation.
3-  They build themselves us (Ego no good)
4- They have no clear next step. What do I want my audience to think, feel & do after experiencing my message.
5- They give loose messages. Not tied to anchors. Use anchors for every point.
6- They present with words, words and more words. Speak in images not in words. 
7- They start with a whimper. Start the first 30 seconds with a bang.
8- They force-fit. Too much info in too little time.
9- They don't build their speech on benefits. Speech needs to be built on benefits.
10- They have an "I" or "we" focus.  "you" and "your" should be the focus 80/20.
11- Delivery is not dynamic. 
12- They lip-sync. Power point spoken.
13- They use Ditch-digging introductions. Give people introducing you an introduction that is not just a rehash of your Bio.
14- Audience not get involved.
15- They forget about the floor. Do not use the stage.
16- They do not connect. Match the energy of the audience.
17- Speak for standing ovations instead of invitations.
