1. Let yourself sit in a centered and quiet way.
Breath softly and feel your body, your heartbeat, the life within you.
Feel how you treasure your own life, how you guard yourself in the face of sorrows.

2. Bring to mind someone close to whom you dearly love, picture them and feel your natural caring for them,  notice how you hold them in your heart. Let yourself be aware of their sorrows, suffering in life.  Feel how your heart opens to wish them well, extend comfort, share their pain and meet it with compassion.

Inwardly recite these phrases:
May you be held in compassion
May your pain and sorrow be eased.
May you be at peace.

3. After a few minutes turn your compassion toward yourself.
4. Extend compassion to others you know.
5. Continue to extend to friends, neighbors, your community, all who suffer, to difficult people, to your enemies, and finally to all beings.
