Ajahn Chah taught, “When you have wisdom, contact with experience is like standing at the bottom of a ripe mango tree. We get to choose between the good and rotten mangoes. It is all to your profit, because you know which fruits will make you sick and which are healthy.”

Kornfield, Jack. The Wise Heart (p. 59). Random House Publishing Group. Kindle Edition.
