Right Speech

Right Livelihood

Speech abstaining from 1) telling lies, 2) backbiting and slander, talk that may bring about hatred, disunity, hostility, disharmony amoung individuals or groups of people or 3) harsh, rude, impolite, malicious and abusive language, or idle, useless, foolish babble and gossip.
Naturaly speak the truth - friendly and benevolent, pleasant and gentle, meaningful and useful.

Right Action - promoting moral, honorable and peaceful conduct - abstain from destroying life, stealing, dishonesty, improper sexual intercourse, help others to lead a peaceful and honorable life.

Right Livelihood - profession that does not harm others, weapons, intoxicants/poisons, killing animals, cheating, etc... profession that is honorable, blameless and innocent of harm to others. 

Goal is to promote with these at promoting happy and harmonious life for individual and society.  No spiritual development without this.
