Albert Einstein once wrote that “Buddhism has the characteristics of what would be expected in a cosmic religion for the future: it transcends a personal God, avoids dogmas and theology; it covers both the natural and the spirirual, and it is based on a religious sense aspiring from the experience of all things, natural and spiritual, as a meaningful unity.”
