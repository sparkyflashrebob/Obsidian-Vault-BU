
Elder's Meditation of the Day - September 29    

"So I prayed, but I had to pray from my heart. All of my
concentration and thoughts went from my head to my heart.
All of my senses - hearing, smell, taste, and feeling - were
connected to my heart."        
-- Wallace Black Elk, LAKOTA  
    
The heart is the gateway to the Unseen World, to the Spirit World. It takes real
concentration to do this. To connect to our own heart is also a mental state. It
starts in the head and transitions to the heart. This mental state is our inner
stillness. Be still and know. This place of the heart is very joyous and peaceful.
It is this place that we become one with God, our Creator.

Great Spirit, teach me to be a heart warrior.
