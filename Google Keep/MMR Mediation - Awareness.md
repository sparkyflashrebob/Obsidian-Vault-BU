We start by getting both the body and mind ready for meditation.
Setting an intention to be present for the next twenty or so minutes. Taking a position either sitting in a chair or on the floor on a cushion or lying down.  Letting the awareness settle in the body. Being aware of the body, aware of the feet on the floor, awareness of the pressure on the sit bones, letting go of any obvious tension. 
Letting this awareness fill the whole body.

For the purpose of this meditation, I would like you to use this awareness as your object.
You don't have to ignoring anything that you hear, or that you feel.  Just don't pay attention to them for the moment.
Likewise allowing thinking, be it thinking of the past or thinking of the future put them aside for the next few minutes. To be just here - to be present in awareness. Not holding onto anything. Being in the knowing of things as they are.
Resting naturally in the knowing.  


In the last few moments we will bring in some kindness into our practice.

May you be filled with loving-kindness.

May you be safe from inner and outer dangers.

May you be well in body and mind.

May you be happy and at ease.
