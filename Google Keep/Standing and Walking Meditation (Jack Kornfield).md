Lying Down Meditation Suggestions:
Keep Your Eyes Open if you can
Eyes Close Body thinks It is time to go to bed
Draw in knees so you are not completely flat
Be aware of hands falling down

Walking Meditation Suggestions:
Balanced energy 
Walk at whatever speed keeps you the most present
Open to sensation of body - the senses
When oriented where you are then into the feet and connect to the earth
Steps as an anchor instead of the breath optional
Let the feet kiss the earth
Pause when thinking - notice here is a thought - feel the earth and begin again
