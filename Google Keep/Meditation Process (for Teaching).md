Base
Sitting - alert - relaxed 
Close eyes - establish mindfulness - inventory - soften heart - face - shoulders
Take full breaths - natural - where easiest - pleasurable - 
Home Base - breath - sensations in hands/feet/posture/whole body
Mindful - return home

Mindfulness of the Body
Scan Body - notice strong sensations that arise- allow home base to recede for the moment
Inquire about sensations in body
hot/cold, tingling, twisting, solid, flowing?
pleasant/unpleasant/neither ?
Arise and fading away
Return to home
If difficult - use breath as an aid -labeling - RAIN what happening - can I be with - 
Return to home - place of strength & refuge
Open to include difficult - ocean/sky

Mindfulness of Emotions
Mindful of any emotions that call your attention (arising)
Same loving awareness as the breath
Labeling
Investigation - how respond to your awareness - you are space of loving awareness
Any resistence? - notice the story - mindful - return to the emotion (temporary home)
Emotion fade away return to body/breath home

Mindfulness of Thoughts
Begin with 1-minute counting exercise

Restart meditation with base
Notice the stream of thoughts in the background
Label
Investigate - notice when mindful the affect - notice patterns of thought
Return home
If return again label - notice in body - space between thoughts - 
Open sky - thoughts clouds floating thru

Metta Phrase

Mindful of what comes up/resistence (self?)
After a few minutes 2nd Person
Really feel the love that you are nurturing
Offer to Self
Offer to Benefactor
Offer to others - evoke loving awareness towards them- difficult people 
Offer to community
Offer to all people, animals, earth
