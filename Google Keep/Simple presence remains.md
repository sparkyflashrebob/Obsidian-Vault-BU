As Pema Khandro Rinpoche explains,
When things fall apart, we can only be as we are. Pretense and striving fall away, and life becomes starkly simple. The value of
such moments is this: we are shown that the game can be given up and that when it is, the emptiness we feared, emptiness of the
void, is not what's there. What is there is the bare fact of being. 
Simple presence remains.
