Spiritual Friendship
Prioritize relationship as a path to liberation
tend towards individualism and isolation

As a teacher - shoulder to shoulder
Buddha referred to himself as kalayan mita

1. one who gives what is hard to give.  (generosity) 
2. someone who does what is hard to do (wise effort) 
3. one who endures what it's hard to endure (stability, endurance, being fortified, high quality) 
4. one who tells you their secrets (open and transparent)  
5. one who keeps your secrets
6. one who, when misfortune strikes, they don't abandon you (loyalty and commitment) 
7. one who, when you're down and out, will not look down on you. (equity - never lose sight of the intrinsic and inherent goodness and decency of others)
