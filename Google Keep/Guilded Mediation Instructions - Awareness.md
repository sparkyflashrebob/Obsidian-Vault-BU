Reflect on what your purpose is being here. Set intention to be present. (mind)
Letting go of everything else become aware of the body sitting or lying down or whatever position it is in. (body)
Make any adjustments necessary to let go of any obvious stress in the body. Establishing both a position that both relaxed but alert.
Having set the mind and body in position open to the awareness of what is present. Awareness of the feeling of the body on the earth, awareness of sounds, awareness of thoughts.  
Not holding onto the feelings of the body, not holding onto our awareness of sounds, not holding onto thoughts - being aware of the awareness of these things as they come and go.
If you notice that you have become swept away by any feelings or emotions or thoughts you can gently acknowledge or note them mentally as feelings, feelings, or sadness/sadness, or planning/planning and then returning to this vast and open awareness. Not clinging to anything in the world.
