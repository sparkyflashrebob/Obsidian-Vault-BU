Mind to the Breath - everything happening here
still as possible so you catch site of all these important things happening. 
Most of the time we just see breath goes in and breath goes out. Automatic pilot. We miss. But when we meditate we stop to look at the process.

When you have the mind on the breath you have all 4 foundations here. Body, feeling, mind-state and MENTAL QUALITIES.

Watching for how the mind shapes things.  If you want to look for ignorance you do not have to look very far.  Look at the fabrications of the mind.
Principle of Dependant Co-arising says fabrications are conditioned by ignorance.  It is right where the mind fashions things. Fashioning body sensations and mental events.
From Fabrication comes consciousness, and consciousness leads to name and form: physical and mental phenomena.
