
We will be talking about "TANHA" in more detail when we explore the Four Noble Truths but I'd like to identify the three types of TANHA as it relates to craving.  

There is Kama-tanha which is a craving for sense pleasures.  It is suggested that these sense-pleasures include such things as wealth and power, ideas and ideals, views, opinions, theories, conceptions, and beliefs.

There is Bhava-tanha, which is a craving for being, to be something, to unite with an experience.   
And there is Vibhana-tanha which is the opposite or the craving for non-existence.
