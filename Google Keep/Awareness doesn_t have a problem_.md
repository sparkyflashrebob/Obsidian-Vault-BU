Awareness has already been open to your experience because it is happening. Awareness doesn't have a problem. In order to have a problem, the mind has to resist.
Awareness doesn't suffer, awareness cannot suffer because to suffer it has to say "I don't like this". 
Suffering is the thought and the feeling that I don't like this.
- Rupert Spira
