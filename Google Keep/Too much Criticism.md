4 Ways to project

1. Establish boundaries - how you want to be treated.  When shutting down practice with breath until you can leave situation. You do not have to consume. Mindful Consumption.
2. You are not the cause. Confrontation does not define you.  People want to create their safety ahead of yours. Attack to prevent attacks.
3. Don't feed confrontation.   "no fuel, no fire"  Se confrontations as waves of someone else's storm.
4. Give yourself the compassion you deserve.  Not respond by being hurtful.  Know you are not suppressing your feelings and fear.  

Mindfulness - observe you feelings, they are valid.
Common humanity: Know that it is normal to feel the way that you do. You are not alone with what you are feeling, be it anger, fear or any other emotion.
Self-kindness.
