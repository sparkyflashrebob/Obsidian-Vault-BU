Hello my name is ____________. I serve this meeting as your Teleservice Rep.

Marin Teleservice is a group of volunteers who answer the phones after hours for the still suffering alcoholic.

If you would like to be of service. We have shifts available to answer the phone from the comfort of your own phone.

To sign up for a shift or to become a 12th step worker go to www.marinteleservice.com and fill in the form, or see me after the meeting for more information.

Marin Teleservice Committee meets on the fourth Tuesday of each month, 7:30 pm, at the Alano Club 1360 Lincoln Avenue Ave. San Rafael,CA.

All Meeting Reps are encouraged to attend.

“New Meeting Rep” Orientation is at 7:00pm to 7:20pm in the front room of the Alano Club also on the 4th Tuesday of each month.

Thank you.
