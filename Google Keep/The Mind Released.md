Consciousness without feature,
         without end,
     luminous all around:
Here water, earth, fire, and wind
      have no footing.
Here long and short
     coarse and fine
     fair and foul
     name and form
are all brought to an end.
With the cessation of [the activity of]
     consciousness
       each is here brought to an end.

(Digha Nikaya 11, trans. Thanissaro Bhikkhu)
