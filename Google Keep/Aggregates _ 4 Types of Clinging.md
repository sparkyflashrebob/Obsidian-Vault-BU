Physical Form - rupa- the body
Feelings- vedena- pleasant unpleasant 
Perception- sanna- memory - labels- categories - eat mate run.
Mental Formations - Sankhara - complex - thought, emotion, volition/intention- like anger, loving-kindness, and mindfulness
Consciousness - Buddhism not psychology - senses of sight, sound, taste, touch, tactile, mind

1- sensual desire - gratification - seeking
2- Precepts & Practices- if only I did this I'd figure it out- Issue is clinging and the aspect itself 
3- Views- opinions, perspectives, right & wrong- fixated on a particular stance
4- Assumption of Self- attachment to the presumption- no particular way to know - chariot or thing- take away parts and it no longer is it. Where did it go? Keeps us in suffering

HOW TO VIEW AGGREGATES 

Form - foam - collected shell
Feeling - bubbles - transitory
Perception - mirage
Mental Formations - banana tree - keep peeling off layers
Consciousness - Magical trick - appear and disappear - sight, sounds, etc
