Stop learning. Start knowing.

If your knowledge of fire has been turned to certainty by words alone, then seek to be cooked by the fire itself. Don't abide in borrowed certainty. There is no real certainty until you burn. If you wish for this, sit down in the fire.

The Cure for pain is in the pain.

He who has led you so far will guide you further.

Let yourself be silently drawn by the strange pull of what you really love. It will not lead you astray.

The lamps are different but the light is the same.

The moon stays bright when it doesn't avoid the night.

We carry inside us, the wonders we seek outside us.

If everything around you seems dark, look again, you may be the light.

I am a spark from the infinite.

The pains you feel are messengers. Listen to them.

There is a voice that doesn't use words. Listen.

The desire to know your soul will end all other desires.

Listen to Silence. It has so much to say.

Knock, And He will open the door. Vanish, And He will make you shine like the sun. Fall, And He will raise you to the heavens. Become nothing, And He will turn you into everything.

Whenever you are alone, remind yourself, that God has sent everyone away, so that there is only you and Him.

Know that one day, your pain will become your cure.

Look for the answer inside the question.

As you start to walk on the way, the way appears.

Your heart knows the way. Run in that direction.

Don't grieve. Anything you lose comes round in another form.

Let the waters settle and you will see the moon and the stars mirrored in your own being.

The wound is the place where the light enters you.

Sell your cleverness and buy bewilderment.

It is your road and yours alone. Others may walk it with you but no one can walk it for you.

The work is always inside you. This knot does not get untied by listening to the stories of other people. The well inside your house is better water than the river that runs through the town.

Be like a tree and let the dead leaves drop.

Don't be satisfied with stories, how things have gone with others. Unfold your own myth.

The quieter you become, the more you are able to hear.

Silence is the language of God. All else is poor translation.

We are stars wrapped in skin. The light you have been seeking, has always been within.

Your task is not to seek love but to seek and find all the barriers within yourself that you have built against it.

Only from the heart can you touch the sky.

Appear as you are. Be as you appear.

Remember... The entrance to the sanctuary is inside you.
