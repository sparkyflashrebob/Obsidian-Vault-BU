You might find it easier to believe what I say - it's easier than finding out for yourself - but believing what I say is not going to nourish you. The wisdom that I use in my life is nourishing me only. It might encourage you to use wisdom, but you have to actually eat the food yourself to be nourished, rather than believe what I say.

-Ajahn Sumedho
