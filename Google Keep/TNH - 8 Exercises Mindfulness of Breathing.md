
Exercises
The 1st 4 are Body-Centered Practice
2nd 4 are with feelings

1.  Aware of in-breath and out-breath (simple)(effect great) pay attention in-breath only - release everything else - past & future - knowing this - you become free - regret sorrow with past - fear and uncertainty for future - awareness centered on that - freedom - not under influence of anger and fear - pleasant. affect great
2. follow in-breath and out-breath all the the way thru - beginning to end  - no interruption - cultivate concentration - energy  - pleasant - do not have to suffer - can do this anytime
3. aware of your body - breathing in aware - home in the body - established in the here and now - fully alive - oneness of body and mind together realized - forget body - alive when the mind is with the body - 
4. breathing in I calm the body - release the tension - may notice tension - like body to have more peace - out-breath allow the tension to be released.  These 4 exercises help the body to be peaceful and help us take care of it.
5. With the 5th Rhelm of Feeling - Generate a feeling of joy - conditions of happiness already available - we are very lucky - not run into the future - Living happily in the present moment - life available only in the present moment - so many conditions are available in this moment - Anapindika business man - not look for happiness in the future - creating that joy is the art of happiness - 
6. Generate Happiness - slight difference to joy and happiness - with joy there is some excitement - with happiness we are calmer - buddhist story of someone thirsty in the desert - suddenly sees and oasis  (trees encircling a pond) - experiences joy as a result.  Has not drank the water yet.   Still thirsty . but with some excitement and hope.  ONce they have arrived and drank the water, that is happiness - very fulfilling.  We will want to learn to make use of mindfulness & concentration to bring ourselves joy and happiness - to nourish ourselves and healing - the conditions for these are already sufficient inside of us. 
7. Aware of painful feeling and emotions - does not try to fight the pain or cover it up or run away - generate energy of mindfulness to embrace the pain gingerly - I will take care of you.  (compassion) be there for it - no fighting - no violence to our suffering - holding a crying baby /being the mother - have to be there for it - breath and walk energy of mindfulness to be with and embrace it - 2 layers of conscious (lower is stored the seed of anger & fear - comes up into the mind - mental formation of anger comes up) - breath invite the seed of mindfulness to come up - also in the stored area - touch lightly and that energy will come up - mindfulness seed will grow as we practice - 2 things for mindfulness - simple recognizing the pain - not fighting 2nd is to embrace
8. Calm the pain - mother holding baby - does not know cause but recognizing and holding will help the suffering right away.  We should learn how to recognize feelings, embrace and deal with them whether they are pleasant or unpleasant.

5 & 6 is the art of happiness - how to generate joy and happiness
7 & 8 the art of suffering - how to take care of suffering/painful feeling & emotion

with the exercises that follow you can go further you can transform pain and sorrow into something more positive.

We are not afraid of pain.  Does not try to run away.
