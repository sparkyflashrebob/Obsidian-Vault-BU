So with your eyes closed, take a moment to utterly and completely relax the breath. 
Can you feel the forehead smooth? 
And could you relax your tongue in a way that it fills your lower jaw?
And could you relax the root of your tongue down into your throat, and at the same time, to feel the whole mechanism of the jaw relaxing?
And could you soften the lower back and the belly? 
Can you feel the palms and the soles of the feet? 

Is there a sense, in this final minute, anything that could relax or soften?
Notice the quality of presence. 
And as you're ready, deepening the breath. 
And when you're ready, you can gently open your eyes.
