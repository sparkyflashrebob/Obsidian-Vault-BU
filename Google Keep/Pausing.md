When we can't get what we want - dukkha
Pause
reactions are normal
read them
learn what they are
learn that they take into suffering
No need to guess
pause is not disapproval or judgement
opening of attention
allows us to respond with mindfulness and compassion
essential, deep and accessible
note affects of losing temper or following impulses
have face
bhava tanha - craving to become
mindful of breathing
at the end of each inhale and exhale - the will to let go
trust letting go into the next moment or of what to do, or of who you are
a growing sense of release
