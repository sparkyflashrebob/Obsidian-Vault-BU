
dukkha-dukkha – the suffering of painful feelings

vipariṇāmadukkha – the unsatisfactoriness inherent in the experience of change

Saṅkhāra-dukkhatā: the suffering which is inherent within all saṅkhāra, all things which arise from determinants; specifically, the five khandhas/aggregates of form, feeling, perception, mental formations and consciousness.

avijjā-taṇhā-upādāna

natural attribute assumes a psychological significance when it is recognized that conditions are incapable of producing any perfect contentment, and as such will cause suffering for anybody who tries to cling to them.
