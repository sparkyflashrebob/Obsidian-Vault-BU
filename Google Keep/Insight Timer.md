
AMOR Fati 
The practice is catch and release to find peace. 
Note and let pass any thoughts, sensations, 
feelings, or emotons pass without any kind of judgement. There states of consciousness of peace and joy, beyond that of "selfing". I have been focusing on concentrations practicing the Jhanas. Once settled, there is joy, then equanimity that one can take back into daily life. 
Life is difficult to comprehend and living is effort-full, by being fully present we can all be more compassionate, loving and kind with all living things. May all beings be happy, peaceful and free from suffering.
