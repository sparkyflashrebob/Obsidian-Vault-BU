Bare Knowing and the Continuity of Mindfulness 
Continuity created by momentum of previous moments of mindfulness. Whatever we repeatedly practice begins to arise more and more spontaneously; 
Insight: Purification of View. In every moment knowing and its object arise simultaneously. We begin to see that everything that we call self is simply this pairwise progression of knowing and object, arising and passing moment after moment.
We build this momentum of mindfulness very simply. Basic instructions. We can start with some primary object of attention, such as mindfulness of the breath or the sitting posture. Bri going our attention back to the meditation object as much as required.
Perception: strong perception is one of the proximate causes for mindfulness to arise. 
use of concepts - contracts bare knowing - we want to establish mindfulness to the extent necessary for bare knowing, which somehow suggests a mind free from conceptual overlay. And on the other hand, the factor of perception, with its attendant concepts, is itself a proximate cause for mindfulness to arise.
