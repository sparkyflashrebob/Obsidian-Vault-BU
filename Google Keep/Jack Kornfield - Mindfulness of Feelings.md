1st step - know feelings in themselves - curiostity
pain as pain, sadness as sadness
gradually become aware of your waves of feelings as the rise and fall
aware you are not your emotions
everchanging feeling and emotions are simply part of the dance of life
emotions not the problem
relationship to them is the problem
bring mindful and kind attention
focusing on difficult emotions can become one-sided
notice happy ones as well as the painful
joy and sorrow - can't have one without the other
ever changing like a river
when not aware can become lost and frightened by them
create space to hold them with mindfulness and wisdom
anger has some truth but also some delusion
rest in loving awareness
know you are not limited by what is arising in the river
grace that comes when we are willing to touch the full measure of feeling, fears, longing, failure, tendernour and great love that inside everyone of us
become the loving witness
The one who knows
as we allow our feelings to be met in the space of loving awareness, where they can come and go, we become free.
