
I know it is difficult to imagine that the notions of “desire” and “will” are applicable to a single lonely cell.


Deprived of conscious knowledge, deprived of access to the byzantine devices of deliberation available in our brains, the single cell seems to have an attitude: it wants to live out its prescribed genetic allowance. Strange as it may seem, the want, and all that is necessary to implement it, precedes explicit knowledge and deliberation regarding life conditions, since the cell clearly has neither.
