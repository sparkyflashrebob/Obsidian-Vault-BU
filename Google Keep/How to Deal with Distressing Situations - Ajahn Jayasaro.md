1. Having a Sense of Readiness
	If we have unrealistic expectations about a situation or people 
	then frustrated then that is on us.
	However, if your approach is that life is not sure, fluid, ever changing and can change in ways that are agreeable or disagreeable, there is this sense of readiness that that can change and we are not stuck on that. According to causes and conditions. The impact is much less severe.

2.  Chants - I am of the nature to age, sickness, dye, everything that I love and is near and dear to me I will become separated from me.
	Recall and investigate every day.
	This how you protect yourself or prepare yourself.

3.  If in a situation and something comes at you that  powerful and distressing is to bring your mindfulness to the body and in the sensations from your head to your toes.  Feeling the impact of what is happening physically, rather than allowing the mind just to follow the usual habitual reactions. 

When you do that, when you feel the tightness in the chest or the belly, make friends with that. 
Lovingkindness to the feelings, breath normally, sensation to be there. Let them drain away naturally.
