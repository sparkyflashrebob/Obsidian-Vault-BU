Sanditthiko, Akaliko, Ehipassiko, Opanayiko. Paccattham veditabbo vinnuhi
Sanditthiko=here and now
akaliko=timeless
ehipassiko=come see for yourself, encouraging investigation
Opanayiko=inclining inward
paccattham veditabbo vinnuhi= known thru direct expereince

apparent here and now, timeless, encourages investigation in this moment, leads inward, to be experienced by each of us using wisdom.
