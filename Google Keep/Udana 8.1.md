There is that sphere of being 
where there is no earth, 
no water, no fire, nor wind; 
no experience of infinity of space, of infinity of consciousness, of no-thingness, 
or even of neither-perception-nor-non-perception; 
here there is neither this world nor another world, 
neither moon nor sun; 
this sphere of being I call neither a coming   
nor a going nor a staying still, 
neither a dying nor a reappearance; 
it has no basis, no evolution, and no 
support: it is the end of dukkha. (ud. 8.1)
