His recommendation is to know the mind-storm and to also recognize it being different from the space from which it appears. The formless witness that is witnessing.  Noticing the noise and the space from which it comes. The noise will come and go but the space will not move, that is the space of awareness - consciousness. He then reminds us to see that the one who is suffering the mind-storm and the mind-storm itself - that they are connected. Without the me there is no storm.
