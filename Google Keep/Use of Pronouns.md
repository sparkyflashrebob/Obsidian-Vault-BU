What are Pronouns?

In everyday communications, pronouns are often used in place of people’s names. These pronouns have historically been associated with gender such as using “he” when referring to a male or “she” when referring to a female. Society is learning that one of the most basic ways to show respect for individual identities is to not assume each other’s pronouns, but to acknowledge how someone identifies and honor their individual identity.

​​​​​​​If you do not feel strongly about your own pronouns, it is OK. If you do not know someone else’s pronouns, that is OK, too. You are not going to know everyone’s pronouns however, to show respect when you are uncertain of someone’s pronouns, there are a few things you can do:

When you meet someone, simply ask them which pronouns they use.
When introducing yourself, state your name and the pronouns you use and ask, “What about you?”
Use gender neutral pronouns such as they/them/their when you do not know the gender of the person you are referring to.
Do your research – learn about the various pronouns used and ways to incorporate them in your everyday communications.
