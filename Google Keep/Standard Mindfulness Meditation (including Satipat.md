Taking your seat or lying down - setting your body erect so that it is both alert and at the same time relaxed - withdraw your attention away from the world to the here and now. 
bringing awareness into your body.
Noticing that you are being supported by the mother earth and by the earth element that is your skeletal structure. Knowing that you can relax into your being without worry. 
If you are feeling any initial tension in the body - purposely letting go of any tension that you can - particularly in the shoulders and in the neck.
Coming to rest in a position that is as comfortable as is possible at the moment again bringing attention to this fathom long body.


Being aware of the body breathing wherever it makes itself known - either at the diaphragm or at the lungs or at the back of the throat or at the tip of nostrils as cool air coming in and warm air going out, cool air coming in - warm air going out.

Being aware of the rhythm of breath - not trying to control it - but allowing the breath to breathe itself.

Other than the sensation of your breath, you might feel sensations in other parts of your body - you might feel pain, you might feel tingling, whatever it is - just notice it -  note if it is a pleasant sensation - note if it is an unpleasant sensation - you might be aware of the feeling of neither pleasant or unpleasant sensations - just be aware.
Riding the rhythm of breath - expand your awareness to the whole body. Using the breathing to nurture and sustain the body.

You might have thoughts arise - thoughts of planning - thoughts of seeing your loved ones - any thoughts that arise.  Or their might we anxiousness, or fear, or sleepiness.  Being aware of the mind and mental qualities.  If you can - when they arise you can give a gentle bow to them - reminding yourself that you are here to meditate and for spiritual practice - gently bringing yourself back to this body and this breath.  

Returning back to the anchor of your body and breath over and over again.
