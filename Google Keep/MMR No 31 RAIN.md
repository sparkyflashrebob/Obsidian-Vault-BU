
I'd like to re-introduce the Western teaching on dealing with the Hindrances, which is the RAIN Method first introduced by Michele McDonald about 20 years ago but recently championed by Tara Brach. 

There have been two primary forms of the RAIN acronym circulating in the meditation circles. 

In the former version of [[RAIN]], the acronym for RAIN meant:
[[Recognize]] what is happening (roots of understanding)
[[Allow]]  life to be just as it is (grounds of love)
[[Investigate]] with kindness (deepens understanding and care)
[[Non-Identification]] (realizing freedom from narrow identity)

In the updated version of RAIN, the acronym for RAIN means:
Recognize what is happening (again this root of understanding)
Allow life to be just as it is (grounds of love)
Investigate with gentle attention (deepens understanding)
[[Nurture]] (awakens love)
After the RAIN (realizing freedom from narrow identity)

The explanation is that in the former version, the I-Investigate, included an [[attitude of care]], but we’ve found that the RAIN process is more transformative when N-Nurture is [[intention]]ally engaged as a full step on its own.

The essence of a healing attention is [[understanding and love]]. In the updated version of RAIN above, each of these qualities is directly cultivated.

You might wonder how the Non-Identification step from the former version fits into the new version. When you’ve completed the active steps of RAIN, it’s important to simply notice your own presence and rest in that wakeful, tender space of awareness. In the same way that the earth blossoms following a spring shower, after RAIN, realization naturally arises as to our true nature. We are no longer identified with passing states like fear or anger; we are free to inhabit the wholeness of our Being.

As you can see from this description, using RAIN can be a valuable tool for suppressing the Hindrances which clears and calms the mind, and removes the obstacles which prevent us from seeing clearly.
