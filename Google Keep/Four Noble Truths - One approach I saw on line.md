- 4 Noble Truths - The true problems that we all face, the true causes of them, what it would be like if there were a true stopping of them so that we didn't have these problems any more, and the way of understanding, acting, and so on, that would bring about this stopping of our problems.
- Our True Problems - Practical Approach - Causes - honest - deep look - not easy process - painful - denial - not admit -
- True Causes of Our Problems - causes with many factors and many circumstances deepest cause is our confusion about reality.  It is our own actions..... Worrying all of the time....  What can we do?  Turning things over - see Removal of Distracting Thoughts  -- Problem of never being satisfied
- True Stopping of Our Problems --  get rid of the causes -- get rid of the fuel their will be no fire -- get rid so they do not come back -- not just relief - not just a holiday -- not just accept your problems and live with them - then we feel helpless - we give up and don't even try. -- The effort to try is important -
- Methods of Stopping Our Problems - the path and training - discipline, develop concentration -  have correct understanding otherwise known as wisdom.
