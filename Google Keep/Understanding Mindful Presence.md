Comments:
Taking a seat with mindful presence
- Extend to other postures, standing, walking, in all we do
- As Jack calls it an "Invitation to dignity and graciousness" 
- to well-being 
- to the possibility of presence just where you are 
- in all aspects of life.

Like with the Lottery 
- you have to play to win 
- here - we would say that you have to to be present to win.
- We can train to tune into be more present

Two aspects 
- at least initially
	- awareness - seeing what is present
	- lovingkindness - a loving presence 
	- not judging 
	- or trying to manipulate or evaluating

From this place of mindful presence a wise response will be born

Other names:
- presence, 
- loving awareness, 
- the art of living, 
- to be present for your life 
	- and not in fantasies of the future or the reruns of the past 
- to be present in the moment - which is all we really have.
- sensitive to the present, 
- immediacy, 
- aliveness.

The Training: 
- calm and quiet the mind, 
- open the heart, 
- learn to work with different energies that we experience and to be able to respond wisely.

There is this STORY OF DIANE FOSSEY 
- HER TEACHER - 
- How did he get so close to the gorillas
- "HE DIDN'T BRING A GUN."

We can use this same approach to training in mindfulness 
- not judging, 
- not trying to blame, 
- not trying to change the way things are
- Take a seat in the mystery 
- allowing yourself to see and hononr what there is to be understood. 
- which begins my honoring our own bodies.

BRIEF OVERVIEW OF BODY, FEELINGS, MIND, MENTAL OBJECTS

Encounter what is present

Sometimes you will encounter the unfinished business of the heart
- grief, 
- sadness, 
- longing,
- angst 
- but also creativity, 
- love.  

All of this is Perfect for learning of the power of steadying attention and loving awareness 
- we can train ourselves to become a refuge in at all times and under any circumstance

It is not only bringing our awareness to lifes difficulites but also one that emphasises the joy and ease that can be brought in as well, to well-being, not as an obligation but with a spaciousness of your well-being to grow.

Learning to take the mindful pause where ever you are 
- to shift the space 
- opening to well-being 
- not about getting around life 
- to be with whatever is happening  
- to say yes 
- not to bypassing 
- but to a presence of heart, mind and body, unified 
- aware of all conditions in their changing manner.

This is a gift for yourself and for all of those around you. 

Not making our burdens more difficult and having courage to meet things as they are.  
To be the light for ourselves and for others.
We are that light.
