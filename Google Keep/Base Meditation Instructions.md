
Taking your seat in an upright and yet comfortable way.
When you are ready - close your eyes. And if not comfortable with that then gently lower and soften your gaze.
Take a few deeper cleansing breaths then let the breath settle into its natural rhythm.
Notice if there is any obvious tension that you can release easily.
Let the eyes and face be soft.
--
Relax the shoulders and let the arms and hands rest easily. 
And let the belly be soft and the breath natural.
--
Let the heart be soft as well - to receive whatever arises with kindness and compassion
----
Now with this embodied presence - bring a kind and gentle awareness to the breath.
You can notice it as coolness in the nostrils (cold air coming in and warm air going out), or as tingling in the back of the throat, or as the rise and fall of the chest or belly - wherever you find it easiest to be relaxed yet alert.
Feel each breath and as you allow it, invite a sense of calm and ease to grow
--
Return gently to the breath whenever you notice the attention has wandered - Wandering is a natural process - this is what the mind does - 
-- longer pause
For now let all other experiences, sounds, sensations, thoughts, and images rise and fall like waves of the ocean around the breath.
--
Know that you are the space of loving awareness that can kindly notice any experience as it arises and passes away. Tune in to this knowing quality of presence whatever your experience is.
