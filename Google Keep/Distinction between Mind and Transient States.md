“Tan Ajahn Mun said they're merely states. Through not
understanding that point we take them to be real, to be the mind itself. In fact they're all just transient states. As soon as he said that, things suddenly became clear. Suppose there's happiness present in the mind; it’s a different kind of thing, it’s on a different level, to the mind itself. If you see that then you can stop, you can put things down. When conventional realities are seen for what they are then it’s ultimate truth. Most people lump everything together as the mind itself, but actually, there are states of mind together with the knowing of them. If you understand that point then there's not a lot to do.”
