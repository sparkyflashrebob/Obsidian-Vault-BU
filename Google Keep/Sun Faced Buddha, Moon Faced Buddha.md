Sun Faced Buddha, Moon Faced Buddha
-Zen Koan

Buddha Sun - lived for 1000 years, Moon - live for a day
Moon Faced Buddha - Life is a succession of moments.  Start over again and again. 
Each in-hale breath like a new life. Every exhale a new death.
Dhama is reality.  Not a philosophy or speculation.
