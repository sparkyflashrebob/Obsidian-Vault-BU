Ānanda, if you were to recite to the mendicant [[Girimānanda]] these [[ten perceptions]], it’s possible that after hearing them his illness will die down on the spot.
What ten? The perceptions of impermanence, not-self, ugliness, drawbacks, giving up, fading away, cessation, dissatisfaction with the whole world, non-desire for all conditions, and mindfulness of breathing.
