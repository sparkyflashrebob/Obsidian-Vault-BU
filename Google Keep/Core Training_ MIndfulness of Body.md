Sitting - alert- with the spine not stiff - so somewhat relaxed
if you wish you can gently close your eyes- establishing a sense of presence in the body 
allow your awareness to scan the body - soften and release obvious tension
-- long pause
take a few breaths - and come to natural breathing --- 
bring attention to where you detect the breath easiest or the breath  that is most pleasurable
set this as you home base - remembering this place of ease and alerness as your anchor in the present moment
--SP
if the breath is not comfortable to you at this moment - find another anchor in the sensations in hands or in your posture or using the body as a whole
-- keep this relaxed and interested attention - noticing the sensation of the breath or chosen anchor
--25 sec
notice where your attention is now - and if you have noticed that the mind has wandered off - gently bring back it back to the in and out of the breath or to your chosen anchor
offering a relaxed, wakeful presence
--40 sec
if you notice any sensations that call your attention, allow the breath or your anchor to recede into the background for the moment and bring a curious kind presence to the sensations
--SP
What do they feel like?
--SP
Aware of heat -cold - tingling - ache -twisting - stabbing - vibrating - moving
Pleasant or Unpleasant?  As you notice do they become more intense or dissipate?
Notice how they change.
--SP
if the sensation fades away - then return back to your anchor
--1 min
If you finding sensation to be difficult - breathe with them - let the breath find balance - 
You might name the sensation - tight - ache - heat - pressure - not trying to find the right words - 
Notice what word arises - repeat in soft tone 5% - 95% on actual experience
--2.5 min
You might ask - what is happening inside me right now?  Noticing any sensations.
Asking - Can I be with this?
Directly feel the flow of sensation - letting life be just as it is
-- 1 min
Returning home - offer a calm and steady attention - if sensations still call you, let your primary anchor recede bringing full attention to what is present - naming and offering presence - and if nothing is calling your attention continuing to rest in the knowing of your being with you home base - relaxed and alert.
-- 1.5 min
Sensing the breath in the foreground - aware of the field of sensation - training to be centered, balanced and present for the breath and the world of sensation - wisely aware of this changing life.
