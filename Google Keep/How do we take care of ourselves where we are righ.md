If Overwhelmed - If you need to you can open your eyes - feel you feet on the floor - hold a physical object in your hand - hand on heart - take a deep breath - whatever you need to do to stay present.  - Something steady that you can turn your full attention too. What you can pay attention to in this minute.  Resting from the busyness.  Staying Steady and mindful. Enjoying every precious moment as it unfolds.

Titrating practice - from object of meditation to difficulty to object of meditation to difficult (with loving mindful awareness).

At first more emotional - defending some  - 2nd time around more neutral - not thoughts coming up
Not falling into them.  Noticing what they are like

Meditation - steadying ourselves - mindful - resting with the object
Noticing this precious moment of life
