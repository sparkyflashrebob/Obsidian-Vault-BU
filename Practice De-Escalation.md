Practice De-escalation

If you are confronted by someone who is angry or agitated (such as a belligerent customer), practice de-escalation techniques, such as:

- Speaking in a calm voice
- Responding in an empathetic and non-judgmental tone
- Keeping your distance and not crowding the person
- Keeping your body language neutral

These techniques may allow you to keep the situation from escalating while you find a safe way to exit.
