- Self-oscillation is where a filter creates its own sine wave with a frequency equal to that of the set cut-off point. By then adjusting the filter’s cut-off, it is possible to tune the sine wave to any frequency. In the early days of synthesis, this provided a solution for the synthesizers that didn’t feature a sine wave. Today, self-oscillation isn’t as important since sine waves are now common, but it can still be useful for creating sound effects.
