- Our Realms of Existence ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hddeb7mj4edprggfvzj1xjbe))
- A human being can be characterized as a composite of four fundamental, parallel, entwined realms of existence that reflect our evolutionary past and account for our present ways of being. These are: biological, neurobiological, cognitive, and conscious. All four are, deep down, biological. But the neurobiological realm transcends the *mere* biological, the cognitive transcends the *mere* neurobiological, and the conscious transcends the *mere* cognitive. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hddebq1grv2wxbvsmwk3k11t))
- The four realms coalesce as an "ensemble of being." Together, the four realms, and the resulting ensemble, account for what and who we are, including those aspects of us that fall under the rubrics of the self and personality. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hddec0xf7mpm1n7s9d6rxmyj))
- The four realms framework is not an exercise in reductionism, where higher psychological ways of being are explained in neurobiological terms, neurobiological ways in terms of biological, and biological in terms of chemistry and physics. It is instead about how symbiotic interactions between the levels sustain the overall existence of the human organism. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hddecbaqjhb0pb0q2mt0zzyw))
- Each realm anatomically permeates and physiologically enables the level above it, and at the same time, the survival potential of the level below is enhanced by the one above. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hddefx0mej9sfjzhzed2gtpc))
