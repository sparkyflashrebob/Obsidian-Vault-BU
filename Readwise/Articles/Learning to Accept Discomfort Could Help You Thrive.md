## Highlights
- Learning to Accept Discomfort Could Help You Thrive ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14ra752pcj9zm0tdwzsvw4))
- Equanimity, a key ingredient of mindfulness meditation, helps people face life’s ups and downs ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14rf5vykhx0bxf1bntmp5d))
- A stream of [scientific articles](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352250X18302239?casa_token=FdPRxk2nygEAAAAA:4jRp9Y2l9K-0XLMB2Kg6KbxqXUjG4g6gnNdtyIhhbLoGVMb4kBi5msTF7gcWpMzno0SjPK82Xw) suggests that there are benefits in turning toward discomfort or negative emotions with acceptance. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14spes6f0ypxdac685nwtw))
- I believe that the cultivation of equanimity, a central element of certain mindfulness meditation practices, can help. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14t7esxcw30a3my3qp5rsm))
- a 2022 study with more than 2,000 people [demonstrated that](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09567976211044685) those participants who were explicitly encouraged to push themselves into awkward, uncomfortable situations across multiple domains—including taking improv classes to boost self-confidence and reading about opposing political viewpoints—later reported the greatest degree of personal growth. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14vc19h1rsz1v1y5p4agj3))
- Another study, published this year, found that people who can face their negative emotions, such as sadness or anger, in a neutral way [are more satisfied](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36913276/) and less anxious and have fewer symptoms of depression than those who judge their negative feelings harshly. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14vmtvdjcntpk9ts8xfndh))
- My own research indicates that meditation provides an ideal way to practice turning toward discomfort—particularly when it trains up one’s equanimity. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14w39thn0wqmsk5pja7bq0))
- Equanimity specifically refers to a mental attitude of being at peace with the push and pull of experience. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14wcz165xyw1k39yq8x49g))
- Our equanimity skills training group had significantly better outcomes on several measures. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14xh7zve72jwjx9ye1bfvq))
- After just 14 days of training, for example, the participants who learned equanimity skills had significantly [lower biological stress responses](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306453017304109) when asked to deliver a difficult speech and solve math problems in front of experts in white lab coats. The equanimity skills group also had significantly lower blood pressure and hormonal stress levels. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14xtk4p2vgddxj4v8yxga8))
- In the days after training, people introduced to equanimity exercises also reported significantly [higher positive emotions and well-being](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2018-63189-003) throughout the day and more [meaningful social interactions](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1813588116) than participants who received mindfulness training without the equanimity component. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hf14y3x2neesdn2rmjah30wg))
