## Highlights
- Marijuana Use Increasingly Linked to Addiction, Psychosis ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk615v6n2q5hhn780jnh0vxy))
- A rising tide of cannabis-related mental health problems is resulting from the widespread legalization of recreational weed in the United States, warns a new evidence review. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk6163qtrkth8d2r1mnv4er0))
- Nearly one in five Americans aged 12 and older used marijuana in 2021, and more than 16 million meet the criteria for a diagnosis of cannabis use disorder, the researchers wrote. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk616a03bdae17d3b5j9v6xh))
- Further, nearly half of those with cannabis use disorder have another psychiatric condition like anxiety, major depression or post-traumatic stress disorder, said review author [Dr. David Gorelick](https://www.medschool.umaryland.edu/profiles/gorelick-david/), a professor of psychiatry the University of Maryland School of Medicine in Baltimore. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk616kz4yrvf9jxsd8pz62wp))
- “There is a lot of misinformation in the public sphere about cannabis and its effects on psychological health, with many assuming that this drug is safe to use with no side effects,” Gorelick said in a university news release. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk616w292x36bdrktr6n5a3g))
- “It is important for physicians and the public to understand that cannabis can have addictive effects and to recognize signs and symptoms in order to get properly diagnosed and treated,” he added. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk6171jf2k6b917dztsbqwst))
- Weed use accounts for 10% of all drug-related ER visits in the United States, according to the review published recently in the *[New England Journal of Medicine](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMra2212152).* ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk6177rxp1qnn7mm1s99cej4))
- There are seven recognized disorders related to cannabis use, the report noted. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk617dtg6vhw85v790s6nhpt))
- Some include cannabis-induced anxiety disorder, cannabis-induced psychotic disorder, cannabis-induced sleep disorder and cannabis-induced delirium. This last one manifests as hyperactivity, agitation and disorientation with hallucinations. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk617kfn2k7s60jk7ts5hn1n))
- There’s also cannabis use disorder, which is defined as problematic marijuana use. Symptoms include craving weed and using it even though it’s wreaking havoc on people's lives, causing them to miss important family obligations or to do poorly in school or at work. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk617zv6v0ammran0eezwkg8))
- Cannabis use disorder is most common in people who use weed more than four days a week, researchers said. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk618370zzd70hfbxj0qc2fj))
- Young adults ages 18 to 25 are disproportionately affected by cannabis use disorder, with more than 14% of people in this age group struggling with problematic weed use. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk6189rg6mkgregfcxq806dt))
- “Approximately one in ten people who use cannabis will become addicted, and for those who start before age 18, the rate rises to one in six,” said [Dr. Mark Gladwin](https://www.medschool.umaryland.edu/profiles/gladwin-mark/), dean of the University of Maryland School of Medicine. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk618e859c6k1xc2m7yb62jq))
- “As use of this drug increases, we must delve deeply into basic research to understand the brain’s cannabinoid system,” Gladwin added. “We must also design translational studies of therapies that target these brain mechanisms to help those with cannabis use disorder -- particularly young adults and pregnant women -- overcome their dependence on this drug.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk618krbfyzzs2n5bkac8vfc))
- Heavy weed use puts a person’s health at risk in other ways as well, the paper noted. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk618xtmgsz24mrfs9ce5p4h))
- For example, cannabis use is associated with a 30% to 40% increased risk of a car accident, researchers said. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hk6191xy3ggy597ejt0sxqqd))
