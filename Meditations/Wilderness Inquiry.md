# Inquiry

Bring your awareness into the body
See if you can feel from the inside out
Rest in the field of changing sensations
Hands, belly, heart
If it helps - use the breath to steady yourself

Ask yourself "What, in this moment, is preventing me to be at home in my body?"
You might be pulled away by thought
or maybe feeling - the sensations - not liking
or it might be fear

It can be helpful to frame entering the body  like entering the wilderness
We are nature.
Notice how it is changing
Notice that it is raw - it is intense  - it is the place of pleasure and pain
It is out of control - this is important to notice - it is a key feature
It's where fear is, but there is also love and the intensity of aliveness.
It is also where the mystery is.
THIS IS THE WILDERNESS
Resurrender to this as best as possible ensuring that you feel safe

You might also ask "Who am I in this wilderness?"
Can you find a self?

Might not share this by
the Discovery -
It Strips away the identity of self
The Controller out of a job - the ego 
The Wilderness is a portal to freedom because it is a place where you can start discovering who you are beyond the self.
