#### 4. Strategic Tax & IRMAA Management (The 12% Bracket Defense)
The "Retirement Low-Income Window" provides a unique tactical opportunity to manage taxes before RMDs begin. Proactive management during these years prevents "tax bombs" later in retirement when Social Security and RMDs begin to stack.
##### Tax-Savvy Action Plan
1.  **Intentional Bracket Filling:**  The goal is to remain within the 12% Federal bracket. It is critical to note that the 12% limit applies to  *Taxable Income*  (Adjusted Gross Income minus the Standard Deduction). For 2026, we are targeting an **AGI ceiling of approximately  $125,000** for a married couple filing jointly to keep Taxable Income below the 12% threshold (~$ 94,000).
2.  **IRMAA Cliff Avoidance:**  Medicare Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount (IRMAA) thresholds trigger significant premium surcharges. For couples, these cliffs begin at approximately $218,000. Because IRMAA is a "cliff," exceeding the limit by one dollar results in a year-long premium increase.
3.  **Roth Conversion Opportunity:**  The years 2026–2030 are the "sweet spot" for moving funds from traditional to Roth accounts. Converting assets at today’s 12% rate creates tax-free growth and reduces the future RMD burden.
Efficient withdrawal management preserves the purchasing power necessary to address the realities of healthcare.

##### Coverage Realities: Wellness vs. Physicals
The "Medicare Wellness Exam" is covered at 100% as a preventative service. However, this is legally distinct from a "Full Routine Physical," which may include extensive laboratory work not covered by traditional Medicare.
##### The "Big 5" Medicare Gaps
Strategic capital allocation must account for the five major gaps where traditional Medicare does not provide coverage:
1.  **Dental:**  Routine exams and major procedures.
2.  **Vision:**  Eye exams, frames, and contact lenses.
3.  **Hearing:**  Routine testing and hearing aids.
4.  **Long-term Care:**  Custodial care for activities of daily living.
5.  **Nursing Home Care:**  Stays exceeding the skilled nursing facility limit.
