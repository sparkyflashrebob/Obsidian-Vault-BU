**Product Basics and Funding**
You purchased the **New York Life Secure Term MVA Fixed Annuity IV** with a single premium of **$100,100** [1-5]. This was funded via a direct rollover from your pre-tax Fidelity Traditional IRA [1, 2, 6]. You (Ronald A. **Surrender Terms and Penalty-Free Access**
You selected a **3-Year Initial Interest Rate Guarantee Period** [2, 5].

* **Years 1–3:** You have a 10% annual free withdrawal allowance (currently up to $10,011.29) [1]. Any withdrawals beyond this amount during the first three years are subject to a **7% surrender charge** and a potential Market Value Adjustment (MVA) [5, 10, 11].
* **Year 4 and Beyond:** Once your 3-year term ends on May 13, 2029, your surrender charge drops to **0%** [1, 12]. As long as you let the policy renew on a 1-year basis instead of locking into a new multi-year guarantee, you will have full, penalty-free access to draw down or fully surrender the funds [12, 13].

**Tax Implications**
Because the annuity was funded with pre-tax IRA dollars, it grows tax-deferred, but **any withdrawals you make—including Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)—are taxed as ordinary income** [14-16]. Taking strategic, partial withdrawals rather than a lump-sum full surrender allows you to slowly fill up your lower tax brackets and helps prevent massive spikes to your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) or Medicare IRMAA premiums.

**Strategic Purpose (Bucket 2)**
This annuity perfectly aligns with the **Bucket 2 (Income Stability)** portion of your 7-Year Bridge Strategy. You specifically chose this product over a CD ladder because it provides the 10% liquidity feature and accommodates RMDs, which CDs do not [17, 18]. It acts as a secure "release valve" that you can draw down in Years 4 and 5 to refill your checking account without selling off volatile stock investments.

**Long-Term Deferral**
Your Annuity Commencement Date is automatically set to **2055** (the later of 10 years or your 95th birthday) [6]. This extended timeline gives you decades of flexibility to use the policy exactly as you intend—for strategic partial withdrawals—without being forced to convert the balance into a fixed, lifetime income stream.
