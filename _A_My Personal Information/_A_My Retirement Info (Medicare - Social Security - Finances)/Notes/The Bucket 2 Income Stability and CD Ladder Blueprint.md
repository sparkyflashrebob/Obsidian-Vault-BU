Your plan for **Bucket 2 (Income Stability)** is specifically designed to act as a bridge for Years 3 through 5 of your 7-Year Retirement Bridge Strategy [1-3].

Here are the exact details and mechanics of your plan for this bucket:

* **Target Amount:** You have allocated **$68,400** to this bucket [1-3].
* **Investment Vehicle:** You will use **New Issue Bank CDs** (or a short-term bond fund) to build a **1-Year and 2-Year FDIC-insured CD Ladder** [4-6]. You will execute this using Fidelity's Model CD Ladder Tool inside your Fidelity Rollover IRA [6, 7].
* **Purpose & Yield:** The primary function of Bucket 2 is principal protection [5]. By utilizing these low-risk vehicles, you aim to lock in yields of approximately **3.79% to 4.1%** [3, 5, 6].
* **Tax Considerations:** The interest generated from these CDs is fully taxable for California state and federal purposes; however, it is projected to be shielded by your standard deductions [5].
* **The "Release Valve" Mechanic:** When you first build the CD ladder, you will enable the "Auto-Roll" feature [4, 6]. **Your main action item will be in Year 2:** as the cash in your Bucket 1 (FDLXX) nears depletion, you will simply disable the Auto-Roll feature on your maturing CDs [4, 6]. As those CDs mature, the principal will safely "flush down" into your Bucket 1, immediately refreshing your 24-month liquid cash runway [4, 6].

By structuring Bucket 2 this way, it serves as a secure, automated refill mechanism for your checking account, ensuring you never have to sell off the equities in your Growth Engine (Bucket 3) during a market downturn [4, 6].
