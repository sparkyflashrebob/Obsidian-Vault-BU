How to sign up for Medicare Part A only
4. Find your benefit verification letter
After your application is approved, go to the benefit verification letter in your SSA online account. This letter has your new Medicare number on it and information about your Medicare hospital coverage. Your physical Medicare card should arrive in the mail within 2–3 weeks.
Create an SSA online account
If you don’t have a Social Security online account yet, go to and click on the blue Sign In button on the top right-hand side of the screen. From here, you can create an account.
3. Check the status of your application
You can start to check the status of your application 5–7 days after submitting it. Sign into your SSA account to see the status.
2. Sign up for Part A only
Go to the on SSA.gov and choose Apply online. Answer no on your application when asked if you want to enroll in Part B (you’ll be enrolled in Part A by default). To do this step over the phone instead, call SSA at 800-772-1213.
