As a senior health coverage expert, I have analyzed Kaiser Permanente's 2026 Medicare offerings for the Northern California region, specifically focused on your area in Larkspur.
Kaiser Permanente primarily operates as an HMO (Health Maintenance Organization). In California, their Medicare plans are consolidated under the "Senior Advantage" brand. Because you are 65, you are in the prime window for these options.
Kaiser Permanente Senior Advantage (HMO)
This is their standard Medicare Advantage (Part C) plan, which integrates hospital (Part A), medical (Part B), and prescription drugs (Part D) into one package. In Marin County for 2026, you generally choose between two main tiers:
Basic Plan: Typically features a low or $0 monthly premium (plus your standard Part B premium). It has higher copays for specialist visits and a higher out-of-pocket maximum (around $6,000).
Enhanced Plan: Features a monthly premium (approximately $89/month in Marin) but offers lower copays, a lower out-of-pocket maximum (around $3,900), and often better coverage for "extras."
"Advantage Plus" (Optional Supplemental Benefits)
For an additional monthly fee (roughly $17/month), you can add the Advantage Plus package to your Senior Advantage plan. This covers services not typically included in Original Medicare:
Dental: Comprehensive coverage including X-rays, fillings, and crowns.
Vision: An allowance toward prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses (usually every 24 months).
Hearing: Coverage for hearing exams and a significant allowance for hearing aids.
Kaiser Permanente Dual Complete (HMO D-SNP)
If you are "dual-eligible" (meaning you have both Medicare and Medi-Cal), this plan offers $0 premiums and coordinated care between both programs, often including extra allowances for food, utilities, or over-the-counter (OTC) health products.
Medicare Part D (Prescription Drugs)
While most of Kaiser's Advantage plans include drug coverage, they also offer stand-alone Part D plans if you choose to stay with Original Medicare. In 2026, all Part D plans feature a $2,000 out-of-pocket cap on covered drugs due to federal law changes.
Key Considerations for Larkspur Residents
Network: You must use Kaiser Permanente doctors and facilities (such as the San Rafael Medical Center or the Larkspur medical offices) for non-emergency care.
SilverSneakers: Most Kaiser Medicare plans in your area include a fitness membership at no extra cost.
World-Wide Coverage: You are covered for emergency and urgent care anywhere in the world.
