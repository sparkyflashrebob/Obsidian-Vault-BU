The golden rules of retirement travel focus on pacing yourself, prioritizing health, staying flexible, and not waiting to take the trips you've always dreamed of. [1, 2, 3]

Key Rules for Retirement Travel

• Go slow and don't overschedule: Treat your trip like a marathon instead of a sprint. Build in downtime or full rest days so you do not wear yourself out early.

• Prioritize health and safety: Talk to your doctor before you leave. Bring extra prescription medications, carry your medical info, and buy travel insurance with medical coverage since standard Medicare usually does not work overseas.

• Embrace flexibility: Enjoy the luxury of time by keeping your schedule loose. Leave room for spontaneity or to change plans if you want to stay longer in a place.

• Don't wait for tomorrow: If there is a major destination or active trip you want to do, book it sooner rather than later while you have the energy and physical health for it.

• Travel during shoulder seasons: Go during the quieter periods just before or after peak tourist times to avoid big crowds and high prices.

• Choose comfort over "shoulds": Pick accommodations, transport, and pacing that fit your personal needs rather than trying to follow rushed "best of" tourist lists. [1, 3]
