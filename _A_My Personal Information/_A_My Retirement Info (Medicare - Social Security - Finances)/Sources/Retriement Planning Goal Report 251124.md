   About Your Retirement Plan
   The analysis of your retirement plan is based on the planning information you entered or confirmed, a model asset mix similar to the accounts you assigned to your retirement goal (asset mix assessment) or a Target Asset Mix you've selected, and certain tool assumptions.
   In assessing asset mix, the Planning & Guidance Center's Retirement Analysis (the "Tool") sorts the holdings in your assigned accounts into several major asset classes, including domestic stocks and foreign stocks (equities), bonds (fixed income), short-term securities (cash, cash equivalents, CDs, money market funds, etc), other, and unknown. Underlying investments held in a mutual fund or other pooled investment vehicle are considered individually. A model asset mix is then identified based on the percentage of equity holdings in your selected accounts.
   Short term: Stock holdings 0% Conservative: Stock holdings 20% Moderate with Income: Stock holdings 30% Balanced: Stock holdings 50% Growth with Income: Stock holdings 60% Growth: Stock holdings 70% Aggressive Growth: Stock holdings 85% Most Aggressive: Stock holdings 100%
   Note that if a large percentage of your holdings is identified as "Unknown" or "Other," your asset mix assessment may not accurately reflect the way your assigned accounts are currently invested. The "Other" category includes the presence of holdings that can't be assigned one of the 4 major asset classes (e.g., derivative securities, options, and precious metals) in some of your investments. The "Unknown" category indicates holdings whose details are unknown or unavailable to Fidelity.
   If the Tool has identified holdings as "Unknown" or "Other," the equity, fixed income, and short-term allocations are proportionately adjusted to cover to create a mix percentage adding up to 100%.
