### Retirement Planning Tool Review
- Andy's retirement planning details had been entered into the Fidelity planning tool, including his partner Andrea's financial information, which he had been actively updating as expenses changed.

- A long-term care cost entry covering the final four years of the plan was discussed as a conservative modeling element, and Andy confirmed he was comfortable removing it while remaining aware of its potential impact.

### Market Volatility Concerns and Investment Strategy
- A key concern raised was the current economic and political climate, with Andy expressing a desire to protect his retirement assets from significant market downturns during the early years of retirement.
- The sequence of returns risk was illustrated using a visual comparison of two retirees, showing how an early market downturn can drastically reduce long-term portfolio value when withdrawals are being made simultaneously.
- Andy's preference was to pursue a conservative, stable strategy for the next five to seven years, targeting approximately 4% returns, before transitioning to a more growth-oriented approach.
- A three-bucket strategy was outlined, with the first bucket covering day-to-day expenses, the second being an indexed fund to periodically replenish the first, and the third being a standard balanced investment portfolio for long-term growth.
- The impact of increasing equity allocation from approximately 55% to 70% stocks was modeled, showing improved average-market outcomes but a choppier short-term trajectory, which was noted as the opposite of Andy's stated conservative preference for the near term.

### CD Ladder as a Conservative Short-Term Option
- A CD ladder was presented as a near-guaranteed, liquid option for parking cash conservatively in the short term.
- Building a ladder means individual CDs mature on a staggered schedule, providing access to cash approximately every quarter for a one-year ladder or annually for a five-year ladder.
- Interest rates on CDs were noted to be in the low 3% to approximately 4% range, offering modest but stable returns.
- Upon maturity, funds would be returned to a core account rather than automatically reinvested, giving Andy full control over reallocation.

### Income Annuity as a Higher-Yield Conservative Option
- A short-term income annuity was introduced as an alternative to CDs, described as offering higher interest rates with a degree of liquidity.
- New York Life's three-year annuity was highlighted as offering approximately 4.6% interest, compounding over the term with full principal and interest returned at maturity.
- A minimum investment of $50,000 was noted for this product, with the ability to withdraw up to 10% of the principal annually without penalty.
- Early full liquidation was possible but would incur a surrender charge of approximately 7%, equivalent to roughly one and a half years of interest.
- Andy proposed a combination approach of splitting available funds between an annuity for higher yield and a CD ladder for greater liquidity, which was acknowledged as a viable strategy.

### IRA Rollover Mechanics and Tax Considerations
- Rolling over the 401k into an IRA was confirmed to be a non-taxable event that significantly expands the available investment options beyond the limited menu of a typical 401k plan.
- Within an IRA, funds can be allocated across a wide range of instruments including money market accounts, CDs, annuities, individual stocks, and other vehicles, with full flexibility to adjust allocations at any time.
- Andy confirmed his intention to withdraw a portion of the IRA funds to pay off his car, factoring this into his overall financial plan.
- Staying within the 12% tax bracket was identified as a priority, with Andy noting his current income of approximately $48,000 and a target of keeping total withdrawals under $100,000.
- A temporary delay in Social Security income was noted due to administrative difficulties, with Andy indicating he had sufficient bank funds to cover two to three months of expenses in the interim.
