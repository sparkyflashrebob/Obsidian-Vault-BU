\*\*Financial Aid Guidelines\*\* Scholarships for Residential Retreat ~ General Information Dana (the Pali word for generosity) has played an essential role in the 2,500 year-old Buddhist tradition. Spirit Rock Meditation Center teachers freely offer the teachings, and are supported in turn by donations from participants who value what the teachings have brought to their lives. In the spirit of this tradition, Spirit Rock Meditation Center is committed to offering scholarship assistance to those who are genuinely unable to afford the low-end sliding scale price of a meditation retreat.
Each scholarship request is carefully assessed in terms of financial need, previous history, and funds availability. Due to limited resources, we cannot guarantee that every application will result in a scholarship award. Your first choice of retreat may not always be possible, as there are a limited number of beds available for scholarship applicants on some retreats. If you can afford to pay the full amount of the retreat, please do so; this allows Spirit Rock to offer scholarship or special rates to those truly in financial need. Deposit Most retreats require a deposit to register, even if you are applying for a scholarship. Please refer to the registration application form of the retreat you wish to attend for specific deposit amounts. How to apply: Send in your registration forms, including deposit, and ask for a scholarship form. You will receive the scholarship form with your confirmation or waiting list notification by email. Print the scholarship form, fill out and return to the address on the form or fax to the number on the form. If you do not receive the funds that you need, your deposit will be refunded. Cancellations If you need to cancel from a retreat, please do so early. This gives us time to open the space up for another applicant. Once you have been accepted into the retreat, cancellation fees will apply. For amounts see application form.
Scholarship Funds Spirit Rock Meditation Center has established various scholarships to support the practice needs of our diverse community. Please apply for a scholarship only if you have a financial need that would otherwise prevent you from attending a retreat. Please read the information below and, if submitting a scholarship application, please select the fund that best suits your situation.
Mudita Scholarships The Mudita Scholarship provides a means for those who find themselves in financial need and who would not otherwise be able to attend a retreat. A deposit is required. See the registration form for the amount.
