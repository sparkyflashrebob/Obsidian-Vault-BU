In this video, breathwork teacher and researcher Mike Maher introduces his framework called the **"Periodic Table of Breathwork."** He organizes all breathing practices into an acronym called **R.E.S.E.T.**
The host focuses on explaining the theory and purpose behind each category, directing viewers to his interactive digital table for step-by-step video tutorials. The following breakdown details what each of the six exercise categories accomplishes, along with practice instructions based on the techniques and concepts highlighted in the video.

---

##### 1. Release (R) — "The Engine"

* **What it accomplishes:** This category acts like "breath yoga." It opens up your rib cage and diaphragm so you can physically inhale and exhale easier [[02:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=154)]. It corrects "tall breathing" (shallow breathing where only the shoulders and chest lift) by restoring proper mechanical expansion [[02:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=174)]. Maher notes that it is pointless to try other exercises if your physical breathing engine is too tight to move [[02:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=168)].
* **How to practice:**
* **Cat-Cow:** Move into a tabletop position on your hands and knees. Inhale as you drop your belly and look up (Cow), then exhale as you arch your spine and tuck your chin (Cat) [[05:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=351)]. Coordinate the movement directly with the pace of your breath to stretch the torso and diaphragm.
* **Apple Picking:** Stand up straight and reach your arms up high one at a time, as if grabbing fruit from a high branch [[06:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=373)]. Inhale deeply into the side of the ribs that you are lengthening to physically expand the intercostal muscles.

##### 2. Energize (E) — Upregulation

* **What it accomplishes:** These are quicker, high-energy techniques designed for short-term and long-term "upregulation" [[03:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=187)]. They provide an immediate energy boost, clear morning grogginess, and shake off chronic fatigue [[07:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=432)].
* **How to practice (Breath of Fire):**
* Sit comfortably with a tall spine.
* Inhale naturally, then force a powerful, rhythmic expiration through your nose by sharply snapping your navel back toward your spine [[06:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=383)].
* Allow the inhalation to happen passively as your belly relaxes.
* Maintain a quick, steady rhythm. The video recommends practicing this in **three distinct rounds** [[06:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=389)].

##### 3. Soothe (S) — Downregulation

* **What it accomplishes:** These are "downregulation" techniques meant to calm the nervous system, lower stress, handle anxiety, and help you fall asleep [[03:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=201)]. They trigger the parasympathetic nervous system to slow down the body's stress response.
* **How to practice:**
* **Box Breathing:** Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold your breath for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold empty for 4 seconds. Repeat the cycle for several minutes [[00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=22)].
* **Coherence Breathing:** Breathe at a steady, rhythmic pace—typically inhaling for 5 seconds and exhaling for 5 seconds—to optimize heart rate variability [[03:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=213)].
* **4-7-8 Technique:** Inhale through the nose for 4 seconds, hold the breath for 7 seconds, and exhale completely through the mouth making a "whoosh" sound for 8 seconds [[00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=32)].

##### 4. Embody (E) — Connection

* **What it accomplishes:** This acts as a form of mindfulness meditation [[03:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=221)]. It is designed for people who feel disconnected from themselves or get overwhelmed by intense emotions [[07:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=450)]. It forces you to look away from digital devices and direct your focus inward to read the physical data and sensations your body is sending you [[03:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=228)].
* **How to practice:**
* Sit quietly and close your eyes.
* Bring your attention entirely to the physical sensation of the air passing through your nostrils, the stretching of your skin, or the feeling of your clothes moving against you as you breathe.
* When your mind drifts, gently anchor your thoughts back to the raw physical sensations of the breath inside the body.

##### 5. Tolerate (T) — Metabolic Efficiency

* **What it accomplishes:** This category focuses on slowing down your resting breathing rate to tolerate higher levels of carbon dioxide [[04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=241)]. It is ideal for people who get out of breath easily, find themselves panting while walking up hills, or have a habit of chronic mouth-breathing [[07:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=455)]. Training your tolerance leads to a slower mind, lower heart rate, and better oxygen delivery [[04:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=241)].
* **How to practice:**
* Consciously practice breathing slower and lighter than normal through your nose.
* Incorporate brief, comfortable breath-retention holds at the bottom of a normal exhale to safely build up carbon dioxide tolerance.

##### 6. Transformational (T) — Advanced Catharsis

* **What it accomplishes:** These are advanced, intense breathing techniques (such as **Holotropic Breathing** ) used to access deep emotional shifts and altered states of consciousness [[04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=247), [07:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=466)].
* **How to practice:**
* Maher explicitly warns that these advanced practices **should never be tried alone** or from a quick tutorial [[04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=254)]. They require a professional setting under the live guidance of a certified teacher who can safely navigate the physical and emotional release [[04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=254)].

##### Core Concept: "How You Breathe" vs. "Breathwork"

To get the most out of these exercises, keep Maher's primary distinction in mind:

* **How you breathe** is your unconscious, 24/7 baseline breathing [[04:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=277)].
* **Breathwork** consists of the targeted exercises you practice intentionally for a few minutes at a time [[04:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=283)].
  The goal of doing these specific exercises is to gradually train and correct your unconscious baseline, restoring the healthy breathing patterns we naturally had as children [[02:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=132), [04:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbjRVaByHbI&t=289)].
