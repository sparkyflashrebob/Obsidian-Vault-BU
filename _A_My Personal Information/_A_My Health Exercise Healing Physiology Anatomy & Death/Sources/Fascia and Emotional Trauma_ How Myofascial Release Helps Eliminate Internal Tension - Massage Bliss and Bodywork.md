How Myofascial Release Helps Eliminate Internal Tension
Pain is not always obvious. It does not shout, but lives quietly inside, in the tension of the shoulders, in the labored breathing, in the feeling, that something is pulling from the inside. It is not physical, rather emotional state.
Our body remembers more than we think, and fascia stores these memories. They react to stress, fear, loss, or insult. When we don’t give ourselves space to experience an emotion, it settles in the tissues. But there is a solution, myofascial release, during which the specialist’s hand work not only with muscles, but also with deeper layers of connective tissues.
What is Fascia: the Invisible Network of Our Body
Fascias are thin, but extremely strong connective tissues, that surround all our organs. They create a three-dimensional network, that permeates the flesh like an internal landscape. We don’t see them, but they shape posture and even emotional reactions. It is a magical “wrap” for muscles that transmits force, coordinates movement, and preserves the body’s memory.
Fascias are extremely sensitive. Injuries, stress, or strain leave their mark on them. When they are normal, the body moves without pain. But if there are failures in the system and their elasticity is lost, then stiffness, discomfort, and imbalance appear immediately.
Fascias can be compared to the Internet: they connect everything to everything, transmit signals, adapt to changes. Working with these connective tissues is not the usual physiotherapy, but a deep restoration of the body’s integrity. Their role is fundamental.
Therefore, the more we understand their purpose and functioning, the better we can take care of ourselves physically and emotionally.

How Fascia Stores Emotional Trauma and Pain?
Our body is a complex mechanism. And fascias are silent guardians of what we could not overcome. They react to emotional upheavals in the same way as to physical injuries: they contract, lose elasticity, and create tension zones. And then pain appears with no obvious cause: tests are normal, but there is a feeling that something “wrong” is happening to us.
This is not fiction. Fascias actually “remember” and accumulate all negativity. If nothing is done about it, the body adapts to such a state and creates a reality with new “norms” of well-being. But in reality it is an imbalance.
Fascial emotional stress can be manifested by the following signs:
Constant tension in the shoulders, lower back, neck, etc.
A feeling of “internal pressure” without physical causes.
Emotional instability accompanied by bodily discomfort.
Fatigue, that does not go away after rest.
These are all signals that you need to identify the pain. And when a specialist works on your connective tissues, it’s as if the body will get a breath of fresh air. After just one session, you will feel changes, and after completing the full course, you will receive real healing.
