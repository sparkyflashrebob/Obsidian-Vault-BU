What are the Factors Leading to Skin Aging?
The natural aging process occurs within the body with time. These factors influence skin aging and can be managed by supplementing the right ingredients.
Factors Influencing Skin Aging
Reasons For Skin Aging
Natural Reduction of Collagen and Elastin:
The production of collagen and elastin, two essential proteins that maintain skin firmness and elasticity, naturally decreases with age. This reduction can lead to the formation of fine lines, wrinkles, and loss of elasticity.
Dehydration:
Aging skin tends to retain less moisture, leading to dehydration. Dehydrated skin can appear dry, rough, and less vibrant, accelerating the formation of fine lines and wrinkles.
Thinning of the Epidermis:
The outermost layer of the skin, the epidermis, becomes thinner with age. This thinning can make the skin more vulnerable to damage from UV radiation.
Cell Turnover:
The ability of skin to shed dead skin cells and replace with new healthy cells diminishes with age. This slow cell turnover can result in a dull complexion, an increased prevalence of age spots, and uneven skin texture.
Oxidative Stress:
The body’s defence mechanism against oxidative stress becomes less efficient with increasing age. Oxidative stress occurs when free radicals damage skin cells, leading to premature aging & an increased risk of skin diseases.
Loss of Subcutaneous Fat:
The layer of fat beneath the skin, known as subcutaneous fat, decreases with age. This fat reduction can lead to a loss of skin volume and a more sunken appearance in some regions of the face, contributing to the signs of aging.

Collagen is the structural protein responsible for skin’s firmness and elasticity. The collagen production decreases with age leading to sagging skin and wrinkles. MSM promotes collagen production, helping the skin to maintain its structural integrity and suppleness. With increased collagen levels, fine lines and wrinkles become less prominent.

The anti-inflammatory properties of MSM can soothe irritated skin, reduce redness, and help prevent the breakdown of collagen and elastin fibres.
