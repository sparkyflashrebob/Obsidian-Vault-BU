Dear Ronald (Andy),

Thank you for your purchase of the Earth Travel Plan by Inman Prepaid Repatriation Service. Your confidence in
us is appreciated! We pledge to you that our service, when needed, will provide repatriation for human
remains when death occurs seventy-five (75) miles or more away from your legal residence.

"Legal residence," for purposes of the Travel Plan, shall mean the participant's fixed and full-time place of
abode. The Travel Plan may require verification of a participant's legal residence through requests for
documentation or information evidencing the residence of the participant at the time of, and prior to, the
participant's death. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if a participant physically lives at an address for a period of
180 days or more within the twelve (12) month period prior to the participant's death, that address, as opposed
to the current legal address identified above, will be considered the participant's legal address. A nursing home
will be deemed a participant's legal residence if the participant stays at the nursing home for a period of 180
days or more.

Your purchase provides prepaid lifetime service and includes:

Contacting a licensed funeral home or mortuary transportation service

Transportation from the place of death to the funeral home or transportation service center
Preparation for transportation

Securing all documentation for transportation including one death certificate

Arranging for transportation to Member's local Earth facility

Remember to keep the enclosed Membership Cards in your wallet or purse so that it will be easily found if
death occurs when you are away from your legal residence. When traveling, please remind your traveling
companions or family that you are covered by the service. It is very important that your companion, associates,
or family call Travel Plan by Inman Service Center at 888-889-8508 (or 260-247-2289 outside the United
States) before others take any action to remove the remains. Our friendly and efficient customer service
representatives will help with information and coordinate the repatriation with your survivors and Earth.

If you should move legal residence, it's important that you let us know as soon as possible. It is important for
our records to be correct so that there will be no confusion when service is requested. Your legal residence will
be the determining factor for service at the time of your request. Please keep us informed.

Please verify that all the information on your permanent identification card is correct. If any changes are
needed; call the Travel Plan by Inman Service Center at 888-889-8508 (or 260-247-2289 outside the United
States) to give us the corrected information for your permanent file. In order to provide you and your family the
very best customer service, we encourage you contact our Travel Plan Service Center and let us know of any
upcoming international travel. Your Travel Plan purchase is good for unlimited international travel.

Once again, thank you for your purchase of the Travel Plan by Inman Service Agreement.
