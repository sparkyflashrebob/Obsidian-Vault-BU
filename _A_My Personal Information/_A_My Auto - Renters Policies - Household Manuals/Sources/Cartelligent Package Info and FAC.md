Below are some helpful hints regarding any DMV, Warranty, or other questions you may have.

1. How long should I keep the operating permit in my car's window?

The operating permit that has been placed in your window is valid for 90 days or until your license plates
arrive. It must not be removed until you receive your new registration, tags, and plates in the mail. (CA
Vehicle Code Section 4456c)

2. When can I expect to receive my new plates?

License plates are issued and will be mailed to you by fourth-class mail. It may take as long as 2-3 months
for the plates to arrive. Please be patient.

3. How will I get my registration cards and tags?

Your registration card and tags will be attached to the outside mailing envelope of your license plates. Do
not discard the envelope until you have accounted for your registration and tags.

4. When will I receive the title for my new car?

If you have financed or leased this vehicle, the title will be sent to the legal owner (the bank). If you did not
finance or lease the vehicle, the title will be sent to you, and will arrive separately from your license plates.

5. Can I just put my personal plates on my new car when I get home?

If you have personal plates, we recommend that you do not put the plates on your new vehicle until you
receive the new registration in the mail. If you do put the plates on the vehicle and you are stopped by an
officer, they may question why you have different plates on the vehicle. Until the transfer is complete, the
DMV records will show your personal plates as belonging to your previous vehicle.

6. When and where should I get my new car serviced?

Your vehicle comes with the manufacturer warranty as well as a service schedule. Please refer to your
warranty and maintenance manual for information regarding the length of the warranty and detailed service
information for scheduled maintenance. You can get your new vehicle serviced at any Toyota dealership
that is convenient for you.

7. What should I do when my lease is up?

If you leased your vehicle, we'll reach out to you before the lease is up to review the lease return process
and offer our assistance.

8. What if I have any other questions?

If you should have any questions concerning your new vehicle or would like to schedule a time to come back
and learn more about its features, please feel free to contact Cartelligent at 888-427-4270 and ask for your
Account Executive or the Client Service Team.
