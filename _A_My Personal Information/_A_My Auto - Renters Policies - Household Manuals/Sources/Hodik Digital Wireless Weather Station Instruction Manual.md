#### **Hodik Digital Wireless Weather Station Instruction Manual**

#### **1. System Features & Technical Specifications**

**● Transmission Range: Up to 328 feet (100 meters) in an open field, unobstructed. ● Wireless Frequency: 433.92 MHz RF transmission frequency. ● Indoor Temperature Range: 14°F to 122°F (-10°C to 50°C). ● Outdoor Temperature Range: -40°F to 158°F (-40°C to 70°C). ● Humidity Range: 20% to 95% Relative Humidity (RH) indoors and outdoors. ● Barometric Pressure Range: 600 hPa/mb to 1100 hPa/mb (17.72 inHg to 32.50 inHg). ● Power Source (Base Station): DC 5V 1.2A power adapter (included) or 3 x AAA**
batteries (backup power, not included). **● Power Source (Remote Sensor): 2 x AA batteries (alkaline or lithium, not included).**

#### **2. Unpacking, Hardware Overview, and Initial Setup**

#### **Hardware Layout**

**● Front Panel Display: Divided into quadrants for Weather Forecast, Barometric Pressure,**
Indoor Temp/Humidity, Outdoor Temp/Humidity/Channel, and Clock/Calendar. **● Top Touch Surface: SNOOZE / LIGHT sensor area. ● Rear Tactile Buttons: SET, ALARM, ▲ (MEM), ▼ (CH/WAVE), and ALERT.**

#### **Crucial First-Time Power Sequence**

To ensure a stable wireless connection, you must follow this exact power sequence. Failure to do so will result in dashed readings (--.-) for the outdoor sensors:
**1. Prepare the Base Station: Plug the DC 5V power adapter into an outlet and connect it to** the base station power jack. Leave the battery compartment empty for now.
**2. Configure the Remote Sensor: Slide open the battery hatch on the back of the remote sensor. Locate the small Channel Slide Switch and select your desired channel ( 1, 2, or 3 ).**
**3. Power the Remote Sensor: Insert 2 fresh AA alkaline batteries into the sensor, paying** close attention to polarity. A red LED light on the front of the sensor will flash briefly.
**4. Initiate Console Search: The base station automatically enters an RF search state for 3** minutes upon power-up, signaled by a flashing antenna reception icon next to the outdoor section.
**5. Establish Connection: Within 1 to 3 minutes, the outdoor temperature and humidity** values will appear on the base station screen.
**6. Insert Backup Batteries: Once the outdoor connection is fully locked, insert 3 AAA** batteries into the base station's battery compartment to act as a fallback memory cache in case of an electricity outage.

#### **3. Remote Sensor Placement & Channel Selection**

#### **Maximizing RF Signal Stability**

**● Line of Sight: Avoid placing the sensor directly behind thick concrete walls, heavy metal**
foil siding, or tinted glass panes. **● Electronic Interference: Mount the sensor at least 3 feet away from routers,**
microwaves, baby monitors, and large household appliances.

#### **Environmental Protection Guidelines**

**● Avoid Direct Sun: Mount the sensor on a North-facing wall or under an eave, porch**
ceiling, or patio awning. Direct sunlight will artifically inflate temperature readings. **● Moisture Protection: While the outdoor sensor is weather-resistant against light rain and**
snow, it is not completely waterproof. Never submerge it or mount it where standing puddles or heavy downpours can flood the internal electronics.

#### **Utilizing Multi-Channel Tracking**

● If you purchase optional secondary or tertiary sensors, set the physical slide switch on
**each sensor to a unique channel (e.g., Sensor A to CH 1 , Sensor B to CH 2 , Sensor C to CH 3 ).**
**● To cycle the base station screen between these channels, tap the ▼ (CH/WAVE) button** on the back of the unit. The display will cycle through Channel 1 $\rightarrow$ Channel 2 $\rightarrow$ Channel 3 $\rightarrow$ Auto-Scroll Mode (indicated by a looping arrow icon next to the channel number).

#### **5. Exhaustive Master Step-by-Step Settings Guide**

#### **5.1 Master Clock & Calendar Customization Mode**

To manually configure your regional format parameters and set the time:
**1. Press and hold the SET button for 3 seconds until the display flashes. 2. Press ▲ or ▼ to change values. Hold either button to cycle rapidly. 3. Tap SET once to save the value and advance to the next parameter.** 4. The system cycles through the setup parameters in this exact sequence:
**○ RCC (Radio-Controlled Clock) On/Off: Choose ON for automated atomic updates** or OFF for purely manual time tracking.
**○ Time Zone: Select your local offset (EST, CST, MST, PST, AST, HAT, AKT). ○ DST (Daylight Saving Time): Turn ON if your region observes seasonal shifts or**
OFF if it does not. **○ 12/24 Hour Clock: Choose 12HR (AM/PM format) or 24HR military formatting. ○ Hour: Adjust the current hour digits. ○ Minute: Adjust the current minute digits. ○ Year Format: Adjust the 4-digit current year. ○ Month Format: Adjust the current calendar month. ○ Date Format: Adjust the current calendar day number. ○ Language Selection: Choose your weekday abbreviation language (GER, ENG,**
FRE, ITA, SPA, DUT, DAN). **○ Barometric Pressure Unit: Select hPa/mb or inHg.**

#### **5.2 Daily Alarm Clock Configuration Mode**

**1. Tap the ALARM button once to switch from standard clock view to the Alarm Time screen**
(indicated by an AL icon). **2. While viewing the AL screen, hold the ALARM button down for 3 seconds. The hour digit**
will flash. **3. Use ▲ or ▼ to set the desired wake-up hour. 4. Press ALARM to save. The minute digits will begin to flash. 5. Use ▲ or ▼ to set the desired minutes. 6. Press ALARM to lock in your settings and exit back to the main layout. 7. To Arm/Disarm: Short-press ALARM on the standard clock screen to turn the alarm**
completely on or off. A bell icon ($\Delta$) appears on-screen when the alarm is active.

#### **5.3 High/Low Temperature & Ice Alert Configuration Mode**

This mode programs the base station to emit an audible chime if conditions breach your predefined thresholds.

##### **Setting Thresholds:**

**1. Press and hold the ALERT button for 3 seconds until the outdoor temperature zone** flashes.
**2. The High Alert Icon ("HI") will flash. Use ▲ or ▼ to adjust the high ceiling cutoff** temperature.
**3. Press ALERT to save. The Low Alert Icon ("LO") will begin flashing. 4. Use ▲ or ▼ to adjust the lower basement temperature floor cutoff. 5. Press ALERT to save, finalize, and exit the setup menu.**

##### **Activating and Deactivating the Chime:**

**● Short-press the ALERT button on the home dashboard to arm or disarm the alert system.** ● A small high/low indicator icon ($\overline{\mathbf{\Delta}}$ or
$\underline{\mathbf{\Delta}}$) will appear next to the temperature data fields to indicate the boundary lines are armed.

##### **Automated Ice Alert Feature:**

● If the outdoor channel currently being monitored drops into an active window between **30.2°F and 37.4°F (-1.0°C to 3.0°C) , a snowflake indicator symbol ($\ast$) will flash** continuously to warn of freezing roads or frost build-up.

#### **5.4 Weather Forecasting and Barometric History Mode**

The built-in electronic barometer reads air pressure changes over time to estimate local
conditions for the next 12 to 24 hours.

##### **Reading the Dynamic Weather Icons:**

**● Sunny: Indicates high pressure, clear clear-sky conditions. ● Slightly Cloudy: Minor pressure drops, occasional sun patches. ● Cloudy: Overcast skies, moisture building up in the local air mass. ● Rainy: Significant low-pressure dip, sustained precipitation likely. ● Heavy Rain/Storm: Deep pressure drop, high winds and storm activity. ● Snowy: Active when a rain trend forms while the outdoor sensor reads below 32°F (0°C).**

##### **Toggling Calibration Formats:**

**● Short-press the SET button to swap the barometric readouts between metric (hPa/mb)** and imperial (inHg) pressure reporting columns.

#### **5.5 Memory History Mode (MAX/MIN Tracking)**

The system logs the highest and lowest points for all indoor and outdoor sensors over a rolling 24-hour cycle.
**● View Peak Points: Press ▲ (MEM) once to view the maximum temperature and humidity** thresholds recorded since the last system flush. A MAX flag illuminates on screen.
**● View Low Points: Press ▲ (MEM) a second time to see the absolute lowest data** parameters logged. A MIN flag illuminates on screen.
**● Resetting the Storage Cache: While actively viewing either the MAX or MIN screen, hold the ▲ (MEM) button down for 3 seconds. The stored extreme metrics will wipe clean** and overwrite with current real-time environmental metrics.

#### **5.6 Display Customization, Backlight, and Power Modes**

**● System-wide Metric/Imperial Toggle: Press and hold the ▼ (CH/WAVE) button for 3**
seconds to convert all system readouts between Fahrenheit (°F) and Celsius (°C). **● AC Adapter Mode (Continuous Power): The display remains perpetually illuminated.**
**Tap the top SNOOZE / LIGHT surface to cycle screen brightness levels through HI $\rightarrow$ LOW $\rightarrow$ OFF .**
**● Battery Power Mode (Eco Power Saver): To conserve internal cells, the backlight remains entirely dark by default. Tap the top SNOOZE / LIGHT sensor to wake up the** screen for exactly 10 seconds before it automatically dims back to black.
