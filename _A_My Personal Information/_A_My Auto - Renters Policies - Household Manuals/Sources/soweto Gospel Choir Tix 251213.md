TICKET EXCHANGES (SUBJECT TO FEES AND AVAILABILITY) MUST BE PERFORMED AT LEAST 24 HOURS PRIOR TO THE SHOW, OR BY 4:00 P.M. THE LAST

EMAIL TICKETS@SONOMA.EDU WITH THE ORDER NUMBER.

BOX OFFICE INFORMATION

LOCATED ON THE FIRST FLOOR OF THE STUDENT CENTER, WHENEVER THE BUILDING IS OPEN. THE BOX OFFICE WILL OPEN AT THE
EVENT VENUE AT LEAST ONE HOUR PRIOR TO THE START TIME, AND CLOSE 30 MINUTES AFTER THE SCHEDULED START TIME (FOR
ATHLETIC EVENTS, THE BOX OFFICE WILL REMAIN OPEN APPROXIMATELY ONE HOUR AFTER THE SCHEDULED START TIME).
