COLLISION DEDUCTIBLE BUYBACK ENDORSEMENT

We will pay for the amount of collision damage which occurs to the covered auto during the policy period
in excess of the collision coverage deductible amount stated in the Declarations. This deductible will be
paid by us if:

1. the collision involves actual, direct physical contact between the covered auto and the
   uninsured motor vehicle and the owner or operator of the uninsured motor vehicle is
   identified or the uninsured motor vehicle is identified by its license number, provided that you
   or someone on your behalf shall have reported the accident within ten business days to us or
   our agent; and
2. it is determined by you and us or, in the event of disagreement, by arbitration conducted by a
   single neutral arbitrator, when you have formally instituted arbitration proceedings within one year
   from the date of accident, that you are legally entitled to recover the amount of the payments for
   property damage from the owner or operator of the uninsured motor vehicle.
