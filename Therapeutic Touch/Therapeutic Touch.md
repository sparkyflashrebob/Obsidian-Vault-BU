To tap into your innate self-healing abilities, try incorporating this fundamental self-treatment into your daily routine:

- Center Yourself: Sit quietly, focus on your breathing, and bring your attention to your heart. Disconnect from your daily worries, visualize a state of peace, and set your intention to nurture your well-being.
- Assess and Unruffle: Slowly move your hands, about 2 to 4 inches away from your body, starting from your head and moving downward. Pay attention to how your energy feels—notice any areas of tension, heat, or congestion. Use smooth, sweeping brush-like motions to clear and smooth out those uneven energy fields.
- Direct Intention: Place your hands lightly over areas of the body that are aching, cramped, or simply in need of extra support. Focus your mind on feelings of compassion and vitality, allowing your body to relax deeply so your internal recuperative powers can kick in.
