**Subtitle:** A 6-Week Practical Guide to Mindfulness and Stress Reduction

### **Program Overview**

This program provides community members with a "mental toolbox" to navigate the pressures of modern life. We strip away the complex terminology to focus on the core mechanics of how the mind works. Each 90-minute session includes a brief educational talk, guided practice, and a group "Check-In" to discuss real-world application.

### **Core Tools We Will Use**

#### **1. The S.T.O.P. Technique**

This is our primary tool for "Real-Life" mindfulness. It is used during traffic jams, difficult conversations, or moments of overwhelm:

* **S** - Stop what you are doing.
* **T** - Take a breath.
* **O** - Observe your thoughts and feelings without judgment.
* **P** - Proceed with a choice that matches your values.

#### **2. The Stress Response Education**

We teach the "Fight-Flight-Freeze" mechanism. By understanding the **Vagus Nerve** and how it signals the body to calm down, participants feel empowered by biology rather than intimidated by philosophy.

### **Logistics & Participation**

* **Who is this for?** Everyone from busy parents to retirees. No flexible clothing or "special" cushions required.
* **What to bring:** A notebook and an open mind. We provide chairs and standard yoga mats.
* **Home Practice:** Participants receive a weekly "Action Card" with one 5-minute exercise to try at home.

---

### **Program Goals**

1. **Reduce Stress:** Measurable improvement in self-reported tension levels.
2. **Community Connection:** Creating a safe space for neighbors to discuss mental well-being.
3. **Practicality:** Every participant should leave with at least three tools they can use while standing in line at the grocery store or during a difficult phone call.
