Choose Your Format

1:1 private coaching vs. group classes vs. corporate wellness — each has different revenue potential and time demands
In-person (Marin County has a very receptive audience), online, or hybrid
Retreats and workshops can command premium pricing

Revenue Streams to Consider

Weekly group classes (steady, community-building)
Corporate mindfulness programs (highest per-hour income)
Online courses or recorded content (passive income)
One-day or weekend retreats
Private clients

Structural Basics

Sole proprietor to start; LLC as you grow
Liability insurance (specific to yoga/meditation teachers — look at ACE or CPH & Associates)
A simple scheduling + payment tool like Mindbody, Acuity, or Calendly + Stripe


Marketing Yourself
Your Differentiators

MMTCP is highly credible — lead with it; it's the gold standard in secular mindfulness training
Your AV/tech engineering background is a genuine asset for corporate wellness clients and for producing high-quality online content

Practical Steps

Website: Simple, clean, with your bio, offerings, and a way to book — Squarespace or Wix work well
Google Business Profile: Critical for local Marin/Larkspur visibility
LinkedIn: Especially valuable for transitioning your AVDG network into corporate wellness leads
Instagram: Effective for mindfulness teachers; short guided practices perform well
Psychology Today profile: Useful if you work with individuals on stress/anxiety

Content Strategy

Short guided meditations (even 3–5 min) on YouTube or Instagram build trust and audience fast
A simple email newsletter keeps your community engaged between sessions
