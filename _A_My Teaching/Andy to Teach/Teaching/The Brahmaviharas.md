Love and compassion are necessities, not luxuries. Without them humanity cannot survive.

Dalai Lama

  

Buddhist 4 Immeasurable Virtues

lovingkindness (metta), 

compassion, 

Sympathetic joy, and

equanimity 

  

Loving-kindness

Friendliness

Goodwill

Active interest in others' well-being

Loving-kindness is the desire to bring about the well-being and happiness of others

  

Compassion is the desire to remove any harm and suffering

Opposite of wanting to bring harm and suffering to others

Is Not wanting to remove suffering partly selfish reasons

Say because this is causing you harm

Genuine concern for others- enough to take action to do so

  

Sympathetic Joy is the pleasure that comes from delighting in other people’s well-being

It is not prideful - it is not unadulterated by self-interest

Practice is to cultivate joy for the success and good fortune of others

Regardless of our personal circumstances

Considered to be the most difficult to cultivate

Not a form of greed nor grasping at pleasant experience out of a sense of insufficiency or lack

  

Equanimity is the unwavering ability to stay neutral in the face of the vicissitudes of life

Loss or gain

Fame or ill-repute

Praise and blame

Sorrow or happiness

Called the eight worldly winds

Not greed or resentment

NOR indifference or apathy

Described as 

Evenness of mind

Unshakeable freedom of mind

Inner equipoise

Thought to be the pinnacle of the four sublime states

Does not override or negate the other them but perfects and consummates them.
