Develop strong practice foundations

1. Practice What You Teach/lead by example/create atmosphere of calm & spiritual presence.
2. Practice When You Teach/center mind and establish presence in oneself
3. Do Not Be Too Mindful/Acting Mindful
4. Leave the Ego Outside
5. Be Clear About Your Role/not a therapist/teach each other
6. Familiarize Yourself with the Core Teachings
7. Focus on the Basics (breathing/mindfulness beyond meditation/gentleness in thoughts, words & actions, mindful reminders (POP), posture, body scan/relax body, mindfulness of feelings/thoughts, basic concepts such as interconnectedness/impermanence/compassion & loving-kindness, karma (cause & effect).
8. Joyfully Persevere/practice letting go
9. Find a Suitable Teacher
10. Uphold the Lineage of Mindfulness
