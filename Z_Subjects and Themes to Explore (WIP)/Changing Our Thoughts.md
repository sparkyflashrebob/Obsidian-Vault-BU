FROM MONICA: 
Changing our way of thinking often requires a conscious effort to challenge our existing beliefs and thought patterns. This can involve questioning our assumptions, seeking out new information and perspectives, and practicing mindfulness and self-reflection. It can also be helpful to surround ourselves with people who have different viewpoints and to engage in open and respectful dialogue with them. Over time, with consistent effort, we can develop new ways of thinking that better serve us and those around us.


From BARD - 
There are many ways to change our way of thinking. Here are a few tips:

-   **Become aware of your thoughts.** The first step to changing your thinking is to become aware of your thoughts. What do you think about yourself, others, and the world around you? Once you're aware of your thoughts, you can start to identify the ones that are unhelpful or negative.
-   **Challenge your thoughts.** Once you've identified your unhelpful thoughts, you can start to challenge them. Are they really true? Are there other ways to look at the situation? Are you being fair to yourself?
-   **Replace your negative thoughts with positive ones.** Once you've challenged your negative thoughts, you can start to replace them with positive ones. This doesn't mean that you have to pretend that everything is perfect, but it does mean that you should focus on the good things in your life.
-   **Practice gratitude.** One way to focus on the good things in your life is to practice gratitude. Make a list of things that you're grateful for, big or small. This can help you to shift your focus from the negative to the positive.
-   **Spend time with positive people.** The people we spend time with can have a big impact on our thinking. If you're surrounded by negative people, it can be hard to stay positive yourself. Try to spend more time with people who make you feel good about yourself and the world around you.
-   **Get help from a therapist.** If you're struggling to change your thinking on your own, you may want to consider getting help from a therapist. A therapist can help you to identify your unhelpful thoughts and develop strategies for challenging them.

Changing your way of thinking takes time and effort, but it's possible. By following these tips, you can start to shift your thinking in a more positive direction.
