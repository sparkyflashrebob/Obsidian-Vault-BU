\*   \*\*Alleyne Ireland\*\* is the author of \`The Province of Burma\`, which includes an extensive bibliography \[7\].
\*   \*\*Allie M. Frazier\*\* is associated with a selection in \`Readings in Eastern Religious Thought II\` \[8\].
\*   \*\*Anandajoti Bhikkhu\*\* is the author of \`Safeguard Recitals\` \[9\] and provided suggestions for \`An Analysis of Pali Canon\` \[10\]. He is also associated with the title \`Mahāparitta\` \[11\].
\*   \*\*Anuradha Seneviratna\*\* is the author of \`The Springs of Sinhala Civilization\` \[12\], \`Buddhist Monastic Architecture in Sri Lanka\` \[12\], \`Mahintale: Dawn of a Civilization\` \[12\], and a two-volume work on the \`Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic\` \[12\].
