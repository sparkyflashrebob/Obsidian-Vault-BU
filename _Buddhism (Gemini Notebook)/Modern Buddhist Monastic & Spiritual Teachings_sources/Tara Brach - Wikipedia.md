(an haife ta a ranar 17 ga Mayu, 1953) masanin ilimin halayyar dan adam ne na Amurka, marubuci, kuma mai goyon bayan tunani na Buddha. Ita ce malama mai jagora kuma ta kafa kungiyar Insight Meditation Community of

Washington, DC

https://ha.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,\_D.C.

Brach kuma yana koyarwa game da tunani na Buddha a cibiyoyin yin tunani da yoga a Amurka da Turai, gami da Cibiyar Meditation na Ruhun Rock a Woodacre, California; Cibiyar Kripalu; da Cibiyar Omega don Nazarin Cikakken.

Brach mai bin addinin Buddha ne, wanda ya kware a aikace-aikacen koyarwar Buddha da tunani mai hankali ga warkar da motsin rai.

Ta rubuta littattafai da yawa a kan waɗannan batutuwa, gami da Amincewa da Radical, Mafarki na Gaskiya, da Jinƙai na Radical .
