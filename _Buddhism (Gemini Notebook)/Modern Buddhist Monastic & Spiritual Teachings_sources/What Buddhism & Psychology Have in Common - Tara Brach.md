One of the great overlaps in Buddhism

and Western psychology is the

recognition of how our suffering

arises from feelings of separation.

And collectively, this expresses this

conflict with people we know, wars,

oppression, cruelty to non-human species,

and really violating our larger body,

this earth, the deep inquiry, and this is

one for us collectively is what will help

us remember the truth of our belonging.
