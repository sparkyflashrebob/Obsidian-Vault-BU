#### Role within the Larger Context of Anapanasati:

Anapanasati, using the breath as an anchor, provides the context for cultivating this awareness of the mind and its conditioners \[9, 11\]. By centering attention on the breath, the meditator has a "home ground" to return to when the mind is agitated \[11\].
