In summary, understanding self and mind in Buddhist practice is a process of deliberate cultivation, rooted in foundational ethics (refuges, precepts, virtue) and right view. It involves actively training the mind through mindfulness, meditation, and investigation, abandoning unwholesome states like the hindrances and identity view, and developing wholesome qualities and clarity. Retreats and monastic settings significantly facilitate this process by providing the necessary structure, simplicity, and community support for focused exploration and direct experiential learning about the nature of self and mind, ultimately leading towards liberation from suffering.
