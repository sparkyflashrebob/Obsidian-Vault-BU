Back at the end of 2006, when Trevor Jackson's Output Recordings folded, I put together a 3 hour tribute mix of my favorite tracks. This went out as 3 separate mixes on Solid Steel and I even made a very limited number of facsimile Output CDRs of the mixes. I've recently had requests to upload it again so, by the miracle that is Soundcloud, it's available. I've also edited it into one piece finally and the track list is embedded in the Lyrics section of the mp3.
