The Paradox of "Not Picking Up"

The core of the teaching involves the subtle layer of practice: not picking up

the idea of not picking things up. This addresses the common trap where the

ego co-opts the concept of letting go, turning "non-attachment" into just

another burden-a rigid standard used to judge one's own experience.

"Asking if a rock was really heavy... 'Well, it's not if you don't pick it up." — Ajahn

True non-picking up is the shift from an active doing (trying to let go) to a

passive allowing. Weight only exists when we attempt to claim or manage the

phenomenon.
