* **Practical Application:** Replacing aggressive self-correction with gentle curiosity and grounded breath awareness.

* **Core Observation:** Hyper-abundant consumer choices create decision fatigue and cognitive overwhelm.
* **Systemic / Contemplative Context:** Voluntary simplicity and intentional limits protect inner tranquility, freeing mental energy for presence, relationships, and dharma practice.
* **Practical Application:** Mindfully filtering incoming stimuli, saying no to non-essential commitments, and appreciating what is already sufficient.
