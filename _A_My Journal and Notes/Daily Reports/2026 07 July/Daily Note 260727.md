### Conditionality of the Physical Container — Standing vs. Suffering
- **Core Observation**: Practitioners often fall into ascetic martyrdom, believing that suffering through intense physical back pain or butt soreness proves meditative devotion.
- **Dhamma / Contemplative Framing**: The Middle Way rejects both sensual indulgence and self-mortification (*attakilamathānuyoga*). Somatic contractions and disc compressions are biological facts of conditionality (*Idappaccayatā*), not spiritual obstacles.
- **Practical Integration**: When our body or stomach is tight or our lower back is spasming, we can honor the body with compassion: stand up, soften the knees into *Zhan Zhuang*, and meditate on our feet.

### Demystifying the "Somatic" — Returning to the Living Senses
- **Core Observation**: Academic and clinical jargon around "somatic therapies" can alienate ordinary people seeking relief from stress.
- **Dhamma / Contemplative Framing**: In the *Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta*, the Buddha kept contemplation rooted in direct everyday reality: knowing when the breath is long or short, knowing when the body is walking, standing, sitting, or lying down.
- **Practical Integration**: There is no need for complex clinical theory; whenever anxiety arises, we can simply notice what has entered our senses (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind) and let the earth support our physical weight.
