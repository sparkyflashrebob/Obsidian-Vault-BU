- **Dhamma / Contemplative Framing**: In the Buddha's teachings on *kāyagatā-sati* (mindfulness directed to the body), the body is honored as a biological organism conditioned by past experiences (*saṅkhārā*). - **Practical Integration**: We can offer ourselves gentle trauma-informed adaptations: placing a comforting hand over the chest, taking long, audible sighs on the exhalation, keeping our gaze softly focused on the floor, and remembering that cultivating felt safety is the true foundation of peace.
