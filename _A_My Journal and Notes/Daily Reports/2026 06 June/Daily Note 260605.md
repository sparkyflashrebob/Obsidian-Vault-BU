- **Dhamma / Contemplative Framing**: The *Sallatha Sutta* (The Arrow) illustrates that physical discomfort or changed plans are merely the First Arrow—unavoidable sensory impacts. Grasping (*upādāna*) and resistance fire the Second Arrow of emotional suffering.
