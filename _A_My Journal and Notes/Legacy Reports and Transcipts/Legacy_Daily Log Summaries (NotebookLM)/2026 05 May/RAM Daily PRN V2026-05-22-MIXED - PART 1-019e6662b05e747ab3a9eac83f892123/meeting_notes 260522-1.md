- Jeffrey Epstein was cited as a prominent example of disparity, having received an 18-month plea deal despite allegedly abusing thousands of people.

- Addressing these systemic inequities was framed as essential to building a society that reduces animosity and improves quality of life for everyone.

- Racial justice was framed as requiring equal representation and systemic correction, not preferential treatment, but an honest reckoning with the structures that have perpetuated inequality.

### Mongol Invasions and Japanese Defense Strategy

- Watched was a video about the Mongol attempts to invade Japan, which prompted a discussion about their military tactics and ultimate failure.

- Mongol forces relied heavily on mounted cavalry and poison arrows, making open land the ideal terrain for their style of warfare.

- During the first invasion attempt, a storm destroyed the Mongol fleet before they could fully establish themselves on shore, forcing a retreat.

- When the Mongols returned years later, Japan had fundamentally altered its coastline by building a large seawall and removing beaches to eliminate landing zones.

- Without the ability to bring horses ashore or hoist them over the wall, the Mongol forces were rendered ineffective, successfully staving off the invasion.

- The broader context noted was that the Mongols had previously swept through Persia and into Turkey during the 1200s, demonstrating the scale of their historical reach before being stopped by Japan.
