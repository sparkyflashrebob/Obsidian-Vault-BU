**Indigenous Spirituality and Interconnectedness**
The sources discuss indigenous perspectives on the "web of life" and animism, describing the natural world not as inert resources but as sentient and responsive. This view suggests that human well-being is intrinsically linked to the well-being of the land, animals, and ancestors, and that the individual self is part of a larger network of relationships.
*   "The self is not a container bound by skin, but a node in a vast dynamic web of relationships."
*   "This we know earth does not belong to us belong to earth. This we know all things are connected like the blood which unites one family."
*   "The distinctive feature of indigenous spirituality is animism. The perception that Natural world is animate, sentient and responsive."
