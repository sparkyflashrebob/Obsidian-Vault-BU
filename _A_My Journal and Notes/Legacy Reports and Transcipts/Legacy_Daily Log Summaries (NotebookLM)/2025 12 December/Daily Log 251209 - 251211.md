1.1. Business-Related Log

The day's business activities were reactive, focused on mitigating on-site installation failures stemming from critical pre-production oversights. Efforts were concentrated on emergency procurement for a significant cable shortage, rectifying incorrect computer specifications, and performing a granular BOM audit to prevent further budget and timeline deviations.

Video Wall Cabling Shortage and Equipment Mismatch

* Subject: A significant cable shortage for a video wall project and incorrect computer specifications were identified and addressed.
* Details Covered:
  * A major discrepancy was discovered between the 12 existing cables on-site and the 24 required by the functional drawings for the LED wall.
  * The issue stemmed from a communication breakdown, where an account manager's plan to use existing wiring conflicted with the design engineer's requirements specified in the drawings.
  * An investigation was launched into the newly delivered computer's I/O, revealing it had USBC/Thunderbolt ports instead of the expected video outputs.
  * The Novastar processor's port configuration was reviewed, and a plan was made to redline the drawings to shift inputs to align with the hardware's capabilities.
* Action Items:
  * Order: An ICO was initiated to order four rolls of Cat 6 cable to address the shortage.
  * Pull: A field task was created to pull 14 new cables (12 for the wall and 2 spares) to match the drawing specifications.
  * Investigate: The field technician was tasked with taking a photo of the new computer's I/O to confirm the model and its specific ports.
  * Redline: The drawings for the Novastar processor will be redlined to shift all inputs down, ensuring the correct ports are used.
  * Procure: USBC to HDMI cables will be ordered to connect the new computer to the system. A shelf for the computer will also be procured.
  * Clarify: The internal process for ordering miscellaneous parts via a personal account versus a company account was clarified to expedite procurement.

Project Bill of Materials (BOM) Audit and Equipment Allocation Planning

* Subject: A comprehensive review of a large project's Bill of Materials was conducted, alongside planning for the allocation of equipment by room.
* Details Covered:
  * The large BOM was filtered by system and room location to organize and verify parts.
  * A plan was established to split bulk items into individual room-specific lists after OFD (Owner Furnished Dealer Installed) items are added to the master list. This will facilitate easier distribution on site.
  * A cost discrepancy of approximately $750 was identified. The decision was made to add a miscellaneous line item to zero out the budget difference rather than seek a change order.
  * A brief discussion occurred regarding the functionality of an Axiom panel intended for volume control in the "all hands" space.
* Action Items:
  * Postpone: The task of splitting the main BOM into individual room lists will be postponed until all OFD items are officially added.
  * Add: A miscellaneous line item will be added to the order to correct the project budget and resolve the cost discrepancy.
  * Begin: The process of organizing, allocating, and finalizing the room-by-room BOMs is scheduled to begin the following day.

These efforts to rectify pre-production issues underscored the high cost of project disruptions, a theme that would unexpectedly carry over into personal events.
