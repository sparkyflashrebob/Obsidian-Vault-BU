    *   **Details:** Sitting on rocks outdoors, Andy felt severe sitbone pain and a numb leg/ankle. ### Source Note: "Wednesday at 7-59 PM.txt" [Text]

    *   **Details:** Andy observed that when concentration is weak and the mind is highly restless, trying to force it to be still creates suffering.     *   **Details:** Dealing with chronic migraines, Andy noted that fighting or worrying about the pain breeds "second arrow" suffering. ### Source Note: "Wednesday at 8-07 PM.txt" [Text]
