> **[08:00 AM] Andy**: "Reflecting on the classic Middle Eastern / Zen parable of the donkey smuggler.
>
> A merchant crosses the border every single day leading a line of donkeys carrying heavy straw bags. The border guards know he is a smuggler, so they rip open the bags, search through the straw, sift through the dirt, and find nothing. Thirty years later, after the smuggler has retired as a wealthy man, the retired border guard meets him at a tea house and begs: 'Tell me, what on earth were you smuggling all those years?' >
> That is the exact mechanism of the ego. We obsessively search the 'bags' of our problems—our neuroses, our bank accounts, our relationship grievances, our physical aches—completely missing the donkey: the manufactured 'me' standing right in plain sight that was smuggled into existence!"
