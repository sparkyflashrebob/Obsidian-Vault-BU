## Journal Text & Reflections

Created 9/6/2000 and last entry as noted on 9/9/2000


What I Hear

Upon reflecting on the day I had these thoughts:
Look at how childhood effected me, for it was more than I thought.  In my session with R. Teaster I meditated and thought I saw myself.  First my eyes looking at me and then as a monkey and other animals.  Then I saw a young clean self.  Then lots of pain.  Says then not ready.  We need to look at our pain for growth.  Find our child self because that is healing and recovery.  Go back very young.  Abuse hurt mopre than I thought.  Robert Harris, wiped my man cause we clkimbed tree.  Family not first.  He shit on us.
Use meditation to search within/cleanse/find self.  Listening and reflection, conviction.
A sign on the chalk board in the step meeting said “Embrace the Child” then it said Family.

TK spoke at Step meeting – Step 3 – Made a decision to our will over to the care of God.
Care of god, get a guy to a meeting.
His wife Suzy started Steps to Freedom meeting 22 years ago.
Patience, Tolerance and Love of family.
Do not pump up our circumstances beyond the truth.  Ego boost.  Fake.  Hid and drank.
Falicy of “if outsides okay then insides are okay”.  Spirit is bad
Alcohol is but a symptom of our real defects of character.  We need a defense against the first drink.
Trouble begins when our drinking effects other aspects of our life (boss calls us in, wife leaves, etc.)
Big AL drink lets him out.  Doesn’t give a shit about us.
The world begins to make decisions for us.
We do not know how to weight the liabilities of things in our lives.
We create a lot of shame and guilt in our using.
Help others – welcome them
Sponsor is a coach of the steps.  BB pages 43 – 47.
How do you get a higher power.  By working steps 4 thru 11.
We need to share our knowledge, DON’T DRINK, GO TO MEETINGS.
Get some gratitude, thru no drink and meetings.
The magnitude of god’s gratitude is he accepts any feable attempt at step 3.
Self-will usually leads us to have a negative attitude w/ the world.  I think this led to my bottom.  RESTLESS, IRITABLE AND DISCONTENT.
As an alcoholic, ask yourself what have you done with your life.  We should want to turn over to anyone.  
3rd Step Prayer.  Page 63.
How do I do it…..Steps 4-9  (Action)
Selfishness and self-centeredness is the root of our problems.
Pg 84. Entered the world of the spirit.  Step 10 – 12 is sobriety.

Come to meetings, listen to god.

September 9, 2000	Just My Day

I have been in a funky mood.  I spoke at Ronald’s meeting on Thursday and felt really serene but the next mornings meeting got me in a bad mood.  Les, John and this new guy all read their parts very fast.  Les definitely has his problems.  I carried this anger with me all day and into the next despite my efforts and discussing it with friends and at a meeting.  Katie called me all day with her laying guilt trips to add to it.  Talked with the kids about what agitates her and asked that they not discuss too much about what we do with her.  I made it clear that was not to hid anything and that I did not them too worry about anything relating to it.  Later that day taking Kiera home, Pattie and I talked about AA, how her mom, Katie, and her husband Chris (who is in denial) are all addicts and alcolholics and things are unmanageable.  Katie got made because I was talking to Pattie.  I split.  The reminder that my program is working got me out of my funk.  (
