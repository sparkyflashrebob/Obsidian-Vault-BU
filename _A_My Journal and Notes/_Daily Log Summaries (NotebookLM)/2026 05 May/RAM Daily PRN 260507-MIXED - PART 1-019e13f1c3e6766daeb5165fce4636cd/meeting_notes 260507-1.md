### Fixed-Term Annuity vs CD Ladder for Bucket Two
- A three-to-five-year fixed-term annuity was discussed as an alternative to a CD ladder for the medium-term bucket, offering a slightly higher guaranteed interest rate of approximately 4.7%.
- Full return of principal at maturity was confirmed as a feature of the fixed-term annuity, distinguishing it from lifetime income annuities where principal recovery is not guaranteed.
- A lifetime income annuity was also briefly explained, noting that while it provides guaranteed income, principal is not returned unless a cash refund rider is selected.
- Liquidity constraints of the annuity were acknowledged, with the provision that up to 10% of the contract value can be withdrawn annually without surrender charges.
- The CD ladder was discussed as an alternative offering more liquidity, with the ability to reinvest maturing tranches annually, though the annuity was ultimately favored for its locked-in rate and tax-deferred growth.

### Tax Treatment of Annuity and Investment Accounts

- Capital gains tax treatment was clarified as applying to non-retirement brokerage accounts upon sale, not within annuity or IRA structures.
- State tax implications were briefly raised, with confirmation that the annuity wrapper itself does not create a separate state tax obligation beyond standard withdrawal taxation.
