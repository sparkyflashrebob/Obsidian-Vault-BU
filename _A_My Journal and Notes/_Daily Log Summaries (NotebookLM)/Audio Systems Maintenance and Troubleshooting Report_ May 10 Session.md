## 6. Technical Action Plan & System Specifications

The following specifications serve as the "ground truth" for the community's current audio configuration and future hardware acquisitions.

### Hardware Requirements
\*   \*\*Connectivity:\*\* Deploy USB hubs and high-quality USB-A to USB-C adapters. Ensure the USB signal is monitored, as USB audio is bidirectional.
\*   \*\*Blackmagic Interface:\*\* While analog connections are currently stable, the Blackmagic card should be researched for potential USB audio transmission to simplify the signal path.
\*   \*\*Power Supplies:\*\* Use the primary power supply for the current mixer; keep the new mixer’s supply boxed as a "spare" to avoid confusion.

### Mixer Configuration
\*   \*\*Routing Logic:\*\* The \*\*Right Channel\*\* serves as the master feed for both the YouTube/Live Stream and the recorder. This ensures a full mono mix is captured. 
\*   \*\*Panning Caution:\*\* The boundary mic should be panned to the \*\*Middle\*\* to be heard in the room and on the stream. Note: If a mic is panned \*\*Left\*\*, it will be missing from the recorder and YouTube feed, as those pull from the \*\*Right Output\*\*.
\*   \*\*Gain Staging:\*\* Set the main gain to the "nominal" area (marked on the board). Use the "trim" knobs to set a reference level that provides a good deflection on the meters without hitting the noise floor.
\*   \*\*Effect Management:\*\* All \*\*white effect buttons\*\* must remain \*\*OFF\*\*. These introduce unwanted reverb and electronic noise that degrade the clarity of the spoken word.

### Monitoring Workflow
\*   \*\*Critical Listening:\*\* Operators must use headphones to detect "noise floors" and electronic "freak-outs" that are not audible in the ambient room air.
\*   \*\*Adapter Procurement:\*\* Obtain additional 1/4" to 1/8" adapters so the recorder and the monitor headphones can remain plugged in simultaneously without swapping cables.
\*   \*\*Monitor Out:\*\* Use the "Monitor Out" for the headset to allow for discrete listening that does not impact the master levels of the recording or stream.

### Immediate Maintenance Tasks
\*   \*\*System Documentation:\*\* Take a clear photograph of the finalized mixer chart. This will serve as a visual "manual" for other community members to reset the board if settings are moved.
\*   \*\*Heat Monitoring:\*\* Actively listen for "capacitor noise" that increases after 30–45 minutes of operation. If noise grows with heat, the mixer or speaker is nearing the end of its life.
