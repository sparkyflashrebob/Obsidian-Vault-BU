It would question how an individual filters, processes, and emotionally regulates this constant input without becoming overwhelmed.
3. The Body as a Barometer for Inner Well-being. This thread would focus on the intimate connection between physical sensation and psychological state. Using the subject's commentary on nausea, pain, and potential illness, it would explore how the body serves as a primary source of information about stress, anxiety, and the need for self-care, often communicating truths the conscious mind avoids.
