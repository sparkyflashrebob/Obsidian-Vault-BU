3 LEVELS OF MINDFULNESS AS A GUIDE TO PEACEFULNESS

  

1. MINDFULNESS OF CONTENT
-Notice what is present
-Expand window of tolerance
-Able to be Present with a greater range of experience
-At peace with it
--"I can be with this"

2. Aware of the Process of Experience
-Impermanent
-Can't grasp
-Nothing can be repeated
-Unsatisfactory
-- How long will this last?
-Empty
--Experiences have no substance
-- Appear and disappear
- Not me or mine
-Metta realm
-Wisdom and Peacefulness

3. Awareness of Consciousness itself
-Who or what is knowing this experience
-Looking directly at mind
- Mun to Chah - Experiences are just Experiences.
--Liberating question: To whom do they happen?
--Turn attention back to the one who knows.
--Become the knowing itself
--Original Mind - Consciousness itself
---Timeless
---Unborn
---Ever Present
---Not even “me”
-Consciousness itself
--Try to stop being aware

True Equanimity is the capacity to be spacious, loving & peaceful as things arise and pass
