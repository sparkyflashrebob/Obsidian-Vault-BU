## 📂 Volume Summaries

### 1. [[Abhayagiri Monastic Retreat 2010-2011 - Handwritten Journal Transcripts.md|Part 1 (2010–2011 Retreat)]]
- **Archival PDF**: [Abhayagiri Retreat Journal 2010-2011 (Little Book Part 1 - 26 pages).pdf](<file:///G:/Google Drive (260611)/Library (Books and Articles)/Research PDFs/Retreat Journals Scans/Abhayagiri Retreat Journal 2010-2011 (Little Book Part 1 - 26 pages).pdf>)
- **Core Contemplative Insights**:
  - **Somatic Dukkha & "The Claw"**: Experiencing head tightness and brain cramping as the physical manifestation of *dukkha* and conditionality, not personal defect.
  - **The Four Foundations of Mindfulness (*Satipaṭṭhāna*)**: Methodical body, feeling, mind, and dhamma observation.
  - **Fire Sermon & Sense Animal Anchor**: Grounding mindfulness at the center while sense impressions pull outwards like animals.
  - **Four Factors of Stream Entry (*Sotāpatti-aṅga*)**: Association with the wise, hearing the Dhamma, wise reflection (*yoniso manasikāra*), and practicing Dhamma in accordance with Dhamma.

### 2. [[Abhayagiri Monastic Retreat 2019 - Handwritten Journal Transcripts.md|Part 2 (2019 Retreat)]]
- **Archival PDF**: [Abhayagiri Retreat Journal 2019 (Little Book Part 2 - 35 pages).pdf](<file:///G:/Google Drive (260611)/Library (Books and Articles)/Research PDFs/Retreat Journals Scans/Abhayagiri Retreat Journal 2019 (Little Book Part 2 - 35 pages).pdf>)
- **Core Contemplative Insights**:
  - **Point of View of Pure Consciousness**: Pure awareness without hierarchical levels; embodiment of stillness.
  - **"The Tiger in the Cage" (Page 29)**: Intense mental desires and agitations are like a trapped tiger—do not jump into the cage with it; allow it to tire itself out.
  - **Ānāpānasati Tetrad on Relinquishment**: Investigating *anicca*, *virāga* (fading of passion), *nirodha* (cessation / non-arising), and *paṭinissagga* (letting go).
  - **The Five Hindrances as Avijjā's Nourishment**: Classical similes (debt, illness, prison, slavery, doubt) and recognizing the mind's trickery ("LIAR!").
