## 📋 Document Overview & Doctrinal Context

This document contains the complete, verbatim transcription of Andy's 35 scanned handwritten notebook pages recorded during the December 2019 Abhayagiri Annual Monastic Retreat held at the Applegate Retreat Center.

### Core Themes Surfacing in Part 2
- **Point of View of Pure Consciousness**: Opening with non-dual direct inquiry—no hierarchy of experience, whether sipping tea or in samādhi.
- **Anāpānasati Sutta Progressive Exploration**: Deep systematic immersion into the 16 steps of breathing mindfulness, particularly focusing on the final tetrad: impermanence (*anicca*), fading of passion (*virāga*), cessation (*nirodha*), and relinquishment (*paṭinissagga*).
- **The "Tiger in the Cage" Simile (Page 29)**: A powerful handwritten contemplative metaphor and box diagram: strong desires and mental storms are like a tiger in a cage. *Don't jump into the cage with it!* Just stand outside, let it wear itself out until it gets tired and purrs like a kitten ("meow").
- **Avijjā and the Five Hindrances (Pages 16–18)**: Classical similes for the hindrances (sensual desire as debt, aversion as illness, sloth/torpor as imprisonment, restlessness as slavery, skeptical doubt as being lost), identifying them as direct nourishment for ignorance (*avijjā*).
- **Upāsikā Kee & Ajahn Sumedho**: Teachings on "unentangled knowing", non-interference, and the realization that *"my personality will not get enlightened."*
- **Somatic Healing & Softening**: Calming the forehead, softening the belly, allowing physical illness (sore throat/cold) to be held in awareness without making a dramatic personal story out of sensation.
