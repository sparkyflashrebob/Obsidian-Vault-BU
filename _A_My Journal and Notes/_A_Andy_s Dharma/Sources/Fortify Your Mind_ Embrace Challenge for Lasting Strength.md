Actively facing fear and challenges—rather than avoiding them—lays the foundation for mental toughness that repels criticism like water off a duck's back.([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/posts/just-dropped-why-131150316?l=es&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Why Your Mind Gets Weaker Every Day (And How to Fix It)"))
