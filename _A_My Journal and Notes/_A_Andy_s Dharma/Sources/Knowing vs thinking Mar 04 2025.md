Knowing vs thinking
Meeting date: March 04, 2025, 06:32 am
Overview
The meeting focused on the distinction between knowing and thinking, exploring the complexities of human experience as it relates to meditation and mindfulness. The speaker reflected on the clarity of direct experience versus the challenges of articulating those experiences through language, emphasizing that while knowing can be immediate and profound, thinking often complicates this clarity by imposing labels and definitions. The discussion highlighted the limitations of language in capturing the essence of experiences, particularly in the context of meditation, where sensations may not fit neatly into categories of pleasant or unpleasant. The speaker expressed a desire to improve their ability to convey these insights to others, acknowledging the importance of vocabulary while also recognizing its constraints in fully representing the richness of human experience.
