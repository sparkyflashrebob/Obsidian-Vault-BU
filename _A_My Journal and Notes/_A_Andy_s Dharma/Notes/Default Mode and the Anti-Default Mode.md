The concept of "AntiDefault Mode" describes a state of avoidance of self, where individuals constantly engage in activities, often bordering on workaholism, to steer clear of internal reflection. This perpetual busyness serves as a mechanism to avoid being present and to escape the discomfort of the now. Essentially, it highlights a pattern of distracting oneself with external tasks to prevent confronting one's own thoughts and feelings.
