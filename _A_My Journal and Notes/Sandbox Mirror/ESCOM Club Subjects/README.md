## 🌿 Core Thematic Pillars & Dhamma Framing

- **The Two Wings of Practice (Tara Brach)**: Balancing *Mindfulness (Clear Seeing)* and *Compassion (An Open Heart & Caring Action)* — head and heart together.
- **The Two Arrows (*Sallatha Sutta*)**: Distinguishing the unavoidable first arrow (raw sensory data, aches, difficult events) from what our thinking mind adds on (judgment, resistance, self-blame).
- **Conditionality (*Idappaccayatā*)**: Somatic reactions (stomach knotted up, shoulder tension) are natural survival responses to perceived stress, not personal or moral flaws.
- **Animals vs. Humans in Threat Response**: Nature shakes off danger when safe; humans keep the threat alive in thought (*"We think about the tiger when there is no tiger"*).
- **Habit of Stress into Skillful Energy**: Shifting away from chronic worry to preserve vital life energy for grounded everyday actions.
