## 🕊️ Facilitation Principles & Grounding Reminders

1. **The Two Wings of Practice (Tara Brach)**:
   - *Mindfulness (Clear Seeing)*: Discerning raw sensory facts from mental stories.
   - *Compassion (Warm Heart & Caring Action)*: Meeting human vulnerability with warmth.

2. **The Two Arrows (*Sallatha Sutta*)**:
   - *First Arrow*: The unavoidable sensation entering our senses (an ache, fatigue, a utility bill, unexpected news).
   - *Second Arrow*: What the frantic mind adds on (self-blame, catastrophizing, resistance). Practice rests at the first arrow.

3. **Conditionality (*Idappaccayatā*)**:
   - Somatic sensations (tightness in the belly, knot in the stomach, pressure in the temples) are natural biological threat responses, never personal or moral failures.

4. **Animals vs. Humans in Threat Response**:
   - A deer flees danger, shakes off the shock, and returns to grazing. As humans, we keep the threat alive in thought: *"We think about the tiger when there is no tiger."*

5. **Rewiring the Habit of Stress into Skillful Energy**:
   - Stress easily becomes a chronic default habit of the mind. Meeting it with awareness frees vital energy for grounded, skillful living.
