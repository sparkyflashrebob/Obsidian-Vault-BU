Core Competencies & Instructional Skills

  • Attention & Cognitive Focus Training: Teaching focused-attention anchoring (breath/sensory anchors) and open-monitoring to mitigate digital distraction and mental fatigue.
  • Somatic Stress Regulation: Helping students deconstruct physical fight-or-flight tension (tight stomach, clenched muscles) as conditioned biological reflexes rather than personal shortcomings.
  • The Parable of the Two Arrows: Training students to clearly separate raw sensory input (First Arrow) from narrative catastrophizing and secondary distress (Second Arrow).
  • Mindful Communication Dyads: Guiding partner listening and speaking exercises to develop non-defensive presence and empathy in personal and professional relationships.
  • Trauma-Informed & Inclusive Pedagogy: Creating welcoming, psychologically safe classroom environments adaptable to students of all backgrounds and physical abilities.
