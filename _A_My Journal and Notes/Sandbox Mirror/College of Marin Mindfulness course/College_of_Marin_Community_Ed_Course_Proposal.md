### Page 2 Form Fields

#### 19. Course Outline (Classroom Content by Week)

| Week # / Hours | Classroom Content & Experiential Exercises |
| :---: | :--- |
| **Week 1**<br>*(Hours 1–2)* | **Foundations of Mindfulness & Attention: The Spotlight, The Floodlight & Everyday Benefits**<br>• Real-world benefits & the "mental gym"; Demystifying mindfulness; sensory anchors; working with cognitive drift; 60-second reset.<br>• The architecture of attention: Focused Attention (the spotlight) vs. Open Monitoring (the floodlight).<br>• Guided exercise: Sensory breath anchoring, working with the wandering mind without self-judgment, and the 60-second daily reset. |
| **Week 2**<br>*(Hours 3–4)* | **Single-Tasking & Focus in the Digital Age: Working with Distraction**<br>• The neuroscience of attention: cognitive switching costs, dopamine loops, and continuous partial attention.<br>• Developing meta-awareness: catching the impulse to check devices or multitask.<br>• Experiential exercise: Mindful single-tasking lab; establishing intentional focus blocks and sensory resets during study and work. |
| **Week 3**<br>*(Hours 5–6)* | **The Two Wings of Practice: Balancing Clear Seeing and an Open Heart**<br>• Clear Seeing (Mindfulness/Head): looking honestly at what is happening without distortion.<br>• The Open Heart (Compassion/Heart): meeting our vulnerability with warmth and caring action.<br>• Why mindfulness without heart can turn into cold self-criticism, and compassion without clarity leads to emotional overwhelm.<br>• Experiential exercise: Combining objective awareness with kind self-dialogue. |
| **Week 4**<br>*(Hours 7–8)* | **Stress Physiology & Somatic Conditionality: Understanding the Body's Reflexes**<br>• The biology of threat: why our stomach becomes tight or knotted up, throat constricts, and shoulders rise under perceived pressure.<br>• Conditionality: recognizing somatic contractions as natural biological survival responses, never personal failures.<br>• Animals vs. Humans in threat recovery: the deer shaking off danger vs. humans "thinking about the tiger when there is no tiger."<br>• Guided practice: Somatic release, progressive relaxation, and soothing the nervous system. |
| **Week 5**<br>*(Hours 9–10)* | **The Parable of the Two Arrows: Discerning Primary Impact from Mental Fiction**<br>• The First Arrow: unavoidable sensory impressions entering through the senses (unexpected bills, rude remarks, checkout counter delays, physical discomfort).<br>• The Second Arrow: what the frantic mind adds on (stories of doom, victimhood, self-blame, and catastrophizing).<br>• Mindful pause: arresting the second arrow before it spirals, freeing up vital energy for grounded problem-solving. |
| **Week 6**<br>*(Hours 11–12)* | **Mindful Communication & Compassion: Deep Listening in Daily Life**<br>• Deep listening: hearing another person fully without preparing a rebuttal or defense.<br>• Recognizing defensive bodily contractions during interpersonal tension.<br>• The neuroscience of compassion: fostering emotional safety, empathy, and goodwill.<br>• Experiential partner dyad: Mindful speaking and reflective listening exercise. |
| **Week 7**<br>*(Hours 13–14)* | **Rewiring the Habit of Stress: A Sustainable Blueprint for Daily Living**<br>• Transforming stress from a chronic mental habit into calm, skillful vitality.<br>• Navigating common obstacles: restlessness, sluggishness, doubt, and perfectionism.<br>• Integrating micro-pauses into routines (commuting, cooking, walking, transitions).<br>• Culminating workshop: Sharing individual personal mindfulness blueprints and closing group reflection. |

### Next Steps in the Community Ed Hiring Process
1. **Email Materials:** Send the 3 attachments to `proposal@marincommunityed.com`.
2. **Staff Review & Interview:** The Director of Community Education reviews the proposal and schedules an interview if the course is viable.
3. **Curriculum Review & Board Approval:** The course proposal is sent to the District Curriculum Committee to ensure no conflict with credit courses, and then to the College of Marin Board of Trustees for final approval.
4. **Course Scheduling & Marketing:** Once approved, you are hired as a COM Community Education instructor at **$53.84/hour**, and your course is published in the Community Education catalog mailed to over 110,000 Marin County residents.
