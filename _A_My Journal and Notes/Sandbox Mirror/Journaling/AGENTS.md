# Journaling Project Guidelines & Rules

## Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to capture, organize, categorize, and archive personal journal entries and writings provided by the user. These entries are prepared and categorized for placement into the user's Obsidian vault folder:
`C:\Users\SparkyDesktop\OneDrive - AVDG, LLC\Documents\Obsidian Vault\Flash Rebob`

This workspace also serves as a central hub for journal writings, thematic categorization, and linking to other related projects.

---

## 1. Non-AI-Generated Content (Strict Core Rule)
- **100% Authentic Human Writing**: All journal entries, reflections, thoughts, and writings are strictly **non-AI-generated in their entirety**.
- **No Ghostwriting or Embellishing**: The AI assistant must NEVER generate synthetic journal entries, fabricate personal reflections, or alter the authentic voice, emotions, or wording of the author.
- **Role of the Assistant**: The AI operates strictly as an archivist, curator, and organizer:
  - Capturing and preserving the author's exact words, dictations, notes, and raw journal writings.
  - Applying clean formatting, frontmatter metadata (dates, tags, categories).
  - Categorizing entries by theme, date, or subject.
  - Placing and syncing entries into `Flash Rebob` in Obsidian as personal journal entries and notes.
  - Maintaining cross-references and links to related projects where appropriate.

---

## 2. Obsidian Integration & Storage (Learned Rule)
- **Obsidian Target Path**:
  `C:\Users\SparkyDesktop\OneDrive - AVDG, LLC\Documents\Obsidian Vault\Flash Rebob`
- **Automatic Copy / Sync**:
  Whenever any journal or journaling entry is captured or processed in Antigravity, it is automatically copied into the `Flash Rebob` Obsidian folder as a journal entry for the user's journal and notes.
- **File Format & Frontmatter**:
  Entries should include clean Obsidian-compatible YAML frontmatter:
  ```yaml
  ---
  Created: YYYY-MM-DD
  tags:
    - journal
    - [relevant-topic]
  category: [Category Name]
  ---
  ```
- **Markdown Standards**:
  - Use clean standard Markdown formatting.
  - Use responsive Markdown tables instead of fixed-width ASCII box-drawing art.
  - Preserve any direct user edits, specific examples, and phrasing adjustments unconditionally.

---

## 3. Project Workflow
1. **Capture**: Any writing or reflection the user adds into Antigravity is recorded accurately without rewriting.
2. **Categorize**: Analyze tags, themes, and categories based on the entry's natural topics and connections to related projects.
3. **Store & Sync**:
   - Save the master entry in this workspace (`g:\Documents\_Antigravity Sandbox\Journaling\Entries`).
   - Copy the entry directly into `Flash Rebob` in the Obsidian vault (`C:\Users\SparkyDesktop\OneDrive - AVDG, LLC\Documents\Obsidian Vault\Flash Rebob`) as a journal entry.
