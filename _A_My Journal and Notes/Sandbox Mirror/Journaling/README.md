# Journaling & Writings Archive

This project captures, organizes, and categorizes personal journal entries and writings, syncing them directly to the **Flash Rebob** folder in Obsidian while maintaining an organized local archive that connects to related projects.

## Learned Instructions & Key Principles
- **Automatic Sync to Flash Rebob**: Whenever any journal or journaling entry is captured in Antigravity, it is automatically copied into:
  `C:\Users\SparkyDesktop\OneDrive - AVDG, LLC\Documents\Obsidian Vault\Flash Rebob`
  as a journal entry for the user's journal and notes.
- **100% Non-AI Generated**: Every entry is purely the author's authentic writing and thoughts. No AI fabrication or synthetic journal content.
- **Categorization**: Entries are categorized by date, tags, and thematic topics with clean YAML frontmatter.

## Workspace Layout
- `GEMINI.md` / `AGENTS.md`: Core workspace rules and learned instructions.
- `Entries/`: Categorized journal entries and writings saved in this workspace.
- `Flash Rebob Sync`: Automatically mirrored in the Obsidian Vault.
