### Guided Meditation Transition (Standard)

I invite you now to find a comfortable, upright posture WHILE we settle into a period of meditation. 
**At the end I will ring a bell like this.** 

[BELL]

Let your body settle into your chair or cushion. 
Feel the support of the earth beneath you holding your weight.
You might do a quick scan of the body relaxing where there is any tension.  
Again taking a relaxing breath
And If it feels right, let your eyes softly close. Bringing your attention into the body.
If not, you can keep a gentle, unfocused gaze resting slightly downward.
The most important thing is that we bring our attention inward. 
Being present in the here and now and in the body.

[PAUSE]

As awareness deepens into the body, 
You might notice sensations, tension or tightness, 
you might notice tingling,
or you might become aware of the movement of the breath.
you feel the breath in the belly
or in the chest
or in the throat
or maybe at the tip of the nose
cool air coming in and warm air coming out
deep or shallow
where ever you feel it is okay
simply resting in this feeling of the rhythm of the breath

[PAUSE]

After a moment or two you might start to notice differing aspect of experience coming up. 

You might notice the mind commenting on things 
Or thinking of things that you carried here into the meeting
Or planning your next meal, or what you have to do tomorrow.

You might gently noting these as thinking, thinking
or planning, planning

And let the thoughts come and go
Returning back over and over to 
the sensation of breathing.

Maybe even asking, what kind of breathing would feel good right now?
And simply resting on this awareness of breathing in the body

Returning over and over to the present and to this body.


[BELL]

We will take a 5-minute break.

# Standard Close

[Pause]

In traditional closing circles, there’s a practice of distilling the whole day down to its absolute essence.

And when we strip everything else away, we realize... awakening isn't about collecting clever theories. It isn't about spiritual status. And it isn't about locking down some flawless, permanent state of calm.

Instead, it’s a simple, moment-to-moment commitment.

A commitment to dropping our defenses. Telling the rigorous truth about what we’re feeling. And meeting our shared human messiness... with kindness, curiosity, and unconditional presence.

[Pause]

The true sanctuary isn't tucked away on a distant mountain peak. It’s right here. In the middle of our ordinary, everyday lives.

So as we step back out into the world tonight, the invitation is simple:

Can we catch our contractions with tenderness?

Can we drop the need to perform, defend, or control... and rest, instead, as the open, unarmored witness of our lives?

[Pause]

To seal our practice tonight, I invite you to place a hand over your heart, or simply rest your hands softly in your lap.

Let us offer these intentions of goodwill—first to ourselves, and then outward to all beings:

[Pause]

_May I be safe and protected from harm._

_May I be healthy, strong, and at ease in this body._

_May I meet whatever arises with wisdom, grace, and an open heart._

_May I live with peace._

[Pause]

Now, expanding this field of goodwill to encompass our loved ones, our neighbors, this group, and all living beings everywhere:

_May all beings be safe and protected._

_May all beings be healthy and at ease._

_May all beings awaken to their true, spacious nature._

_May all beings live in peace._

Thank you, everyone, for your practice, your presence, and your heartful sharing tonight. I wish you a gentle and restful week ahead.
