
DRUM CARE AND MAINTENANCE

With proper care and maintenance, a drum should give long-lasting performance. Environmental conditions play a key role in maintaining a healthy drum. Optimal conditions for a drum are temperatures between 69 and 78° with moderate humidity. It takes practice to know what a drum needs to keep it in range of good tone. I encourage drum owners to develop a relationship with a drum just as you would with a person or a pet. Each drum has its own personality.

High temperatures are drums worst enemy. Heat, accompanied by low humidity, can ruin a drum. The drum will tighten and the pitch will increase above 78°. A drum exposed to dry temperatures of 90° or more for long periods will suffer damage.  Overexposure to high temperatures or any direct heat source will tighten the skin and cause the lacing to break and or the wood to warp.  If a drum becomes overheated you can quickly cool it down by splashing water on the skin and lacing or placing a wet cloth or towel around it.  Test the tone until it reaches the comfort zone. Moderate amounts of water will not harm the drum unless it becomes soggy or remains wet for an extended period of time.

An ideal way to travel with your drum in hot water is to place it in a plastic bag along with a wet cloth tucked in a ziplock bag with the top open.  Seal the drum and open the Ziploc bag inside the plastic bag.  This will keep moisture around the drum. keeping it relatively safe from heat.

*Humidity affects the tone.* Dry air tightens the skin; moist air loosens it. If humidity dampens your drum to a dull tone, bring in into a warm room or place it in sunlight or near another heat source to tighten the skin.  Test the drum periodically until it reaches the desired tone. *<u>Never overexpose your drum to any heat source.</u>*

*Clean your drum* by sponging off with warm soapy water then letting it dry in a warm environment. In time, a drum will need to be released or reskinned, procedures best left to a drum maker.  If maintained with care, however, a drum should last many years before requiring any other than an occasional cleaning.
