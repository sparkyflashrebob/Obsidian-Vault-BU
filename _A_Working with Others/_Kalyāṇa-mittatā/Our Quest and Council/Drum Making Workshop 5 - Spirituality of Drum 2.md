"Grandfather, it is me. There will be a time, I will come, you will hear my voice, you will live forever." They could hear voices of women and men trilling. Then came a heartbeat and all the animals came and danced, all the birds and insects and lightning came out of the earth. The stone people came out of the water and danced. Trees and plants came out and danced in the heal the people. The rhythm regulated their heartbeats and they began to shake as if they were electrified. It was the rhythm of the drum that did the healing. They kept drumming and hearing all the sounds of the Earth in the drum. The drum became a sacred way and everyone gathered around the drum and began to dance. When people feel good they dance. People who could not see were healed; those who could not walk they got up and danced. Even those who could not taste or smell or healed with the rhythm of the drum.

We dedicate, we affirm, we hear the voice of God when the drums beaten. In the drum are all the animals, plants, and natural things; there is lightning and thunder; everything is there and the sound of the drum. That is the way the story of the drum was told. The drums a special gift to the people. The voice of [[Tunkashila]], Grandfather and all that is holy in the drum- it is recreation for the people; when the drum sounds, the people become recreated with the heartbeat of the drum.

The world became contaminated with a smoke that came over the water; it came from the place where we now called the Mediterranean Sea. The sound of the drum will clear this contamination; through the drum a messenger can come and pray with that beating voice.

The sound of the drum is the sound of the Holy Mother. When men deny God and Holy Mother, when they say "God who are you?" - that is when the animals pray for us.  Men want to create something different to God; men create nothing. [[Tunkashila]] sparks a spirit around the world; they offer their lives and their blood become stone. We make our sacred pipes from the stone.

Fire was the center of grandmother and grandfather and around the fire we drum. Here we contact our ancestors; we are not separate. We can bring back this with the drum we bring back the spirit.

All people have drums they make drums from wood, trees, and skin. Black, yellow, red, and white - they all have drums, and we don't remember who had them first. The word drum remains what means wooden bucket.  It is also called She-he, because we all came out of the drum or womb in She, we came out of woman.

When you were born, that is your second birthday. Before your second birthday, you were in She's womb, you were listening to the drum beat of the great mother. That is why the drum is so healing because you remember, you go back into the safety of where you came from. you get reconnected from where you came from.

Everyday is sacred, not just one day of the week. When you drum, you connect with the sacred.  The drum contains all the animals, plants, minerals, lightning and thunder. All of the natural and sacred world is in the drum. The voice of God and the Holy Mother and all of the the two leggeds are in the drum, so when we drum we link ourselves to the tones of the rocks, water, air, wind and rain into She-he and He-she.

The drum represents the whole world, she knows everything, she gave birth to all that is living. When we make a drum, we make an imitation of the mother; she is wisdom. We play on her and beat and throb with her rhythm when we dance, pray, and sing with the sacred drum.
