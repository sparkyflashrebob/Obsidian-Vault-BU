## The Drum is the heart, the Heart is the drum.

The drum connects our hearts to the heart of mother Earth, it gives the soul a voice to sing our life's song to the Creator. 

The heart is the home of our intelligence (Higher Power). 

The heart,  which is our connecting link to creator and the universe, integrates (brings together) our own unique (matchless) center of experience, an awareness that is beyond our understanding. 

The heart is magnetic (attracting), silent and still.  The feeling of being like resting in a peaceful and beautiful lake, or floating in a vacuous (empty) space.

As a magnetic center (attracting), our Hearts are the generator (the maker) of all our life's energy, and whatever we empower (allow) our Hearts to be open, we raise our energy level physically, mentally, emotionally, and spiritually.

Within the heart, we may also find clarity, resolution, steadfastness, intent, stillness, respect, justice, and kindness.

The heart is essentially (fundamentally-deeply and vitality) the center point of stillness in space from which we can view (experience) our connection to the Creator.

It is in our Hearts that we are able to know that our soul is the integrated (included) in the oneness of love, spirit, experience, deeds, hopes and dreams that comprise (make up) our lives.

The heart is the soul's gateway, both on this earthwalk, and the Spirit-world. The heart is the timeless and indestructible (unbreakable) source of all are higher knowledge.

It is the one point within each of us where the scene and unseen worlds come together and are the same.

Within our hearts, the will of the Creator and our own will wills may be brought to into harmony. 

this is the point within each of us where the physical, spirituals emotionals intellectual parts of our earthwalk are in perfect harmony.  At that point, there is no difference in time and space, this is the "[[zero point]]" which we knew before we came to our earthwalk, and we will know again when we enter the Spirit- world, and anytime in between when our will is in perfect harmony (our life song)
