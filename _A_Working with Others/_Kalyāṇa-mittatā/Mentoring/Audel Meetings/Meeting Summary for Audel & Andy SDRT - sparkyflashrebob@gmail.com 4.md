## Summary

### Trump Birthday Protest Planning Discussion

Andy and Audel discussed Andy's recent experience with COVID-19, which left him feeling flu-like for several days. They also talked about a protest organized by a friend on a bridge, which had a smaller turnout than previous events. Andy mentioned that there are over 1,300 events planned across the country for June 14th, not for Juneteenth as initially thought, but to mark Trump's birthday and the 250th anniversary of the U.S. military. They briefly touched on the importance of combining politics with personal experiences and the need for unity rather than division.

### Mud Run and Summer Updates

Audel discussed his recent participation in a mud run and his subsequent 4-mile treadmill run, noting his soreness due to the different muscle engagement from the obstacles. He mentioned that his nephew won the adult wave of the mud run, and Audel's family wave placed third. Audel also shared that his wife and son, along with her two dogs, are the only ones currently at home, and he highlighted the start of summer school, which aims to help students catch up or stay on track academically. Andy expressed his fatigue due to traffic congestion caused by construction on the Richmond Bridge, which has led to significant delays in the area.

### Meditation and Gut-Brain Insights

Andy and Audel discussed their meditation experience, noting how they felt relaxed and drifted into a meditative state. Andy shared his research on the gut-brain connection and mentioned positive experiences with burping, which Audel humorously supported with a "Better out than in" comment. They agreed to extend their meditation session, with Andy adjusting the volume and Audel expressing willingness to continue. Both acknowledged the challenges of staying awake during meditation and reflected on their thoughts and experiences.

### Democratic Socialist Values Discussed

Audel mentioned attending an all-day board meeting for the Nebraska School of Counseling Association this Saturday. Andy shared information about a Democratic Socialist Alliance meeting where a new member of the Milwaukee City Council was discussed. The council member, who was appointed to replace a deceased politician, spoke about democratic socialist values and caring for people, housing, and food.

### Powerful Ordination Speech Recording

Andy shared a recording of a powerful speech given by a person during a city council meeting, which was met with a standing ovation. He discussed topics like life on life terms, awareness, and conditionality, mentioning Buddhism in relation to some concepts. Andy explained that the speech was part of a ceremony where the person was being ordained, and he provided a link to the recording on Granicus, the city's recording system. Audel expressed interest and agreed to watch the recording.

### Community Media Management Insights

Andy shared his extensive experience working in local government and broadcasting, including managing city council meetings, planning commissions, and a cable TV channel. He described his work with various community figures and religious shows, highlighting his interactions with individuals like Charlene Jackson and Dorothy Mullen. Andy also mentioned his role in managing Chuck Johnson's Soul Beat, a 24-hour station, and the challenges he faced with Johnson's late bill payments.

### Lost Opportunities and Disappointment

Andy shared two personal stories: one about smoking pot with black businessmen and passing out, and another about creating a documentary on black cowboys that was later edited without his credit. He expressed disappointment that his name was removed from the documentary and the footage was not preserved, despite his efforts to find it years later.

### Exploring Historical Figures and Archives

Andy and Audel discussed historical figures and events, including Chuck Johnson and Dorothy Mullen. They explored digital archives and shared personal experiences. Andy mentioned plans to compile a folder of personal history and go camping in the desert. They agreed to stay in touch and support each other as needed.
