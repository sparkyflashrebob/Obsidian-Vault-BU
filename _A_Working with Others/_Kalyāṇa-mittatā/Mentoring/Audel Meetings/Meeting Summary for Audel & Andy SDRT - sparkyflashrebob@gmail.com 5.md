They also talked about Andy's practice of meditation and his concerns about current global issues, which have motivated him to take action. Andy and Audel discussed a spirituality and awakening program, which Andy had already completed and was sharing with Audel. They agreed that Andy would write a blog post about this meditation method, and Andy also planned to create a non-AI graphic to accompany it.
