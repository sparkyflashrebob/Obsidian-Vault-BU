**Summary** **Standardized Tests and Spiritual Practice** Andy and Audel discuss the end of the school year and upcoming final exams. They talk about standardized tests like the SAT and ACT, noting their regional preferences and the bias found in these tests against low-income and first-generation students. Andy mentions reactivating his Marin Mindful Recovery website, focusing more on spiritual practice and resilience building. Audel shares his experience of posting radical content on social media and the surprising positive reactions from friends, including his nephew who is learning about social issues in college. 

**Social Media and Work Phone Anxiety** Andy and Audel discussed their experiences with social media and cell phones. Andy shared his anxiety when he first received a work phone and how it has evolved into a tool for him. They also talked about the challenges of keeping up with social media notifications and the importance of setting boundaries. Andy mentioned his intention to reach out to people more effectively and his struggles with personal issues. **Andy's Personal 

Experiences and Grounding** Andy shared a personal experience about a tragic car accident involving a 14-year-old girl named Olive. The accident occurred when a driver crossed a divider into the wrong lane, causing a car to burst into flames. Andy also mentioned attending a march for immigration and encountering a homeless man experiencing a mental health crisis. The discussion highlighted the importance of being around others and the grounding effect of observing different personalities and situations. 

**Protesting and Meditation Practice Discussion** Andy shares his experience of protesting and connecting with people at a recent event. He feels it's a positive way to channel his energy and take action on issues he cares about, rather than just holding onto frustration. Andy values the sense of community and connection he gets from being around others during these activities. The conversation then shifts to their meditation practice, with Andy expressing frustration over recent changes to the Insight Timer app that make it difficult for him to access the basic timer function. They eventually decide to use a simple timer for their 10-minute meditation session. 

**Meditation and Awareness in Life Changes** Andy and Audel discussed their meditation experiences. Andy shared his practice of monitoring his awareness during meditation and the importance of balancing relaxation and awareness. They also talked about the challenges of using a timer during meditation, with Andy expressing concerns about his confidence in the clock. Additionally, Andy reflected on the constant changes in life and how it can be easier to see them when looking at the long-term. 

**Andy's Presence and Mindful Agency** Andy discussed the importance of being present and not getting caught up in expectations or goals. He emphasized the need to focus on what's right in front of you and not get anxious about what might happen. Andy also touched on the concept of the mind constantly seeking something to grasp onto, and how this can lead to a sense of agency. Audel shared her experience of feeling exhausted due to the busy nature of April and May. 

**Andy's Hawaii Trip and Housing Issues** Andy and Audel discussed their upcoming 10-year anniversary trip to Hawaii. Andy mentioned that he had booked flights, accommodations, and a rental car, and was now focusing on planning the itinerary. Audel confirmed that Andy would be cancelling a previous event in October. Andy also shared that he and his partner, Andrea, were planning to visit a bed and breakfast in Volcano Forest Retreat, near the Kilauea volcano. Additionally, Andy discussed the issue of affordable housing in California, specifically in Marin City, where a proposed housing structure was met with objections from the neighboring Sausalito community. 

**Community Building and Identity Concerns** Andy and Audel discussed a community event in Marin City, where they plan to socialize with the local community over tea and cookies. Andy expressed his understanding of the importance of community building but also raised concerns about the focus on identity and the potential for this to cause problems. They ended the conversation with a shared hope for a more harmonious future in the next 500 to 1000 years.
