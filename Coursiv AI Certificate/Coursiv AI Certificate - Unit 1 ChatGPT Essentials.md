# Lesson 2 Modes & Features chat GPT's Toolkit

The Job Hunt Challenge
Imagine you're preparing for a job interview. You need 
- company research, 
- a tailored answer to 'tell me about yourself,' 
- and three practice questions 
 
-— all from ChatGPT. Using the same approach for all three would slow you down.

This lesson shows you how to match the right ChatGPT mode and feature to the right task.

Introduction
Previously, ChatGPT operated on different models. If you've tried it before, you might've seen models like GPT-3, o-3, GPT-4, and so on.

Each model was best at its specific field: some at writing, some at coding, some were fast, some took their time to respond.

Say you need to quickly write a summary section in your resume ASAP. What would you choose?

A mode that generates summary options immediately

A mode that asks clarifying questions about your preferences first
Correct answer
Exactly! When speed matters and the task is straightforward, you want immediate output without extra steps.

For quick, straightforward tasks, Instant Mode is your best choice. It prioritizes speed and delivers results without additional questions.

Complex tasks like this need more processing power. Instant Mode won't deliver the depth required.

Instead, Thinking Mode is specifically designed for advanced, multi-step tasks that require reasoning and synthesis.

In fact, ChatGPT can recognize task complexity automatically — if you enable Auto-switch to Thinking in the Configure settings, it will decide when to use Thinking Mode based on your request.

But if you want to guarantee deep processing for a complex task, manually selecting Thinking Mode ensures full power.

💡 First Discovery
In a nutshell, think of the ChatGPT modes as different engines:
"Instant" is built for speed
"Thinking" is built for complex tasks
Auto-switch to Thinking is built to go deep automatically (enable in Configure → Intelligence)

Match the mode to the task, and you'll get better results faster.

Choosing the right mode is just the beginning. ChatGPT also has specialized features that enhance specific types of tasks.

Feature 2: Deep Research
Deep Research is built for long, comprehensive, multi-source analysis. It examines dozens of sources, synthesizes findings, and produces detailed reports.

Use Deep Research for: academic papers, market analysis, and competitive landscape reports.

Beyond processing images, ChatGPT can also Create Images. Describe what you need visually, and ChatGPT generates it.

Use this for: portfolio visuals, brand logos, presentation graphics, social media visuals, or just for fun.

Shopping Research compares products and finds deals across retailers.
Study and Learn creates study plans and quizzes from uploaded materials.

# Lesson 3 Voice Mode

## The Voice Mode Opportunity
Imagine you're trying to brainstorm ideas for a new project, but every time you sit down to type, the thoughts won't come. Or you're commuting and have 20 minutes to plan your week — but your hands are full. What if ChatGPT could just listen and chat with you in real life?

That's what Voice Mode does! By the end of this lesson, you'll know when to use it and how to make it part of a real workflow, not just a one-time experiment.

### Introduction
You've already learned how to pick the right mode and feature. Now there's one more layer that most people overlook: how to talk to ChatGPT with a Voice Mode.

Before we dive in — what do you think Voice Mode actually does?

It lets you speak to ChatGPT and hear responses back

It transcribes your voice into text and sends it as a regular prompt

It reads your typed messages out loud so you don't have to read the screen

Correct answer
Voice Mode is a fully conversational experience — you speak, ChatGPT responds out loud, and the exchange feels more like a dialogue than a text chat.

### What Is Voice Mode?
Voice Mode is a two-way spoken conversation with ChatGPT. It's different from Dictation because it doesn't just capture what you say: it listens, responds out loud, follows the thread, and adapts to your tone.

# Lesson 4 ChatGPT & Apps

You're planning a trip and ask ChatGPT to find flights, add the best option to your calendar, and create a packing list — all without leaving the chat. Sounds like too much? ChatGPT's Apps can help with that.

By the end of this lesson, you'll know how to connect the apps you already use and let ChatGPT take real actions on your behalf.

ChatGPT Got More Powerful
So far, ChatGPT has been answering your questions, helping you write, and thinking through problems with you. All of that happens inside the chat: it gives you text, and you do the rest.

Apps change that. When you connect an app, ChatGPT can do things in the real world: search for flights, play music, or build a design, all from inside the conversation.

Text Tool vs. Connected Assistant
Without apps, ChatGPT is like a friend who gives great advice — but you still have to make all the calls yourself. With Apps connected, that same friend can pick up the phone for you. Let's see how it is different in practice.

ChatGPT's text tool did a good enough job: it pulled real hotel options with prices directly in the chat.

But with a connected app like Booking.com, it can now complete the booking on your behalf without you ever leaving the chat.

💡 Keep in Mind
- Only connected apps work: ChatGPT can't reach a tool you haven't linked.
- Actions stay inside the app's scope: It can search and create, but not access your private account data unless you've granted permission.
- New apps are added regularly: What's available today is just the beginning. It's worth checking what's new every few weeks.


Let's try another app, but this time for creation. Imagine you want to post a travel photo on Instagram and need an eye-catching visual to go with it — just ask Canva in ChatGPT Apps.

Connect Canvafrom the Apps panel and press "Start chat" to ask it to create the visual directly in the chat.

You've described what you needed, and Canva built a ready-to-use visual straight inside the chat.

But it didn't stop there: ChatGPT also suggested a caption and hashtags to go with the post. That's what happens when Apps and ChatGPT work together.

Key Takeaways
Apps turn ChatGPT from a text tool into a connected assistant: It can take real actions inside Spotify, Booking.com, Canva, and more.
You describe the outcome — ChatGPT figures out the steps: There's no need to switch tabs or do it yourself.
More apps are added regularly: It's worth checking what's new — today's limitation might be gone next week.

# Lesson 5 Image Generation with ChatGPT

Creating Images with ChatGPT
In this lesson, you'll learn how to use ChatGPT's Image Generation feature to turn your ideas into visuals.

You'll discover how to create everything from photorealistic scenes to illustrated designs, edit existing images, and write effective prompts that get you exactly what you need.

Let's dive in!

How Image Generation Works in ChatGPT
With ChatGPT, you can describe an image in text and receive a visual output directly in the chat — no separate tools required.

You can create infographics, diagrams, photorealistic scenes, illustrated designs, and more, all within seconds of typing your request.
Where to Find It
To generate an image, you can use one of two ways.

The first is to click the "+" icon, select "Create image", and type your prompt into the input box.

The second way is to go to the "Images" tab in the left menu, where you can also see templates and all your previous results, stored for quick access.

After using either of the two ways, the model will then return your image within seconds.

What makes this feature particularly powerful is how well it understands what you're asking for. It can:
Render complex scenes with multiple elements
Create images with readable text, like signs or menus
Generate realistic people with accurate features
And, all of this isn't just for artistic illustration.

With ChatGPT, you can create functional imagery for presentations, marketing materials, educational content, and business documents.

Check it out by trying it yourself in the following exercises!

This is one of ChatGPT's strengths — it can generate images that make text look natural and legible, which is great for signage, product labels, or instructional diagrams.

💡 First Discovery
To summarize what you covered so far, ChatGPT's Image Generation excels at three things:
Rendering text that's actually readable
Creating realistic human features, including natural-looking hands and faces
Following complex multi-part instructions accurately

Whether you need photorealism or artistic styles, the tool adapts while maintaining this precision.

Editing Existing Images
In addition to creating new images, ChatGPT lets you modify existing images by providing new instructions.

This works well for iterating on designs or adjusting visuals based on feedback.

💡 Second Discovery
Two things to remember:
You can edit images iteratively without starting over — just describe what needs to change, and the tool updates that specific element.
Image Generation is practical for real workflows where you're refining materials based on feedback.

The combination of these two factors means you can quickly produce professional visuals!

Summing Up
All in all, you've discovered through practice what makes the Image Generation feature work.

As always, be specific about what you want, mention style when it matters, include any text or details that need to appear, and describe the purpose or context.

A few practical tips:
State your core request clearly first.
Add details about style or colors if they're important.
Put specific text in quotes, and mention what the image is for.
When editing, focus on one or two changes at a time.
Keep these in mind, and you'll master the skill of creating images with ChatGPT!

Key Takeaways
ChatGPT generates images with readable text, realistic human features, and accurate multi-element scenes.
You can create both photorealistic and stylized images suitable for business and educational materials.
The tool follows detailed instructions precisely, translating your prompts into usable visuals.
