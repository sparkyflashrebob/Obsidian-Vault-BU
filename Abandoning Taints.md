**Practical Methods for Abandoning Taints:** Drawing from the _Sabbasava Sutta_, the speaker outlines specific categories for practice:

1. **Seeing (Wisdom):** Moving away from speculative questions about the "self" and instead focusing on the **Four Noble Truths** (10:04, 13:00).
2. **Restraining:** Practicing mindful sense restraint so the mind isn't constantly pulled out by sights, sounds, and thoughts (15:53).
3. **Using:** Utilizing requisites like food and clothing wisely, avoiding the trap of excessive desire (20:17).
4. **Enduring:** Developing _kanti_ (patience) to coexist peacefully with uncomfortable or difficult experiences (23:07).
5. **Avoiding/Removing:** Being mindful of one's environment and actively removing thoughts of ill will, cruelty, and sense desire (24:52).
6. **Developing:** Cultivating the **Seven Factors of Awakening** (starting with mindfulness and investigation) to move the mind toward tranquility and equanimity (29:44).
