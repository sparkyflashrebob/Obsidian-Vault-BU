Daily Report from Recordings

Create a daily log report using today's recordings.  Break down by order of conversation, the general subject matter and a brief decription of the subject matter discussed.  With each subject use a sub list any actions that might need to be taken. Avoid any items of a personal nature and not related to business.

Base Personality

﻿﻿﻿A clear, direct answer to my request.

﻿﻿﻿Alternative perspectives or solutions I might not have thought of.
