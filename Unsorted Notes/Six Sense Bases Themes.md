[[Six Sense Bases Themes]] from the Suttas:
- Abandoning - what is not ours
- Burning - six senses - feeling - burning with lust, hatred & delusion - dukkha (birth, old age, death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, dejection and misery) - becoming disenchanted - dispassionate - liberated
- Abandoning Ignorance - knowing & seeing seeing what is impermanent
- Full Understanding of clinging - dependence on the eye and forms - eye-consciousness arises - meeting of the three is contact - with contact comes feeling 
- Empty of Self
- Dyads of Consciousness - eye & forms then eye-consciousness - all impermanent
- Contacted one feels - one wills - one perceives - they too are impermanent
- Delight becomes dependence leads to clinging
- The Ocean - same as the raft - dangers in the waters - Endures the currents - crosses over
- Baited
- Fetter not eye or forms but the desire-and-lust that arises in dependence on them - if they were then there would be no escape
- Not Grasping the flow of experience - Right View slopes towards the ocean - towards nibbana - wishing for rebirth is a whirlpool - not become inwardly rotten
- “And what, monk, is [[inward rottenness]]? Here, someone is of bad behavior, of an evil nature, of impure suspect behavior, concealing his action, not an ascetic but claiming to be an ascetic, not celibate but claiming to be celibate, inwardly rotten, corrupt, rubbish. This is called ‘inward rottenness.’”
  
  Bodhi. Noble Truths, Noble Path: The Heart Essence of the Buddha's Original Teachings (p. 107). Wisdom Publications. Kindle Edition. 
- Bring me the sound of the lute - dependence - signs - break  into pieces - no lute to be found - 
- Six Animals - bind with a rope - snake - crocodile - bird - dog - jackal - monkey - knot in middle pull each other all over the place - Mindfulness of the Body as a stake - not reach out far from mindfulness of the body
