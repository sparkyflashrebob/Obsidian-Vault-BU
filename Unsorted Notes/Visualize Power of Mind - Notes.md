Practice Exercise

Imagine you're standing on a snow covered mountainside. 
Close your eyes for a moment. 
See the endless expanse of white snow around you. 
Feel the crisp, cold air on your skin. 
Hear the soft crunch of snow under your feet. 
Smell the fresh pine in the air. 
Notice the sensation of warmth despite the cold. 
Wrapped up in your cozy winter gear.
