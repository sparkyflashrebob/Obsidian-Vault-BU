Samvega is a complex Buddhist and Jain term that describes an emotional state of spiritual urgency, shock, and dismay arising from the realization of the futility and suffering inherent in worldly existence. It is not a negative emotion but a crucial catalyst for spiritual practice, motivating individuals to seek liberation from the cycle of suffering (samsara) by prompting a serious and urgent approach to the path to enlightenment. Key triggers for samvega include confronting impermanence, the inevitability of aging, sickness, and death, as was the case for Prince Siddhartha. 

Components of samvega

- **Shock and dismay**: A profound sense of alienation and shock at the meaninglessness of conditioned life, often stemming from experiences of loss, illness, or a direct encounter with mortality.
- **Complacency and foolishness**: A chastening feeling of one's own complacency and foolishness for having lived so blindly, often felt when the illusions of ordinary life are shattered.
- **Spiritual urgency**: An intense feeling of urgency and yearning to find a way out of the cycle of suffering and to pursue spiritual goals with a new seriousness. 

Triggers for samvega

- **Impermanence**: The sudden realization that all things are impermanent and subject to change, decay, and death.
- **Mortality**: Direct experiences or contemplations of death, aging, and sickness.
- **Personal crises**: Events like grief, relationship crises, or illness can bring about this feeling.
- **Spiritual encounters**: Sites or relics associated with the Buddha's life can evoke this feeling of urgency in practitioners. 

How it functions in spiritual practice

- **Motivator**: Samvega serves as a powerful motivator to abandon spiritual complacency and to take the teachings seriously.
- **Catalyst**: It acts as a catalyst for change, propelling one onto a spiritual path by providing a deep understanding of why such a path is necessary.
- **Balancing emotion**: In Buddhism, samvega is often balanced by **pasada**, a feeling of serene confidence, clarity, and hope that provides the stability to continue the path.
