Alaliko:
- timeless
- contemplate timelessness
	- not analyze
	- not define it in any way
- recognize it
- realize it

Let go of trying to analyze or figure it out
- not thru labels, words, or perceptions
- recognize the reality of timelessness
- which is here and now

How?:
- we practice mindfulness

The word mindfulness (Buddhism - [[sati]])
- just words
- [[Sampajanna]] (awareness)
- when we define words we want to know what they mean
- so we attach definitions, meanings, explanations
- think we understand because we have a good explanation

In the realm of analysis and explanation
- these definitions are still limited to the thinking mind
- encouraged to go beyond thinking
	- go beyond the the thinking process
- recognize what is behind thought
	- recognize what is behind the emotions that arise
- go beyond liking or disliking 
	- or preferences, ideas, opinions and views
- this is what we call realizing the [[dhamma]]

[[Dhamma]]: 
- intended to not be defined and described
- to be recognized, realized

[[Dhamma as refuge]]:
- awareness of what 'is' as refuge
- being aware of the dhamma at this moment
- this is an awakened, conscious state of being
	- that's not a person
	- you can't define it
- It is the reality beyond definitions

Once you start thinking:
- you are in the time realm again
	- whether you are aware or not
	- whether if you understand what I am talking about or not
- If you are thinking about it you are caught in doubting mind
- Doubt is always as a result of thinking
- When you try to figure it out, 
	- claim it in any way, 
	- thru language or thought
	- then it is something other than what it is

We want to get beyond thought & language
- to recognize and realize reality
- that which is real

Learn to trust in our own awareness

Usually we are attached:
- bound to our thinking as reality itself
- When we have a name for something,
	- or a definition for it
- then we feel we understand it or know something about it
- and then we form an opinions, 
- When we think we have a feeling about this or that
	- then we think that is what it really is

The practice is to be in the present moment
- to go into the reality of this moment
- and to not claim it or define in a personal way
- or look at it in any conventional way
- but to just recognize the reality of the experience as it is

For instance [[Being in a human body]]
- at this present moment
- What is that like?
- That is something we usually don't question
- because we identify with it
- have views an opinions and opinions about our own bodies
- we think we know them or understand our bodies thru scientific explanations
	- is that it?
- How is that compared to just simply recognizing the reality of our body as we are experiencing it right now?

Taking [[The Four Postures]]:
- sitting, standing, walking & lying down

	- Mindfulness of the Body

- you don't need to open your eyes
	- trying to recognize thru vision

	- as knowing the body
		- as it is present in consciousness 
		- as it is experienced at this time
			- not thru a perception
			- not by defining it
	- we have this intuitive awareness of the body 

Noticing sensations
- maybe in our legs or feet
- or recognizing the elbow touching the back of the seat or the arm of the chair
- contact
- the feeling of our clothes on our skin
- opening up to that which is actually happening
- what one is experiencing right now
	- especially if it is not extreme
		- like feeling pain
- this receptivity
- awareness is being receptive to reality
- the experience of here and now
- which is different from the norm
- because it is not a thought process

In terms of pain or something similar:

	- usually react to it
	- try to get rid of it

The practice is to come back to this receptivity
- recognize consciousness at this very moment
- it is like this

It is a foundation of mindfulness:

- so is the breath
- something we can always refer to
