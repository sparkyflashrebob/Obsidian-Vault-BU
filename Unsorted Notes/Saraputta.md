Śāriputra was one of the top disciples of the Buddha.
 * Key Role: He is considered the first of the Buddha's two chief male disciples, together with Maudgalyāyana (Pali: Moggallāna). Śāriputra had a key leadership role in the ministry of the Buddha and is considered in many Buddhist schools to have been important in the development of the Buddhist Abhidharma (higher teachings).
 * Known for:
   * Wisdom: He was renowned for his wisdom and is often called "the disciple of the Buddha who was foremost in wisdom."
   * Strict Adherence: Śāriputra was known for his strict adherence to the Buddhist monastic rules.
   * Teaching Ability: He was a highly skilled teacher and is often depicted alongside the Buddha in Buddhist art, usually to his right.
Śāriputra is a highly revered figure in Buddhism, particularly in Theravada Buddhism where he is given a status close to a second Buddha.
