Head and Heart Together, is space to present my ideas about spiritual practice, recovery, society and healing. Head and Heart Together hopes to be a source of wellbeing and healing for this planet – starting with the individual. It questions the conventional beliefs that society values of more, better, faster, etc. It helps to realign our thoughts, beliefs and attitude about things, and moves us towards a more grounded and spiritual way of living, with the belief that there are other ways of being individually and as a whole that are more emotionally healthy, enjoyable, peaceful and sustainable for all.
