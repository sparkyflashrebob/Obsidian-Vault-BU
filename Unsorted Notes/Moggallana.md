
Maudgalyāyana (Pali: Moggallāna) was one of the top disciples of the Buddha.
 * Key Role: He is considered the second of the Buddha's two chief male disciples, together with Śāriputra.
 * Known for:
   * Psychic Powers: Moggallāna was renowned for his powerful psychic abilities (iddhi). He was known to travel to different realms, including the heavenly realms, and bring back reports to the Buddha.
   * Liberation: Despite his powerful psychic abilities, Moggallāna recognized the limitations of these powers and sought liberation through the Buddha's teachings.
   * Compassion: Moggallāna is known for his deep compassion, particularly for his mother, who had passed away and was suffering in the lower realms.
Moggallāna is a significant figure in Buddhism, particularly in East Asian Buddhism where he is often depicted in art and plays a role in various rituals and beliefs.
