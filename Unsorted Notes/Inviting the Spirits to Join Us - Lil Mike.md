Inviting the Spirits to Join Us (Lil Mike)

Although we now occupy this land where deep healing from trauma, alcohol and addiction takes place - one day at a time, we recognize that there have been those before us and there will be those who follow.
This land was home of the Coast Miwok before the Europeans arrived. Our lives rest firmly on the foundation of their sacrifice. They are our ancestors on this land in blood and spirit.
Also, each of us owes deep gratitude to our parents, siblings, and relatives. And those who have been our companions, friends, mentors, teachers, and guides. Who were themselves, as best as they could be, and so with us.

So in that spirit we invite them to join us in honoring our brother Lil Mike, who now resides with Great Spirit - with the Great Mystery.

    
Spirits of the Universe. We are here.
Spirits of the Universe. We humbly ask you to join us. We welcome you to join us in sacred circle as we honor our friend Mike Lumberg.

We ask you to inform us, so that we may carry the Sacred within.

In the absence of the sacred,
we open to find it here in our hearts, within us.
We ask you to inform us.

Grandfather Sun.
We call you.

Grandmother Earth
We call you.

We call on the sacred plants.
We call on the sacred animals.
We call on sacred humans.

We call on all the ancestors, our ancestors, the ancestors of this place, the ancestors of these people, all of our spiritual ancestors.
We invite you to be with us.

We invite you all here to be with us, in this space, right now in our hearts, for guidance, and to honor the life that is all around and within us. To honor and guide our friend Lil Mike in the hereafter. Be gentle with him as he was gentle with us.

Aho. Mita'kuye Oyasin. Mitákuye Oyás’iŋ - We are All Related.
