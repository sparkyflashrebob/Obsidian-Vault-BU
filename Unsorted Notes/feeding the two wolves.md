
which wolf are you feeding
native american saying
[[well-being]] or [[ill-being]]

The story of the two wolves is a metaphor for inner conflict. The story goes that a grandfather uses the metaphor to explain inner conflict to his grandson. The grandfather says that the two wolves are fighting within him and that whichever wolf you feed will win.  The story teaches that our future is in our control and that we can feed our minds to achieve success. 

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Wolves#:~:text=The%20legend%20is%20a%20story,%22whichever%20one%20you%20feed%22.

The two wolves are the white wolf and the black wolf. The black wolf is filled with fear, anger, envy, jealousy, greed, and arrogance. The white wolf is filled with peace, love, hope, courage, humility, compassion, and faith. The two wolves battle constantly. 

https://www.intepeople.co.nz/internal-conflict-white-vs-black-wolf/#:~:text=%E2%80%9CThere%20is%20a%20White%20Wolf,They%20battle%20constantly%E2%80%9D.

The story teaches that if you feed the bad wolf, you will do bad things. The more you feed the bad wolf, the easier it is for the bad wolf to grow bigger and stronger. The more you feed the good wolf, the more good things the wolf will encourage you to do. 

https://medium.com/the-canadian-way/5-strategies-to-feed-the-right-wolf-634feaa9199c#:~:text=If%20we%20feed%20the%20bad,will%20encourage%20us%20to%20do.

The story also teaches that if you feed both wolves right, they will work together. The wolf of rage and arrogance, if fed with proper food, becomes the wolf of courage and strength. 

https://medium.com/wholistique/we-must-feed-the-dark-wolf-cacada59053b#:~:text=The%20battle%20will%20continue%2C%20and,account%20of%20the%20grandfather's%20message:

The story can be seen in the Yin Yang symbol, which represents the two halves that together complete wholeness.
