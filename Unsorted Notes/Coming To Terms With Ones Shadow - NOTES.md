Psychological and Buddhist way similar
- Buddhist way of [[seeing]] rather than [[investigating]]
	- presumed as far deeper and penetrating
-  liken to washing your own dirty linen rather than to the laundry
	- we ourselves discover the stains
- No need to scrub
- Act of seeing cleanses them
- similar to other modern teachings such as [[John Sarno]]

To explain [[shadow]]
- 1st turn spotlight on discrimination of [[good]] and [[bad]]
	- starts our suffering
	- followed by craving
		- 2nd Noble Truth
- Brought out by our erroneous I-am-I consciousness
	- [[I want]] [[I don't want]] 
	- I want the good. I do not want the bad.
	- applied to our emotions too

Socially created pattern of ethical conduct
- show off so-called good in us
- repress the so-called bad
- all of seem to have these same tendencies
- repressed bad constitutes what we call [[the shadow]]

Here call it simply bad
- if we call bad character or bad personality
	- goes against Buddhist belief in anicca and anatta
	- impermanence and non-self
- in Buddhism better termed as unskilled and skilled
- also in terms of skilled and unskilled reactions to the past and present

Trouble starts with our discrimination of what is good and what is bad
- on one hand, bear in mind, the basis for this discrimination - our ethical code - is essential
- 5 precepts
- most of us will find it easier to not kill
	- at least in the wider sense
- or not steal
- other three much more difficult
	- difficult to not take alcohol or indulge in sensuality
	- wrong speech - most likely - beats all of us
- also have to consider our unskilled habits in thinking, emotions, and acting

One thing we must understand
- unskilled habits constitute our shadow
- it is when repressed
	- when we attach to our unconscious 
	- and owning to our underlying fear of them
	- that they form a relationship with us
	- a habit-pattern
- this habit-pattern that the ego rules - plagues us

Habit patterns
- result of previous karma
- our responsibility
- we cannot change
	- past is the past
- can change our reaction to them
	- in the present moment
- Practice of Mindfulness
- In time a complete break-through
- also stop forming new habits
- comes thru as the result of insight
- true nature of things

Accept rather than repress
Detachment instead of attachment

1st Step
- have a better understanding of the ego's general insecurity
- causing our karmic makeup
- rather than our constant emotional judgement

Some say 'we must never accept our faults'
'instead we must try all we can to improve ourselves'
- Buddhist answer is Yes and No

Fact of improving ourselves is right
but cannot be done by us - by our ego
Ego stands for total ignorance.
How can ignorance improve ignorance

Einstein quote: “**You cannot solve a problem with the same mind that created it**.”

Might be good to differentiate between 
- our every-day mode of awareness
	- when the ego is in full swing
		- and the intellect decides our actions
- and a deeper mode of awareness
	- when there is a [[seeing]]
		- a true understanding
- depth depends on how much the ego is out of the way
- when we say we have to accept our weaknesses 
	- our unskilled reactions
	- this acceptance not done by our ego
- Is the acceptance of the ego

EXAMPLES
- Accept laziness
	- will get worse
	- wrong attitude
- If "i" try to put a stop to my laziness
	- an improvement but still not right solution
- ego's way is to repress this habit in order to super-impose a new habit
- usually comparatively weak
	- much of the energy spent on repression
	- it is forced - unnatural
	- unless doing something I am interested in
- when this happens it might make my shadow more involved
- if my repression get strong it becomes a source of frustration
	- can play out in many ways

Person wants to be less selfish
	- decides to do some form of social welfare work
	- might succeed to a certain extent
		- disciplines themselves
		- get up early in the morning
		- faces long distance travel on a cold winter-day
		- reaches their destination
		- Used to a comfortable home
		- clean area
		- find the smell and dirt and the poverty depressing
		- Wants to show other egos how charitable it is
	- Mood changes 
	- gets fed up
	- falls apart
	- even with one who is genuinely good and kind-hearted
		- mistake to drive ourselves too hard
	- also can become a perfectionist in being virtuous
	- false holiness
	- touch of narrow mindedness
	- lack of sense of humor
	- not emotionally balanced
	- has not come to terms with her shadow
	- come to the danger of not wanting to face ones shadow

Person who was a fusser
- always agitated
- always nervous
- never admit it
	- even to themselves
- projected their weakness on their partner
- tell them to stop fussing
- their partner started to believe this to be true
- only after this person died did the partner realize the truth
- [I have a similar story in my own life]
