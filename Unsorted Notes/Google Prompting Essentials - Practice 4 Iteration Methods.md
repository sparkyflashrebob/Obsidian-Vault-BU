
Always be iterating

4 Methods
1. Revisit the prompting framework
2. Separate your prompt into shorter sentences
3. Try different phrasing or switch to an analogous task
4. Introducing Constraints

## Revisit
- make sure providing enough specifity in task - context & reference
- for instance if your prompt is to the point you might want to include 
	- the persona (sports nutrician expert, audience of physical therapits, pro basketball players)
	- the format (five blog post headlines, industry trends)

## Separate the Prompt with shorter sentences.
- take long input and put into smaller tasks.
	- summarize the key data points and information in this report
	- follow up with a prompt
		- create visual graphs with the data you summarized
	- shorten key information YOU SUMMARIZED into bullets

## Try different phrasing or switch to an analogous task
- one similar to the one you are trying to get
- but different enough to get a different response

i.e  write a marketing plan to
- write a story


## Introducing Constraints
- help to focus a gen AI's outputs
- Playlist for a road trip and want to figure out artists to include
	- have genre but results boring
	- heard songs over and over
	- maybe specify artists from a certain region or who specialize in some manner, last 5 years
