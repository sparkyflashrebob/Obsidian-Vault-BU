Manna from heaven is a term from the Book of Exodus in the Bible, describing a miraculous food God provided for the Israelites during their 40 years in the wilderness. It is often used metaphorically to mean an unexpected but welcome gift or source of help. The manna appeared as a fine, flaky substance like frost, which could be ground into flour and baked into bread that tasted like flour with honey or oil. [[1](http://tulsafmc.org/blog/2024/04/16/manna-from-heaven), [2](https://onmissionmedia.com/what-did-manna-from-heaven-taste-like/#:~:text=After%20all%2C%20Exodus%20described%20manna%20as%20small,Manna%20sustained%20people%20well%2C%20but%20spoiled%20easily.), [3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manna#:~:text=Manna%20is%20from%20Heaven%2C%20according%20to%20the,and%20left%20for%20them%20to%20grind%20themselves.)]

  

The biblical account

- The provision: Manna was provided each morning for the Israelites after they complained about having no food while traveling from Egypt. God also sent quail in the evenings to provide meat.
- The appearance: It is described as a "fine flake-like thing, fine as frost on the ground".
- The taste: It tasted like "flour with honey" or "bread with oil".
- The purpose: The miraculous provision was intended to teach the Israelites about God's faithfulness and to build their trust in Him.
- The spoilage: Any manna not collected for the day would melt away once the sun grew stronger. Extra manna kept overnight would spoil, but on the sixth day, a double portion could be collected to last for the Sabbath. Symbolic and metaphorical meanings

- Spiritual nourishment: Manna is often seen as a symbol of God's daily provision, which believers are encouraged to recognize and accept with faith.
- "Bread of angels": In some interpretations, manna is referred to as the "bread of angels," a miraculous food that falls from heaven at God's command to nourish the Israelites.
- "Bread of life": In the New Testament, Jesus described himself as the "bread of life that came from heaven," linking the concept of manna to spiritual sustenance, according to [Discipleship Evangelism](https://delessons.org/tools/the-kingdom-of-god/jesus-is-the-manna-from-heaven/).
- Unexpected blessing: In modern usage, the phrase "manna from heaven" refers to any unexpected stroke of luck or a timely and welcome gift. What the manna was

- A supernatural substance: The Bible describes it as a miraculous, edible substance that sustained a large population for 40 years.
- A natural phenomenon?: Some naturalistic explanations suggest it was a sugary exudate from certain plants, like the tamarisk, which contains compounds like mannite, glucose, and fructose. However, the biblical account presents it as a divine miracle, not a natural occurrence.
