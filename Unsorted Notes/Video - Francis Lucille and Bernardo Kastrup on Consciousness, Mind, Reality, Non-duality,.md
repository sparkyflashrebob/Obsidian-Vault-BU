
Here's a summary of the definitions and key points discussed in the video:

### Definitions:

- **Consciousness/Awareness:** The reality that is experiencing. [[00:53](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=53)]
- **Reality:** The thingness of things, encompassing all things, including physical and perceived elements. [[01:39](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=99)]
- **Mind:**
    - Experientially: The phenomenal aspect of human existence, including thoughts, sensations, and external perceptions. [[02:56](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=176)]
    - As a model: The conceptual part of thinking, involving abstract representations. [[05:10](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=310)]

### Key Points:

- **Direct Experience:**
    - Defined as whatever is directly experienced by Consciousness. [[08:19](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=499)]
    - Includes bodily sensations, external sense perceptions, and thoughts/concepts. [[09:09](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=549)]
- **Pure Consciousness:**
    - The experience of Consciousness by itself, without any perception. [[15:30](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=930)]
    - Characterized by absolute certainty and timelessness. [[17:28](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=1048)]
    - It is always experienced, even in the absence of perception. [[17:07](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=1027)]
- **Metacognition:**
    - Metacognition A: The experience of pure Consciousness. [[23:45](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=1425)]
    - Metacognition B: The reflection of this experience as a mind construct. [[23:57](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=1437)]
- **Non-locality of Intelligence:** Understanding is non-local, placing one at the center of the universe. [[28:58](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=1738)]
- **Philosophy:**
    - The power of philosophy lies in its self-referential words (e.g., Consciousness, reality) that lead to a timeless experience. [[01:01:24](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=3684)]
    - The goal of philosophy is happiness. [[01:03:07](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=3787)]
- **Wisdom:** Living from a place of not knowing, allowing reality/consciousness to be whatever it is at every moment. [[43:12](https://youtu.be/JI2gCGq-KpM?si=ogIzfcSLxPHpTHAY&t=2592)]
