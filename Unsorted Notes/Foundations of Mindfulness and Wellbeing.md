This is not the whole story:
- wisdom traditions have always said and modern psychology is confirming that it is not our real life situations that are the cause suffering. Life does create suffering.
- But fundamental well-being does not require that we get all of the conditions lined up in our favor.
- Suffering is the result of the condition of our mind.
- Not what we are experiencing but our reactions to it.

Spiritual Practice and our Well-being is determined by the state of our mind.
- this is compounded by the fact that we believe in what we are experiencing is what is in fact the way the world is.
- If we think it is true, then it is true for us whether or not it is true in the world.
- if we think there is a tiger around the corner coming to get us or, in my case, if the ocean has a shark nearby, then our fear of what might be can hijack what is true.
- The brain is a calculator
- computing what is good for us in terms of our basic needs
