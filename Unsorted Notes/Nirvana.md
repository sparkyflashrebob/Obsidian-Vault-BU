nirvana not a place
realized when the mind stops defining itself in terms of place: of here, or there or between the two
neither [[samsara]] nor nirvana is a place
samsara is a process of creating places [[becoming]] and then wandering thru them [[birth]]
Nirvana is the end of this process
Also is realized thru unestablished consciousness - one that does not come or go or stay in place.
