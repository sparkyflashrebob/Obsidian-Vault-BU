# Plan a Vacation

Start by reviewing the **Instructions** button, which provides an overview of how to complete this activity. It also suggests AI tools that you may find helpful.

In this activity, you'll practice your prompting skills by using gen AI to help you plan a vacation. Remember to apply the prompting framework when working with the generative AI tool of your choice:

- **T**houghtfully: Describe your _**task**_, specifying a _**persona**_ and _**format**_ preference.
    
- **C**reate: Include any _**context**_ the gen AI tool might need to give you what you want.
    
- **R**eally: Add _**references**_ the gen AI tool can use to inform its output.
    
- **E**xcellent: Next, _**evaluate**_ the output to identify opportunities for improvement. 
    
- **I**nputs: Then, _**iterate**_ on your initial prompt to attain those improvements.
    

Check out this guide for a quick refresher about the prompting framework: [Google Prompting Essentials: Key takeaways](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15wTi-9NwxF8AgOXuSEWesDb1JGWWc_jx/view).

**Plan your perfect vacation**

Think of your ideal getaway, the one you daydream about. It might be a beachside escape, or an adventure in a city you’ve never been to before. Gen AI tools are great at helping you plan when to travel, how to get there, what to pack, and even how to budget. 

First, you’ll write an initial prompt asking the gen AI tool to identify the necessary planning steps for your vacation. Then, you’ll iterate on your prompt to get more details.
