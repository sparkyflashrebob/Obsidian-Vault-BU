Other Notes:

- Cost of lift rental excluded from this proposal.
- Cost of local storage or equipment staging site excluded.
    - Materials will be delivered to site on a scheduled basis and will require a signature from the site supervisor.
- Storage by others must be temperature/humidity controlled and secure.
- All racks and equipment will be built and tested in an AVDG facility.
- Racks to be seismically anchored by others where applicable.

Data & AV Systems, Racks & Power:

- An enterprise grade network system is planned for the AV system
- All data cable will be CAT6 and it will be terminated within the AV equipment rack.
- All AV Rack locations interpretational wiring to be supplied by others.
- Twelve (12) 42-unit equipment rack will be installed in the equipment closets to house a majority of the low voltage equipment specified for the project
- AVDG will provide a network monitoring appliance to allow for remote troubleshooting of the installed systems
- The owner will supply an Internet modem for the selected service provider. (Comcast, AT&T, etc.)

**Site Conditions**

- The equipment rack and associated equipment (displays, touch panels, electronics, etc.) will be loaded and tested at an AVDG facility
    - these items will be delivered to site **ONLY** after the rack room and other equipment locations are dust free and no longer under construction.
- in most cases speakers, display mounts, and wiring will be installed in a pre-wire phase based on supporting substructure completion; which can be scheduled when the site is still considered under construction.
    - Devices may need to be wrapped in plastic to protect from debris and paint

**Networking**

- AVDG to furnish and provide network equipment required for the AV systems
- Data connections between AV racks by others
- AVDG will require all VPN, IP and other information prior to delivery to site so that equipment can be configured and tested at an AVDG facility
