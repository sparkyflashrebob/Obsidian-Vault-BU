There are many arguments for and against the Buddhist concept of karma, including: 

- Motivation
    
    Some traditional Buddhists believe that karma and rebirth motivate people to be good, as they want to avoid suffering in the future and earn a happy life. 
    
- Objective standards
    
    Some Buddhists believe that karma demonstrates the existence of objective standards of right and wrong. 
    
- Explanation for suffering
    
    Some scholars believe that karma provides a satisfying explanation for suffering and loss. 
    
- Fatalism
    
    Others argue that karma is tantamount to fatalism or determinism, and that it would make free will an absurdity. 
    
- Slavery
    
    The belief in karma and reincarnation has been used to justify slavery, with the reasoning that a person's enslavement is a result of punishable actions in a previous life. 
    
- Causality
    
    Some say that there is a regularity to causality, where acting on certain mental states generally brings weal or woe. 
    
- Choice
    
    Others say that the more choice we have, the more responsibility we can take.
