Mentor Group Instructions

Guided Meditation #4

Self-Forgiveness: One student will come prepared to offer a short (10-15 minute) guided meditation on Self-Forgiveness to the group. You can use one of the two reflections offered in the Self-Forgiveness: Guided Reflections handout in Module 6 as a basis for creating your own script—or you can improvise a meditation based on these principles. You can also refer to Tara's guided meditation in the Self-Forgiveness transcript in Module 6.

Be sure to take a few minutes at the beginning of the guided meditation to settle and ground your students in body and breath. During the meditation, build in frequent silent pauses of anywhere from 30 seconds to two minutes. Remember, the best preparation to teach a practice is to practice it yourself on a regular basis. 

Practice steps 1-4 of the Four-Step Feedback Loop Process.

Review your responses to the journaling prompts on self-forgiveness in July and come prepared to discuss your insights and challenges with this practice and how you might guide students who are having difficulty forgiving themselves for unskillful behavior or other past actions.

Guided Meditation #5

Student Choice. One student will come prepared to offer a short (10-15 minute) guided meditation practice of their choice (body, emotions, thoughts, metta, RAIN, forgiveness).

Practice steps 1-4 of the Four-Step Feedback Loop Process.

The remainder of the Mentor Group time will be spent in an open Mentor-led discussion so please bring any questions you have from the course material thus far.

Note that some of the scripts provided (Mindfulness of Body, Mindfulness of Emotions) are word-for-word scripts that you can follow verbatim—they indicate details such as where to pause and for how long. The others are more like guidelines or instructions for guiding the meditation that you can use to create your own scripts if you wish. You can supplement those by looking at the transcripts of Jack and Tara’s guided meditations on the same topics. For all of these meditations, you should feel free to create your own scripts--or simply improvise and guide from your own practice, if you feel ready to do so. Remember that the best preparation for guiding a meditation is to practice that meditation repeatedly yourself over many days and weeks—first with guidance (such as a recording), then on your own while guiding yourself.
