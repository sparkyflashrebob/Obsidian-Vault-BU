[[Adittapariyaya Sutta]]
- All is burning
- eye, forms, eye-consciousness, eye-contact is burning
- whatever is felt as pleasant, unpleasant, or neither-painful-nor-pleasant that arises with eye-contact
- burning with the fire of lust, hate, delusion
- burning with dukkha (birth, aging & death, sorrow, pain, lamentation, grief & despair)
- likewise the other senses
- with mind
	- mind is burning, ideas, mind-consciousness, mind-contact...

Noble follower:
- one who has heard (the truth) see this
- finds estrangement in the eye, forms,etc...
- when one finds estrangement, passion fades, with passion faded, one is liberated, etc...
