 Brahmaarpanam Brahma Havirhi: The offering made to Brahman is Brahman itself.  
* Brahmaagnau Brahmanaahutam: It is offered into the fire of Brahman by Brahman.  
* Brahmaiva- Tena Gantavyam: One should strive to attain Brahman through this offering.  
* Brahma Karma Samaadhina: Through the realization of Brahman, one achieves liberation from the cycle of birth and death.  
In essence, the verses emphasize the importance of dedicating one's actions to the divine and seeking liberation through self-realization.
