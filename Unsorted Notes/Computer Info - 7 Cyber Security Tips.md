Here is a summary of the actions to take for better online security, as mentioned in the video:

- **Create a separate email address for banking and investments:** This helps limit the impact of phishing attacks.
- **Use virtual credit cards or tap-to-pay:** This protects your actual credit card number from being compromised.
- **Create unique usernames and strong passwords:** This adds an extra layer of security to your online accounts.
- **Set up a credit freeze:** This prevents unauthorized access to your credit information.
- **Use a service like DeleteMe to remove your personal information from data brokers:** This helps protect your privacy.
- **Tether to your mobile data instead of using public Wi-Fi:** This gives you more control over your internet connection.
- **Turn off Wi-Fi and Bluetooth when not in use:** This reduces the risk of your devices being compromised.
- **Set up stolen device protection on your phone:** This helps protect your data if your phone is stolen.

By taking these simple steps, you can significantly improve your online security and protect yourself from cyber threats.
