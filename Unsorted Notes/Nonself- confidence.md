Sister Boi Nghiem

3 subtle obstacles 
- superiority complex
- inferiority complex 
- equality complex

Each unique suffering
Trap of comparison and judgement
With mindfulness find freedom from noncomparison

Superiority complex
- better than others
- our needs and desires matter most
- root of many of struggles
- put ourselves above others
- even with generosity can this be expressed
- in what ways do we feel better than others
- recommend to reflect on this
- interconnection to others

Inferiority Complex
- less than
- lead to other harmful emotions
- jealousy 
- resentment 
- unworthy
- unlovable 
- illusions not reality
- I am enough as I am

Equality Complex 
- can overlook our individual uniqueness 
- can lose our balance
- by losing this we cultivate understanding and compassion
- respect for each other's individuality 

Practicing Nonself and Confidence
