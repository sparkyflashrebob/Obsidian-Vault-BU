
“As you start to walk on the way, the way appears.” - Rumi

There are usually a few ways that Spiritual Practice is expressed; 

Inwardly disciplines
- Meditation, 
- Prayer, 
- Fasting, 
- & Study 

Outward disciplines:
- simplicity or renunciation, 
- Solitude, 
- Submission  
- Service 
- and adding to this Ethics, which, for some reason in the West, is usually excluded from discussion.

Our discussions here, in this group, are intended to be mostly non-sectarian in nature.  
Although we are primarly a Buddhist leaning group. 
This means that we will mostly exclude the purely devotional aspects of chanting and prayer in practice. 
There will be some discussion of these aspects though.
And we will discuss the impacts of karma and rebirth in our day to day and present lifetime. 
These things cannot be really disputed being that our actions have consequences and we are living and breathing human beings.

In this group we will primarily about the benefits of Mindfulness and Wakefulness Practices and insights. 
There will be a lot of talk about the Buddha’s teachings but you do not need to be Buddhist to follow them.  
We will present teachings from Buddhism and from many other paths that promote wakefulness, kindness and inner peace.  
And that serve as skillful tools for you to use on your path, should you choose to do so.  

We encourage you to meditate, to look inside, to have a path and a direction while you do so, which is to awaken to the wisdom that is already inside of you.
