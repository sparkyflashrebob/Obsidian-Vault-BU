 ChatGPT-3 summarizes [[The Doctrine of perception]] as a philosophical doctrine which states that [[perceptual knowledge]] is acquired through the senses, and that this knowledge is acquired through the experience of [[direct contact]] with external objects. It also states that [[the mind can only know what it has experienced directly]], and that this knowledge is distinct from any other type of knowledge.
