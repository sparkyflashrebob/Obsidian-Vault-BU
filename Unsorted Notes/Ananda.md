
The Buddha's primary attendant was Ānanda.
 * Key Role: Ananda served the Buddha closely for 25 years, assisting him in various ways and acting as an intermediary between the Buddha and others.
 * Exceptional Memory: Ananda is renowned for his exceptional memory. He is credited with recollecting and preserving much of the Buddha's teachings, which were later compiled into the Sutta Pitaka (the collection of discourses in the Buddhist canon).
While other monks assisted the Buddha during his ministry, Ananda held a unique and significant position as his primary attendant.
