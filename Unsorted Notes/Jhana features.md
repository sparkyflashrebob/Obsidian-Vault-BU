The Buddha called the seven factors the anti-hindrances, as they counteract the five hindrances that keep us stuck in delusion. The seven factors are sometimes described as a progression, with each quality building on the one before it and leading to the one that comes after it.

The seven factors are:
Mindfulness (sati)
Keen investigation of the dhamma (dhammavicaya)[3]
Energy (viriya)
Rapture or happiness (piti)
Calm (passaddhi)
Concentration (samadhi)
Equanimity (upekkha)
