Six Persons:
- Examine [[Attitudes To Life]] thru Buddhist eyes
- way a person looks at life and re-acts to it
- only know life through our senses and elaborate on these sense impression in our mind
- when we speak about life - not as it is
- thru the mental image of it we have formed ourselves
- as many images as people
- groups of people who look at their sense-impressions in a similar pattern
	- similar attitude
- six people she knows/knew
- each representing a group
- Labeled as:
	- > 1. The philosopher.
	  > 2. The materialist.
	  > 3. The perfect mother.
	  > 4. The woman who is afraid of life.
	  > 5. The non-accepter of dukkha (frustration, suffering), and
	  > 6. The accepter of dukkha.


The Philospher:
- well-known doctor, 
- highly intellectual & cultured
- considered very successful
- admired 
- very good income
- owns a house and a car
- writes book on medicine and psychology
- charming and interesting
- a little condescending at times
- as you get to know him
	- way of thinking sharp and logical
	- abstract and schematic
	- loves a juicy argument
- favorite topics are politics, economics & philosphy
	- anything created by human intellect
- good wit bu little sense of humor
- strong opinions
- conventional convictions***
- lover of personalities and traditions

From Buddhist perspective there is something fundamentally wrong
- mistakes the intellect for life
- not that the intellect is a bar to spiritual development
- need the intellect we can develop to understand the teachings
	- how they apply to life
	- without it cannot life the cloud of delusion we all suffer from
	- or understand the [[Eightfold Path]] laid our for us
- Once on [[The Path]]  the intellect is not sufficient!!!
- Thru [[Self-Observation]] in meditation and [[daily awareness]], we start to develop an [[awareness of ourselves]], [[our surroundings]], and [[other people]]
	- Everything we call [[our life]]
-  this [[Readwise/Books/Awareness]] dim at first 
- but with perseverance and sincere effort it can become [[sharp]] and [[one-pointed]]
- ceases to become awareness but [[insight]]
- then we can transcend the intellect
 
Sum of [[Different between Intellect and Insight]]
- intellect is sharpness of mind still ego-bound
- [[insight]] is sharpness of mind no longer ego-bound
	- [[universal and all-embracing]]

Philosopher:
- Does not want to admit the limitations of his intellect
- instead of using it to understand the path of practice, he uses it a a shield between himself and life
- between himself and his own sense-impressions of the outside world
