### Latin Etymology of *Mundus*
- *mundus* m (genitive *mundī*, 2nd declension):
  1. Toilet ornaments, decorations, dress (of a woman).
  2. Implement, gear.
  3. The universe, the cosmos, the world, the heavens and heavenly bodies (= Greek *kósmos*).
  4. The inhabitants of the earth, mankind.
  5. (Eccl. Latin): The world as opposed to the spiritual realm; worldly existence.
