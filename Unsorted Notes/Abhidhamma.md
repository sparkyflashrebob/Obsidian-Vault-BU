Fourfold Ultimate Reality (catudha paramattha)
- Consciousness
- mental factors
- matter
- nibbana

Two kinds of Realities
- conventional reality [[sammuti]]
  -  ordinary conventional thought [[pannatti]]
    - conventional modes of expression [[vohara]]
    - living beings, and apparently stable objects do not possess ultimate validity
    - do not exist in their own right
    - conceptual
    - products of mental construction [[parikappana]]
    - not realities existing by reason of their own nature
- ultimate reality [[paramattha]]
	- exist by reason of their own intrinsic nature [[sabhava]]
	- the dhammas
		- final, irreducible components of existence
		- utlimate intities which result from correctly performed analysis of experience
		- no further reduction
	- parama = ultimate,highest, final
	- attha = reality, thing
