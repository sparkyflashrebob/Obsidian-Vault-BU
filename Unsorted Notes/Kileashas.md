[[Mana]]
In Buddhism, "mana" is a term that translates to pride, arrogance, or conceit.
Here's a breakdown:
 * Core Meaning: It refers to an inflated sense of self-importance, often based on perceived superiority in areas like wealth, social status, knowledge, or spiritual attainment.
 * Impact: Māna is considered a significant obstacle to spiritual progress. It fuels:
   * Disrespect for others: Leading to prejudice, discrimination, and conflict.
   * Suffering: Both for oneself and those around them.
 * Types: Māna can manifest in various forms, such as:
   * Pride in one's own views: Insisting on being right and dismissing others' opinions.
   * Pride in one's accomplishments: Bragging about achievements and looking down on those who haven't achieved as much.
   * Pride in one's social status: Feeling superior to those perceived as lower in social standing.
Overcoming Māna:
Buddhist practices like mindfulness meditation and cultivating compassion are crucial for overcoming māna. These practices help to:
 * Recognize the illusory nature of self: Understanding that the ego is a temporary construct.
 * Develop humility: Acknowledging one's limitations and appreciating the qualities of others.
 * Cultivate equanimity: Maintaining a balanced and impartial attitude towards all beings.
By overcoming māna, individuals can cultivate genuine inner peace and harmonious relationships with others.
