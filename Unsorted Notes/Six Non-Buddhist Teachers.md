At time of the Buddha


Six Teachers - not buddhist - their view - lots of influence at the time
1. Purana Kassapa - doctrin of inefficancy of action - denies volitional actions (karma) bear fruit - rejects moral judgements and distinctions - nihilistic naturalist - rejects morality - free will - akiriya (non-action) - no cause and affect
2. Makkhali Gosala - niyati - destiny or fate - fatalism - everything predetermined
3. Ajita Kesakambala - materialism - person identical with their body
4. Pakudha Kaccayana - Ajivaka - non-interference in predetermined course of events - seven element theory of atomism - 7 eternal and unchangeable elements 
5. Nigantha Nataputta - founder of Jainism - goal of life to achieve liberation from suffering - practice of non-violence, non-attachmment, and right knowledge. They believe in the Soul/Self - eternal, uncreated universe 
6. Sanjay Belatthaputta - skeptic - agnostic - question possibility of knowing definively the universe, the afterlife or the existence of a soul.
