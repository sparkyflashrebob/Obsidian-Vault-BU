While these files will _eventually_ be discovered by search engines organically, the fastest way to manually tell them you exist is to submit your site directly:

1. **Publish Changes**: First, ensure you push these new files (
    
    ```
    sitemap.xml
    ```
    
     and 
    
    ```
    robots.txt
    ```
    
    ) to your GitHub repository so they are live on your website.
2. **Google Search Console**:
    - Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
    - Sign in with your Google account and add a new "Property" using your domain: 
        
        ```
        dragonflylodgehealingarts.com
        ```
        
        .
    - Once verified, navigate to the **"Sitemaps"** section on the left sidebar.
    - Enter 
        
        ```
        sitemap.xml
        ```
        
         and submit it. This forces Google to crawl your site immediately and understand all of your service offerings.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools (Optional but Recommended)**:
    - You can do the exact same process over at [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/about). Bing even allows you to import your site directly from your Google Search Console account with one click!

By doing this, whenever you make changes in the future, these platforms will automatically revisit your 

```
sitemap.xml
```

 and update their search results with your new information!
