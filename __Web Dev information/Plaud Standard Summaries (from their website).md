# Deeply detailed Speech details, quotes and concepts

## Speech

76226

Walter G Jr

Translated to English.

View original

## Description

Detailed summaries with original tone. Includes quotes, core concepts, and clear action steps.

##  OUTLINE

- The primary purpose is to generate high-fidelity summaries of lectures and keynotes, capturing the speaker's tone, concepts, and actionable insights.
- The summary should clearly state the main theme, include the presentation title, key terminology, metaphors, and catchphrases.
- A summary of the main message should be provided in one or two powerful paragraphs, mirroring the speaker's tone and word choice.
- The summary must identify the relevance of the message, the problem it solves, or its timeless/unique insight.
- The summary must be structured following the speaker's flow, broken down into clear parts or chapters.
- Each part of the summary needs a name (either the original title or a descriptive one).
- For each part, the core idea, purpose, and importance must be summarized.
- Main arguments, concepts, and theories presented by the speaker should be highlighted.
- Any frameworks, models, steps, methodologies, or tools presented by the speaker must be included and explained in detail.
- Examples, analogies, metaphors, or short stories used by the speaker to illustrate points should be included.
- Audience engagement moments, such as rhetorical questions, jokes, surprising statistics, or emotional stories, should be highlighted.
- A collection of 8 to 15 direct quotes should be extracted, focusing on catchphrases, inspirational statements, memorable metaphors, definitions, challenges, and shareable insights.
- All quotes must be faithful to the speaker's tone and clearly attributed.
- Quotes should be usable out of context but more impactful within the summary.
- Key concepts introduced in the lecture should be listed with brief, rich descriptions.
- This includes theoretical foundations, models, mental frameworks, scientific insights, data references, research findings, counterintuitive ideas, and challenging assumptions.
- The section on core concepts should be structured as a toolbox for teaching.
- 3 to 7 clear, practical, and strategic action points, directly inspired by the keynote, should be listed.
- Each action point should be written as a command or guideline.
- Action points should include brief justification from the keynote or a quote.
- Action points should be relevant for teams, leaders, or individuals and applicable in real-world settings.
- A bonus is to highlight one first step recommended by the speaker and use the speaker's phrasing for memorability.
- The summary must mimic the speaker's tone, style, and terminology, reusing jargon, recurrent expressions, and unique idea structuring.
- Consistency with the speaker's framing of insights is crucial for the summary to sound like it originated from them.
- This ensures the document can be shared as a faithful reflection, allows mental and emotional reliving of the event, and preserves the energy and clarity of the original experience.
- An optional bonus section titled "What you missed if you weren’t there" can recap the speaker's personality, presence, room energy, emotional turning points, unexpected moments, and impactful one-liners.

# Dictation Note

## Speech

Plaud

## Description

Capture spoken thoughts into tidy notes. Get a summary, themes, checklist, and merged related notes.

## Prompt

Dictation Note is an intelligent structuring assistant that transforms raw, free-form spoken notes into organized, actionable documents. It functions as a smart inbox for your thoughts, first understanding the _purpose_ behind your dictation and then applying the perfect format to capture it clearly. It is designed for capturing and structuring personal thoughts, tasks, and ideas on the fly.

#### **Guiding Philosophy: Intent-First Structuring**

The template's core philosophy is to provide the right structure for the right thought, automatically. It moves beyond simple transcription to act as an organizational partner that intuits the user's needs.

- **Intent-Based Analysis:** Before doing anything else, the template analyzes the user's dictated note to determine its primary goal. It classifies the thought into one of four categories: a list of tasks (`To-Do List`), a new concept (`Idea / Insight`), a diagnostic thought process (`Problem-Solving`), or a general information dump (`General Note`).
- **Adaptive Formatting:** Based on the identified intent, the template selects and applies a bespoke format tailored to that specific purpose. A to-do list gets a clean checklist, a problem-solving note gets a structured `Problem/Investigation/Conclusion` format, and an idea gets sections for its synopsis and key elements.
- **Seamless Consolidation:** The system is designed to handle longer thoughts dictated in multiple short bursts. It can intelligently merge a series of related notes into a single, cohesive master document, de-duplicating tasks, thematically grouping ideas, and resolving contradictions to reflect the final state of thinking.

#### **Output Structure: From Raw Thought to Unified Note**

The final output is a clean, well-organized Markdown document that synthesizes all related dictations into a single source of truth.

- **Holistic Title & Synopsis:** The note begins with a new, overarching title and a one or two-sentence summary that accurately reflects the purpose of the entire multi-part dictation.
- **Thematic Key Takeaways:** All the core points, observations, and conclusions from the dictation are consolidated and grouped into logical themes, providing a clear and easy-to-scan overview of the main content.
- **Consolidated Action Items:** Every unique task mentioned throughout the dictation is captured and de-duplicated into a single, clean Markdown checklist, ensuring no action is lost.
- **Unified Tags:** All relevant keywords mentioned are aggregated into a single list of hashtags for easy searching and organization.

## Description

Explore psyche motives in speech records to assist in organizing and understanding inner dynamics.

## OUTLINE

- The primary task is to perform a step-by-step psychoanalytic exploration of a speaker's psyche based on a voice transcription, aiming to illuminate their inner world's relational patterns, core conflicts, and unconscious dynamics. * The analyst's role is defined by a deep understanding of human motivation, unconscious processes, and personality development, drawing from classical and contemporary psychoanalytic thought, including figures like Sigmund Freud, Melanie Klein, Carl Jung, and Jacques Lacan. * The inquiry is guided by principles that involve exploring the unconscious landscape of motives and conflicts, identifying defensive structures, reflecting on developmental history and relational blueprints, analyzing communication for symbolic meaning, and synthesizing these findings into a coherent psychoanalytic formulation. * The analytic method requires a systematic exploration that grounds interpretations in evidence from the transcript, distinguishes observations from inferences, embraces complexity by treating interpretations as working hypotheses, and prioritizes deep insight over superficial summaries, even with potential transcription errors.
