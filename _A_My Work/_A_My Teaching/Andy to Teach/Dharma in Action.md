
Ajahn Sumedho Key Points
- Start day consciously
	- be aware of our surroundings
- Make Effort
	- No passive
	- not just following our moods
- Not go thru motions
	- reflect on our expectations
- Notice internal goings on
	- what are we focusing on
		- mood
		- thoughts
		- feelings
		- greed, hatred & delusion
		- taking things for granted
		- Habitual
	- what are our dispositions
- What are out motivations
	- fear, guilt or external motivations
	- looking bad
	- not a good person
Use awakening as our model/aspiration
- Buddha, Dhamma, Sangha
