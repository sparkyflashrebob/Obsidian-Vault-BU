### EARTH
So asking the question 
How might we develop this understanding of emptiness?
In the early suttas it is said cautioning,
if one wishes to dwell in emptiness that we 
	"not give attention to the perception of people, 
	not give attention to the forest
	but should give attention to the unitary perception of earth"

As with the aggregates speaking to how one might perceive using the early Buddhist perspective
	if we seeing the earth as having hills and hollows, with animals and bushes,....things in it, then that is what we see  (just as we what is in the room but ignore the space that it is in)
	(if this is the case then) one should not attend to it so
	but if instead one sees this earth as level and flat like the palm of the hand, then one's manner of looking at it is beneficial and it should be frequently attended to in this way.

Speaking to what we are paying attention to

So if we attend look at the earth or land as a whole but not the specific features
The teachings call this "non-emptiness: or just the unitary perception of earth" 
	As a result one is not weary because of the perception of people 
	(we would not be placing our attention on them) 
	and the same for the forest 
	Having being seen in this way, our 
	weariness would only be affected because of this unitary perception of earth
	the people and the forest would not be as source of weariness

Whatever is not present, one sees as empty
whatever else is present, one sees as truely present
	further clarifying what it means to "truely dwelling in emptiness, without distorion"

So the Gradual entry into emptiness requires us to leave behind our earlier perceptions
	having emptied one's perception of these objects one give attention to the next step 
	which in this case is perception of the earth element

The whole point here is to arrive at a **unitary perception**
	the perception of one thing
	disregarding all the irregularities
	only perceive the basic notion of earth

Once we get to this point
	The idea from here on out is that our modes of perceiving are cultivated involving some degree of **abstraction**

Previously the instructions were to just direct attention to what would be visible.
This was refined so that one viewed the earth with no distinct qualities
