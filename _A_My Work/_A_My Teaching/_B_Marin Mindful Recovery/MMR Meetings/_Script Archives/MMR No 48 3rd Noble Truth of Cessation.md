According to Thubten Chodron, Nirodha is the final disappearance of all bad experiences and their
causes in such a way that they can no longer occur again.
The Buddha taught that the way to extinguish desire, which causes suffering, is to liberate
oneself from attachment. This is the third Noble Truth - the possibility of liberationing
consciousness.
Nirodha - Seeing cessation and specifically the cessation of sanya (perception) and vedana
(feeling). In Buddhism this is just this knowing of what is pleasant or unpleasant or neither
pleasant nor unpleasant.
This is not a thinking process. One does not experience the attainment of nirodha by thinking.
It just knows cessation.
