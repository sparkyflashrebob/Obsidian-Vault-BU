We may not need “Mindfulness” to be able to see the goodness in ourselves but it sure
can be a gateway. MIndfulness is the gateway to the deathless, to the unconditioned.
Mindfulness is like putting the brakes on when we are lost in self and in our habitual
ways of relating to our experience, to relating to our world.
MIndfulness serves as a means for us to wake up from our trance, our delusions or the
illusion we live in so that we might start to see more clearly another way. To freedom
from suffering, to freedom from the bonds that tie us down.
Mindfulness is setting the intention to wake up. Mindfulness is the practice of being
curious in our being, to waking from our contracted self, to being at peace and having
compassion for the suffering that we all experience.
I offer this for your consideration.
