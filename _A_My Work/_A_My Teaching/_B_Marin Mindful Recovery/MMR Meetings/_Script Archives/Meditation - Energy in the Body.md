MMR #28 Meditation on Energy in the Body
Turning our attention to the task as hand to be present for ourselves in meditation for the next few minutes.
Getting our bodies in position. Be it lying down, standing or sitting. Taking the position you plan to keep. Aware that you are being supported by Mother Earth.
Letting go of any obvious tension, maybe purposely rotating the shoulders, or the neck or any tension elsewhere that you can let go of.
Settling into position. Bringing mindfulness to the fore, awareness anchored in the body.
Aware of the body - aware of the body breathing.
Aware of any feelings that might arise. Aware of thoughts, of our mental state.
Of enjoying being present in the body, or of being uncomfortable mentally.
Whatever is present at this moment - just being aware. Observing. Knowing. Seeing the way things are. Without judgement.
Taking your place in the knowing of your own being.
