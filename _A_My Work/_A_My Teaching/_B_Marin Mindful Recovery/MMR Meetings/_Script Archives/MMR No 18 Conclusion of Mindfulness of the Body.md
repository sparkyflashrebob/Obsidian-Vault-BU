MMR#18 CONCLUSION OF MINDFULNESS OF THE BODY
--There is a sutta dedicated specifically to Mindfulness of the Body called the Kaya-gata-sati
Sutta, Majjhima Nikaya 119. That expands of the teachings here in the Satipatthana
It progresses from mindfulness of breathing, to the postures, to full awareness, contemplation of
body parts, the elements, and the nine charnel ground contemplations - detailing how this
practice leads to jhana practices all the way to liberation or release from all suffering.
