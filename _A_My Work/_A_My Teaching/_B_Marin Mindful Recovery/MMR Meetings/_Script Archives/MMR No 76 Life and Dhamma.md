Life on Life’s Terms in terms of dhamma - the way it is - Awakening

Thinking Our Thinking is going to free us
If I can get conditions straight then I can be happy
Putting off the moment
Only thing we have is this moment - here in this place where we are at
Everything else is an idea, a concept, a belief, a thought, a memory, a yearning for the way we
want things to be
Wanting to have certain experiences that are agreeable, pleasant, conditions we like
Not wanting to be who we are
Thinking of ourselves as being better versions of ourselves or like others, comparing, jealous,
envious of the appearance of things,
That we are better than others or worse than others
Craving - clinging -
Sense desire
Becoming
Not becoming
And then we come to dhama - to the way it is
That which is not known to the rest of the world
Which is against the stream
It not gaining, not losing, its not conditioned
It is not something we think about
It is not a concept or a belief, a perspective
Or a passing feeling or memory
Not a construction of the mind.
This understanding of the way it is
Where we start to slow down - to see things the way they are
Seeing things as change - always changing - coming from nowhere and returning to nowhere
Watching as things come and go of their own accord
Seeing how we grab onto our experience - take ownership - identify with experience
I see you - I see me
Because I see you and i believe my thoughts, believe my feelings as being me, and my
emotions as being me, and my memories as being me,
Seeing my beliefs and my opinions as the way the things are then I know the world
And how I know the world is the way the world is.
Is that true? My way is the way! What about your view, and your belief......
Is your way true? Well - if it fits mine it is.

And by the way
if it is the same way that I say it is, then I like you, and if not, then I am do not like you. But if
you do not know the way of the world - then I am not so sure. And I am not so sure about you
either.
But if you are cute, agreeable, friendly, do not have any conflicts with me, then that makes me
happy.
But if are not so attractive, disagreeable, have an abrasive disposition, difficult, non-conforming
then I do not like you and don’t want to have anything to do with you.
I don’t want to have to deal with things that are disagreeable, that I don’t want. I don’t want to
think about getting old, being sick, and dying. I don’t want to think about that with my parents,
and my siblings, and cousins, and my nephews and my nieces. It’s sad when it happens to
those I do not know. But I don’t want to think about it. It makes me sad and I do not want to be
sad. I only want to be happy, happy all the time. I want people around me to be happy all the
time cause that makes me happy.
Not seeing all of this as a mental construct - a soap bubble - not seeing our mental constructs
as as a best guess - as an illusion of the mind.
Taking ownership of a process - an aggregate of forms, feelings, perceptions, mental formation
and sense consciousness.
Not seeing that all of this is a process - a process that is ever unfolding - always changing -
These things that are always changing, these things that we cannot rightfully claim as who we
are, that we cling to. That causes so much suffering.
Coming back to our inability as a culture, as a society, our failure to recognize that all the
suffering that we create in our lives is around what we think - around what we believe - about
what we've been conditioned to think and believe in.
It's to recognize that good and evil are conditions that arise and cease according to other
conditions.
In its true state consciousness is naked, immaculate, clear, vacuous, transparent, timeless,
beyond all conditions. O Nobly Born, remember the pure open sky of your own true nature.
—Tibetan Book of the Great Liberation
