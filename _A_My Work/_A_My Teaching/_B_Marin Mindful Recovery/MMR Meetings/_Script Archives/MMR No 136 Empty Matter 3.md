All perception of form are left behind

This is a step in training perception
refining and deconstructing ordinary perception up to the point where meditation can culminate in the experience of Nirvana

The issue at stake is to reveal the empty nature of matter, not proclaim its non-extence

> For one who rightly knows and sees as it really is the arising of the world, there will not be [the notion] that the world does not exist; for one who rightly knows and sees as it really is the cessation of the world, there will not be [the notion] that the world exists.

Analayo. Compassion and Emptiness in Early Buddhist Meditation . Windhorse Publications Ltd. Kindle Edition.

The point here is
that for <u>one who sees the world that is so</u>.
for one <u>who sees cessation of the world then the world does not exist</u>

Here we turn our attention away from the characteristics of whatever is in the mind and towards the space it occupies

Typically everything that happens in the mind can be thought of existing "somewhere"

Now we are turning our attention to the space that holds experience.
This can be thought of as the mind as the space, the medium, the channel, or the vehicle of our object of awareness

The mind is equanimous and one-pointed because it is not distracted by anything else.
All but the tiniest suggestion of the material senses remain.
