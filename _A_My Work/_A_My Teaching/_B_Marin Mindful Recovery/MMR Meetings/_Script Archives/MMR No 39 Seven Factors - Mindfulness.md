Factor of Awakening (Mindfulness)
● Reminder that generally the Buddha defined mindfulness as the ability to remember for a
long time what’s been done and said. It’s the ability to keep things in mind.
● gatekeeper for a frontier fortress.
● There’s another passage where the Buddha says that mindfulness is like a goad. Mindful
of how we talk to ourselves. Guide us thru the minefield.
● You keep that in mind, you keep in mind your desire to do the right thing, and you
motivate yourself to act on that desire. That’s right mindfulness.
Teachings from the Retreat
● Talk that Referenced to 4th Foundation of Mindfulness - Mindfulness of Mind
● Khandhas (Aggregates) described as a spiritual term by the Buddha
● Aggregates as bundles of heaps - pile - collection around function of creating a sense of
self
● Physical Form being the body and all material things (Rupa)
● Feeling (Vedana)
● Sanna (Perception) Aspect of Memory - Association - Labels - Categories
● Sankhara - Mental Formations --thoughts, emotion, volition, intensions - includes anger,
lovingkindness, & MINDFULNESS.
● Consciousness & Fundamental Cognizing - Sense Impression of our experience
● ALL of THIS CONDITIONED.
● Just as when we describe a flower - parts - conjoined - cannot take as separate - at
same time - any description is not what it is - it is conventional - describing love is not
love - same a description of you is not you - you are indescribable.
● When we contemplate mindfulness in terms of the aggregates - they are in terms of
clinging
● Things we can look for in terms of clinging - 4 types of clinging
● 1-Sensual-desire- seeking gratification or stimulation from the senses - beautiful -
likeable and ugly not-likeable - Asking ourselves - Is this peaceful?
● 2-Precepts and Practices -- If I do this right then I will have it figured out - I will be happy
● Understand that the problem is not with the experience itself - it is the clinging

● 3-Views - opinions, perspectives, right & wrong. -- great source of suffering - making
things a problem!!!
● 4- Assumption of Self - where do you find yourself that you can call you? - Does not
have a foundational existent nature -
● Sense of self liken to a chariot - take off the parts - where did it go? - the flame
● Issue is a sense of self identification with a self - propping up - circles around the
aggregates.
● Senses being the animals tied to the body - all have their own nature - pull us in different
directions - Mindfulness is like a post in the ground.
● Mindful when we pick it up that we can also put it down.
● We develop/culitvate the 8fold path as our path of awakening.
● How to view the aggregates -
● Body - as foam at the ocean made up of material - no substance
● Feelings - bubbles - transitory
● Perception - naming as a mirage - untrustworthy
● Mental Formation - banana tree - layer upon layer of leaves - peel off no substance
● Consciousness as a magical trick - senses appearing and disappearing
