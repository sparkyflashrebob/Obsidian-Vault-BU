Taking your seat or lying down - setting your body in a position so that it can be both relaxed and at the same time alert - withdrawing your attention away from the external world into the present here and now.

Bringing your awareness into the body.

Being aware of the space you are in. 
Aware of the posture.
Aware that you are being supported by the earth, be it on a cushion or couch or bed.
Whether you standing, sitting, or laying down. 
Knowing that you can relax into your being without worry.

If you are feeling any initial tension in the body - purposely letting go or releasing it. You might rotate the shoulders and neck and come to rest in a position that you intend to hold for the next 20 or so minutes.

Being aware of the body breathing wherever it makes itself known - aware of the diaphragm moving up or down, or the lungs expanding and contracting, or as a sensation at the back of the throat or at the tip of nostrils as cool air coming in and warm air going out, cool air coming in - warm air going out. Being aware of the rhythm of breath - not trying to control it - but allowing the body to breathe itself.

Expanding this awareness so that breath energy fills the whole body.

Taking your place in the awareness of your whole being.

For the purpose of this sitting we again shift your awareness to being mindful of the feeling tone.
In the feeling of the breath and to feeling of other sensations in the body.
Being aware of whether a feeling is a pleasant feeling. If the feeling is an unpleasant feeling,
knowing it is unpleasant, and if feeling is neither pleasant or unpleasant then that this is neither

a pleasant nor unpleasant feeling. This knowing quality.
