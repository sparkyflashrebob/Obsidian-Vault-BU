“Close your eyes gently, or keep a soft downward gaze. Bring your awareness directly into the physical body.
Feel the weight of your body pressing against your chair, the contact of your feet on the floor. Take a few deep, intentional breaths...
As we sit, notice the nature of the mind. 
It is constantly flitting from sound, to physical sensation, to a passing thought. 
This is a divided knowing. 
The mind moves so quickly that it creates a tornado of mental activity. 
For the next twenty minutes, we are not trying to stop this movement by force. 
Instead, we are giving the mind a peaceful anchor. 
Whenever you find yourself swept away by thoughts of the past or plans for the future, gently but firmly direct your attention back to the breath, or to the physical contact of your body on the seat. Let yourself land right here, on life's terms.”
