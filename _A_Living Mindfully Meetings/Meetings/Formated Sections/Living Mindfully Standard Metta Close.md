**Let us offer these intentions of goodwill—first to ourselves, and then outward to all beings:**

**[Pause]**

_**May I be safe and protected from harm.**_

_**May I be healthy, strong, and at ease in this body.**_

_**May I meet whatever arises with wisdom, grace, and an open heart.**_

_**May I live with peace.**_

_**May all beings be healthy and at ease.**_

_**May all beings awaken to their true, spacious nature.**_

_**May all beings live in peace.**_
