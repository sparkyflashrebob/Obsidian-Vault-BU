# Head & Heart Together - Marketing Strategy & Exposure Plan

## 1. Current Platform Assessment & Constraints

### Meetup.com
- **Status:** Unaffordable at current subscription cost.
- **Pivot:** Focus on zero-cost event discovery platforms and organic community building.

### Luma (lu.ma)
- **Status:** Functional, but manual creation (one event at a time).
- **Pivot:** Reserve Luma for high-value special sessions or recurring templates. 

### Eventbrite
- **Status:** Multiple events listed, currently 0 registrants.
- **Analysis:** Eventbrite algorithm suppresses plain event titles or new accounts without external initial signups.
- **Optimization Action Items:**
  1. **Tweak Event Titles to focus on the Pain Point / Solution:**
     - *Old:* "Living Mindfully Interactive Session"
     - *New:* "Overcoming Burnout & Finding Inner Peace: Weekly Meditation Circle (Zoom)"
     - *New:* "Emptying the Boat: A Guided Mindfulness & Reflection Circle"
  2. **Seed Initial Attendees:** Ask 2–3 friends, past participants, or personal contacts to register on Eventbrite first. Eventbrite prioritizes events that already have 2–5 registered attendees.
  3. **Visual Cover Images:** Ensure cover images have clean, warm, high-contrast imagery (e.g., fingers meditating, serene nature) with readable title text.

## 2. Organic Exposure Strategy (Zero Cost)

### A. Tapping into Insight Timer & Audio Platforms
* **Insight Timer:** Apply to become an Insight Timer Teacher. Upload 5 to 10-minute guided meditations or short reflection audio talks. Insight Timer has a built-in audience of millions looking for daily mindfulness.
* **YouTube Shorts & Spotify Podcasts:** Record 60-second audio snippets of your blog parables (e.g., *The Empty Boat*, *The Sky and the Cloud*) and pair them with simple nature footage.

### B. Reddit & Community Discussions
* Share genuine reflections and engage in discussions in subreddits: `r/Meditation`, `r/Mindfulness`, `r/spirituality`.
* Focus on sharing wisdom first; include a link to [headhearttogether.com](https://headhearttogether.com) in your profile or when specifically asked for resources.

### C. Social Media Repurposing Pipeline
* **Carousel Posts (Instagram / LinkedIn / Threads):** Take 3–5 core takeaways from existing blog posts (see `Quotes and Key Takeaways.md`) and create visual slides.
* **Call to Action:** "Read the full essay at headhearttogether.com/blog.html or join our free weekly circle."
