"I am not going to do anything that is going to take me out of my body"

Our conditioning is, when it's pleasurable, holding on, which is a kind of contraction away from the aliveness of the moment. When it's unpleasant, push it away, or run away. Again, pulling away from the present moment.
And bottom line is, our bodily experience is out of control. It's just happening. And our survival instinct is to try to control things. So to really inhabit this body means to be willing to, to be surrendering into what feels out of control.
