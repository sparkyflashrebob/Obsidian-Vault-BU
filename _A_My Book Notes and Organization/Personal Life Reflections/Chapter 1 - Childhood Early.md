When we think about our lives it is quite natural to go back to your earliest thoughts. There is some research that says when you remember back to something you change it because you are adding your feelings about it now about that.  So over time how you "feel" about things change. This is quite natural as now it is getting clearer and clearer to me that we are not fixed entities, but constantly evolving and changing.

I was born in Riverside, California but have no memories of it.  My ideas for this time come from the pictures in my head that my mom passed on to me from her descriptions of growing up.  There are a few actual pictures but almost everything I think about that time comes from here.
Recently, I visited my Aunt and I asked her to fill in some history from those early days.  They were mainly about my mother, who died in 2019. Her perspective of my mother is something I had to put in a container as her perspective because it does not entirely jive with my understanding.  Everything we say to others has some influence, for good or for ill. I think it is best to also consider the source and their perspective.
