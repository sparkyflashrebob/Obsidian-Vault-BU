#right_in_fact_wrong_in_dhamma
- Ajahn Chah's reminder to Ajahn Sumedho
- being intellectually or legally correct, but acting with an agitated, angry, or arrogant heart
- "Winning" an argument at the expense of kindness, harmony, and peace of mind
- the elderly jaywalker, the dishwasher dispute—street-level moments where self-righteousness causes more suffering than the mistake itself
- Abhaya Sutta filter: is it factual? is it beneficial? is it the right time? If not beneficial, set it aside

#cleaning_the_inner_projector
- running up to the movie screen to scrub off an unpleasant image while the film reel keeps projecting it from behind

#stoic_inversion_of_responsibility
- Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius: we cannot control what happens, only our response
- 100% responsibility is NOT blame or victim-blaming—it is complete sovereignty over my own heart and mind
- refusing to surrender my peace to another person's bad behavior
- Akkosa Sutta: if someone offers me a tray of rotten food and I refuse to take it, to whom does the food belong?

#ho_oponopono_and_kala
- *Pono*: living in alignment, right relationship, balance
- *Hihia*: the tangled, knotty cords of resentment, unfinished business, and old grudges
- *Kala*: untying, unbinding, setting free
- the four gentle, heartfelt phrases used as inward cleaning rather than giving advice or fixing someone else:
  - "I am sorry" (acknowledging my part in the disturbance)
  - "Please forgive me" (asking to lay down the burden of judgment)
  - "Thank you" (gratitude for the mirror and the opportunity to wake up)
  - "I love you" (reconnecting to basic goodness and shared humanity)

#rohini_river_contemplation
- the Buddha stepping between two warring clans over the Rohinī River: "Is water worth more than the blood of kings and living human beings?"
- modern translation: life, breath, and human connection vs. quarterly profit margins, corporate extraction, and rigid ideologies
- asking myself in every conflict: "What is truly priceless here?"

#intellectual_superiority_and_credentialism
- the ancient caste of the mind: dividing humanity into "educated" vs. "uneducated", wealthy vs. non-wealthy (tribalism from caveman days)
- the broadcast vectoroscope metaphor: all human skin tones fall along the same line and are generally identical in terms of phase; one shared spectrum
- warrior knowledge vs. book knowledge: lived experience vs. theoretical concepts; neither ranked, both respected
- life experience as primary, positive education: hands-on field career before desk-bound design; learning what works and doesn't work through direct, tactile contact with technology and people
- experience coloring perspective: guarding against becoming jaded or cynical; jadedness as a heavy karma we take on for ourselves that contracts the heart and blinds us to the fresh moment
- navigating technological overwhelm: ever-expanding new technology; admitting what you cannot keep up with, grasping what you can, staying curious and grounded without fear
- corporate culture pressure: the demand to always be the smartest and never make mistakes vs. the true engineer's stance (not knowing all answers, but quietly finding the right answer to fix what's broken)
- respecting ancient Buddhist terms and facts while avoiding rigid materialism; "the way it is" (Ajahn Sumedho) vs. analyzing meditation without practicing
- *Māna* (conceit / comparison as a branch of *Sakkāya-Diṭṭhi*): catching my own mind judging people's correctness; self-consciousness vs. authentic peace
- recovery in Napa (April 2000): Dalai Lama's *The Four Noble Truths*; recovery rooms and the Buddha's Sangha where rich/poor, educated/uneducated meet as absolute equals
- words are just pointers: a description of a flower is not the flower; descriptions of people are not the people
- the relief of setting down the burden of having to be educated, clever, or always *right*
