Introduction

  
Humans approach living on this planet is from the wrong perspective. I imagine our human ancestors primitively dealt with surviving life by looking at what was right in front of them, acquiring the basics of life to that they might continue living.   This meant continually searching for a place to stay that provided shelter from heat and cold and from preditors, that was close to food and water source being ideal. 

It was also very obvious that going it alone was not the best method.  We found out that we were safer, and better able to survive if we were part of a group of likeminded beings. We were able to pool our limited resources and share our responsibilities.
