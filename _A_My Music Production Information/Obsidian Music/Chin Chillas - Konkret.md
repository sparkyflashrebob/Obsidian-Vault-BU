Got it — **“Konkret” by Chin Chillaz** has a very distinct feel: downtempo, trip-hop inspired, lo-fi groove with dusty textures and a hypnotic vibe. To make something like that, you’d want to focus on **mood, groove, and texture** more than flashy melodies. Here’s a breakdown of how you could build a similar track:

---

### 1. **Drums & Groove**

- **Style**: Laid-back, broken beat, often swung. Think downtempo hip-hop but with jazz/funk syncopation.
    
- **Sound**: Use dusty, lo-fi drum samples (vinyl-ripped kicks, snappy snares, brushed hats).
    
- **Processing**: Saturation, tape emulation, or light vinyl crackle to make it sound aged. Keep the groove _lazy_ and human.
    

---

### 2. **Bassline**

- **Type**: Warm, rounded sub or upright/double-bass sample.
    
- **Feel**: Minimal and repetitive, carrying the groove without stealing attention.
    
- **Processing**: Low-pass filtered, maybe a bit of tape wobble or sidechain compression for movement.
    

---

### 3. **Chords & Harmony**

- **Instruments**: Rhodes, Wurlitzer, or jazzy electric piano samples.
    
- **Chords**: Modal or extended (7ths, 9ths), played slowly with plenty of space.
    
- **Texture**: Let the chords drift, with reverb/delay giving a hazy, urban atmosphere.
    

---

### 4. **Melodic Elements**

- Sparse motifs — maybe a trumpet, sax, or vibraphone sample.
    
- Could also use vocal chops (pitched down or filtered) to create an atmospheric hook.
    
- Keep them repetitive and hypnotic rather than flashy.
    

---

### 5. **Atmosphere & Texture**

- Add field recordings: city sounds, subway ambience, faint chatter.
    
- Layer vinyl noise or tape hiss under the mix.
    
- Use dub-style delay throws to create space and depth.
    

---

### 6. **Arrangement**

- Start minimal with drums + bass.
    
- Gradually introduce chords and atmosphere.
    
- Drop elements in/out to keep hypnotic variety without big builds.
    
- End with texture fading into nothing.
    

---

### 7. **Mixing Style**

- Prioritize warmth and grit over clinical clarity.
    
- Gentle compression for “glue.”
    
- Slightly muffled highs (lo-fi feel).
    
- Wide reverb on melodic parts, but keep drums/bass centered and dry for punch.
    

---

👉 In short: **dusty drums + warm bass + jazzy chords + sparse melodic textures + atmospheric noise** = the “Konkret” vibe.
