**Chase & Status – “Saxon”**, which is one of their iconic tracks from the _More Than Alot_ era (2009). That tune blends **dubstep, grime, and heavy sound system culture** with a stripped-down, weighty production. Here’s how you could approach making something similar:

---

### 1. **Tempo & Structure**

- **Tempo**: Around **140 BPM** (classic dubstep/grime tempo).
    
- **Structure**: Intro → Build → Drop → Mid-section → Second drop → Outro.
    
    - “Saxon” uses a simple, almost hypnotic structure with repetition and layering.
        

---

### 2. **Drums**

- **Kick & Snare**:
    
    - Deep, sub-heavy kick (short, clean low end).
        
    - Snappy, tight snare with a little reverb for space.
        
    - Pattern: Half-time feel → Kick on 1, Snare on 3, with fills and syncopation.
        
- **Hi-Hats & Percussion**:
    
    - Sparse at first, then shuffle patterns and triplet fills.
        
    - Use metallic hats, ride cymbals, and maybe some Jamaican-inspired percussion hits.
        

---

### 3. **Bassline**

- **Sub-bass**: Pure sine or triangle wave, very heavy and clean.
    
- **Mid-bass growls**: Layer distorted reese bass or filtered saws.
    
- Movement: Lots of filter modulation, LFO wobble, but kept minimal — the bassline carries the track without over-complicating.
    

---

### 4. **Samples & Atmosphere**

- **Main Vocal Sample**:
    
    - “Saxon” famously uses a sample from The Clash’s _The Guns of Brixton_.
        
    - Find a similarly gritty, rebellious vocal hook — something with cultural weight.
        
    - Process: EQ → distortion/saturation → reverb/delay for space.
        
- **Atmospheric FX**:
    
    - Air raid sirens, dub delay stabs, tape echoes.
        
    - Vinyl crackle and sound system chatter to keep that “pirate radio / dubplate” vibe.
        

---

### 5. **Sound Design & Processing**

- **Distortion/Saturation**: A must — run basslines and samples through analog-style distortion (Decapitator, FabFilter Saturn, or hardware emulation).
    
- **Reverb & Delay**: Dub-style long delays and spring reverbs.
    
- **Sidechain Compression**: Sub and kick need room — sidechain the bass slightly to the kick.
    
- **Lo-fi Texture**: Add grit, tape hiss, and vinyl noise to give it that underground system feel.
    

---

### 6. **Mixing**

- Keep it **minimal and heavy** — not crowded.
    
- The sub-bass should dominate the low end.
    
- Vocals and FX sit in the mid/highs.
    
- Drums punch through cleanly.
