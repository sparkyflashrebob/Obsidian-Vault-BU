## The 1-Minute Check-In & Questions

Since there are only just a couple/few of us, I would like to start the meeting by giving us an opportunity for a very brief check-in.

   
I invite you to state your name and give a brief, 60-second or less, status report on your internal weather situation, maybe noting that you are happy to be here,  or may that you are experiencing some "tightness in the shoulders,"  have a "foggy mind,"  or "feeling a bit sluggish").   Or anything else you would like to share. 

And, If not, you can ask any clarifying questions about the group flow or the format before we enter into a longer period of silence.   
  
Note that there will be plenty of time in the 2nd half of the meeting for more open discussion about what is covered here today or anything else you would like to bring up.
