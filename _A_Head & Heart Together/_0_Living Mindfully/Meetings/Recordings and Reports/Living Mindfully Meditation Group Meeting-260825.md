## 📌 Executive Summary

The Living Mindfully Meditation Group met on August 25, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM PT, hosted and facilitated by **Will Meacham**. The session combined guided sitting practice, an informal remembrance for **Robert**, a **Qi Gong movement exercise portion led by Mandy Meacham**, and an open inquiry into coincidences, pragmatism, selective attention, and working with physical and emotional difficulties.

Key highlights included:
1. **Remembrance of Robert**: The group honored Robert, a longtime participant who recently passed away, recalling his humor, warmth, and his final meeting before taking a cruise.
2. **Qi Gong Movement Session (Led by Mandy Meacham)**: Mandy guided the movement and somatic exercise portion of the meeting, leading participants through relaxed breathing and Qi Gong forms including *"waving hands like clouds,"* *"scooping the ocean,"* and *"pushing the waves"* with coordinated breathwork.
3. **Skeptic's Inquiry into Synchronicities (Dave Moss)**: Dave Moss emphasized multiple times during the meeting that he approaches life as a **skeptic**. He shared an anomalous experience with a photo appearing unexpectedly on his office iMac screen, seeking rational and psychological explanations for strange coincidences rather than jumping to supernatural conclusions.
4. **William James & Pragmatism (Will Meacham)**: Will connected Dave's skeptical inquiry to William James's philosophy of **Pragmatism**—when an event cannot be proven beyond doubt, we adopt the interpretation that best serves wholesome, present-moment living.
5. **Selective Attention & Vision Quests (Andy)**: Andy explained perceptual filtering using vision quests in the desert: quiet observation reveals aspects of nature and reality that were always present but previously filtered out by the brain.
6. **Community Outreach & Class Promotion**: Mandy Meacham, Will Meacham, and Andy discussed class promotion, balancing Facebook, web pages, and word-of-mouth against high advertising costs.
