Being Uncomfortable




We don't like being uncomfortable 
- not being in control

One of our primary motivators is to avoid what is uncomfortable
It is in our DNA
Our survival extinct

You can test this on a moment to moment basis
Sit here for a few seconds
Notice when you get uncomfortable you move
or at least have the urge to move

Notice how in our seeing
when we get uncomfortable we move our eyes
likewise with all of our senses

Not only that
when we hear something our mind darts over to see what it is
least it be something that can harm us, or find attractive
can I mate with it, eat it or will it kill me

and if not, we are mostly disinterested, or bored with it

when we like what we like
we have this tendency to give it the benefit of the doubt
and to even encourage it
This especially is true when it comes to our view of self

We prop it up 
we don't even want to consider that
because we love ourselves
we need to love ourselves
Our Ego's coming up with these thoughts 
these stories
these explanations
of how we think it is 

And many of us sit in judgement of ourselves and others
because this Ego knows all.

And it doesn't want you to hear anything else

These perceptions and thoughts and feelings and beliefs are real to us
But they are fleeting

As new data comes in 
it can draw our attention
the sense impressions that were so important before 
fade away
because all of them arise and cease 

And then something else takes over the 
I need to attend to this perspective
one after another, over and over
and we hardly notice it
because we aren't mindful for the most part

It is like the illusion of the film screen
frame by frame
or pixel by pixel
painting a picture for us to see
We see the content and forget that it is a series of pictures and sounds
Getting lost in the story playing out in our minds.

What is below our thoughts has an even greater impact on our being
These aspect are the silent ones
our belief systems and views
what we identify with
our programming
sociatal and cultural programming
we hardly notice or challenge them
Are mostly unable to sense them
or if we are
Prop this up

this even affects what we consider right and wrong

 One of my favorite descriptions of the path of practice
 and mindfulness in particular
 Is: "It's like putting on the breaks"
 
 The mind can be spinning out
 But with mindfulness one knows.  
 
 Spinning, Spinning
 Thinking, Thinking
 Feeling, Feeling
 Aware of this, aware of that
 all coming and going
 
 and then there is the One who knows
 We can know all these comings and goings
 endlessly changing things
 The silent watcher behind experience
This is our refugee
This is where we are free

The teachings teach us
Everything is conditioned
Everything has a cause

Everything is here 
and then it is gone
Constantly changing

Most of it is happening without any input from our own actions.
Sit still and see this for yourselves.

There is only so much we can do
regarding the conditioned
Putting on the brakes
being mindful
observant
non reactionary

And things pass away 
on their own

Thing calm down
become quiet
cool down
become still

Except for this Witness
that knows things as they are

Resist what is
and it persists

Its like ringing a bell
things seem to keep going on and on
and never really changing
history repeating itself

Seeing the suffering this causes
is really important
Seeing this in ourselves
or seeing this in others

At some point seeing this
and we will want to do something about it
And that is compassion
Being willing to take action

That is the beautiful thing about being mindful
being observant
being equanimous

this is the kind of action that allows things to pass away
that allows for the mind and heart to come together as one
to clear the way
so that we can see clearly
so that wisdom can shine forth

Mindfulness needs to be present for any progress on the path to be possible.
Yes, this means being with what is difficult.
And yes, this takes some skill.
But it is not beyond us.

We will need to be able to develop compassion for ourselves.
Even if just a little bit to start
We will need to kind to ourselves
Even if a little bit to start 
Despite the circumstances we find ourselves in. 
Despite the state of the world
despite our thoughts about things.

Those will change.
Our hearts can soften
We can heal
We can learn to let go of what is not ours in the first place.
These things that are coming and going.
We can be free in this moment
Peace is possible in this moment
Joy is here too at this moment.
The only moment that we have.
