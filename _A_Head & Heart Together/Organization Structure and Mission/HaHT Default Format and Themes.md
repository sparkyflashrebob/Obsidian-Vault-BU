Welcome
Head and Heart Togethers' purpose is to remind us of 
this simple practice of Calming the heart 
and opening the mind



Here We use this opportunity to take a Sacred Pause 
- to come into Presence 
- to be mindful
	- that is - to come inward
	- to be ardent 
		- to make this a priority in our practice (aka our life)
		- to be aware of our effort and motivations and interest
	- to be alert
		- to diligently observe what is our actual experience
		-  not just our thoughts about things
	- to be mindful
		- to create the habit of remembering this teaching in all moments
		- to be in the hear and now
		- to reflecting on what is skillful and unskillful and to take the appropriate action
		- remembering what our teachers have taught us and what we have taught ourselves
	- to be clearly knowing
		- to know when we are clear minded or not so clear minded
		- when we are confused or not sure what is going on
		- when we are draw towards or wanting to pull away from experience
		- both externally with the things we sense
		- with beauty and ugliness, love and hate, the things we like and also those we don't like
	- And for the period of time of this group
		- doing our best to put aside those things that are external to us (the world) and internal to us (our fears, desires, thoughts, feelings and emotions) that take us away from the sacred
		- You can return to this after this group session
		- But for now putting your effort to be fully present for those of us here and for yourself.
		- To give you this gift.
	- I hope you find this group helpful and supportive, and make it part of your regular spiritual practice. 
	- Our goal is to bring our head and our heart together
	- turn towards this intuitive knowing of what promotes peace and serenity within our selves and the world
	- Not how we think about it - for the mind cannot always be trusted
	- but to build trust thru this mindful loving awareness that is our core
	- this core that knows patience, wisdom and selfless compassion
