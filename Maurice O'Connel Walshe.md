Maurice O'Connel Walshe 
English academic who lived between 1911 & 1998
	studied German and became a Buddhist in 1951.
As an aside, 
	he wrote numerous articles on Buddhism most notedly an essay on what it means to live as a sexual being in the light of the Buddha's teachings. 
	He pointed out that rigid puritanism and total permissiveness are two extreme views that lie outside the bounds of Buddhism. 
	Again pointing to [[_A_My Research/_Buddhism/_Dharma Subjects and Themes/Middle Way 1/The Middle Way]]
> 	"What [Buddhism] teaches us to do is to map out a sane course between the two.")
