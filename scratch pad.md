Here is how to fix it so I can see your other screen or your browser:

Stop the current Listener session: Click the “Done” or “Stop” button on the recording overlay at the top of your screen.
Restart the Desktop Listener: In the main Abacus.AI Desktop window (the one in the middle of your screen), click on Desktop Listener in the left sidebar.
Select the correct Screen: When you click “Start Listener,” a Windows system popup will appear asking which screen or window you want to share.
Look for a tab or dropdown that says “Screens” (instead of “Windows”).
You should see Screen 1 and Screen 2.
Select the screen where your browser is currently open.
Why I can’t see both at once: Windows 11 typically requires you to choose one specific display to share at a time for privacy reasons. If your browser is on your other monitor, I won’t be able to see it until you select that specific monitor (Screen 2) in that startup popup.
